    Common questions
    
    
    Does the EU Deforestation Regulation apply to my business?
    
    
    It applies if you place cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya or wood products listed in Annex I on the EU market, make them available on it, or export them from the EU. Operators carry the main duties; downstream operators and traders carry information and record-keeping duties.
    
    
    When does the EUDR apply?
    
    
    From 30 December 2026 for large and medium enterprises, and from 30 June 2027 for micro and small enterprises, except that micro and small enterprises already covered by the EU Timber Regulation start on 30 December 2026. The date has been postponed more than once, so confirm the text in force.
    
    
    Do I need a due diligence statement?
    
    
    Operators placing products on the market or exporting them must submit a statement with the geolocation of the plots of land. After the 2025 amendment, downstream operators and traders do not file their own, but the first downstream operator collects the reference number.
    
    
    Are printed books still in scope?
    
    
    The December 2025 amendment removed printed products such as books and newspapers (Chapter 49) from Annex I. Confirm against the current Annex I.
    
    
    What is the penalty?
    
    
    Member states set penalties, and the maximum fine must be at least 4% of the operator's EU turnover (Article 25), with confiscation and exclusion from public procurement possible.
