    Questions people ask

    How do I price a stump grinding job?

    Estimate the wood to remove: stump face area times depth below grade plus the height above it, with an allowance for surface roots. Divide by how fast your grinder cuts, scale for hardness, add setup, drive time and per-stump repositioning and cleanup, then cost the crew, machine and truck and divide by one minus overhead and margin. The calculator does that and applies your minimum charge.

    Should I charge per inch of diameter?

    Per-inch pricing is a fair shortcut, but wood removed grows with the square of the diameter and setup is a fixed cost, so a flat rate underprices big stumps and tiny jobs. The quote check compares your per-inch rate to the price the cost build-up needs.

    Why does grinding several stumps on one visit cost less each?

    Setup, loading and the drive are paid once per trip. The bundling table shows the price per stump falling as more stumps share a visit.
