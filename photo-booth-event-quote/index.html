      Common questions

      Why count setup and teardown time when the guest only sees the booth running for the booked hours?

      Because you and your equipment are committed to the job for that time too. A 3-hour package with an hour of setup and 45 minutes of teardown is 4.75 hours on site before travel — pricing only the 3 booked hours quietly cuts your real hourly rate every time.

      Why does the calculator treat print cost as a risk, not just a cost line?

      Unlimited-print packages are quoted from an estimated print count, but the actual count depends on how many guests show up and use the booth — a busy open bar can print far more strips than a seated dinner. The calculator reports what share of your price the print line already represents, so you can see how much room is left before extra prints erase the margin you built the quote on.

      How is the recommended overtime rate different from just charging my hourly rate for the extra time?

      It marks the extra hour up the same way the rest of the quote is marked up — attendant pay plus your overhead rate, divided by your target margin — so an event that runs long still earns the same margin instead of just covering the added labor at cost.

      Does anything about my event details get uploaded?

      No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and nothing you type is saved anywhere — it exists only in the page while it is open. The shareable link is the one thing that travels, and only when you press the button for it: it carries your numbers inside the URL itself rather than uploading them anywhere, so whoever you send it to opens the same quote — send it only to the person you are quoting. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
