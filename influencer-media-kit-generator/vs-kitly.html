What each tool is for

Kitly is an account-based influencer media-kit and analytics platform. Per its own public positioning, you sign up, connect the social accounts you want reflected, and it builds a media kit whose follower counts, engagement rate, and audience breakdown update automatically as your accounts change, alongside campaign tracking and collaboration history — useful if you want one link that stays current without rebuilding it. It offers a free tier for a single connected social account and media kit, with paid tiers unlocking more connected accounts, more media kits, and more reports (Kitly's own site was not reached by a live fetch from this session to confirm today's exact tier names or pricing, so no dollar figure is stated here as verified fact).

This generator does a narrower, one-time job. It needs no account and connects to no social platform. You fill in a form — bio, platforms and reach, audience notes, past partnerships, testimonials, a rate card — and it builds a self-contained HTML media kit and a plain-text one-sheet, both ready to download immediately. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere, and no social account is ever linked; the numbers on the kit are exactly the ones you typed, at the moment you typed them.

Common questions

Should I use this generator or Kitly?

They solve related but different jobs. Kitly is an account-based media-kit and social-analytics platform for content creators: per its own public positioning, you sign up, connect your social accounts, and it builds a media kit whose follower and engagement numbers update automatically as your accounts change, alongside campaign and collaboration tracking. It offers a free tier for a single connected account and media kit, with paid tiers unlocking more social accounts, more media kits, and more reports. This generator does a narrower, one-time job: fill in a form -- bio, platforms, audience, past partnerships, rate card -- by hand, with no account and no social-media account linking of any kind, and it builds a self-contained HTML media kit and a plain-text version to download immediately. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere. If you want your media kit's stats to update automatically as your following grows, an account-based platform like Kitly is built for that. If you want a media kit to send a brand today without connecting any account, this generator does that and nothing more.

Does the downloaded media kit update automatically the way Kitly's does?

No, and that is the deliberate trade. This generator produces a single, self-contained HTML file and a plain-text one-sheet from the numbers you typed at the time you built it -- open it in a browser, attach it to an email, or host it anywhere you like. It will not refresh itself as your follower count changes, because it never connects to your social accounts in the first place; nothing you type is uploaded or linked to a live account. Kitly's core mechanism, per its own public positioning, is the opposite: it connects to your accounts so the numbers on your kit stay current without you rebuilding it. A creator who updates a media kit rarely, or wants full control over exactly what a brand sees with no account anywhere, is better served by this generator; a creator who wants one always-current link is better served by an account-based platform.
