    Common questions
    
    
    What is a creator media kit?
    
    
    A one-page summary a content creator sends to a brand considering a sponsorship — bio, platform reach and engagement, audience demographics, past partnerships, and a rate card — so a brand can evaluate the fit and know what to offer without a back-and-forth email. More background: what belongs in a media kit, and what doesn't.
    
    
    Is this free, and do I need an account?
    
    
    Yes, and no. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored anywhere but your own browser. Fill in the form and download the result — there is no account, no watermark, and no upsell.
    
    
    Where does my photo come from?
    
    
    You paste a link to a photo you already host somewhere (your own site, an Instagram post, a public Google Drive/Dropbox link, an image host). This tool does not accept file uploads, so nothing you have not already made public ever leaves your device.
    
    
    Should I include a rate card if I've never charged for a post before?
    
    
    Yes, even a first guess. A brand reading a media kit with no rates still has to ask what you charge, which is exactly the back-and-forth this tool exists to skip — and a stated starting rate is easy to revise upward once you have a few paid partnerships to compare against.
    
    
    How is this different from Kitly?
    
    
    Kitly links your social accounts for a media kit that updates itself automatically; this tool needs no account and no social-media linking at all — you fill in a form and download a self-contained file right now. See this generator vs. Kitly for the full comparison.
    
    
    Why not just build one in a document editor?
    
    
    You can — a word processor or slide deck holds the same content. What it won't do on its own is warn you a rate card or contact email is missing, or keep a plain-text copy in sync with the formatted one. See this generator vs. assembling one by hand for the full comparison.
