WFS vs seller fulfilled: which fulfillment path leaves more on the same listing?

Sellers search for “WFS vs seller fulfilled” because the referral fee is not the decision. Both Walmart Fulfillment Services and seller-fulfilled listings usually pay the same category referral percentage — and both can hit Walmart’s per-item referral floor on cheap SKUs. The gap is everything around that.

WFS adds a size-tier fulfillment fee, inbound to a Walmart warehouse, and monthly storage while the unit sits. Two-day shipping is Walmart’s problem after that. Seller fulfilled skips those WFS costs and replaces them with the label, packaging, and time you pay to ship the order yourself.

Walmart listings also get squeezed by a lower offer. Matching that price does not change inbound or your cost of goods — it only shrinks the revenue the referral is taken from. On a typical mid-price example — a $24.99 home unit, 15% referral, a standard WFS fulfillment default, $1.10 inbound, one month of storage, and a $4.50 seller label — one path can keep several dollars more than the other, and a $3 match can wipe the unit out.

Rates change by size tier, season, category, and account. This page does not publish Walmart’s live schedule. Common questions

Is WFS or seller fulfilled cheaper on the same listing?

Neither is always cheaper. Both usually pay the same category referral percentage, and both can hit Walmart's per-item referral floor on cheap SKUs, so the decision is everything around that. WFS adds a size-tier fulfillment fee, inbound to a Walmart warehouse, and monthly storage while the unit sits, while seller fulfilled replaces those with the label, packaging, and time you pay to ship the order yourself.

Does matching a lower Walmart offer still leave a profit?

Not always. Matching a lower price does not change inbound or your cost of goods, it only shrinks the revenue the referral is taken from. On a $24.99 home unit at a 15% referral, a $3 match can wipe the unit out. Run the match price through the calculator before you accept it.
