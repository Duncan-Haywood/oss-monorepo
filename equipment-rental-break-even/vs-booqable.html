This calculator vs. Booqable

Two different shapes: Booqable runs your whole rental operation — inventory, online booking, checkout scanning, invoicing — as an ongoing subscription account. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one booking cost, and how many like it does it take to break even — before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Booqable is rental and inventory management software, sold under an ongoing subscription account to operators running a real fleet of rentable equipment. It tracks every asset's availability across your inventory, runs an online booking storefront customers can reserve from directly, checks equipment out and back in with barcode or QR scanning, and generates quotes, contracts, and invoices, all synced across staff and locations. Setting it up means creating an account, importing your inventory, and configuring a storefront and checkout workflow — not a one-off calculation. Booqable's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names, feature tiers, or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not track inventory, run a booking storefront, or scan equipment in or out. It takes one proposed booking — price, rental days, delivery, cleaning, and damage-reserve costs — plus the asset's purchase cost and recovery life, and works out the booking-specific costs, the per-day ownership cost, the minimum price that recovers ownership and your monthly fixed overhead, the price that hits a target contribution margin, and how many bookings like this one it takes each month to break even and what share of your available rental days that consumes. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and nothing you type is uploaded.
