Should I include shipping on Reverb?

A used guitar is listed at $800 plus $75 shipping. You pay $65 to ship it. You paid $450 for the guitar and $10 to set it up. Reverb marketplace is entered at 5% of the item, payment at 2.9% + $0.30 on item plus shipping charged. These are starting points, not live schedules — overwrite them on the calculator.

Reverb marketplace fee sits on the item, not on postage. Free shipping that keeps the same $875 checkout folds the $75 into the list, so marketplace rises from $40.00 to $43.75. Local pickup keeps the $800 list and drops both shipping charged and the $65 you would have spent.

Common questions

Should I include shipping on Reverb or charge the buyer?

On the worked example — $800 list, $75 shipping charged, $65 postage you pay, $450 gear cost, 5% marketplace, 2.9%+$0.30 payment — charging shipping leaves $284.33. Folding the $75 into an $875 list leaves $280.58 because marketplace fee rises by $3.75. Local pickup at $800 leaves $276.50. The included list that matches buyer-pays cash is $879.07.

Does Reverb charge marketplace fees on shipping?

Marketplace and Reverb Gives apply to the item price. Payment processing applies to item plus shipping charged. Baking postage into the list moves that postage onto the marketplace line. Enter the rates from your last payout; this page does not claim Reverb's live schedule.

Does local pickup beat shipping on Reverb?

Local pickup uses your list with no shipping charged and no postage you pay. On the worked example that leaves $276.50 versus $284.33 if the buyer pays $75 shipping and you spend $65 to ship. Pickup can win when postage you pay is larger than shipping charged, or when shipping charged is already zero.
