    Common questions
    Does an evidence pack prove a change order or delay claim?
    No. The footer says it outright: evidence, not a compliance or payment guarantee. It gives you a dated, structured JSON record of what you typed on the day; whether that supports a claim still depends on your contract terms and what actually happened on site.
    What do the flags under the evidence pack mean?
    They point out gaps in what you filled in — no crew listed, no site conditions, or a change/delay entry with no requester or no description — so you notice a thin record before it's the only thing you have. They check completeness, not whether what you wrote is accurate.
    Does my log get saved anywhere, or do I need to download it before closing the tab?
    Download it. The form itself is not auto-saved between visits — only an anonymous count of which buttons you used persists in this browser. Close the tab before downloading and today's entry is gone.
    Does anything I type leave my browser?
    No. The log, the change/delay details, and the generated evidence pack are all built and downloaded locally; nothing is uploaded to a server.
