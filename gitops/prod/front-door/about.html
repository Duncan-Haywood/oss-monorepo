About these tools

275 free calculators and checks for people who run a small business. They run in your browser, they cost nothing, and nothing you type is uploaded.

What this is

A working set of 275 small, single-purpose web tools across 10 areas — pricing a job, checking what a marketplace sale actually nets, planning cashflow, normalising freight quotes, and assembling the evidence a compliance obligation asks for. Each one exists because the arithmetic behind it is easy to get wrong under time pressure and expensive to get wrong in front of a customer.

They are deliberately narrow. A tool here answers one question for one kind of business, rather than being a spreadsheet template that answers none of them well.

What happens to what you type

Nothing. There is no server that could receive it. Every tool on this site is a static page, and the calculation happens in your browser, in JavaScript you can read with your browser's own View Source — no build step, no minification, nothing hidden from you.

  • No account and no sign-up. There is no login, no email capture, and no "enter your address to see the result" gate.
  • Nothing you enter is transmitted. These pages make no network request with your input — no form post, no background beacon.
  • No third-party scripts. No analytics vendor, no tag manager, no advertising network, no hosted fonts, no CDN. The only outside addresses on this site are links you can choose to click.
  • No cookies and no cross-site identifiers.
  • It works offline. Once a tool has loaded, its page keeps working with the connection off — the plainest demonstration that the answer is computed where you are.

What this site's server records

One line per request, and it holds four things: the time, the HTTP status, the path that was asked for, and whether the page was loaded inside somebody else's page as an embed. That is the whole record.

It deliberately does not include your IP address, your user agent, or the page you came from. Those are the fields a normal web server log carries by default and this one leaves out, so "we do not build a profile of you" is a property of the configuration rather than a promise about what we do with it afterwards. The log exists to answer "is anything on this site being used, and is anything broken" — nothing finer.

Why it is free

Because keeping it free costs almost nothing. A static page has no per-user cost, so there is no usage to meter and no reason to build a paywall around arithmetic. There is no paid tier, no trial, and no feature held back — the version you are using is the whole product.

If one of these has saved you money, sponsoring on GitHub is welcome and entirely optional. Declining changes nothing about what any tool does, and nothing here is gated behind it.

What these tools are not

They are estimates and working records, not professional advice and not a determination made on anyone's behalf. They do not replace a quantity surveyor, an accountant, a lawyer, or an engineer, and the output of any of them is a starting figure to check against your own numbers before you quote it to a customer or file it anywhere. The full terms are in the footer of this and every other page.

Embedding a tool on your own page

Every one of the 275 tools here can be framed on someone else's page — a trade association's resource page, a supplier's blog post, a forum's sidebar. Open any tool and scroll to the bottom for a ready-made, copyable snippet built from that page's own address and title; there is nothing to write by hand. The framed page is the calculator's own page rather than a stripped-down copy, so it keeps the tool's full working and its liability notice, and it adds no script, cookie or request of its own. This needs no permission and no message to us first.
