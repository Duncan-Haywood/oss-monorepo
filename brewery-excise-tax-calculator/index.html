    The rates this applies, and what it leaves out

    • $3.50 per barrel on the first 60,000 barrels removed in a calendar year, for a domestic brewer producing 2 million barrels or fewer (IRC 5051(a)(2)).
    • $16 per barrel on the remaining barrels up to 6 million, and $18 above that. A barrel is 31 gallons.
    • Brewers under common control share one 60,000-barrel allotment.
    • Tax attaches when beer is removed for sale or consumption, including taproom sales, not when it is brewed.
    • Not modelled: state and local beer tax, importer and contract-brew assignment rules, tax-free removals and losses, returns and refunds, and your actual filing period. Check current TTB guidance.

    Common questions

    How much is the federal excise tax on beer?

    Federal law taxes beer removed from a brewery at $18 per barrel, but the Craft Beverage Modernization Act lowers that to $16 per barrel on the first 6 million barrels and to $3.50 per barrel on the first 60,000 barrels for a domestic brewer that produces no more than 2 million barrels a year. A barrel is 31 gallons.

    How much federal excise tax is that per keg or case?

    At the $3.50 rate a half-barrel keg carries about $1.75 of federal tax and a 24 x 12 oz case about 25 cents. At $16 the same keg carries $8.00 and the case about $1.16. The calculator works out your own mix.

    What happens when I pass 60,000 barrels?

    Only the barrels above 60,000 move to $16, so the rate on those barrels is about 4.6 times higher. The earlier barrels keep the $3.50 rate. The calculator shows the effective rate and the next-barrel rate.

    Do brewers under common ownership each get 60,000 barrels?

    No. Brewers in a controlled group share a single 60,000-barrel reduced-rate allotment. Enter your share in the allotment box if you split it.

    Is tax due when I brew the beer or when I sell it?

    Beer is taxed when it is removed from the bonded premises for sale or consumption, such as a keg leaving or beer sold in the taproom, not when it is brewed. Enter what you remove.

    How often do I file?

    Brewers whose federal excise tax is expected to be $50,000 or less a year can generally file quarterly, and those at $1,000 or less can generally file annually. Above that it is semi-monthly. Confirm your own eligibility with TTB.
