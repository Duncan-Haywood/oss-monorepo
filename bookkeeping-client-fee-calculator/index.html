    Common questions

    Is this a market rate or tax advice?

    No. Every volume, minute per task, hourly rate and software cost in the answer is a number you typed. It does not look up what other bookkeepers charge and gives no tax, accounting or legal advice. It does the arithmetic on your own time so you can see what a client costs you.

    Does it upload my clients?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    How is the minimum monthly fee calculated?

    Monthly minutes are transactions x your minutes per transaction for the state of the books, plus reconciliation per account, payroll runs, AR and AP items, sales-tax filings and a fixed review block. Hours x your target hourly rate plus software is the minimum; the recommended fee adds your buffer.

    What is the walk-away price?

    Hours x a floor rate (a percent of your target, 75 percent by default) plus software. Below it the client costs you more than the work is worth to you, so you decline or re-scope rather than discount.

    How does the roster mode work?

    Paste one row per existing client with the monthly fee, the hours you actually spend and optional software cost. It ranks clients by effective hourly, worst first, and shows the fee increase each needs to reach your target rate.
