    Common questions
    
    
    What is the Personal Savings Allowance?
    
    
    The amount of savings interest you can earn tax-free: £1,000 for basic-rate taxpayers, £500 for higher-rate taxpayers and nothing for additional-rate taxpayers.
    
    
    What is the 0% starting rate for savings?
    
    
    Up to £5,000 of interest is taxed at 0% if your other taxable income (after the personal allowance) is small. Every £1 of other taxable income above the personal allowance reduces it by £1, and it is gone at £17,570 of other income.
    
    
    Does the bank take the tax off?
    
    
    No. Banks and building societies pay interest gross. If tax is due you tell HMRC through Self Assessment, or HMRC adjusts your tax code or sends a P800.
    
    
    Does interest in a cash ISA count?
    
    
    No. Interest inside a cash ISA is ignored for income tax and does not use any of the allowances above.
    
    
    Which tax year and nations does this cover?
    
    
    2026/27 rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Scottish income tax bands differ and are not modelled; check GOV.UK.
