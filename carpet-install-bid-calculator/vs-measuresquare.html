This calculator vs. MeasureSquare

Two different shapes: MeasureSquare is a subscription takeoff and estimating platform for a flooring company's whole measuring and quoting workflow. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one carpet install cost, given its floor yards, stairs, and scope, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

MeasureSquare is a flooring takeoff and estimating platform used by flooring contractors and retailers to measure a job from a floor plan, a laser scanner, or a mobile app, generate a material list and waste report across carpet, tile, hardwood, and vinyl, and produce a bid proposal, purchase order, and work order from that takeoff. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. MeasureSquare's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — flooring takeoff and estimating software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for MeasureSquare appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what MeasureSquare does. It does not measure a floor plan, generate a purchase order, or hold a takeoff record on a server. It takes one carpet install — floor square feet converted to yards, stair count priced per step, a choice between stretch-in, glue-down, double-stick, and carpet tile, solid-versus- patterned waste, tear-out, a furniture move, and haul-away — and turns that into the job price, the price per yard, and the walk-away price below which a flat per-yard special loses money, plus a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or MeasureSquare?

They do different jobs, and a flooring company of any size may end up using both. MeasureSquare is a flooring takeoff and estimating platform used by flooring contractors and retailers to measure a job from a floor plan, a laser scanner, or a mobile app, generate a material list and waste report across carpet, tile, hardwood, and vinyl, and produce a bid proposal, purchase order, and work order from that takeoff, sold as an ongoing subscription. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from floor square feet converted to yards, stair count priced per step, a choice between stretch-in, glue-down, double-stick, and carpet tile, solid-versus-patterned waste, tear-out and a furniture move, it turns that into the job price, the price per yard, and the walk-away price below which a flat per-yard special loses money, plus a printable customer proposal, before any of it touches a takeoff or measuring system. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A flooring company already measuring and quoting every job type across a whole showroom is better served by MeasureSquare or a comparable takeoff platform; an installer who wants an instant, private, correctly-estimated price for one carpet job before a bid goes out is in the right place here.
