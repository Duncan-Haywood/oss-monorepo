    Common questions

    What is the short-term rental tax loophole?

    If a rental's average guest stay is 7 days or less it is not a rental activity under the passive-loss rules, so it is passive only if you fail to materially participate. Materially participate and the loss, including bonus depreciation from a cost segregation study, is non-passive and can offset wages and other income, subject to the at-risk, basis and excess business loss limits.

    How is the average guest stay calculated?

    Divide the total number of days in all rental periods during the year by the number of rentals (IRS Publication 925). 210 rented days over 60 stays is an average of 3.5 days. The threshold is 7 days or less, or 30 days or less if you also provide significant personal services.

    What counts as material participation for a short-term rental?

    You need to meet one of the seven tests in Treasury Regulation 1.469-5T. The three this tool computes are: more than 500 hours; your participation is substantially all of everyone's participation; or more than 100 hours and not less than any other individual, including cleaners and managers. Your spouse's hours count as yours. Keep a contemporaneous time log.

    Does the $25,000 rental loss allowance apply to a short-term rental?

    Only if the property is still a rental activity. The $25,000 allowance (for active participation) phases out by 50 cents per dollar of modified adjusted gross income between $100,000 and $150,000 and is not indexed for inflation. IRS Publication 925 limits it to rental real estate activities, so a short-term rental with a 7-day average that fails material participation does not get it.

    How much can a cost segregation study add in the first year?

    It reclassifies part of the building basis into 5, 7 and 15-year property, which qualifies for 100% bonus depreciation when the property was acquired after January 19, 2025. The rest of the building is depreciated straight-line over 27.5 years (39 if the property is treated as nonresidential transient lodging) with the mid-month convention.

    What limits can stop the loss from offsetting my other income?

    Even a non-passive loss is subject to the at-risk and basis limits and to the excess business loss limit, which for 2026 is $256,000 for single filers and $512,000 for joint filers. Any excess becomes a net operating loss carryforward. A passive loss that cannot be used is suspended and carried forward.
