This calculator vs. Buildxact

Two different shapes: Buildxact is account-based estimating and project-management software for running a construction business across trades and jobs. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this hang-and-finish job cost, given its rooms and finish level, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Buildxact is construction estimating and project-management software founded in Australia in 2011, with North American headquarters in Austin, Texas. It serves builders, remodelers, and contractors across trades — including carpentry, painting, roofing, and flooring — combining material takeoffs, quoting, job costing, scheduling, and client communication into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every trade on the crew. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Buildxact's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — multi-trade construction estimating and project-management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Buildxact appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Buildxact does. It does not manage takeoffs across trades, schedule crews, track job costs over time, or store any project record on a server. It takes one drywall job's rooms — each with wall and optional ceiling area net of openings — a waste percentage, a ceiling-height labor multiplier, Gypsum Association finish levels 0-5, texture, tear-out of existing board, add-ons, and the drive, plus loaded wage, dumpster, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price per hung square foot, the sheet count, the labor-hour split between hang and finish, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
