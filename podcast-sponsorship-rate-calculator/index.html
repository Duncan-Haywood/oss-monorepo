    Common questions

    How much should I charge for a podcast ad?

    It depends on your average downloads per episode, niche, which ad placement you're selling, and whether it's host-read or produced. This calculator applies commonly cited market CPM-per-1000-downloads ranges to your own numbers to give a starting price range for a pre-roll, mid-roll, or post-roll spot.

    Why does this calculate price from downloads instead of subscriber or listener count?

    Sponsors are paying for a spot that actually got served. Podcast ad marketplaces and network rate cards routinely price against downloads in the first 7 days of an episode (the industry-standard IAB/Podtrac window), not a lifetime subscriber count that includes people who stopped listening long ago. See why that gap can mean a 4x overask if you price off the wrong number.

    Is this price a guarantee of what a sponsor will pay?

    No. It is a starting point for negotiation based on general market ranges, not a quote, valuation, or offer performed on your behalf. Actual rates vary by audience quality, host endorsement strength, niche demand, and how much a specific sponsor wants your specific audience this quarter.

    What is the difference between a host-read and a produced ad?

    A host-read ad is read live by the host in their own voice and words, usually woven into the episode — it commands a premium because listeners trust the host's personal endorsement. A produced ad is a pre-recorded, studio-produced spot dropped into the episode, similar to traditional radio or streaming audio ads, and typically prices lower.
