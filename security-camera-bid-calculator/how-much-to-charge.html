How much should I charge to install security cameras?

There is no flat per-camera price, because the same eight-camera job can be two very different labor tickets. Eight cameras through an accessible attic is one job. Eight through masonry with a lift, longer runs, and a switch that has to be sized for the PoE load is several times the labor — before either quote has priced the storage the customer is actually paying for.

What a per-camera price cannot see

Access and construction type. Accessible attic runs and masonry runs with a lift are not the same drop labor, even at the same cable length.

Storage math. Eight 4 MP cameras on H.265, recording continuously, use roughly 0.35 TB per day — about 10.4 TB for 30 days of retention before usable-space headroom. Quoting "30 days of storage" without doing that math is quoting a number the drives may not actually hold.

PoE budget and run length. A switch has a wattage budget the cameras have to fit inside, and Ethernet has a hard run-length limit. Both are pass/fail checks a per-camera price skips entirely.

A bid you can defend

  1. Price labor hours per drop by construction type, not a flat per-camera rate.
  2. Size storage from resolution, codec, camera count, and hours recorded per day, then check it against the drives actually being quoted.
  3. Check PoE load against the switch budget and flag any run past the Ethernet limit.
  4. Load the wage, then add overhead, and price = cost ÷ (1 − target margin) for a walk-away number.

Worked example

Eight 4 MP cameras on H.265, recording continuously for a 30-day retention target, need roughly 10.4 TB before usable-space headroom — a number many quotes skip entirely in favor of "whatever drive comes with the NVR." Separately, the actual drives a customer is willing to pay for may deliver fewer retention days than promised, and a switch sized for eight cameras' PoE draw is a different bill of materials than one sized for four. The calculator checks storage, PoE budget, and run length together, so the gap between "30 days quoted" and "30 days delivered" doesn't surface at the handover.
