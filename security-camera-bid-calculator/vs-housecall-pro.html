This calculator vs. Housecall Pro

Two different shapes: Housecall Pro is an account-based platform for running a home service company's whole operation, including scheduling the crew and invoicing the customer once a job is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one camera install cost, and do the storage, PoE, and cable-run numbers on the quote actually hold up — before you send it.

What each tool is for

Housecall Pro is a field service management platform used by home service businesses — lawn care, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, security and other trades that dispatch technicians among them — to run client management and online quoting, scheduling and crew dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client-facing hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Housecall Pro's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that kind of field service software describe scheduling, invoicing and collecting payment for a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe resolving camera count, retention, PoE budget, and run length into a correct price before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Housecall Pro does. It does not manage a client list, dispatch a crew across a season, or collect a payment. It takes one camera job's count and construction type (accessible attic, drop ceiling, masonry, or a lift job), resolution, codec, frame rate and the motion-only share of the day, the retention days you are quoting and the drives you actually plan to buy, the PoE switch's rated budget and each camera's draw, run length, and your loaded installer wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the storage the quoted retention needs versus the days the fitted drives will actually hold, the PoE headroom against an 80% planning ceiling, any run past the 328 ft Ethernet limit, the installed price, the price per camera, the walk-away floor, and a printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Should I use this calculator or Housecall Pro?

They do different jobs, and a security camera installer of any size will likely end up touching both. Housecall Pro is a field service management platform used across home service trades to run client management and online quoting, scheduling and dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs.
