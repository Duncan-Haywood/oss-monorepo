How much does Fiverr actually take?

Unlike Upwork's fee, this one is short to answer: Fiverr keeps a flat 20% of every order. There are no tiers, no volume discount, and no difference by seller level — a brand-new seller and a Top Rated Seller both pay the same 20%. It applies to the whole order value, including tips and any extras a buyer adds, not just the base package price.

It was not always flat. Before late 2022, Fiverr ran a tiered commission structure that rewarded sellers with more lifetime order volume with a lower cut, reportedly falling toward the 10% range for its highest tiers. Retiring that structure in favor of one flat rate for every seller was a real pay cut for the sellers who had worked their way into a lower tier — worth knowing if an older page or forum post still describes a tiered Fiverr fee as current.

The 20% commission is also not the only figure between an order and your bank account. Fiverr holds order funds in escrow for a clearance period after the order is marked complete — around 14 days for most sellers, shorter for Top Rated Sellers — before they can be withdrawn. Withdrawing to PayPal carries no fee from Fiverr, though PayPal can charge its own currency-conversion fee if your account's default currency differs from the payout currency; withdrawing by bank transfer carries a flat fee of a few dollars per withdrawal. Those are separate from, and on top of, the 20% order commission.

Common questions

What percentage does Fiverr take from sellers in 2026?

A flat 20%. Fiverr keeps 20% of every order's value, with no tiers, no volume discount, and no exception for seller level (New Seller through Top Rated Seller all pay the same rate). The 20% also applies to tips and to any extras added to an order, not just the base package price.

Has Fiverr's seller fee always been flat 20%?

No. Before late 2022, Fiverr ran a tiered commission structure that rewarded sellers with more lifetime volume with a lower cut, reportedly as low as around 10% for its highest tiers. That structure was retired in favor of the single flat 20% every seller pays today, which was a real pay cut for the sellers who had worked their way into a lower tier.

Does Fiverr charge a fee to withdraw earnings?

It depends on the payout method, and it is separate from the 20% order commission. Withdrawing to PayPal carries no Fiverr-side fee, though PayPal itself can charge a currency-conversion fee (commonly a few percent) if your PayPal account's default currency differs from the payout currency. Bank transfer carries a flat fee of a few dollars per withdrawal. Fiverr also holds funds in escrow for a clearance period after an order completes — around 14 days for most sellers, shorter for Top Rated Sellers — before they can be withdrawn at all.

How do I work out what I actually keep from a Fiverr order?

Take the order total (including any tip and extras), subtract Fiverr's flat 20%, then subtract any withdrawal fee for the payout method you use and whatever share you set aside for taxes.
