    Common questions

    How much does Upwork actually take from freelancers?

    Since May 1, 2025, Upwork no longer uses its old 20%/10%/5% lifetime-billing tiers. It now charges a variable freelancer service fee between 0% and 15% per contract, set by Upwork’s own pricing system per client relationship and shown to you only when you send a proposal or receive an offer. There is no public formula this page — or anyone else’s — can compute for you: type in the percentage Upwork actually quoted you for this contract, and the page runs the arithmetic from there.

    How much does Fiverr take from sellers?

    Fiverr charges a flat 20% seller commission on every order, with no tiers and no volume discount, and it applies to tips as well as the order price. That figure is public and has not changed in years, so this page uses it as the default — you can override it if Fiverr changes its rate. See how much Fiverr actually takes for withdrawal fees, the escrow clearance period, and the tiered structure Fiverr retired in 2022.

    Should I list a job on Upwork or Fiverr?

    That depends on your own quoted Upwork fee for that client, since Upwork’s fee varies and Fiverr’s does not. Enter the same job price on both sides of this calculator with your actual quoted Upwork percentage, and it reports which platform nets you more for that specific job, by how much.

    What price should I charge to take home a specific amount after fees?

    Pick a platform and type a take-home goal, and the page solves backwards through that platform’s fee, its withdrawal fee, and your tax reserve to the job price that would leave you with that goal. It is the reverse of the main comparison: instead of pricing forward from a job price, it prices backward from what you want to keep.

    How much should I set aside for taxes as an Upwork or Fiverr freelancer?

    This page will not tell you a percentage — that depends on your filing status, state, total income, deductions, and quarterly-estimate schedule, and picking one for you would be a claim about what you owe. What it does is arithmetic once you tell it your own reserve percentage: it holds that share out of proceeds after the platform fee and withdrawal fee, on each platform, so you can see what a reserve actually does to a real job’s take-home. Ask a tax preparer for your percentage.
