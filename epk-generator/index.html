    Common questions

    What is an electronic press kit (EPK)?

    A one-page summary a musician or band sends to venues, festivals, playlisters, and press — bio, photo, streaming/social links, upcoming shows, and any press coverage — so a booker or writer can evaluate the act without a back-and-forth email. More background: what belongs in an EPK, and what doesn't.

    Is this free, and do I need an account?

    Yes, and no. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored anywhere but your own browser. Fill in the form and download the result — there is no account, no watermark, and no upsell.

    Where does my photo come from?

    You paste a link to a photo you already host somewhere (your own site, an Instagram post, a public Google Drive/Dropbox link, an image host). This tool does not accept file uploads, so nothing you have not already made public ever leaves your device.

    What do I do with the generated file?

    Download the HTML file and host it anywhere (your own site, GitHub Pages, or just attach it to an email), or copy the plain-text version straight into an email or a festival submission form.

    Why not just build one myself in a document editor?

    You can — see this generator vs. assembling one by hand for what a blank document does and doesn't catch.
