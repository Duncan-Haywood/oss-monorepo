This calculator vs. Cents

Two different shapes: Cents runs your whole wash-dry-fold and delivery operation — point of sale, routes, online ordering, marketing — as an ongoing subscription account. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what does a pound actually cost you, and does your price cover it — before you set or repost a rate.

What each tool is for

Cents is laundry business management software, sold under an ongoing subscription account to laundromat and laundry delivery operators. It runs point-of-sale for counter and drop-off orders, manages pickup-and-delivery routes and driver dispatch, offers an online ordering storefront customers can book from directly, and includes marketing tools to bring customers back, all synced across staff and locations. Setting it up means creating an account, configuring your services and delivery zones, and onboarding your team — not a one-off calculation. Cents' own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names, feature tiers, or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not take orders, dispatch drivers, or run a storefront. It takes your labour minutes per pound, fixed minutes per order, wash and dry cycle costs, supplies, delivery stops, and a share of overhead, and works out your cost per pound, the break-even price per pound, the margin at what you charge today, and the monthly volume a profit goal needs. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and nothing you type is uploaded.
