    Questions people ask

    How do I price handmade pottery?

    Start from cost: clay, glaze, your share of the bisque and glaze firings, your time and studio rent, all divided by the pieces that survive. Then divide by one minus your target margin and any selling fees. The calculator does that and shows the wholesale price too.

    How much does a kiln firing cost per piece?

    Kilowatt-hours times your electricity rate, plus wear on elements and furniture, divided by the pieces in the load. A half-empty kiln roughly doubles the firing cost of every piece in it, which the load table shows.

    Should I include breakage in my prices?

    Yes. A cracked or crawled piece has already used clay, two firings and your hours. The calculator charges that loss to the pieces that survive.

    Is this accounting or tax advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in. Sales tax, business structure, insurance and income tax are out of scope.
