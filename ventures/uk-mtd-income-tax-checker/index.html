Common questions

    
Who has to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?

    
Sole traders and landlords whose gross self-employment plus property income is more than GBP 50,000 (tax year 2024-25) from 6 April 2026, more than GBP 30,000 (2025-26) from 6 April 2027, and more than GBP 20,000 (2026-27) from 6 April 2028.

    
Is the threshold profit or turnover?

    
Gross income before expenses. Employment income, dividends, interest and partnership profit do not count; for jointly held property only your share counts.

    
What do I have to do?

    
Keep digital records, send four quarterly updates through compatible software (due 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May), and submit a final declaration by 31 January after the tax year, which replaces the Self Assessment return.

    
Can I join early?

    
Yes. Voluntary sign-up is open to anyone with self-employment or property income.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent.
