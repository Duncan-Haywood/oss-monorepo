# UK Making Tax Digital Checker

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-mtd-income-tax-checker/>

Free, private browser tool: enter your gross self-employment and property income for tax years 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27 and see whether Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is mandatory for you, from which date (6 April 2026, 2027 or 2028), your four quarterly update deadlines and your final declaration date.

After using this, you know the date you must start keeping digital records and the exact dates to file by, which used to mean reading three separate GOV.UK pages and an accountant's email.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-mtd-income-tax-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
