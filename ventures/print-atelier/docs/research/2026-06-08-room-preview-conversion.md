# Room Preview Conversion Research — 2026-06-08

## Why this matters

Print Atelier sells visual goods where buyer uncertainty is concentrated in scale, room fit, color mood, and confidence that the print will look premium on a real wall. A room-preview experiment is therefore not just a buyer feature; it is a demand-side CAC lever that should feed channel forecasts before paid spend scales.

## Sources

- Saatchi Art launched a web-based "View in My Room" experience that lets users visualize more than 1 million original works on their own wall with no app download. The case study frames AR as a way to improve buyer confidence and notes that buyers using Saatchi Art's AR feature are significantly more likely to purchase. Source: [Rock Paper Reality — Saatchi Art WebAR case study](https://rockpaperreality.com/our-work/saatchi-art/).
- Shopify describes 3D ecommerce as a way for shoppers to see what a product will look like in a physical space, including an art example where a phone camera layers a true-scale item onto the buyer's wall. Shopify reports merchants using 3D commerce see an average 94% conversion increase and cites a 2021 study where 40% of online shoppers were willing to pay more for 3D ecommerce experiences. Source: [Shopify — 3D ecommerce](https://www.shopify.com/blog/3d-ecommerce).
- Rebecca Minkoff's Shopify Plus case study reports shoppers who interacted with a 3D model were 44% more likely to add to cart and 27% more likely to order; shoppers who used AR were 65% more likely to purchase. Source: [Shopify — Rebecca Minkoff 3D/AR case study](https://www.shopify.com/au/case-studies/rebecca-minkoff).
- Cylindo's Interior Define case study reports web AR adoption rates 33 times higher than a traditional AR mobile app and says customers who engage with Cylindo AR are eight times more likely to convert than those who do not. Source: [Cylindo — Interior Define case study](https://www.cylindo.com/customer-case-studies/interior-define/).

## Product implication

Use conservative, operator-tunable lift assumptions. The first Print Atelier version should not claim guaranteed AR performance; it should model how preview engagement would change blended conversion and effective CAC, then block spend on listings without approved proofs or room mockups.
