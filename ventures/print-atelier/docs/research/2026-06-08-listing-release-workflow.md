# Listing release workflow research — 2026-06-08

## Sources checked

- [INPRNT Artist Terms of Use & Code of Conduct](https://www.inprnt.com/info/artist_terms/)
- [BetterShared Artist Terms of Use](https://bettershared.co/pages/artist-terms-of-use)

## Takeaways

Artist marketplace terms commonly separate copyright ownership from the platform license: INPRNT says artists retain rights except for the license granted to the platform, while requiring a non-exclusive, sublicensable license to reproduce, display, distribute, create products, and market the artwork [INPRNT Artist Terms](https://www.inprnt.com/info/artist_terms/).

BetterShared uses a similar structure: artists retain copyright while granting a non-exclusive worldwide license for platform operation, order fulfillment, storefront promotion, social media, and paid advertising [BetterShared Artist Terms](https://bettershared.co/pages/artist-terms-of-use).

Payout language is strongest when tied to a concrete formula and schedule: BetterShared defines artist payout as net profit after sales commission, production costs, shipping, and card handling fees, paid weekly four weeks after order completion [BetterShared Artist Terms](https://bettershared.co/pages/artist-terms-of-use/).

Society6 also requires a license to promote and sell works through its channels, and its community guidance says artists retain full rights to published content while granting Society6 distribution rights for products and collaborations [Society6 Terms](https://society6.com/policies/terms-of-service), [Society6 Community Guidelines](https://help.society6.com/en-US/community-guidelines-2567793).

Society6 frames artist earnings around product-specific markups, with several wall-art products listed at a 10% markup and other products at 5%, which reinforces the need for Print Atelier to show why 30% of net profit is better on a SKU-by-SKU basis before outreach copy uses that promise [Society6 earnings FAQ](https://help.society6.com/en-US/how-much-do-i-earn-on-the-sale-of-my-products-1261644).

## Product implication

Print Atelier should not expose artist-facing payout proof as static copy. The admin release workflow should first prove SKU economics, proof status, room-scene consent readiness, and payout benchmark advantage. When a listing is blocked, artist-facing copy should explicitly be withheld until ops or finance clears the blocker.
