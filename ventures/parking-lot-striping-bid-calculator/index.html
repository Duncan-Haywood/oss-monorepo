    Common questions

    Does this tell me how many accessible stalls I need?

    No. ADA and local-ordinance accessible-stall counts depend on total lot size and jurisdiction, and this calculator does not look them up. Type the ADA stall count your plan or inspector requires and the tool prices the hash aisle, symbol, and sign work around it — it does not decide how many you need.

    Why does a per-stall special undercut this number?

    A flat “$8 a stall” price assumes every stall is the same, but ADA hash aisles, new-layout string lines, a second coat, and night work all add hours a restripe of faded 4-inch line does not have. Use “Compare ADA as regular stalls” to see the exact dollars and hours a flat rate would be missing on this lot.

    Where do the coverage numbers (360 lf/gal, 18 lf/stall) come from?

    They are common ballpark figures for 4-inch traffic paint and a standard 90-degree stall, not a manufacturer spec or an industry standard. Your paint, nozzle, and lot condition will differ — edit them in calc.mjs or just watch how the gallons and price move as you change stall counts, and true them up against your own paint tickets.

    Does anything I type here get sent anywhere?

    No. The bid, your wage and margin inputs, and any saved company details for the proposal all stay in this browser’s local storage. A bid link you copy encodes those numbers directly in the URL, so treat a shared link like a rate sheet, not a private note.
