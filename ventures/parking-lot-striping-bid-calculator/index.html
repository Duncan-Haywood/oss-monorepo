FOR PARKING LOT STRIPING CONTRACTORS

Bid the lines, the ADA hash, and the coats.

Price a pavement-marking job the way the trade actually loses money: stall lines, hashed ADA aisles, arrows, fire lanes, and whether this is a restripe or a new layout. You get the lot price, what it works out to per stall, the extra an $8-a-stall special hides, and the number below which the job loses money.

Also read how much to charge to stripe a parking lot.
