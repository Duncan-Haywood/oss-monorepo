# Bowling Center Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/bowling-center-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people planning or running a bowling center: lanes, opening hours, peak and off-peak lane-hour prices and how full each is, league discounts, parties and events, food and drink, arcade and shoe rental, staff hours, rent, build cost and a loan become monthly profit at planned bookings, break-even lane utilization, the price change that reaches a target margin, how slower bookings, price changes and wage rises move profit, a sixty-month ramp-up with the cash reserve and payback, and loan coverage.

After using this, you know what share of your lane-hours you have to sell to pay rent, staff and the loan, how much cash to hold while bookings ramp up, and how much of the plan rides on peak evenings.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/bowling-center-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
