Questions

What is the penalty for depositing payroll taxes late?

Under IRC section 6656 the failure-to-deposit penalty is 2% of the late amount if it is 1 to 5 days late, 5% if 6 to 15 days late, and 10% if more than 15 days late. It rises to 15% if the amount is still unpaid more than 10 days after the first IRS notice or the day the IRS demands immediate payment. Interest is charged on top.

What is the trust fund recovery penalty?

Income tax withheld from employees and the employee share of Social Security and Medicare are held in trust for the government. If they are not paid over, the IRS can assess a penalty equal to 100% of the unpaid trust-fund amount against each responsible person personally, under IRC section 6672. The tool shows the trust-fund portion of any deposit still unpaid, using the share you set.

Can the penalty be reduced or removed?

Sometimes. The IRS can abate penalties for reasonable cause, and first-time penalty abatement may be available to filers with a clean recent record. Whether you qualify depends on facts this tool cannot see; ask a tax professional or the IRS.

Does waiting to pay make it worse?

Yes. Each late deposit moves up a penalty tier at day 6 and day 16 and keeps accruing interest. The calculator shows how many days remain before each unpaid deposit steps up, and what 30 more days would cost.
