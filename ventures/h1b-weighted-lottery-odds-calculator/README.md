# H-1B Weighted Lottery Odds Calculator

Free, private browser tool for employers, founders and candidates: list candidates with their wage level and whether they hold a US master's or higher, enter your guess at the registration pool, and see each person's estimated chance of selection under the weighted H-1B lottery (Level I = 1 entry ... Level IV = 4), versus the old flat lottery, and what one wage level higher is worth.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/h1b-weighted-lottery-odds-calculator/

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/h1b-weighted-lottery-odds-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

Not legal advice. The model approximates successive weighted draws; the default pool is illustrative, not data. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
