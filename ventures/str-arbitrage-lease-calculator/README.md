# STR Arbitrage Lease Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/str-arbitrage-lease-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people who lease a unit to sublet it by the night: rent, utilities, furnishing and setup cost, nightly rate, occupancy, stay length, cleaning fee, turnover cost and commission become profit in a steady month, break-even occupancy, the month furnishing is earned back, what the lease nets after ramp-up and resale, the occupancy and nightly rate needed to net zero over the lease, and what a rent increase does.

After using this, you know whether a rental-arbitrage unit earns back its furniture before the lease ends, and what occupancy or rate makes it work.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/str-arbitrage-lease-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not legal, tax or financial advice. Every figure is your own input. Check that your lease and city allow short-term subletting. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
