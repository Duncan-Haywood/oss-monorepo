Price the drive and the stations, not the house.

A 2,000 sq ft house with eight rodent stations and a 22-minute drive is not the same stop as an exterior spray next door. This calculator times the work on the lot, adds the drive both ways, and shows the initial invoice, the recurring stop, the hourly rate once drive is inside the number, and how many later visits recover a free initial.

Route and costs

Questions operators actually ask

How do I price a pest control route stop?

Time the work on the lot, add the drive both ways, add chemicals and station materials, then load wage burden, overhead, and the margin you keep. House size is a sales phrase, not a cost driver.

Why does drive time change the bid?

A 22-minute one-way drive is 44 minutes on every visit. On a cheap quarterly that is often more than a third of the stop. The hourly rate with drive is the number to check before you accept the house.

Should I give away the initial?

Only if later stops can recover the first-visit cost. Station installs and knockdown spray often cost more than a year of profit on a cheap quarterly. Tick the box above to see how many later visits it takes.
