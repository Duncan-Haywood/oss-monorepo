This calculator vs. PestPac

Two different shapes: PestPac is an account-based platform for running a pest control company's whole operation, including scheduling and billing a route stop once it is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this stop cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

PestPac, sold by WorkWave, is a field service management platform built specifically for pest control businesses, from single-truck operators to national multi-branch companies. It is used to run CRM and customer records, scheduling and routing, billing and payments, marketing, reporting, and a technician mobile app, tied to one account used on an ongoing paid basis across every stop the company runs. PestPac's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that scheduling and billing software describe running, routing and invoicing a stop once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a new stop should cost from station counts, on-site minutes and drive time before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what PestPac does. It does not store a customer record, build a route across a season, or invoice anyone. It takes one stop's services — each one's on-site minutes, its rodent or insect station count, and its material cost — plus one-way drive time, service frequency, technician wage, payroll burden, vehicle cost, overhead and target margin, and turns that into the initial invoice, the recurring invoice, the hourly rate once drive time is inside it, the walk-away recurring price, and how many later visits it takes to recover a waived initial — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or PestPac?

They do different jobs, and a pest control company of any size will likely end up touching both. PestPac is a field service management platform built for pest control businesses and sold by WorkWave as an ongoing subscription, used to run CRM and customer records, scheduling and routing, billing and payments, marketing, and a technician mobile app across a whole operation. This calculator does one narrower thing: it takes a route stop's services, each one's on-site minutes, rodent and insect station counts and material cost, plus one-way drive time, technician wage, payroll burden, vehicle cost, overhead and target margin, and turns that into the initial invoice, the recurring invoice, the hourly rate once drive time is inside it, and how many later visits it takes to recover a waived initial, before you have quoted anything. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A company already scheduling routes and billing customers across many stops is better served by PestPac or a comparable field service platform; an operator who needs an instant, private answer to what one stop should cost before it is offered is in the right place here.
