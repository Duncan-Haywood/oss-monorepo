How much to charge to install a sump pump

The number a homeowner wants is a published “$450 pump swap” special, or a price they saw for a replacement on a coupon site. That number is not a takeoff. A 1/3 HP swap in an existing pit with fifteen included feet of discharge is not the same job as a new basin cut through a concrete slab, a 1/2 HP pump, forty feet of discharge, a battery backup, and a high-water alarm. The trip is the same. The hours are not.

Count the pit and the backup, not just the pump

Basement-waterproofing calculators price interior drain tile, French drains, and crawl encapsulation. Well-pump calculators price depth, a pitless adapter, and a pressure tank. A sump install is a different job: the pump, the pit, the discharge past the included first fifteen feet, and the backup that is not in the coupon. Quoting all of that from a published swap special is how the first saw cut eats the field margin.

A job with the same trip can cost much more

Each extra foot of discharge past the included first fifteen is time that never appears on the coupon. A new pit is billed as its own hours. A concrete slab cut, a battery backup, and an alarm are adders that get forgotten on a brochure special. On the sample rates a new-slab 1/2 HP install with forty feet of discharge, a battery backup, and an alarm prices about $3,455.03 (12.2 crew-hours, walk-away $2,418.52). The same shop’s existing-pit 1/3 HP swap prices about $738.90 (2.5 hours, walk-away $517.23). Matching the brochure job’s $450 special on the slab house is how that job becomes a loss — the $450 special already loses $67.23 even on the brochure swap.

Battery backup and a dirt-crawl new pit are not automatically a markup on the swap

Adding a battery backup and an alarm to the existing-pit swap is not the $450 special plus 15%. On the sample rates that package prices about $1,644.70 (3.9 crew-hours). A new dirt-crawl 3/4 HP install with a water-powered backup, fifty feet of discharge, freeze protection, and a permit prices about $3,443.62 (11.9 hours). Treating either as a blended add-on is how the special that looked fine turns into a loss after the first basin set.

Load the wage, then keep a walk-away number

A $42/h installer loaded at 34% payroll burden is $56.28. Margin is a share of the price you send, not a markup on cost. The walk-away price is total cost — below that the job makes nothing before you take a dollar home. Replace every rate with your invoices before you send either number.

What this is not

This is not a waterproofing design, a drainage plan, a building-code determination, a permit, an engineering stamp, or a recommended market price. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type. Pump size, pit type, discharge, backup, alarm, and freeze protection are yours to confirm. It does not price a basement waterproofing system or a well-pump replacement.

Common questions

How much to charge to install a sump pump?

Charge from the trip, the pump swap, a new pit versus an existing-pit swap, extra discharge past the included first fifteen feet, battery or water-powered backup, alarm, freeze protection, loaded labor, overhead, and a margin that is a share of price. A default 1/3 HP existing-pit swap with fifteen included feet of discharge, at the sample rates in this calculator, prices around $739 — about 2.5 crew-hours.

Why is a $450 sump pump special dangerous?

Homeowners count a pump. Pit excavation, a concrete slab cut, extra discharge, and the backup that is often more than the primary do not scale with that number. Matching a cheaper special written for an existing-pit swap is how a new-pit-and-battery job becomes a two-day loss. On the sample rates the $450 special is already $67 under the walk-away cost of the brochure swap.

How much to charge to install a sump pump with a new pit and battery backup?

A new basin cut through a slab with a 1/2 HP pump, forty feet of discharge, a battery backup, and an alarm is not a swap special. On the sample rates that job prices around $3,455 (12.2 crew-hours, walk-away $2,419), while the existing-pit 1/3 HP swap prices around $739 (2.5 hours).
