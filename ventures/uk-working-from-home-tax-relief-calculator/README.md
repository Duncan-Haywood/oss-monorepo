# UK Working From Home Tax Relief Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-working-from-home-tax-relief-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: the £6 a week homeworking relief ended on 6 April 2026, but 2022/23 to 2025/26 can still be claimed. Enter your weeks at home per tax year and see the refund per year, the claim deadline for each, and what the deadline has already cost.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-working-from-home-tax-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
