# Ireland Self-Employed Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2026)

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/ie-self-employed-tax-set-aside-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter business income and expenses and get income tax after credits, Universal Social Charge, Class S PRSI and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags preliminary tax (Form 11) and VAT registration.

After using this, an Irish sole trader knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill on 31 October.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/ie-self-employed-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
