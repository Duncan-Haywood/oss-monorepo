Common questions

Does this connect to my Databricks workspace?

No. It reads the rows you paste and never contacts Databricks. It cannot see your contract, so every dollar figure is the DBUs you pasted times a per-DBU price you can edit.

How do I get the numbers?

DBUs per cluster: SUM(usage_quantity) grouped by usage_metadata.cluster_id and sku_name over 30 days from system.billing.usage. Autotermination is in each cluster's configuration. DBU per hour is shown in the cluster UI. Starts come from the cluster event log.

Why is scheduled work on all-purpose compute expensive?

All-purpose compute lists at roughly 3.5 times the DBU rate of Jobs compute. A notebook job pointed at a shared all-purpose cluster pays the higher rate for work that a job cluster would do for less. Moving it is a job-definition change; VM cost is the same.

What autotermination should I use?

30 minutes is a common starting point for interactive clusters; shorter saves more but restarts cold. Autotermination off keeps the cluster, and its DBUs and VMs, billing until someone stops it.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The browser page analyses the text in your own browser and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
