Questions

Is a truck rental a cost of the job?

The tool files Penske and U-Haul truck rentals under vehicle and fuel. Whether you treat them as a direct job cost is your call; ask an accountant.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
