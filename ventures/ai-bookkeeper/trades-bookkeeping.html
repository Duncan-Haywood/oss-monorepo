Trades contractor bookkeeping: job deposits, supply houses and tools

If you run a plumbing, HVAC, electrical or handyman business, money arrives from field-service apps and lead sites, and goes out to supply houses, tool stores and fuel. The bank statement is where they meet, and where a profit gets lost if parts and tools are not separated from fuel and software.

Questions

Are Jobber or Housecall Pro deposits my income?

Yes, as sales (Schedule C line 1 in the US) when your customers pay through the app. The deposit is net of card fees, so compare it with the app's payout report; the fee is a deductible expense.

Do tools and parts from the supply house count as expenses?

Parts for a job and small tools are normally expenses in the year bought. Larger equipment may be depreciated or expensed under a safe harbour, so check with a tax professional. Materials you resell on an invoice can be tracked as cost of goods sold.
