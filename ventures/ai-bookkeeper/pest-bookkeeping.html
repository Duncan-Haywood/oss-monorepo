Questions

Where do chemicals and bait go?

A pest-control distributor is suggested as supplies and materials, route and scheduling software as software, and commercial insurance as insurance. Check each suggestion before you accept it.

Where does the pesticide licence go?

A state pesticide applicator licence is suggested as licences and fees. Requirements and renewal rules differ by state, so check your regulator.
