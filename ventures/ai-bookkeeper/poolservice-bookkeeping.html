Questions

Are pool chemicals a supply or a cost of sales?

The tool files purchases from Leslie's, SCP Pool Corp and similar distributors under supplies. Whether you treat them as cost of sales is your call; ask an accountant.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
