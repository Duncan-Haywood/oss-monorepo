The $900.00 Zelle to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), not a cost. Seed and feed are cost of goods for some farms; the tool files them as supplies and does not split them. Livestock and equipment depreciation, the farm schedule (Schedule F) and sales tax on direct sales are separate questions: ask an accountant.

Questions

Are seed and feed supplies or cost of goods?

The tool suggests supplies and materials. Whether a farm tracks them as inventory or cost of goods, and whether it files on a cash or accrual basis, is your choice; ask an accountant.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
