Questions

Is a Gusto payroll debit software?

No. A Gusto payroll or wages debit is suggested as wages; only the Gusto monthly fee line is filed as software.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
