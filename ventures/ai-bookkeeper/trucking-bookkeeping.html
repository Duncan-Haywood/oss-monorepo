Owner-operator trucker bookkeeping: factoring deposits against fuel, scales, ELD, insurance and highway-use tax

An owner-operator is paid by a factoring company or a broker; the costs are the fuel card, scales, a load board, ELD, truck insurance, parts, IFTA and the heavy-vehicle use tax. The bank statement holds both, so the month's profit starts there.

Questions

Where do factoring advances and the fuel-card payment go?

Factoring and broker payments are suggested as sales; the Comdata or fleet fuel-card payment, scales and truck washes go under vehicle and fuel; Form 2290, IFTA and PrePass go under taxes and licenses. The tool suggests each from the payee, so check every line.

Is a Zelle to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
