Questions

Where do Square and Vagaro payouts go?

The importer files a Square deposit or a Vagaro payout under sales. Both processors keep their fee before paying you, so the deposit is net of the fee and understates what clients paid.

How are lotions, linens, dues and licences filed?

MassageBook and similar booking software go under software and subscriptions; Biotone, wax, sugaring supplies and linen service under supplies; ABMP or AMTA dues and NCBTMB continuing education under education and dues; the state board licence under taxes and licenses; a salon or treatment suite rent under rent. An ABMP charge that mentions insurance is left to the insurance rule.

Why are a Zelle and a grocery run left alone?

A Zelle from a person could be a client or a friend, so it stays in review for you to file. Nothing here guesses a personal purchase into a business cost.
