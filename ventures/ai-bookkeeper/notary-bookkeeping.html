Questions

Are notary dues and E&O insurance legal fees?

No. NNA dues are filed as education and dues, E&O cover as insurance, the stamp as supplies and the state commission as a licence. Only a bare payee with the word notary and nothing else is filed as a legal and professional fee.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
