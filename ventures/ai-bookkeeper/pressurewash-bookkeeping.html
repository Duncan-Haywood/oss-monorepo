Questions

Is a bleach or sodium hypochlorite purchase a pool bill?

Not for a washing crew. A sodium hypochlorite line is suggested as supplies, the same account as detergent and squeegees.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
