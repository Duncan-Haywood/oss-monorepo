Questions

Where do booth rent, needles and ink go?

Booth or chair rent is rent; needles, ink, stencils, gloves and sharps pickup are supplies and materials, and the tool suggests each from the payee. Check each line: a supply store can also sell things for personal use.

Where do the health-department licence and the pathogens course go?

The artist licence goes under taxes and licenses and the bloodborne-pathogens course under education and dues. Requirements differ by county and state, so check yours.
