Questions

Where do Square deposits go?

The importer files a Square deposit under sales. Square keeps its fee before paying you, so the deposit is net of the fee and understates what clients paid.

How are ink, needles, permits and training filed?

Ink, needles and cartridges, gloves, stencil paper and sharps or medical waste pickup go under supplies; Procreate and booking apps under software and subscriptions; the county body art permit under taxes and licenses; bloodborne pathogen courses and tattooist or piercer association dues under education and dues; booth or studio rent under rent.

Why is a Zelle left alone?

A Zelle from a person could be a client's deposit or a friend, so it stays in review for you to file. Nothing here guesses a personal payment into income or a cost.
