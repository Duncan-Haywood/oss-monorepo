Club and PTO treasurer bookkeeping: donations, dues and a few bills

Booster clubs, PTOs, robotics teams and small nonprofits usually have one bank account, a volunteer treasurer and a board that asks, every month, where the money went. The bank statement is where that answer starts.

What the tool does for a treasurer

  1. Imports the bank CSV and suggests income for Zeffy, Givebutter, Donorbox, Classy, GoFundMe, MemberPlanet, dues and fundraiser deposits.
  2. Files the usual bills (website, insurance, state filings, printing, office) and leaves ambiguous warehouse-club and big-box trips for review.
  3. Reports an income and cost statement and a balance sheet you can hand a board, and exports QuickBooks, Xero or IIF journals if you outgrow it.

Rules are written from the platforms' names; no real treasurer's statement has been tried.

Questions

Is a donation-platform payout the whole donation?

Usually not. Platforms pay out net of card fees or tips, so the deposit is less than what donors gave. Compare with the platform's report and book the difference as fees, so donor receipts and your books agree.

Does this handle restricted funds or fund accounting?

No. It keeps one set of books with income and cost accounts. If a gift is restricted, track that separately or with your treasurer's own notes; this is not a substitute for a nonprofit accountant, a Form 990 or state filings.
