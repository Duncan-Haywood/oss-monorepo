eBay bookkeeping: why your payouts understate your sales

eBay pays out what is left after its final value fees, shipping labels and refunds, and the payout also carries sales tax it collected for your buyers. Book the payouts as income and your books show less than you sold, and mix in money that was never yours.

Worked example: one payout, two orders

The tool ships a synthetic Seller Hub transaction report for April 2026: two orders, a shipping label, a refund and one payout, plus a third order not paid out yet. Read by the payout importer, the payout splits like this.

Booked from the bank line alone, the seller shows $111.70 of income and no expenses. Booked from the report, it shows $135.00 of sales and $28.30 of eBay costs, with the $5.00 of tax kept out of income: the same $106.70 profit, but income and expenses that match what was sold and spent. That matters when the numbers are set against a 1099-K, which reports gross payments, and when you claim fees and shipping on Schedule C. The third order ($30.00, $26.10 after fees) has no payout yet; the tool lists it as pending rather than guessing a date.

How to book it

  1. Download the transaction report from Seller Hub (Payments, Reports) as a CSV, and the payout lines from your bank.
  2. Import the bank statement in AI Bookkeeper, then run the payout import on the eBay CSV (payouts ebay-transactions.csv). Each payout is matched to its bank line and split into sales, refunds, fees and shipping, with tax kept apart.
  3. Check that the payout equals the activity. If the rows for a payout do not net to the Payout row, the tool says so and does not split it. Download the whole period again.
  4. Keep sales tax out of income. Marketplace facilitators collect and remit it; do not book it as your sales.

Questions

Why don't my eBay payouts match my sales?

eBay pays out sales net of final value fees, shipping labels bought through eBay and refunds, and the payout includes sales tax eBay collected for the buyer's state. In the worked example, $145.00 of item and shipping sales plus $5.00 of tax became a $111.70 payout.

Is the sales tax eBay collects my income?

No. In most US states eBay collects and remits sales tax as a marketplace facilitator. The tax appears in your transaction report and in the payout, but it is not your sales; the tool books it apart from income.
