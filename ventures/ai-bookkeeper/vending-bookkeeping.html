Stock is cost of goods for most routes; the tool files the restaurant-supply run as supplies and does not split stock from supplies. Machine depreciation, sales tax on vended items and state vending licences differ by state: ask an accountant.

Questions

Is vending stock cost of goods?

For most routes yes, but the tool cannot tell a warehouse-club run for stock from one for supplies. Restaurant-supply runs are suggested as supplies and warehouse-club runs are left for you to review; moving stock to cost of goods is your call, ask an accountant.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
