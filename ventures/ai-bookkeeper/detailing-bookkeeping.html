Mobile detailer bookkeeping: card deposits against chemicals, parts, fuel and insurance

A mobile detailer is paid by Square, Stripe or Zelle; the costs are chemicals and polish, hand tools, parts, fuel, commercial auto insurance, job software and a city licence. The bank statement holds both, so the month's profit starts there.

Questions

Where do detailing chemicals and polish go?

Chemicals, polish, wax and hand tools are supplies and materials; parts and fuel for the work van go under vehicle and fuel. Check every line.

Is a Zelle to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
