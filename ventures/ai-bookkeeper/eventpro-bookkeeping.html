Wedding DJ, planner and florist bookkeeping: event deposits against flowers, gear, rentals and insurance

An event professional is paid by Stripe or Square, often in deposits months before the date; the costs are wholesale flowers, sound gear, rentals, a WeddingWire listing, event liability insurance, planner software, fuel and a city licence. The bank statement holds both, so the month's profit starts there.

Questions

Where do wholesale flowers and event supplies go?

Wholesale flowers and floral supplies are supplies and materials; sound gear is equipment and office; table and chair rentals are equipment rental. Check every line, and ask a tax professional how to treat big gear purchases.

Is a Zelle to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
