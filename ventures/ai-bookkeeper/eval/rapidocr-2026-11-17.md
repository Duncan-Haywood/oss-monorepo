# A second OCR engine for receipt photos: RapidOCR against tesseract (2026-11-17)

Flagship item (5), receipts OCR. The last note
([`cord-train-photos-2026-11-17.md`](cord-train-photos-2026-11-17.md)) left 49 of 81 CORD
train misses **OCR-bound**: the total was in none of the four tesseract readings, so no
parser rule could fix them. It suggested more readings: a different binarisation, or a crop
to the bottom third. This run tried both. Neither helped much, so it tried a different
engine, and that changed the picture.

Data as before: CORD-v2 (Clova AI, CC BY 4.0, revision `7f0115a4`; train shard 0, 200
photos, as the development set; validation 100; test 100, held out) and ExpressExpense's
200 US receipt photos (SRD, `eval/srd-fetch.mjs`). No photo or reading is copied here.

## 1. More tesseract readings: little gain

**Bottom crops.** Each photo cropped to its bottom 50% and its bottom 34%, grey, enlarged to
2,000 px, read with `--psm 6` and `--psm 4`. Measured on the first 146 train photos (143
labelled; stopped there, the answer was clear):

| readings | totals right |
|---|---|
| tesseract four (CLI `--four-pass`) | 84 |
| + bottom-50% crop, two modes | 85 (gained train-117, -180; lost -162) |
| + bottom-34% crop, two modes | 85 (gained train-180) |
| + all four crop readings | 85 |

Of 41 OCR-bound misses in those photos, a crop reading printed the total in 3. Looking at the
photos explains it: the total is not cut off. Most OCR-bound photos are faded thermal print,
tinted paper (blue, pink, green), a receipt small in a busy frame, or a curled slip; the
bottom third of such a photo is just as hard to read as the whole.

**Image variants on the 49 OCR-bound train photos.** Each read with `--psm 6` and
`--psm 4` after: grey `-normalize`; the green channel; the brightest channel (white paper on
tinted stock); the darkest channel (faint ink); a local adaptive threshold (`-lat 40x40-6%`).

| variant | total printed in a reading | right once added to the four |
|---|---|---|
| grey, normalised | 6 of 49 | 3 |
| green channel | 1 | 1 |
| brightest channel | 3 | 2 |
| darkest channel | 2 | 2 |
| local threshold | 7 | 1 |

At most 3 of 49, before counting what an extra reading costs elsewhere. Tesseract's
preprocessing is not where these photos are lost.

## 2. A different engine: RapidOCR

[RapidOCR](https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR) (`rapidocr-onnxruntime` 1.4.4 on PyPI,
Apache-2.0) runs PaddleOCR's text-detection and recognition models through ONNX Runtime, on
the CPU, offline; the models ship in the wheel. `rapidocr_rows.py` (new, in the venture)
prints its word boxes as rows, top to bottom and left to right, and the CLI reads a photo
with it under `receipts FILE --rapidocr`. One reading, the **parser unchanged**: every rule
in `receipts.mjs` was written for tesseract's output.

Totals right, CLI. Tesseract's column is the CLI's four readings and their vote
(`--four-pass`), read in this session with tesseract 5.3.4 from the same commit:

| set | tesseract, four readings | **RapidOCR, one reading** |
|---|---|---|
| CORD train shard 0 (development) | 122 / 196 (62.2%) | **172 / 196 (87.8%)** |
| CORD validation | 70 / 98 (71.4%) | **83 / 98 (84.7%)** |
| **CORD test (held out)** | 55 / 95 (57.9%) | **80 / 95 (84.2%)** |
| SRD, US photos | 156 / 196 (79.6%) | **180 / 196 (91.8%)** |

Tax, on receipts that print one:

| set | tesseract, four readings | **RapidOCR** |
|---|---|---|
| CORD train shard 0 | 38 / 85 (5 wrong) | **63 / 85** (2 wrong) |
| CORD validation | 27 / 47 (4 wrong) | **36 / 47** (1 wrong) |
| **CORD test (held out)** | 16 / 41 (3 wrong) | **28 / 41** (1 wrong) |
| SRD | 135 / 185 (11 wrong) | **142 / 185** (7 wrong) |

Nothing about RapidOCR was chosen on these photos: no setting was tuned and the row grouping
was written once, before the first score. So validation, test and SRD are all unseen for this
change, and the gain holds on each, on Indonesian and US receipts alike.

OCR-bound misses on the train shard went from 49 to 11. RapidOCR reads about 3.6 s per photo
on one CPU core here (200 photos in about three minutes on four).

### Mixing the engines

On the train shard, RapidOCR's reading with tesseract's four (five readings voting) read 170
or 169 totals depending on order, fewer than RapidOCR alone (172), but more taxes (70 and 68
of 85 against 63). RapidOCR read twice with tesseract `--psm 6` as the second reading
(`parseReceiptPasses`' two-reading path): 173 totals, 65 taxes. The vote's weights were set
for four tesseract readings of similar quality; a reading this much better needs its own
weight before mixing pays. Not shipped.

## 3. What is left on RapidOCR's readings

The train misses are now mostly **parser** misses, the opposite of before:

- no total line read, so the cash handed over (50,000) was taken as the largest amount
  (train-115, -160, -172; `line: null` in `--misses`);
- `TL  Rp57,000` read as 57.00 (train-153): `Rp` glued to the amount defeats the whole-unit
  style check on a reading this clean;
- `Total:  29,89` for 29,999 (train-116), `Total  14,000` for 74,000 (train-197): the
  receipt's arithmetic could decide these, as it does for tesseract's readings.
- train-135's label (558,345,580,000) is itself wrong; RapidOCR reads 55,800.

## What shipped

- `rapidocr_rows.py`: RapidOCR to row text (`--selftest` checks the row grouping without
  RapidOCR).
- `ocr.mjs` `receiptTextRapid` and the CLI flag `receipts FILE --rapidocr` (runs `python3`, or
  the interpreter in `RAPIDOCR_PYTHON`; a clear error when RapidOCR is missing).
- `eval/rapidocr-photos.py`: reads a folder of photos for `eval/cord-receipts.mjs`.
- Tests: `rapidocr.test.mjs` (4).

The page (tesseract.js in the browser) is unchanged. RapidOCR's models also run in
ONNX Runtime Web, which is the next step for the page.

## Reproduce

```
python3 -m venv ro && ro/bin/pip install rapidocr-onnxruntime
python3 -m venv v && v/bin/pip install pyarrow
H=https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2/resolve/7f0115a4/data
curl -LO $H/test-00000-of-00001-9c204eb3f4e11791.parquet
v/bin/python -I eval/cord_images.py test-00000-*.parquet test photos
for k in 1 2 3 4; do OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 ro/bin/python eval/rapidocr-photos.py photos out $k 4 & done; wait
node eval/cord-receipts.mjs eval/cord/test.jsonl --ocr out
```

Train shard 0 and validation the same way (file names in
[`cord-train-photos-2026-11-17.md`](cord-train-photos-2026-11-17.md)); SRD with
`node eval/srd-fetch.mjs photos` and `eval/srd/labels.jsonl`. Tesseract's column:
`eval/srd-cli.mjs` as in that note.
