# RapidOCR's total is not outvoted by an unconfirmed tesseract majority (2026-11-17)

The open item: read CORD train shards 1-3 with tesseract's four readings (about 100 minutes on
4 cores) so the five-reading vote (`--rapidocr --four-pass`, and the page: RapidOCR first, then
tesseract's four) is scored on the development set with today's parser, not only RapidOCR alone.

## What the five-reading vote did on train

With every parser change of 2026-11-17 in place, RapidOCR alone reads **539 of 583** train totals
(reproduced exactly from [`rapidocr-parser-misses-2026-11-17.md`](rapidocr-parser-misses-2026-11-17.md)).
RapidOCR plus tesseract's four reads **526**: the vote loses 13 totals to the reading it leads.
Per receipt, against RapidOCR's own reading:

- **21 lost**: 19 are the vote replacing RapidOCR's total with one that more tesseract readings
  give and that no check on the receipt confirms (the note reads "which most readings give but
  no check confirms"). Typical: `770,000` read as `70,000` by two tesseract readings (train-310,
  train-665), `31,500` read as `31,508` by all four (train-408), `32,500` as `32,300` (train-474).
  The other two: a zero-last-digit fix (train-692) and an arithmetic-confirmed wrong total (train-744).
- **8 gained**: 5 confirmed by the receipt's arithmetic or a zero-last-digit fix; 3 unconfirmed
  majorities (train-386, train-392, train-788).

Tesseract misreads digits on these phone photos in correlated ways (the same `3` as `6` in two or
three readings of one photo), so three readings agreeing is weaker evidence than it looks. RapidOCR
reads digits better (539 against tesseract's 367 here).

## The change

`parseReceiptPasses(texts, { lead: true })`: when the first reading is a stronger engine's, a total
the other readings outvote it with is taken only when the receipt's arithmetic confirms it (some
check, such as subtotal plus tax or cash less change, gives it) or it is the subtotal-plus-tax
amount the vote derived. Otherwise the lead's total is kept, with a note: "kept X from the first
engine's reading over Y, which more readings give but no check confirms: check the total". The
page shows the same note, so a kept total is still flagged for a look.

`receiptPassesFromFile` marks its readings (`lead`, not enumerable) when a lead engine read the
photo, `addReceipts` passes it on, and the page passes it when RapidOCR read the photo
(`readPhotos`, `settleReadings(readings, { lead })`). Tesseract-only reading is unchanged.

**Chosen on train only.** Three variants were scored there: keep the lead unless the majority is
confirmed (542), the same only when the lead read a total line (540), keep the lead unless the
majority has more support than it (543). The first, the simplest, was fixed before validation,
test or SRD were read in this session.

| CORD train shards 1-3 (chosen here), of 583 | 539 | 526 | **542** |
| tax, of 267 | 177 / 10 / 80 | 205 / 18 / 44 | **209 / 17 / 41** |
| the page's text (`--settled`), train | | 525, wrong silent 14 | **540**, wrong silent 14 |
| CORD validation, of 98 | 85 | 87 | **87** |

| CORD test (held out), of 95 | 83 | 83 | **83** |
| tax, of 41 | 28 / 1 / 12 | 32 / 3 / 6 | **32 / 3 / 6** |
| the page's text, test | | 84, wrong silent 8 | 83, wrong silent 8 |
| SRD US photos, of 196 | 188 | 186 | **188** |
| tax, of 185 | 144 / 7 / 34 | 163 / 9 / 13 | 161 / 10 / 14 |
| the page's text, SRD | | 187, wrong silent 2 | **188**, wrong silent 2 |

Read plainly: the gain is on train, where the rule was chosen (+16). On the three other sets it is
small and mixed: validation level (3 receipts fixed, 3 broken), test level from the CLI and one
lower on the page (1 fixed, 2 broken), SRD +2 (CLI) and +1 (page), two SRD taxes fewer. What it
buys clearly: the five-reading mode no longer gives up totals against RapidOCR alone on any set
(542/539, 87/85, 83/83, 188/188) and keeps most of the tax gain the four readings bring.

The page here is scored with Python RapidOCR as the lead, not the page's JavaScript port; the port
reads SRD a little differently (189 against 187 in [`rapidocr-web-srd-2026-11-17.md`](rapidocr-web-srd-2026-11-17.md))
and was not re-run.

## Integrity notes

- CORD test was scored once for this rule, after it was fixed; nothing was changed after.
- Validation and SRD were used to build the vote rules this change sits on top of (voteTotal,
  checkedAcrossReadings, zeroLastDigit), so they are not unseen for the vote as a whole, only
  for this rule.
- The tesseract readings of train are new; RapidOCR's reproduced the earlier run's 539 exactly.

## Reproduce

```
apt-get install -y tesseract-ocr imagemagick
python3 -m venv v && v/bin/pip install rapidocr-onnxruntime==1.4.4 pyarrow
H=https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2/resolve/7f0115a4/data
for f in train-00001-of-00004-7dbbe248962764c5 train-00002-of-00004-688fe1305a55e5cc train-00003-of-00004-2d0cd200555ed7fd; do
  curl -LO $H/$f.parquet; v/bin/python -I eval/cord_images.py $f.parquet train photos; done
for k in 1 2 3 4; do OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 v/bin/python eval/rapidocr-photos.py photos rows $k 4 & done; wait   # ~10 min
for k in 1 2 3 4; do OMP_THREAD_LIMIT=1 node eval/srd-cli.mjs photos t $k/4 & done; wait                    # ~100 min
node eval/cord-receipts.mjs eval/cord/train.jsonl --ocr rows,t/psm6,t/psm4,t/g6,t/g4 --lead [--settled] [--misses]
```

Validation and test the same way from their parquet files; SRD photos from `node eval/srd-fetch.mjs`.
Tests: `receipt-lead-vote.test.mjs` (4).
