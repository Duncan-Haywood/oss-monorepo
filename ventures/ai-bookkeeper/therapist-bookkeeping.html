The bank statement holds both, so the month's profit starts there.

Questions

Where do insurance payouts and client payments go?

Payouts from panels such as Headway and Alma, and client card payments that arrive as processor transfers, are filed under sales. Reimbursements are income when received, so your accountant decides how to treat any clawback or a pending claim.

How are CE, supervision, dues and the licence filed?

CE courses, clinical supervision and association dues go under education and dues, a directory listing such as Psychology Today under advertising, the board licence under taxes and licences, and malpractice cover under insurance. A Venmo cash-out is filed as an owner draw, not a cost.
