Questions

Is commissary rent a rent expense?

Yes. A commissary kitchen fee is filed as rent, propane as utilities, and event or festival fees as advertising and marketing.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
