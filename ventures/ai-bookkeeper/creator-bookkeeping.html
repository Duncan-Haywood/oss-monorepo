Creator bookkeeping: five payout platforms, one bank statement

If you earn from YouTube, a newsletter, Twitch, a course or Patreon you are self-employed, and each platform pays you on its own schedule. The bank statement is the one place they all land. This turns it into a profit figure and a Schedule C worksheet.

Questions

Is my YouTube, Substack or Patreon payout my income?

Book what the platform pays you as sales (Schedule C line 1), and the software and gear you buy as expenses. A payout is usually the figure after the platform's own cut or card fees, so compare it with the platform's earnings report and any 1099 (NEC, MISC or K) it sends.

Do I owe tax if no platform sent me a 1099?

Self-employment income is taxable whether or not a form arrives. The tool gives a Schedule C worksheet and a self-employment tax estimate to take to your preparer; it is not tax advice.
