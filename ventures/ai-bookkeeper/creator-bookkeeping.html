Questions

Is AdSense, Patreon or Substack money income when it lands in my bank?

Yes, as self-employment sales (Schedule C line 1 in the US). Platforms pay on their own schedule and sometimes hold back tax or fees, so compare the deposit with the platform's statement and the 1099 it issues.

Do I count the camera and microphone as an expense?

Usually, if they are for the business. A $349 purchase is an expense this year in the tool's chart (supplies); larger gear may be depreciated or expensed under a safe harbour, so check with a tax professional. Personal-use share is not deductible.
