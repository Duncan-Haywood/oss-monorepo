What the tool does for a restaurant

  1. Imports the bank CSV and suggests Sales for Toast, DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub payouts.
  2. Files food-service distributors (Sysco, US Foods, Restaurant Depot, WebstaurantStore) as supplies and POS and scheduling subscriptions as software.
  3. Reports a P&L, Schedule C worksheet and sales tax return worksheet, and exports QuickBooks, Xero or IIF journals.

Rules are written from company names; no real restaurant's statement has been tried.

Questions

Are DoorDash, Uber Eats and Toast payouts my full sales?

No. Each payout is net of the platform's commission and fees, and Toast and the apps pay on different days. Book the deposit as sales here, then compare each platform's sales report to see the commission and tips; the gap is a cost your statement does not show line by line.

Is a Sysco or US Foods invoice supplies or cost of goods?

The tool files food-service distributors under Supplies (6100) and has no separate cost-of-goods account by design. Restaurants usually report food cost as cost of goods sold, so move those lines if your accountant wants that split.
