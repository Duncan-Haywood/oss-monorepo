The $500.00 Zelle to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), not a cost, so it does not reduce profit. A drone bought for the business may be depreciated instead of expensed, and licence rules vary by country: ask an accountant.

Questions

Where do the drone, batteries and mapping software go?

A drone store is suggested as supplies and materials, mapping and flight-planning subscriptions as software, and drone insurance as insurance. A drone that lasts more than a year may be a depreciable asset rather than a one-month cost: ask an accountant before expensing it.

Where does the Part 107 exam go?

The FAA Part 107 exam and recurrent training are suggested as education and dues. Rules differ by country and change, so check your aviation authority.
