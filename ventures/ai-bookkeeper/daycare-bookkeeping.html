Home daycare bookkeeping: tuition and food-program money against the supplies and dues behind it

A home daycare's income arrives as tuition by Zelle or a childcare app and, if you are in the food program, a CACFP reimbursement; the costs are the app, snacks, craft and toy supplies, accreditation dues, a first-aid class and insurance. The bank statement holds both, so the month's profit starts there.

Questions

Where do tuition and food-program reimbursements go?

Parent tuition by Zelle or a childcare app, and a CACFP reimbursement from the state agency, are filed under sales. The CACFP money is income too, but its rules differ from tuition's, so ask your sponsor or accountant how to report it.

How are supplies, software, dues and classes filed?

Brightwheel and similar apps go under software and subscriptions, craft and toy buys under supplies, NAFCC accreditation, CACFP sponsor fees and infant CPR classes under education and dues, and childcare liability insurance under insurance. A Venmo cash-out is filed as an owner draw, not a cost.
