Home inspector bookkeeping: report-software payouts against E&O insurance, gear, dues and licence

A home inspector is paid through the inspection software or by the client; the costs are the software subscription, errors-and-omissions insurance, gear such as a thermal camera, association dues, the state licence and fuel. The bank statement holds both.

Questions

Where does a thermal camera go?

It is suggested as equipment. Whether to expense or depreciate it is a tax decision for you and your accountant.

Is a transfer to myself a cost?

No. A transfer to the owner is suggested as an owner draw (equity), so it does not reduce profit.
