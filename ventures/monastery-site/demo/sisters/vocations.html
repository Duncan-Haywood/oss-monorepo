Vocations

Is God calling you to this life?

We welcome Catholic women who desire to give their whole life to God in prayer, poverty and enclosure.

Write to the vocation directorCome and see

Who may apply

  • Catholic women between 18 and 35
  • In good health, able to keep the night Office and the fasts of the Rule
  • Free of debts and family obligations

The path of formation

  1. Correspondence and a visit

    Write to the vocation directress, then visit the parlour and spend time with the community.

  2. Aspirancy 1 to 3 months

    Live inside the enclosure to share the life before asking to enter.

  3. Postulancy 1 year

    Learn the life, the chant and the customs of the monastery.

  4. Novitiate 2 years

    Receive the habit and a new name, and study the Rule of Saint Clare.

  5. Temporary vows 3 years

    Vows of obedience, poverty, chastity and enclosure, renewed until solemn profession.

  6. Solemn vows for life

    Lifelong consecration as a Poor Clare in this monastery.

Questions people ask

Will I ever see my family again?

Yes. Families visit in the parlour, and the community is glad to welcome them.

What is the enclosure?

The part of the monastery reserved to the nuns, so that our life can be given wholly to prayer.

Do you need a dowry?

No. Come as you are; Providence provides.
