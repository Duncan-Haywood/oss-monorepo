Visit

Visit us

Guests are received as Christ (Rule of Saint Benedict, chapter 53). Come for an hour of prayer, a day of quiet or a private retreat.

Mass and the Office

All are welcome at the Divine Office and at Mass in the abbey church. Visitors sit in the nave; the choir is reserved for the monks.

Times of prayer

The guesthouse

Twenty single rooms for men and women making a private retreat of up to a week. Guests share the monks' meals in silence and may speak with a monk on request.

  • Stays of one to seven nights
  • A suggested offering per night, and no one is turned away for lack of means
  • Two rooms with step-free access
