This calculator vs. FieldCamp

Both end with a bag count and a price, built on opposite assumptions about what you are willing to install and pay for.

What FieldCamp is

FieldCamp is a paid job-management platform aimed at insulation and other field-service businesses: schedule crews, estimate and send jobs, invoice, and track cost and profit across every job the business runs, on an ongoing subscription. It is built to run day-to-day operations for a company doing many jobs, not to price a single attic once.

Getting there means creating an account and paying an ongoing subscription priced by users and features.

What this calculator is

This page does not schedule anything and keeps no account. You open it in a browser, type an attic's square footage, existing R-value, target R-value, and bag yield, and it shows the bag count and walk-away price for that one job. Close the tab and it is gone unless you save a share link or print a proposal — there is no crew schedule, no invoicing, and no subscription.

QuestionWhere to answer it
I want to schedule crews, estimate, and invoice every job my insulation business runs, and track cost against each one. FieldCamp or a comparable paid platform — ongoing job management is exactly its job, and this calculator has no schedule or invoicing.
I have one attic to bid right now and want the bag count and walk-away price before a flat-rate special goes out. This calculator — type the R-value delta and square footage, get a price, no account.
I do not want to create an account or start a subscription to price a single job. This calculator — nothing to sign up for.
I want the tool to remember every job I have bid and compare estimated to actual material cost over time. FieldCamp or a comparable paid platform — this calculator has no job list and no memory between visits beyond what your own browser keeps.

Neither tool is the wrong choice in general. An insulation company scheduling crews and running many jobs a season is the case a paid platform is built for. A single attic, priced once against a flat-rate special before the bid goes out, is the case this page is built for.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or FieldCamp?

They fit different points in the job. FieldCamp is a paid job-management platform built for insulation and field-service businesses: schedule crews, estimate jobs, invoice, and track costs across every job a company runs, on an ongoing subscription. This page does one thing: you type an attic's square footage, existing and target R-value, and bag yield into a browser, and it shows a bag count and a walk-away price for that one job, right now, with no account and no subscription. If you want a platform that schedules crews and runs job costing for an ongoing insulation business, that is what FieldCamp is built for. If you want to price one attic before a square-foot special goes out, this page does that with nothing to sign up for.
