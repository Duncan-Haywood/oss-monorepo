FOR INSULATION CONTRACTORS

Bid the bags, not the attic.

Bags come from the R-value delta, not the floor area. An attic already at R-30 going to R-38 is not the same truckload as the same attic sitting at R-13 going to R-49, and a flat price per square foot cannot see the difference. Price the areas, the product, the air sealing, and the baffles — then see the walk-away number before a $1.50-a-foot special eats the week.

The job

R-per-inch and bag yield follow common product charts as a time-and-material budget you can edit — not a manufacturer coverage table, an energy-code requirement, or a finding that any attic is airtight, permitted, or rebate-eligible. Deeper fills multiply blow hours on that section because the bags have to be hauled and raked.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, respiratory workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. A blower paid $32 often costs more than $41 in the attic. Underestimating that is how a $1.50-a-foot special loses money.
