      Common questions

      What does this exterior paint estimate include?

      Base setup, extra hours for elevation and stories beyond your included allowance, a scrape and prime pass if you flag them, trim beyond the included footage, shutters, occupied-home protection, hard-access time, paint, primer, and caulk from your coverage and coat count, travel and equipment, and overhead and margin on top.

      What’s the difference between this and a flat house-special price?

      A flat price treats every house as the same job. This tool splits out a base allowance for elevation, stories, and trim from what goes beyond it, so a two-story colonial with extra trim, a scrape-and-prime pass, and hard second-story access prices differently than a one-story ranch with sound existing paint, instead of both landing on one number.

      Does changing the job type overwrite my own hourly rates?

      No. Picking a job type only suggests a starting setup time, paint cost per gallon, and coat count, and it will not replace a rate you have already typed over that suggestion. Every hour and dollar figure stays something you can edit.

      Does this cover interior painting or cabinet refinishing?

      No, this prices an exterior paint job specifically: elevation, stories, scrape and prime, and trim. Interior rooms and cabinet doors are a different scope with their own labor and material patterns and are not part of this calculation.

      Does it check for lead paint or the condition of the existing paint?

      No. Scrape and prime are flags you set from your own inspection, and the tool does not test for lead paint, assess paint condition, or perform any building-code check. Confirm those separately before starting work, especially on an older home.
