# UK Dividend Tax Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-dividend-tax-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter your other income and your dividends and see dividend tax for 2025/26 and 2026/27 by band, the dividend allowance used, the cost of the next £1,000 of dividends and when to report.

After using this, you know what a dividend will cost in tax and what it does to your bill, which used to mean reading GOV.UK rates pages and stacking the bands by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-dividend-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
