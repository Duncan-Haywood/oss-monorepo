Snowflake warehouse cost: AUTO_SUSPEND, idle warehouses and sizing

Short answer: a warehouse bills credits for every second it runs, whether or not a query is running. Most avoidable spend is idle tail after queries finish, warehouses nobody uses, and sizes bigger than the queries need.

What it costs (illustrative)

  • Credits per hour double with each size: X-Small 1, Small 2, Medium 4, Large 8, X-Large 16, and so on.
  • Price per credit depends on edition and contract; this tool assumes $3.00 and lets you change it.
  • Resume minimum: each resume bills at least 60 seconds.

Where the money is

  • Long AUTO_SUSPEND: every wake-up idles for the whole setting before suspending. Hundreds of dashboard wake-ups a month multiply it.
  • Always-on warehouses: AUTO_SUSPEND off keeps the meter running 24/7, 730 hours a month.
  • Idle warehouses: a retired pipeline or sandbox that still bills.
  • Oversized for short queries: a Large averaging 3-second queries.

Measure and fix

  1. Credits: SELECT warehouse_name, SUM(credits_used) FROM snowflake.account_usage.warehouse_metering_history WHERE start_time >= DATEADD(day, -30, CURRENT_TIMESTAMP()) GROUP BY 1;
  2. Size and AUTO_SUSPEND: SHOW WAREHOUSES;. Queries and average runtime from query_history, resumes from warehouse_events_history.
  3. Paste rows into the advisor or run node cli.mjs analyze warehouses.csv. It prints an ALTER WAREHOUSE command per fix.
  4. Change one warehouse at a time and watch query latency for a week before rolling out.

Questions

What AUTO_SUSPEND should a Snowflake warehouse use?

60 seconds is the practical minimum, because every resume bills at least 60 seconds anyway. Longer settings pay for idle time after each burst of queries. Keep a longer value only for a warehouse whose warm cache you rely on and that wakes up rarely.

Why is my Snowflake bill high when queries are fast?

Usually idle time and size, not query work: an AUTO_SUSPEND of 600 seconds on a Large warehouse that wakes 500 times a month bills about 78 hours of idle tail, around 620 credits. Warehouses with AUTO_SUSPEND off run around the clock; a Small is 1,460 credits a month.

Does a smaller warehouse always cost less?

No. Credits per hour halve with each size, but a query that does not scale down can take more than twice as long. Short queries are the safe candidates; try one size down on one warehouse and compare runtimes before rolling it out.
