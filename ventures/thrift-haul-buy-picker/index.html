    Common questions

    Is this a price guide or a sales forecast?

    No. Every resale value, sell-through chance, fee and time figure in the answer is a number you typed. It does not look up sold listings or predict what anything will sell for. It does the arithmetic on your own estimates and shows which items need a better estimate or a lower price.

    Does it upload my list?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    What is the most I should pay for an item?

    The walk-away price: the ask at which your expected profit, after marketplace fees, shipping, supplies, the value of your time and the cost of tying up cash, equals the minimum return you set. It is solved exactly, not searched for. Above it, you are paying for the privilege of working.

    How does it choose between items when cash or time is limited?

    With a cash, item or hands-on-hours cap it searches subsets exactly (branch and bound) instead of ranking and cutting off. One big-ticket item can crowd out two smaller ones that together earn more; ranking misses that.

    Which marketplaces and sources does it cover?

    Any: eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, your own store. Enter that marketplace's fee percent and fixed fee. Sources can be thrift stores, estate sales, garage sales, auctions or liquidation lots.
