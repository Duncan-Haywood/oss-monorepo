# UK Inheritance Tax Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-inheritance-tax-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter the estate, debts, the home and what is left to a spouse or charity, and see UK Inheritance Tax for deaths from 6 April 2025 step by step: nil-rate band, residence nil-rate band and its taper above GBP 2 million, transferred allowances from a late spouse, and the 36% reduced rate for charity gifts.

After using this, you know roughly what an estate will owe, which allowances apply, and whether leaving the home to children or giving to charity changes the bill.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-inheritance-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
