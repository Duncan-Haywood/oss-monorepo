What CMMC costs a small business

When DoD wrote the CMMC rule it had to estimate what an assessment costs, and it showed its working: for each level, who does the work, for how many hours, at what hourly rate, and how long the C3PAO's team spends on site. Those figures are below as the rule prints them. Put in what your own people and your IT provider actually cost, and your C3PAO's quote if you have one, and the page recomputes the assessment and the three years that follow. Then add the part DoD's number leaves out: what it costs you to get compliant in the first place.

    What DoD counted

    For each level the rule splits the assessment into three phases (planning and preparing, conducting it, reporting into SPRS), then adds the affirmation your Affirming Official makes after the assessment and every year after it. For a certification it adds the C3PAO's team: 3 assessors for 120 hours for a small business, 5 for 200 hours for a larger one, at $260.28 an hour. For small businesses it assumed small entities are likely to outsource IT and cybersecurity to an External Service Provider (ESP), so most of the hours sit with an ESP at $260.28 an hour; The ESP/C3PAO rate represents an estimate for outsourced labor and includes the labor rate, overhead expense, G&A expense, and profit.

    The rule's totals for a small business: $5,977 a year for Level 1 (Self), $37,196 over three years for Level 2 (Self), and $104,670 over three years for Level 2 (C3PAO), of which $31,234 is the C3PAO. Recomputed here from the lines, they agree to within the rule's own rounding.

    What DoD left out

    The biggest cost for most shops is not in the number. The rule says plainly that cost estimates are not included for an entity to implement the CMMC Level 1 or 2 security requirements, maintain implementation of these existing security requirements, or remediate a plan of action for unimplemented requirements. DoD's reasoning is that FAR 52.204-21 and DFARS 252.204-7012 already required those safeguards, so CMMC only adds the cost of proving them. If your shop has not implemented all 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements, the multi-factor authentication, the FIPS-validated encryption, the logging, the incident response plan and the rest are on top, and so is a CUI enclave if you choose one. The rule also says Assessment costs assume the OSA passes the assessment on the first attempt: a failed assessment, or a Conditional status with a POA&M to close out within 180 days, costs more.

    How to bring the number down

    • Shrink the scope. Every asset that touches CUI is assessed. Keeping CUI on fewer machines, or out of the shop floor network altogether, cuts the hours of everyone involved. The scoping worksheet sorts your assets into the rule's categories.
    • Check what level you need. A shop that only ever receives FCI needs Level 1, a yearly self-assessment, not a certification. The Level 1 vs Level 2 guide and the flow-down rules say which applies.
    • Do the paperwork yourself. Much of the ESP's preparation time in DoD's estimate is gathering evidence and writing the system security plan. A draft SSP, a scored SPRS self-check and a POA&M you wrote before the provider arrives means paying for review rather than authorship.
    • Get more than one C3PAO quote. Assessors price by scope and readiness. The Cyber AB marketplace lists authorized C3PAOs.

    Common questions

    How much does CMMC certification cost a small business?

    DoD's own estimate in the CMMC final rule (89 FR 83092) is $104,670 over three years for a small business at Level 2 (C3PAO): $101,752 for the assessment and its affirmation, of which $31,234 is the C3PAO, plus two annual affirmations of $1,459. Level 2 (Self) is $37,196 over three years and Level 1 (Self) $5,977 a year. This page recomputes those lines with your own rates.

    Does DoD's CMMC cost estimate include implementing the controls?

    No. The rule says its estimates do not include the cost to implement the Level 1 or Level 2 security requirements, maintain them, or remediate a plan of action, because FAR 52.204-21 and DFARS 252.204-7012 already required them. If your shop is not yet compliant, that cost is on top; enter your own figure for it here.

    What hourly rates did DoD assume for CMMC?

    $190.52 an hour for a director, $86.24 for a small business's staff IT specialist, and $260.28 for an outside service provider (ESP) or C3PAO assessor, which the rule says includes overhead, G&A and profit. For small businesses DoD assumed most of the work goes to an ESP.
