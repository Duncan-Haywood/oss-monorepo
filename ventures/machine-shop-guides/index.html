Machine shop guides: SPRS score, quoting and CMMC

Free tools and plain-language guides for small machine shops that quote parts and work on defense contracts. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is uploaded.

SPRS score calculator

Mark the 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements met or not met and get the DoD Assessment Methodology score: 110 minus 5, 3 or 1 point for each gap. Includes the partial credit for MFA and FIPS cryptography, the 88-point Conditional check, and a downloadable POA&M starter.

Job-shop quote calculator

Material, setup, programming, cycle time, secondary operations and markup from your own rates, with the price per part at every quantity break. No built-in rates.

Guides

  • CMMC Level 1 vs Level 2 for a small machine shop: FCI or CUI, the clauses to look for, what each level asks, and where CMMC Phase 2 stands (suspended since 13 July 2026; checked 24 November 2026).
  • What goes in an SSP and a POA&M: scope, implementation statements, and the POA&M limits in 32 CFR 170.21.
  • How to price a machined part: shop rate, material yield, setup, cycle time, outside processes, markup versus margin and quantity breaks, from your own records.

Why these are free

Quoting and cybersecurity compliance land on the same person in a small shop, usually the owner. These pages answer the questions that come up first. They are also the front door to two paid products we are building for the same shops, described below. The tools on this page stay free and open source (MIT).

Sources for every regulation cited, with the date each was checked, are listed in SOURCES.md in the source repository.

In development — early access

We are building two paid products for small machine shops: an RFQ-to-quote assistant that turns a drawing and an email into a priced quote from your own rates, and a CMMC Level 1/2 and ISO 9001 workspace that keeps your SSP, POA&M and SPRS score in one place. Neither is released yet. Read what is planned and how to get early access on the manufacturing page.

Neither product certifies anyone. Only an authorized C3PAO (or the government) can issue a CMMC Level 2 certification; software can help you prepare, nothing more.
