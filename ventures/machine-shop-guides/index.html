Machine shop guides: SPRS score, quoting and CMMC

Free tools and plain-language guides for small machine shops that quote parts and work on defense contracts. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is uploaded.

CMMC Level 1 self-assessment

The 15 FAR 52.204-21 requirements, word for word: mark each met or not met, note your evidence, and download a worksheet with the scope, CAGE codes and your next annual date. Level 1 has no score and no POA&M, so every requirement must be met.

CMMC scoping worksheet

List your computers, CNC controllers, CMM, printers, phones and cloud services; answer a few questions each and see which 32 CFR 170.19 category your answers point to (CUI, Security Protection, Contractor Risk Managed, Specialized, Out-of-Scope), what has to be documented, and what is still open. Download the asset inventory.

SPRS score calculator

Mark the 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements met or not met and get the DoD Assessment Methodology score: 110 minus 5, 3 or 1 point for each gap. Includes the partial credit for MFA and FIPS cryptography, the 88-point Conditional check, and a downloadable POA&M starter.

NIST 800-171 SSP template

Write your system security plan in the browser: boundary, environment, connections, and an implementation statement, owner and evidence for each of the 110 requirements. See what an assessor would send back, and download the SSP (HTML or Markdown) and the POA&M.

DFARS 7012 incident reporting

Found a cyber incident? Enter when, and get the 72-hour DIBNet deadline, the date to keep images until (90 days from your report), and every step of DFARS 252.204-7012 with its paragraph: review, report, DC3 malware, subcontractor report number. Download the incident record.

CMMC affirmation due dates

Enter your CMMC Status Date and last SPRS affirmation: the next annual affirmation, the 180-day POA&M closeout deadline for a Conditional status, the next Level 1 or Level 2 assessment, the years in between, and a calendar file that reminds you 60 and 7 days ahead.

Stock size from a STEP or STL file

Drop the customer's model: bounding box, the round bar it turns from, and the stock to cut with your allowance and rounding, plus weight and material cost from your own price. Read in your browser; the file is never uploaded.

Job-shop quote calculator

Material, setup, programming, cycle time, secondary operations and markup from your own rates, with the price per part at every quantity break. No built-in rates.

RFQ review checklist · Drawing review

Paste an RFQ and see its parts, revisions, quantities, material, finish, tight tolerances and dates, the DFARS and FAR clauses that change the job (CUI, SPRS, CMMC, specialty metals, export control, DPAS), and the questions to send the buyer as one email.

Drawing review

Drop the customer's drawing PDF: title block, revision table (and whether it matches the title block), general tolerance block, numbered notes tagged by what they cost, the dimension and thread callouts in the views, distribution and export markings, and the questions to send the buyer. The callouts open in the first article checker with the general tolerances filled in. Read in your browser; the file is never uploaded.

First article inspection checker

Paste the ballooned characteristics and what you measured: each requirement read into its limits (bilateral, limit, MAX/MIN, GD&T, finish, general tolerance), every result judged, and the gaps that get an FAI report sent back. Download an AS9102 Form 3 style CSV.

Quoted vs actual hours

Paste your job history and see, per machine, how far quoted setup hours and cycle times ran from what the jobs took, what the misses cost at your rate, your win rate, and a correction factor the quote calculator applies.

Common questions

What is an SPRS score and how is it calculated?

It is the NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment score a contractor posts in the Supplier Performance Risk System. You start at 110 and subtract 5, 3 or 1 point for each of the 110 requirements not met. The SPRS score calculator here does that arithmetic, with the partial credit for MFA and FIPS cryptography.

Do I need CMMC Level 1 or Level 2?

It depends on what the contract sends you. Federal Contract Information (FCI) only, under FAR 52.204-21, means Level 1: 15 requirements and a yearly self-assessment. Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), such as a drawing marked CUI, puts the systems that touch it in Level 2 scope: the 110 requirements of NIST SP 800-171.

How do I price a machined part?

From your own records: a shop rate built from your books, material per part from the stock price and yield, setup and programming spread over the lot, cycle time at the machine rate, secondary and outside operations, then markup. Re-run it at each quantity for the price breaks. The job-shop quote calculator does the arithmetic and ships no rates of its own.

Is anything I type uploaded?

No. Every tool on these pages runs in your browser; drawings, models, RFQs and answers are not uploaded. Some pages can keep a draft in your browser if you turn that on.
