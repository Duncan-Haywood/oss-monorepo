CMMC Level 1 self-assessment checklist

If your contracts carry FAR 52.204-21 and you handle only Federal Contract Information (FCI), CMMC Level 1 is the 15 requirements below. The rule says how to judge each one: against its assessment objectives from NIST SP 800-171A, 59 in all, reading FCI wherever an objective says CUI. Mark each objective met, not met or N/A, write down how you meet the requirement, and download the worksheet. Level 1 has no score and no plan of action: every one of the 15 has to be met.

The requirement text is FAR 52.204-21(b)(1), quoted word for word. This page is a self-check you do before the real thing: the self-assessment you enter in the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS) is affirmed by a senior official of your company, so mark a requirement met only when it is actually in place. What the rule says about Level 1

  • The requirements. 32 CFR 170.14(c)(2) makes the 15 safeguarding requirements of FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(i)–(xv) the CMMC Level 1 requirements. Each identifier on this page (AC.L1-b.1.i and so on) is the domain plus the FAR paragraph.
  • Judged by 59 objectives. 32 CFR 170.15(c)(1) says the self-assessment "must be performed using the objectives defined in NIST SP 800-171A" for the NIST requirement each Level 1 requirement maps to (its Table 2), and that "where an objective addresses CUI, FCI should be substituted for CUI". Requirement (ix) maps to three NIST requirements, one per phrase, so 15 requirements carry 17 NIST numbers and 59 objectives. The five objectives where we read FCI for CUI are marked.
  • Met, not met or N/A. Under 32 CFR 170.24(b) an objective is met when evidence in final form shows it (drafts, working papers and unapproved policies do not count) and not met when any part is missing. An objective that does not apply is N/A, which "is equivalent to the same assessment objective being assessed as MET"; the rule's own example is separating public-facing systems (b.1.xi) when nothing in scope is publicly accessible. Write the reason in the evidence note. A requirement is met only when all its objectives are met or N/A, and the result is "MET or NOT MET in their entirety" (170.24(c)(1)).
  • All met, no POA&M. Under 32 CFR 170.15 you reach Final Level 1 (Self) status only with every requirement MET, and 32 CFR 170.21 does not allow a plan of action and milestones at Level 1. A gap is fixed before you claim the level, not after.
  • Every year. The self-assessment is repeated annually and its result entered in SPRS; an Affirming Official affirms compliance at the end of each self-assessment and every year after (32 CFR 170.22). The worksheet prints the date a year on.
  • What goes in SPRS. The entry records the CMMC level, the date, the assessment scope, the CAGE codes it covers and the result. That is why this page asks for those fields; it does not submit anything.
  • Flow-down. The clause's paragraph (c) makes you include its substance in subcontracts where the subcontractor may have FCI in its systems, including commercial items other than COTS. If you send government jobs to a heat treater or a plater with the drawing, check what you flow down.

Handle any CUI, such as a drawing marked CUI? Then those systems are in Level 2 scope as well: see CMMC Level 1 vs Level 2 and the SPRS score calculator for the 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements.

The "examples of evidence" under each requirement are ours, written for a small shop. They are not text from any rule and not a list an assessor is bound by. The objectives are NIST SP 800-171A's own "determine if" statements. The DoD's Level 1 assessment guide adds discussion and examples for each; read it on the DoW CIO CMMC page before you affirm. Common questions

What are the CMMC Level 1 requirements?

The 15 safeguarding requirements of FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(i) to (xv). 32 CFR 170.14(c)(2) makes them the CMMC Level 1 requirements, and each is judged against its NIST SP 800-171A assessment objectives, 59 in all, reading FCI wherever an objective says CUI.

Does CMMC Level 1 have a score or a POA&M?

No. Each requirement is met or not met in its entirety, there is no point score, and 32 CFR 170.21 allows no plan of action and milestones at Level 1. Every one of the 15 has to be met before you claim the level.

How often is the Level 1 self-assessment done?

Every year. The result goes into SPRS with the level, date, scope and CAGE codes, and an Affirming Official affirms compliance at the end of each self-assessment and every year after (32 CFR 170.22).
