Four Corners Rural Food Access Fund — Request for Proposals (fictional, for testing)

Funder: High Desert Community Trust (fictional)
Program: Rural Food Access Grants 2027

The Fund supports nonprofit organizations that increase access to fresh, healthy food for families in rural communities of New Mexico, Arizona, Colorado and Utah.

Award range: $25,000 to $100,000 for a 12-month project.
Eligibility: 501(c)(3) nonprofits and tribal organizations serving rural communities in the Four Corners region.
Application deadline: September 30, 2027.

Applications must include:
1. Executive summary (up to 300 words)
2. Statement of need
3. Project description and activities
4. Organizational capacity
5. Budget and budget narrative
