Mesa Ridge Community Pantry — Mission Statement

Mesa Ridge Community Pantry is a fictional 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded in 2012 in Farmington, New Mexico.

Our mission is to end hunger in rural San Juan County by delivering fresh, culturally appropriate food to families who live far from a grocery store.

In 2025 we distributed 412,000 pounds of food to 3,150 households through 14 mobile pantry routes.

Our team includes 9 full-time staff and more than 220 volunteers.

Our annual operating budget for 2025 was $1,240,000, and 38% of our revenue came from individual donors.

Our Grow Local program partners with 27 local farmers and ranchers to buy produce and meat for the pantry.

We serve families in Farmington, Shiprock, Kirtland, Aztec and surrounding chapter communities.
