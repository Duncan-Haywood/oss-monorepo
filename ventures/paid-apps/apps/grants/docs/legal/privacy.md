# Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-11-24** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

> **Draft.** This document is a plain-language draft. Have counsel review it before launch.

This policy explains what information Grant Autopilot (operated by Mary Stella Labs, LLC, "we") collects, why, who processes it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account information:** name, email address, sign-in details (passwords are stored only as secure hashes), organization memberships and roles.
- **Organization information:** organization profile (legal name, address, identifiers such as EIN or UEI, contacts), settings, and projects.
- **Documents you upload:** for example mission statements, annual reports, budgets and past applications, plus the text we extract from them and the facts we derive from that text.
- **Interview answers:** your typed answers and, if you choose to use voice, the audio you record. Audio is transcribed; the recording itself is kept only if your organization turns on "store voice recordings".
- **Website pages:** only if your organization turns on website crawling for its own site.
- **Applications and activity:** drafts, reviews, submissions, follow-ups and an audit log of consequential actions.
- **Usage and billing:** usage counts (for plan limits and cost tracking), subscription status, and payment events from the merchant of record. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
- **Technical data:** hashed IP addresses (we store a one-way hash, never the raw address), browser user-agent, and security logs.
- **Grant guide emails (optional, no account needed):** if you sign up on our website, your email address, the page you signed up on, the version of the sign-up wording you saw, a one-way hash of your IP address, and when you confirmed or unsubscribed. We only add you after you click the confirmation link we email you.

## Why we use it

- To provide the Service: build your organization's fact base, find and score opportunities, draft and verify applications, route reviews, submit and track follow-ups.
- To secure the Service, prevent abuse and keep an audit trail.
- To bill you and enforce plan limits.
- To support you when you ask us to.
- To send grant guide emails, only if you signed up for them and confirmed your address (a short welcome series, then occasional emails about new guides). We do not add account holders or anyone else to this list automatically.
- To meet legal obligations.

## Subprocessors

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf to run the Service:

| Subprocessor | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Hosting, database, file storage, search index, and AI models for document conversion, embeddings and speech-to-text |
| Anthropic, PBC | Large language model (Claude) for drafting, interviews and fact extraction. Data sent through Anthropic's commercial API is not used to train its models. |
| Email delivery provider | Sending account, notification, grant guide (if you subscribed) and (when you choose) application emails |
| Merchant-of-record payment processor | Checkout, payments, invoicing and sales tax |

We will update this list before adding a new subprocessor.

## What we do not do

- We do **not sell** personal information or organization data.
- We do **not use your data for advertising**.
- We do **not use your documents or interviews to train AI models**, and we do not share them with other organizations using the Service.

## Retention

- Your data is kept while your organization's account is active.
- Organizations may set a retention period for original uploaded files; after processing and that period, originals are deleted and the extracted text is kept.
- Data exports are deleted 7 days after they are created.
- Grant guide sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 30 days. When you unsubscribe, we keep your email address and the date on a suppression list so we never email you again by mistake; ask us if you want it removed entirely.
- When an organization is deleted, its data is permanently deleted from our database, file storage and search index. Backups and provider logs roll off on their normal schedules.
- We keep a minimal, non-identifying record that a deletion happened, and billing records the merchant of record or the law requires.

## Security

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (provider-managed storage encryption).
- Tenant isolation: every record and file is scoped to one organization and checked on every request.
- Role-based access controls within each organization.
- An append-only audit log of consequential actions such as uploads, approvals, submissions, exports and deletions.
- No document contents in application logs or background job messages.

No system is perfectly secure. We will notify affected customers of a security incident involving their data as required by law.

## Your rights and choices

- **Export:** owners and admins can download a full export of the organization's data at any time.
- **Deletion:** owners can delete the organization and all of its data. Members can delete individual documents.
- **Correction:** you can edit your profile, organization details and facts at any time.
- **Consent choices:** website crawling, voice recordings and autopilot submission are off until your organization turns them on, and can be turned off again.
- **Email choices:** every grant guide email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Team notification emails (weekly digest, deadline reminders) have an unsubscribe link too, and members can change them in their notification settings.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights. Contact us to exercise them.
