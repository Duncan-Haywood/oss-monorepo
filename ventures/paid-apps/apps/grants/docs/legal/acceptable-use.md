# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 2026-11-19** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

> **Draft.** This document is a plain-language draft. Have counsel review it before launch.

Grant Autopilot exists to help organizations tell funders the truth about their work, efficiently. You agree not to use it to do any of the following.

## Honesty with funders

- Prepare or submit applications that are **fraudulent, false or misleading**, including inventing outcomes, numbers, partners, staff or endorsements.
- Remove or work around the Service's grounding checks or review flags in order to submit unsupported statements.
- Apply on behalf of an organization you are not authorized to represent, or impersonate another person or organization.
- Submit applications that violate a funder's rules (for example, eligibility or duplicate-submission rules).

## Other people's data

- Upload **confidential information or personal data belonging to others** unless you have the right to share it with us for this purpose.
- Upload sensitive personal information about the people you serve (such as health, immigration or case records) unless it is necessary, lawful, and marked restricted.

## The Service and other systems

- Attempt to access another organization's data, probe for vulnerabilities, or bypass access controls or usage limits.
- Overload the Service, or use it to scrape, spam or attack funders' portals or other websites.
- Upload malware or unlawful content.
- Resell or share access outside your organization without our agreement.

## Enforcement

We may remove content, suspend autopilot, or suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy, with notice where practical. Report misuse or security issues to us.
