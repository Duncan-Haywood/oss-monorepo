# Refund Policy

**Version 2026-11-19** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

> **Draft.** This document is a plain-language draft. Have counsel review it before launch.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

We do not offer free trials. Instead, if Grant Autopilot is not right for your organization, you can request a **full refund within 30 days** of your first paid subscription payment. No questions asked.

## How to request a refund

- Use the billing page in the app, or contact us. Tell us which organization the subscription is for.
- Refunds are issued through our merchant of record to the original payment method, including any sales tax charged.
- Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

## After the first 30 days

- You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
- Because our prices are set close to our costs, renewals after the first 30 days are generally not refundable, except where required by law or in cases of billing error or extended service outage, which we will review in good faith.

## Promo codes and plan changes

- A refund covers the amount you actually paid after any promo code discount.
- When you change plans, any credit or charge is handled as shown at checkout.

## Abuse

We may decline repeated refund requests for the same organization, or refunds on accounts terminated for violating the Acceptable Use Policy.
