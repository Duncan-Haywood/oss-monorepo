# Refund Policy

**Version 2026-11-25** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

> **Draft.** This document is a plain-language draft. Have counsel review it before launch.

## Try it free first

CMMC Workspace has a free Level 1 plan with no card required, so you can work through the Level 1 requirements and see how the Service fits before you pay for Level 2.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

If a paid plan is not right for your organization, you can request a **full refund within 30 days** of your first paid subscription payment, monthly or annual.

## How to request a refund

- Contact us, or reply to the receipt from our merchant of record (Paddle). Tell us which organization the subscription is for.
- Refunds are issued through the merchant of record to the original payment method, including any sales tax charged.
- Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

## After the first 30 days

- You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep the paid plan until the end of the period you paid for, then the free Level 1 plan's limits apply. Everything you recorded stays readable and downloadable.
- Renewals after the first 30 days are generally not refundable, except where required by law or in cases of billing error or an extended outage, which we will review in good faith.
- Unused AI drafts in a month do not carry over and are not refunded.
- A refund does not depend on any assessment outcome: the Service prepares documents and does not promise a score, a certification or a contract.

## Plan changes

When you change plans, any credit or charge is handled as shown at checkout.
