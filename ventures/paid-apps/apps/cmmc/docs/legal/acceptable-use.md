# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 2026-11-25** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

> **Draft.** This document is a plain-language draft. Have counsel review it before launch.

CMMC Workspace exists to help organizations prepare honest self-assessments of their own systems. You agree not to use it to do any of the following.

## Honest assessments

- Record a requirement as implemented, or accept a statement, that you know is not true, or use the Service's output to support an affirmation you know is false.
- Present the Service's score, SSP or POA&M as a certification, an assessment by a C3PAO, or a government approval, or say that we or any government body reviewed it.
- Prepare documents for a company you are not authorized to represent, or impersonate another person or company.

## Controlled and other people's data

- Upload or enter Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), classified information, or technical data controlled under ITAR or the EAR.
- Upload files you do not have the right to share with us, or that a customer or prime contractor has forbidden you to share with service providers.
- Enter personal information about others unless you have the right to share it with us for this purpose.
- Invite people to your organization who should not see its security gaps and evidence.

## The Service

- Attempt to access another organization's data, probe for vulnerabilities, or bypass access controls, plan limits or AI spending limits.
- Overload the Service, or enter text or upload files designed to make the AI model misbehave.
- Resell or share access outside your organization without our agreement.

## Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy, with notice where practical. Report misuse or security issues to us.
