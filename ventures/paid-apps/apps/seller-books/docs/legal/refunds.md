# Refund Policy

**Version 2026-11-26** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

> **Draft.** This document is a plain-language draft. Have counsel review it before launch.

## Try it free first

Seller Books has a free plan (one platform and a monthly order limit) with no card required, so you can see your own profit before you pay.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

If a paid plan is not right for you, you can request a **full refund within 30 days** of your first paid subscription payment.

## How to request a refund

- Contact us, or reply to the receipt from our merchant of record (Paddle). Tell us which shop the subscription is for.
- Refunds are issued through the merchant of record to the original payment method, including any sales tax charged.
- Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

## After the first 30 days

- You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep the paid plan until the end of the period you paid for, then the free plan's limits apply. Your records stay in your account.
- Renewals after the first 30 days are generally not refundable, except where required by law or in cases of billing error or an extended outage, which we will review in good faith.
- An annual plan cancelled after its first 30 days runs to the end of the year paid for.

## Plan changes

When you change plans, any credit or charge is handled as shown at checkout.
