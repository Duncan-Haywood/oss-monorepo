# GPSR Product Safety Checker

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/gpsr-product-safety-checker/>

Free, private browser tool for sellers of consumer goods in the EU: pick the product, your role and where you are established to see whether Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (the General Product Safety Regulation, applying since 13 December 2024) covers it, whether an EU responsible person stands behind it, and which basic duties are still open for your role.

After using this, you know whether you need an EU authorised representative or importer before listing, and what to put on the product and the listing, which used to take reading a 50-page Regulation.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/gpsr-product-safety-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
