Common questions

    
Does the EU General Product Safety Regulation apply to online sellers?

    
Yes. Regulation (EU) 2023/988 has applied since 13 December 2024 to consumer products made available to consumers in the EU, including products sold online from outside the EU. Products with their own sector law, such as toys and CE-marked electricals, are covered for the risks that law does not address.

    
Do I need an EU responsible person to sell on Amazon, Etsy or my own shop?

    
If you sell a consumer product to EU buyers, an EU-established manufacturer, importer, authorised representative or fulfilment service provider must stand behind it. A seller established outside the EU therefore needs one of them, and their name and address belong on the product and the listing.

    
What must an online listing show?

    
The manufacturer's name and contact details, the EU responsible person, an image of the product, its type, batch or serial identification and any warnings or safety information.

    
Are food, medicines and antiques covered?

    
No. Food, feed, medicinal products, antiques and products that need repair before use are outside the scope in Article 2.
