# UK Umbrella Company Take-Home Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-umbrella-company-take-home-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter the day rate your agency pays your umbrella company and see the umbrella margin, employer National Insurance and Apprenticeship Levy taken out first, then your income tax and National Insurance (England, Wales and Northern Ireland, 2026/27 rates), your take-home pay per year, month, week and day worked, what a higher margin costs, and the day rate you need for a target take-home.

After using this, a UK agency contractor knows what a day rate is really worth and can compare offers without a spreadsheet.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-umbrella-company-take-home-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
