Travel, labor, and costs

Drive time is billed time. A forty-minute trip each way plus twenty minutes of setup is a full hour that never touches a surface — and on a small driveway that hour is most of the job. Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, and insurance.

How contractors actually use this

How do I price a pressure washing job?

Split the property by surface. A driveway, a house wash, and a deck are three different production rates. Add the round-trip drive and the setup, then layer wage burden, chemicals, water, wear, overhead, and the margin you intend to keep. If that number is below your minimum job, bid the minimum.

Why does a per-square-foot quote lose money?

House siding soft-washes much slower than open concrete, and the drive does not care how many square feet are on the ticket. Two jobs of identical size can differ by an hour of unpaid travel. The after-travel hourly is the number that catches that before the quote is sent.

Are the starting production rates a standard?

No. They are common ballpark figures so a contractor who has never timed a surface can see that a roof and a parking lot are not the same job. Replace every rate with one timed from your own crew and equipment.

    Common questions

    How do I price a pressure washing job?

    Split the job by surface (driveway, house siding, deck, fence), divide each by a production rate in square feet per hour, add drive time and setup, then load wage, chemicals, overhead, and margin. A flat price per square foot cannot see that house siding washes much slower than a driveway, or that a 40-minute drive each way can cut the after-travel hourly in half.

    Why is my pressure washing bid unprofitable even when the per-foot price looks fine?

    Travel and setup are billed hours. A $200 driveway with 80 minutes of driving is a different job from a $200 driveway around the corner. This calculator reports the after-travel hourly so the drive is visible before the quote goes out.

    What is a typical pressure washing production rate?

    Ballpark starting points only: about 1,000 sq ft/hour for a clean concrete driveway, 500 for a house soft wash, 350 for a wood deck, 250 for a roof. Real rates depend on equipment, soil, storey height, and hand work. Time your own crew and replace the starting figures.
