How should photographers price usage rights?

Start with the business value and scope of the license, not only the hours spent shooting. A paid advertising campaign, national territory, long term, or exclusivity gives the client more commercial reach and restricts how you can reuse the work. This worksheet makes those assumptions visible so you can adjust them to your market and relationship.

Does this replace a licensing contract?

No. It creates a quote draft and records assumptions. You remain responsible for agreeing to and reviewing the final terms. Ask a qualified lawyer about contracts, copyright, model releases, and local requirements.
