# buildingSMART sample files

`bsi-simple-scene-architecture-ifc2x3.ifc` and `bsi-simple-scene-architecture-ifc43.ifc` are the
`Simple-Scene/Building-Architecture.ifc` files from
https://github.com/buildingSMART/Sample-Test-Files (IFC 2x3 TC1 and IFC 4.3 ADD2 folders),
(C) buildingSMART International Ltd., licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Unmodified. Used by `ifc.test.mjs`.

`bsi-wall-with-opening-and-window-ifc4.ifc` is `IFC 4.0.2.1 (IFC 4 ADD2 TC1)/ISO Spec - ReferenceView_V1.2/wall-with-opening-and-window.ifc`
from the same repository, same licence, unmodified: a 3 m wall with an `IfcOpeningElement` (x = 1000 mm, z = 500 mm) filled by a
1000 x 1000 mm `IfcWindow` nested in the opening's placement. Used by `ifc.test.mjs`.
