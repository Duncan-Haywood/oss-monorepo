Fence calculator

Type each straight run of fence in feet, add your gates, and you get posts (end, corner, line and gate), rails, pickets with waste, post concrete bags, fasteners, hinges and latches, and a cost if you type prices. Bays are cut equal so every rail ends on a post. Every default (post spacing, picket width, hole size, burial depth) is a figure to replace with your own. Nothing leaves your browser.


  
Do the whole house: framing, sheathing, plumbing and more in one takeoff


  
How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Bays and posts. Each run, less its gates, is cut into the fewest equal bays no wider than the post spacing. Corners are shared by the two runs that meet there; each gate adds two posts.
    2. 
    
  2. Rails and pickets. Rails run whole bays so each piece ends on a post; pickets fill each section and gate leaf at width plus gap, plus waste.
    3. 
    
  3. Posts and concrete. Post length is height plus burial, rounded up to a listed 8, 10 or 12 ft stock; concrete is the hole less the post, in whole bags.
    4. 
  


  
Questions

  
How many posts, rails and pickets do I need for 100 feet of 6 ft privacy fence with one gate?

  
A 40 ft, 20 ft and 40 ft run with a 48 in gate in the first run needs 16 posts: 4 end and corner posts, 10 line posts at no more than 8 ft apart, and 2 gate posts. That is 14 bays, so with 3 rails per bay it takes 42 rails of 8 ft, and 5-1/2 in pickets with a 1/4 in gap and 5% waste come to 222. The run lengths, spacing and picket size are the ones you type; the calculator only adds them.

  
How much concrete and how many fasteners does that fence need?

  
A 10 in hole 30 in deep, less a 3-1/2 in post, takes about 1.92 bags of 80 lb mix at 0.6 cubic feet a bag, so 16 posts need 31 bags. At 2 fasteners per picket per rail that is 1,332 fasteners, 2 boxes of 1,000. The 6 ft fence with 30 in buried needs a 10 ft post. Your frost depth and local code may call for deeper holes.
