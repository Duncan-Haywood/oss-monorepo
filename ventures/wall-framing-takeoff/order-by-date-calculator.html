Order-by date calculator

Trusses, windows, a steel beam and cabinets arrive weeks after you order them. Type one line per item (name, supplier lead time in calendar days, a delivery buffer in days, the date you need it on site, and optionally the supplier) and get the weekday to order by. Add today's date and it flags what is already late and when it would arrive if ordered now. The same date engine as the whole-house takeoff. Lead times are yours; nothing here forecasts a supplier. Nothing leaves your browser.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Order by. The need date less the lead time and the buffer, in calendar days (partial days round up), rolled back to the previous weekday.
    2. 
    
  2. Late. With today's date, an order-by date in the past is late; the item arrives today plus its lead time at the earliest.
    3. 
    
  3. Delay. Days between that arrival and the need date, if any. The buffer is a margin, so a late item only delays work once arrival passes the need date.

Questions

  
Trusses take 28 days to build and 3 days to ship; I need them on site Monday 1 June 2026. When do I order?

  
The need date less 28 days of lead and a 3-day buffer is 31 calendar days earlier: Friday 1 May 2026. Windows with a 42-day lead for the same date come to Monday 20 April 2026. If a result falls on a weekend the date rolls back to the Friday before, since nobody takes an order on a Sunday.

  
What if I am already past the order-by date?

  
Type today's date. An item that should already have been ordered is marked late, with the earliest date it can arrive if you order today (today plus its lead time) and how many days after the need date that is. The buffer is a margin, so a late item only delays work once arrival passes the need date itself.
