Board foot calculator

A board foot is 144 cubic inches: thickness in inches times width in inches times length in feet, divided by 12. Add a line for each kind of board (thickness may be typed in quarters, like 4/4 or 8/4), and you get board feet, cubic feet, waste, and a cost if you type a price per board foot, per piece or per linear foot. Lumber is priced on the size it is sold as, so a 2x4 counts as 2 by 4 even though it is milled smaller; the tool shows both. Nothing leaves your browser.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Each line. Thickness (in) x width (in) x length (ft) / 12, times the count. Quarters are converted: 4/4 is 1 in, 5/4 is 1.25 in, 8/4 is 2 in, and a thickness typed in quarters is hardwood, never swapped for a milled softwood size.
    2. 
    
  2. Sold or milled. Softwood dimension lumber and rough hardwood are priced on the size they are sold as. The milled column uses the usual finished sizes (a 2x4 is 1-1/2 by 3-1/2, a 2x6 is 1-1/2 by 5-1/2, a 1x6 is 3/4 by 5-1/2); sizes not on that list are used as typed.
    3. 
    
  3. Cost. A price per board foot is applied to the sold size (or the milled size if you pick milled). A price per piece or per linear foot ignores board feet. Waste is a percentage you type and is added on top.

Questions

  
How many board feet are in a 2x4x8?

  
A 2x4x8 is 2 x 4 x 8 / 12 = 5.33 board feet on the size it is sold as, which is how lumber is priced. Milled to its usual finished 1-1/2 by 3-1/2 inches it holds 3.5 board feet of wood. A bundle of 10 is 53.33 board feet sold. Your lumberyard decides which size it prices on, so ask.

  
How many board feet and what cost for ten 1x6x12 boards at $4.50 a board foot?

  
Each 1x6x12 is 1 x 6 x 12 / 12 = 6 board feet, so ten are 60 board feet and cost 60 x $4.50 = $270.00. With 10% waste you would buy about 66 board feet, $297.00. The price is the one you type; the calculator only multiplies.

  
How do I convert 8/4 hardwood to board feet?

  
Quarters are eighths of an inch over four: 8/4 is 2 inches thick, 4/4 is 1 inch and 5/4 is 1.25 inches. Two 8/4 boards 6 inches wide and 10 feet long are 2 x 6 x 10 / 12 = 10 board feet each, 20 in all; at $14 a board foot that is $280.00, or $308.00 with 10% waste (buy 22 board feet).
