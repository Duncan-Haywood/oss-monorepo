Baseboard and casing calculator

Type the house, its openings and the boards you buy. The framing engine's trim module cuts every baseboard run and every casing leg, head and sill, then packs the pieces into stock lengths. Nothing leaves your browser.


  
Add trim to a whole-house framing takeoff


  
The method

  
    
    
  1. Baseboard runs. Each wall's length less its door widths, split into the runs the doors leave. Outside walls get one face, interior walls two. Windows do not break a run.
    2. 
    
  2. Casing pieces. Two legs (door height plus the reveal) and a head for every door face; two legs, a head and a sill piece for every window face.
    3. 
    
  3. Pack. Pieces go into boards longest first; a run longer than a board is split.
    4. 
    
  4. Waste. Your percentage is added to the board count, rounded up.
    5. 
  


  
Questions

  
How much baseboard do I need for a 40 x 30 ft house?

  
For a 40 x 30 ft rectangle with one 3 ft exterior door and two 3 ft windows, plus a 20 ft interior wall with three 30 in doors, baseboard runs 162 ft: 137 ft along the outside walls (the door width is left out, windows do not break it) and 25 ft on the two faces of the interior wall. In 12 ft boards with 10% waste, that is 17 boards.

  
How much door and window casing do I need?

  
Casing goes on the interior face of every opening and on both faces of an interior wall's openings: two legs and a head for a door, two legs, a head and a sill piece for a window. The same house has 7 door faces and 2 window faces, which is 143 ft of 2.25 in casing with a 3/16 in reveal. Cut and packed into 8 ft boards with 10% waste, that is 25 boards.

  
Why not just add up lineal feet and divide by the board length?

  
Because pieces are cut, not poured. A 7 ft casing leg wastes most of an 8 ft board, and a 17 ft wall run needs a splice. The calculator packs each piece into boards (longest first) so the count reflects the offcuts you can reuse, then adds your waste percentage on top.

  
What is not counted?

  
Corner mitres, scarf joints unless you type an overlap, door stops, closet shelving, exterior trim, crown and chair rail. Stock lengths, casing width and reveal are what you type from the trim you buy. It is an estimate to check against your supplier.
