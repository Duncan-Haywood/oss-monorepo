Wall panel calculator

Framing walls flat in a shop or on a slab and tipping them up needs panels short enough for the truck, the table or two people. Type one wall and the longest panel you can handle. The framing engine lays out every stud, king, jack, header and cripple, then cuts only where no member crosses the line, so each panel is built from whole members. Weights use kiln-dried SPF at 28 lb/ft3 and 40 lb/ft3 for the treated bottom plate. Nothing leaves your browser.

The method

  
    
    
  1. Layout first. The wall is laid out with studs on layout, kings, jacks, headers and cripples exactly as in the takeoff tool.
    2. 
    
  2. Cut lines. A panel edge may fall only where it crosses no stud, king, jack, cripple, header, sill or block. The fewest panels win, then the most even widths. If no clear line exists under your limit the page says so.
    3. 
    
  3. Plates. Top and bottom plates are trimmed to each panel, so the panel weights include only the plate length in that panel.
    4. 
    
  4. Weight. Lumber weight is volume times density; sheathing is the area on the panel less the openings, times your pounds per sq ft. A handling estimate only. Lifting, bracing, rigging and shipping are for the builder.

Questions

  
How long can a prefab wall panel be?

  
It depends on your truck bed or trailer, table, crane or crew, not on a code number. Many shops keep panels near 12 ft so two people can lift a bare frame. Type your own limit above.

  
How heavy is a framed wall panel?

  
The 40 ft wall in the form above, with a window and a door, is four panels; a bare 2x4 frame of about 10 to 12 ft weighs roughly 90 to 160 lb, and sheathing adds roughly the same again. The page prints each panel.

  
Does this check lifting or bracing?

  
No. It reports lumber and sheathing weight only.
