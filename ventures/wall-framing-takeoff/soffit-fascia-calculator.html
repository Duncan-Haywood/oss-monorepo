Soffit and fascia calculator

Type the house and the overhangs. The framing engine lays out the roof, finds the eave and rake runs under the overhangs, packs soffit panels, fascia boards and J-channel into stock lengths and adds your waste. Nothing leaves your browser.


  
Add soffit and fascia to a whole-house framing takeoff


  
The method

  
    
    
  1. Runs. Eaves run the roof's long sides on a gable roof and all four sides on a hip, each the wall length plus the overhang at both ends. A gable's rakes run up the slope.
    2. 
    
  2. Soffit. Panels lie parallel to the wall, so an overhang takes as many courses as it has panel widths. Each course's run is packed into stock panels, first fit decreasing, then your waste is added. Rakes get soffit only if you turn it on.
    3. 
    
  3. Fascia. One run along every eave and, on a gable, up every rake, packed into boards the same way.
    4. 
    
  4. J-channel and frieze. J-channel follows the wall the soffit meets; the optional frieze board follows the same runs.

Questions

  
How much soffit and fascia do I need for a 24 x 40 ft gable roof?

  
For a 24 ft wide, 40 ft long house with 12 in overhangs and the rakes left open, a gable roof has two eave runs of 42 ft (40 ft plus the 12 in rake overhang at each end). That is 84 ft of eave, so 84 sq ft of soffit in one course of 12 in panels, which is 8 panels of 12 ft once 10% waste is added. Fascia runs along the eaves and up the four rakes, 142 ft in all, which is 11 boards of 16 ft, and the J-channel where the soffit meets the wall is 8 pieces of 12 ft.

  
What changes if I box the rakes and add a frieze board?

  
Turn on rake soffit and frieze and the same 24 x 40 ft gable roof counts soffit under the rakes as well: 142 sq ft in all, 14 panels of 12 ft, 14 pieces of J-channel and 11 frieze boards of 16 ft along the wall. Most houses leave the rake open or box it with a fascia only, so rake soffit and frieze are off until you ask for them.

  
How much does a hip roof need?

  
A hip roof has eaves on all four sides and no rakes. A 24 x 40 ft hip roof with 18 in overhangs has 140 ft of eave, so with 12 in panels it takes 2 courses of soffit under each side, 210 sq ft, 27 panels of 12 ft, 11 fascia boards of 16 ft and 14 pieces of J-channel.

  
What is not counted?

  
Corner mitres, drip edge (the roof calculator counts it), nails, caulk, paint, and a cross wing of a different pitch. Panel widths, stock lengths and the waste figure are yours to type; check the result against your plans and your supplier's catalogue.
