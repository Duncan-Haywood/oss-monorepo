Paint calculator

Type your walls (length in inches, doors and windows with width and height) and the paint follows: wall area less openings on each painted face, door leaves, ceiling rectangles, coats, coverage and waste, rounded up to whole gallons, with a separate primer coat and a cost if you type prices. The same paint engine as the whole-house takeoff. Cutting in, rollers, tape, drop cloths and texture are not counted. Nothing leaves your browser.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Wall area. Length times paint height, less each opening at its full size (window glass is not painted), on every painted face: one for an exterior wall, two for an interior wall ("sheathed": false).
    2. 
    
  2. Doors and ceilings. Each door leaf is 21 sq ft a side by default, two sides; ceilings are width by depth rectangles you type.
    3. 
    
  3. Gallons. Area times coats over coverage (350 sq ft per gallon), plus waste, rounded up to whole gallons; primer is counted on its own coverage (300).

Questions

  
How much paint and primer does a front wall, a hall and a ceiling need?

  
The 20 ft front wall at 8 ft is 160 sq ft less a 3 ft door (20 sq ft) and a 4 ft window (16 sq ft), 124 sq ft on its one painted face. The 12 ft interior hall wall is 96 sq ft less its door (17.8 sq ft), 78.2 sq ft on each of two faces. Together with the 120 sq ft ceiling and 2 door leaves of 21 sq ft on both sides (84 sq ft), two coats at 350 sq ft per gallon with 10% waste come to 3.05 gal, so buy 4 gal. One primer coat on walls and ceiling is 1.47 gal, so buy 2 gal.

  
What does that cost, and how do I change the coverage?

  
With the typed prices (38.00 per gallon of paint and 28.00 per gallon of primer) that is 4 x 38 + 2 x 28 = 208.00. A five-gallon pail price, if you type one, replaces gallons only when it is cheaper. The 350 sq ft per gallon figure is a typical label number: textured walls, new drywall and dark-to-light changes cover less, so type your can's figure as coverage.
