Questions

  
How much flex duct and trunk do I need for three rooms?

  
With supply runs of 14, 18 and 12 ft, one 10 ft return, and 30 ft of rigid trunk, the flex runs total 54 ft. With 10% waste that is 59.4 ft, which is 3 rolls of 25 ft flex, and the trunk is 33 ft, which is 7 sticks of 5 ft. The lengths are the ones you type from your own layout; the calculator only adds them.

  
How many boots, registers and hangers?

  
The same layout has 4 registers and 4 boots (the living room has 2), 1 return grille and 4 fittings. At a 4 ft hanger spacing you type, the 84 ft of duct needs 21 hangers, and at 0.5 ft of tape per duct foot it needs 42 ft of sealing tape, 1 roll of 60 ft.
