Change order calculator

Type the budget before and after a design change as lines of section, item, unit, qty, and amount (leave amount out for an unpriced line). The tool matches lines by section and item, lists what was added, removed or changed, and totals the difference. Nothing leaves your browser.

Questions

  
What is a change order?

  
A change order records a change to the agreed work and what it does to the price. This tool compares two priced lists of the same job, before and after, and shows the difference line by line.

  
How are the two lists matched?

  
By section and item name. A line only in the new list is added, one only in the old is removed, and a changed quantity or amount is listed with its difference. Lumber lines whose names differ only in the count of lengths are paired as one change.

  
Does this tool know what materials cost?

  
No. It ships no prices. Every quantity and amount is from the lists you type, and a line priced on one side only is listed and not netted. The sample is made up.
