Concrete block (CMU) wall calculator

Type each wall's length and height in inches, its openings, the block thickness and the rebar you plan. You get blocks to order with waste, mortar bags, grout in cubic feet and yards (cells at each vertical bar, plus any bond beam courses), vertical and bond beam rebar with laps, and ladder joint reinforcement. Block count, mortar per bag, cell void share and grout waste are rules of thumb you can change. Nothing here checks bar size, spacing, lintels or grouting against a code or an engineer, and it does not design a masonry wall.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Blocks. A nominal block is 16 by 8 in with the joint included, so net wall area (length times height, less each opening) divided by 128 sq in gives the blocks, 1.125 per sq ft, plus waste (5% by default). Courses are height over 8 in, rounded up; a height or length that is not a multiple of 8 in is flagged as a cut.
    2. 
    
  2. Mortar and grout. Mortar bags come from blocks per bag (35 by default, a rule of thumb: use your supplier's). Grout fills the cells at each vertical bar (both wall ends and every spacing) and the whole course length for each bond beam; a cell is the void share (0.45 by default) of half a block's cross-section. Waste (10%) is added and the order is shown in cubic yards.
    3. 
    
  3. Rebar. Verticals are the wall height plus a lap of 48 bar diameters; bond beam bars run the wall length with the same lap in 20 ft stock; weight is length times the standard pounds per foot. Ladder joint reinforcement is counted every N courses.

Questions

  
How many concrete blocks, how much mortar and grout does a 40 by 8 ft block wall need?

  
A 40 ft by 8 ft wall of 8 in block with one 36 by 80 in door is 300 sq ft net, which is 337.5 blocks at 1.125 per sq ft; with 5% waste order 355. At 35 blocks per mortar bag that is 11 bags. With a #5 bar in a grouted cell every 32 in (16 bars) the grout is 25.6 cu ft, 28.16 with 10% waste, or 1.04 cu yd, and the rebar is about 175 lb.

  
What happens to the grout if I add a bond beam every 6th course?

  
Each bond beam course is grouted along the whole wall length, so grout rises with the number of courses: a 20 ft by 8 ft wall with a bar every 48 in and a bond beam every 6 courses needs 17.6 cu ft before waste, against 9.6 without the beams. Block size, bar size, spacing and where beams go are your inputs from your plans: this page does not check them against a code or an engineer's design.
