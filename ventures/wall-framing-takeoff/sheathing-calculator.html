The method

  
    
    
  1. Wall height. A precut stud plus the bottom plate and the double top plate, so an "8 ft" wall is 97-1/8 in tall.
    2. 
    
  2. Panels. Sheets are 4 ft wide. Joints land on studs, a sliver narrower than 12 in is avoided by pulling the last joint back, and the openings are cut out of the panels they cross.
    3. 
    
  3. Order. Panels are packed into sheets and the sheet length is chosen to minimise area bought; the lower bound shows how close to the best order it is.
    4. 
    
  4. Openings. This page spreads your windows and doors evenly. A real plan has them where the architect put them, which the takeoff tool takes exactly.

Questions

  
How many sheets of sheathing do I need for a 40 x 24 ft house with 8 ft walls?

  
With no openings, 32 sheets: the 40 ft walls take 10 sheets each and the 24 ft walls 6 each, on vertical 4 ft sheets. The walls stand 97-1/8 in tall (a precut stud plus the bottom plate and double top plate), so a 4x8 sheet is too short and the order is 4x9 sheets: 1,152 sq ft bought for 1,036 sq ft of wall.

  
Is a 4x8 sheet long enough for an 8 ft wall?

  
No. A wall framed with 92-5/8 in precut studs is 97-1/8 in tall with its three plates, 1-1/8 in more than a 4x8 sheet. Either buy 9 ft sheets, or use 8 ft sheets with a second narrow course and a blocked joint, which the whole-house takeoff can lay out. This page buys the cheapest single-length sheet that covers the wall.

  
What do 6 windows and 2 doors change?

  
On the same 40 x 24 ft house, 6 windows of 36 x 48 in and 2 doors of 36 x 82-1/2 in spread round the walls take 113 sq ft off the wall area (923 sq ft left to sheathe) but still add a sheet: 33 sheets of 4x9, 1,188 sq ft bought, 22% offcut. Cut-outs are mostly waste, because a panel is cut around an opening and the piece rarely fits anywhere else.

  
Horizontal or vertical sheets?

  
Vertical sheets run floor to top plate and every edge lands on a stud. Turned sideways, the same house with the windows and doors takes 27 sheets of 4x12 (38 panels), but each course joint needs blocking. The page runs both so you can compare; the choice is a design one.
