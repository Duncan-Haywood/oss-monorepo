Questions

  
How many 3 x 6 inch tiles do I need for a 5 ft wide shower with two 3 ft side walls?

  
A 60 x 80 in back wall with a 12 x 24 in niche and two 36 x 80 in side walls is 73.3 sq ft before the niche and 71.3 sq ft tiled. With 10% waste that is 78.5 sq ft, and a 3 x 6 in tile with a 1/8 in joint covers 0.133 sq ft, so you need 591 tiles. The sizes are the ones you type; the calculator only adds them.

  
How much backer board, membrane, thinset and grout does that shower need?

  
The backer board covers the 73.3 sq ft gross area (the niche is cut out, not saved) plus 10% waste, which is 6 sheets of 15 sq ft, held with 108 screws (2 boxes of 100). The membrane at 50 sq ft per gallon is 2 gallons. The tiled area takes 1 bag of thinset at 80 sq ft per bag, and about 11.2 lb of grout, which is 1 bag of 25 lb.
