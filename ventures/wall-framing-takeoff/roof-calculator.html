Roof calculator

Type the house, the roof pitch and the overhangs. The framing engine's roof module places every rafter, cuts the sheathing courses up each slope and counts squares, bundles, felt and drip edge. Nothing leaves your browser.

The method

  
    
    
  1. Geometry. Run is half the width less half the ridge board; rafter length is (run + overhang) times the slope factor, the square root of 1 + (pitch/12)2, to the nearest 1/16 in.
    2. 
    
  2. Rafters. One at each gable end, the others on your spacing; a hip roof adds hips and jack rafters.
    3. 
    
  3. Sheathing. 4 x 8 ft sheets in courses up each slope, end joints staggered, the top course ripped to fit.
    4. 
    
  4. Roofing. Slope area in squares, times your waste, at three bundles a square; underlayment rolls, drip edge, ridge cap and starter strip from the edge lengths.
    5. 
  


  
Questions

  
How many squares of shingles do I need for a 24 x 40 ft gable roof?

  
For a 24 ft wide, 40 ft long house with a 6:12 pitch and 12 in overhangs on every side, the two planes come to 12.2 roofing squares (a square is 100 sq ft). At 3 bundles per square with 10% waste that is 41 bundles of shingles, plus 2 ridge cap bundles and 1 starter bundle.

  
How long are the rafters for a 6:12 roof on a 24 ft wide house?

  
Each rafter is 173-9/16 in (14 ft 5-9/16 in) along the slope, counting a 12 in eave overhang and the room a 2x10 ridge board takes. The roof rises 72 in over the plates, and a 2x8 rafter cut with a 3-1/2 in seat has a plumb cut 1-9/16 in deep square to its edge, 22% of the board.

  
How many sheets of roof sheathing does a roof need?

  
The same roof takes 44 sheets of 4 x 8 ft plywood or OSB for 1221 sq ft. The calculator lays courses up each slope with end joints staggered, so the count includes the offcuts you can reuse rather than dividing area by 32.

  
What is not counted?

  
Valleys and cross wings, dormers, vents, flashing, nails, trusses and any check of rafter span against a table or code. Rafter size and spacing are what you type. Check the result against your plans and your supplier.
