Questions

  
What is the critical path in a construction schedule?

  
The critical path is the chain of activities with zero slack: if any one of them runs late, the finish date moves by the same number of days. This tool finds it with a forward and a backward pass over the predecessors you type.

  
Does this tool know how long framing or drywall takes?

  
No. It ships no durations. Every duration, predecessor and crew count is yours, from your own crews and past jobs. The sample is only there to show the format.

  
How are weekends, holidays and cure time handled?

  
Weekends are skipped. Holidays and weather days are dates you list. Cure time or an inspection wait is a lag in working days after the predecessors finish. Weather is not forecast.
