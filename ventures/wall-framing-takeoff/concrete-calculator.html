Questions

  
How many cubic yards of concrete for a 12 x 12 slab 4 inches thick?

  
A 12 x 12 ft slab 4 in thick is 48 cu ft, which is 1.78 cu yd. With 10% waste that is 1.96, so order 2 cu yd of ready-mix, or about 88 bags of 80 lb mix at 0.6 cu ft each.

  
How many 80 lb bags of concrete make a cubic yard?

  
A cubic yard is 27 cu ft and an 80 lb bag yields about 0.6 cu ft, so 45 bags before waste, 50 with 10% waste. Past about a yard, ready-mix is usually the easier order.

  
Why add 10% to a concrete order?

  
Subgrade is rarely dead flat, forms bow a little and some mix is left in the truck and the wheelbarrow. Running short mid-pour leaves a cold joint, so most people over-order slightly. The percentage is yours to change.

  
Is there a minimum order for ready-mix concrete?

  
Many plants charge a short-load fee below roughly 3 to 4 cu yd, and the figure varies by plant, so call yours. For one or two yards, compare that fee against bagged mix.
