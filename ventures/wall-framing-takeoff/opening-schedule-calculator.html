Door and window schedule calculator

Type your walls and their openings (offset, framed width and height in inches, header size) and get the schedule a permit set, a window order and a framer's walk-through all start from: one mark per distinct opening (D1, W1, ...), how many there are, the rough opening, a unit size less a clearance you type, the header and its height, the sill height and where each one sits. The same engine as the whole-house takeoff. Width and height are the framed (rough) opening; the unit size is a shim clearance, not a maker's figure, and product choice is not made here.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Grouping. Two openings share a mark when kind, framed width and height, header size, header plies and header height all match. Header plies default to 2 on a 2x4 wall and 3 on a 2x6 wall.
    2. 
    
  2. Sizes. Rough opening is the framed size; unit size is that less your allowance in each direction (0.5 in by default).
    3. 
    
  3. Sill. A window's sill height is the header height less the opening's height; a door's is 0.

Questions

  
How many marks does a house with two doors and four windows get?

  
Three walls here (a 20 ft front wall with two 48 by 60 windows and a 36 by 82 door, a 12 ft side wall with a 36 by 48 window and a 20 ft 2x6 back wall with a 72 by 82 door and a 48 by 60 window) have 2 doors and 4 windows in 5 marks: D1 72 by 82 (unit 71.5 by 81.5), D2 36 by 82, W1 48 by 60 x2 with 2-2x10 headers and a 22 in sill, W2 48 by 60 with a 3-2x10 header and W3 36 by 48 with a 34 in sill.

  
Why is the back window a different mark from the two front windows?

  
The same 48 by 60 window gets its own mark when the header differs: the back wall is 2x6, so its header defaults to three plies against two on the 2x4 front wall, and a framer needs to see that on the schedule. Marks match only when kind, framed size, header size, plies and header height all agree.

  
Is the unit size the size to order?

  
No. Unit size is the rough opening less a typed allowance (0.5 in by default, a shim clearance, not a maker's figure). Type unitAllowance 0.75 and every unit size drops another 0.25 in, such as W1 from 47.5 by 59.5 to 47.25 by 59.25. The manufacturer's rough-opening sheet governs the order.
