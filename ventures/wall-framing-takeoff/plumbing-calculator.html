Questions

  
How much drain and vent pipe do I need for a bathroom with a toilet, sink and tub?

  
With a toilet (6 ft drain, 4 ft vent), a lavatory (5 ft drain, 5 ft vent) and a tub/shower (8 ft drain, 6 ft vent), the drain and vent runs total 34 ft. With 10% waste that is 37.4 ft, which is 4 sticks of 10 ft pipe. The lengths are the ones you type from your own plan; the calculator only adds them.

  
How many PEX coils for a bathroom's hot and cold water?

  
Using the same bathroom, hot runs of 10 ft and 14 ft total 24 ft and cold runs of 12, 10 and 14 ft total 36 ft. Each is one 100 ft coil with 10% waste, so the order is 1 hot coil and 1 cold coil, with 3 traps and 5 stops (the toilet gets one cold stop, the lavatory and tub one per hot and cold run as typed or defaulted).
