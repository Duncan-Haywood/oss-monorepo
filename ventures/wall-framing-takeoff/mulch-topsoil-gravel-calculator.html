Mulch, topsoil and gravel calculator

Type each bed, path or round tree ring (length and width, or a diameter, in feet) and a depth in inches, and pick mulch, topsoil, pea gravel, crushed stone, road base or sand. You get the cubic yards in place, the cubic yards to order with compaction and waste, tons at your supplier's weight, bags, and a cost if you type a price. Every default is a figure to replace with your own. Nothing leaves your browser.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Volume in place. Each area times its depth: a rectangle is length times width, a ring is pi times the radius squared, or you type square feet. Cubic feet over 27 is cubic yards.
    2. 
    
  2. What to order. Volume times your compaction factor (1 for mulch, about 1.1 to 1.25 for a packed base) times one plus waste, rounded up to the quarter yard, never below your supplier's delivery minimum.
    3. 
    
  3. Tons, bags and cost. Tons are the order volume times your tons per yard; bags are the order volume over the bag size; cost is the rounded yards at a yard price or the tons at a ton price.

Questions

  
How much mulch do I need for a 24 by 6 ft bed and an 8 ft round tree ring, 3 inches deep?

  
The bed is 144 sq ft and the ring 50.27 sq ft, 194.27 sq ft in all. At 3 in deep that is 48.57 cu ft, or 1.8 cu yd in place. With 5% waste you order 1.89 cu yd, which a yard sold to the quarter makes 2 cu yd, or 102 bags of 0.5 cu ft. At a typed $38 a yard the load is $76. The depth, waste and price are the ones you type.

  
How many tons of crushed stone for a 40 by 3 ft path, 4 inches deep?

  
The path is 120 sq ft, 40 cu ft or 1.48 cu yd in place. Allowing 20% for compaction and 5% waste, the order is 1.87 cu yd, sold as 2 yd, and at a typed 1.4 tons a yard about 2.61 tons. At a typed $32 a ton that is $83.63. Your supplier's ticket weight for the stone you pick wins over the typed figure.
