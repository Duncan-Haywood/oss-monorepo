Wall brace calculator

Diagonal wall braces (let-in 1x4, metal T-brace) stiffen a wall until the sheathing goes on. Type one wall length per line, the wall height and your own rule, and get how many braces each wall takes, how long each brace is and the feet to buy. The rule is the same one the takeoff tool uses: every wall at least as long as your minimum gets one brace per spacing, each as long as the wall height over the sine of the angle. Nothing leaves your browser.


  
Lay out and order a whole house, braces included


  
The method

  
    
    
  1. Which walls. Every wall at least your minimum length. Which walls are braced wall lines under your code is the engineer's or building department's call; this counts a rule you type.
    2. 
    
  2. How many. The wall length divided by your spacing, rounded up. Shorter walls get none.
    3. 
    
  3. How long. Wall height divided by the sine of the brace angle: 135.8 in for an 8 ft wall at 45 degrees. A brace at that angle also needs height divided by the tangent in horizontal run, 96 in here, so the page warns when a wall is too short for the braces you asked for.
    4. 
  


  
Questions

  
How long is a 45 degree brace in an 8 ft wall?

  
About 11 ft 4 in measured on the diagonal (96 in over sin 45 degrees = 135.8 in). Buy a 12 ft 1x4 and cut it.

  
Does this meet code?

  
It does not check any code. Braced wall length and method come from your code edition and your engineer.

  
Can I count metal T-braces the same way?

  
Yes, if they run at your angle; the number and length are the same arithmetic. Follow the manufacturer's installation for the actual product.
