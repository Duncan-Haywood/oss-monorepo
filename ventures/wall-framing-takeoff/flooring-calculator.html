Flooring calculator

Type the room and pick the floor. You get boxes of plank (hardwood, laminate, vinyl plank) with waste, tiles with thinset and grout, or carpet cut from the roll with seams, pad and tack strip. The same flooring engine as the whole-house takeoff. Nothing leaves your browser.

How it is counted

  
    
    
  1. Plank. Room area plus 7% waste (12% on the diagonal), rounded up to whole boxes.

  2. Tile. Area plus 10% waste (15% on the diagonal), divided by one tile plus its joint; thinset at 80 sq ft a bag; grout from the joint volume, in 25 lb bags.
    3. 
    
  3. Carpet. The roll runs along the long side with a 6 in trim allowance, one strip per roll width across the short side, seams between strips; pad is the area plus 5%; tack strip is the perimeter.

Questions

  
How many boxes of laminate or hardwood flooring do I need for a 15 x 12 ft room?

  
A 15 x 12 ft room is 180 sq ft. With 7% waste that is 192.6 sq ft to buy, which is 10 boxes at 20 sq ft a box, leaving about 7.4 sq ft over. Buying by area alone (9 boxes) leaves you short once the cuts are made.

  
How much waste should I add to flooring?

  
This calculator uses 7% for planks laid straight and 12% for a room laid on the diagonal, and 10% or 15% for tile. These are typical figures you can change, not a maker's rule; a room with many corners or a pattern needs more.

  
How many 12 x 12 in tiles for an 8 x 5 ft bathroom?

  
The floor is 40 sq ft. With 10% waste you buy 44 sq ft of tile, and each 12 in tile with a 1/8 in joint covers 1.02 sq ft, so the calculator counts 44 tiles, 1 bag of thinset and about 2.6 lb of grout.

  
How much carpet do I need for a 12 x 12 ft room?

  
Carpet comes off a roll, usually 12 ft wide. A 12 x 12 ft room is one strip the long side plus a 6 in trim allowance, 12.5 linear feet, which is 16.7 sq yd of carpet, with 16.8 sq yd of pad and 48 ft of tack strip. A room wider than the roll needs a seam.
