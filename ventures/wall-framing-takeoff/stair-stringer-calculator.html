Questions

  
What is the formula for a stair stringer?

  
Divide the total rise (finished floor to finished floor) by the largest riser you may use, 7-3/4 in under the model residential code, and round up to get the number of risers. Divide the total rise by that number for the riser height. There is one fewer tread than risers, and each tread is at least 10 in. The pitch line along the nose points is the hypotenuse of the riser and tread.

  
How many stringers does a stair need?

  
It depends on the width and the tread board. It depends on the width and the stringer spacing you choose. The calculator defaults to 11-1/2 in between stringers (4 on a 36 in stair) and notes when the gap is more than a tread board of that thickness usually spans (12 in under 1-1/4 in thick, 16 in under 1-1/2 in, 24 in for 2x). Your local code and the tread board decide.

  
Why is the first riser cut shorter?

  
The first step's riser is cut one tread thickness short at the bottom stringer so that once the tread board is on top, the first finished step is the same height as all the others.

  
How much stairwell opening do I need for headroom?

  
About (80 in + floor thickness) x tread / riser, so for a 9 ft rise with 10 in treads and a 12-1/2 in floor, at least 9 ft 11-15/16 in. The calculator solves it for your numbers.
