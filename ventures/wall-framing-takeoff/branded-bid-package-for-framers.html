A bid package that looks like your company

You already counted the studs, headers and sheets. The bid package turns that takeoff and your own prices into the document the customer reads: your company name, logo and colour on top, the price by section with your markup spread through it, the validity period, and a quantities appendix counted from the plans. What costs money and what does not

The takeoff, stud layout, cut list, lumber order, budget, schedule and the plain customer letter are free and stay free. Only the branded bid package (your company name, logo and colour, the proposal and a quantities appendix on one printable page) is planned as a paid extra, as a yearly licence key. It is not on sale yet: there is no checkout, so nothing here can charge you. The sample is an invented job with invented prices.

Does the customer see my prices or margin

No. The customer sees the price by section and the total. The appendix lists quantities from the plans, never unit prices, labor lines or allowances.

Who it is for

Small residential framers, remodelers and owner-builders who bid jobs by hand and want the proposal to look like their company. Nothing you type is uploaded; the package is built in your browser. The code is MIT licensed.
