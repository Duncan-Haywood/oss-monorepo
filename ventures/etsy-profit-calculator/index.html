Do Offsite Ads still leave a profit?

Offsite Ads are a percentage Etsy takes on the order total when it attributes the sale to Google or social. That is not on-site Etsy Ads — ACOS is a click you buy. Does an Etsy shipping label still beat Pirate Ship?

Buying the label is not the same decision as free shipping or Star Seller. Free shipping is what you charge the buyer. Star Seller is a faster label to stay on-time. Common questions

What does "Return on cash" measure, and why does it sometimes show "—"?

Return on cash is profit divided by the money you laid out before the sale — shipping cost, materials, and other per-order cost — not divided by revenue or the price you charged. Etsy's fees come out of the proceeds rather than out of your pocket up front, so they stay out of that denominator on purpose. If those three inputs are all zero, there is nothing invested to divide by, so the field reads "—" instead of a divide-by-zero result.

Does the break-even item price include Offsite Ads or on-site Etsy Ads?

It includes whatever offsite ad rate is set on the order form above, spread across the price the same way transaction and payment fees are — raise that rate and the break-even price moves with it. It does not include the separate on-site Etsy Ads section further down the page, because that is a per-click cost you set as an ACOS percentage, not a fee charged on every order.

How does the production partner's reject rate change the comparison?

It reduces how many good units the partner path actually delivers, not the price you pay per unit. At a 5% reject rate, sending 40 orders' worth of demand to the partner yields only 38 sellable units, so the expected cash-out and expected keep per finished sale rise to cover the units that never arrive sellable, on top of the unit cost and handling fee you typed in. Your own handmade capacity and cost are unaffected.

Why is there a separate Offsite Ads table when the order form already has an offsite ad rate field?

The order form's rate is your current best guess and feeds every other number on the page — the hero result, break-even price, and every comparison table. The Offsite Ads section further down re-runs that same order at organic (0%), 12%, and 15% side by side, because Etsy sets the rate by a shop's trailing 12-month sales rather than letting a seller choose it, so seeing all three shows what changes if a shop crosses that threshold.
