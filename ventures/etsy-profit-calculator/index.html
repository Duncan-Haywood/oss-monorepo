Is Etsy Plus worth it this month?

Plus is a monthly subscription. The cash it returns is only the listing credits and Etsy Ads credit you would have paid for anyway — unused credits expire. Shop banners and restock requests are not priced unless you assign them a dollar value. This is arithmetic on your inputs, not Etsy's live Plus schedule.

Does this personalization still pay?

A custom name, color, or size looks like easy extra cash until Etsy takes a cut of the higher ticket and you spend twenty minutes plus extra supplies. If this buyer would have bought the listing as written, only the extra keep matters. If they would have walked away, the personalized order is extra demand — take it when that path still leaves a profit. This is arithmetic on your inputs, not Etsy's live fee schedule.

Do on-site Etsy Ads still pay?

Etsy Ads is a click you buy. Offsite Ads is a percentage Etsy takes when it sends the buyer — that rate stays in the order form above. ACOS is spend divided by attributed sales, the same definition Ads Manager uses. If this buyer would have bought anyway, Ads cost you the click. If they would have walked away, the advertised order is extra demand — take it when that path still leaves a profit. Monthly spend is the same figure as in the Plus table. This is arithmetic on your inputs, not Etsy's live CPC.
