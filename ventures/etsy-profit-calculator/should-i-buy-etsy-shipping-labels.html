Should I buy Etsy shipping labels or use Pirate Ship?

Sellers search for “Etsy shipping labels vs Pirate Ship” because the cheaper quote looks like free leftover. It is not. Four minutes to buy and upload a label plus a higher item-not-received rate usually eat a $0.90 postage save.

Open the Etsy profit calculator, keep your shop fees, and type the Etsy quote and the Pirate Ship, Shippo, or carrier-account quote you would actually buy. The table below uses the calculator defaults: $30 item, $5.00 shipping charged, 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 payment, $6.00 Etsy label, $5.10 third-party label, 0.8% vs 2.5% claims, 4 minutes at $18/hour, 40 shipments/month.

This is not free shipping — that is what you charge the buyer — and it is not Star Seller. Purchase Protection, label quotes, and claim rates are inputs, not Etsy’s live policy.

Four minutes wipe a $0.90 postage save

The $6.00 Etsy label at a 0.8% claim rate expects $20.98 after the typed claim mix — a claim on that path leaves −$10.00. A $5.10 third-party label at a 2.5% claim rate after $1.20 of upload time expects $20.14 (−$0.84 per shipment, −$33.60 across 40). The same third-party postage with no extra time expects $21.34 (+$0.36). No third-party claim rate on the timed path can match the Etsy label. The quote has to stay at about $4.27 before it matches.

Zero upload minutes flip Pirate Ship ahead by $0.36

Keep the default fees and drop upload time to zero. The $5.10 quote expects $21.34 — $0.36 more than Etsy’s $20.98. Third-party claims then have to stay at about 3.7% before that path matches. Matching the 0.8% Etsy claim rate and keeping the four minutes still loses −$0.30 ($20.68 vs $20.98). A $4.00 quote and one upload minute expects $22.14 (+$1.16).

15% Offsite Ads still leave the Etsy label ahead

Turn on 15% Offsite Ads and keep the default labels. The Etsy label expects $15.77. The timed third-party path expects $15.03 (−$0.74). Postage-only expects $16.23. The cheaper quote still loses after four minutes.

Common questions

Should I buy Etsy shipping labels or use Pirate Ship?

On the worked example — a $30 listing, $5.00 shipping charged, 6.5% transaction plus 3%+$0.25 payment, $6.00 Etsy label, $5.10 third-party label, 0.8% vs 2.5% item-not-received claims, and 4 minutes at $18/hour — the Etsy label expects $20.98. When does Pirate Ship beat an Etsy shipping label?

When upload time is already sunk and the claim-rate gap stays small. Drop the four minutes to zero on the same $30 listing and the $5.10 quote expects $21.34 — $0.36 more than Etsy’s $20.98. Third-party claims then have to stay at about 3.7% before that path matches. A $4.00 quote and one upload minute expects $22.14 (+$1.16). Matching the 0.8% Etsy claim rate and keeping the four minutes still loses $0.30.

Is this the same as free shipping or Star Seller?

No. Free shipping is what you charge the buyer. Star Seller is a faster label to stay on-time plus message time.
