Common questions

What is the Etsy Offsite Ads fee?

Etsy Offsite Ads typically charge 15% of the order total for shops under $10,000 in trailing 12-month sales, or 12% for shops over that threshold. The fee applies on top of listing, transaction, and payment-processing fees when Etsy attributes the sale to an offsite ad. Those percentages are starting points you replace with the rate on your statement.

Can Offsite Ads make an Etsy order unprofitable?

Yes. On a $14 handmade print with $3 shipping charged, $5.50 shipping cost, and $7 in materials, the order still clears $2.44 organically and $0.39 at 12%, but loses $0.12 at 15%. Cash invested is $12.50. The order stays at or above $0.00 leftover until Offsite Ads reach about 14.3%.

Are Offsite Ads the same as Etsy Ads?

No. On-site Etsy Ads is a click you buy; ACOS is spend divided by attributed sales. Offsite Ads is a percentage Etsy takes when it sends the buyer from Google or social. They stack. On the calculator's default $30 listing, 15% Offsite Ads still leave $15.98 versus $21.23 organic — the $14 print is the case that flips to a loss.
