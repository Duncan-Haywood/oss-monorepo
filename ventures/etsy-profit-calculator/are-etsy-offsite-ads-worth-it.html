When Etsy Offsite Ads turn a handmade sale into a loss

Sellers search for “are Etsy Offsite Ads worth it” because the fee is easy to miss on a profitable-looking listing. Offsite Ads are charged on the order total (item price plus shipping charged), on top of the listing fee, the 6.5% transaction fee, and Etsy Payments. Shops under $10,000 in trailing 12-month sales typically pay 15%; shops over that threshold typically pay 12%.

Those percentages are starting points you should replace with the rate on your own statement. This page does not publish Etsy’s live schedule.

Worked example: a $14 print

A handmade print listed at $14 with $3 shipping charged. The label costs $5.50 and materials cost $7. Listing fee $0.20, transaction 6.5%, payments 3% + $0.25. Cash invested is $12.50. Revenue is $17.

PathOffsite feeNet profitROI on cash
Organic (no offsite ads)$0.00$2.4419.5%
12% Offsite Ads (over $10k shop)$2.04$0.403.2%
15% Offsite Ads (under $10k shop)$2.55−$0.12−0.9%

Organically the sale is worth taking. At 12% it is barely worth the packing time. At 15% you pay Etsy to make and ship the print. The order stays profitable only up to about 14.3% Offsite Ads — under the 15% rate most newer shops are charged.

This comparison does not assume the sale would have happened without the ad. If Offsite Ads is the only reason the order exists, a $0.40 contribution can still be the right take — as long as you see the number before you ship.
