Does free shipping still leave what charging shipping leaves?

Percentage fees apply to item price plus shipping charged. Baking the label into the item is a tie if the buyer pays the same total. Free shipping only changes the money when you cut (or raise) that total — or when it actually brings more orders. Enter both and see the lift.
