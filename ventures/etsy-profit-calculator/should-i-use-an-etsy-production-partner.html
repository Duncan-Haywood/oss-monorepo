Should I use an Etsy production partner?

Sellers search for “should I use an Etsy production partner” because a $14.00 all-in quote looks like it kills margin. Handmade leftover is materials plus the time it actually takes you. The partner path is their unit cost plus handling — they buy the materials. Fees stay the same because the ticket does not change. The part most shops miss is capacity: leftover on the orders you cannot make is $0.00.

Open the Etsy profit calculator, keep your shop fees, and enter the quote and the hours you actually have. The table below uses the calculator defaults: $30 item, $5.00 shipping charged, 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 payment, $6.00 postage, $4.00 materials, $8.00 of your time, a $14.00 partner quote, $1.00 handling, 20 units you can make, and 40 you can sell.

The $14 quote loses per piece and still wins the month

Making it yourself keeps $13.23 per order. The $14.00 partner quote, after $1.00 handling, keeps $10.23 — $3.00 less per sale (−$3.00). You can only make 20 of the 40 you can sell, so staying handmade leaves $264.60 this month. Sending every order to the partner leaves $409.20. Making what you can and sending the overflow keeps $469.20 — $204.60 more than staying handmade (+$204.60). The partner quote has to fall to about $11.00 before it matches your handmade leftover. Raising the listing to about $33.32 keeps the handmade leftover on the partner path.

A $10 quote beats handmade on the unit and the month

Drop the partner quote to $10.00 and keep the $1.00 handling. That path keeps $14.23 — $1.00 more than handmade (+$1.00). Sending every order leaves $569.20 this month. Hybrid leftover is $549.20 because you would still be making the expensive units yourself. If the quote is cheaper than your materials plus time, send the whole book.

Rejected units still cost you

A 10% reject rate on the original $14.00 quote raises the effective cost per sale to $16.67 and drops leftover to $8.56. The break-even quote falls to about $9.80. When you can already fill the book

If you can sell only 15 and you can make 20, overflow is not a path. Handmade leftover this month is $198.45. The partner path is $153.45. Stay handmade until demand outruns your hours.
