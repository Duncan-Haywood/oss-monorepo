How it works, and what it is not

    
Do babysitters have to pay taxes?

    
Yes, in the US babysitting income is taxable, cash included. A babysitter who runs their own business reports it on Schedule C, and once net profit reaches $400 for the year also owes self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of profit, for Social Security and Medicare) on Schedule SE. This app adds up what you were paid, subtracts expenses and mileage, and suggests how much to set aside and when the quarterly estimated payments are due.

    
Am I self-employed or a household employee?

    
It depends on who controls the work. A sitter who sets their own rates and schedule and works for many families is usually self-employed. If one family controls how and when you work in their home, the IRS can treat you as that family's household employee (Publication 926), and once they pay you $3,000 or more in 2026 they owe household employment taxes and give you a W-2. The app flags any family that crosses that line so you can talk it through.

    
What do families need from me for the child care tax credit?

    
To claim the child and dependent care credit on Form 2441, or to be reimbursed from a dependent-care FSA, parents need your name, address and taxpayer ID, and the amount they paid. The app prints a year-end statement per family with every payment. A free EIN from the IRS lets you give a taxpayer ID without sharing your Social Security number.

    
How do I decide which babysitting inquiries to take?

    
Set your own limits once (how far you travel, how many children, the youngest age you take, your availability and rate) and the app scores each inquiry against them, says why, and drafts a reply: a warm yes with your rates for a good fit, or a kind no for one that does not fit. Recurring work and referrals score higher because they book more often.

    
How do I ask families for references and testimonials?

    
The References tab drafts the request, reminds you to follow up after five days, and keeps a separate yes or no for whether a quote may appear on your website. Only quotes marked yes are ever put on the site the app builds, shown with first name and last initial.

    
Is my families' information uploaded anywhere?

    
Not by this page. Families, kids, allergies and payments stay in this browser's storage and in the backup file you download. If you choose to run the optional server on your own hosting account, the app syncs to it with your own admin key, and your website's inquiry form posts to it.

    
When to use something else

    
Marketplaces such as Care.com and Sittercity bring you families you have not met; this app is for running the business once they find you, and for the families who come by word of mouth. If you take on a family as their regular nanny, use a payroll service for household employees. The tax figures are a set-aside, not a return: your bracket, deductions and credits are for you or a tax preparer to finish.
