# Farmers Market Booth Calculator

**Find which market, craft fair or festival is actually worth your day: profit per hour after booth fee, drive, product cost, waste and card fees, break-even sales, the sales needed to hit your target wage, and what a 20 percent worse day does.** Browser page, no install.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/farmers-market-booth-calculator/

After using this, you know which market to keep, reprice or drop.

## Your numbers, not advice

Every value is a number you type. Not accounting, tax or business advice.

## How it computes

Profit = sales - product cost - waste - card fees - percent-of-sales fee - booth fee - miles x cost per mile. Per hour = profit / hours. Break-even = (booth fee + drive) / (1 - product cost - waste - card cost - percent fee). Target sales adds target wage x hours to the fixed costs.

## What it is not

Not financial, tax, legal or business advice, and no warranty. It ignores sales tax, tent and equipment depreciation, and the value of repeat customers.

## Privacy

No network call, no account, no analytics vendor. Anonymous feature counts (`calculate_run`, `example_loaded`) live in local storage only and can be switched off on the page; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured.
