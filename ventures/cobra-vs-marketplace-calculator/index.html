Common questions

Is this insurance or legal advice?

No. It adds premiums and expected out-of-pocket cost for the numbers you type and computes deadlines from the dates you give. It cannot see your network, drugs, state law or plan documents. Confirm deadlines with your plan administrator or the marketplace.

Where do I get the marketplace premium?

From a healthcare.gov or state-exchange quote for the months you need. Enter the monthly premium after your premium tax credit; this tool deliberately holds no subsidy tables, so it cannot go out of date on that.

Why does COBRA sometimes win even though it costs more every month?

COBRA keeps the same plan, so the deductible and out-of-pocket maximum you already paid this plan year carry over. A marketplace plan starts both at zero. If you have already met much of the deductible and expect real care, that head start can outweigh a premium gap.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local arguments and makes no network call.
