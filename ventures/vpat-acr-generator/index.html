Common questions

Does this tool test my product for accessibility, or fill in the conformance levels for me?

No. You choose every conformance level and write every remark yourself, based on your own testing. This tool only formats your answers into the standard VPAT/ACR table -- it does not scan, crawl, or assess your product, and it never decides a conformance level on your behalf.

What is a VPAT and what is an ACR?

VPAT (Voluntary Product Accessibility Template) is a standardized template published by the Information Technology Industry Council (ITI) for documenting how a product conforms to accessibility standards such as WCAG. A completed VPAT is called an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR). U.S. federal agencies require one under Section 508, and many state, university, and enterprise procurement processes now ask for one too.

Which standard does this generator cover?

WCAG 2.1 Level A and Level AA -- the 50 success criteria most current ACRs are scored against. It does not cover WCAG Level AAA or the WCAG 2.2 additions.

Is my draft uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything you type stays in this browser tab. A draft auto-save to your browser's own localStorage is optional and can be turned off; nothing is ever sent to a server.
