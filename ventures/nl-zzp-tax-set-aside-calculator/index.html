Common questions

    
How much of my ZZP income should I set aside for tax in the Netherlands?

    
Often 20% to 30% of profit once you add income tax and the Zvw contribution, but it depends on your profit, the zelfstandigenaftrek and the heffingskortingen. Enter your turnover and expenses here for a number built from the 2026 rates.

    
What is the zelfstandigenaftrek in 2026?

    
A deduction from profit for entrepreneurs who meet the 1,225-hour test. It is €1,200 in 2026 (it was €2,470 in 2025) and is scheduled to fall further in 2027.

    
What is the MKB-winstvrijstelling?

    
An exemption of 12.7% of the profit left after the entrepreneur deductions, which is not taxed in Box 1.

    
What is the Zvw bijdrage for self-employed people?

    
An income-related contribution to the health insurance system, 4.85% of taxable profit up to a ceiling of about €79,412 in 2026, set by the Belastingdienst in your assessment.
