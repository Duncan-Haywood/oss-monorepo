Common questions

Does this read my cloud account?

No. It uses only the GPU, GPU-hours, utilisation and storage you type and rate cards you can edit. It cannot see your instances, quotas or invoice. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate on-demand list prices per GPU-hour as best known in 2025, shown with an as-of line, and every number is editable. GPU rental prices move quickly and vary by region and availability, so check each pricing page before deciding.

What is not included?

Spot or reserved discounts, committed-use contracts, egress, multi-GPU instance shapes that force you to rent more GPUs than you use, regions, support plans and queue or cold-start time. Interruptible capacity is modelled only as a percentage of re-run hours. Use this to compare the hourly lines, not to forecast the whole invoice.

When does buying a GPU beat renting one?

When your busy hours a month pass the breakeven shown: depreciation of the card and host over your horizon, divided by what each busy hour saves against the cheapest rental after electricity. Below it, renting is cheaper; above it, owning is. It ignores your time, noise, cooling, failures and resale uncertainty.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local files and makes no network call.
