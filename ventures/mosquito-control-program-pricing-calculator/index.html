Questions mosquito control operators ask

How much should I charge for mosquito control?

Work up from your cost per visit: product for the yard, the applicator's time on site and driving, truck cost, free re-treats, then overhead and card fees as a share of price. Divide the direct cost by one minus those percentages and your wanted margin. This tool shows that minimum price per visit and for a prepaid season.

Why does the prepay discount matter?

A discount comes straight off your margin because the costs do not fall. If you give 8% off to get paid up front, the list price has to be about 8% higher than the pay-per-visit minimum to keep the same margin. The tool shows both.

What are free re-treats costing me?

Every visit you redo after rain costs product and a trip. The tool adds your re-treat share to the direct cost of every visit, so a bad weather season shows up as a lower margin before you sign the program.

How many customers do I need to cover startup costs?

Divide the startup cost by the profit per season-program customer. The tool also shows how many customers one truck can serve from your stops per day, days a week and season length.

Is this legal or business advice?

No. It is arithmetic on figures you type. Pesticide applicator licences, product labels, local limits and insurance requirements vary; check yours and use a qualified professional.
