# UK 60% Tax Trap Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your income (and optionally a pension contribution) and see, for 2025/26 and 2026/27, the personal allowance you lose between £100,000 and £125,140, your real marginal rate (60% income tax, 62% with employee NI), and the pension payment that wins the allowance back and what it nets out to cost.

After using this, you know whether an extra £1,000 of pay is really worth £380 to you, and how much to pay into a pension to stop it, which used to mean stacking the taper, bands and NI by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-60-percent-tax-trap-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
