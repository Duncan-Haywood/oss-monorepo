This calculator vs. Airbnb's own "Estimate your earnings" tool

Airbnb publishes a free Estimate your earnings tool for people considering listing a property: type an address and a few details, and it predicts a nightly price and monthly income on Airbnb. It is forward-looking and single-channel — a forecast for a listing that may not exist yet, already netting out Airbnb's own fee, with no way to add a Vrbo or direct-booking leg and no field for a stay you have already booked or priced.

That makes Airbnb's tool the right choice for "what might this property earn on Airbnb" before you list. This calculator answers a different question: for a stay you already have real numbers for, which channel actually pays the most — Airbnb, Vrbo, or direct — and what it would take to do better.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Airbnb's Estimate Your Earnings tool?

Both, for different questions. Airbnb's own estimator, free on airbnb.com, predicts a nightly price and monthly income for a property you have not listed yet, on Airbnb only, already netting out their fee. This calculator instead takes a stay you already have numbers for — nightly rate, nights, cleaning fee, occupancy tax, deposit — and runs it through Airbnb's 15.5% host-only fee, the retired 3% split, Vrbo's pay-per-booking and legacy annual plans, and direct booking, side by side, plus the listed-price raise needed to restore an old 3% payout and the occupancy you can afford to lose going direct. Airbnb's tool cannot do any of that: it has no Vrbo or direct-booking leg and no way to enter an actual booked stay.

Does Airbnb's estimator account for Vrbo or direct booking?

No. It is single-channel by design — it forecasts what Airbnb itself would pay for a listing on Airbnb. It has no field for a Vrbo commission, Vrbo's annual subscription, or a direct-booking site's processing fee, so it cannot answer which channel actually pays best on a given stay.
