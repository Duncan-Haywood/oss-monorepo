        Common questions

        What does this gate estimate include?

        The price adds up operator kits by leaf count, the gate leaves themselves by opening width and material, new columns or posts when you are not reusing existing hardware, AC trenching or a solar kit, vehicle loops, a keypad, battery backup, extra remotes, edge sensors, a permit line, travel and equipment, overhead, and your target margin — all from the opening, style, and rates you enter, not a flat “automatic gate” price.

        Why does a dual swing cost more than a single swing on the same opening?

        A dual-swing gate is priced as two operators and typically three new columns against a single swing’s one operator and two columns, and its own starting hours and material rates run a little higher besides. The three-way comparison prices your same opening under single-swing, dual-swing, and slide starting kits so you can see which style actually wins once columns and operators are counted.

        Does the gate material change more than just the leaf price?

        Yes. Steel and wood carry a weight factor above aluminum, which raises both the leaf material cost and the hang hours for the same opening width, while vinyl and chain-link carry a weight factor below aluminum and lower both. Everything else — columns, power, loops, and access — prices the same regardless of leaf material.

        Is my data uploaded anywhere?

        No account and no network requests — the calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your bid is saved in this browser’s local storage between visits, and a bid link you copy carries those same numbers in the page’s URL hash rather than sending anything to a server.

        What’s the difference between the bid price and the walk-away price?

        The bid price already includes your target margin on top of labor, materials, and overhead. The walk-away price is the total cost underneath it — operators, leaves, columns, power, access hardware, and labor — the point below which the job stops being profitable at all.

        Does this check UL 325 safety requirements or electrical code?

        No. This is not a gate specification, a UL 325 determination, a permit, an electrical design, or an engineering stamp. Loops, keypads, battery backup, and edge sensors are line items you choose to include; whether a specific installation meets the safety standard is a judgment call for whoever pulls the permit, not something this arithmetic decides.
