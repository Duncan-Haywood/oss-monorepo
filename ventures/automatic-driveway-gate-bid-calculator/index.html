FOR GATE CONTRACTORS

Bid the style, the operators, and the extra work.

Price an automatic driveway gate the way the trade actually loses money: single swing versus dual swing versus slide, operators from leaf count, masonry columns, vehicle loops, and solar versus an AC trench. A 12-foot single aluminum swing on existing posts is not the same bid as a 16-foot dual steel swing that needs columns. You get the price, the operators, the extra-work share, and the walk-away number below which the job loses money.
