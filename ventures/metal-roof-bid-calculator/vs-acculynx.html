This calculator vs. AccuLynx

Two different shapes: AccuLynx is an account-based platform for running a roofing company's whole sales-to-production pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one metal-roof job cost, given its panel type, tear-off, stories, and trim, before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

AccuLynx is a cloud-based business management platform built specifically for roofing contractors, headquartered in Beloit, Wisconsin. It is built around running a roofing company's whole pipeline in one account: lead capture and CRM, aerial roof measurement, estimating and proposal generation, material ordering with supplier integrations, production and crew scheduling, and job costing, used by a sales team, a production manager, and office staff together across every job the company runs, on an ongoing subscription. It is a large enough business in that niche that in July 2025 Verisk Analytics — the NYSE-listed insurance-data and analytics company — signed a definitive agreement to acquire it in an all-cash deal valued at $2.35 billion, a deal a Delaware Chancery Court later ordered Verisk to complete after a dispute over its regulatory review. AccuLynx's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity, category, and the acquisition — reported by Verisk's own newsroom and multiple trade and financial press outlets — are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no subscription price for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not track a lead, order material, schedule a crew, or hold a record across jobs. It takes one metal-roof job — panel type (exposed-fastener 5V/R-panel/corrugated, snap-lock standing seam, or mechanical-lock standing seam), the building's length, width, and pitch converted to pitch-adjusted panel squares, waste, whether it is an overlay or a full tear-off, stories, ridge, hip, valley, rake, and eave trim by the lineal foot, snow guards, pipe boots, ice-and-water shield, loaded labor, mobilization, dumpster, permit, overhead, and the margin you intend to keep — and turns that into the job price, the price per square, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, plus a printable customer proposal with a deposit split and a price-held date. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or AccuLynx?

They do different jobs. AccuLynx, headquartered in Beloit, Wisconsin, is a cloud-based, roofing-only business management platform used by residential and commercial roofing contractors to run a company's whole sales-to-production pipeline: lead and CRM tracking, aerial measurement and estimating, proposal generation, material ordering, production and crew scheduling, and job costing, tied to one account used on an ongoing subscription basis across every job the company runs. It is a large enough business in that niche that in July 2025 Verisk Analytics, the NYSE-listed insurance-data company, signed a definitive agreement to acquire it in an all-cash deal valued at $2.35 billion, a deal a Delaware Chancery Court later ordered Verisk to complete after a dispute over its regulatory review. This calculator does none of what AccuLynx does. It takes one metal-roof job's panel type (exposed-fastener, snap-lock, or mechanical-lock standing seam), pitch-adjusted panel squares, waste, overlay or tear-off, stories, ridge/hip/valley/rake/eave trim, snow guards, pipe boots, ice-and-water, loaded labor, mobilization, dump, permit, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the job price, the price per square, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, plus a printable customer proposal, instantly, with no account and nothing uploaded. A roofing company running leads, crews, and material orders across many jobs at once is better served by AccuLynx or a comparable platform; a metal-roofing crew that wants an instant, private price for one job — panel type, tear-off, and trim included — before a bid goes out is in the right place here.
