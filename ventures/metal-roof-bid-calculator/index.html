FOR STANDING-SEAM AND EXPOSED-FASTENER CREWS

Bid the panels, the tear-off, and the trim.

Price a residential metal roof the way the trade actually loses money: panel squares a per-square special cannot see, then the tear-off, the stories, the trim, ice-and-water, extras, permit, and the margin you intend to keep. You get the job price, what it works out to per square, and the number below which the job loses money.

Looking up how much to charge for a metal roof? Start here, then replace every starting figure with your own crew times.
