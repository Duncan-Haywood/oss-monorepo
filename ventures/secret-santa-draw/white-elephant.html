      How it works

      A turn. The player whose turn it is opens a wrapped gift or steals an opened one. Whoever was stolen from moves next with the same choice. The turn ends when someone opens a gift, and the next number in the order goes.

      Rules it enforces. A gift is frozen once it has been stolen the number of times you set. With no stealing straight back, a player cannot take back the gift that was just taken from them in the same turn. With a per-turn limit, once that many steals have happened someone must open.       Saved on this device. The game is kept in this browser's storage after every move, so a refresh or a dead battery does not lose it. Undo steps back one move at a time.

      Checked: the test suite plays 3,000 random games with random house rules and checks after every move that nobody holds two gifts, everyone who has had a turn holds one, no gift is stolen past its limit, every refused steal is refused with a reason, and no game runs longer than the rules allow.

      Common questions

      How do steals work in white elephant?

      On your turn you either open a wrapped gift or steal an opened one. If you steal, the person you took it from gets the same choice right away: open a new gift or steal from someone else. The turn ends when somebody opens a new gift.

      What does it mean when a gift is frozen?

      A frozen gift has been stolen the most times your group allows, usually three, and stays with whoever holds it. The host marks it frozen the moment the last allowed steal happens.

      Can you steal a gift straight back?

      Most groups say no: if someone takes your gift, you cannot take that same gift back in the same turn. You can steal it back on a later turn if it is not frozen. The host enforces this rule unless you switch it off.

      Does the first player get a final turn?

      In many groups, yes. Player one never had a gift to steal from, so once every gift is open they may swap once with any gift that is not frozen, or keep theirs. Switch the final swap off if your group does not play it.
