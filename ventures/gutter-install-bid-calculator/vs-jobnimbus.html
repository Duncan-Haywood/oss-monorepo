This calculator vs. JobNimbus

Two different shapes: JobNimbus is an account-based CRM and job management platform for running an exterior contractor's whole lead-to-job pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one gutter job cost, given its footage, profile, corners, and drops, before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

JobNimbus is a CRM and job management platform, founded in 2013 by Ben Hodson, Nick Wood, and Jason Wood, headquartered in Lehi, Utah. It is built for roofing, siding, gutter, solar, and other exterior contractors, covering lead and pipeline tracking, estimates and proposals, scheduling, job costing, and invoicing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. JobNimbus's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a CRM and job management platform serving exterior trades including gutter contractors, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for JobNimbus appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what JobNimbus does. It does not track a lead, schedule a crew, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one gutter job's footage, profile (5-inch, 6-inch, or copper), stories, inside and outside miters, downspouts and downspout footage past the special's included allowance, fascia repair, tear-off, steep access, and hidden hangers, plus loaded installer wage, payroll burden, travel, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per foot of gutter, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
