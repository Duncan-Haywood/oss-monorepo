    What it does and does not do

    • Maths in whole seconds and cents. Each entry is rounded up to your increment, converted to two-decimal hours and multiplied by its rate. The same hours go on the invoice, so the tracker and the invoice always agree.
    • Unbilled means not yet on an invoice. Making an invoice marks those entries as invoiced; if you throw the invoice away, press Un-invoice on the entry to bill it again.
    • Utilisation. Billable share is billable time divided by all tracked time; effective rate is the amount divided by billable hours.
    • Not modelled: multiple users, fixed-price or retainer billing, expenses, overtime multipliers, taxes beyond a single rate, and any rule about what you may bill for.

    Common questions

    Is this time tracker really free?

    Yes. There is no account, no trial, no project limit and no watermark. It runs in your browser, so it costs nothing to serve, and it stays free.

    Does the timer keep running if I close the tab?

    Yes. The start time is saved on your device, so reopening the page shows the real elapsed time, not a paused one. If you forget it and it runs overnight, the page warns you before saving a 24-hour-plus entry.

    How does rounding work?

    Each entry is rounded up to the increment you choose (none, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes) before it is priced. Six minutes gives tenths of an hour, fifteen gives quarters. The hours on screen are the hours on the invoice, so the totals match to the cent.

    How do I turn tracked time into an invoice?

    Press Invoice unbilled time next to a client. A draft invoice is added to Invoice Maker on this device (if your browser blocks that, you get a file to restore instead), with one line per entry, per day or per description, at each entry's rate. The entries are then marked as invoiced so they are not billed twice.

    Can I set a different rate for one job?

    Yes. Give a client a default hourly rate and override it on any single entry. Lines at different rates are never merged on the invoice.

    Where is my data stored?

    In your browser on this device only. Clearing site data removes it, so download a backup now and then; the file restores on any device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Is this tax, legal or accounting advice?

    No. It adds up the hours and rates you enter. Whether time is billable, what you may charge, which tax applies and what records you must keep depend on your contract and location; check with a professional.
