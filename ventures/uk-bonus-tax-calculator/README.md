# UK Bonus Tax Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-bonus-tax-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter a salary and a gross bonus and see what you keep after income tax, National Insurance, student loan and the High Income Child Benefit Charge (2026/27 rates), the effective rate on your last pound including the 60% personal allowance trap, and what 0 to 100% of the bonus paid into a pension by salary sacrifice would change.

After using this, you know how much of your bonus is yours and whether putting part of it into your pension keeps more of it.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-bonus-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
