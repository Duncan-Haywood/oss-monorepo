Questions

How do I reply to a negative Google review?

Thank the reviewer, apologise for the experience without arguing or explaining it away, name one specific thing they raised, and move the conversation offline with a phone number or email. Keep it short, use their first name only, and do not offer a refund or ask them to change the review. Paste the review into Review Reply Desk and it writes a reply that follows those rules and checks the result.

Should I reply to every review, including five-star ones?

Yes, if you can keep it up. Replies to positive reviews are short thank-yous that name something specific, and a steady habit of replying is visible to people deciding whether to contact you. Paste a whole batch and the page drafts every reply, with the complaints and anything risky sorted to the top.

Is it legal to reply to a patient or client review?

Not in the way most owners expect. A health practice that confirms in public that a reviewer was a patient can disclose protected health information, even when the reviewer shared details themselves. When you choose Dental, medical or therapy practice, Review Reply Desk writes general-terms replies that confirm nothing, and its checker rejects any reply that refers to the reviewer's visit or care. This is not legal advice; ask your own adviser about your rules.

What do I do about a review that mentions a lawyer, an injury or a theft?

Do not argue and do not describe what happened. Reply with two short sentences that take it offline, investigate privately, and tell whoever advises your business before saying more. The page flags these reviews and writes a short offline reply that says nothing about the claim.

Can I ask the reviewer to change or remove their review?

Not in exchange for a refund, a discount or any other benefit, which platforms prohibit, and asking at all can read as pressure. Resolve the problem privately, then let the reviewer decide. The checker flags any reply that asks for a changed review.

What if the review looks fake?

Look the reviewer up in your own records first. If there is no match, report the review to the platform as not reflecting a real experience, and do not accuse the reviewer in public. The page flags reviews that say things like I have never been there so you can check before you reply.

Does it use AI, and is my data sent anywhere?

Only if you choose to. The reply writer works with no key, using rules and templates in your browser. If you add your own API key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, the single review you click Polish on is sent from your browser straight to that provider, never to us, and the model's reply is run through the same checks and replaced by the template if it fails them.

Can I offer a discount or gift for leaving a review?

No. Google's policy prohibits offering incentives for reviews, and the FTC's rule on fake and bought reviews also targets reviews procured for something of value. Do not ask only your happy customers either, which is called review gating and is also against Google's policy. Ask everyone, the same way, for honest feedback. The review request writer on this page checks every message for rewards, gating, requests for a particular rating and missing opt-out lines. This is not legal advice.
