This calculator vs. ServiceTitan

Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is an account-based field service management platform for running an electrical company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one panel replacement or service upgrade cost, given its feeder, circuit landing, and path, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan, headquartered in Glendale, California. It serves HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other trades with scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, reporting, and job costing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. ServiceTitan's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software serving the electrical trade among others, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceTitan appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not schedule technicians, dispatch crews, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one job's service path (overhead mast or underground lateral), panel style, and panel location, with feeder feet, circuits to land, AFCI/GFCI breakers, meter socket replacement, new grounding, and temporary power as their own line items, plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, permit fee, utility disconnect/reconnect fee, travel and equipment, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the bid price, the price per amp of new service, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
