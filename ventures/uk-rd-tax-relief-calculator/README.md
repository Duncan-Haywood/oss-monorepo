# UK R&D Tax Relief Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your qualifying R&D costs (staff, subcontractors, externally provided workers, consumables, software, cloud) and your profit or loss to see the merged R&D expenditure credit, the enhanced support for R&D-intensive loss-making companies, the cash or tax saved, and what an adviser's percentage fee would cost you.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-rd-tax-relief-calculator/

After using this, you know roughly what an R&D claim is worth before you hand 15-20% of it to a claims agency.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-rd-tax-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rates are from April 2024 and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
