# Submit requirements outside Part 1, read on UFGS (2026-11-24)

What shipped: `buriedRequirements` in `engine.mjs` lists every sentence outside
a section's Part 1 submittal articles that obliges someone to submit something
(Part 2, Part 3, and Part 1 articles such as QUALITY ASSURANCE), with its type,
any deadline it states (`When`), and the Part 1 row it seems to restate
(`Covered By`). CLI `--buried-csv` / `--with-buried`; a panel on the page.

## Corpus and command

690 UFGS sections from github.com/mttvnst-HA/secwriter `reference/UFGS_M`
(sparse checkout, as in `ufgs-2026-11-19.md`), rendered and joined into one
book by the same code as `ufgs-eval.mjs`.

```
$ node eval/buried-ufgs.mjs <UFGS_M>
690 sections, 12870 Part 1 rows
outside Part 1 lists: 5072 sentences in 622 sections; by part {"1":1641,"2":1437,"3":1994}
matched to a Part 1 row: 3194; unmatched: 1878; with a When: 725
```

The Part 1 register is unchanged by this work: `ufgs-eval.mjs` still gives
recall 99.3% (12,858/12,952), precision 99.9%, SD right 99.7%, G right 99.9%.

## No tagged truth, so a hand read

UFGS tags Part 1 submittal items but not the "submit" sentences in Parts 2
and 3, so there is no automatic truth here. One reader (the session that built
it) read seeded random samples of 40 found sentences (`--sample 40 --seed N`)
and judged two things per row: is it a real obligation to submit something,
and is the `Covered By` row right. For uncovered rows the sample prints the
section's Part 1 labels (cut at 600 characters), so "absent" could be judged
only when the list was visible in full.

- **Seed 11 (development).** Used to tune the matcher: the article title as
  context ("3.6 LOAD TESTS ... Submit the test report"), the object of the verb
  instead of the whole sentence (the first version matched "Detail Drawings" to
  a spare-parts sentence that only mentioned them later), generic words
  ("test", "plan", "list") never matching on their own, run-in sub-article
  numbers ending a sentence. Not scored.
- **Seed 23 (held out, first read)** — `buried-read-seed23.txt`, output of
  the code at that moment:
  - real submit obligations: 39 of 40 (the miss: "Submit matters of
    interpretation ... for resolution", an RFI, not a submittal);
  - `Covered By` right: 24 of 27 (wrong: "Installation" for installation
    instructions, "Grout Mix Design" for a work-plan item, "Non-Destructive
    Field Seam" for destructive seam tests);
  - uncovered 12: 2 clear misses (Roof Plan and Flashing Details behind a
    run-in heading; a colour scheme inside the shop drawing package), 6
    clearly absent from Part 1, 4 not judgeable from the truncated list.
  - After this read: a short run-in heading before the verb now counts
    toward the subject (fixes the roof plan miss), and an exact label phrase
    in the object wins. Re-run on seed 23, the destructive seam and the
    maintenance log rows also moved to the right Part 1 row.
- **Seed 31 (held out, second read)** — `buried-read-seed31.txt`:
  - real submit obligations: 40 of 40;
  - `Covered By` right: 19 of 22 (wrong: "Windows" for Energy Star
    documentation, "Factory Test Report" for proposed test procedures,
    "Formwork" for release-agent product data);
  - uncovered 18: 4 clear misses ("Fireproofing Material; G, NVFAC", where the
    approval tail diluted the label; "Lubrication" for lubricant data; a
    welder's employment records under "Welder ... Qualification"; "Cut CMU
    Drawings" with the subject 15 words after the verb), 6 clearly absent, 8 not
    judgeable.
  - After this read: the `; G, ...` approval tail is dropped from labels before
    matching (fixes the fireproofing row). The other three stand.

Totals over the two held-out reads, as first read: **79 of 80** real
obligations; **43 of 49** `Covered By` suggestions right; of 30 uncovered rows,
**6** were in Part 1 after all, **12** clearly were not, and **12** could not be
judged from the printed list.

## What this means for a user

- The list is trustworthy as "things the spec says to submit"; nearly
  every row is one.
- `Covered By` is a word match and is wrong about one time in eight; an
  uncovered row is a candidate missed submittal, and about one in five of the
  judgeable ones was in Part 1 under other words. So the page says to read
  each one, and only the uncovered rows are added with `--with-buried`
  (flag `outside-part-1`).
- Single reader, UFGS only. A SectionFormat book writes Part 3 more tersely;
  the sample book's Part 3 is in the unit tests, but no commercial project
  manual has been read yet (README, "Not yet measured").
