Should I take a Saturday boiler call?

Owners call with a weekday number: “Can you come Saturday? The house is cold. Same $4,500?” That sounds like the same plant. It is not the same crew day. After-hours is time-and-a-half. A Friday-night no-heat call is not a stop you combine with a weekday route, so it is a second dispatch. Working in a house they stay in because the heat is out is the same wage on a slower hang.

Saturday is not the weekday bid

A weekday slot still carries the wage you already timed. After-hours raises that wage to time-and-a-half and adds a starting second trip. On a typical 80 MBH gas hot-water like-for-like — $48 wage, 32% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, 10 miles — the weekday bid is $6,740.98. After-hours is $7,634.02. The walk-away jumps from $5,392.78 to $6,107.22. Taking $5,800 to “just do Saturday at a weekday-looking number” covers the weekday slot and loses $307.22 after hours. The Facebook $4,500 special loses even on Monday.

A steam conversion loses even on the weekday

The Saturday special is more tempting when the house is an oil steam plant and they want gas hot water tonight. The weekday bid is $19,253.10. Same-day with no heat is $19,566.42. After-hours is $21,998.71 and the walk-away is $17,598.97. Taking $4,500 to “just swap it tonight” still loses money — even on the weekday.

This is not a patch visit or a customer-supplied crate

A $280 circulator patch is a service call, not a changeout — see should I patch this boiler for one more winter. Customer-supplied plants are their crate and a hang-only number — see should I install a customer-supplied boiler. After-hours is when you can stand in the basement. A toilet Saturday is an overflowing commode. A kitchen backsplash Saturday is tile overtime in a kitchen they still cook in. A walk-in shower Saturday is a wet-area crew. How to use the comparison

Open the boiler replacement bid calculator, pick the cheap-special or losing-job preset, and put their Saturday number in “price you’re considering.” The access table scores that number against a weekday slot, same-day with no heat, and after-hours / Saturday. Nothing is uploaded.

This is the same arithmetic as how much to charge to replace a boiler. It is not a labor-law determination, a union scale, or a reason to refuse a customer — it is the walk-away number on each access window.

Common questions

Should I take a Saturday or after-hours boiler replacement call?

Only after you price time-and-a-half plus a second emergency dispatch. A Friday-night no-heat call is not a stop you combine with a weekday route. A $4,500 Saturday special on an 80 MBH gas like-for-like sits under that walk-away number even on a weekday slot.

Does a same-day no-heat call change the bid?

Yes. Same wage, slower plant hang and punch-list. On a typical 80 MBH two-zone gas hot-water swap the same-day bid is $6,899.46 versus $6,740.98 when they can wait for a weekday slot.

Can I take the weekday $5,800 number on Saturday?

Usually not on the job they actually want. On a cheap gas like-for-like the after-hours walk-away is $6,107.22. $5,800 covers a weekday slot and loses $307.22 after hours. On a steam-to-hot-water conversion, $4,500 still loses money even on the weekday.
