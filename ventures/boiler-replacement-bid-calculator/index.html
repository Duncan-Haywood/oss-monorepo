Bid the plant, the system, and the extras.

Price a boiler replacement the way the trade actually loses money: an 80 MBH gas hot-water like-for-like swap versus a steam-to-hot-water conversion with four zones, a liner, and an indirect — not the published $4,500 special. You get the job price, what it works out to per input MBH, and the number below which the changeout loses money.

The plant
