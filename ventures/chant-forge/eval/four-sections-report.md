Percentages are over the notes of the files that fit with that choir. "Full choir" is every section singing; "three or more" and "two or more" count the notes sung by at least that many sections; "one alone" the notes a single section carries. "Sections per note" is the mean, over fitting files, of the number of sections singing a note, and "share of the choir" that number over the sections in the choir (with four sections the full choir is harder to reach, so the share compares the choirs more fairly than the full-choir column). Ranges are assumptions, not measurements.
