# Loudness range (EBU Tech 3342) in master.mjs (2026-11-14)

`loudnessRange(channels, fs)` in `master.mjs`; `master-cli.mjs` prints it and
`analyze()` returns it as `loudnessRange` (LU). Source: EBU Tech 3342, November 2023
(V4), read from tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3342.pdf on 2026-11-14 (8 pages, sha256
a12cb59f...89d8); section 3.1 and the MATLAB listing in section 5.

What the code does, and where the text says so:

| Step | Tech 3342 | master.mjs |
|---|---|---|
| Input | ungated BS.1770 loudness, 3 s sliding window, "at least 10 Hz" (>= 2.9 s overlap) | 3 s windows every 0.1 s, first one at 0 s, last one the last full window |
| Absolute gate | -70 LUFS (`>=`) | same |
| Relative gate | -20 LU under the power mean of the abs-gated levels (`>=`) | same; `relativeGateLu` option only for the control below |
| Percentiles | `sorted(round((n-1)*p/100 + 1))`, p = 10 and 95 | same rank rule, 1-based |
| Trailing silence | "followed by at least 1.5 s of silence" before the final value (a live meter's latency) | off by default; `tailSeconds: 1.5` adds it; both run below |

## Table 1 cases 1-4

`node eval/ebu-tech3342.mjs` (also `ebu-tech3342.test.mjs`). Stereo 1 kHz sines, in
phase, 20 s per level, per-channel peak, 48 kHz, 64-bit floats. Each case is also run
twice and three times over (the table: "the expected response is unchanged if the
test signal is repeated one or more times") and with 1.5 s of trailing silence.

| Case | Levels (dBFS) | LRA read | Wanted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | -20, -30 | 10.000 | 10 +/-1 |
| 2 | -20, -15 | 5.000 | 5 +/-1 |
| 3 | -40, -20 | 20.000 | 20 +/-1 |
| 4 | -50, -35, -20, -35, -50 | 15.000 | 15 +/-1 |

24 of 24 readings (4 cases x 1/2/3 repeats x 0/1.5 s tail) read the same to 0.001 LU.

**Control:** with BS.1770's integrated-loudness gate (-10 LU) instead of -20, case 3
drops its -40 dBFS tone and reads 1.33 LU, so the cases do test the gate. Case 4 does
not tell the two gates apart (its abs-gated mean is about -26.7 LUFS, so -35 passes
either), and cases 1-2 cannot: a pass on those alone would not show the gate is right.

Not run: cases 5 and 6, authentic programme only in the EBU's WAV set (403 from this
session for Tech 3341's set).

## Against ffmpeg's ebur128 (independent implementation)

`node eval/ffmpeg-lra-check.mjs` (ffmpeg on PATH; skips otherwise). Stereo, 48 kHz,
float WAV.

| Signal | s | windows kept | ffmpeg LRA | ours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3342 case 1-4 tones (ffmpeg `sine`, lifted from its -18.06 dBFS) | 40-100 | 371-627 | 10.0, 5.0, 20.0, 15.0 | 10.00, 5.00, 20.00, 15.00 |
| pink noise, steady -23 dB | 30 | 271 | 0.1 | 0.07 |
| pink noise, 6 dB steps every 5 s | 30 | 241 | 24.0 | 24.00 |
| pink noise, 20 s fade-out | 40 | 371 | 11.7 | 11.57 |
| pink noise, loud 10 s then a -60 dB bed | 40 | 100 | 4.8 | 4.35 |
| render: Dies irae, metal, half-time | 380.9 | 3779 | 0.8 | 0.77 |
| render: Dies irae, deep house, `--sections` | 191.2 | 1882 | 2.5 | 2.45 |
| render: Pange lingua (full), EDM, `--sections` | 106 | 1031 | 5.0 | 5.01 |
| render: Te Deum, no backing | 206.7 | 2037 | 3.9 | 3.93 |

11 of 12 within 0.2 LU (ffmpeg prints one decimal). The 12th, the loud-then-bed
signal, is 0.45 LU apart, and it is a rank rule, not a level: only 100 windows pass
the gates, 29 of them on the 3 s slope out of the loud part, where neighbouring
windows are 0.3-3 LU apart. The listing's 10th-percentile rank is the 11th lowest
(-30.94 LUFS); the 10th lowest (-31.35) gives 4.76, which ffmpeg prints as 4.8. The
script accepts a gap over 0.2 only when ffmpeg's value sits inside the LRA read one
rank either side of both percentiles (here 3.98-4.77); master.mjs keeps the
listing's rule, since the text says other implementations "might yield slightly
different LRA measurements".

## What the renders say

The preview renders read 0.8-5.0 LU: a steady metal backing barely moves; `--sections`
(intro, verse, build, drop, outro energy changes) is what widens it, 2.5 LU on Dies
irae in deep house and 5.0 on Pange lingua in EDM. This is a reading of the preview
synthesiser, not of a real mix, and no streaming service publishes an LRA limit, so
the CLI reports it and does not pass or fail it.
