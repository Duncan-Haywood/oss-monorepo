# Choir presets: the fewest sections each choir needs

Generated by `node eval/choir-presets.mjs` on 67 corpus files. For each `--choir` preset the tool tries unison in the band every section reaches, then tenors and basses, then tenors, baritones and basses, then TTBB (`fewestSections` in voicefit.mjs; splits at a phrase, at a word only where no phrase split fits). The ranges are rules of thumb, not measurements of a choir. For comparison, the default unison range E2-E4 (the textbook bass range, which tenors cannot all reach) fits 67 of 67.
