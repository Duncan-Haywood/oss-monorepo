# Private Tutor Effective Hourly Rate Calculator

Free, private browser tool for private tutors: sessions a week, rate, prep, travel, cancellations, platform fees and admin time become your real hourly pay, yearly net, the session rate that breaks even or hits your hourly target, and a table by sessions per week.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/tutor-effective-hourly-rate-calculator/

After using this, you know what tutoring really pays per hour once the unbilled time is counted, which used to be a guess from the rate you quote, and what to charge to reach the pay you want.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/tutor-effective-hourly-rate-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
