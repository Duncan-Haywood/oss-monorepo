This calculator vs. ServiceTitan

Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is an account-based field service management platform for running a plumbing company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one water-heater replacement cost, given its type, location, vent, and gas or electrical path, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan, headquartered in Glendale, California. It is used by plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and other trades for scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, reporting, and job costing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. ServiceTitan's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software serving the plumbing trade among others, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceTitan appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not schedule technicians, dispatch crews, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one water-heater replacement's heater type (electric tank, atmospheric or power-vent gas tank, condensing gas tankless, electric tankless, or heat pump), tank gallons or tankless GPM, location, venting and vent length, gas line and gas-line length, electrical path, and add-ons — expansion tank, drip pan, condensate, recirculation pump, haul-away, seismic straps, a mixing valve, and tankless service valves — plus loaded installer wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the installed price, the price per gallon or per GPM, the effective hourly rate, and a comparison against a typical 50-gallon electric garage swap at the same rates — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears the walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
