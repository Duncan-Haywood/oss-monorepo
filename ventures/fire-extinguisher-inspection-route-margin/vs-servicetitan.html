This calculator vs. ServiceTitan

Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is account-based field-service software for running a route business across scheduling, dispatch, and every technician. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — is this site actually profitable once recharge, hydro-test, and replacement materials come out.

What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan and headquartered in Glendale, California, used by HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other trades to run scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, and reporting across a business, sold as an ongoing subscription account. ServiceTitan's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field-service management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceTitan appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not manage a customer list, schedule a technician's route, dispatch a crew, or collect a payment, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes a site's extinguisher count, its certified visits per year, the inspection and tag fees and the tag's own cost, the percentage of units needing a recharge, a hydrostatic test, or a replacement on this visit along with each one's fee and materials cost, on-site inspection minutes, round-trip drive time and mileage, your target hourly rate, and a monthly recordkeeping cost, and turns that into real monthly profit for that one site — plus, once you have several sites priced, a roll-up of the whole route's monthly profit against an income goal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
