Common questions

Why does the inspection fee alone not tell me whether a site is worth keeping?

Because the inspection fee only covers the guaranteed part of the visit. Whether the visit is actually profitable depends on how many units need a recharge, are due for hydrostatic testing, or fail and get replaced — each of which carries its own materials cost against its own fee — plus the drive time and mileage to reach the site at all.

Does this tool tell me whether an extinguisher or a site passes inspection?

No. This calculator only prices your own labor, materials, and route economics from numbers you supply. It does not perform, certify, or replace a professional fire-extinguisher inspection, and it does not determine or record NFPA 10 (or any other) compliance status.

Why is this priced per year instead of per month like some of your other route calculators?

Because certified fire-extinguisher maintenance is almost always an annual visit under NFPA 10, not a monthly one — the customer's own staff does a monthly visual check. This calculator prices one certified visit, then spreads that visit's profit over the year to get a monthly figure you can compare against sites serviced more than once a year.

Does anything about my sites or pricing get uploaded?

No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and the site list you type into the route section is never saved anywhere — it exists only in the page while it is open. The visit inputs above and the target monthly income setting are different: those are saved locally on this device, so you don't retype them, and a shareable link can carry them to another device. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
