What this does, and what it does not

The headline commission is not the decision

A 27% DoorDash plan on a $32 ticket is $8.64. Packaging and eight extra bagging minutes at $18/hour add another $3.65 the percentage comparison never shows. The decision number is the listed price that leaves you the same cash as dine-in — often around 45% higher on that ticket — and the monthly gap if you do not raise it. DoorDash fees on one restaurant ticket works the default example through in full.

What it deliberately leaves out

Fees are not the whole decision. An app fee is partly a payment for demand you would otherwise have to create, and this page has no way to know how many of those tickets you would have sold as dine-in or pickup. It also does not model refunds, marketing credits, DashPass discounts you absorb, or occupancy you lose when the kitchen is full of delivery tickets. Read the gap as one input, not the decision.
