Missed a call? Text them back before they call someone else.

Trades miss calls because their hands are busy. Many callers do not leave a voicemail; they ring the next name on the list. This tool works out what that costs you from your own numbers, checks the automatic text for the rules that matter, and gives you an open-source agent that sends it from your own Twilio number. See also the guide for contractors.

1. What do missed calls cost you?

Type your own numbers. Nothing is assumed: we do not know your reply rate, and an industry average would be a guess. Track a month of calls first if you can.

Fill the boxes, then click Work it out.

2. Preview and check the text

Add your business name, then click Check the message.

    3. Run it on your own Twilio number

    1. Get a Twilio number and register it for business texting (US: 10DLC or toll-free verification; this takes days and is the slow part).
    2. Deploy server.mjs from the source anywhere Node 20 runs. Set BUSINESS_NAME, TIMEZONE, TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID, TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN, TWILIO_FROM, PUBLIC_URL.
    3. Point the number's call status callback at /voice and incoming messages at /sms. Without keys it runs as a dry run and only logs what it would send.

    Rules built in: one text per number per 24 hours; nothing from 21:00 to 08:00 local (night calls wait); STOP, START and HELP answered; replies other than keywords are forwarded to your phone; a daily cap; every webhook checked against Twilio's signature. Tested with a fake Twilio only: no real call has been through it yet.

    Questions

    What is missed-call text-back?

    When a call to your business number goes unanswered, your phone system sends the caller one text within seconds, such as "Sorry we missed your call, how can we help?" The caller can reply, and you answer from your own phone when you are free. It is aimed at plumbers, electricians, HVAC, roofers, cleaners and other trades whose phone rings while their hands are busy.

    Does it cost per month?

    The code is free and MIT licensed. You run it on your own server with your own Twilio account, so you pay Twilio's per-text fee (see smsSegments in the calculator for how many segments a message uses) and a small server, or nothing if you run it on a free tier. There is no per-seat price from us.

    Is automatic texting legal?

    It depends on where you and the caller are. In the US, carriers require business texting numbers to be registered (10DLC or toll-free verification), every text must say who you are and how to stop, and STOP must be honoured at once. This page lists the common requirements. It is not legal advice; ask a lawyer before you turn it on.

    Does it ever text someone twice or at night?

    No by default: one text per number per 24 hours, nothing outside 08:00 to 21:00 in your time zone (night calls wait until morning), STOP and START are handled, and there is a daily cap that stops a runaway loop.
