# UK Self Assessment Late Penalty Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-self-assessment-late-penalty-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter the tax year, the tax you owed and the dates you filed and paid, and see the late filing penalties, the late payment penalties, the interest, and what another 30 to 365 days of delay would add.

After using this, you know what a missed 31 January deadline costs today and what each extra month adds, which used to mean reading three GOV.UK pages and doing the day-count arithmetic by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-self-assessment-late-penalty-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rules were written from memory and must be verified against the source each result names. The interest rate is entered by you, never assumed. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
