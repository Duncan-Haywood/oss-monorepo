FOR ACCESSIBILITY AND DECK CREWS

Bid the landings, the rails, and the rise.

Price a residential wheelchair ramp the way the trade actually loses money: rise to run, then every landing a lineal-foot special cannot see, both-side handrails, surface, demo, permit, and the margin you intend to keep. You get the job price, what it works out to per foot of run and per walking square foot, and the number below which the job loses money.

Looking up how much to charge to build a wheelchair ramp? Start here, then replace every starting figure with your own crew times.

A starting job

Pick a common call to fill the rise, slope, layout, and surface. Changing a kit never overwrites hours or material costs you have already typed.

The opening

Rise is the step from grade to the threshold. Slope is inches of run per inch of rise — 1:12 is twelve inches of run for each inch of rise. These are starting points, not a code opinion.

Hours and extras

Hours per 100 sf apply to the whole walking surface — sloped run plus every landing. Handrail and footing counts start from the geometry; change them when the site disagrees.

Materials and your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. A lineal-foot special written on the run never sees it — or the landings.
