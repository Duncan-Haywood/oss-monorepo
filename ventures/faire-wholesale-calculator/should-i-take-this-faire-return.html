Choice Leftover vs keep-it

The first-order fee and outbound label are already sunk on the completed order. Taking the return claws back that slice of payout and recovers only restockable COGS. When does taking a Faire return still beat a keep-it credit?

When only some units come back. Returning 2 of 10 with an $8 label leaves $46.52 and flips ahead of the $41.90 keep-it credit. A full return at these costs does not. Denying at 70% they'll-drop-it leaves $53.13 and flips ahead of the credit.
