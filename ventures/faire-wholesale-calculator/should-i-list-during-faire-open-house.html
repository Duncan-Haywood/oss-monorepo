Should I list during Faire Open House?

Brands search this when Faire waives marketplace commission for a short window and retailers expect a deeper opening discount. Listing everyday at 15% + the new-customer fee is the baseline. Open House is not Faire Direct — you still did not bring the retailer — and it is not a reorder. The main calculator’s first-order table cannot show whether the waived commission pays for the extra markdown.

On the default $20 × 10 first order, everyday keeps $75.90 after 15% + $10. Open House at 0% commission and a 20% retailer discount keeps $66.66 (−$9.24). The waived commission still funds about 15.3% off before leftover matches everyday. Keep your everyday discount during the window and leftover is $105.90 (+$30.00) — the leak is the deeper discount, not the event. Two extra new retailers flip the 20% path to $199.98 vs $75.90 (+$124.08). Two Open House first orders (1 extra) match one everyday order.

Is Faire Open House the same as Faire Direct?

No. Faire Direct is 0% commission when you bring the retailer with your link. Open House is a seasonal window when Faire typically waives marketplace commission on retailers who still found you on Faire. The new-customer fee can still apply. This page holds Direct and off-Faire invoices off the table so the comparison stays an everyday-versus-event first-order question.

When does a deeper Open House discount still beat listing everyday?

When you already fund an everyday opening discount, or when the window actually produces extra new first orders. On these defaults a 20% event discount loses $9.24 per order against an everyday 0% list, but two extra new retailers flip the pile by $124.08. If you already list at 15% off everyday, the 20% Open House path wins per order.
