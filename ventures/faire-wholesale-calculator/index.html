What this checks

Generic “Faire fee calculator” pages stop at 15%. The decision a brand actually faces is whether a first marketplace order still works after the new-customer fee, how that fee looks after the reorders you expect from the same retailer, whether a Faire Direct link is worth sending to an existing account, whether Open House still leaves more after the deeper discount retailers expect, and whether next-day payout is worth the extra processing. A boutique buying the same order has a different question: landed cost versus shelf price.

It does not recommend a wholesale price, a payout speed, or a channel, and it is not tax or financial advice. The gaps are an arithmetic comparison of the rates you entered.
