Common questions

Should I enroll this book in Kindle Unlimited?

On the worked example — $4.99 ebook on the 70% plan, 2 MB file, $0.15/MB delivery, 80 paid ebook sales, 12,000 KU page reads at $0.0045, $14.99 paperback with 280 black-and-white pages, 20 KDP paperback sales, 40 other-store ebook sales at $2.50 net, and 10 Ingram paperbacks at a 40% wholesale discount and $5 print — exclusive keeps $405.12 and wide keeps $491.06. Wide is ahead by $85.94. That is arithmetic on those starting numbers, not a recommendation to enroll or leave.

How many KU page reads equal one paid sale on this example?

One paid sale nets $3.193. At $0.0045 per KENP page that is 709.6 pages. A 250-page book that is read all the way through is not automatically 250 KENP; KENP is Amazon's own normalized count from the file you uploaded.

How many KU page reads would match the wide extras?

The wide extras on this example are $100 of other-store ebook net plus $39.94 of Ingram paperback net, $139.94. At $0.0045 per page that is 31,097.8 KENP. The starting 12,000 reads do not reach it. Type a different KENP rate or a different wide volume on the calculator and the target moves.
