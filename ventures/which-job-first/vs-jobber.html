This tool vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber is account-based field-service software for running a home-service business across clients, scheduling, and crews. This page is a free, private ranker for one question — which job on your list to take first — before any of it touches a business system.

What each tool is for

Jobber is field-service management software founded in 2011 and headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, used by home-service businesses across HVAC, plumbing, electrical, lawn care, and cleaning trades. It combines client management, quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payment collection into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every crew, and it says it serves over 200,000 users. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Jobber's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — home-service field-service management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Jobber appears on this page. Jobber's calendar and scheduling views can show every job that is open, but they do not answer which one to take first — that judgement is still left to whoever is looking at the board.

This tool does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage clients, schedule crews, send invoices, or collect payments, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes a pasted list of possible jobs and turns it into a ranked work order by asking a handful of this-or-that questions — comparing two jobs at a time instead of asking you to score each one out of ten — plus a share link that carries the list (not the answers) so a partner or foreman can rank the same jobs independently. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
