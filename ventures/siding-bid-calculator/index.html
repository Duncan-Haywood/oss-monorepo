FOR SIDING CONTRACTORS

Bid the squares and the waste, not the house.

A one-story vinyl wrap with four windows is not the same job as a two-story fiber-cement tear-off with gables. Price the net wall, the waste, the height, the openings, and the linear trim — then see the walk-away number before a price-per-square-foot special eats the week.

The job

A square is 100 sq ft of wall. Waste covers cuts, starter, and damaged pieces — vinyl often needs about 10%, fiber cement more. Stories multiply install hours. None of this is a manufacturer table or a weathertightness finding — it is a time and material budget you can edit.

Linear trim

Trim is where a price-per-square-foot bid goes quiet. A small house with many windows can spend as much on J-channel and corners as on a whole extra square of siding.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation (ladders and tear-off are not cheap), liability insurance, and paid time off. An installer paid $32 often costs more than $42 on the wall. Underestimating that is how an $8-a-foot special loses money.
