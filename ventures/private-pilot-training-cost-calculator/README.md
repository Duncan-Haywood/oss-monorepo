# Private Pilot Training Cost Calculator

Free, private browser tool for student pilots: flight hours, aircraft and instructor rates, exam fees and gear become the total cost of a private pilot certificate, the cost at 40 to 100 hours, the price of each extra hour, a monthly budget at your lesson schedule, and whether a flying club beats renting.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/private-pilot-training-cost-calculator/

After using this, you know what the certificate will cost at your school's real rates, and what going 10 or 20 hours over the minimum does to the bill.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/private-pilot-training-cost-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
