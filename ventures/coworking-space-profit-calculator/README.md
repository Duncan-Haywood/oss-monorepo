# Coworking Space Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/coworking-space-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people planning or running a coworking or flex-office space: floor area, private offices, dedicated desks, hot-desk memberships, day passes, meeting rooms, lease, staff, fit-out and a loan become monthly profit, break-even occupancy, the price change that reaches a target margin, a sixty-month ramp-up with the cash reserve and payback, and loan coverage.

After using this, you know how full the space has to be to pay its lease and staff, how much cash to hold while members join, and whether your mix of rooms fits the floor.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/coworking-space-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
