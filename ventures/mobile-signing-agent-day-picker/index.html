    What's recognised

    InputHandling

    The plan is exact for the numbers you type (a search over every set and order, up to 14 signings). One agent and one home base; real traffic, one-way streets, a second notary, signings that must follow each other, customer cancellations and the value of a repeat customer are not modelled. Distance is straight-line miles times your circuity factor, not a routing service.

    Common questions

    Does it upload my signing list?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    How is the best day found?

    The tool tries the signings as a search over which have been done and which was last, keeping every way to get there that is not both later and less profitable than another. That finds the exact best set and order for the numbers you give, and the tests compare it with trying every ordering of every subset.

    Why would it turn down a signing I would normally take?

    Because an agent can only be in one place. A signing that pays well but drives you across the map, or sits in a window that collides with two better signings, can cost more than the two it displaces. The tool says what the signing would need to pay to earn its place.

    Where do the costs come from?

    From your own books: fuel and wear per mile, and what an hour of your time costs you. They are your estimates, so treat the plan as a decision aid, not a forecast.
