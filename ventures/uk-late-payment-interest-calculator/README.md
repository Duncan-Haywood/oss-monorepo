# UK Late Payment Interest Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter an overdue business invoice and its due date to see the statutory interest (8% over the Bank of England base rate under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998), the fixed compensation (£40, £70 or £100), the daily rate, a 30/60/90 day projection and a demand letter with your figures filled in.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-late-payment-interest-calculator/

After using this, you know what you can add to an overdue invoice and have a letter ready to send.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-late-payment-interest-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
