# European Accessibility Act Checker

Free, private browser tool: pick what you make or sell (a product or service in Article 2 of Directive (EU) 2019/882), your role and your whole-group size to see whether the European Accessibility Act likely applies, whether the Article 4(5) microenterprise exemption covers you, which Article the call rests on, the result at the other size class, and the main duties for your role.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/eaa-accessibility-act-checker/

After using this, you know whether to start an accessibility programme, which duties follow from your role, and which question (exact product reach, national rules, disproportionate burden) to put to your national authority or counsel.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eaa-accessibility-act-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not legal advice. It reads the Directive's text, not any Member State's transposition. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
