This calculator vs. JobTread

Two different shapes: JobTread is account-based project-management and job-costing software for running a home-building or remodeling business across clients, crews, and jobs. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this pole barn cost, given its bays, eave, doors, and floor, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

JobTread is project-management and job-costing software marketed to residential home builders and remodelers, including post-frame and pole-barn shops. It is used to run budgets and estimates, a client communication portal, scheduling, purchase orders and change orders, and job costing across a whole company's pipeline, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job. JobTread's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — construction project-management and job-costing software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for JobTread appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what JobTread does. It does not manage clients, schedule crews, track purchase orders, or run job costing across a pipeline, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes building length and width, post spacing, eave height, roof pitch, overhead and walk door counts, a lean-to, wainscot, insulation, electrical, and a gravel, 4-inch, or 6-inch slab floor, plus loaded wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the walk-away price below which the job loses money, the recommended price to quote, and a copyable bid summary — plus a printable customer proposal with scope lines and a signature block. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
