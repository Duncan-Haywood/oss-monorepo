    Common questions

    Why is my price so different from a $45-a-square-foot special?

    A flat per-square-foot number treats a bare gravel ag shell as the same job as a shop with extra eave height, overhead doors, a slab, and a lean-to. This calculator prices those separately — bays and posts from the spacing, extra hours for eave height, doors, lean-to, and floor type — so two buildings with the same footprint but different specs come out at different prices, the way real material and labor tickets do.

    Does switching from gravel to a slab only change the floor cost?

    No. Picking a floor preset also fills in that floor's own hours-per-square-foot, concrete or gravel rate, and rebar rate, because pouring and finishing a 6-inch reinforced slab takes real crew time a gravel floor does not. Once you have hand-edited a rate it stops auto-filling on preset changes, so your own numbers are never silently overwritten.

    Does the walk-away price include any profit for me?

    No. The walk-away price is your total cost with zero margin — labor, shell material, floor, doors, and overhead, but no profit. The price above it is the one built from your target net margin; quoting at the walk-away number means the job cannot lose money, but it also cannot pay you.

    Does my bid data leave my browser?

    No. Every calculation runs locally, and a copied bid link carries your own rates in the URL rather than on a server — send it to yourself or your crew, not the customer. Your saved bid lives in this browser only; “Start a fresh bid” deletes it.
