FOR POST-FRAME AND POLE-BARN BUILDERS

Bid the bays, the eave, and the floor.

Price a pole barn the way the trade actually loses money: posts and trusses from the bay spacing, extra eave height, overhead doors, a lean-to, and gravel versus a slab — not the published $45-a-foot ag special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot, and the number below which the barn loses money.

The building

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with your lumber ticket and a crew you have timed. Changing floor, insulation, or electrical fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.
