# UK Landlord Section 24 Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your rent, running costs, mortgage interest and other income and see, for 2025/26 and 2026/27, your tax bill with the 20% finance cost credit, how much more you pay than under the old full-deduction rules, and your tax as a share of your real profit.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-landlord-section-24-calculator/

After using this, you know what Section 24 really costs your rental property, which used to mean working the credit and the tax bands out by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-landlord-section-24-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
