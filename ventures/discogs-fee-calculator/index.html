        Common questions

        How much does Discogs take per sale?

        A 9% selling fee, charged on the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays, with a $0.10 minimum and a $150 maximum per order, plus payment processing on top. A $50 record with no shipping pays $4.50 in selling fee; at an assumed 2.9% + $0.30 processing, about $1.75 more, so you keep about $43.75 before postage and what you paid for it.

        Does Discogs charge its fee on shipping?

        Yes. The 9% applies to the item price and the shipping together, so a $20 record with $6 shipping pays 9% of $26 ($2.34), not 9% of $20 ($1.80). Processing is charged on the same total. It makes no difference whether you raise the price or the shipping: the fee follows the total the buyer pays.

        What is the Discogs processing fee?

        It is separate from the 9% selling fee and is taken by the payment provider on the same order. Discogs does not publish a single rate for every seller, and third-party guides disagree (figures such as 2.7% + $0.25, 2.9% + $0.30 and 3.49% + $0.49 all circulate). This calculator starts at 2.9% + $0.30 and lets you overwrite both numbers from your own payout statement.

        Is there a maximum Discogs fee?

        Yes. Discogs caps the selling fee at $150 per order, which is reached at an order total of about $1,667. Above that the selling fee stops growing, though processing still applies to the whole order. There is also a $0.10 minimum.
