How much does Discogs take per sale?

Discogs charges sellers a 9% selling fee on the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays, with a $0.10 minimum and a $150 maximum per order. Listing is free. The rate rose from 8% to 9% on May 22, 2023. On top of that, payment processing comes out of each order. Discogs does not publish one processing rate for every seller, so the examples below use 2.9% + $0.30 as an assumption; replace it with the figure on your own payout statement in the calculator.

The formula

selling fee = 9% × (item price + buyer-paid shipping), limited to $0.10–$150

processing = rate × (item price + buyer-paid shipping) + flat fee

payout = item price + buyer-paid shipping − selling fee − processing − your own postage cost

Worked example 1: a $50 record, no buyer-paid shipping

Worked example 2: the fee reaches shipping too

LineAmount
Item price$20.00
Buyer-paid shipping$6.00

Selling fee (9% of $26.00)$2.34
Processing (2.9% of $26.00 + $0.30)$1.05

The selling fee is $2.34, not $1.80 (9% of the $20 item alone). Because the fee follows the total, moving money between the price and the shipping line changes nothing: what moves your profit is the total the buyer pays and what the label actually costs you.

Cheap records and grails are different problems. On a $3 single, the flat $0.30 is a tenth of the sale and the whole cut is about 22%. On a $2,500 record the $150 cap holds the selling fee to 6%. The calculator’s fee ladder shows both on your own postage and costs.

Common questions

How much does Discogs take on a $50 record?

A 9% selling fee of $4.50, plus payment processing. At an assumed 2.9% + $0.30 processing that is $1.75 more, so about $43.75 reaches you before postage and what you paid for the record.

Does Discogs charge its fee on shipping?

Yes. The 9% applies to the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays. A $20 record with $6 shipping pays 9% of $26, which is $2.34, not 9% of $20.

What are the Discogs minimum and maximum fees?

The selling fee is at least $0.10 and at most $150 per order. The cap is reached at an order total of about $1,667.
