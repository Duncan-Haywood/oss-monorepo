The Discogs fee calculator vs. doing the fee maths yourself

Most Discogs sellers price a record with a rule of thumb: take off about 10% and call it done. It is close for a mid-priced record sold with free shipping and wrong in four places the calculator handles.

Where a spreadsheet is fine

If you sell one kind of record at one price with one shipping rate, a spreadsheet with your own processing figure does the same thing. The calculator is for pricing a crate where the answer changes record by record, and for comparing Discogs against eBay, Etsy or Reverb on the same item through the where-to-sell calculator.

Common questions

Why not just multiply by 0.91 in a spreadsheet?

Because the 9% is charged on item plus shipping, the processing has a flat per-order part, and the selling fee is capped at $150, so a flat percentage is wrong at both ends of your price range.

Do I need to know the Discogs processing rate?

It helps. Discogs does not publish one rate for every seller, so the calculator takes it as two boxes that start at 2.9% + $0.30. Copy your real figure from a payout statement and every result updates.
