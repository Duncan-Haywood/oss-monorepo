Common questions

    
How much of my sole trader income should I set aside for tax in Australia?

    
Often 20% to 30% of profit once you add income tax and the ACC earners' levy, but it depends on your profit. Enter your income and expenses here for a number built from the actual rates.

    
Do I pay ACC as self-employed?

    
Yes. Self-employed people pay the ACC earners' levy on profit, collected through your tax return, plus a CoverPlus work levy billed by ACC. KiwiSaver contributions for yourself are voluntary.

    
Do I pay tax in instalments?

    
If your residual income tax is above $5,000 you become a provisional taxpayer. With a 31 March balance date the standard-option instalments are due 28 August, 15 January and 7 May.

    
When must I register for GST?

    
When your turnover reaches $60,000 in the last 12 months or is expected to in the next 12. GST you collect belongs to IRD, so keep it out of your set-aside.
