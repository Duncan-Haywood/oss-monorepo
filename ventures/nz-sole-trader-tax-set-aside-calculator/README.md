# New Zealand Self-Employed Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2025-26)

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/nz-sole-trader-tax-set-aside-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter business income and expenses and get income tax, the ACC earners' levy, an optional student loan repayment and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags provisional tax and the $60,000 GST registration line.

After using this, a New Zealand sole trader knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill after filing the IR3.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/nz-sole-trader-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
