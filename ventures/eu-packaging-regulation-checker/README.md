# EU Packaging Regulation Checker

Free, private browser tool for makers, importers and online sellers: pick the packaging type, your role and where you are established to see which duties of the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR, Regulation (EU) 2025/40) reach you, the date each applies from (PFAS and heavy-metal limits, producer registration, labelling, recyclability, recycled content, the 50% empty-space rule), whether an EU operator stands behind the packaging, and which duties are still open.

After using this, you know which deadlines are already live (12 August 2026) and which are coming (labels 2028, recyclability and empty space 2030), instead of reading a 100-article Regulation.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-packaging-regulation-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
