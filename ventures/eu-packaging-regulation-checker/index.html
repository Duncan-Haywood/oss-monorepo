Common questions

    
Does the EU packaging regulation apply to my online shop?

    
Yes if you ship to EU buyers. Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR) covers packaging of every kind placed on the EU market, including the mailer, carton and filler around an order, and sellers outside the EU need an EU authorised representative for producer-responsibility registration.

    
When does the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation start to apply?

    
Most of it applies from 12 August 2026: substance limits including PFAS in food-contact packaging, conformity documentation and producer registration. Labelling follows from August 2028, and recyclability, recycled content, packaging minimisation and reuse targets from 1 January 2030.

    
What is the 50% empty space rule?

    
Grouped, transport and e-commerce packaging must keep empty space to at most 50% of its volume from 1 January 2030, and packaging weight and volume must be reduced to the minimum needed.

    
Do I need a registration in every EU country I sell to?

    
Producers register in each Member State where they first make packaged goods available, and a seller outside the EU appoints an authorised representative there. The national registers and fees differ, so confirm with each country's authority.
