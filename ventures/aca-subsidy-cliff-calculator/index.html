Common questions

What is the ACA subsidy cliff in 2026?

The enhanced premium tax credits that removed the income limit expired after 2025. For 2026 coverage, a household whose modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) is above 400% of the federal poverty line gets no premium tax credit at all. For one person that line is $62,600 (based on the 2025 poverty guideline of $15,650); one dollar over it loses the whole credit.

How is the 2026 premium tax credit calculated?

The credit is the benchmark plan's premium (the second-lowest-cost silver plan for your household and county) minus your expected contribution. The expected contribution is your MAGI times an applicable percentage that rises from 2.10% of income below 133% of the poverty line to 9.96% between 300% and 400%, per IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-25.

What counts as MAGI for the marketplace?

Adjusted gross income plus any tax-exempt interest, non-taxable Social Security benefits and excluded foreign income. For a self-employed person, deductions that reduce AGI, such as contributions to an HSA, a solo 401(k) or SEP-IRA, and half of self-employment tax, lower MAGI. This tool takes the MAGI you enter and does not compute it.

Do I have to repay advance credits if my income is higher than estimated?

Yes. For tax year 2026 and later there is no cap on repaying excess advance premium tax credit. If final MAGI is above 400% of the poverty line, all advance credits received for the year are owed back when you file Form 8962.
