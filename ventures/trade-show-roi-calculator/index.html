    Common questions

    Does it upload my show list?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    How is ROI worked out?

    Expected leads x lead-to-qualified % x qualified-to-deal % gives deals; deals x average first-year deal value x lifetime multiplier x gross margin gives expected gross profit. ROI is gross profit minus total cost, divided by total cost. Every rate is your estimate.

    Why skip a show that is profitable on its own?

    The budget (and the quarter limit, if you set one) can only buy so many. The tool compares every allowed combination and keeps the set with the highest total net gain, and states what swapping a skipped show in would cost.

    Is the plan guaranteed to be best?

    It is the exact best answer for the numbers you typed, found by branch-and-bound and checked against trying every combination in the tests. Leads and conversion rates are your estimates, so the result is a decision aid, not a forecast. The page also re-checks the plan with leads cut.

    Who is it for?

    Small B2B exhibitors, manufacturers, software firms and local-service companies, deciding whether a booth pays back and which shows to buy in a year.
