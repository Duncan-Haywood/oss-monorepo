What costs money and what does not

The designer, plans, cut sheet, shopping list, instructions and HOA packet are free and stay free. Only the branded build pack is a planned paid extra, a one-off price rather than a monthly plan. It is not on sale yet: there is no checkout, so nothing here can charge you. The sample is generated by the real code from an invented builder and the 8x12 preset.

Shed builders, kit sellers and handymen who give customers a plan and a materials list. Nothing you type is uploaded; the pack is built in your browser. The code is MIT licensed.
