How much lumber does a 8x16 shed take?

A 8x16 gable shed on skids, framed the common way, takes 152 framing pieces cut from 103 boards. The cut sheet's boards cost $812.06 (no way of cutting those pieces from those lengths can cost less than $806.48); a few spare boards bring the lumber to about $920.57. The whole shed, with siding, roofing, sheathing, fasteners and a gravel pad, comes to about $4,088.87 before sales tax, $31.94 per square foot.

Open Shed Designer and change the size, door and windows: the counts update to your design.

The design behind these numbers

16 ft along the ridge, 8 ft across the gable, 8 ft walls (91-1/2" studs under three 1-1/2" plates), a 6/12 roof with a 12" overhang, 2x4 walls 16" on centre, pressure-treated 4x6 skids on a gravel pad, a 3/4" plywood floor, T1-11 siding and shingles. Rafters are cut 69" long including the plumb cut. Doors and windows are those of the tool's 8x10 preset, with kings, jacks, headers, sills and cripples framed around each.
