This calculator vs. Aspire

Two different shapes: Aspire is an account-based platform for running a landscape or irrigation company's whole operation, including scheduling and invoicing a job once it is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this sprinkler-system install cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

Aspire is a field service business management platform built for landscape, irrigation, lawn-care, and snow-and-ice removal companies (a product of ServiceTitan). It is used to run CRM and lead tracking, estimating and proposal generation, crew scheduling and routing, purchasing, invoicing, payroll and accounting integration, and job costing across a whole operation, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Aspire's own site does not publish a fixed price list — it prices by company size and complexity on a sales call — so no dollar figure for it appears on this page; only its well-documented identity and category are stated here. Public descriptions of that estimating and scheduling software describe building, tracking and billing a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a new sprinkler-system install should cost from zone count, trench footage, soil, and backflow device before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Aspire does. It does not track a lead, schedule a crew across a season, or run payroll. It takes one install's zones, extra heads, trench footage and soil (dirt versus rock/clay), controller, backflow device (a 3/4-inch PVB versus a 1.5-inch RPZ), drip conversion, rain sensor, and master valve, plus loaded crew cost, overhead and target margin, and turns that into the recommended price, the walk-away floor, the price per zone, and the effective crew hourly rate — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
