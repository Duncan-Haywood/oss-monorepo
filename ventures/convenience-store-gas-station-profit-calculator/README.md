# Convenience Store and Gas Station Profit Calculator

Free, private browser tool for convenience store and gas station owners and buyers: gallons, pump price, delivered fuel cost, card fees, inside sales by category, labor, fixed costs and loan payments become net fuel margin in cents a gallon after card fees, monthly operating profit, break-even gallons, debt coverage, the share of profit that rides on fuel, and how margin and volume changes move profit.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/convenience-store-gas-station-profit-calculator/

After using this, you know how many cents a gallon you keep after card fees, how many gallons pay the store's costs and loan, and how exposed the store is to a few cents of fuel margin.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/convenience-store-gas-station-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
