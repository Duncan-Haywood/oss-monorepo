FOR MASONRY CONTRACTORS

Bid the joints, the access, and the extras.

Price tuckpointing the way the trade actually loses money: a ground-level smear versus a grind-and-point, scaffold versus a swing stage, Type N versus historic lime, then lintels, replacement brick, and caulk — not the published $8 / sq ft special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot, and the number below which the wall loses money.

The wall

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a mortar ticket and a crew you have timed. Changing method, access, or mortar fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the method, access, and mortar you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a mortar catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs
