What's recognised

InputHandling

Top-up and checkout drop-off are assumptions you set (defaults: 25% of orders right under a threshold add items, fading to none 30% below it; 10% of buyers abandon when charged shipping); returns, repeat purchases and lifetime value are not modelled; margin is the profit share of merchandise before shipping.

Common questions

Does this connect to my store?

No. It reads the order rows you paste and never contacts Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy or any carrier. Every figure is arithmetic on your numbers plus two behaviours you set, so nothing here is a measurement of your customers.

How do I get the numbers?

Export orders from your store and keep two columns: the merchandise subtotal before shipping and tax, and what you paid to ship it. In Shopify that is the Subtotal column of the orders export; the shipping cost comes from your carrier or label report. The shipping column is optional.

What is the top-up rate?

Of orders just under the free-shipping minimum, the share who add an item to reach it. It cannot be read off past orders, so you set it and the tool shows how the answer moves. 25% is a placeholder, not a benchmark.

What is checkout drop-off?

The share of would-be buyers who abandon when they have to pay shipping. Free shipping is mostly a bet that this number is large enough to pay for the shipping you give away, so the tool reports the drop-off at which each threshold breaks even rather than pretending to know yours.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The browser page runs on the text in your own browser and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
