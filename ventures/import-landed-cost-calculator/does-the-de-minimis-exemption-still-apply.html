Does the $800 de minimis exemption still apply?

Until 2025, a shipment valued at $800 or less could generally enter the US without paying duty at all — the "de minimis" exemption. That coverage has been narrowed or eliminated for a growing set of countries and shipment types since then, most visibly for goods originating in or shipped from China and Hong Kong, and the rules have changed more than once. If you built your pricing around shipping small parcels duty-free, that assumption is exactly the kind of thing worth re-checking before your next order lands.

What it costs when the exemption no longer applies

A worked example: a $200 item, $20 freight to the US, no supplemental tariff, an informal entry, shipped by air (no Harbor Maintenance Fee). At a 3% duty rate on the $220 customs value:

That is $14.22 of duty and fees on a shipment that would have landed at exactly $220 under the old duty-free exemption — a 4.61% tariff burden on the $200 item cost that a seller pricing off the old assumption would not have budgeted for. Common questions

Does the $800 de minimis exemption still apply to my shipment?

For a growing set of countries and shipment types, no — the exemption that let shipments valued under $800 enter the US duty-free has been narrowed or eliminated since 2025, most visibly for goods originating in or shipped from China and Hong Kong. Coverage has changed more than once and varies by origin country and shipment type, so check current CBP guidance or ask your customs broker for your specific lane rather than assuming either the old or the new rule applies by default.

What changes for a small seller who used to ship duty-free under $800?

A shipment that used to clear with zero duty, zero tariff, and no processing fee now goes through the same arithmetic as a larger, always-dutiable shipment: duty on the customs value, any applicable supplemental or Section 301 tariff, and a Merchandise Processing Fee (flat for an informal entry, percentage-based with a floor and ceiling for a formal one). On the worked example above — a $200 item, $20 freight, no supplemental tariff, informal entry, air freight — that is $14.22 of fees that used to be $0.
