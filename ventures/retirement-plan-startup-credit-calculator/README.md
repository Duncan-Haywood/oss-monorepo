# Retirement Plan Startup Credit Calculator

Free, private browser tool for small employers: enter headcount, setup cost and employer contributions to see the SECURE 2.0 startup, contribution and auto-enrollment credits (IRS Form 8881) for each of the first five plan years, and what they are worth after tax.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/retirement-plan-startup-credit-calculator/

After using this, you know how much of a new 401(k) or SIMPLE IRA the IRS pays for, which used to take reading Form 8881 and the 45E rules.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/retirement-plan-startup-credit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. The indexed pay cap is an editable field. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
