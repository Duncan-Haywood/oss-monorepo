This calculator vs. JobNimbus

Two different shapes: JobNimbus is an account-based CRM and job management platform for running an exterior contractor's whole lead-to-job pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this driveway cost, given its method, thickness, apron, and failed base, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

JobNimbus, founded in 2013 by Ben Hodson, Nick Wood, and Jason Wood and headquartered in Lehi, Utah, is a CRM and job management platform built for roofing, siding, gutter, and other exterior contractors. It covers lead capture and tracking, estimating, scheduling, invoicing, and job management for a whole company across every crew and every job, sold as an ongoing subscription account. JobNimbus's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity, founders, headquarters, and category are stated, and no dollar amount for JobNimbus appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what JobNimbus does. It does not track a lead, schedule a crew, or invoice a customer across jobs. It takes one asphalt-driveway job — square footage, paving method (overlay, mill-and-overlay, or full-depth reclaim), asphalt thickness, street apron square footage, failed-base area needing full excavation and new stone, geotextile fabric, sawcutting, tack coat, demo and dump, plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, travel, overhead, and target margin — and turns that into the price, the mix tons ordered, the price per square foot, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, plus a printable customer proposal with a deposit split and a price-held date. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
