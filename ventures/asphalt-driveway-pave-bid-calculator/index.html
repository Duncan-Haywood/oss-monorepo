Bid the method, the thickness, and the extra work.

Price an asphalt driveway the way the trade actually loses money: overlay versus mill-and-overlay versus full-depth, mix tons from thickness, the street apron, failed base, and the sawcut at the garage. 800 square feet of sound asphalt is not the same bid as 1,000 square feet that has to come out. You get the price, the tons, the extra-work share, and the walk-away number below which the job loses money.
