Common questions

Why does the calculator price material from a waste-inclusive square footage instead of the vehicle's bare surface area?

Because that is what actually gets bought and consumed. Wrap film comes off a roll, and cutting around mirrors, door handles, pillars, and compound curves wastes a real share of every roll — typically 15-30% depending on the vehicle. Pricing material at the bare surface area quotes the vehicle, not the roll, and under-buys the job every time.

What happens if I quote material at the bare surface area and ignore the waste factor?

You still have to buy and consume the waste-inclusive material to actually cover the vehicle, so the gap between the two prices comes straight out of the job's margin — the calculator reports that gap directly as the waste-blind loss per job.

Why is there a separate complexity multiplier for install hours?

A flat panel — a box truck side, a straight fleet van — installs much faster per square foot than a sedan or SUV with mirrors, handles, and compound curves. A single flat installed rate per square foot underprices labor on the harder vehicle every time; the multiplier keeps that difference visible instead of averaging it away.

Does anything about my costs or roll usage get uploaded?

No. No account and no network requests — the vehicle, your rates, your prices, and your proposal terms stay in this browser. The quote itself saves to this browser's local storage as you type, so it is still here on your next visit, and "Start a fresh quote" clears it.
