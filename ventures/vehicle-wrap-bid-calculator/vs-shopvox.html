Vehicle Wrap Bid Calculator vs. ShopVOX

Two different jobs: ShopVOX is an account-based shop management platform for running a sign or wrap shop's whole quoting-to-invoicing pipeline. This page is a free, private calculator for one wrap's waste-inclusive material price before you send the estimate.

What each tool is for

ShopVOX is a shop management platform built for sign, print, and vehicle wrap businesses: online quoting, order and production tracking, scheduling, and invoicing, run through an ongoing account your team signs into. ShopVOX's own current pricing and full feature list are not reachable from this session to confirm, so no dollar figure or plan name for ShopVOX is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — an account-based shop management platform covering quoting, production, and invoicing — are stated.

This calculator does none of what ShopVOX does. It has no account, no order tracking, no production schedule, and no invoicing — it stores and uploads nothing. It takes one vehicle's surface area, a material waste factor, laminate, install complexity, and labor rate, and prices that single wrap from the waste-inclusive material total the roll actually consumes, then shows exactly what a bare-surface-area quote would have lost on the job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or ShopVOX?

Both, for different jobs, and a shop that already runs its business through ShopVOX can still use this calculator for the narrow moment of pricing one wrap. ShopVOX is a shop management platform built for sign, print, and vehicle wrap businesses: online quoting, order and production tracking, scheduling, and invoicing, run as an ongoing account your team signs into. This calculator does none of that. It takes one vehicle's surface area, a material waste factor, install complexity, and labor rate, and prices that single wrap from what the roll actually consumes, entirely in your browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A shop that wants quoting, production tracking, and invoicing run through one system is better served by ShopVOX or a comparable platform; a shop that wants an instant, private, no-login price check for one job, including the dollar loss from ignoring roll waste, is in the right place here.

Can ShopVOX calculate the waste-inclusive material cost this calculator reports?

ShopVOX's quoting module can carry a wrap job through its own pricing and production workflow once your shop's materials, labor rates, and margins are configured in that account. This calculator is not a substitute for that workflow — it is a fast, account-free way to see, before you touch any shop system, what a bare-surface-area quote is about to lose against what the vinyl roll will actually consume once the vehicle's curves and cutouts are accounted for.
