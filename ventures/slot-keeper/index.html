A booking page you host yourself, with no per-seat fee.

Slot Keeper is the open-source scheduling page for one person or a small team. Set your weekly hours and it offers only the times you are really free, in each visitor's own time zone, then sends the invitation, a reminder, and a link to cancel or reschedule. Below, try the slot finder with your own hours and a pasted calendar. Everything on this page runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Run your own copy

Fork the repository, open https://render.com/deploy?repo=<your fork URL>, and Render builds it in your account with a generated admin key. Or docker compose up anywhere, or Fly.io with the steps in the repository. Add your calendar's secret iCal address as BUSY_ICS_URL and your Resend key to start sending invitations. Nothing runs on the author's accounts.

Questions

Why not just use Calendly, Cal.com or SavvyCal?

Those are polished and the right call if you need teams with round-robin routing, payments, workflows and a mobile app. Calendly and similar tools charge per seat per month for the features beyond a single basic link, and your invitees' details live on their servers. Slot Keeper is one small Node server in your own account: unlimited event types and bookings, your calendar's busy time respected, invitations with calendar files, reminders and a cancel or reschedule link, with no seat fee. If you are one person or a tiny team that mostly needs a booking link, it covers the job.

How does it know I am busy elsewhere?

Paste your calendar's secret iCal address (Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar all offer one) into BUSY_ICS_URL. The server reads it every five minutes and never offers a time that overlaps an event, including recurring events, all-day events and different time zones. If the feed cannot be read it keeps the last good copy and warns you on the admin page. It reads busy time only; it does not write to your calendar, so subscribe to the bookings feed it gives you to see bookings there.

Does it handle time zones and daylight saving?

Yes. Your weekly hours are in your zone and each invitee picks theirs; times shift correctly across daylight-saving changes, and a time that does not exist on the spring-forward day is never offered. The demo on this page uses the same code as the server.

How are invitations and reminders sent?

Through your own Resend account with your verified sending domain. It starts in dry-run mode: bookings are saved but no email is sent until you set DRY_RUN=false with RESEND_API_KEY, MAIL_FROM and HOST_EMAIL. Each confirmation carries a calendar file the invitee's app adds to their calendar, and cancelling sends a cancellation file that removes it. A reminder goes out once, a day ahead by default.

Can invitees cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Every booking gets a private link. Rescheduling keeps the old time until the new one is confirmed and then cancels it, so nobody is left with nothing. Only the person who booked, with the right link and email, can change it.

What does it store?

Event types, and each booking's name, email address, optional note and time, in one JSON file you control. IP addresses are held in memory only for rate limiting. Set RETENTION_DAYS to delete old bookings. You are the operator and responsible for your privacy notice and data-protection duties.
