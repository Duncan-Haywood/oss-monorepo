# UK Pension Annual Allowance Calculator

Free, private browser tool for UK high earners and defined benefit scheme members: your tapered annual allowance (threshold income £200,000, adjusted income £260,000, floor £10,000), carry forward from the previous three tax years, how much you can still pay in, and the annual allowance charge on any excess.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-pension-annual-allowance-calculator/

After using this, you know whether a pension input will trigger a charge and how much carry forward covers it, which used to mean reading HMRC guidance and a pension savings statement side by side.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-pension-annual-allowance-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
