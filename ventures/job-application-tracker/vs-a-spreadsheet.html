Job application tracker vs. a spreadsheet

If you already keep a spreadsheet and it works, keep it. This page is for when it stops working: the follow-up dates live in your head, and you cannot tell which job board is worth your time.

SpreadsheetThis tracker
SetupBuild columns and formulasOpen the page
Today's follow-upsA formula and a filter you maintainShown at the top, most overdue first
Silent for a monthManualFlagged, you decide whether to close it
Reply rate by sourcePivot tableBuilt in, with sample-size caveat
RemindersYour calendar, by handDownload a .ics with every pending follow-up
Sharing / editing anywhereStrong: cloud sheets sync across devicesWeak: data lives in one browser, export to move it
PrivacyDepends where the sheet livesNever leaves your browser

Moving over

Export the sheet as CSV with headers company, role, source, link, salary, status, applied, followUp, notes (dates as YYYY-MM-DD), then import it. Unknown columns are ignored and duplicates are skipped.
