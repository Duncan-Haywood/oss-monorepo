This calculator vs. Paved

Paved is a newsletter sponsorship marketplace: you list your newsletter, advertisers browse hundreds of publications by category and audience size, and they book a sponsorship at the price you set (or a rate its Smart Pricing tool suggests from marketplace demand). Paved handles discovery, booking, and payment for a 30% commission on every sponsorship it books.

That makes Paved the right choice for finding advertisers you don't already have, and running the whole booking and payment process through a marketplace. This page answers a narrower question: for a sponsor you already have — your own network, a cold pitch, a returning advertiser — what should you actually charge, free, with no listing and no cut of what you negotiate directly.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Paved?

They solve different problems. Paved is a marketplace: you list your newsletter, advertisers browse and book a spot at the price you set (or Paved's Smart Pricing suggests), and Paved handles discovery, booking, and payment in exchange for a 30% commission on the sponsorship. This page is a free calculator: no listing, no advertiser discovery, no commission. You enter your subscriber count, open rate, and niche, and it gives you a price range to quote a sponsor you already found yourself — through your own network, a cold pitch, or a returning advertiser.

Does Paved price the same way this calculator does?

Not exactly the same inputs, but a similar idea. Paved's own guidance says sponsorship rates vary enormously by reach and reputation — indie newsletters listing spots as low as $100, with market averages cited around $5,000 and larger publications commanding thousands more — and its Smart Pricing tool suggests a rate from marketplace demand data it collects across its network. This calculator has no marketplace data to draw on; it applies commonly cited CPM-per-1000-opens benchmark ranges to your own subscriber count, open rate, and niche instead, so the range is grounded in your own numbers rather than what Paved's advertiser pool happened to book this quarter.

Is Paved free?

Not to publish through. Paved does not charge advertisers a platform fee, but it charges publishers a 30% commission on every sponsorship booked through its marketplace. This calculator has no listing, no booking flow, and takes no cut of anything — it is a free static page that only computes a price range.
