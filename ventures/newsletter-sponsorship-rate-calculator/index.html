Common questions

How much should I charge for a newsletter sponsorship?

It depends on your subscriber count, open rate, niche, and which ad placement you're selling. This calculator applies commonly cited market CPM-per-1000-opens ranges to your own numbers to give a starting price range for a dedicated send, a primary in-issue placement, a secondary placement, or a classified/text link.

Why does this calculate price from opens instead of raw subscriber count?

Sponsors are paying for eyes on the ad, not names on a list. Newsletter ad marketplaces routinely price against opens rather than subscribers, because a 20,000-subscriber list with a 15% open rate reaches fewer real readers than a 5,000-subscriber list with a 50% open rate.

Is this price a guarantee of what a sponsor will pay?

No. It is a starting point for negotiation based on general market ranges, not a quote, valuation, or offer performed on your behalf. Actual rates vary by list quality, click-through history, niche demand, and how much a specific sponsor wants your specific audience that week.

What counts as a dedicated send versus a primary or secondary placement?

A dedicated send is a solo email to your list containing only the sponsor's message — it commands the highest price because it has no competing content. A primary placement is the top ad slot inside your regular issue; a secondary placement is a lower slot in the same issue. A classified or text-link placement is the smallest, cheapest unit, usually a line of text with a link.
