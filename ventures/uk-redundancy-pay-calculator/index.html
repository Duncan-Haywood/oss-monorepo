Common questions

    
How much statutory redundancy pay will I get?

    
One week's pay for each year worked while aged 22 to 40, one and a half weeks for each year aged 41 or over, and half a week for each year under 22, counting back from the redundancy date for at most 20 years. A week's pay is capped at £719 for dismissals from 6 April 2025.

    
Who qualifies?

    
Employees with at least two years' continuous service who are dismissed by reason of redundancy. Workers without employee status and the self-employed do not qualify. Northern Ireland has its own rules.

    
What is the maximum statutory redundancy payment?

    
30 times the weekly cap: £21,570 for dismissals from 6 April 2025 (30 weeks at £719). Contractual or enhanced schemes can pay more.

    
Is redundancy pay taxed?

    
Redundancy payments are free of income tax up to £30,000 in total termination payments. Anything above is taxed as pay, and pay in lieu of notice is taxable in full.

    
How much notice must my employer give?

    
At least one week for each complete year of service, up to 12 weeks, once you have worked a month. Your contract may promise more.
