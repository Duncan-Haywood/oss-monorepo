# UK Redundancy Pay Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter age, complete years of service and pay and see statutory redundancy pay for dismissals from 6 April 2025 and 6 April 2026 (England, Wales, Scotland): the age-band weeks, the weekly pay cap, the 20-year limit and how much falls inside the £30,000 tax-free limit.

After using this, you know what you are legally owed and whether your employer's figure is right, which used to mean working the age bands out year by year by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-redundancy-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
