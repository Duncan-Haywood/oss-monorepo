# UK Redundancy Pay Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your age, years of service and weekly pay and see statutory redundancy pay (with the weekly cap and 20-year limit), whether your employer's offer is below the statutory minimum, your notice entitlement and the tax on anything over £30,000.

After using this, you know what you are owed and whether an offer is fair, which used to mean working the age bands and the cap out by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-redundancy-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
