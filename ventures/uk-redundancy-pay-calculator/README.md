# UK Redundancy Pay Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-redundancy-pay-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter your dates and weekly pay and see statutory redundancy pay in Great Britain (weeks by age and service, the weekly cap), your minimum notice, whether an employer's offer meets the statutory minimum, and how much is inside the £30,000 tax-free limit.

After using this, you know what you are owed and can check an offer, which used to mean reading the age bands and capping the week's pay by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-redundancy-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
