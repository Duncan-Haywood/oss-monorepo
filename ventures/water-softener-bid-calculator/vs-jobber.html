This calculator vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber is an account-based platform for running a plumbing or water-treatment company's whole operation, including scheduling the crew and invoicing the customer once a job is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this water-softener install cost, before you offer it.

What each tool is for

Jobber is a field service management platform used by home service businesses — plumbing, HVAC, lawn care, cleaning and general contracting among them — to run client management and online quoting, scheduling and crew dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client-facing hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Jobber's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that kind of field service software describe scheduling, invoicing and collecting payment for a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a water-softener install should cost from grain capacity, pipe run, copper versus PEX, iron filtration and haul-away before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage a client list, dispatch a crew across a season, or collect a payment. It takes a water-softener install — grain capacity, salt or salt-free system, softener-only or with an iron filter or under-sink RO, pipe material and extra footage past the included run, the brine drain, haul-away of the old unit and a brine receptacle — plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, travel and equipment, and overhead, and turns that into the install price, the walk-away floor, the price per foot of extra pipe, the effective crew hourly rate and, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, plus a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
