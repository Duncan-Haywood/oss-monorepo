    Common questions

    Why does the same zone count cost more or less depending on head type?

    Because spray, rotor, and drip zones each get their own minutes-per-zone rate, not one flat per-zone number. Drip laterals need a longer, lower-pressure purge than a spray zone, so a system with the same zone count but more drip runs takes longer to blow out and should cost more — a flat “$8 a zone” rate cannot see that difference.

    Is a backflow drain-and-insulate included in the per-zone price?

    No. Backflow work is priced separately, from the number of backflows and the minutes you set per backflow. Treating it as bundled into the zone count is exactly the shortfall this tool exists to catch.

    What does the freeze-warning toggle actually change?

    Turning it on applies your after-hours labor premium percentage on top of the loaded labor cost for the whole stop, because a freeze call is a wage problem, not a compressor problem — the same compressor and fittings cost either way. Leave it off for a normal daytime route.

    Does a low price here mean the system is actually winterized?

    No. This is a price calculation from the minutes and rates you enter, not a finding that any system was correctly drained or insulated. Confirm your drain method and local freeze-risk timing yourself; the tool has no way to check either.
