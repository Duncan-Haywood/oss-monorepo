This calculator vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber is an account-based platform for running a home service company's whole operation, including scheduling the crew and invoicing the customer once a job is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this sprinkler winterization stop cost, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Jobber is a field service management platform used by home service businesses — irrigation, lawn care, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and cleaning among them — to run client management and online quoting, scheduling and crew dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client-facing hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Jobber's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that kind of field service software describe scheduling, invoicing, and collecting payment for a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a sprinkler winterization stop should cost from zone counts, backflow drain time, and a freeze-warning premium before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage a client list, dispatch a route across a season, or collect a payment. It takes each system's spray, rotor, and drip zone counts — each with its own minutes-per-zone rate, since a drip lateral needs a longer, lower-pressure purge than a spray zone — backflows to drain and insulate, a freeze-warning / after-hours labor premium, travel and minimum on-site hours, loaded tech wage, compressor and fittings cost, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the job price, the price per zone, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the stop loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one stop. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
