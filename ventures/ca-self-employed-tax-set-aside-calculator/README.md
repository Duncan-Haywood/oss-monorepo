# Canada Self-Employed Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2025 / 2026)

Free, private browser tool: enter business revenue and expenses and get CPP/CPP2 (both halves), federal income tax, a provincial estimate and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags quarterly instalments and the $30,000 GST/HST small-supplier line.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/ca-self-employed-tax-set-aside-calculator/

After using this, a Canadian freelancer or sole proprietor knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill in April.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/ca-self-employed-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
