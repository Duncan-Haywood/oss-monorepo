Shifts from your sales

Paste an hourly sales export (a date-time column and a sales column). Each weekday-hour is averaged, heads needed is sales divided by your sales per labor hour (at least your minimum), and each day's headcount is cut into shifts. Copy the result into the roster builder. Nothing is uploaded.

Planned from the average, an hour is short about half the time. With a number here each hour is staffed for that share of its past days instead: 1 staffs for the median day, 3 for 3 days in 4 (75%), 0.5 for 1 in 3.

Test it on your past weeks

    
Each full week after the first four is planned from the weeks before it only, then compared hour by hour with what that week's sales needed. Tried for the plain average, three half-lives, the last four weeks and last week alone; with a short-cost number, each of those staffed for that share of past days too, scored as that many times the short hours plus the spare ones. Needs dated rows and at least five full weeks.
