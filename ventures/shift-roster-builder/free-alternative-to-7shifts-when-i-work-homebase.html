A free alternative to 7shifts, When I Work, Homebase, Deputy, Sling and Humanity for building the rota

Scheduling apps bundle several jobs: building the week, telling staff, trading shifts, clocking in and paying. If what hurts on Sunday night is the first one, you do not need all of them. This page says plainly which part the free roster builder covers and which it does not.

What this tool does differently

It runs in your browser, so your staff list and their wages are never uploaded and nobody makes an account. It treats availability, rest gaps, weekly and daily limits, minors' hours, split-shift premiums and overtime as rules, searches for the cheapest roster that covers the most hours, and tells you before the week starts which shifts cannot be filled and what blocks each one. The source is MIT-licensed, so a small team can also run its own copy of the team page without a per-user price.

Try it without typing: pick a template (cafe, salon, shop, clinic) and it builds a roster for an invented team straight away.

Where it falls short

  • It is a heuristic search, not a proof of the cheapest possible roster on large problems.
  • It does not know local predictive-scheduling, break or minor-labor law; you enter the rules that apply to you.
  • The self-hosted team page has no accounts and no automatic notifications.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to 7shifts, When I Work, Homebase, Deputy, Sling or Humanity?

For the rota-building part, yes: the free roster builder fills as many shifts as your rules allow, names the ones nobody can take, and runs in your browser with no account. It does not replace time clocks, payroll, push notifications or a staff app. Teams that need those still need a scheduling suite.

Can I run my own team schedule without paying per user?

Yes. The roster builder repository includes a small self-hosted server: you publish the week, your team opens one link to see their shifts and add them to a calendar, and it sends you their availability and shift-trade requests. It is MIT-licensed, has no dependencies and runs on a disk you own.

What does the roster builder do that a spreadsheet does not?

It checks availability, rest gaps, hour limits and overtime for you, searches for the cheapest roster that still covers the most shift-hours, and tells you which shifts cannot be filled and why before the week starts.
