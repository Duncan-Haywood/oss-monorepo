Retail staff schedule: covering the floor, late nights and Saturdays

A small shop needs one person on the floor on a quiet weekday, two on late-night shopping evenings and three on Saturday, from a team that is mostly part-time and available only some days. The useful question is which of those blocks cannot be covered before the week is posted, not after a closing shift finds itself alone.

A worked example

An invented boutique has four people (wages $17, $15, $16 and $14 an hour) and 19 floor-shifts: one person 9 to 2 and one 2 to 7 Monday to Friday, two on late-night shopping Thursday and Friday 5 to 8, three on Saturday 10 to 6 and two on Sunday 11 to 5. Availability: Ava Mon-Sat 9-8, Ben Thu-Sun 10-8, Chloe Mon-Fri 9-3 and Saturday 10-6, Dev Fri-Sun 10-8. This is the CLI's real output:

17 of 19 floor-shifts are covered for $1,445 with no overtime. Two stay open: the second person on Thursday's late-night shopping and on Friday's. For Thursday it says two people are free then but already on another shift; for Friday it adds that one would break the 10-hour rest rule. That is the answer to give the owner: late nights need a fifth person, or a single-person evening with a rule for who is alone in the shop. Switching the rest rule off covers 18 of 19 for $1,496, and shows what that cover costs: someone closing late and opening early.

Hand-building this usually hides the Thursday gap until Wednesday. Listing the open shifts with reasons first turns it into a hiring or opening-hours decision.

Questions

How many people should be on the floor at once?

Start from the busiest hour you can name, not the average. Enter the heads you need per block as the people needed; if you have sales by hour, the sales-per-labor-hour page turns them into shifts, and the roster says which blocks it could not fill.

Does it know about late-night shopping, sales or holidays?

Only what you type: add a shift line for the extended evening or the sale weekend with more people needed, and leave out dates you do not trade. It does not forecast footfall.

Can I use it for part-time students and weekend staff?

Yes. Give each person their availability, a weekly maximum and a minimum if you owe them hours. Under-18 rules differ by place and are not modelled, so check them yourself.
