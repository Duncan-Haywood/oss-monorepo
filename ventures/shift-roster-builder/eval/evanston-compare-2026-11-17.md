What the rows show: Evanston and Emeryville share the under-24-hour cut rule (the lesser of the scheduled shift's
hours and 4 h), so an hour shaved off an 8-hour shift the night before costs 4 h of pay there, against Berkeley's 1 h
(hours taken away) and Chicago's and Seattle's half of the lost hour. Evanston has no de minimis grace in any summary
read, so 5 minutes added the evening before owes the hour, as in Seattle; Emeryville, Berkeley and Chicago let it go.
Seattle pays half the lost hours however early inside 14 days, so the 3-day cancellation is its dearest row.
