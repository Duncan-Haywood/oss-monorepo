Restaurant staff schedule: covering lunch, dinner and brunch with a small team

A small restaurant schedules services, not just people: lunch on weekdays, two or three on dinner, brunch on weekends, and each person works only some days and hours. The useful question is which services cannot be covered with the team you have, and whether a close-then-open shift would close the gap, before the week is posted.

A worked example

An invented restaurant has seven people (wages $14 to $22 an hour) and 30 service-shifts: two people at weekday lunch 10 to 3, two at dinner 4 to 10 every day, one extra on Friday and Saturday dinner, and two at weekend brunch 10 to 3. Availability runs from Eli's weekday days (Mon-Sat 9-18) to Gus's weekends only. This is the CLI's real output with the default 10 hours of rest:

PersonShifts
AnaTue and Wed lunch, Thu to Sat dinner
BenMon, Tue, Wed, Fri dinner, Thu lunch
CleoWed dinner, Fri and Sat extra dinner, Sun dinner
DevFri lunch, Sat and Sun brunch
EliMonday to Friday lunch
FayTue, Thu, Sat, Sun dinner
GusSat and Sun brunch

28 of 30 service-shifts are covered for $2,800 with no overtime. Two stay open on Monday: one lunch place and one dinner place, because only Ben and Eli can work Mondays. The roster says who is free but already on another shift, which is the answer to give a candidate: Monday needs a second weekday person, not a longer week for the same two. It also notes that Eli gets 25 hours, 11 under their 36-hour minimum, and that without Fay 17 more hours go uncovered.

Allow close-then-open shifts (rest set to 0) and it covers 29 of 30 for $2,886, $86 more. Whether that is worth a tired opener is a decision the numbers let you make; the default says no.

Hand-building this usually hides the Monday gap until the weekend before. Listing the open shifts with reasons first turns it into a hiring or availability question.

Questions

How many people should a restaurant schedule per service?

Schedule to covers you expect, not a fixed headcount. Enter the people needed for each service block, from your own sales by day, and the builder says which blocks it could not fill.

Why does the builder avoid close-then-open shifts?

By default it keeps 10 hours between shifts, so nobody closes at 10pm and opens at 10am. In the worked example, allowing it covers one more shift for $86 more payroll; whether that is worth it is your call.

Does it handle tipped staff and tip pooling?

It schedules hours at the wage you enter. The command line version also has a tip-pool split by hours worked (--tips); it does not know tip-credit rules in your state.
