A schedule you can post and hand out

A roster in a spreadsheet gets screenshotted and lost. The staff pack turns the week you already built into something that looks like your business: your name, logo and colour on a wall copy that shows everyone's shifts in one grid, and one page per person to hand out or send as a PDF. Open shifts are listed, and a notes page carries house rules.

No pay or wages appear anywhere on it, so it is safe to put on the back-room wall.

What costs money and what does not

The roster builder, its calendar files, texts and exports are free and stay free. Only the branded pack is a planned paid extra, a one-off price rather than a per-employee monthly plan. It is not on sale yet: there is no checkout, so nothing here can charge you. The sample is an invented café.

Who it is for

Owners and managers of cafés, salons, shops and daycares with a handful of staff who want a clean schedule without paying per seat. Nothing you type is uploaded; the pack is built in your browser. The code is MIT licensed.
