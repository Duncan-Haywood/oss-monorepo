Analyst's notes

  • Daily log returns in percent, 2012-2018, Federal Reserve H.10 via FRED (public domain).
  • The franc change is the Swiss National Bank ending its 1.20 euro floor on 15 January 2015.
  • The pound change is the day after the 23 June 2016 EU referendum.
  • The yen has no change every setting agrees on; outside the franc's January 2015 fortnight and the pound's referendum weeks, the joint fit's changes are shifts in volatility (every other level shift is under 1 noise sd).

Assumptions and limits. Each column is checked alone with six settings of PELT (Killick, Fearnhead and Eckley 2012; level and slope models, with and without a noise floor or a trend with autocorrelated noise), and a change is listed when all six find it; on held-out labelled series this summary was the most reliable one the tool offers. The one segmentation for all columns sums each column's cost, scaled by that column's noise, and treats the columns as independent given the segments; it can cut a smooth, trending or autocorrelated column into steps. A change found in several columns at once is not evidence that one caused another, and a detected change does not show what caused it. 3 columns, 1751 points analysed.
