A change analysis you can forward

The detector tells you when a metric really shifted and how sure to be. The report puts that on your letterhead: a cover with your name and logo, the finding in a sentence, the chart, each change with its 95% range and Bayesian probability, the segments, and the assumptions written down, including that a detected change is not a cause. Print it or save it as a PDF.

What costs money and what does not

The detector, command line, tables and CSV are free and stay free. Only the branded report is a planned paid extra, a one-off price per report pack rather than a monthly plan. It is not on sale yet: there is no checkout, so nothing here can charge you. The sample uses the public-domain Nile flow series and an invented firm.

Who it is for

Analysts, growth and operations people who need to show a manager or client that a metric changed, without sending the data to a vendor. Nothing you type is uploaded; the report is built in your browser. The code is MIT licensed.
