    Effective rate is invoiced revenue this year divided by hours tracked this year for the client, so it is only shown where both exist. Match the client names across tools for the two to line up.

    Questions

    Is Freelancer Desk really free?

    Yes. No account, no trial and no limit. It runs in your browser, so it costs nothing to serve, and it stays free.

    Where does the data come from?

    From the four companion tools on this site: Estimate Maker, Time Tracker, Invoice Maker and Expense Tracker. Each keeps its data in your browser, and this page reads it on this device. It never writes to those tools unless you restore a backup, and nothing is uploaded.

    Do I need all four tools?

    No. Each card says which tool feeds it and links to it. Use one tool or all four; the desk shows what it can see and tells you what is missing.

    What does the at-stake number mean?

    The money that is yours if everything goes right: invoices sent but unpaid, time you have worked but not billed, and the value of estimates still open. Open estimates are valued at the lowest option, the one you can count on.

    How is income and the tax set-aside worked out?

    Income is the paid share of each non-void invoice issued this year, without sales tax and shipping. Net profit is that income minus the deductible expenses in Expense Tracker. The set-aside is the rate you choose times net profit. It is a planning figure, not a tax calculation.

    Can I back everything up in one file?

    Yes. Download one backup with all four tools' data, and restore it on any device. Restoring replaces the data in the tools that are in the file, after you confirm.

    Why does it say sample data?

    If you have not used the other tools yet you can load a made-up freelancer to see what the desk shows. Sample data is held in memory only and is never saved.

    What this does not model

    Not modelled: sales tax owed, state and local taxes, self-employment tax and income tax rules (the set-aside is just your chosen share), multiple businesses, currencies other than a single total, invoices paid in instalments by date (income counts the paid share of invoices issued this year), and estimates or time kept in a different browser. Clearing site data removes the underlying tools' data, so download a backup now and then.
