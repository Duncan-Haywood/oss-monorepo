Common questions

    
Do I need to tell HMRC about side income under £1,000?

    
No. If your gross trading income for the tax year is £1,000 or less, the trading allowance covers it: no tax, no Self Assessment registration and no need to declare it. The £1,000 covers all your trading and miscellaneous income together, not each activity.

    
Should I claim the £1,000 trading allowance or deduct my expenses?

    
Whichever leaves less taxable profit. If your real expenses are under £1,000 the allowance is better and needs no receipts. If they are over £1,000, deducting them usually wins. You cannot claim both on the same income.

    
What if my side income is over £1,000?

    
You must register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the end of the tax year, then file a return and pay by 31 January. Profits over the personal allowance of £12,570 also attract Class 4 National Insurance.

    
Who cannot use the trading allowance?

    
It is not available on income from your employer or a company you or your partner control, or from a partnership you are in.

    
Does this cover Scotland?

    
No. Scottish income tax bands differ; this tool uses the rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent.
