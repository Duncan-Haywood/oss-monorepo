# UK Trading Allowance Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-trading-allowance-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter your side-hustle income, expenses and other taxable income and see whether to claim the £1,000 trading allowance or deduct real costs, the income tax and Class 4 NIC either way, and your Self Assessment registration and filing dates.

After using this, you know which route costs less and whether you must register with HMRC, which used to mean working out two tax computations by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-trading-allowance-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rules were written from memory and must be verified against the source each result names. England, Wales and Northern Ireland only. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
