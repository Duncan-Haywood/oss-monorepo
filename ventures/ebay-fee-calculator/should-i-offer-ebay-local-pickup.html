What this checks

Resellers search “should I offer local pickup on eBay” because skipping the label looks free and the meetup is easy to forget. A 25-minute handoff at $18/hour plus a 20% no-show rate can wipe the postage you saved on a $40 SKU with a $5 label.

On the defaults — $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns if you ship, 70% ship sell-through vs 35% pickup, 20% no-show, 25 minutes at $18/hour — shipping expects $12.68 and pickup expects $3.46. Pickup keeps $9.22 less (−$9.22) on this listing and −$73.77 across eight listings. The outbound label has to rise to about $18.17 before shipping matches pickup. Set the outbound label to $40.00 (a freight box) and pickup expects $3.46 against −$11.82 — $15.28 more.

The bulky case flips harder. A $180 dresser, $85 freight, $8 pack, $45 return postage, and an 8% return rate expects −$6.36 if you ship it at 70% sell-through. The same listing as local pickup at 50% sell-through, a 20% no-show rate, and the same $7.50 meetup expects $33.54 — $39.90 more.

It does not tell you to offer or refuse pickup, and it is not tax or financial advice. When does eBay local pickup beat shipping?
