What this checks

Clothing, card, and small-goods sellers search “eBay labels vs Pirate Ship” and “is eBay shipping cheaper” because a $0.75 cheaper quote looks like free leftover. It is also the path that usually loses eBay seller protection and the minutes you spend buying the label and uploading tracking.

On the defaults — $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 eBay label, $4.25 Pirate Ship quote, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns, 0.8% eBay claims, 2.5% third-party claims, 4 minutes at $18, forty shipments — the eBay label expects $17.82, the timed third-party path expects $16.76, and the same third-party postage with no extra time expects $17.96. Four minutes keeps $1.06 less on this decision and −$42.55 across forty shipments. The postage-only path keeps $0.14 more (+$5.45 / 40). No third-party claim rate on these timed labels can match eBay. The third-party quote has to stay at about $3.19, or extra time has to stay under about 0.5 minutes, before the timed path matches. If they keep the item, leftover is $21.34 eBay versus $20.89 timed third-party.

A higher claim rate or a heavier sunk cost flips the postage-only path too. Keep the same $40 listing, drop extra time to zero, and raise third-party claims to 4%. eBay still expects $17.82; third-party expects $17.42 — $0.41 less. Claims have to stay at about 2.9% before that path matches. A $180 item with $12 shipping charged, $85 cost, a $12 eBay label, a $9.50 third-party quote, and $2 pack expects $56.68 eBay versus $55.32 timed third-party (−$1.37). Third-party claims only have to reach about 1.6% before eBay matches, because 2.5% of a high sunk cost is already more than a $2.50 postage save.

Common questions

Should I buy eBay shipping labels or use Pirate Ship?

A cheaper Pirate Ship, Shippo, or carrier-account quote is not automatically more leftover. eBay labels auto-upload tracking and usually keep a lower item-not-received claim rate. On the calculator defaults ($40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 eBay label, $4.25 third-party label, 10% returns, 0.8% eBay claims, 2.5% third-party claims, 4 minutes at $18) the eBay label expects $17.82, the timed third-party path expects $16.76, and the same third-party postage with no extra time expects $17.96. Four minutes wipes a $0.75 postage save. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to buy a label.

When does Pirate Ship beat an eBay shipping label?

When the quote is cheap enough, the extra upload time is already sunk, and the claim-rate gap stays small. On the same $40 listing, a 4% third-party claim rate with no extra time expects $17.42 against $17.82 on the eBay label. A $180 item with $85 cost and a $12 eBay label versus a $9.50 third-party quote still prefers eBay ($56.68 vs $55.32) because 2.5% of a high sunk cost is already more than the postage save.

Is this the same as shipping insurance or a Standard Envelope?

No. Package protection is an add-on on a tracked label you already bought. Standard Envelope is cheap untracked mail versus a tracked label.
