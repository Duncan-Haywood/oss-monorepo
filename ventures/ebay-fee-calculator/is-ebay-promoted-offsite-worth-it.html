What this checks

Resellers search “is eBay Promoted Offsite worth it” because the campaign looks like Promoted Listings with a bigger reach. It is not. Standard takes a percent only when the listing sells. Offsite bills the campaign even if they bounce.

On the defaults — a $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns, $20 Offsite spend, two extra sales, no extra Promoted Listings on those sales — skip expects $18.11 and the campaign expects $34.33. Offsite keeps $16.22 more (+$16.22) on this campaign and +$64.88 across four campaigns. Extra sales have to stay at about 1.10, or spend has to stay at about $36.22, before Offsite matches skip.

One extra sale flips. Keep the same $20 campaign and get one extra $40 sale. Skip still expects $18.11; the campaign expects $16.22 — $1.89 less. The click you paid for did not cover the bill.

It does not tell you to turn Offsite on, and it is not tax or financial advice. Promoted Listings Standard, combined shipping, Standard Envelope, and a return-rate mix are different questions. Common questions

Is eBay Promoted Offsite worth it?

Promoted Offsite is billed whether the buyer purchases or not. Promoted Listings Standard is a percent of a completed sale. On the calculator defaults (a $40 item, $6 shipping, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% returns, $20 Offsite spend, two extra sales) skip expects $18.11 and the campaign expects $34.33. Offsite keeps $16.22 more. Extra sales have to stay at about 1.10 before Offsite matches skip. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn Offsite on.

When does skipping Promoted Offsite beat the campaign?

When the extra sales do not cover a bill that is charged either way. On the same $40 listing, one extra sale for a $20 campaign expects $16.22 against $18.11 for skip — $1.89 less. The click you paid for did not cover the campaign.

Is Promoted Offsite the same as Promoted Listings Standard?

No. Promoted Listings Standard is a percent taken only when the listing sells. Promoted Offsite is a campaign spend billed even if they bounce. Extra Offsite sales can still pay Standard too if the listing is already promoted — set the Offsite sale promoted rate to match that listing, or to 0 if those extra sales would not also pay Standard.
