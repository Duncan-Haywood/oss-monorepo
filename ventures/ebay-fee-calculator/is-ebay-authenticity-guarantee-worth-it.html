What this checks

Resellers search “is eBay Authenticity Guarantee worth it” because the badge looks free and the hub label is easy to forget. A 3% fee plus $8.00 to the authenticator can wipe the conversion lift on a $40 sneaker even when sell-through jumps from 55% to 80%.

On the defaults — $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns without AG, 3% authenticity fee, $8 extra hub shipping, 55% vs 80% sell-through, 4% AG return rate — skip-AG expects $9.96 and Authenticity expects $8.72. Authenticity keeps $1.24 less (−$1.24) on this listing and −$9.93 across eight listings. Extra hub shipping has to fall to about $6.45 before Authenticity matches. Set extra hub shipping to $0.00 (a prepaid label) and Authenticity expects $15.12 — $5.16 more. Raise the without-AG return rate to 25% and Authenticity wins even with the $8 label.

Common questions

Is eBay Authenticity Guarantee worth it?

Authenticity Guarantee adds a typed fee and often an extra label to the hub. It usually raises sell-through and lowers authenticity-claim returns. On the calculator defaults ($40 item, $6 shipping, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 outbound, 10% returns without AG, 3% authenticity fee, $8 extra hub shipping, 55% vs 80% sell-through, 4% AG return rate) skip-AG expects $9.96 and Authenticity expects $8.72. Extra hub shipping has to fall to about $6.45 before Authenticity matches. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to enroll or skip.

When does eBay Authenticity Guarantee beat skip-AG?

When the extra hub label is prepaid or cheap, or when skip-AG authenticity claims are high. On the same defaults a $0 hub label flips Authenticity ahead by $5.16 per listing ($15.12 vs $9.96). A 25% without-AG return rate also flips Authenticity ahead even with the $8 label.

Does the Authenticity Guarantee fee come back on a return?

This page treats the authenticity fee like a percentage marketplace fee that comes back on a return. The extra hub label does not. Enter the rates from your last payout; this is not eBay’s live Authenticity Guarantee schedule.
