What this checks

Resellers search “should I offer free shipping on eBay” because free-shipping badges look like a ranking cheat until they leave the list at $40 and still buy the label. On eBay, final-value fee sits on item plus shipping, so a $46 free-ship list is cash-identical to $40 plus $6 on a completed sale. Reverb is the opposite — marketplace fee sits on the item only.

On the defaults — a $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns, 55% buyer-pays sell-through, 70% free-ship sell-through — buyer-pays expects $9.96 and baked-in free shipping expects $12.68. Free shipping keeps $2.72 more (+$2.72) on this listing and +$108.66 across forty listings. Free shipping has to convert about 55.0% of the time, or the free-ship list has to stay at about $41.01, before it matches buyer-pays.

Leaving the list at $40 flips. Keep the 70% lift and still buy the $5 label. Buyer-pays still expects $9.96; free shipping expects $9.41 — $0.55 less. Sell-through then has to reach about 74.1%. A $44 list still wins (+$1.63). A 50% free-ship close rate on the baked-in $46 list expects $9.05 — $0.91 less.

It does not tell you to offer free shipping, and it is not tax or financial advice. Local pickup, combined shipping, Standard Envelope, and a quantity discount are different questions. Common questions

Should I offer free shipping on eBay?

On eBay, final-value fee sits on item plus shipping. Folding the exact shipping charged into the item therefore leaves the same completed-sale leftover. The decision is sell-through versus an under-baked list. On the calculator defaults (a $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% returns, 55% buyer-pays sell-through, 70% free-ship sell-through) buyer-pays expects $9.96 and baked-in free shipping expects $12.68. Free shipping keeps $2.72 more. Leave the list at $40 and free shipping expects $9.41 — $0.55 less. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to offer free shipping.

Does eBay charge final-value fee on shipping?

This calculator treats final-value fee as a percent of item plus shipping charged, which is how eBay bills most categories. Baking $6 of shipping into a $46 list therefore leaves the same $18.11 leftover after a 10% return mix as charging $6. Reverb is different: marketplace fee sits on the item only, so folding postage into the list costs extra. Enter the blended rate from your last payout.

What if I offer free shipping and leave the list at $40?

You still buy the $5 label. Revenue drops from $46 to $40, so leftover after returns falls to $13.44. At 70% sell-through that listing expects $9.41 against $9.96 for buyer-pays — $0.55 less. Free shipping then has to convert about 74.1% of the time before it matches. The free-ship list that ties buyer-pays leftover at 70% sell-through is $41.01.
