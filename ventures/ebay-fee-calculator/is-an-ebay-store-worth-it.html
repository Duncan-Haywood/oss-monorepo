Is an eBay store worth it?

Resellers search for this because a store subscription is a fixed monthly bill, while the promised saving is a slightly lower final value fee on every order. If you do not sell enough of those orders, the subscription is just another cost.

This page does not publish eBay’s live store prices. It uses the same example assumptions as the eBay fee calculator so you can replace them with the rates on your invoice.

Worked example: $40 item, $6 shipping

Order total $46. No-store final value fee 13.25% plus $0.30. You paid $12 for the item, $5 for the label, and $1 for packaging. Net profit on that sale is $21.61 before any store subscription.

A Starter example at 12.95% and $7.95 / month saves $0.138 per order ($5.96 vs $6.10 of final-value fee). Break-even is 58 orders like this one. At the calculator’s default 40 orders / month the store still costs $2.43.

Insertion credits, promoted-listings discounts, and selling limits are outside this arithmetic. Open the calculator, type your real rates and monthly volume, and compare the store table against staying put.
