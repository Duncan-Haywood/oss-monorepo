Should you accept this eBay Best Offer?

Resellers search “should I accept this eBay offer” because the inbox number is not the cash they keep. Final value fee, the per-order fee, promoted listings, and international fees all move with the sale price, so a $6 discount never costs only $6.

On the calculator’s worked example — a $40 item plus $6 shipping, 13.25% final value fee, $0.30 fixed, and $18 of cash in the item — list leaves $21.61. A $34 offer still leaves $16.40 (41% margin), $5.21 less than list. If you only have a 50% chance of later selling at list, waiting is worth $10.80. The offer wins.

A $16 offer still clears break-even ($0.79) but misses a 20% target margin. Counter $21.42 to hold that target. A $15 offer loses money after fees — decline it.

Rates change by category, store subscription, and seller status. This page is not eBay’s fee schedule. Open the eBay fee calculator, type the buyer’s number and your guessed chance it still sells at list, and read accept vs wait on your own costs.

The calculator is arithmetic, not advice. It does not tell you to accept or decline. It does not model a later, lower offer if you wait.
