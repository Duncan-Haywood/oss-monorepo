What this checks

Card, sticker, and small-collectible sellers search “is eBay Standard Envelope worth it” because a $0.66 envelope looks like free money next to a $5 Ground or First Class label. An item-not-received claim on an untracked envelope can wipe the sourcing cost you thought you had saved.

On the defaults — $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns, $0.66 envelope, $0.35 rigid mailer, 8% claims, 90% tracked sell-through vs 85% envelope — the tracked label expects $16.30 and the envelope expects $19.71. The envelope keeps $3.41 more (+$3.41) on this listing and +$136.39 across forty listings. Claims have to stay at about 18.2%, or envelope postage has to rise to about $4.67, before the envelope matches the tracked label.

A vintage card flips. Keep an $80 list, charge $0 shipping, pay $40 to source it, use a $4.25 tracked label with 2% returns, and keep the $0.66 envelope at 8% claims. The tracked label expects $21.07 and the envelope expects $18.88 — $2.19 less. Even free envelope postage cannot match the label on those rates. Claims have to stay at about 4.3%.

Common questions

Is eBay Standard Envelope worth it?

Standard Envelope saves most of the outbound label, then takes an item-not-received claim rate because there is no tracking. A claim is not a return: the buyer is refunded and the item is gone, so cost of goods is lost. On the calculator defaults ($40 item, $6 shipping, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% returns, $0.66 envelope, $0.35 rigid mailer, 8% claims, 90% tracked sell-through vs 85% envelope) the tracked label expects $16.30 and the envelope expects $19.71. The envelope keeps $3.41 more. Claims have to stay at about 18.2% before the envelope matches the label. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to use the envelope.

When does a tracked eBay label beat Standard Envelope?

When the item is expensive enough that an item-not-received claim eats the sourcing cost. On an $80 vintage card with $40 cost, $0 shipping charged, a $4.25 tracked label, 2% returns, and the same 8% envelope claims, the tracked label expects $21.07 and the envelope expects $18.88 — $2.19 less. Even free envelope postage cannot match the label on those rates.

Is an eBay Standard Envelope claim the same as a return?

No. A tracked return usually restocks the item; you eat the outbound label and return postage. An envelope item-not-received claim refunds the buyer and leaves the item gone, so cost of goods is lost. Percentage fees usually come back on the case; that refund rate is an input, not eBay’s seller-protection policy.
