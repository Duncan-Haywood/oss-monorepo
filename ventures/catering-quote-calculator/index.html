Common questions

Does this connect to a POS or accounting system?

No. It reads the cost lines you paste and never contacts Square, Toast, QuickBooks or any supplier. Every figure is arithmetic on the lines you gave it, so nothing here leaves your device.

How is the per-guest price calculated?

Total cost divided by (1 minus your target margin minus your overhead share), then divided by the guest count. Per-guest lines scale with the guests, flat and staff lines do not, which is why a bigger event costs less per head.

What are the three line types?

guest is a cost per person (food, plates), with a waste allowance added; flat is a cost for the whole event (van, rentals, permits); staff-hour is total staff hours times the hourly rate, with a payroll-burden allowance added.

What is the break-even guest count?

The number of guests at which the price you name covers the fixed costs after overhead. Below it the event loses money, so it is the number to put in a minimum-guest clause.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The browser page runs on the text in your own browser and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
