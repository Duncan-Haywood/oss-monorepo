This calculator vs. Podcorn

Podcorn is a self-serve podcast sponsorship marketplace: you create a show profile, brands browse and send offers (or you pitch them directly), and Podcorn handles matching, contracting, and payment for a commission on the value of each deal it processes.

That makes Podcorn the right choice for finding brands you don't already have, and running the whole matching, contracting, and payment process through a marketplace. This page answers a narrower question: for a sponsor you already have -- your own network, a cold pitch, a returning advertiser -- what should you actually charge, free, with no profile and no cut of what you negotiate directly.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Podcorn?

They solve different problems. Podcorn is a marketplace: you create a show profile, brands browse and send sponsorship offers (or you pitch them), and Podcorn handles matching, contracting, and payment in exchange for a commission on the deal. This page is a free calculator: no profile, no brand discovery, no commission. You enter your average downloads per episode, niche, and read type, and it gives you a price range to quote a sponsor you already found yourself -- through your own network, a cold pitch, or a returning advertiser.

Does Podcorn price the same way this calculator does?

Not exactly the same inputs. On Podcorn, podcasters typically set their own asking price per deliverable and brands accept, counter, or pass, with the platform's own market data informing what a competitive ask looks like. This calculator has no marketplace data to draw on; it applies commonly cited CPM-per-1000-downloads benchmark ranges to your own downloads, niche, and read type instead, so the range is grounded in published market rates for your show's numbers rather than what Podcorn's brand pool happened to offer this quarter.

Is Podcorn free?

Not to transact through. Podcorn does not charge podcasters to create a profile, but marketplaces like it commonly take a percentage-based commission on the value of a booked deal -- check Podcorn's current published terms for the exact rate, since fee structures change. This calculator has no listing, no deal flow, and takes no cut of anything -- it is a free static page that only computes a price range.
