How much does Poshmark take per sale?

Poshmark publishes one schedule with two rules, not a single percentage. Sales under $15.00 pay a flat $2.95 fee. Sales of $15.00 or more pay 20% of the sale price. That is a real cliff — the whole price is taxed under whichever rule applies, not just the part above $15 — and it is why "Poshmark takes 20%" is only true above the line.

The formula

commission = price < $15.00 ? $2.95 : 20% × price

payout = price − commission

Poshmark's flat buyer-paid shipping fee is not part of this at all — it funds a prepaid label and Poshmark takes no commission on it, unlike a marketplace that commissions the checkout total.

Worked example 1: Poshmark's own $50 sale

LineAmount
Sale price$50.00
Commission (20% of $50)$10.00
Payout$40.00

This is the number on Poshmark's own "How Selling Works" page: sell for $50, keep $40.

Worked example 2: a $10 item — the flat fee is a worse deal than it looks

LineAmount
Sale price$10.00
Commission (flat fee, under $15)$2.95
Payout$7.05
Effective rate29.5%

29.5%, not 20% and not "a small flat fee." The cheaper the item, the worse this gets: a $5 item pays $2.95 on a $5 sale — an effective 59% rate.

The break-even price: $14.75

Twenty percent of $14.75 is exactly $2.95. That makes $14.75 the price where the flat fee and the percent rate cost the same. Above $14.75 (and still under $15.00) the flat fee is a bargain against 20%. Below $14.75 the flat fee costs more than 20% would, and the gap widens fast as price drops — which matters most for jewelry, accessories, and other low-price closet items where $2.95 is a large share of the sale.

Common questions

How much does Poshmark take on a $50 sale?

Poshmark's own published example: a $50 item pays 20% commission, which is $10.00, leaving a $40.00 payout. That is the standard rate for any sale of $15.00 or more.

How much does Poshmark take on a $10 sale?

$2.95 — the flat fee that applies under $15.00, regardless of how far under. On a $10 item that is a 29.5% effective rate, well above the 20% rate that applies at $15 and up.

What is the Poshmark break-even price?

$14.75. Twenty percent of $14.75 is exactly $2.95, the flat fee. Above $14.75 (and below $15) the flat fee is cheaper than 20% would be. Below $14.75 the flat fee costs more than 20% would, and the gap widens the cheaper the item gets.
