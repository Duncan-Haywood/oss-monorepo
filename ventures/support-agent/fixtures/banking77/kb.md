---
title: When will my card arrive?
slug: card-delivery
---
Cards are posted within 2 working days of ordering. Delivery usually takes 3 to 5 working days in the UK and up to 10 working days elsewhere.

## Tracking your card

The app shows the date your card was posted under Card > Card status. If it has not arrived 10 working days after it was posted, order a replacement from the same screen and we will cancel the first card.

=====
---
title: Activate your new card
slug: activate-card
---
When your card arrives, open the app, go to Card and tap Activate. Enter the 6-digit code printed on the letter that came with the card.

The card works for online payments straight away. Contactless starts working after your first chip and PIN payment.

=====
---
title: Change your PIN
slug: change-pin
---
You can see your PIN in the app under Card > Show PIN.

To change it, put your card in any ATM that offers PIN services, choose PIN services and then Change PIN. The new PIN works straight away.

=====
---
title: PIN blocked after too many attempts
slug: pin-blocked
---
Your PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts in a row.

To unblock it, open the app, go to Card > Unblock PIN, then make a chip and PIN payment or use an ATM with the correct PIN.

=====
---
title: Add money with a debit or credit card
slug: top-up-by-card
---
To add money with a card, tap Add money, choose Card and enter the card details. The money appears in your account instantly.

## Fees for card top-ups

Topping up with a UK debit card is free. Topping up with a credit card, or with a card issued outside the UK, costs 0.5% of the amount.

=====
---
title: Add money by bank transfer
slug: top-up-bank-transfer
---
You can add money by sending a bank transfer from another account in your name. Find your account number and sort code (or IBAN) under Account > Details.

Transfers in pounds usually arrive within 2 hours; international transfers can take up to 5 working days.

## Fees for bank transfer top-ups

Adding money by bank transfer is free. The bank you send from may charge its own fee.

=====
---
title: Top-up limits
slug: top-up-limits
---
You can add up to £10,000 a day and £30,000 a month, and hold up to £50,000 in your account.

The limits apply to card top-ups and bank transfers together. Higher limits are available once you have verified your source of funds.

=====
---
title: Exchange rates and currency exchange fees
slug: exchange-rates
---
We use the interbank exchange rate, updated every few seconds while markets are open.

## Fees

Exchanging up to £1,000 a month is free. Above that, a 0.5% fee applies. At weekends, when markets are closed, a further 1% is added to protect against rate changes.

## Exchanging money in the app

To exchange money, tap Exchange, pick the two currencies, enter an amount and confirm. The money moves between your currency balances straight away.

=====
---
title: Virtual and disposable cards
slug: virtual-cards
---
A virtual card is a card number for online payments that lives only in the app. To get one, go to Card, tap New card and choose Virtual. It is free and ready straight away.

## Disposable virtual cards

A disposable virtual card gets a new card number after every payment, which protects you on sites you do not trust. You can have one disposable card at a time, and each payment with it can be up to £1,000.

=====
---
title: Verifying your identity
slug: verify-identity
---
To verify your identity, open the app and tap Verify. Take a photo of your passport, driving licence or national ID card, then take a selfie. Most checks finish within 10 minutes.

## Why we ask

The law requires us to confirm who our customers are before they can use the account. This protects you and us against fraud and money laundering.

=====
---
title: Forgot your passcode
slug: passcode
---
If you forgot the passcode you use to open the app, tap Forgot passcode on the sign-in screen. We will send a code by text message to your phone number, and then ask you to take a selfie before you choose a new passcode.

=====
---
title: Close your account
slug: close-account
---
To close your account, first move your money out. Then open the app, go to Account > Settings and tap Close account.

Your card stops working as soon as the account is closed. Closing an account is free.

=====
---
title: Change your personal details
slug: edit-details
---
You can change your address, email and phone number in the app under Account > Personal details.

To change your name, for example after marriage, send us a photo of a document showing your new name from the same screen.

=====
---
title: How long transfers take
slug: transfer-timing
---
Transfers in pounds to UK accounts arrive within 2 hours, usually within seconds.

Transfers in euros to SEPA countries arrive within 1 working day. Other international transfers take 2 to 5 working days, depending on the currency and the receiving bank.

=====
---
title: ATMs and cash withdrawal fees
slug: atm-cash
---
You can withdraw cash at any ATM that accepts Visa, in any country.

## Fees

Withdrawals up to £200 a month are free. After that, each withdrawal costs 2% of the amount. Some ATM operators charge their own fee, which the ATM shows before you confirm.

=====
---
title: Card about to expire
slug: card-expiry
---
We send you a new card automatically about 4 weeks before your card expires. It has the same card number, a new expiry date and a new security code.

Activate the new card when it arrives. Your old card keeps working until its expiry date.

=====
---
title: Order a physical card or a spare card
slug: order-card
---
To order a physical card, go to Card, tap New card and choose Physical. The first physical card is free; each extra card costs £5 for delivery.

You can have up to 3 physical cards at once on the same account, for example a spare card for travel.

=====
---
title: Contactless not working
slug: contactless
---
Contactless starts working after your first chip and PIN payment with a new card.

There is also a limit on contactless payments: after £100 of contactless spending in a row, the terminal will ask for your PIN once. If contactless still fails, check that it is switched on under Card > Security.
