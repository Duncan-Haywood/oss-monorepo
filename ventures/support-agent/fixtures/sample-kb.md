---
title: Shipping times and costs
slug: shipping
---
We roast on Mondays and Thursdays and ship within one working day of roasting.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery in the US takes 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days.

## Costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $35. Below that it costs $5.95. Express shipping costs $12.

=====
---
title: Track your order
slug: track-order
---
When your order ships we email you a tracking link from USPS or UPS.

You can also see the tracking link under Account > Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

If your parcel has not arrived 7 working days after it shipped, write to us and we will send a replacement or look into it with the carrier.

=====
---
title: Returns and refunds policy
slug: returns
---
Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

Opened coffee cannot be returned, but if you did not enjoy a coffee tell us and we will suggest another one and credit your next bag.

Refunds are issued within 5 working days of us receiving the return. Return postage is paid by the customer unless the item was faulty.

=====
---
title: Damaged or wrong item
slug: damaged-item
---
If a bag arrives torn, crushed or is not what you ordered, send us a photo of the bag and the label within 14 days of delivery.

We will send a replacement at no cost. You do not need to return the damaged item.

=====
---
title: Pause or skip a subscription delivery
slug: pause-subscription
---
You can skip the next delivery or pause your subscription for up to 3 months.

1. Sign in and open Account > Subscriptions.
2. Choose Skip next delivery, or Pause and pick a restart date.
3. Changes made before 9am Eastern on a roast day apply to that roast.

=====
---
title: Cancel a subscription
slug: cancel-subscription
---
You can cancel any time with no fee.

1. Sign in and open Account > Subscriptions.
2. Click Cancel subscription and confirm.

A bag that has already been roasted for you will still ship. You can restart a cancelled subscription from the same page.

=====
---
title: Change how often your coffee arrives
slug: change-frequency
---
Subscriptions can deliver every 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks.

To change the frequency, open Account > Subscriptions, click Edit next to Frequency and pick a new interval. The next delivery date updates straight away.

=====
---
title: Grind options
slug: grind
---
Every coffee is available as whole bean or ground for espresso, filter, French press or moka pot.

We recommend whole bean and grinding just before brewing for the freshest cup. You can change the grind for a subscription under Account > Subscriptions > Edit.

=====
---
title: Change your delivery address
slug: change-address
---
Open Account > Addresses to add or edit an address, then choose it as the default for your subscription.

If an order has already shipped, the address cannot be changed. Write to us within an hour of ordering and we will try to update it before it ships.

=====
---
title: Payment methods
slug: payment-methods
---
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.

To update the card used for your subscription, open Account > Payment and click Replace card. We never store your full card number; payments are handled by our payment processor.

=====
---
title: Reset your password
slug: reset-password
---
On the sign-in page click Forgot password and enter your email address. We will email you a reset link that works for 60 minutes.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then try again after 10 minutes.

=====
---
title: Change your email address
slug: change-email
---
Open Account > Profile, enter the new email address and click Save. We send a confirmation link to the new address; the change takes effect when you click it.

=====
---
title: Gift cards and gift subscriptions
slug: gifts
---
Gift cards come in $25, $50 and $100 and are sent by email. They never expire.

A gift subscription is prepaid for 3, 6 or 12 months and does not renew automatically. Enter the gift card code at checkout to redeem it.

=====
---
title: Discount codes
slug: discount-codes
---
Enter a discount code in the Promo code box at checkout. Only one code can be used per order.

Codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed, and they cannot be combined with gift subscriptions.

=====
---
title: International shipping
slug: international
---
We ship to Canada, the UK and the EU. International delivery takes 7 to 14 working days and costs $18 per order.

Import duties and taxes may be charged by your country when the parcel arrives and are paid by the customer.

=====
---
title: Contact us and opening hours
slug: contact
---
Our team answers email Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern. We aim to reply within one working day.
