Common questions

Why doesn't the stock I don't sell count as a cost of the event?

Because it is still yours. A caterer who over-preps throws food away, but a maker who brings 60 candles and sells 22 drives home with 38 candles and sells them at the next market or online. So the materials that belong in one event's break-even are the materials in what actually sold. Counting the whole table against one weekend is how a fine event gets recorded as a bad one. The stock figure is used only to express the break-even as a sell-through percentage, so you can see what share of the table has to move.

Why is my break-even higher than the booth fee?

Because sales are not profit. Every dollar you take arrives with materials already spent on it and a card fee taken out of it, so only part of each dollar is left to cover the booth. At 30% materials and a card mix around 2.6% plus 10c, roughly 68c of each dollar is available — so a $370 day of fixed cost needs about $547 of sales, not $370. The calculator shows the kept-per-dollar figure it used.

What is the most a booth should cost?

That depends on what you expect to sell there and what you want your own hours to earn. Given both, the calculator solves for the booth fee at which the day lands exactly on your target hourly rate — that is the number to compare against an application before you send it. If it comes out negative, the expected sales cannot clear your target rate even at a free stall, which is a fact about the event's sales rather than about its organiser.

Why does the average order value change the answer so much on cheap items?

Your processor's fixed fee is charged per transaction, not per dollar. Ten cents on a $6 sticker sale is 1.7% of it; the same ten cents on an $80 print is 0.1%. A headline "2.6% + 10c" hides that difference completely, so a sticker table and a print table with identical percentages keep very different shares of each dollar. The calculator converts the fixed fee into a rate using your average order value, which is why that field matters more than it looks.

Does anything about my event or my sales get uploaded?

No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only, there is no account, and nothing you type is uploaded. Nothing leaves this device unless you choose to send it: the Copy shareable link button puts the figures on the form into the page's URL fragment, which a browser never sends to a server or into a Referer header, so it reaches only the person you hand the link to. Anyone who has that link can read the numbers in it, so send it the way you would send the numbers themselves. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
