GTFS FEED CHECK · EVERY CHECK, AND WHERE ITS RULE COMES FROM

GTFS validation errors explained

Each of the 88 finding codes the GTFS feed checker can report, what it means and its source. Reference means the GTFS Schedule Reference, revised 2026-04-27, read for this tool on 2026-11-19. This tool's threshold means a sanity limit the reference does not set, chosen loose so a hit is worth a look. Convention means housekeeping, not a rule.

Errors break a rule a consumer may reject the feed for. Warnings are probably wrong. Notes are worth knowing.

What four real feeds turned up

Checked on 2026-11-19 with the same engine (full output and file hashes):

  • Google's GTFS sample feed (2007): expired since 2010-12-31; 13 short rows in stop_times.txt; a header field drop_off_time that should be drop_off_type.
  • BART: no errors. feed_info.txt ends 2026-08-30 while the calendar runs to 2027-01-10.
  • Halifax Transit: no errors. Service ends 2026-11-29; 153,548 times carry a leading space; 60 routes print black on pure blue (2.44:1).
  • TriMet: no errors in 2.6 million stop times; 70 stop names end in a space; one route has no trips.

These are format checks, not a judgement of anyone's service.
