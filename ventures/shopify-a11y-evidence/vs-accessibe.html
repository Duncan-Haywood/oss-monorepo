This tool vs. accessiBe

Two different shapes: accessiBe installs an ongoing widget and audit subscription on your live storefront. This page formats an axe-core scan you already ran into a dated, portable evidence record, free and local — no widget, no installation, nothing shipped to your customers.

What each product is for

accessiBe sells accessWidget, a JavaScript widget installed on a live storefront that applies automated interface adjustments and AI-driven remediation aimed at WCAG conformance every time a visitor loads the page, alongside audits, a generated accessibility statement, and ongoing monitoring — described on accessiBe's own site as a paid, ongoing subscription product. It is built for a merchant who wants an installed layer running on the storefront itself, plus a vendor relationship for audits and monitoring over time. accessiBe's own current pricing and contract terms are not repeated here, since this session cannot verify today's figures against accessiBe's site; only its well-documented product shape — an installed widget plus an audit/monitoring subscription — is described.

This tool does none of what accessWidget does. It installs nothing on a storefront, changes nothing a customer sees, and has no ongoing subscription. It takes the axe-core JSON export you already generated somewhere else — the axe DevTools browser extension, the axe-core CLI, or a similar tool — and formats it into a dated, readable evidence record: violation count, affected nodes, passing rules, and items needing manual review. It runs entirely in your browser, needs no account and no site access, and uploads nothing you paste.

Common questions

Should I use this tool or accessiBe?

They are different shapes of product. accessiBe sells accessWidget, a JavaScript widget installed on a live site that applies automated interface adjustments and AI-driven remediation aimed at WCAG conformance, plus audits, an accessibility statement, and ongoing monitoring — described on accessiBe's own site as a paid, ongoing subscription that runs on your storefront every time a visitor loads it. This page does none of that. It has no widget, installs nothing on your storefront, and changes nothing a customer sees. It takes an axe-core JSON export you already generated somewhere else and turns it into a dated, readable evidence record in your browser — violations, passes, and items needing review — that you can copy or download for a client file or a remediation ticket. A merchant who wants an installed layer that adjusts the live storefront on every visit, plus ongoing audits and a vendor accessibility statement, is buying a different product than a merchant who wants a free, private record of one automated test.
