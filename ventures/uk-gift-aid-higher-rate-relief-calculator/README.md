# UK Gift Aid Higher Rate Relief Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-gift-aid-higher-rate-relief-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for UK higher and additional rate taxpayers who give to charity: enter the donation and your income to see what the charity receives, the relief you can reclaim, whether carrying the gift back to last tax year pays more, and whether a gift would win back a tapered personal allowance.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-gift-aid-higher-rate-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rates were written from memory and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
