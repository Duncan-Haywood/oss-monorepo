# Australia Sole Trader Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2025-26 / 2026-27)

Free, private browser tool: enter ABN business income and expenses and get resident income tax, the 2% Medicare levy, the small business income tax offset and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags the $75,000 GST threshold and PAYG instalments, and takes an optional personal super deduction.

After using this, an Australian sole trader knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill after 30 June.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/au-sole-trader-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
