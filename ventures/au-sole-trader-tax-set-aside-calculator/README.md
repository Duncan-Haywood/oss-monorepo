# Australia Sole Trader Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2025-26 / 2026-27)

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/au-sole-trader-tax-set-aside-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter business income and expenses and get income tax, Medicare levy, an optional HELP repayment and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags PAYG instalments and the $75,000 GST registration line.

After using this, an Australian sole trader knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill after lodging.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/au-sole-trader-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
