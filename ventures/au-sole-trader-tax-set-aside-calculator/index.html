Common questions

    
How much of my sole trader income should I set aside for tax in Australia?

    
A common planning range is 20% to 30% of profit once you add income tax and the Medicare levy. Use your income and expenses here for a number built from the actual rates.

    
What are the tax rates for sole traders in Australia?

    
Sole traders pay the same resident rates as everyone: nothing up to $18,200, then 16% (15% from 1 July 2026) to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above that, plus the 2% Medicare levy.

    
Do sole traders have to pay super?

    
No. You are not required to pay super for yourself. You can make personal contributions and claim a deduction, up to the concessional cap.

    
When must I register for GST?

    
When your GST turnover reaches $75,000. GST you collect is not your income, so keep it out of your set-aside.
