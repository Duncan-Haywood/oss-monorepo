Common questions

    
Does this connect to my AWS account?

    
No. It reads the text you paste and never contacts AWS or any server. It cannot see your real bill, reserved instances, Savings Plans or region, so every dollar figure is an estimate from an editable list-price table.

    
Are the prices accurate?

    
They are editable on-demand list-price estimates for common db.t3, t4g, m5, m6g, m6i, m7g, r5, r6g, r6i and r7g classes, about us-east-1 single-AZ for open-source engines. Edit the table to match your region, engine and discounts; Oracle and SQL Server licence costs are not modelled.

    
Is one week of CloudWatch stats enough to resize an RDS instance?

    
It depends on your cycle. Sample a representative period that includes your busiest day, month-end or batch window, and use peak and p95 values rather than averages. Resizing causes a restart or failover, so verify in a non-prod window first.

    
Is my pasted text uploaded anywhere?

    
No. The browser page analyses the text in your own browser and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads local files or stdin and makes no network call.
