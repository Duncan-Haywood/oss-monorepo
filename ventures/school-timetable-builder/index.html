School Timetable Builder

Type the lessons each class has in a week. Get a timetable where no class, teacher or room is double-booked, doubles stay inside a block, teachers' days off are kept and gaps are squeezed out. Free, source available, and nothing leaves your browser.

How to write it
  • days: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri and periods: 6 set the week.
  • breaks after: 2, 4: double lessons never span the break after period 2 or 4.
  • rooms: Lab 1 (lab), Lab 2 (lab), Gym (gym): rooms, with a type in brackets. Lessons without a room are taught in the class's own base room.
  • 7A: Maths / Okafor / 5: class, subject, teacher, periods per week.
  • 7A: Science / Patel / 4 / 2 doubles / lab: two double periods, in any lab. / double makes all of them doubles; / room Lab 1 pins one room.
  • 7A+7B: PE / Garcia / 2 / double / gym: two classes taught together. Okafor+Ng puts two teachers on one lesson.
  • teacher Okafor: off Mon 1-2, Fri; max 5 per day and class 7A: off Fri 6: periods off and a daily maximum.
  • Anything after # is a comment.

Who it is for

Deputy heads and office managers at small schools, heads of department who timetable their own subject, tutoring and language centres, and homeschool co-ops. If you are doing the timetable in a spreadsheet, moving one lesson breaks three others, and you only find the clash when two teachers turn up to the same room, this does the search for you. It is not a cover or substitution planner. Scheduling one tutor's private students instead? Try the tutor weekly schedule optimizer; for sports fixtures, the league schedule generator. Worked example and when to use something else.

Questions

What does it guarantee?

When the result says no hard problems, a separate checker has rebuilt the week from the finished timetable and confirmed that no class, teacher or room is in two places at once, nobody teaches in a period you marked off, every double lesson sits inside one block, every lab or gym lesson has a room of that type, and every lesson is placed. If it cannot find such a timetable it lists exactly what still clashes instead of hiding it.

What if my timetable cannot fit?

Before solving, the page counts: a teacher with more lessons than free periods, a class with more lessons than the week, or more lab lessons than lab time is reported with the numbers, because no solver can fix that. It also says when a subject must fall twice on one day, for example five English lessons for a teacher who is off on Fridays.

Is my school's data uploaded?

No. The list you type and the timetable it builds stay in your browser tab. The share link keeps the list in the part of the address after #, which browsers do not send to any server.

How is this different from aSc TimeTables, Untis or FET?

Those are mature products. aSc TimeTables and Untis are paid desktop and cloud suites with substitution planning, drag-and-drop editing and school information system links. FET is free, open source and very powerful, but it is a desktop program with a long learning curve. This page is for a small school, a department or a tutoring centre that wants a correct first timetable in minutes without installing anything; if you need cover planning or hundreds of constraint types, use one of them.

How big a school can it handle?

In our benchmark of synthetic secondary schools where every class is fully booked (30 lessons in 30 periods, with doubles, labs, gyms and teachers' days off), it placed every lesson with no clash for 4 to 40 classes, in about 2 seconds or less each on one 2.1 GHz server core. The script that produces those numbers is in the repository. Real schools have constraints the benchmark does not, so check the result.
