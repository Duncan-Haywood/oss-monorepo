# Scottish Income Tax Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter a gross salary, pension and student loan and see Scottish income tax band by band (2026/27: 19% starter to 48% top), National Insurance and loan deductions, take-home pay per year, month and week, your marginal rate, and what the same salary would pay in the rest of the UK.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-scottish-income-tax-calculator/

After using this, you know what lands in your bank account in Scotland and how much more or less tax you pay than a colleague in England, without a payslip or a spreadsheet.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-scottish-income-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
