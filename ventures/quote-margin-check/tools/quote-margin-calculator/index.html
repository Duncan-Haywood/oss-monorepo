What this checks

Fixed-price quotes usually price the visible delivery work and quietly skip the rest: admin time, revision rounds, overhead, and subcontractor costs. This calculator adds all of it back in and reports the effective hourly rate the job really pays — alongside the price you'd need to charge to hit your own target rate.

It does not recommend a price, provide tax or financial advice, or claim a quote is profitable. Gap to target price and left after direct costs are arithmetic comparisons against numbers you supplied — you decide what costs and target rate are right for the job.

Need to compare several quotes at once, or script this? The same maths ships as a Python CLI with JSON, Markdown, and CSV output, plus a --batch mode for a folder of quotes — see the Quote Margin Check README.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your prices, hours, and client figures are never uploaded — there are no accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics vendor, and no server for them to go to. This page counts anonymous local feature-use events (e.g. "a report was generated") in your own browser's localStorage, only so the maintainers can tell whether the tool gets used at all. It stays on your device.
