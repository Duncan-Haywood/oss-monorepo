    What this checks

    Fixed-price quotes usually price the visible delivery work and quietly skip the rest: admin time, revision rounds, overhead, and subcontractor costs. This calculator adds all of it back in and reports the effective hourly rate you're actually being paid — alongside the price you'd need to charge to hit your own target rate.

    It does not recommend a price, does not provide tax or financial advice, and does not claim a quote is profitable. The gap to target price and after direct costs figures are arithmetic comparisons only — you decide what costs and target rate are appropriate for the job.

    Prefer the command line, or need to batch several quotes into a spreadsheet? The same logic ships as a Python CLI that reads/writes JSON, Markdown, and CSV.
