FOR HOMEOWNERS, BUILDERS AND HVAC CONTRACTORS

How big should the heat pump really be?

Enter the house room by room: the walls and which way they face, the windows, the attic and the floor, how leaky it is. The page works out each room's heating and cooling load, the hour it peaks, the airflow it needs and the whole-house size, from sun angles and heat-transfer physics you can read. It is free and runs in your browser.

How the load is worked out, with the formulas

1. Where and how hot

Design temperatures are the outdoor conditions you size for, not record extremes. Your local code office, an energy-code compliance tool or a weather-data source lists them for your county (the 99% heating and 1% cooling values).

2. The shell

Window U and SHGC are on the NFRC sticker. ACH50 comes from a blower-door test (about 3 for a new code-built house, 7 to 15 for older ones). Ducts inside the conditioned space lose 0%; ducts in a vented attic commonly 10 to 25%.

3. Rooms

List the walls that face outdoors, with the direction each one faces and the window and door area in it. Interior walls don't count. The slab perimeter is the length of exposed slab edge under that room.

Questions

Is this a Manual J?

No. ACCA Manual J is a proprietary procedure with its own tables, and many permits ask for a report from approved software. This tool uses an open first-principles method (sun geometry, U-values, air leakage, psychrometrics) with every formula shown. Use it to sanity-check a quote or a design before paying for a full report, not in place of one.

Why is the answer smaller than 1 ton per 500 square feet?

Because rules of thumb date from leaky, poorly insulated houses. A house with R-13 walls, an R-38 attic and low-e windows often needs one ton per 700 to 1,000 square feet. Oversized equipment short-cycles, removes less humidity and costs more.

Why is the whole-house load less than the sum of the rooms?

Rooms peak at different times: east rooms in the morning, west rooms late in the afternoon. The equipment sees the hour when the sum is largest, which is smaller than adding every room's own worst hour. Ducts and room airflow are sized from each room's own peak.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser. An optional anonymous counter in this browser's local storage records only that a calculation ran and the day.
