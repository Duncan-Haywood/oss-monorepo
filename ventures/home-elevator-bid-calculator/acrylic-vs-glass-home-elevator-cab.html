Acrylic vs glass home elevator cab

Owners shop the $18,995 pneumatic home elevator installed special, then ask: “Can we do glass instead of the tube?” That sounds like a finish upgrade. It is a cab invoice and more set hours. The cheap special is an acrylic through-floor tube. Glass is a different ticket on the same house.

The tube is not a glass cab

On a typical two-stop through-floor cheap special — $52 wage, 32% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, ten miles — acrylic bids $28,108.09 (walk-away $22,486.47). A standard enclosed cab adds $2,834.17. Wood-panel adds $5,044.01. Glass adds $8,354.52 and bids $36,462.61 (walk-away $29,170.09). Those add-ons are the cab kits. They do not change the drive or the shaft.

Matching the acrylic number loses the cab ticket

Common questions
