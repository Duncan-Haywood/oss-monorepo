FOR ACCESSIBILITY AND ELEVATOR CONTRACTORS

Bid the stops, the shaft, and the extras.

Price a residential home elevator the way the trade actually loses money: a two-stop pneumatic through-floor tube versus a three-stop hydraulic in a new wood shaft with a pit, a 240 V feeder, and a state inspection — not the published $18,995 pneumatic special. You get the job price, what it works out to per stop, and the number below which the install loses money.

The house

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a unit invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing drive, hoistway, or cab fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the drive, hoistway, and cab you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not an elevator catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how an $18,995 pneumatic special that looked profitable was not.
