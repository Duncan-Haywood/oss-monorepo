Should I expedite a home elevator for hospital discharge?

Families shop accessibility contractors on “Mom comes home Friday — same $18,995 on Saturday?” A home elevator unit ships from the factory. Next-day air is a ticket on top of the unit invoice. Working around someone who just came home is a slower set. Saturday is overtime, a second dispatch, and a weekend inspector. Matching the weekday Facebook special on that rush is how a sold aging-in-place install becomes a loss.

A discharge week is not the weekday bid

On a typical two-stop through-floor cheap special — $52 wage, 32% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, ten miles — weekday they leave bids $28,108.09 (walk-away $22,486.47). Discharge week while they stay, with next-day air freight, bids $32,645.87 (+$4,537.78, walk-away $26,116.70). Saturday / after-hours bids $35,919.76 (+$7,811.66, walk-away $28,735.80). Those add-ons are the access kits. They do not change the drive or the shaft.

This is not a stair-lift rail

A stair lift is a chair on a rail. Next-day rail freight is a few hundred dollars. A home elevator is a 400–1,400 lb unit, a hoistway or a floor cut, and an AHJ. Saturday still needs an inspector. Common questions

Should I expedite a home elevator so mom can come home Friday?

Only if the Saturday or discharge-week number still covers cost. On a typical two-stop pneumatic cheap special — $52 wage, 32% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin — a weekday empty-house set bids about $28,108. Discharge week with next-day air freight and a slower occupied set bids about $32,646. Saturday overtime plus a weekend inspector bids about $35,920. Matching the published $18,995 pneumatic dump loses money on every window.

Is a hospital-discharge home elevator the same bid as a weekday install?

No. A weekday slot is factory ground freight, an empty house, and a weekday inspector. Discharge week is next-day air on a 400–1,400 lb unit plus a slower set while they stay home. Saturday is overtime, a second dispatch, the same air ticket, and a weekend inspector. A stair-lift rail is a $485 next-day crate. A home elevator is not that crate.

What happens if I match the $18,995 special on a Saturday discharge?

You eat the air freight, the overtime, and the weekend inspector. Matching $18,995 on the cheap special loses money even on a weekday slot — walk-away after-hours is about $28,736. Sending the weekday $28,108 on Saturday still loses about $628. On a three-stop hydraulic shaft the after-hours walk-away is about $82,729.
