# Solomon VRPTW benchmark, 2026-11-18

`vrptw.mjs` (string-removal ruin and recreate with simulated annealing, after
Christiaens & Vanden Berghe 2020) on all 56 Solomon instances of 100
customers, scored against the best-known solutions (BKS) under the DIMACS
convention: Euclidean distances truncated to one decimal, travel time equal to
distance, objective total distance, fleet capped at the instance's vehicle
count. Instances and BKS route files: [PyVRP/Instances](https://github.com/PyVRP/Instances)
`VRPTW/Solomon` (MIT; BKS from CVRPLIB). Not committed here; `eval/solomon.mjs`
says how to fetch them.

**Checks before any number below was kept.** Every BKS route set was re-scored by
`verify()` (windows, capacity, depot hours, each customer once) and matched its
file's `Cost` line on all 56 instances, so the reader and the rounding agree with
the published figures. Every solver plan passed the same `verify()`: 56 of 56
feasible, every customer served.

**Run:** `node eval/solomon.mjs DIR --iterations 200000 --seeds 1 --jobs 4`, one
seed, Node 22 on a 4-core cloud container, four instances at a time.

| **all** | **56** | **0.44%** | **6.43%** |

26 of 56 equal the BKS; none beats it. Mean time 14.2 s per instance (8.5 to
21.2 s). A 5,000-iteration run (0.3 s) on six instances gave a mean gap of 1.85%.

**Where it loses.** C204 (4.95%) and C206 (6.43%) end with 4 routes where the BKS
uses 3: in the clustered, wide-window C2 set, dropping a whole route takes more
than the 10-customer strings the ruin step removes, so the search settles one
vehicle too many. A route-elimination phase (empty the shortest route, reinsert
with a penalty on the unplaced, as in Nagata & Bräysy's route minimisation) is the
next item. Elsewhere the gap is under 1.3% and mostly under 0.5%.

**What this is not.** One seed per instance, not the 10-seed average papers report;
the BKS come from the best published methods (hybrid genetic search and
others), and this engine has not been run beside one here, so read it as a good
heuristic, not the state of the art. Running PyVRP on the same files and budget
is the fair comparison still to make. The instances are
100 customers; the Gehring & Homberger sets (200 to 1,000) are untested.
