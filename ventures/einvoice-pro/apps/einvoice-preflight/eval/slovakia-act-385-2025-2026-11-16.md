## What Article VIII puts in force when

- **1 January 2026**: everything not listed below, including § 76a (delivery
  service, the register, certification) and § 85o(18) to (22) (the 2026
  voluntary year: no consent needed for an e-invoice sent through a delivery
  service to a domestic business that can receive one).
- **1 January 2027**: Article I points 23 to 27, point 31 § 71(5), point 35
  § 72(12), points 41, 56, point 69 § 85o(1) to (16) and (23), point 70;
  Article II points 2 and 4; Article III points 4 to 6; Articles V and VII.
- **1 July 2030**: Article I points 7 to 12, 17, 19 to 21, 28 to 30, point 31
  § 71(4) and (6), points 32, 33, point 35 § 72(9) to (11), points 36 to 40,
  42, 43, 45 to 48, 50, 53 to 55, 58 to 60 and point 69 § 85o(17) and (24).

So the domestic B2B duty of 2027 is the transitional § 85o, and the general
"an invoice is an e-invoice" rule (§ 71(4)) is 2030.

## § 85o, as read (from 1 January 2027 to 30 June 2030)

- (2) A payer under § 4, 4b or 4c supplying goods (§ 13) or services (§ 15,
  § 16) with a Slovak place of supply to a person under (3), or receiving a
  prepayment for it, must issue an e-invoice; not for supplies exempt under
  §§ 28 to 43 and 47 or simplified invoices under § 74(3)(a), (b). No consent
  to issue; consent to send other than by delivery service. Must not e-invoice
  supplies to the Slovak Information Service, Military Intelligence or
  involving classified information.
- (3) Persons: a taxable person with a seat, place of business, establishment,
  residence or usual abode in Slovakia; a non-taxable legal person with a seat
  or fixed establishment there.
- (4) E-invoice: § 74(1) particulars; automated-processing format; data
  structure under the standard and its syntax list (footnote 28s:
  Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/1870).
- (5) Corrections are e-invoices too, carrying the original number and the
  changed data, and go by delivery service if the original did.
- (6), (7), (8) Within 15 days of supply, prepayment or the base correction
  event; a monthly summary e-invoice within 15 days of the month's end.
- (9), (10), (11) Reporting: the supplier at issue (or within 5 days if the
  buyer self-bills); a Slovak payer buyer within 5 days of receipt; both done
  by handing the invoice to the delivery service.
- (12) to (14) Up to EUR 10,000; repeat up to EUR 100,000; no fine for an
  obvious error corrected or a proven outage at the provider.
- (15) Keep e-invoices 10 years from the end of the year.

Also from 2027: § 71(5) (everyone obliged to issue must be able to send and
receive by delivery service; everyone supplied where an e-invoice is due must
be able to receive) and Act 215/2019 § 2 (public contracting authorities must
receive by delivery service; procurement invoices carry the notice number);
§§ 3 to 6 of 215/2019 are repealed.

From 1 July 2030: § 71(4) default e-invoice, § 71(6) simplified invoice (up to
EUR 100; eKasa or fuel-machine receipt up to EUR 400), § 73 10-day deadline,
§ 74(1)(p), (q) (original number; supplier accounts), § 80 cross-border and
§ 80a domestic reporting (§ 78a control statement repealed), § 49(11)
deduction only with an e-invoice for supplies reported under § 80a.

## The Slovak field table, and what became a rule

Terms the table marks "vyžaduje sa" where EN 16931 has 0..1, or where the
note adds a Slovak condition:

| Term | EN 16931 | SK table | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| BT-35, BT-37, BT-38 seller street, city, postcode | 0..1 | required, § 74(1)(a) | SK-001 |
| BT-50, BT-52, BT-53 buyer street, city, postcode | 0..1 | required, § 74(1)(b) | SK-002 |
| BT-34-1, BT-49-1 electronic address scheme | 1..1 (Peppol) | "0245 = DIČ" for Slovakia | SK-003 (warning, Slovak parties only), SK-004 (0245 id not 10 digits) |
| BT-84 payment account | 0..1 | "Číslo účtu dodávateľa je POVINNÉ" | SK-005 (warning: the Act adds it to § 74(1) only from 1 July 2030) |
| seller DIČ vs SK VAT number | - | - | SK-006 (warning; a VAT group has its own IČ DPH, so it can differ) |

BT-34 and BT-49 themselves are already PP-003/PP-004 in the Peppol profile,
which `profile=slovakia` also runs.

Left out, on purpose:

- "Podmienene vyžadované" terms whose condition a file cannot show: BT-30 and
  BT-47 (IČO "if one was assigned"), BT-31 and BT-48 (IČ DPH "if assigned"),
  BT-7 (tax point date, if it can be determined), BT-6 (VAT accounting
  currency), the tax representative block.
- BT-33 (legal form and register entry under the Commercial Code § 3a): the
  table asks for it in a free-text field; no reliable test.
- The tax data document (docs.peppol.eu/tdd/sk/tdd-sk/) a delivery service
  sends; not read this run.
- The UBL sheet "UBL Pravidlá" (750 rows still marked TODO in v1.13).
