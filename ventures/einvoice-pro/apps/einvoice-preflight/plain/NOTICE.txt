# Rules in this folder

The `*.json` files are the official e-invoicing Schematron rules, compiled to JSON
by `../build-plain-rules.mjs`. They are derived works of:

- `en16931-*.json`: ConnectingEurope/eInvoicing-EN16931, EUPL-1.2.
- `xrechnung-*.json`: itplr-kosit/xrechnung-schematron, Apache-2.0.
- `peppol-*.json`: OpenPEPPOL/peppol-bis-invoice-3, MPL-2.0.

`manifest.json` has the upstream commit each file was built from.
`fontoxpath.esm.js` is fontoxpath 3.34.0 (MIT, `fontoxpath.LICENSE.txt`) bundled with its
dependencies xspattern 3.1.0 and prsc 4.0.0 (both MIT, copyright 2019 Stef Busking;
`xspattern.LICENSE.txt`, `prsc.LICENSE.txt`, added 2026-11-19 from the npm packages).

The pint-*.json sets are OpenPeppol PINT Schematron (Singapore, Australia/New Zealand; MPL-2.0), read from the compiled XSLT in com.helger.phive.rules:phive-rules-peppol (Apache-2.0) on Maven Central by build-pint-rules.mjs.

The zatca-*.json sets are ZATCA's e-invoice validation rules (rules version 3.4.6; the ZATCA e-invoicing toolkit SDK, LGPL-3.0), read from the compiled XSLT in com.helger.phive.rules:phive-rules-zatca (Apache-2.0) on Maven Central by build-zatca-rules.mjs. They are converted data files; the LGPL text is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt.

The pint1-*.json sets are the current OpenPeppol PINT releases (Singapore 1.4.1, Australia/New Zealand 1.1.3), read from the compiled XSLT in com.helger.phive.rules:phive-rules-peppol-pint 4.6.4 (Apache-2.0) on Maven Central by build-pint-rules.mjs. That artefact's NOTICE names OpenPeppol's PINT repositories (https://github.com/OpenPeppol/PINT and its jurisdiction siblings) and https://docs.peppol.eu/poac/ as their origin; the licence text of those upstream repositories was not re-read for this build (the older PINT sets above are MPL-2.0).

`cius-hr-ubl.json` is Croatia's HR-CIUS 2025 with the HR extension (eRačun, Fiskalizacija 2.0): the Porezna uprava's HRUBLSchematron of 13.03.2026 (numbered 1.0.4 by phive), read from the compiled XSLT in com.helger.phive.rules:phive-rules-eracun 4.6.4 (Apache-2.0) on Maven Central by build-hr-rules.mjs. Source: Ministarstvo financija, Porezna uprava, Republika Hrvatska, https://porezna.gov.hr/fiskalizacija/bezgotovinski-racuni/eracun. No licence is stated for the rules; the site says its content may be reproduced without special permission with the source named ("Sadrzaji s ovih stranica se mogu prenositi bez posebne dozvole uz navodjenje izvora"), which this note does.

`cius-fr-ubl.json` and `cius-fr-cii.json` are France's CTC e-invoicing rules (BR-FR, AFNOR XP Z12-012, version 1.4.0.04 of 2026-09-04), FNFE-MPE's Schematron from https://github.com/fnfempe/France_RFE (the repository's LICENSE is Apache-2.0; the .sch header says "Licensed under European Union Public Licence (EUPL) version 1.4.0", both permit this redistribution with attribution), compiled by build-fr-rules.mjs. The Schematron was written by Quentin Houard and Cyrille Sautereau for FNFE-MPE. Their rule messages are in French, as published.
