Common questions

    
How much is Statutory Maternity Pay in 2026/27?

    
SMP is 90% of average weekly earnings for the first 6 weeks, then the lower of the standard weekly rate (about £194.32 from April 2026) and 90% of earnings for 33 weeks, 39 weeks in all. Check GOV.UK for the current rate.

    
Who qualifies for SMP?

    
An employee with 26 weeks of continuous employment ending with the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth, earning at least the lower earnings limit on average in the 8 weeks to that point.

    
When can SMP start?

    
No earlier than 11 weeks before the expected week of childbirth.

    
What if the employee does not qualify?

    
They may be able to claim Maternity Allowance from Jobcentre Plus.
