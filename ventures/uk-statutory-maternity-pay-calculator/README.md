# UK Statutory Maternity Pay calculator (2026/27)

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-statutory-maternity-pay-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter the expected due date, average weekly earnings and employment start to see the qualifying week, the 26-week and earnings tests, the week-by-week 39-week SMP schedule with April uprating, and the matching 2-week Statutory Paternity Pay.

After using this, a small employer or bookkeeper knows whether an employee qualifies and exactly what to pay each week, which used to mean reading several GOV.UK pages and a spreadsheet.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-statutory-maternity-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
