This calculator vs. MoeGo

Two different shapes: MoeGo is account-based business management software for running a grooming business's whole scheduling-to-payment workflow. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one groom cost, given its size, coat, matting, and add-ons, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

MoeGo is cloud-based business management software built for pet grooming professionals. Its feature set covers 24/7 online booking, appointment scheduling and smart-schedule automation, digital intake forms with e-signatures, a two-way client messaging hub, automated reminders, integrated payment processing, memberships and packages, a client portal, and reporting, with route optimization and map-based scheduling built for mobile groomers specifically. It was founded in late 2019 by Ethan Dong and is headquartered in Culver City, California; it closed a $24 million Series A funding round led by Base10 Partners, with Mars Petcare's Digitalis Ventures also participating, and by 2025 reported serving thousands of pet care businesses across more than 69 million appointments. It is sold as an ongoing monthly subscription, priced per van for mobile groomers or per location for a shop, across tiered plans (reported publicly as roughly $49–$159 per month per van, or $79–$239 per month per location, depending on tier and add-ons such as SMS volume) — this page states no dollar figure as current pricing on MoeGo's own behalf, since plan prices change; see MoeGo's own pricing page for today's numbers.

This calculator does none of what MoeGo does. It does not run a booking calendar, hold a client or appointment record on a server, or process payments. It takes one groom — a dog or cat's size and coat type, matting severity, a checklist of add-ons (nail trim, nail grinding, teeth brushing, ear cleaning, de-shedding, flea/tick treatment, anal gland expression), optional mobile travel time and mileage, hourly wage and payroll burden, supplies, job overhead, payment processing fee, and target margin — and turns that into the contribution left after costs, the break-even quote, the price that hits your target margin, the effective hourly rate, plus a printable customer proposal with scope, deposit split, and a signature line. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. It also does not decide whether an animal is fit or safe to groom — see the calculator's own FAQ on that.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or MoeGo?

They do different jobs. MoeGo is cloud-based business management software built for pet grooming professionals — online booking, scheduling, digital intake forms, two-way client messaging, route optimization for mobile groomers, and integrated payment processing — sold as an ongoing monthly subscription per van or per location. This calculator does none of that. It takes one groom — size, coat, matting severity, add-ons, optional mobile travel, loaded wage, supplies, overhead, and payment fees — and turns that into the contribution after costs, the break-even quote, the price needed to hit a target margin, plus a printable customer proposal, instantly, in the browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A grooming business running scheduling, client communication, and payments across every groomer and every appointment is better served by MoeGo or a comparable platform; a groomer who wants an instant, private, correctly-sized price for one job before it's quoted is in the right place here.
