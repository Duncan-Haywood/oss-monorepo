# Pet Grooming Bid Calculator

The free pet grooming bid calculator shows the minimum quote and target-margin price for a dog or cat groom after size, coat type, matting severity, add-ons, loaded hourly wage plus payroll burden, optional mobile travel, supplies, overhead, and payment fees. After using this, you can price a groom that reflects a giant doodle's real coat time and a matted coat's real extra labor, which used to be a guess. A $75 default quote stays in the black on the medium/medium-coat worked example; putting a real wage and burden on a large, curly-coated, lightly matted mobile groom with add-ons can turn a $150 quote into a thin margin. Fee and margin are both slices of the final price — the form names how much of the ticket they already claim, and it will not print a negative quote when they jointly exceed 100%.

**This tool prices a groom. It does not decide whether an animal is fit to be groomed.** A coat matted to the skin can hide cuts, pressure sores, or skin infection that a shave-down can worsen, and matting that advanced often means sedation or a referral to a veterinarian or vet tech rather than a routine groom. A fearful or aggressive animal is a safety call for the groomer, not a pricing input. The calculator lets you select a matting-severity level that drives a legitimate time and price surcharge — matted coats genuinely take longer to work through — but it will not turn a "severe" selection into a quote until you check a separate box confirming you have inspected the skin and judged the animal safe and healthy to groom. **If you are not confident an animal is fit and safe to groom, refer it out rather than pricing it in.** This is a welfare and safety judgment for you or a veterinarian, and this tool makes no such determination on your behalf.

Once the number is right, **Print proposal** turns it into the one-page sheet you hand the customer: your business details, the scope drafted from the job you just priced, the price, a price-held date, and a signature line. Your wage, burden, supplies, overhead, and margin are not on it and are not printed. Print or "Save as PDF" from the browser.

**Copy share link** encodes the whole job — size, coat, matting, every add-on, mobile travel, wage, burden, supplies, overhead, fee, and target margin, plus the deposit/terms from the proposal panel — into a URL fragment you can send to a second groomer, the shop owner, or the customer; nothing is uploaded, and opening the link reopens the same quote instead of the form's defaults. See [`share.mjs`](share.mjs).

Open `index.html` in a browser. It runs without an account, network request, API key, or paid dependency. Numbers are saved only in the browser and can be shared as a URL when you choose to copy one.

## Privacy and usage

Everything runs in your browser. Your prices, costs, and job or pet details are never uploaded. There are no accounts, cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics vendor. Unless you opt out, the browser stores only anonymous event names and the day a feature was used (including `share_link_copied` when you copy a bid link); it never stores inputs in the usage counter. The footer checkbox turns this counter off.

## Opportunity assessment

The target user is an independent or mobile dog/cat groomer pricing a one-off appointment. Generic per-breed price lists and flat "$X per dog" menus commonly ignore coat type, matting severity, and the individual add-ons a specific pet needs, causing underquoted mats and undercharged large/giant breeds. The differentiated result is a transparent, groom-specific quote that shows break-even, target-margin price, and the time/cost breakdown by size, coat, matting, and add-on — while explicitly refusing to let a severe-matting surcharge substitute for the groomer's own safety judgment.

A well-known paid competitor is [MoeGo](https://www.moego.pet/) — cloud-based grooming business management software (booking, scheduling, client messaging, payments, mobile route optimization) sold as an ongoing monthly subscription per van or per location; see [`vs-moego.html`](vs-moego.html) for a sourced, nominative-fair-use comparison. Acquisition can come from searches such as "pet grooming price calculator", "how much to charge for a matted dog", and "dog grooming pricing by size". A later hosted tier could add saved client templates or optional AI explanations using the user's own API key, but the free arithmetic core has zero inference and hosting cost. Risk is concentrated in the welfare/safety judgment call rather than the arithmetic; the severe-matting acknowledgment gate, the explicit on-page disclaimer, and the printed proposal's own disclaimer are the containment for it.
