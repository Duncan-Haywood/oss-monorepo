This calculator vs. the Form 8995-A worksheet by hand

The QBI deduction is arithmetic anyone can do with the IRS's own worksheet. The problem is that the worksheet has a few steps whose order matters, and a quick by-hand pass tends to apply a limit before or after it actually belongs.

Common questions

What's the most common mistake with the taxable-income threshold?

Applying the SSTB phase-out or the W-2 wage/UBIA limit at all when taxable income is still below the threshold for the filing status. Below threshold, the deduction is simply 20% of QBI -- neither limit applies yet, regardless of how low W-2 wages or UBIA are.

What's the most common mistake with the SSTB phase-out inside the range?

Applying the wage/UBIA limit to the FULL W-2 wages and UBIA figures, then just capping the result at $0 at the top of the range. Inside the phase-in range, an SSTB must first scale QBI, W-2 wages, AND UBIA by the same 'applicable percentage' -- and only then apply the 20% and the wage/UBIA test to the scaled-down figures.

What's the most common mistake with the overall cap?

Stopping at the QBI-based amount and never checking the second, independent limit: 20% of taxable income minus net capital gain. On a return with a low taxable income relative to QBI -- for example after large itemized deductions -- this second cap can bind well below what the QBI formula alone would allow.

What's the most common mistake with a business that lost money?

Treating negative QBI from one business as a $0 deduction and stopping there. It is $0 for THAT business this year, but the loss carries forward and reduces next year's QBI from the same business -- a step a quick by-hand estimate often drops entirely.

Does this calculator know my complete tax situation?

No. Neither this calculator nor a by-hand worksheet built from this page knows your full return, or how to combine more than one qualified trade or business. Both apply the same published federal formula to the numbers you enter for one business; verify against the IRS's own Form 8995-A instructions or a qualified tax professional before filing.
