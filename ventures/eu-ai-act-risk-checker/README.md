# EU AI Act Risk Checker

Free, private browser tool: say what your AI system does and what your role is (provider, deployer, importer, distributor) to see whether Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 makes it prohibited, high-risk, transparency-only or minimal, which Article the call rests on, your duties with the dates they apply from, and a downloadable compliance checklist (CSV).

After using this, you know which tier to plan for, which Articles to put in front of your engineers, and what to ask counsel, in minutes rather than in a paid scoping call.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-ai-act-risk-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not legal advice. It reads the Regulation's text and a proposed delay is noted, not applied. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
