Common questions

How do I price Good, Better and Best HVAC repair options?

Price each option the same way: your true cost per billable hour times its hours including the drive, divided by one minus your target margin, plus parts or equipment at a tiered markup, grossed up for a callback reserve and rounded up. On the default numbers that gives $745.00 for Good, $1,480.00 for Better and $8,890.00 for Best. Enter your own figures; the defaults are invented.

What is my true cost per hour as an HVAC contractor?

Your loaded wage spread over the hours you can actually bill, plus van, insurance, tools and overhead over the same hours. On the defaults that is $78.00 for wages and burden plus $35.00 for van, insurance, tools and overhead, $113.00 for every billable hour, because only 1,200 of 2,080 paid hours are sold.

What should an HVAC diagnostic fee cover?

At least the drive and the time on site to find the fault, at true cost and your margin. On the defaults that is 1.5 hours, $169.50 of cost and a $265.00 fee, which many shops credit against the repair the customer approves.

How much does offering three options add to an average call?

On the defaults, with 50, 35 and 15 percent of approvals choosing Good, Better and Best and 20 percent of calls ending at the diagnostic fee, an average call earns $580.14 against $249.82 when only the Good repair is offered, a gain of $330.32. Your mix and decline rate are assumptions to replace with your own ticket counts.
