Common questions

    
How much of my freelance income should I set aside for tax in South Africa?

    
Often 15% to 30% of profit, depending on how much you earn: SARS taxes personal income on a sliding scale from 18% to 45%. Enter your income and expenses here for a number built from the actual brackets, rebates and medical credits.

    
What is provisional tax?

    
The way freelancers and other people with untaxed income pay SARS during the year: two compulsory payments, about half the estimated tax by the end of August and the balance by the last day of February, plus an optional top-up after year end.

    
Do I pay UIF as a freelancer?

    
No. UIF and SDL are employer and employee levies. A self-employed person pays income tax on profit and, when registered, VAT.

    
When must I register for VAT?

    
Compulsorily when taxable turnover exceeds R1 million in any 12 months; voluntarily once it exceeds R50,000. VAT you collect belongs to SARS, so keep it out of your set-aside.
