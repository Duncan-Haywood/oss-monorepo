Enter the equity, the cash the deal distributes each year (sale proceeds in the last year) and your preferred return and promote tiers. You get the LP IRR and multiple, the sponsor IRR, the promote in dollars and as a share of profit, and the split tier by tier and year by year.

Nothing is uploaded. Your numbers are read in this browser and never leave your device — see the privacy note in the footer.

1. The deal

Year 1 is one year after the equity goes in. Use cash after debt service, reserves and fees, before any split between partners.

2. Preferred return and promote tiers

Up to the preferred return, cash goes to all partners pro rata to capital. In each later tier the sponsor takes the promote percentage of that tier's cash and the rest goes to all partners pro rata.

  • Your operating agreement. Real waterfalls differ: GP catch-up, simple rather than compounding pref, hurdles on equity multiple, return of capital before pref, separate classes. The document controls.

Common questions

What is a distribution waterfall?

The order in which a real estate deal's cash is paid out. Typically all partners first get their capital and a preferred return pro rata, then the sponsor (general partner) earns a growing share of each extra dollar, called the promote, as the investors' IRR clears higher hurdles.

What is a preferred return?

A minimum annual return, such as 8%, that investors must receive before the sponsor earns any promote. It usually compounds on unreturned capital, and this calculator compounds it annually.

What is a promote?

The sponsor's share of profits above the preferred return, on top of the share it earns as a co-investor. A common structure is 20% above an 8% pref, rising to 30% above 12% and 40% above 15%.

What is the difference between deal IRR and LP IRR?

Deal IRR is the return of the property before splitting. LP IRR is what passive investors keep after the sponsor's promote. The gap is the cost of the structure to the investor and the sponsor's reward for running the deal.
