New York certified payroll: the NYSDOL bulk-upload XML

From 1 January 2026, contractors and subcontractors on New York Labor Law Article 8 public work file certified payroll electronically with the Department of Labor, at least every 30 days and up to five weekly payrolls per cycle. You can type each week into the portal, or upload an XML file checked against the XSD NYSDOL publishes. There is no API.

Where it goesNYSDOL Electronic Certified Payroll Submissions (the MPWR portal)
Since1 January 2026, all Article 8 public work (Article 9 building service is not included)
How oftenAt least every 30 days; up to five weekly payrolls per cycle
HowOnline entry, or a bulk XML upload on NYSDOL's XSD (version 2.0)

What the NYSDOL file holds that the WH-347 does not

The hours, rates, fringes and deductions are the same numbers as on the federal WH-347. The state's file wraps them with more:

  • The project's PRC number (the prevailing rate case number, 10 characters), which has to match a project the portal already knows.
  • Each worker's address with the state spelled out, not abbreviated.
  • The last four digits of the SSN, or else the date of birth: one of the two, never both.
  • Whether the worker is a New York registered apprentice.
  • For every row, a classification name from NYSDOL's XML Work Classification List (261 names); the portal rejects any other spelling.
  • Deductions by type (state and local income tax, disability and paid family leave, union dues and the rest) and supplements by type per classification, each with its straight-time and overtime hourly amount.

Upload errors to check before you send

  • A prcNumber that does not match an existing project.
  • Field lengths and minimum or maximum values: wage rates up to 999.99, each deduction up to 9,999.99, at most 10 deductions and 50 supplements per worker, at most 500 employees.
  • Date formats.
  • The same employee twice in one payroll, or a worker sent with both an SSN's last four and a date of birth.
  • Bulk upload works only for pay weeks that have no employee entries yet: a week half typed in by hand cannot then be uploaded.

Keep one set of numbers

The usual mistake is retyping the week: once on the WH-347 for the GC, once into the state portal. Two copies drift. Compute the week once, then produce the WH-347 and the state's file from it, so gross, deductions and net agree on both.

Questions

Do I have to file with NYSDOL on a federal job?

The electronic filing covers New York Article 8 public work. A purely federal Davis-Bacon job follows the federal rules; the contract says which apply.

Can I upload several weeks at once?

NYSDOL takes up to five weekly payrolls per 30-day cycle. Each week you upload must not already have employee entries typed in.

Why does the portal reject my classification?

The XML has to use a name from NYSDOL's XML Work Classification List exactly as spelled there.
