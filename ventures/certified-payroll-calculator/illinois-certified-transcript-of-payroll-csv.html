Illinois certified payroll: the IDOL Certified Transcript of Payroll CSV

Contractors and subcontractors on Illinois Prevailing Wage Act work file their certified payroll with the Illinois Department of Labor each month (820 ILCS 130/5), in the Certified Transcript of Payroll (CTP) portal, signed in with an Illinois Public ID. A payroll can be typed in, or sent by Full Import: a CSV in the exact columns of IDOL's own template.

Where it goesIDOL Certified Transcript of Payroll portal (Illinois Public ID login)
How oftenMonthly (820 ILCS 130/5)
HowOnline entry, or a Full Import CSV on IDOL's template
WhoContractors and subcontractors on Prevailing Wage Act public works

What the IDOL CTP file holds that the WH-347 does not

The hours, rates, fringes and deductions are the same numbers as on the federal WH-347. The state's file wraps them with more:

  • 102 columns in the template's order and with its header names, misspellings included (Wednsday, Satday): IDOL's FAQ says the template order cannot be changed.
  • One row per worker per classification worked.
  • Hours by weekday, Sunday to Saturday, whatever day your payroll week ends.
  • Hours and gross on other, non-prevailing projects that week, alongside this project's.
  • Fringe as hourly credits in IDOL's five columns (pension, health and welfare, vacation, training, other insurance).
  • Race, ethnicity, gender and veteran status, which the Act asks for.
  • Project county, the public body, and the contractor's contact, phone and email.

Upload errors to check before you send

  • An address that does not match the project address sends the payroll to "Need Info".
  • Changed, renamed or reordered columns.
  • Dates in a format other than MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Missing required fields: project county, contractor contact, phone and email, the public body.
  • Cash paid in lieu of fringe has no column in the template: check how IDOL wants it shown before you upload.

Keep one set of numbers

The usual mistake is retyping the week: once on the WH-347 for the GC, once into the state portal. Two copies drift. Compute the week once, then produce the WH-347 and the state's file from it, so gross, deductions and net agree on both.

Questions

How often is Illinois certified payroll due?

Monthly, under 820 ILCS 130/5, through the IDOL portal.

Do I file a payroll for a week with no work on the project?

IDOL's FAQ says a week with no work needs no payroll.

My payroll week runs Thursday to Wednesday. Which columns?

Each day's hours go under its own weekday; IDOL's FAQ says to start your hours on Thursday.
