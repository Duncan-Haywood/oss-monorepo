          What it does and does not do

          • Fixed rules, no model. Depreciation, the passive loss allowance and the Schedule E columns are plain arithmetic you can check, to the cent.
          • Residential rentals. 27.5-year straight line, mid-month. Commercial property (39 years), short-term rentals with substantial services, cost segregation, bonus depreciation and sales are not modelled.
          • Federal only. No state rules, no qualified business income deduction, no net investment income tax, no real-estate-professional status, no at-risk limits.
          • Repairs versus improvements is your call. The page flags large repairs; you decide, with a preparer when the amount is large.
          • No bank sync, no tenant screening, no rent collection. That is the trade for being free and private. Import a CSV instead.

          Is this rental ledger really free?

          Is anything uploaded?

          No. Your properties, rents and expenses are stored in your browser on this device and every sum is done there. There are no accounts, cookies or analytics vendors.

          Is this a replacement for Stessa, Baselane or a spreadsheet?

          It is a lighter, private alternative for a landlord with a handful of units who wants Schedule E by property without connecting a bank. There is no bank sync, no tenant portal and no rent collection. You enter or import rows, and it does the Schedule E, depreciation and passive-loss arithmetic.

          How is depreciation calculated?

          Residential rental property is depreciated straight line over 27.5 years with the mid-month convention: the month you place it in service counts as half. The basis is the price plus closing costs you add to basis, minus the land value, because land is never depreciated. Each improvement is depreciated separately from its own date.

          What is the $25,000 rental loss allowance?

          If you actively participate in managing your rental, you can deduct up to $25,000 of rental loss against other income. It shrinks by 50 cents for every dollar your modified adjusted gross income is over $100,000 and is gone at $150,000. Anything you cannot use is suspended and carried forward, which this page tracks.

          Is a security deposit income?

          Not when you receive it, if you will return it. Enter it as a deposit so it stays out of rent. It becomes income in the year you keep it for unpaid rent, and a deduction for damage you repair is a normal expense.

          Is a new roof a repair?

          Usually not. Work that betters, restores or adapts the property is an improvement that you depreciate, not a repair you deduct in one year. The page flags large repairs so you can decide, and items of $2,500 or less per invoice can often be expensed under the de minimis safe harbor if you elect it on your return.

          Can I import from a spreadsheet or my bank?

          Yes, from a CSV with the columns date, property, type, category, amount, vendor and note. Most banks let you export a CSV; add the property and type columns in a spreadsheet and import it. Rows that cannot be read are listed with the reason instead of being dropped silently.
