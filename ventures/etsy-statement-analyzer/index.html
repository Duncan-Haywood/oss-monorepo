The model, and what it leaves out

  • Each row counts by its Net cell (or Amount if there is no Net column). Sales, refunds and every fee type are grouped by the row’s Type and Title. Deposits to your bank are payouts and are excluded; sales tax is excluded.
  • Take rate is Etsy’s own charges, less credits, divided by sales. Shipping labels are shown separately.
  • Per listing uses orders that contain exactly one item, found through the “Transaction fee: item” line, and adds that order’s sale, refund and fee rows by order number.
  • Profit is what Etsy paid you on those orders minus unit cost × orders. A listing without a cost has no profit shown.
  • Not modelled: materials and labour you have not entered, packaging, taxes, currency conversion, and anything outside the exported months.

Common questions

Can I see my real Etsy fees and profit from the payment account statement?

Yes. In Shop Manager go to Finances, Payment account, Monthly statements, download the CSV for the month (or several months, pasted together) and drop it here. The tool adds up sales, refunds and every fee line (transaction, processing, listing, Offsite Ads, Etsy Ads, regulatory, shipping labels) from the file. Nothing is uploaded; the file is read in your browser.

How is the take rate worked out?

Etsy's own charges (transaction, processing, listing, advertising, regulatory fees, less credits) divided by sales in the same file. Shipping labels are shown separately because they are usually paid for by the buyer inside the sale amount.

How does the tool find Offsite Ads charges?

Rows titled Offsite Ads Fee are grouped and matched to their order by order number, so you see the total, the number of orders and the share of sales. Etsy charges 12% or 15% of the order total on those sales.

Why are some orders missing from the per-listing table?

The statement names an item only on the transaction fee line. Orders with exactly one item are attributed to that listing. Orders with two or more different items are counted in the totals but not attributed, because the file does not say which item earned which sale.

Where do unit costs come from?

You. Etsy's statement has what Etsy paid you, not what your materials and labour cost. Type a cost per unit next to a listing or paste lines like Listing title,cost. Profit is left blank for a listing without a cost rather than counted as free.

What does the tool not see?

Materials, labour and packaging you have not entered, shop-level costs, taxes and income tax, sales tax handling, buyer-paid shipping separated from item price, currency conversion, and anything outside the months you exported. Treat the result as an estimate from the file, not your books.
