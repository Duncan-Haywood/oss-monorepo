This calculator vs. Workiz

Two different shapes: Workiz is an account-based field service management platform for running a locksmith company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one service call cost, with the after-hours premium and minimum service charge floor built in, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Workiz is a field service management platform, founded in 2015 by Idan Kadosh, Erez Marom, and Saar Kohanovitch and headquartered in San Diego, California. Two of its founders worked as locksmiths before building it. It serves locksmith, junk removal, carpet cleaning, garage door, appliance repair, and other on-demand service trades with scheduling, dispatch, estimates, invoicing, and communication tools (including AI call handling) across a whole business, with a mobile app for technicians in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. Workiz's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software serving the locksmith trade among others, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Workiz appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Workiz does. It does not schedule calls, dispatch technicians, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes labor and travel minutes, a target hourly rate, an after-hours/emergency premium applied to billed time only (never to parts, since a cylinder costs the same at 2am as at 2pm), a flat trip fee, parts cost and markup, a callback/warranty reserve, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price for one call and the minimum service charge floor below which a short job — a five-minute car unlock, a single cut key — would lose money once drive time and overhead are counted. A second section checks whether a day's worth of calls, at that average profit, actually clears a target daily income, and how many more calls close the gap if it does not. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Workiz?

They do different jobs, and a mobile locksmith of any size will likely end up touching both eventually. Workiz is a field service management platform, founded in 2015 by three former locksmiths (Idan Kadosh, Erez Marom, and Saar Kohanovitch) and headquartered in San Diego, California, used by locksmith, junk removal, garage door, appliance repair, and other on-demand trades to run scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and communication across a business, sold as an ongoing subscription account. A locksmith who already schedules calls, dispatches technicians, and invoices across a whole business is better served by Workiz or a comparable field service platform; a locksmith who wants an instant, private answer to what one call should cost before it is quoted — with the after-hours premium and the minimum charge built in — is in the right place here.
