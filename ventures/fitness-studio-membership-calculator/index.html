Questions people ask

What is a good churn rate for a boutique fitness studio?

It varies a lot by format, price and how members join, so this tool does not claim a benchmark. Use your own cancellations divided by members at the start of the month, averaged over a few months. The tool then shows what each point of churn is worth in profit.

How do I calculate member lifetime value?

Multiply what a member contributes each month after card fees and variable costs by the expected number of months they stay, which is 100 divided by monthly churn. At 6% churn the average member stays about 17 months. Lifetime value here is contribution, not revenue, and is not discounted.

What is a good LTV to CAC ratio for a studio?

Many owners aim for at least 3 to 1, but that is a rule of thumb, not a standard. The tool shows your ratio and the months it takes a new member to repay what you spent to win them, using your marketing spend divided by new members.

Why does the member count stop growing?

Each month a share of members leaves and a fixed number joins, so the count settles where joins equal churned members: new members per month divided by monthly churn. Lowering churn raises that ceiling, which is why retention often beats more ads.
