Bid the opening, the cut, and the extras.

Price an egress window the way the trade actually loses money: a vinyl hopper into an existing hole with a plastic well versus a casement in a new poured-wall cut with a stone well, a drain, a cover, a ladder, and a dirt haul — not the published $1,800 installed special. You get the job price, what it works out to per window, and the number below which the install loses money.
