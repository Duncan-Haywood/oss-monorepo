This calculator vs. JobNimbus

Two different shapes: JobNimbus is account-based CRM and project software for running an exterior-trades business across leads, jobs, and crews. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this egress-window job cost, given its opening type and cut, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

JobNimbus is business-management CRM and project software founded in 2013 and headquartered in Lehi, Utah. It began as roofing-industry software and has since expanded across roofing, solar install, and other exterior-renovation trades, combining lead management, estimating, job scheduling, production tracking, and a customer portal into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every crew. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. JobNimbus's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — exterior-trades CRM and project-management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for JobNimbus appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what JobNimbus does. It does not manage leads, schedule crews, track production across jobs, or store any customer record on a server. It takes one egress-window job — window count, opening type (hopper, slider, casement, fiberglass), whether the opening is a retrofit into an existing hole or a wood, masonry, or poured-concrete cut, well material, extras, who supplies the sash and well, and whether the crew works a weekday, an occupied-basement day, or a Saturday or after-hours call — and turns that into the extra-work share and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, before any of it touches a CRM. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
