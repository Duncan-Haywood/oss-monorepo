Why does my text bill as multiple SMS segments?

Providers charge per segment, not per message. A one-line reminder can bill as three segments because of a single curly apostrophe. Paste your message into the calculator to see its encoding, segment count and the exact characters responsible.

How many characters fit in one SMS segment?

160 if every character is in the GSM-7 set, 70 if any character is not. Once a message is split into several segments, 153 and 67 fit in each, because a few bytes of every segment carry the join header.

Which characters force UCS-2?

Curly quotes and apostrophes, en and em dashes, the ellipsis character, non-breaking spaces, emoji and non-Latin letters. Plain straight quotes, hyphens and three full stops are all GSM-7.

What do the characters in braces and brackets cost?

The characters ^ { } \ [ ] ~ | and the euro sign are in the GSM-7 extension table and each counts as two characters, though the message stays GSM-7.

How do I stop my phone or editor adding curly quotes?

Compose the template in a plain-text editor, turn off smart punctuation in your word processor, and run the final text through this calculator before sending it to a provider.
