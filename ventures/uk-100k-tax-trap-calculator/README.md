# UK £100k Tax Trap Calculator

Free, private browser tool for UK earners between £100,000 and £125,140: see your real marginal rate (about 62% with NI), the pension contribution that restores your personal allowance and childcare support, and what it truly costs out of pocket.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-100k-tax-trap-calculator/

After using this, you know whether a pension contribution that "costs" £10,000 really costs you £3,800, which used to mean rebuilding the allowance taper and NI in a spreadsheet.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-100k-tax-trap-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
