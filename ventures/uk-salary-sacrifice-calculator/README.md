# UK Salary Sacrifice Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-salary-sacrifice-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter a salary and a pension percentage and see the same contribution paid by salary sacrifice, net pay arrangement and relief at source (2026/27 rates), with income tax, National Insurance and student loan under each, the employer NI saving, the announced April 2029 cap on NI-free sacrifice, and the marginal rate on your next pound.

After using this, you know what your pension really costs in take-home pay, and whether your employer's salary sacrifice scheme is worth taking.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-salary-sacrifice-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
