Should I expedite a stair lift for hospital discharge?

Families call with a weekday number: “Mom comes home Friday. Can you put it in Saturday? Same $3,995?” That sounds like the same stair. It is not the same freight ticket and it is not the same crew day. A stair-lift rail ships from the factory. Next-day air is a starting expedite on top of the rail invoice. Working around someone who just came home is the same wage on a slower set. Saturday is time-and-a-half plus a second dispatch, because a Friday discharge is not a stop you combine with a weekday route.

Discharge week is not the weekday bid

A weekday slot still carries the wage and the ground freight already in the rail invoice. Discharge week adds a starting next-day rail ticket and clock hours on the rail, the set, the chair, the attach, and the punch-list. On a typical cheap special — $42 wage, 30% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, eight miles — the weekday bid is $5,333.37. Discharge week is $6,190.67. After-hours Saturday is $6,692.99. The walk-away jumps from $4,266.69 to $5,354.39. Taking $4,500 to “just do Saturday at the weekday number” covers the weekday slot and loses $854.39 after hours.

A curved heavy-duty rail loses even on the weekday

The rush special is more tempting when the stair is already a custom bend and they want it before discharge. The weekday bid is $20,983.85. Discharge week is $22,046.79. After-hours is $23,222.14 and the walk-away is $18,577.71. Taking $2,495 to “just hang something this weekend” still loses money — even on the weekday.

This is not a customer-supplied lift or a walk-in tub

Customer-supplied lifts are their Facebook unit and an install-only number — see should I install a customer-supplied stair lift. Discharge week is when the rail can arrive and who is in the house. A walk-in tub Saturday is alcove plumbing overtime. A toilet Saturday is an overflowing bowl. This prices a stair lift: the rail, the expedite ticket, and the extras.

How to use the comparison

Open the stair lift bid calculator, pick the cheap-special or losing-job preset, and put their Saturday number in “price you’re considering.” The access table scores that number against a weekday slot, discharge week while they stay home, and Saturday / after-hours. Nothing is uploaded.

This is the same arithmetic as how much to charge to install a stair lift. It is not a medical-discharge plan, a labor-law determination, or a reason to refuse a customer — it is the walk-away number on each access window.

Common questions

Should I expedite a stair lift for hospital discharge?

Only after you price next-day rail freight plus a slower occupied set, or Saturday overtime plus a second dispatch. A $3,995 Facebook special on a straight indoor rail sits under that walk-away number even on a weekday slot.

Does a discharge-week install change the bid?

Yes. Same wage, slower rail, set, chair, and punch-list, plus a starting next-day rail ticket. On a typical straight indoor rail the discharge-week bid is $6,190.67 versus $5,333.37 when they can wait for a weekday slot and ground freight.

Can I take the weekday $4,500 number on Saturday?

Usually not on the job they actually want. On a cheap straight indoor rail the after-hours walk-away is $5,354.39. $4,500 covers a weekday slot and loses $854.39 after hours. On a curved heavy-duty job, $2,495 still loses money even on the weekday.
