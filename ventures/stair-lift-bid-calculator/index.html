Bid the rail, the chair, and the extras.

Price a stair lift the way the trade actually loses money: a straight indoor rail with a standard seated chair on wood treads versus a curved custom-bent rail with a heavy-duty power-swivel chair, open-riser attach, a folding rail, and a dedicated circuit — not the published $3,995 installed special. You get the job price, what it works out to per lift, and the number below which the install loses money.

The stair

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a rail invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing rail, chair, or attach fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the rail, chair, and attach you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a lift catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $3,995 straight-rail special that looked profitable was not.
