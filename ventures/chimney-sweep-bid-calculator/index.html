The stop

Stories and roof access multiply the sweep hours. Creosote, inspection, and animal work add time per flue. None of this is a CSIA, NFPA 211, or fire-safety determination — it is a time budget you can edit.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation (roof work is not cheap), liability insurance, and paid time off. A sweep paid $28 often costs more than $36 on the truck. Underestimating that is how a $199 special loses money.
