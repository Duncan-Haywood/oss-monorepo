    Common questions

    What does this chimney sweep estimate include?

    The price adds up sweep hours per flue — hours each × quantity × the story multiplier — then a roof-access percentage on top of the whole on-site stop, creosote, inspection, and animal-removal time per flue, any add-ons you enable (rain cap, damper, smoke test), setup/breakdown, and round-trip travel. Consumables, glazed-creosote chemical, add-on parts, and a trip fee are added as cost lines, then overhead and your target margin.

    Why do stories and roof access change the price differently?

    The story multiplier (1.0 / 1.35 / 1.75) applies only to the base sweep hours per flue — climbing higher takes longer to sweep, but doesn't change how long the creosote, inspection, or animal-removal work takes once you're up there. Roof access (steep, snow/ice) instead adds a percentage on top of the whole accessed on-site total, because a steep or icy roof slows every minute of the stop, not just the climb.

    Why does the on-site total sometimes not match my hours?

    A minimum on-site hours floor applies whenever there's at least one flue: if your calculated hours come in under that minimum, the tool bills the minimum instead and marks it “(minimum)” next to on-site hours — the same logic that protects a quick gas-fireplace sweep from being priced as if the truck, ladder, and drop cloths cost nothing to bring out.

    Why does glazed creosote add a chemical cost but light and moderate don't?

    Creosote condition adds sweep time at every level (light adds none, moderate and glazed add more per flue), but the chemical-cleaner cost line only triggers at “glazed / stage 3” — that's the condition where a mechanical sweep alone typically isn't enough and a chemical treatment gets added to the job, not lighter creosote levels.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No account and no network requests — the calculator runs entirely in your browser. This calculator doesn't save your quote between visits on its own; instead, “Copy share link” encodes only the allowlisted quote fields (flues, stories, access, creosote, add-ons, your rates, deposit and validity terms) into the page's own URL hash, which is never sent to a server — treat a link you copy like your rate sheet. Only your business name and contact details for the printed proposal are kept in this browser's local storage.

    Does this tell me if a chimney or flue is safe to use?

    No. Hours and cost figures are ballpark shop numbers you can edit, not a CSIA, NFPA 211, or insurance inspection, and a sweep plus visual inspection is not a guarantee the flue is free of defects — concealed damage can require further investigation. It is not a carbon-monoxide or gas-safety test either: a gas fireplace or furnace flue priced here still needs its own appliance and venting safety check from a qualified technician.
