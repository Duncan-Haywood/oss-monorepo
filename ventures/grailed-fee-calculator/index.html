Grailed's commission is not a flat 9% any more.

Grailed charges sellers a 6% commission on sales under $120 (minimum $1.99) and 9% on sales of $120 or more, set on 2026-05-20. Source: third-party fee guides that agree with each other; not read from Grailed's own help centre. Payment processing is extra. As of 2026-10-25.

Since 20 May 2026 the commission is 6% under $120 (minimum $1.99) and 9% from $120, plus 3.49% + $0.49 payment processing. Because the tier is a step, a $120 sale can pay you less than a $119.99 one.       Common questions

      How much does Grailed take per sale?

      Two fees, nothing else. A commission of 6% on sales under $120 (minimum $1.99) or 9% on sales of $120 and over, plus payment processing of 3.49% + $0.49 on a domestic sale. On a $100 sale that is $6.00 + $3.98, so you keep $90.02. There is no listing fee, subscription or buyer fee.

      Why does a $120 sale pay less than a $119.99 sale?

      Because the commission tier is a step, not a slope: at $120 the 9% rate applies to the whole sale, not just the part above $120. A $119.99 sale keeps $108.11 while a $120.00 sale keeps $104.52, and you need to list at about $124.10 to net the same as $119.99. Pricing just under $120, or well clear of it, avoids the dead zone.

      Is Grailed still a flat 9% commission?

      Not since 20 May 2026, according to the fee guides this tool is built from: sales under $120 moved to 6% (with a $1.99 minimum) and sales of $120 and over stayed at 9%. Many older articles and fee calculators still show a flat 9%, which overstates the fee on cheaper items.

      Does the commission include shipping?

      It includes shipping the buyer pays you directly (your own label). With a Grailed-provided label the buyer's shipping goes to the carrier, so the commission is on the item price alone. Enter buyer-paid shipping only if you collect it yourself; the amount is added to the sale before the tier and the processing fee are worked out.
