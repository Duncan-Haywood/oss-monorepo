Vendor Change Evidence vs. UpGuard

Different scopes: UpGuard continuously scans, scores, and questionnaires a whole portfolio of vendors for security risk. This page compares two snapshots you already captured of one vendor's policy page and reports what changed — free, local, no account.

What each tool is for

UpGuard is third-party and vendor risk management software. Its Vendor Risk product continuously scans vendors' external attack surfaces and vulnerabilities, maintains ongoing security ratings, runs security assessment questionnaires built on frameworks such as NIST, ISO, and SIG, and monitors for data exposures — reviewed and severity-rated by UpGuard's own analysts — across an organization's entire vendor portfolio, with alerting and reporting aimed at security teams and boards. It is sold as an ongoing subscription platform, and its own pricing is not something this session can verify, so no figure for it is stated here.

This tool does none of that. It does not scan a vendor's infrastructure, assign a security rating, run a questionnaire, or watch a portfolio on an ongoing basis. It takes exactly two inputs — a before and after JSON snapshot you already captured of one page (its URL, when you captured it, whether the capture succeeded, and its text) — and reports what changed between them: changed, unchanged, or inconclusive when a capture failed, never a false "unchanged". Comparison runs in your browser tab; nothing you paste is uploaded, and the tool never contacts the vendor itself. There is no account, no ongoing scan, and no per-vendor subscription.

Common questions

Should I use this tool or UpGuard?

They cover different scopes. UpGuard is third-party and vendor risk management software: it continuously scans vendors' external attack surfaces, maintains security ratings, runs security assessment questionnaires (including frameworks such as NIST, ISO, and SIG), and detects data exposures across an organization's whole vendor portfolio, with alerting and board-level reporting built in. It is sold as an ongoing subscription platform aimed at security and procurement teams managing many vendor relationships at once. This tool does one narrower thing: you paste two dated snapshots you already captured of a single vendor's privacy, terms, subprocessor, or security page, and it reports what changed between them, entirely in your browser. It does not scan, score, rate, or continuously monitor anything, and it has no concept of a vendor portfolio. A team that needs ongoing, automated coverage across dozens or hundreds of vendors is the case UpGuard is built for; a team that already has two snapshots of one page and wants a fast, free, private diff and a dated record of that one review is the case this tool is built for.
