      Common questions

      How much does Depop take per sale?

      For US and UK sellers, Depop's selling fee is 0%. A payment-processing fee still applies: 3.3% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, plus a flat $0.45, in the US; 2.9% plus a flat £0.30 in the UK. Sellers outside the US and UK still run Depop's older flat 10% selling fee on the item price plus shipping, with no separate processing fee.

      Is Depop really free to sell on now?

      No. The 0% figure is only the selling fee. A separate payment-processing fee (3.3%+$0.45 in the US, 2.9%+£0.30 in the UK) still comes out of every sale, so a typical sale still pays a few percent in total — just less than the old flat 10%, and calculated differently.

      Does Depop's buyer Marketplace fee come out of my payout?

      No. Depop charges the buyer a separate Marketplace fee at checkout (up to 5% + $1 of the item and shipping cost, on a sliding scale Depop has not published in full). It is added to what the buyer pays on top of your price and never reduces what the seller receives.

      How much extra does a Boosted Listing sale cost?

      Depop charges an additional 12% fee, on the item price, on any sale that happened while the listing was boosted — on top of the normal regional selling and processing fees.
