# Bid Price Engine

After using this, you know the price that pays you the margin you need on a
job, the walk-away price below which the job loses money, and how far labor can
run over before it stops paying. One calculator covers every trade.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/bid-price-engine/

Free, no account, runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; a share link keeps the inputs in the address after `#`.

**What it does:** labor (hours at wage plus payroll burden), materials (plus the waste you buy), subcontractors, equipment and travel, then contingency and overhead, then `price = full cost / (1 - margin)`. It shows the walk-away and cash-floor prices, the margin a rival's bid would leave you, a price ladder, and the markup trap (a 25% markup is only a 20% margin). A helper works your overhead % out from last year's books.

**Why use it instead of what you have:** a spreadsheet markup (the usual way, and wrong whenever markup gets typed as margin), a paid field-service app at $10-60 a month, or one of the ~120 single-trade pages in this repo. The trade picker loads starting inputs for that kind of work and links to the specialist calculator for its particulars (`../<trade>-bid-calculator/`), so those pages are the depth and this is the shared engine.

Files: `calc.mjs` (pure arithmetic), `trades.mjs` (generated by `node build-trades.mjs` from the `*-bid-calculator` directories), `index.html`, `markup-vs-margin.html`. Run the tests with `node --test`.
