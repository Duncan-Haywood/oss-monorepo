# India Freelancer Tax Set-Aside Calculator (FY 2025-26)

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/in-freelancer-advance-tax-set-aside-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter professional receipts and expenses and get new-regime income tax with the section 87A rebate and 4% cess, the section 44ADA presumptive scheme compared with actual expenses, the advance tax schedule, and the share of every invoice to set aside.

After using this, an Indian freelancer knows how much to move out of each payment and whether 44ADA would cost less tax, instead of finding a bill at the 15 March advance tax deadline.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/in-freelancer-advance-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
