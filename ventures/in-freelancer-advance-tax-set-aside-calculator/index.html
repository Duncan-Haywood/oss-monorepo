Common questions

    
How much of my freelance income should I set aside for tax in India?

    
Often 5% to 20% of receipts, depending on your profit: under the new regime income up to ₹12 lakh is effectively tax free after the section 87A rebate, and rates then rise from 15% to 30%, plus 4% cess. Enter your receipts and expenses here for a number built from the actual slabs.

    
What is section 44ADA presumptive taxation?

    
A scheme for specified professionals such as consultants, engineers, architects, doctors and lawyers with receipts up to ₹50 lakh (₹75 lakh if at least 95% is received digitally). You declare 50% of receipts as profit without keeping expense records. This tool shows the tax both ways.

    
When is advance tax due for freelancers?

    
If your tax for the year is ₹10,000 or more: 15% by 15 June, 45% by 15 September, 75% by 15 December and 100% by 15 March. Presumptive taxpayers under 44ADA can pay everything by 15 March.

    
When must a freelancer register for GST?

    
When aggregate turnover from services exceeds ₹20 lakh in a year (₹10 lakh in most special category states). GST you collect belongs to the government, so keep it out of your set-aside.
