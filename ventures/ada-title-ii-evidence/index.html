Common questions

Does this evidence record prove my government website is ADA Title II compliant?

No. It formats whatever axe-core found into a dated, readable record split into WCAG 2.1 AA required findings and best-practice extras — it does not run a scan itself, does not check anything axe-core cannot detect (screen-reader usability, keyboard traps, and most manual-review items are outside any automated tool's reach), and does not determine legal compliance. Automated testing typically finds a minority of WCAG issues. Treat this as documentation of one automated test at one point in time, not a compliance certificate.

What compliance date applies to my city, county, school, or special district?

It depends on your entity's population and type. Use the deadline lookup: large state/local government entities (population 50,000+) have until April 26, 2027; smaller entities and any special district government (school, water, fire, transit, or other special-purpose district) have until April 26, 2028, per the U.S. Department of Justice's 2026 rule extension.

Where do I get the axe JSON to paste in?

Run axe-core (the axe DevTools browser extension, axe-core CLI, or a similar automated tool) against your site and export its JSON results — this page only formats output you already generated elsewhere, it does not crawl or scan your site itself.

Does my scan data get uploaded anywhere?

No. The JSON you paste is parsed and formatted entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to a server. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events in localStorage, with an opt-out checkbox above.
