# Paid Sick Leave Accrual Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/paid-sick-leave-accrual-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: pick your state (14 with statewide paid sick leave laws), your headcount, hours and wage, and get the accrual per pay period, the annual use and balance caps, whether leave must be paid at your size, the front-loading alternative and the dollar cost.

After using this, you know what to accrue per pay period and what the leave can cost, which used to mean reading a statute per state.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/paid-sick-leave-accrual-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
