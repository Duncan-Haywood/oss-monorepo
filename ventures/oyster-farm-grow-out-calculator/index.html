How the numbers work, and what they leave out

  • Oysters sold = seed × survival × share making grade. Bags = (seed + survivors) ÷ 2 ÷ oysters per bag, rounded up.
  • Gear wear = bags × bag cost × (months ÷ 12) ÷ gear life. Labor = bags × handlings × minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate. Lease and boat scale with months. Cost of money = (seed + gear purchase) × rate × years.
  • Break-even survival is found by searching survival values with every other input held fixed.
  • Not modelled: nursery or upweller costs, storm and theft loss beyond the survival you enter, discounted culls, depuration or processing, shipping, marketing, insurance, taxes, and your state's harvest-area closures.

Common questions

How much does it cost to grow an oyster?

Add up seed, the wear on bags and floats, labor, the lease, boat running costs and the cost of money tied up, then divide by the oysters you actually sell. The tool does this from your numbers and shows each line, so you can see whether seed, gear or labor is what drives your cost per oyster.

What survival rate do I need to break even?

The tool searches for the seed-to-harvest survival at which profit is zero, holding your price, gear and labor fixed. If your real survival from past batches is below that number, the cycle loses money at your price.

How many bags do I need for 100,000 seed?

Bags depend on how many oysters each holds on average over the cycle: small seed is crowded in, big oysters are thinned out. The tool uses seed plus survivors averaged, divided by your oysters-per-bag figure, rounded up.

How much seed should I buy for a sales target?

Divide the oysters you want to sell by survival times the share that makes grade. The tool does the division for a target you enter.

Does a longer grow-out cost more?

Yes. Lease, boat, the wear on gear and the cost of money all scale with months, so a slower-growing site needs a higher price or better survival to earn the same.

Where do the numbers come from?

From you. The tool contains no seed prices, survival data or market prices. The example values are made up.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser tab and nothing is sent anywhere. There are no accounts.
