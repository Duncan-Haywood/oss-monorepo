This calculator vs. ServiceCore

Two different shapes: ServiceCore is an account-based platform for running a portable sanitation yard's whole fleet, routes, and billing. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this event rental cost, given its days, pump-outs, and fleet mix — before you send the quote.

What each tool is for

ServiceCore is business-management software, founded in 2017, built specifically for portable restroom, septic, and dumpster rental operators — a narrower niche than general field-service platforms, and the company states it hires more people out of the portable sanitation and dumpster industries than any other software company in that space. It bundles customer and asset (unit and truck) management, route planning and scheduling, and accounting tools including estimates, invoices, and payment processing into one ongoing account, aimed at operators running multiple trucks across a standing customer base rather than a single one-off rental. ServiceCore's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's plan names or any dollar figure, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — fleet- and route-management software for portable sanitation and rolloff operators, sold as an ongoing account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceCore appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceCore does. It does not track a customer, route a truck, or bill recurring service, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one event — attendance, duration in days, a mix of standard, deluxe, ADA-accessible, and hand-wash units, pump-out visits, and the two round trips (delivery and pickup) a flat headcount rate hides — plus your rates, loaded labor, and target margin, and turns that into the event price, the price per unit-day, a starting fleet-size estimate from attendance and duration, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one event. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
