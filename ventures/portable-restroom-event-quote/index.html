FOR PORTABLE SANITATION YARDS

Bid the extra days and the pump-outs, not the Saturday headcount.

Price a porta potty / portable restroom event rental the way the yard actually loses money when it skips the math: every day the boxes sit, every extra pump-out, and two round trips of truck miles. You get the quote, the price per unit-day, and the number below which the job loses money. A starting fleet estimate from attendance is optional — it is not a health-code determination.

The event

Days on site includes drop day and pickup day. Friday drop through Sunday pickup is three days, not one Saturday. Attendance is optional and only used for a starting fleet estimate.

The fleet

One row per unit type. Daily rent is what the box earns sitting; service each is the pump-out price for that type. Starting rates are ballpark figures — replace them with your book.

Haul and costs

Mileage is two round trips: one to drop and one to pick up (four times the one-way miles). A Saturday-special that only prices the event day never sees that line, and it is often the largest cost on a short rental.
