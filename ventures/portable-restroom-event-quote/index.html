    Common questions

    Why is a Friday-drop, Sunday-pickup weekend billed as three rental days, not one?

    Because the unit sits on site and the yard's capital is tied up for every day between drop-off and pickup, not just the day of the event. A flat “Saturday special” that prices only the event day undercounts the rent by however many extra days the box actually occupies the lot.

    Does the suggested fleet size tell me how many units my event legally needs?

    No. The people-per-unit figures are a starting planning estimate by event length, not a health-department code table or a permit requirement — local ordinances and venue rules may require more (or specify accessible-unit ratios) than this tool suggests. Confirm with your local health authority before finalizing a fleet for a permitted event.

    Why does mileage get charged twice?

    Delivery is a round trip to drop units off, and pickup is a separate round trip to retrieve them — two trips, not one, even though it's the same event. If your yard also does a mid-event service run, that's an additional round trip the pump-out count should reflect.

    Does alcohol at the event actually change the numbers, or is that just a note?

    It's a real adjustment: checking it shortens the people-per-unit figure by 20%, which raises the suggested fleet size, because alcohol consumption is a common industry planning adjustment for restroom demand. It is not a health-code rule, just a starting assumption you can override.
