# UK Company Car Tax Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter a car's list price and type (electric, plug-in hybrid by electric range, or petrol/diesel with its quoted percentage) and see the 2026/27 benefit in kind, the income tax you pay each month, the employer's Class 1A cost, the fuel benefit if private fuel is paid, the scheduled zero-emission rise to 2029/30, and a side-by-side against a second car.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-company-car-tax-calculator/

After using this, you know what a company car really costs you in tax before you choose between an electric car and a petrol one.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-company-car-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
