How much to charge for a cord of firewood: cost, hours and delivery

Short answer: add up the cash cost of a cord, add the hours it takes at the wage you want, and divide by the share of wood you actually sell. The calculator does it for your own numbers.

A worked cord

$90 of logs, $25 of saw, splitter and fuel, $10 other, 4 hours to cut, split and stack, a 20 mile round trip at 80 cents a mile carrying 1.5 cords in 1.5 hours, a $25 an hour wage, 5 percent of the wood never sold, and a $300 price.

  • Cash cost per cord cut: $135.13, of which wood is $90.00, 66.6%. Time: 4.95 hours.
  • Revenue per cord cut is $285.00 after the unsold share, so the margin is $149.87 a cord, $30.28 an hour, and $8,992.00 over 60 cords in about 297 hours.
  • Pay yourself $25 an hour and $26.12 a cord is left, $1,567.00 over the season. The price that earns your wage is $272.51 a cord, $90.84 a face cord; cash breakeven is $142.25.

Wood cost and delivery decide it

Wood at $135 instead of $90 cuts profit per hour to $21.19 and lifts the price you need to $319.88; at $180 it is $12.09 an hour and $367.25. Delivering twice as far (40 miles, 3 hours a load) takes the profit per hour to $23.68, leaves a loss of $7.77 a cord after wage, and needs $308.18; at 60 miles it needs $343.84. A tough winter with $220 a cord, $150 wood, 40 miles, 3 hours a load and 15 percent unsold loses $158.63 a cord after wage, $9,518.00 over 60 cords, and needs $406.63 a cord. Try it with the long-haul, low-price winter button in the calculator.

How to measure your own numbers

  • Time a whole day of cutting, splitting and stacking, then divide by the cords made.
  • Count leftover stacks in spring to get the share that never sold.
  • Put a mileage cost on the truck that includes tires and repairs, not only fuel.

Common questions

How much should I charge for a cord of firewood?

Enough that the price covers your cash cost and pays your time. On the default numbers that is $272.51 per cord for a $25 an hour wage, or $90.84 per face cord, against a cash breakeven of $142.25.

What costs the most?

Wood, at $90 of the $135.13 cash cost per cord, 66.6 percent. After that your own hours matter: 4.95 hours a cord at $25 is $123.75, almost as much as all the cash costs together.
