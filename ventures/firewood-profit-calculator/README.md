# Firewood Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/firewood-profit-calculator/>

**Find what a cord of firewood costs you in cash and hours, your profit per cord and per hour, the price per cord and face cord you need to pay yourself a wage, and how wood cost and delivery distance move it.** Browser page, no install.

After using this, you know whether your price per cord pays you more than a part-time job, the price you need for the wage you want, and whether the long deliveries are worth it.

## Your numbers, not advice

Every value is a number you type. Prices, costs and times are yours to check. Not affiliated with any firewood association or marketplace.

## How it computes

Cash cost per cord cut = wood + equipment and fuel + other + the share sold x (miles x cost per mile / cords per load). Hours per cord = processing hours + the share sold x (hours per load / cords per load). Revenue = share sold x price. Price for your wage = (cash cost + wage x hours) / share sold.

## What it is not

Not financial, tax, legal or business advice, and no warranty. It ignores seasoning time, sales tax, seasonal price swings and the value of your trailer or land.

## Privacy

No network call, no account, no analytics vendor. Anonymous feature counts (`calculate_run`, `example_loaded`) live in local storage only and can be switched off on the page; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured.
