Common questions

What does a cord of firewood cost to produce?

On the default numbers $135.13 in cash per cord: $90 of logs, $25 of equipment and fuel, $10 of other costs and $10.13 of delivery vehicle cost, plus 4.95 hours of your time. Wood is the largest cash line at 66.6 percent. Enter your own figures; the defaults are invented.

What price per cord do I need to earn a wage?

$272.51 per cord, or $90.84 per face cord, to pay yourself $25 an hour on those numbers. Selling at $300 leaves $26.12 a cord after that wage, $1,567.00 over 60 cords, and $30.28 an hour before any wage is deducted.

How much does delivery distance matter?

Doubling the round trip from 20 to 40 miles, with the trip twice as long, cuts the profit per hour from $30.28 to $23.68, turns the margin after wage into a loss of $7.77 a cord, and lifts the price you need to $308.18.
