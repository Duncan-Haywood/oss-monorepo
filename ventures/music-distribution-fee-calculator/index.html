Common questions

Which music distributor is cheapest?

It depends on your release count and your streams. A free or revenue-share distributor is cheapest while your royalties are small; a flat yearly plan that keeps no cut wins once your royalties are large enough that the percentage would exceed the plan fee. This calculator finds that crossover for your numbers.

How much does a stream pay?

Roughly a third of a cent to a few tenths of a cent per stream, varying by service, country and listener plan. The default here is an approximate starting point; use your own statement's average payout per stream and edit it.

Do distributors take a cut of royalties?

Some do and some charge only fees. Per-release fees, yearly plans and a percentage of royalties are all common, sometimes combined. Each row here has all three.

Are the preset prices current?

They are approximate starting points, not verified numbers. Distributors change pricing often, so check each pricing page and edit the figures before relying on the result.

Is this financial advice?

No. It does arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It does not compare features, payout speed, takedown terms or tax treatment of music income.
