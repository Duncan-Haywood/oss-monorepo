# DAC7 Seller Reporting Checker

Free, private browser tool for people who sell goods, rent out property or transport, or sell services through online platforms (Etsy, eBay, Vinted, Airbnb, Amazon and others) in the EU: enter your year's transactions and total received on a platform and see whether the platform will report you to the tax authority under DAC7, how much headroom you have under the 30 sales / EUR 2,000 goods exemption, what gets sent, and the 31 January and tax-number deadlines.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/dac7-seller-reporting-checker/

After using this, you know whether to expect a tax-authority report this year and what to get ready, instead of finding out from a platform email that threatens to close your account.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/dac7-seller-reporting-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
