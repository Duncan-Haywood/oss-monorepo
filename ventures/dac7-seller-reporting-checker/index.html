Common questions

    
What is DAC7?

    
DAC7 is the EU directive (2021/514) that makes online platforms collect seller details and report each seller's earnings to the tax authority every year, starting with 2023. The platform sends the report by 31 January and gives the seller a copy.

    
What is the DAC7 threshold for selling goods?

    
A seller of goods is not reported if, on that platform in the calendar year, they made fewer than 30 sales and received no more than EUR 2,000. Crossing either limit makes the whole year reportable.

    
Do services and rentals have a threshold?

    
No. Personal services, renting out property and renting out transport have no de minimis exemption, so even a single transaction can be reported.

    
Does being reported mean I owe tax?

    
No. The report only tells the tax authority what you earned through the platform. Whether the income is taxable is decided by your national tax rules.

    
What happens if I do not give the platform my tax number?

    
After two reminders the platform must close your account or withhold payments, about 60 days after its first request.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent; a test in the code asserts there are no network calls.
