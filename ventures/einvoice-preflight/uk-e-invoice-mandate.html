The UK's e-invoice mandate: what is confirmed

The UK will require structured e-invoices for VAT invoices from April 2029. Below: what the sources agree on, what is still open, and how to test a file before it goes to a customer.

The timeline (as secondary sources describe it)

DateWhat changes
Nov 2025The government response to the HMRC and Department for Business and Trade consultation, and Autumn Budget 2025, confirm that e-invoicing goes ahead.
Jun 2026Peppol reported as the core interoperability network.

Unconfirmed: these dates come from secondary sources (law firms, accountancy bodies, software vendors), not from gov.uk, which this page did not read. No size-based phasing, exemption or penalty amount was found, and none is stated here. This is a summary, not tax advice.

Which file will count, and how it travels

The standard is reported as EN 16931, so a PDF alone will not qualify, and Peppol is the network. Until the UK specification is published, the safest test is an EN 16931 UBL or CII file that also passes Peppol BIS Billing 3.0.

Common questions

When does the UK e-invoicing mandate start?

The government confirmed at the Autumn Budget 2025 that e-invoicing will go ahead from April 2029. Sources read describe it as covering VAT invoices for business-to-business and business-to-government sales; consumer (B2C) sales are out of scope at first. The implementation roadmap, including any phasing and enforcement, was reported as due with Budget 2026, which had not happened when this page was written. These dates come from secondary sources (law firms, accountancy bodies and software vendors), not from gov.uk, which this page did not read. Confirm with HMRC or your accountant.

Which format and network will the UK use?

Peppol is reported as the core interoperability network, confirmed on 23 June 2026, in a decentralised four-corner model with no real-time reporting of invoices to HMRC in the first phase. The data standard is reported as EN 16931, and public bodies such as the NHS have received Peppol e-invoices since 2019. No UK-specific profile or syntax rule set had been published in the sources read, so this checker tests EN 16931 and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 files, the likely starting point, not a confirmed UK profile.

What are the penalties for not e-invoicing in the UK?

None are announced in the sources read, so this page states no amount. Enforcement is expected to be covered by the roadmap.

Pick a Peppol access point or check that your accounting software will issue EN 16931 UBL or CII, and test sample invoices.
