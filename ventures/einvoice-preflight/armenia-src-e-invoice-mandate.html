Armenia: SRC e-invoicing

Armenia's State Revenue Committee (SRC) runs the electronic invoice system for VAT tax invoices. Below: what guides report about when it became mandatory, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

Common questions

What is the SRC e-invoicing system in Armenia?

The State Revenue Committee's online system through which VAT-registered taxpayers issue tax invoices, adjustment invoices and consignment notes, according to e-invoice.app.

When did electronic invoicing become mandatory?

Guides disagree: 2011 (system launched, paper still allowed), 1 January 2015 (e-invoice.app, B2B and B2G) and 2016 (VATupdate, Armenian Lawyer). Check the SRC and the Tax Code.

Does it cover consumers?

Sources disagree: e-invoice.app lists B2C as none, VATupdate says B2C is covered in principle. Unconfirmed.

Can this checker validate an Armenian invoice?

No. It has no Armenian rules, does not know the SRC format and does not connect to the SRC. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not SRC acceptance.
