Dominican Republic: DGII e-CF

The Dirección General de Impuestos Internos (DGII) is moving every taxpayer to electronic fiscal receipts (e-CF), in waves by size. Two waves land in November 2026. Below: what the sources say and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelElectronic fiscal receipts (e-CF) issued by authorised electronic issuers, validated by the DGII. Becoming an issuer is applied for in the DGII Oficina Virtual; one guide says the authorisation itself is free.
TimelineLarge national taxpayers have issued e-CF since 2024, per one guide. A DGII news item says large local and medium taxpayers issue only electronic invoices from November 2026; a guide gives 1 November 2026, with paper type B sequences valid to 31 October 2026 apart from declared contingencies.
Small and microOriginally due 15 May 2026 (DGII Notice 14-25). Guides report a six-month extension to 15 November 2026, called the final one, with penalties under Law 32-23 after that.
FormatXML under DGII technical specifications, not EN 16931. Dominican rules and codes apply.
Pending obligationsOne guide cites Decree 587-24 as barring taxpayers with pending DGII obligations from becoming electronic issuers.

Written from web search summaries and vendor guides (Beancount, Siemprealdia, a DGII news item headline), not from DGII texts (only a DGII news headline was seen, not the notices). Dates and thresholds may have changed.

Common questions

What is the e-CF in the Dominican Republic?

The comprobante fiscal electrónico, the DGII's electronic fiscal receipt. Authorised issuers send structured XML, which the DGII validates.

When must businesses switch to e-CF?

Guides report large local and medium taxpayers from November 2026 (paper sequences valid to 31 October 2026) and small, micro and unclassified taxpayers from 15 November 2026 after a final extension. Check the DGII before relying on a date.

Is the e-CF the same as EN 16931 UBL?

No. It is DGII-specified XML with Dominican codes.

Can this checker validate an e-CF?

No. It has no Dominican rules and does not call the DGII. For a UBL or CII file it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not DGII acceptance.
