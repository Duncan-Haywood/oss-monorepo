Guatemala: FEL

Guatemala runs e-invoicing as FEL (Factura Electrónica en Línea) under the tax authority, the SAT, with certifiers in the middle. Below: what advisory firms report and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelFEL: the issuer signs the invoice, a SAT-registered certifier validates it with the SAT in real time and returns an authorisation number. Not EN 16931.
ScopeSources disagree. One adviser says FEL is mandatory for all taxpayers; another describes a voluntary phase from 1 January 2019 followed by B2B, and Edicom describes a rollout by taxpayer size and sector set by the SAT.
Legal basisOne source cites SAT Directorate Agreement 13-2018 as the framework instrument. Not checked against the text.
CertifiersA taxpayer applies as an electronic invoice issuer and contracts a certifier. Certifiers register with the SAT first. The current roster was not found.

Common questions

What is FEL?

Factura Electrónica en Línea, Guatemala's e-invoicing regime run by the SAT, where invoices are signed by the issuer and authorised through a certifier.

Who certifies a Guatemalan e-invoice?

A certifier registered with the SAT. The issuer contracts one, and it validates the document with the SAT and returns an authorisation number.

Is FEL mandatory for everyone?

Sources disagree: one says all taxpayers, others describe a staged rollout by size and sector. Confirm with the SAT.

Can this checker validate a Guatemalan FEL?

No. It has no Guatemalan rules and does not call a certifier or the SAT. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not FEL authorisation.
