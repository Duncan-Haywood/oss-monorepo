Côte d'Ivoire's e-invoice rules: the DGI's FNE, mandatory, with sources split on the dates

Côte d'Ivoire's tax authority, the Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), requires a facture normalisée électronique (FNE) certified through its own platform. Sources agree on that and disagree on when each group had to comply. Below: what the press and vendor pages report, where they conflict, and how to test a file first.

The timeline (as press and vendor pages report it, and where they conflict)

DateWhat is reported
10 Jan 20252025 tax annex in force; reported as the legal basis, covering economic operators in Côte d'Ivoire whatever their tax regime or legal form, with exceptions.
9 May 2025Arrêté n°0337/MFB/DGI, cited as the implementing text.
1 Jun 2025Start for normal-regime companies, per one vendor guide.
1 Sep 2025Entry into force across the territory, per another report.
1 Jan 2026The DGI's stated aim of full operation for all categories of company, per one source. A LinkedIn post reports a 1 June 2026 extension for part of the base. Not reconciled.
1 Sep 2026A control operation on use and issue of the FNE and the RNE (receipt equivalent) across the territory, per a press report. A business federation (FENACCI) asked to postpone the controls and suspend automatic financial penalties.
29 Oct 2026Deadline for users of the FNE API to meet the technical specifications, per an aggregator citing a DGI notice. Not read at the DGI.

Where sources disagree: the start date (June 2025, September 2025, January 2026 or June 2026), and whether penalties are automatic. No fine amounts were found. These are press, vendor and aggregator pages, not the DGI or the Journal Officiel, which could not be read from this build. Confirm with the DGI before acting on any date. A summary, not tax advice.

Which file counts, and how it travels

Reports describe an invoice certified by the DGI platform (portal reported as fne.dgi.gouv.ci, not opened here), carrying a QR code, an authentication visual and continuous electronic numbering, issued either on the portal or from accredited software over an API. The schema was not read from a primary source, so this page does not say which syntax is used.

Common questions

Does Côte d'Ivoire have an e-invoicing mandate?

Yes, on every source read, but they disagree on dates. The legal basis is reported as the 2025 tax annex (in force 10 January 2025) and arrêté n°0337/MFB/DGI of 9 May 2025, which require economic operators to issue a facture normalisée électronique (FNE) through the DGI system. Sources give 1 June 2025, 1 September 2025 and 1 January 2026 as start dates, one reports an extension to 1 June 2026, and a DGI control operation on FNE use is reported from 1 September 2026. These are press and vendor pages, not the DGI or the Journal Officiel, so confirm there. This is a summary, not advice.

What format does the FNE use?

Reports describe an invoice certified by the DGI platform, carrying a QR code, an authentication visual and continuous electronic numbering, with an API route for accredited software. A DGI notice reported by an aggregator gives API users until 29 October 2026 to meet technical specifications. The schema was not read from a primary source, and nothing here says it is Peppol or EN 16931.

What does this checker cover for Côte d'Ivoire?

EN 16931 arithmetic and tax-breakdown rules for UBL, CII and Factur-X files. It does not apply Ivorian rules and does not talk to the DGI, so it cannot certify an FNE. A clean result is not DGI acceptance.

How do I check an invoice before sending it?

Drop the file into the checker. It tests totals, tax breakdown arithmetic, mandatory fields and VAT, IBAN and BIC typos, each with a rule ID and a fix. Nothing is uploaded. It is an offline observation, not the tax authority's acceptance and not legal advice.
