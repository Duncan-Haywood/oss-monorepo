Bangladesh: NBR e-invoicing

Bangladesh's National Board of Revenue runs fiscal-device and VAT-portal systems, and sources disagree on whether a binding e-invoicing mandate is in force. Below: what guides report, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
StatusDisputed. A 2026 country booklet says there is no live, legislated mandatory domestic B2B or B2G e-invoicing regime and that Finance Bill 2026 uses enabling language ("may be made mandatory"). Other pages describe B2B and B2G e-invoicing phasing in since July 2019 under real-time reporting to the NBR.
RetailSales Data Controllers and Electronic Fiscal Devices from about August 2020, reported to apply to VAT-registered businesses with penalties for non-use or tampering. Not confirmed from a primary source.

Common questions

Is e-invoicing mandatory in Bangladesh?

Sources disagree. One 2026 country booklet says there is no live, legislated domestic B2B or B2G e-invoicing mandate and that the Finance Bill 2026 only says it may be made mandatory. Other vendor pages describe a phased NBR rollout since 2019. Check the National Board of Revenue's notices.

What are EFD and SDC?

Electronic fiscal devices and sales data controllers, which guides describe as the NBR's retail point-of-sale regime from about August 2020. Its current scope is not confirmed from a primary source here.

Is the 2026 eVAT return order e-invoicing?

No. NBR General Order 12/Mushak/2026 of 20 September 2026 is reported to make online VAT return filing mandatory for specified manufacturers and service providers. It concerns returns, not invoices.

Can this checker validate a Bangladeshi invoice?

No. It has no Bangladeshi rules and does not connect to the NBR. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not NBR acceptance.
