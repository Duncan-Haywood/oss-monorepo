Austria's e-invoice rules: voluntary for B2B, mandatory for federal suppliers

Austria has no domestic business-to-business e-invoicing mandate that the sources found put a date on. Suppliers to federal bodies have had to invoice electronically since 2014, through the Unternehmensserviceportal (USP) or Peppol, and EU-wide rules reach cross-border B2B in 2030. Below: what is reported, where sources disagree, and how to test a file before it goes to a customer.

The timeline (as vendor guides and Commission sheets report it)

DateWhat changes
1 Jan 2014Federal public bodies accept only electronic invoices from suppliers, per vendor guides. Länder and municipalities can receive them through e-Rechnung.gv.at but are reported not to require them from suppliers.
TodayB2B e-invoicing is voluntary and by agreement between the parties. A vendor guide dated 13 October 2026 reports no bill, draft or finance-ministry announcement for a mandatory B2B duty; one advisory firm expects an obligation under ViDA around 2027 to 2028, which other sources do not repeat.
1 Jul 2030EU-wide ViDA e-invoicing for intra-EU B2B transactions, in a structured EN 16931-based format.

Where sources disagree: whether the federal duty reaches regional and local bodies (one guide says all public administrations, another says only federal), and when, if ever, a domestic B2B duty arrives. Which file counts, and how it travels

Austrian federal bodies are reported to take ebInterface (the Austrian national XML standard) or Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, a profile of EN 16931, sent through the USP portal or the Peppol network. A UBL or CII file that passes EN 16931 and the Peppol rules is the safe common ground for a Peppol-reachable buyer. A plain PDF is not a structured e-invoice. This checker does not read ebInterface files.

Common questions

Does Austria have a B2B e-invoicing mandate?

Not yet, per 2025 to 2026 vendor and advisory guides: domestic B2B e-invoicing is voluntary and agreed between the parties, and no bill or ministry announcement for a mandatory duty was found. One advisory firm expects an obligation under ViDA around 2027 to 2028, which is not confirmed elsewhere. The first fixed date is the EU-wide 1 July 2030 for intra-EU B2B. These are not the law or the Federal Ministry of Finance, so confirm there. This is a summary, not advice.

What format do Austrian federal bodies accept?

Vendor guides say ebInterface or Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, delivered through the Unternehmensserviceportal (USP) or the Peppol network, and that suppliers to federal bodies have had to invoice electronically since 1 January 2014. Sources differ on whether regional and local bodies require it.

What does this checker cover for Austria?

EN 16931 and the Peppol BIS 3.0 rules it knows, for JSON, UBL, CII and Factur-X PDFs. It has no ebInterface schema or Austrian national rules, and no USP upload or Peppol network lookup. A clean result means the invoice passes EN 16931 and the Peppol rules it knows, not that an Austrian buyer or portal will accept it.
