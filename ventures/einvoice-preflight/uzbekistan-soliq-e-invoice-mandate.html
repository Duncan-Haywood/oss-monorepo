Uzbekistan: EHF

Uzbekistan has cleared e-invoices (EHF) through the State Tax Committee's system for years, in a national JSON format. Below: what advisory firms report, where they disagree and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelReal-time clearance: invoices created on the State Tax Committee's portal or through one of roughly 15 authorised operators (for example Didox, Faktura.uz). National JSON standard, not EN 16931 or Peppol.
TimelineThomson Reuters and e-invoice.app report mandatory B2B and B2G e-invoicing from 1 January 2020, and e-invoice.app reports B2C from 1 January 2021.
ThresholdOne source puts the mandate at businesses with annual turnover above UZS 1 billion. Another reports the VAT/CIT turnover threshold rising to UZS 5 billion from 1 June 2026; it is unclear whether the e-invoicing threshold moved.
2026One briefing reports AI-based risk scoring of VAT invoices in real time from 1 January 2026. Not confirmed elsewhere.
Retention and penaltiesOne source reports five years' retention under the Tax Code and penalties for non-compliance. Amounts not checked.

What this checker does and does not cover

The main checker reads UBL, CII and Factur-X and applies EN 16931 and Peppol rules. It does not apply local rules, sign an invoice or call the State Tax Committee system. It is still useful on the numbers: wrong totals, a tax breakdown that does not add up and mistyped identifiers are faults any clearance system rejects.

Common questions

What is EHF?

Elektron hisob-faktura, Uzbekistan's electronic VAT invoice, cleared through the State Tax Committee's system.

Do I have to use the state portal?

Reportedly not: authorised private operators connect to the State Tax Committee's system. Confirm the current list.

Did the threshold change in 2026?

Sources disagree. A VAT/CIT turnover threshold change to UZS 5 billion from 1 June 2026 is reported; whether the e-invoicing threshold changed is not confirmed.

Can this checker validate an Uzbek EHF?

No. It has no Uzbek rules and does not call the State Tax Committee. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not EHF clearance.
