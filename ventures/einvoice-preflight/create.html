Create an XRechnung or Peppol e-invoice

Germany already requires businesses to receive structured e-invoices, and sending becomes mandatory from 2027 (2028 for smaller firms); Belgium, Poland and France follow. A PDF is no longer enough. Fill in the form, get a UBL or CII XML file, and see it checked against the preflight rules before you download it. Totals and the per-rate VAT breakdown are worked out for you. Nothing is uploaded.
