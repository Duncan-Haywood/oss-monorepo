Croatia (HR-CIUS 2025)

Fiskalizacija 2.0 adds these to every domestic B2B invoice. Give each line a KPD 2025 class (e.g. 62.20.30) in the lines table. An exempt (E) line or charge, or an amount with an HR label (deposit, PP, PPMV, other non-taxable fee), makes the creator add the HR extension (HRFISK20Data) that HR-BR-26 requires. Seller and buyer e-addresses default to scheme 9934 and the OIB from the VAT ID.

Allowances and charges on the whole invoice

A discount on the whole invoice is an allowance, a delivery or packaging fee a charge; each counts in its VAT rate's row (EN 16931 BG-20, BG-21). In Croatia, an amount outside the VAT base takes an HR label: POVNAK (refundable deposit), PP (consumption tax), PPMV (special tax on motor vehicles) or N (another non-taxable fee, e.g. tourist tax). The label also works on a line, in the HR label column there. It is written as the Porezna uprava's own samples write it: exempt (E) in the EN layer, outside the VAT base (O) in the HR extension.

0% needs a category: Z zero-rated, E exempt (e.g. Kleinunternehmer § 19 UStG), AE reverse charge, K intra-EU supply, G export, O outside scope. All but Z need a reason.
