Which e-invoicing deadline applies to me?

Pick a country and type one number. You get the start date for your size, and a link to the country page with the sources.

The cut-offs this page uses

Germany2027-01-01: You must issue structured e-invoices for domestic B2B sales (prior-year turnover above EUR 800,000).
2028-01-01: You must issue structured e-invoices for domestic B2B sales; until then you may use other formats (prior-year turnover of EUR 800,000 or less).
Spain2027-10-06: B2B e-invoicing starts (prior-year turnover above EUR 8 million), under Order HAC/1028/2026.
2028-10-06: B2B e-invoicing starts for all other businesses.
United Arab Emirates2027-01-01: E-invoicing is mandatory (revenue of AED 50 million or more).
2027-07-01: E-invoicing is mandatory for the rest.
Poland2026-02-01: KSeF was mandatory for you from 1 February 2026 (sales above PLN 200 million).
2026-04-01: KSeF was mandatory for you from 1 April 2026 (most other VAT payers), unless you are in the smallest wave described below.
Oman2027-04-01: Fawtara is reported as mandatory (annual supplies above OMR 5 million), Decision 189/2026.
2027-10-01: Fawtara is reported as mandatory for the others.
Malaysia2024-08-01: MyInvois e-invoicing started for you (turnover above RM100 million).
2025-01-01: MyInvois e-invoicing started for you (turnover RM25 million to RM100 million).
2025-07-01: MyInvois e-invoicing started for you (turnover RM5 million to RM25 million).
2026-01-01: MyInvois e-invoicing started for you (turnover RM1 million to RM5 million, Phase 4 announced June 2025).
2026-07-01: Reported start for turnover up to RM1 million, with a RM500,000 exemption announced; some sources say businesses below RM1 million are exempt. Check LHDN.
France2026-09-01: You must issue e-invoices for domestic B2B sales from 1 September 2026 (large and mid-size companies, ETI) and report other transactions.
2027-09-01: You must issue e-invoices from 1 September 2027 (small and micro businesses).

Cut-offs are those on each country page, which cite public guidance and say where it is only secondary. Other countries' dates do not depend on size and are in the hub table.
