# E-invoice preflight: answers

The questions each page answers in its "Common questions" section, with the answer as the page states it. Each link opens that section; the page has the sources.

- [Does this submit an e-invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/#faq): No. The page only reads files or pasted JSON in your browser and reports observations. It never contacts a tax authority or submission network.
- [How do I check an e-invoice before submission?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/#faq): Drop a JSON, UBL, or CII (XRechnung / Factur-X) XML invoice or credit note, a Factur-X / ZUGFeRD / XRechnung PDF with an embedded invoice, or paste the JSON shape. The checker unwraps the PDF in the browser when needed and reports missing fields, date-shape mistakes, a header allowance or charge whose amount does not match its percentage, a purchase-order reference that repeats the invoice number or is a placeholder, payment terms days that do not match the due date, a SEPA or credit-transfer payment means without an IBAN, an invoice period that is incomplete or ends before it starts, totals that do not match the line items, mixed tax rates without a per-rate breakdown, a buyer electronic address that fails its GLN or EAS scheme, a credit sent as a commercial invoice, a missing original-invoice reference, a 0% or exempt line without a tax reason, prepaid versus amount-due mismatches, a missing remittance on an IBAN invoice, and optional VAT, IBAN, BIC, and RF typo catches, with a rule ID and a suggested fix. It does not decide tax treatment or legal validity.
- [How do you calculate a mixed-VAT-rate tax breakdown?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/#faq): Group the lines by rate, sum each rate's net, multiply by that rate, and round to the cent — see the worked example the INV-037/INV-038 checks are built from.
- [What does this e-invoice error code mean?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/#faq): Every rule this checker reports has an INV- code naming one specific defect and the field it was found in — the rule reference lists all of them with a fix for each. They are this tool's own identifiers, not EN 16931 BR- rules or a submission network's rejection codes.
- [When must German businesses issue e-invoices?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/#faq): Receiving structured e-invoices has been mandatory since 1 January 2025; issuing is mandatory from 2027 (businesses above 800,000 euro prior-year turnover) and 2028 (everyone) for domestic B2B. See the dates, the formats that count and a free XRechnung check.
- [Should I use this checker or a platform like Avalara?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/#faq): Different jobs: Avalara (or a comparable platform) calculates and files tax and routes e-invoices across a whole operation as an ongoing subscription. This checker is a free, local, no-account validation of one invoice file's fields and arithmetic before it reaches that pipeline — see this checker vs. Avalara.
- [Does Albania have an e-invoicing mandate?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/albania-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Yes. Vendor and compliance guides say Albania has required e-invoicing under Law 87/2019 in phases: government buyers from 1 January 2021, cashless business-to-business sales from 1 July 2021 and cash sales to consumers from 1 September 2021. The General Directorate of Taxes (DPT) runs the system and clears each invoice. These are not the law or the DPT itself, so confirm there. This is a summary, not advice.
- [What format and numbers does Albania use?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/albania-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Guides say UBL 2.1 or UN/CEFACT Cross Industry Invoice XML, signed with a certificate from the national agency AKSHI. The tax system checks each invoice and returns a unique invoice number (NIVF) and a security code (NSLF), which appear on the printed or PDF copy as a QR code. This is not Peppol and not the EU's EN 16931 model.
- [What does this checker cover for Albania?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/albania-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): EN 16931 arithmetic and tax-breakdown rules for UBL, CII and Factur-X files. It does not apply Albania's own schema or fiscal rules, cannot sign with an AKSHI certificate and does not talk to the DPT, so it cannot issue an NIVF. A clean result is not clearance.
- [How do I check an invoice before sending it?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/albania-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Drop the file into the checker. It tests totals, tax breakdown arithmetic, mandatory fields and VAT, IBAN and BIC typos, each with a rule ID and a fix. Nothing is uploaded. It is an offline observation, not the tax authority's acceptance and not legal advice. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.
- [What is a CAE?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/argentina-arca-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): The código de autorización electrónico: the code ARCA assigns to an electronic invoice when the issuer requests authorisation. Without a CAE, vendor guides say the invoice has no tax validity, so it cannot support an IVA credit or a deductible expense. This is a summary from vendor guides, not advice; confirm with ARCA or your accountant.
- [How do I check an invoice I received?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/argentina-arca-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): ARCA has a public check, “Constatación de Comprobantes”. You enter the issuer’s CUIT, the type and number of the invoice, the amount, and the CAE with its date. This page does not do that check; it only tests a CUIT’s check digit.
- [What is a valid CUIT?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/argentina-arca-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Eleven digits whose last digit follows from the first ten by a mod 11 rule. A matching digit does not mean the CUIT is registered or active; ARCA’s public register answers that. The test on this page catches typos only.
- [Can this checker validate an Argentine invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/argentina-arca-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): No. It does not read ARCA’s formats and cannot obtain or verify a CAE. It does test a CUIT’s shape and check digit on this page, and a clean result is not an authorisation by ARCA. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.
- [What is the SRC e-invoicing system in Armenia?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/armenia-src-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): The State Revenue Committee's online system through which VAT-registered taxpayers issue tax invoices, adjustment invoices and consignment notes, according to e-invoice.app.
- [When did electronic invoicing become mandatory?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/armenia-src-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Guides disagree: 2011 (system launched, paper still allowed), 1 January 2015 (e-invoice.app, B2B and B2G) and 2016 (VATupdate, Armenian Lawyer). Check the SRC and the Tax Code.
- [Does it cover consumers?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/armenia-src-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Sources disagree: e-invoice.app lists B2C as none, VATupdate says B2C is covered in principle. Unconfirmed.
- [Can this checker validate an Armenian invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/armenia-src-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): No. It has no Armenian rules, does not know the SRC format and does not connect to the SRC. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not SRC acceptance. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.
- [Is e-invoicing mandatory in Australia?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/australia-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Not for business-to-business invoices as far as we could confirm: sources agree sending a Peppol e-invoice is voluntary for suppliers, and the proposed Business eInvoicing Right was reported as not enacted. It is a requirement on federal government agencies, which have had to be able to receive Peppol e-invoices since 1 July 2022. Confirm with the ATO or your accountant; this page is a summary, not advice.
- [What format does Australian e-invoicing use?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/australia-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Peppol, using the PINT A-NZ specification shared with New Zealand (Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 is reported as retired from the AU/NZ network, with PINT A-NZ the only supported specification from 15 May 2025). When an invoice's specification identifier (BT-24) names the A-NZ profile, this checker runs OpenPeppol's A-NZ rules, including the ABN rules, in your browser.
- [Do government agencies pay e-invoices faster?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/australia-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Yes, reportedly: the Commonwealth pays eligible Peppol e-invoices within five calendar days, a policy that began for invoices up to A$1 million in 2020 and was reported as extended without the cap from 1 July 2022. Check the Treasury or Department of Finance guidance for the terms that apply to your contract.
- [What can this checker do for an Australian invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/australia-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Drop the file into the checker: it tests the totals and tax breakdown arithmetic against the EN 16931 core rules, each failure with a rule ID and a fix. For a UBL invoice that names the A-NZ profile it runs the PINT A-NZ rules instead, including ABN and GST checks; it is still not an access point's acceptance. Nothing is uploaded when you use the page. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.
- [Is B2B e-invoicing mandatory in Austria?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/austria-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Not yet. Sources read in October 2026 agree that business-to-business e-invoicing in Austria is voluntary and that no domestic B2B mandate date has been adopted in law; commentary that guesses at 2027 to 2028 or later is speculation. Confirm with the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF) or your accountant; this page is a summary, not advice.
- [Who must send an e-invoice in Austria today?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/austria-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Suppliers to federal government. Electronic invoicing to federal bodies has been required since 1 January 2014, and since 18 April 2020 that includes foreign suppliers, with invoices that comply with EN 16931. The reported channels are the federal e-Rechnung.gv.at portal and Peppol; the formats are ebInterface XML (the Austrian standard) and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0. This comes from vendor and guide summaries, not the legal text.
- [Which format should an Austrian business use?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/austria-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): For federal customers, ebInterface or Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (UBL). For private customers there is no required format; an EN 16931 UBL or CII file is the safest bet if you want to be ready for the EU's cross-border digital reporting, which is reported to start on 1 July 2030 and does not itself create a domestic Austrian obligation.
- [Can this checker validate an ebInterface invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/austria-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): No. It reads UBL, CII, Factur-X and JSON and applies the EN 16931 arithmetic, tax breakdown, mandatory-field and identifier checks, plus the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 rules when you tick that option, and VAT, IBAN and BIC typo catches, each with a rule ID and a fix. It does not read ebInterface XML or run the e-Rechnung.gv.at validation, so a clean result is an offline observation, not an acceptance by the portal.
- [What are the penalties for not e-invoicing in Austria?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/austria-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): None were found in the sources read, so this page states no amount. For federal suppliers the practical consequence reported is that a non-compliant invoice is not processed; check the BMF guidance. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.
- [What is e-qaimə in Azerbaijan?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/azerbaijan-e-qaime-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): The electronic invoice system of the State Tax Service (Dövlət Vergi Xidməti), issued through the taxpayer portal at e-taxes.gov.az. Guides describe it as covering goods and services for commercial purposes.
- [When did electronic invoicing become mandatory?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/azerbaijan-e-qaime-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Guides disagree: one gives 1 January 2017 for VAT-registered taxpayers, another ties it to Tax Code amendments from April 2017, and Edicom says all taxpayers, whatever their status, since 1 January 2018. One source says 2010, which conflicts with the rest. Check the State Tax Service and the Tax Code.
- [Is the VAT invoice a separate document?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/azerbaijan-e-qaime-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): One source reports that the separate electronic VAT invoice requirement was dropped in 2020 and a single electronic invoice now serves billing and VAT purposes. Unconfirmed against the Tax Code.

- [When did Belgium's B2B e-invoicing mandate start?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/belgium-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): On 1 January 2026 structured e-invoicing became mandatory for domestic B2B transactions between Belgian VAT-registered businesses; the same date applies to receiving and issuing. A tolerance period without fines ran for the first quarter of 2026. Exceptions exist (for example sales to consumers and some exempt activities). A PDF or a paper invoice no longer counts as the invoice for domestic B2B sales. This checker reads JSON, UBL, CII and Factur-X PDFs and has a Peppol BIS 3.0 option.
- [What are the fines for not e-invoicing in Belgium?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/belgium-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): The law sets fines of EUR 1,500 for a first offence, EUR 3,000 for a second and EUR 5,000 for a third or later offence in a calendar year, after the first-quarter 2026 tolerance period. Confirm the current amounts with FPS Finance before relying on them.

- [What is a facture normalisée in Benin?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/benin-e-mecef-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): An invoice issued through a DGI-certified system: a physical MECeF device, the online e-MECeF platform, or your own billing software (an SFE) approved by the DGI. It counts as normalised only once it is finalised and carries the DGI security elements, including the MECeF/DGI code.

- [Who must issue one?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/benin-e-mecef-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Reportedly taxpayers subject to corporate income tax, business profits tax, TPS or VAT, apart from legal exemptions. - [What is a BR-FR error?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/br-fr-error-codes.html#faq): BR-FR rules are the French controls (AFNOR XP Z12-012, the "Flux 2" controls) that approved platforms (plateformes agréées, PA) apply to invoices exchanged under France's e-invoicing reform, on top of the EN 16931 rules. FNFE-MPE publishes them as Schematron files with French messages; a platform that rejects an invoice usually quotes the code, such as BR-FR-08/BT-23.
- [Is this the official English text?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/br-fr-error-codes.html#faq): No. FNFE-MPE publishes the messages in French only. The English on this page was translated for this tool from FNFE-MPE's BR-FR Schematron and is shown beside the French original, which remains the reference. A platform may word its own rejection differently.
- [Can I check an invoice against these rules before sending it?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/br-fr-error-codes.html#faq): Yes. The BR-FR rules run in your browser, the file is not uploaded, and each finding is shown in English with FNFE-MPE's French under it.
- [What is an NF-e?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/brazil-nfe-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): An NF-e (Nota Fiscal Eletrônica) is Brazil's electronic invoice for goods: an XML file the seller sends to the state tax authority (SEFAZ), which validates it and returns an authorisation protocol before the goods move. The printed DANFE is only a representation of the XML. This is a summary from vendor and professional-body notices, not advice; confirm with your accountant and the Portal da NF-e.
- [What is the alphanumeric CNPJ?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/brazil-nfe-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Per the notices read, the CNPJ stays 14 characters, but for new registrations the first 12 may be digits or capital letters; the last two remain numeric check digits. Existing CNPJs do not change. Technical note NT 2025.001 set the general rules and NT 2026.004 changed the NF-e and NFC-e schemas, including the access key, to accept letters, with production reported for 1 July 2026. Sources differ on the start of the test environment.
- [How is the CNPJ check digit calculated?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/brazil-nfe-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Each character is worth its ASCII code minus 48, so digits are 0 to 9 and A to Z are 17 to 42. Weights 2 to 9 are applied from the right and repeat; the digit is 0 if the sum modulo 11 is below 2, otherwise 11 minus the remainder. The second digit repeats the sum over the first 13 characters. This page implements that rule and was pinned on examples I know, not read from the Receita Federal specification.
- [Can this checker validate a Brazilian NF-e?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/brazil-nfe-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Partly, since 2026-11-12. Drop an NF-e or NFC-e 4.00 XML file on this page or on the main checker and it is read against the NF-e 4.00 schema (package PL_010_V1.30, which includes the IBS, CBS and IS tax-reform groups: element order, required fields, codes, patterns and lengths), the access key and its check digit, the CNPJ and CPF check digits, each item's quantity times unit price, the totals against the items and vNF against its parts, the IBS and CBS totals against the items (since 2026-11-12) and vNFTot against the items' vItem, each item's CST and cClassTrib against the published classification table with its rate reduction (since 2026-11-13), and CFOP against the direction of the operation. Where a finding matches a SEFAZ rejection, it names the cStat. It does not check the signature, the state's registration records, tax rates (including the IBS and CBS rates themselves), NCM or CEST lists, and a clean result is not an authorisation by SEFAZ.
- [Can this checker validate a Brazilian NFS-e?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/brazil-nfe-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Yes for the national layout, since 2026-11-12. Drop a DPS or an issued NFS-e in the national standard (Padrão Nacional) and it is read against the schema of the 2026-10-20 package, with the DPS Id, the access key and its check digit, CNPJ and CPF check digits, and the ISSQN and IBS/CBS sums as the schema's own field notes state them, and the CST and cClassTrib against the published classification table (since 2026-11-13). It names what moved when a file from an earlier layout fails. Since 2026-11-13 it also reads municipal NFS-e in the ABRASF layout (2.00 to 2.04 schemas; identifier and value checks for 1.00). It does not check the signature or municipal rates, and a clean result is not an acceptance by the national system or the city. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.

This is a draft, not final rules.

- [What does an INV rule code from the e-invoice preflight mean?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/what-does-this-e-invoice-error-code-mean.html#faq): Each INV code names one specific defect the preflight found in the invoice you gave it, so that a report can point at a single field rather than saying the document is bad. INV-012 means a line's net does not equal quantity times unit price. INV-017 means gross does not equal net plus tax. INV-018 means the tax total does not match what the line tax percentages add up to. INV-025 means an IBAN fails its published country checksum. INV-045 means a credit-transfer or SEPA payment means was given without an IBAN. These are the tool's own identifiers for its own checks, not codes issued by a tax authority or a submission network.
- [Are these the same codes my e-invoicing network sends back on a rejection?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/what-does-this-e-invoice-error-code-mean.html#faq): No. INV codes are internal to this preflight and are not EN 16931 BR- rule identifiers, Peppol validation artefact codes, or any authority's rejection codes. The preflight runs before submission and reports arithmetic and structural observations about the file in front of it. A network's own validator may accept a document this tool flags, or reject one it passes. Use the INV code to find and fix the underlying field; quote your network's code, not this one, when you contact them.
- [Why did the preflight flag my invoice when the totals look right?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/what-does-this-e-invoice-error-code-mean.html#faq): The most common cause is cent rounding applied at a different stage than the checker applies it. The preflight rounds half-up to cents and compares the rounded figures, so a line net carried at full floating precision in an ERP export can disagree with the printed total by a cent and raise INV-012, INV-014 or INV-018. The second most common cause is a field that is present but empty rather than absent — an empty string is not a missing field, and several checks treat the two differently. The report names the JSON path of the value it compared, which is the fastest way to see which of the two it is. This page is a reference for the tool's own checks. It is not tax or legal advice, and it does not tell you whether a document is legally valid or will be accepted by any network. Open the preflight and drop a JSON, UBL, CII, or Factur-X file to see which of these fire on your own invoice. Nothing you provide is uploaded.
- [What is Smart Invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/zambia-smart-invoice-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): The Zambia Revenue Authority's system through which VAT-registered taxpayers issue and report invoices.
- [When did it become mandatory?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/zambia-smart-invoice-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Most guides say 1 July 2024, with a grace period to 30 September 2024. One timeline says 21 July 2024. Check the ZRA's notices.
- [Does it affect my input VAT?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/zambia-smart-invoice-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): Guides report that from 1 January 2025 input VAT is deductible only for transactions recorded through Smart Invoice, except for suppliers exempted by law. Confirm with the ZRA.
- [Can this checker validate a Smart Invoice?](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/einvoice-preflight/zambia-smart-invoice-e-invoice-mandate.html#faq): No. It has no Zambian rules and does not connect to the ZRA. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not ZRA acceptance. Open the E-invoice Preflight checker to validate your own invoice file.
