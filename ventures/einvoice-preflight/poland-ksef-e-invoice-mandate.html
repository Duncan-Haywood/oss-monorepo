Poland's KSeF e-invoice mandate: what applies now

Since 2026, VAT invoices in Poland go through KSeF, the national e-invoicing system, in a structured XML format called FA(3). Below: the dates, the format that counts, and how to test the invoice data before it goes to a customer.

The timeline (domestic invoices)

DateWhat changes
1 Feb 2026KSeF is mandatory for taxpayers whose sales in the reference year were above PLN 200 million.
1 Apr 2026KSeF is mandatory for the other VAT-registered businesses.
1 Jan 2027Businesses whose monthly invoiced sales stay at or below PLN 10,000 join; until 31 December 2026 they may stay outside.

Exceptions and transitional rules exist, and sources disagree on when penalties for invoices issued outside KSeF begin. This is a summary, not tax advice; confirm with your accountant or the Ministry of Finance KSeF guidance.

Which file counts, and how it travels

The invoice is an FA(3) XML document submitted to the KSeF platform, which returns a KSeF number; the buyer retrieves it from KSeF. It is a national schema, not Peppol BIS or XRechnung. Businesses that already produce EN 16931 invoices for other countries map them to FA(3) for Poland. A PDF is only a visualisation.

Common questions

When did Poland's KSeF e-invoicing mandate start?

KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur, the national e-invoicing system) became mandatory on 1 February 2026 for taxpayers whose sales in the reference year were above PLN 200 million, and on 1 April 2026 for other VAT-registered businesses. Businesses whose monthly invoiced sales stay at or below PLN 10,000 may stay outside the system until 31 December 2026 and join on 1 January 2027. Confirm your own date with your accountant or the Ministry of Finance; this page is a summary, not advice.

Which invoice format does KSeF require?

A structured XML invoice in the national FA(3) schema, issued and received through the KSeF platform, which assigns each invoice a KSeF number. It is not the Peppol or EN 16931 format; EN 16931 and Peppol invoices have to be mapped to FA(3) for Poland. A PDF is only a visualisation. This checker does not validate FA(3); it checks EN 16931, Peppol BIS 3.0, XRechnung and Factur-X files, which helps when you sell across borders or build the invoice data before mapping it.

What are the penalties under KSeF?

Sources disagree on timing, so check the Ministry of Finance guidance. Reporting that the penalty regime for invoices issued outside KSeF applies from 1 January 2027 is common, with penalties tied to the VAT on the invoice. Treat any amount on this page as unconfirmed and check the current statute before relying on it.

How do I check an invoice before sending it to a Polish customer?

If the customer takes a Peppol or EN 16931 invoice, drop the file into the checker and tick the Peppol option: it tests identifiers, electronic addresses, totals and tax breakdown arithmetic, and VAT, IBAN and BIC typos, each with a rule ID and a fix. For a Polish domestic invoice in FA(3) use the Ministry's own validation and test environment. Nothing is uploaded when you use the page; it is not legal advice.
