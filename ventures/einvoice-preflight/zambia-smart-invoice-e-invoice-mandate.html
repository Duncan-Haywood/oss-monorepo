Zambia: ZRA Smart Invoice

Zambia requires VAT-registered taxpayers to issue invoices through the Zambia Revenue Authority's Smart Invoice system. Below: what guides report, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelInvoices are issued through ZRA Smart Invoice and sent to the authority. A Zambian system, not EN 16931.
Legal basisGuides cite Statutory Instrument No. 58 of 2023 on electronic invoicing for VAT-registered taxpayers.
DatesA pilot on 15 December 2023 and a launch on 19 March 2024 are reported. Most guides give 1 July 2024 as the mandatory date; one timeline says 21 July 2024.
Grace periodZRA is quoted as granting a grace period for penalties from 1 July to 30 September 2024, with enforcement from 1 October 2024.
Input VATFrom 1 January 2025 input VAT deductions are reported to be allowed only for transactions recorded through Smart Invoice, apart from exempt suppliers.

Common questions

What is Smart Invoice?

The Zambia Revenue Authority's system through which VAT-registered taxpayers issue and report invoices.

When did it become mandatory?

Most guides say 1 July 2024, with a grace period to 30 September 2024. One timeline says 21 July 2024. Check the ZRA's notices.

Does it affect my input VAT?

Guides report that from 1 January 2025 input VAT is deductible only for transactions recorded through Smart Invoice, except for suppliers exempted by law. Confirm with the ZRA.

Can this checker validate a Smart Invoice?

No. It has no Zambian rules and does not connect to the ZRA. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not ZRA acceptance.
