BR-DE-25-b: Direct debit also carries transfer account or card details

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Wenn BT-81 "Payment means type code" einen Schlüssel für Lastschriften enthält (59), dürfen BG-17 und BG-18 nicht übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-81, BG-17, BG-18

What does BR-DE-25-b mean?

A payment means with the direct debit code 59 in BT-81 must not also contain a payee account for credit transfer (BG-17) or payment card information (BG-18). In CII the test also refuses the payee's and the payer's bank (BIC) elements inside that payment means.

Usual causes

  • The seller's own IBAN is added to every payment means, including direct debits.
  • The seller's IBAN was entered where the buyer's debited account belongs.
  • In CII, a BIC element (ram:PayeeSpecifiedCreditorFinancialInstitution or ram:PayerSpecifiedDebtorFinancialInstitution) was left in the direct debit payment means.

How do I fix BR-DE-25-b?

  1. Remove cac:PayeeFinancialAccount and cac:CardAccount from the direct debit cac:PaymentMeans (UBL); the buyer's IBAN belongs in cac:PaymentMandate/cac:PayerFinancialAccount.
  2. In CII remove ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount, ram:ApplicableTradeSettlementFinancialCard and both financial institution elements from that payment means.
  3. If the buyer may also pay by transfer, add that as a separate payment means with code 58.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>59</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cac:PaymentMandate>
    <cbc:ID>MANDAT-2026-001</cbc:ID>
    <cac:PayerFinancialAccount>
      <cbc:ID>DE02120300000000202051</cbc:ID>
    </cac:PayerFinancialAccount>
  </cac:PaymentMandate>
</cac:PaymentMeans>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
