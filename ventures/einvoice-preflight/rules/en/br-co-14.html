E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-CO-14: Total VAT does not equal the sum of the VAT breakdown

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Invoice total VAT amount (BT-110) = Σ VAT category tax amount (BT-117).
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-110, BT-117

What does BR-CO-14 mean?

The invoice total VAT amount (BT-110) must equal the VAT amounts of all VAT breakdown rows (BT-117) added up and rounded to two decimals. For example, 19.00 at 19 % plus 3.15 at 7 % gives a total VAT of 22.15. In UBL, a tax total with no breakdown rows inside it is not checked, which is how the second tax total in the VAT accounting currency (BT-111) passes.

Usual causes

  • The total VAT was computed line by line while the breakdown was computed per rate, so they differ by rounding.
  • A VAT rate or category was added or removed in the breakdown without updating the total.
  • In UBL, the breakdown rows were put in a second cac:TaxTotal instead of the one carrying BT-110.

How do I fix BR-CO-14?

  1. Add up the VAT amount of every breakdown row (19.00 + 3.15 = 22.15) and use that as the total VAT.
  2. In UBL put the total in cac:TaxTotal/cbc:TaxAmount with the rows as cac:TaxSubtotal in the same cac:TaxTotal; in CII put it in ram:TaxTotalAmount with currencyID equal to the invoice currency.
  3. If rounding per line and per rate disagree, take the breakdown amounts as the reference and adjust the total.
