BR-DE-23-a: Credit transfer without the payee account

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Wenn BT-81 "Payment means type code" einen Schlüssel für Überweisungen enthält (30, 58), muss BG-17 "CREDIT TRANSFER" übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-81, BG-17

What does BR-DE-23-a mean?

When a payment means uses a credit transfer code (30 or 58 in BT-81), it must carry the credit transfer group (BG-17), that is the account the buyer should pay into. The check is made for each payment means separately.

Usual causes

  • The bank details are printed only on the PDF or in a note, not in the structured payment means.
  • Code 30 or 58 was chosen as a default even though no account is given.
  • The account was put in the wrong place, for example under the payer's account instead of the payee's.

How do I fix BR-DE-23-a?

  1. Add cac:PayeeFinancialAccount with your IBAN in cbc:ID inside the same cac:PaymentMeans (UBL), or ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount with ram:IBANID (CII).
  2. Optionally add the account name and the BIC in the same group.
  3. If the buyer pays another way (card, direct debit), use the code that matches it instead of 30 or 58.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>58</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
    <cbc:ID>DE02120300000000202051</cbc:ID>
    <cbc:Name>Beispiel GmbH</cbc:Name>
  </cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
</cac:PaymentMeans>

CII

<ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans>
  <ram:TypeCode>58</ram:TypeCode>
  <ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount>
    <ram:IBANID>DE02120300000000202051</ram:IBANID>
    <ram:AccountName>Beispiel GmbH</ram:AccountName>
  </ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount>
</ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
