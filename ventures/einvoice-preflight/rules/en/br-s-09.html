E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-S-09: Standard-rated VAT amount is not taxable amount times rate

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

The VAT category tax amount (BT-117) in a VAT breakdown (BG-23) where VAT category code (BT-118) is "Standard rated" shall equal the VAT category taxable amount (BT-116) multiplied by the VAT category rate (BT-119).
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-117, BG-23, BT-118, BT-116, BT-119

What does BR-S-09 mean?

In each VAT breakdown entry with category S, the VAT amount (BT-117) must equal the taxable amount (BT-116) multiplied by the rate (BT-119), rounded to two decimals. The check compares absolute values and accepts a difference of less than 1.00, so a failure means the amount is clearly wrong, not just a rounding cent.

Usual causes

  • The VAT was added up line by line and the per-line results drifted, or the taxable amount was changed afterwards without recalculating the VAT.
  • The rate in the breakdown is not the rate actually used for the calculation (for example 19 written, 7 applied).
  • The VAT of two rates was put into one breakdown entry.
  • The rate was written as a fraction (0.19) instead of a percentage (19).

How do I fix BR-S-09?

  1. Write the rate as a percentage, for example 19 for 19 %.
  2. Calculate the VAT amount as taxable amount × rate ÷ 100 and round to two decimals.
  3. Make the total VAT amount (BT-110) the sum of the breakdown VAT amounts and update the payable amount.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:TaxTotal>  <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">26.60</cbc:TaxAmount>  <cac:TaxSubtotal>    <cbc:TaxableAmount currencyID="EUR">140.00</cbc:TaxableAmount>    <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">26.60</cbc:TaxAmount>    <cac:TaxCategory>      <cbc:ID>S</cbc:ID>      <cbc:Percent>19</cbc:Percent>      <cac:TaxScheme>        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>      </cac:TaxScheme>    </cac:TaxCategory>  </cac:TaxSubtotal></cac:TaxTotal>

CII

<ram:ApplicableTradeTax>  <ram:CalculatedAmount>26.60</ram:CalculatedAmount>  <ram:TypeCode>VAT</ram:TypeCode>  <ram:BasisAmount>140.00</ram:BasisAmount>  <ram:CategoryCode>S</ram:CategoryCode>  <ram:RateApplicablePercent>19</ram:RateApplicablePercent></ram:ApplicableTradeTax>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
