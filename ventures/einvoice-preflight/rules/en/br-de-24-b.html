BR-DE-24-b: Card payment also carries transfer or direct debit details

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Wenn BT-81 "Payment means type code" einen Schlüssel für Kartenzahlungen enthält (48, 54, 55), dürfen BG-17 und BG-19 nicht übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-81, BG-17, BG-19

What does BR-DE-24-b mean?

A payment means with a card payment code (48, 54 or 55 in BT-81) must not also contain a payee account for credit transfer (BG-17) or direct debit information (BG-19). In CII the test also fails if the invoice anywhere carries a mandate reference, a creditor identifier (BT-90) or a debited account IBAN.

Usual causes

  • The seller's bank account is added to every payment means by default.
  • Several ways to pay were put into one payment means instead of separate ones.
  • In CII, direct debit fields from another customer's template were left in the invoice.

How do I fix BR-DE-24-b?

  1. Remove cac:PayeeFinancialAccount and cac:PaymentMandate from the card cac:PaymentMeans (UBL).
  2. In CII, remove ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount and ram:PayerPartyDebtorFinancialAccount from the card payment means, and ram:CreditorReferenceID and ram:DirectDebitMandateID from the invoice.
  3. If the buyer may also pay by transfer, add a second payment means with code 58 and the account there.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>54</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cac:CardAccount>
    <cbc:PrimaryAccountNumberID>1234</cbc:PrimaryAccountNumberID>
    <cbc:NetworkID>VISA</cbc:NetworkID>
  </cac:CardAccount>
</cac:PaymentMeans>
<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>58</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
    <cbc:ID>DE02120300000000202051</cbc:ID>
  </cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
</cac:PaymentMeans>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
