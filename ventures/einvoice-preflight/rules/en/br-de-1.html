E-invoice rule explained · XRechnung

BR-DE-1: Payment instructions are missing

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Eine Rechnung (INVOICE) muss Angaben zu "PAYMENT INSTRUCTIONS" (BG-16) enthalten.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BG-16

What does BR-DE-1 mean?

XRechnung requires every invoice and credit note to carry payment instructions (BG-16). The check only looks for the group itself (cac:PaymentMeans in UBL), but the group always needs at least a payment means code saying how the money is paid, for example 58 for a SEPA credit transfer.

Usual causes

  • The invoicing software leaves the payment block out because it is optional in plain EN 16931.
  • The bank details are only printed in a free-text note or the footer of a PDF, not in the structured fields.
  • A credit note was exported without payment instructions because no payment was expected.

How do I fix BR-DE-1?

  1. Add one payment instructions group with a payment means code (BT-81), such as 58 for SEPA credit transfer or 59 for SEPA direct debit.
  2. For a credit transfer, give the account the buyer should pay into (IBAN in BT-84) and, if you like, a remittance reference (BT-83).
  3. In UBL this is cac:PaymentMeans; in CII it is ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans inside ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeSettlement.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>58</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cbc:PaymentID>RE-2026-0042</cbc:PaymentID>
  <cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
    <cbc:ID>DE02120300000000202051</cbc:ID>
  </cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
</cac:PaymentMeans>

CII

<ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeSettlement>
  <ram:PaymentReference>RE-2026-0042</ram:PaymentReference>
  <ram:InvoiceCurrencyCode>EUR</ram:InvoiceCurrencyCode>
  <ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans>
    <ram:TypeCode>58</ram:TypeCode>
    <ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount>
      <ram:IBANID>DE02120300000000202051</ram:IBANID>
    </ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount>
  </ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans>
  <!-- VAT breakdown and totals follow -->
</ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeSettlement>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
