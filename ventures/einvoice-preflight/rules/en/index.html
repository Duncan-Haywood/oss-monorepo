Where do these rule ids come from?

From the official validation artefacts: the KoSIT XRechnung Schematron for BR-DE rules, the CEN/TC 434 EN 16931 validation artefacts for BR- rules, and OpenPeppol's BIS Billing 3 rules for PEPPOL- rules. Each page names the release and links it.

Is a warning a rejection?

No. A rule with severity warning or information does not reject the invoice on its own in the official rule sets, but a receiving platform may be stricter. An error (fatal) rejects it.
