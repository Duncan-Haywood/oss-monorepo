BR-DE-19: Payee account is not a valid IBAN for a SEPA credit transfer

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

"Payment account identifier" (BT-84) soll eine korrekte IBAN enthalten, wenn in "Payment means type code" (BT-81) mit dem Code 58 SEPA als Zahlungsmittel gefordert wird.
Severity
Warning (should be fixed; not a rejection on its own)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-84, BT-81

What does BR-DE-19 mean?

When the payment means code (BT-81) is 58 (SEPA credit transfer), the payment account identifier (BT-84) should be a valid IBAN: two capital letters for the country, two check digits, then the account part, with a correct check sum. Spaces are ignored. This is a warning, and it is not applied to code 30.

Usual causes

  • A typo or a swapped digit makes the IBAN check sum fail.
  • A national account number, a BIC or text such as "see letterhead" was put in the account field.
  • The country code is in lower case (de02...) or the IBAN carries extra characters such as dashes.

How do I fix BR-DE-19?

  1. Copy the IBAN from a bank statement or your bank's online banking rather than retyping it.
  2. Put only the IBAN in cbc:ID of cac:PayeeFinancialAccount (UBL) or ram:IBANID of ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount (CII); spaces are allowed, dashes and other text are not.
  3. If you are not paid to an IBAN, use payment means code 30 instead of 58.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>58</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
    <cbc:ID>DE02120300000000202051</cbc:ID>
  </cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
</cac:PaymentMeans>

CII

<ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans>
  <ram:TypeCode>58</ram:TypeCode>
  <ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount>
    <ram:IBANID>DE02120300000000202051</ram:IBANID>
  </ram:PayeePartyCreditorFinancialAccount>
</ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementPaymentMeans>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
