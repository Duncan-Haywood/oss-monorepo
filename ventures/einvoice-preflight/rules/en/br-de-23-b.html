BR-DE-23-b: Credit transfer also carries card or direct debit details

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Wenn BT-81 "Payment means type code" einen Schlüssel für Überweisungen enthält (30, 58), dürfen BG-18 und BG-19 nicht übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-81, BG-18, BG-19

What does BR-DE-23-b mean?

A payment means with a credit transfer code (30 or 58 in BT-81) must not also contain payment card information (BG-18) or direct debit information (BG-19). In UBL the check looks inside that payment means; in CII it also fails if the invoice anywhere carries a mandate reference, a creditor identifier (BT-90) or a debited account IBAN, because CII keeps those outside the payment means.

Usual causes

  • A template fills in card or mandate fields for every invoice, whatever the payment means.
  • The invoice offers several ways to pay but puts them all into one payment means.
  • In CII, a creditor identifier or mandate reference from a direct debit customer was left in a credit transfer invoice.

How do I fix BR-DE-23-b?

  1. Remove cac:CardAccount and cac:PaymentMandate from the credit transfer cac:PaymentMeans (UBL).
  2. In CII, remove ram:ApplicableTradeSettlementFinancialCard, ram:PayerPartyDebtorFinancialAccount, ram:CreditorReferenceID and ram:DirectDebitMandateID when you are paid by transfer.
  3. If the buyer can choose, give each way of paying its own payment means with its own code.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:PaymentMeans>
  <cbc:PaymentMeansCode>58</cbc:PaymentMeansCode>
  <cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
    <cbc:ID>DE02120300000000202051</cbc:ID>
  </cac:PayeeFinancialAccount>
</cac:PaymentMeans>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
