PEPPOL-EN16931-R046: Item net price does not equal gross price minus price discount

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Item net price MUST equal (Gross price - Allowance amount) when gross price is provided.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL
Business terms
BT-146, BT-147, BT-148

What does PEPPOL-EN16931-R046 mean?

When a line gives a gross price (BT-148, cbc:BaseAmount in the price's allowance), the net price (BT-146, cbc:PriceAmount) must be exactly the gross price minus the price discount (BT-147, cbc:Amount). There is no rounding tolerance. If no gross price is given, the rule does not apply.

Usual causes

  • The net price was rounded to fewer decimals than the gross price and discount produce, for example 8.33 against 9.99 - 1.665.
  • The discount is a percentage and the software filled in the percentage, or the discount per line total, instead of the amount per unit.
  • The gross and net prices were swapped, or the net price already includes another discount.

How do I fix PEPPOL-EN16931-R046?

  1. Make cbc:PriceAmount = cbc:BaseAmount - cbc:Amount exactly, using the same decimals in all three.
  2. Give the discount as an amount per price unit (the same unit as the price), not as a line total or a percentage.
  3. If you do not need to show the gross price, remove the price allowance and give only the net price.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:Price>
  <cbc:PriceAmount currencyID="EUR">90.00</cbc:PriceAmount>
  <cbc:BaseQuantity unitCode="C62">1</cbc:BaseQuantity>
  <cac:AllowanceCharge>
    <cbc:ChargeIndicator>false</cbc:ChargeIndicator>
    <cbc:Amount currencyID="EUR">10.00</cbc:Amount>
    <cbc:BaseAmount currencyID="EUR">100.00</cbc:BaseAmount>
  </cac:AllowanceCharge>
</cac:Price>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
