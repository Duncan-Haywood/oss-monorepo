E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-CO-17: VAT amount in the breakdown does not match base times rate

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

VAT category tax amount (BT-117) = VAT category taxable amount (BT-116) x (VAT category rate (BT-119) / 100), rounded to two decimals.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-117, BT-116, BT-119

What does BR-CO-17 mean?

In each VAT breakdown row, the VAT amount (BT-117) should be the taxable amount (BT-116) times the rate (BT-119) divided by 100, rounded to two decimals; for example 145.00 × 19 % = 27.55. The official test is looser than its message: it accepts any amount less than 1.00 away from that result (up to 1.00 inclusive in CII). With a rate of 0, or no rate, the VAT amount must round to zero, that is be under 0.50.

Usual causes

  • The VAT amount was copied from another row or another invoice.
  • The taxable amount was changed (for example by a document-level allowance) without recalculating the VAT.
  • A 0 % row (exempt, reverse charge, export) carries a VAT amount.
  • The rate was entered as a fraction (0.19) instead of a percentage (19).

How do I fix BR-CO-17?

  1. Recalculate each row: taxable amount × rate ÷ 100, rounded to two decimals (145.00 × 19 ÷ 100 = 27.55).
  2. For a 0 % category, set the VAT amount of the row to 0.00.
  3. Enter the rate as a percentage in cbc:Percent (UBL) or ram:RateApplicablePercent (CII).

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:TaxSubtotal>
  <cbc:TaxableAmount currencyID="EUR">145.00</cbc:TaxableAmount>
  <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">27.55</cbc:TaxAmount>
  <cac:TaxCategory>
    <cbc:ID>S</cbc:ID>
    <cbc:Percent>19</cbc:Percent>
    <cac:TaxScheme>
      <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>
    </cac:TaxScheme>
  </cac:TaxCategory>
</cac:TaxSubtotal>

CII

<ram:ApplicableTradeTax>
  <ram:CalculatedAmount>27.55</ram:CalculatedAmount>
  <ram:TypeCode>VAT</ram:TypeCode>
  <ram:BasisAmount>145.00</ram:BasisAmount>
  <ram:CategoryCode>S</ram:CategoryCode>
  <ram:RateApplicablePercent>19</ram:RateApplicablePercent>
</ram:ApplicableTradeTax>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
