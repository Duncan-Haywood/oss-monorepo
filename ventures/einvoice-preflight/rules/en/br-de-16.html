E-invoice rule explained · XRechnung

BR-DE-16: Seller VAT ID, tax number or tax representative is missing

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Wenn in einer Rechnung die Steuercodes S, Z, E, AE, K, G, L oder M verwendet werden, muss mindestens eines der Elemente "Seller VAT identifier" (BT-31), "Seller tax registration identifier" (BT-32) oder "SELLER TAX REPRESENTATIVE PARTY" (BG-11) übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-31, BT-32, BG-11

What does BR-DE-16 mean?

If any invoice line or document-level allowance or charge uses VAT category S, Z, E, AE, K, G, L or M, XRechnung requires at least one of: the seller's VAT identifier (BT-31), the seller's tax registration number such as the German Steuernummer (BT-32), or a seller tax representative (BG-11). In practice that is almost every invoice; only one where everything is category O (not subject to VAT) escapes the rule.

Usual causes

  • The company's VAT ID and tax number are not set up in the invoicing software.
  • They are printed on the PDF footer but not written into the XML.
  • In CII, the tax registration was sent without schemeID "VA" (VAT ID) or "FC" (tax number), so the check does not count it.

How do I fix BR-DE-16?

  1. Send the seller's VAT ID, its tax number, or both: in UBL each as cbc:CompanyID in the seller's cac:PartyTaxScheme (with tax scheme VAT for the VAT ID); in CII as ram:SpecifiedTaxRegistration/ram:ID with schemeID="VA" or schemeID="FC".
  2. If a tax representative issues the invoice for you, send the tax representative group (BG-11) instead.
  3. Store these numbers in your company settings so every invoice carries them.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:AccountingSupplierParty>  <cac:Party>    <!-- endpoint, name and address come first -->    <cac:PartyTaxScheme>      <cbc:CompanyID>DE123456789</cbc:CompanyID>      <cac:TaxScheme>        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>      </cac:TaxScheme>    </cac:PartyTaxScheme>    <!-- legal entity and contact follow -->  </cac:Party></cac:AccountingSupplierParty>

CII

<ram:SellerTradeParty>  <ram:Name>Example GmbH</ram:Name>  <!-- contact and postal address come here -->  <ram:SpecifiedTaxRegistration>    <ram:ID schemeID="VA">DE123456789</ram:ID>  </ram:SpecifiedTaxRegistration></ram:SellerTradeParty>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
