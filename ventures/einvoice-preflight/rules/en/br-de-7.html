E-invoice rule explained · XRechnung

BR-DE-7: Seller contact email address is missing

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Das Element "Seller contact email address" (BT-43) muss übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-43

What does BR-DE-7 mean?

When the seller contact group is present, XRechnung requires an email address (BT-43) in it. This rule only checks that the field is not empty; other XRechnung rules check the address's form.

Usual causes

  • The contact carries a name and phone number but no email address.
  • The email address is only shown on the PDF or in the sender's email, not in the XML.
  • The address was put in the seller's electronic address (BT-34) instead of the contact.

How do I fix BR-DE-7?

  1. Add the email address: cbc:ElectronicMail in the seller's cac:Contact (UBL) or ram:URIID in ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication (CII).
  2. Use a mailbox someone reads, such as your accounts address.
  3. Keep it in the contact even if you already send it as the seller's electronic address (BT-34): the two are separate fields.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:Contact>  <cbc:Name>Accounts team</cbc:Name>  <cbc:Telephone>+49 30 1234567</cbc:Telephone>  <cbc:ElectronicMail>invoices@example.com</cbc:ElectronicMail></cac:Contact>

CII

<ram:DefinedTradeContact>  <ram:PersonName>Accounts team</ram:PersonName>  <ram:TelephoneUniversalCommunication>    <ram:CompleteNumber>+49 30 1234567</ram:CompleteNumber>  </ram:TelephoneUniversalCommunication>  <ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication>    <ram:URIID>invoices@example.com</ram:URIID>  </ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication></ram:DefinedTradeContact>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
