E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-CO-26: Seller has no identifier, registration number or VAT ID

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

In order for the buyer to automatically identify a supplier, the Seller identifier (BT-29), the Seller legal registration identifier (BT-30) and/or the Seller VAT identifier (BT-31) shall be present.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-29, BT-30, BT-31

What does BR-CO-26 mean?

So that the buyer can identify the seller automatically, the seller must have at least one of: a seller identifier (BT-29), a legal registration identifier such as a commercial register number (BT-30), or a VAT identifier (BT-31). A national tax number (BT-32, the German Steuernummer) does not count, and in UBL neither does a party identification with schemeID SEPA, which is the creditor identifier for direct debits.

Usual causes

  • Only the seller's name and address were filled in.
  • The seller only gave a national tax number (Steuernummer) and no VAT identifier, for example a small business without a VAT ID.
  • In UBL, the only cac:PartyIdentification is the SEPA creditor identifier.
  • In CII, the VAT identifier was sent with schemeID FC instead of VA.

How do I fix BR-CO-26?

  1. Add the seller's VAT identifier if it has one: in UBL a cac:PartyTaxScheme with tax scheme VAT, in CII ram:SpecifiedTaxRegistration with schemeID VA.
  2. Or add the commercial register number or other legal registration in cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:CompanyID (UBL) or ram:SpecifiedLegalOrganization/ram:ID (CII).
  3. Or add a seller identifier, such as a GLN or an ID the buyer assigned, in cac:PartyIdentification/cbc:ID (UBL) or ram:ID / ram:GlobalID (CII).

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:AccountingSupplierParty>
  <cac:Party>
    <cac:PartyTaxScheme>
      <cbc:CompanyID>DE123456789</cbc:CompanyID>
      <cac:TaxScheme>
        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>
      </cac:TaxScheme>
    </cac:PartyTaxScheme>
    <cac:PartyLegalEntity>
      <cbc:RegistrationName>Muster GmbH</cbc:RegistrationName>
      <cbc:CompanyID>HRB 12345</cbc:CompanyID>
    </cac:PartyLegalEntity>
  </cac:Party>
</cac:AccountingSupplierParty>

CII

<ram:SellerTradeParty>
  <ram:Name>Muster GmbH</ram:Name>
  <ram:SpecifiedLegalOrganization>
    <ram:ID>HRB 12345</ram:ID>
  </ram:SpecifiedLegalOrganization>
  <ram:SpecifiedTaxRegistration>
    <ram:ID schemeID="VA">DE123456789</ram:ID>
  </ram:SpecifiedTaxRegistration>
</ram:SellerTradeParty>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
