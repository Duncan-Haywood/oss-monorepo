E-invoice rule explained · Peppol BIS

PEPPOL-EN16931-R001: Business process (profile ID) is missing

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Business process MUST be provided.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-23

What does PEPPOL-EN16931-R001 mean?

Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 requires the Business process type (BT-23) on every invoice and credit note. In UBL this is cbc:ProfileID; in CII it is the ID inside ram:BusinessProcessSpecifiedDocumentContextParameter. The rule only checks that the element is there; PEPPOL-EN16931-R007 checks its value.

Usual causes

  • The software exports plain EN 16931 or XRechnung, where the business process is optional, and the file is then sent over Peppol.
  • The element was dropped by a converter or a hand-made template.
  • The value was put in cbc:CustomizationID (BT-24) instead of a separate cbc:ProfileID.

How do I fix PEPPOL-EN16931-R001?

  1. Add cbc:ProfileID right after cbc:CustomizationID (UBL), or ram:BusinessProcessSpecifiedDocumentContextParameter/ram:ID in rsm:ExchangedDocumentContext (CII).
  2. Use the standard Peppol value urn:fdc:peppol.eu:2017:poacc:billing:01:1.0 unless your receiver has agreed another approved process.
  3. Validate again; PEPPOL-EN16931-R007 will then check that the value is an approved one.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cbc:CustomizationID>urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017#compliant#urn:fdc:peppol.eu:2017:poacc:billing:3.0</cbc:CustomizationID><cbc:ProfileID>urn:fdc:peppol.eu:2017:poacc:billing:01:1.0</cbc:ProfileID>

CII

<rsm:ExchangedDocumentContext>  <ram:BusinessProcessSpecifiedDocumentContextParameter>    <ram:ID>urn:fdc:peppol.eu:2017:poacc:billing:01:1.0</ram:ID>  </ram:BusinessProcessSpecifiedDocumentContextParameter>  <ram:GuidelineSpecifiedDocumentContextParameter>    <ram:ID>urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017#compliant#urn:fdc:peppol.eu:2017:poacc:billing:3.0</ram:ID>  </ram:GuidelineSpecifiedDocumentContextParameter></rsm:ExchangedDocumentContext>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
