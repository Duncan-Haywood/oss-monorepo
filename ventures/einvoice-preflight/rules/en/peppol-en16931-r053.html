E-invoice rule explained · Peppol BIS

PEPPOL-EN16931-R053: There must be exactly one VAT total with VAT breakdown

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Only one tax total with tax subtotals MUST be provided.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-110, BG-23

What does PEPPOL-EN16931-R053 mean?

In UBL the invoice must contain exactly one cac:TaxTotal that has cac:TaxSubtotal children (the VAT total, BT-110, with its breakdown, BG-23); none or two or more fail. A second cac:TaxTotal is allowed only without subtotals, for the VAT total in accounting currency. The CII version checks that exactly one ram:TaxTotalAmount in the invoice currency appears in the document totals.

Usual causes

  • One cac:TaxTotal was written per VAT rate instead of one cac:TaxTotal with several cac:TaxSubtotal entries.
  • The VAT total in accounting currency was also given tax subtotals.
  • The VAT total is missing completely, for example on an invoice with only exempt items (CII: ram:TaxTotalAmount missing or written twice in the invoice currency).

How do I fix PEPPOL-EN16931-R053?

  1. Write a single cac:TaxTotal with the total cbc:TaxAmount and one cac:TaxSubtotal per VAT category and rate.
  2. Keep it even when VAT is zero: TaxAmount 0.00 with a subtotal for the exempt or zero-rated category.
  3. In CII, give ram:TaxTotalAmount once in the invoice currency in ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation; a second one is only for the accounting currency.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:TaxTotal>
  <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">26.00</cbc:TaxAmount>
  <cac:TaxSubtotal>
    <cbc:TaxableAmount currencyID="EUR">100.00</cbc:TaxableAmount>
    <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">19.00</cbc:TaxAmount>
    <cac:TaxCategory>
      <cbc:ID>S</cbc:ID>
      <cbc:Percent>19</cbc:Percent>
      <cac:TaxScheme>
        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>
      </cac:TaxScheme>
    </cac:TaxCategory>
  </cac:TaxSubtotal>
  <cac:TaxSubtotal>
    <cbc:TaxableAmount currencyID="EUR">100.00</cbc:TaxableAmount>
    <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">7.00</cbc:TaxAmount>
    <cac:TaxCategory>
      <cbc:ID>S</cbc:ID>
      <cbc:Percent>7</cbc:Percent>
      <cac:TaxScheme>
        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>
      </cac:TaxScheme>
    </cac:TaxCategory>
  </cac:TaxSubtotal>
</cac:TaxTotal>

CII

<ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>
  <ram:LineTotalAmount>200.00</ram:LineTotalAmount>
  <ram:TaxBasisTotalAmount>200.00</ram:TaxBasisTotalAmount>
  <ram:TaxTotalAmount currencyID="EUR">26.00</ram:TaxTotalAmount>
  <ram:GrandTotalAmount>226.00</ram:GrandTotalAmount>
  <ram:DuePayableAmount>226.00</ram:DuePayableAmount>
</ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
