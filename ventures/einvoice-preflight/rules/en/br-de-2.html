E-invoice rule explained · XRechnung

BR-DE-2: Seller contact is missing

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Die Gruppe "SELLER CONTACT" (BG-6) muss übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BG-6

What does BR-DE-2 mean?

XRechnung requires a seller contact (BG-6) on every invoice, so the buyer knows whom to ask about it. This rule only checks that the contact group exists; rules BR-DE-5, BR-DE-6 and BR-DE-7 then require a name, a telephone number and an email address inside it.

Usual causes

  • The software only exports the company's name and address, not a contact person.
  • The contact details are printed on the PDF but not written into the XML.
  • The contact was placed under the buyer or the payee instead of the seller.

How do I fix BR-DE-2?

  1. Add a contact group to the seller party: cac:Contact in UBL, ram:DefinedTradeContact in CII.
  2. Fill in a contact name or department, a telephone number and an email address at the same time, so BR-DE-5 to BR-DE-7 pass too.
  3. If your software has a field for an invoicing contact or clerk, fill it in your company settings so every invoice carries it.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:AccountingSupplierParty>
  <cac:Party>
    <!-- endpoint, name, address and tax details come before the contact -->
    <cac:Contact>
      <cbc:Name>Accounts team</cbc:Name>
      <cbc:Telephone>+49 30 1234567</cbc:Telephone>
      <cbc:ElectronicMail>invoices@example.com</cbc:ElectronicMail>
    </cac:Contact>
  </cac:Party>
</cac:AccountingSupplierParty>

CII

<ram:SellerTradeParty>
  <ram:Name>Example GmbH</ram:Name>
  <ram:DefinedTradeContact>
    <ram:PersonName>Accounts team</ram:PersonName>
    <ram:TelephoneUniversalCommunication>
      <ram:CompleteNumber>+49 30 1234567</ram:CompleteNumber>
    </ram:TelephoneUniversalCommunication>
    <ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication>
      <ram:URIID>invoices@example.com</ram:URIID>
    </ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication>
  </ram:DefinedTradeContact>
  <!-- postal address and tax registration follow -->
</ram:SellerTradeParty>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
