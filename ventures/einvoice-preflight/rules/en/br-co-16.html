E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-CO-16: Amount due does not equal total minus prepaid plus rounding

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Amount due for payment (BT-115) = Invoice total amount with VAT (BT-112) -Paid amount (BT-113) +Rounding amount (BT-114).
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-115, BT-112, BT-113, BT-114

What does BR-CO-16 mean?

The amount due for payment (BT-115) must equal the total with VAT (BT-112) minus the amount already paid (BT-113) plus the rounding amount (BT-114); a missing paid or rounding amount counts as zero. For example, 172.55 − 50.00 = 122.55, or with a rounding amount of −0.05, 172.55 − 50.00 − 0.05 = 122.50. The UBL test rounds to two decimals; the CII test compares the values exactly.

Usual causes

  • A prepayment or deposit was deducted from the amount due but not entered as paid amount (BT-113).
  • A cash rounding was applied to the amount due without entering it as rounding amount (BT-114), or with the wrong sign.
  • The paid amount was subtracted from the total with VAT (BT-112) instead of only from the amount due.

How do I fix BR-CO-16?

  1. Compute BT-115 as BT-112 − BT-113 + BT-114 (172.55 − 50.00 + 0.00 = 122.55).
  2. In UBL use cbc:PrepaidAmount, cbc:PayableRoundingAmount and cbc:PayableAmount; in CII ram:TotalPrepaidAmount, ram:RoundingAmount and ram:DuePayableAmount.
  3. Enter a rounding down as a negative rounding amount and a rounding up as a positive one.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:LegalMonetaryTotal>
  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">145.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>
  <cbc:TaxExclusiveAmount currencyID="EUR">145.00</cbc:TaxExclusiveAmount>
  <cbc:TaxInclusiveAmount currencyID="EUR">172.55</cbc:TaxInclusiveAmount>
  <cbc:PrepaidAmount currencyID="EUR">50.00</cbc:PrepaidAmount>
  <cbc:PayableAmount currencyID="EUR">122.55</cbc:PayableAmount>
</cac:LegalMonetaryTotal>

CII

<ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>
  <ram:LineTotalAmount>145.00</ram:LineTotalAmount>
  <ram:TaxBasisTotalAmount>145.00</ram:TaxBasisTotalAmount>
  <ram:TaxTotalAmount currencyID="EUR">27.55</ram:TaxTotalAmount>
  <ram:GrandTotalAmount>172.55</ram:GrandTotalAmount>
  <ram:TotalPrepaidAmount>50.00</ram:TotalPrepaidAmount>
  <ram:DuePayableAmount>122.55</ram:DuePayableAmount>
</ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
