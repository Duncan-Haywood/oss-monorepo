E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-27: Item net price is negative

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

The Item net price (BT-146) shall NOT be negative.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-146

What does BR-27 mean?

The item net price (BT-146) on an invoice line must be zero or more. Returns and credits are shown with a negative quantity (and so a negative line amount), or as an allowance, never with a negative price.

Usual causes

  • A returned item or a refund was entered as a line with a negative unit price.
  • A discount was entered as its own line with a negative price.
  • A credit note was built by flipping the sign of every amount, including the price.

How do I fix BR-27?

  1. Keep the unit price positive and make the quantity negative instead; the line net amount then becomes negative.
  2. For a discount, use a line allowance or a document-level allowance rather than a negative-price line.
  3. For a full reversal, consider issuing a credit note with positive amounts.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:InvoiceLine>
  <cbc:ID>2</cbc:ID>
  <cbc:InvoicedQuantity unitCode="H87">-1</cbc:InvoicedQuantity>
  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">-50.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>
  <cac:Item>
    <cbc:Name>Returned widget</cbc:Name>
  </cac:Item>
  <cac:Price>
    <cbc:PriceAmount currencyID="EUR">50.00</cbc:PriceAmount>
  </cac:Price>
</cac:InvoiceLine>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
