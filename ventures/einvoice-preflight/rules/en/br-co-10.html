E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-CO-10: Sum of line net amounts does not match the invoice lines

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Sum of Invoice line net amount (BT-106) = Σ Invoice line net amount (BT-131).
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-106, BT-131

What does BR-CO-10 mean?

The sum of invoice line net amounts in the totals (BT-106) must equal the net amounts of all invoice lines (BT-131) added up and rounded to two decimals. For example, lines of 100.00 and 50.00 need a BT-106 of exactly 150.00; 149.99 or 150.01 fails.

Usual causes

  • Line amounts were rounded differently from the total, for example the total was computed from unrounded line values.
  • A line was added, deleted or changed after the totals were calculated.
  • The software put the amount after document-level allowances or charges into BT-106 instead of the plain sum of lines.

How do I fix BR-CO-10?

  1. Add up the net amount of every invoice line as written in the file (100.00 + 50.00 = 150.00).
  2. Write that sum in cbc:LineExtensionAmount in cac:LegalMonetaryTotal (UBL) or ram:LineTotalAmount in the header monetary summation (CII).
  3. Put document-level allowances and charges in their own totals (BT-107, BT-108), not into BT-106.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:LegalMonetaryTotal>
  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">150.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>
</cac:LegalMonetaryTotal>
<cac:InvoiceLine>
  <cbc:ID>1</cbc:ID>
  <cbc:InvoicedQuantity unitCode="C62">2</cbc:InvoicedQuantity>
  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">100.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>
</cac:InvoiceLine>
<cac:InvoiceLine>
  <cbc:ID>2</cbc:ID>
  <cbc:InvoicedQuantity unitCode="C62">1</cbc:InvoicedQuantity>
  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">50.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>
</cac:InvoiceLine>

CII

<ram:IncludedSupplyChainTradeLineItem>
  <ram:SpecifiedLineTradeSettlement>
    <ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementLineMonetarySummation>
      <ram:LineTotalAmount>100.00</ram:LineTotalAmount>
    </ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementLineMonetarySummation>
  </ram:SpecifiedLineTradeSettlement>
</ram:IncludedSupplyChainTradeLineItem>
<ram:IncludedSupplyChainTradeLineItem>
  <ram:SpecifiedLineTradeSettlement>
    <ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementLineMonetarySummation>
      <ram:LineTotalAmount>50.00</ram:LineTotalAmount>
    </ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementLineMonetarySummation>
  </ram:SpecifiedLineTradeSettlement>
</ram:IncludedSupplyChainTradeLineItem>
<ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeSettlement>
  <ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>
    <ram:LineTotalAmount>150.00</ram:LineTotalAmount>
  </ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>
</ram:ApplicableHeaderTradeSettlement>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
