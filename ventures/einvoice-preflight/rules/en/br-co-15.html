E-invoice rule explained · EN 16931

BR-CO-15: Total with VAT does not equal total without VAT plus VAT

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Invoice total amount with VAT (BT-112) = Invoice total amount without VAT (BT-109) + Invoice total VAT amount (BT-110).
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-112, BT-109, BT-110

What does BR-CO-15 mean?

The invoice total with VAT (BT-112) must equal the total without VAT (BT-109) plus the total VAT amount (BT-110), rounded to two decimals; for example 145.00 + 27.55 = 172.55. In UBL the test also needs exactly one tax total amount whose currencyID is the invoice currency, so a missing or duplicated one fails too. In CII the rule also passes when BT-112 simply equals BT-109.

Usual causes

  • The total with VAT was computed from unrounded amounts or before a last change to the VAT.
  • In UBL, the VAT total's currencyID differs from cbc:DocumentCurrencyCode, or two cac:TaxTotal elements both carry an amount in the invoice currency.
  • The prepaid amount or a rounding amount was subtracted from BT-112 instead of from the amount due (BT-115).

How do I fix BR-CO-15?

  1. Compute BT-112 as BT-109 + BT-110 (145.00 + 27.55 = 172.55).
  2. In UBL, give exactly one cac:TaxTotal/cbc:TaxAmount the currencyID of the invoice currency; a second tax total in another accounting currency is fine.
  3. Leave prepayments and rounding to BT-113, BT-114 and the amount due.

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cbc:DocumentCurrencyCode>EUR</cbc:DocumentCurrencyCode>
<cac:TaxTotal>
  <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">27.55</cbc:TaxAmount>
  <cac:TaxSubtotal>
    <cbc:TaxableAmount currencyID="EUR">145.00</cbc:TaxableAmount>
    <cbc:TaxAmount currencyID="EUR">27.55</cbc:TaxAmount>
    <cac:TaxCategory>
      <cbc:ID>S</cbc:ID>
      <cbc:Percent>19</cbc:Percent>
      <cac:TaxScheme>
        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>
      </cac:TaxScheme>
    </cac:TaxCategory>
  </cac:TaxSubtotal>
</cac:TaxTotal>
<cac:LegalMonetaryTotal>
  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">145.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>
  <cbc:TaxExclusiveAmount currencyID="EUR">145.00</cbc:TaxExclusiveAmount>
  <cbc:TaxInclusiveAmount currencyID="EUR">172.55</cbc:TaxInclusiveAmount>
  <cbc:PayableAmount currencyID="EUR">172.55</cbc:PayableAmount>
</cac:LegalMonetaryTotal>

CII

<ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>
  <ram:LineTotalAmount>145.00</ram:LineTotalAmount>
  <ram:TaxBasisTotalAmount>145.00</ram:TaxBasisTotalAmount>
  <ram:TaxTotalAmount currencyID="EUR">27.55</ram:TaxTotalAmount>
  <ram:GrandTotalAmount>172.55</ram:GrandTotalAmount>
  <ram:DuePayableAmount>172.55</ram:DuePayableAmount>
</ram:SpecifiedTradeSettlementHeaderMonetarySummation>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
