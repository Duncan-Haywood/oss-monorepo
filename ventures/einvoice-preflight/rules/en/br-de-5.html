E-invoice rule explained · XRechnung

BR-DE-5: Seller contact name is missing

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Das Element "Seller contact point" (BT-41) muss übermittelt werden.
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-41

What does BR-DE-5 mean?

When the seller contact group is present, XRechnung requires a contact point (BT-41): a person's name or a department. In UBL this is cbc:Name in the seller's cac:Contact; in CII either ram:PersonName or ram:DepartmentName is accepted.

Usual causes

  • The contact group carries only a phone number or email address.
  • No clerk or invoicing contact is set up in the software, so the name is written empty.
  • The name was put in another field, such as the company name.

How do I fix BR-DE-5?

  1. Fill in a contact name or a department such as "Accounts": cbc:Name in the seller's cac:Contact (UBL), ram:PersonName or ram:DepartmentName in ram:DefinedTradeContact (CII).
  2. Set a default invoicing contact in your company settings so every invoice carries one.
  3. Keep the telephone number and email address in the same group (BR-DE-6, BR-DE-7).

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:Contact>  <cbc:Name>Accounts team</cbc:Name>  <cbc:Telephone>+49 30 1234567</cbc:Telephone>  <cbc:ElectronicMail>invoices@example.com</cbc:ElectronicMail></cac:Contact>

CII

<ram:DefinedTradeContact>  <ram:DepartmentName>Accounts</ram:DepartmentName>  <ram:TelephoneUniversalCommunication>    <ram:CompleteNumber>+49 30 1234567</ram:CompleteNumber>  </ram:TelephoneUniversalCommunication>  <ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication>    <ram:URIID>invoices@example.com</ram:URIID>  </ram:EmailURIUniversalCommunication></ram:DefinedTradeContact>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
