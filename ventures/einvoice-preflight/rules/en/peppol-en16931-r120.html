PEPPOL-EN16931-R120: Line net amount does not match quantity, price and line allowances or charges

DeutschFrançaisAll rules

Official message

Invoice line net amount MUST equal (Invoiced quantity * (Item net price/item price base quantity) + Sum of invoice line charge amount - sum of invoice line allowance amount
Severity
Error (the invoice is rejected)
Checked in
UBL, CII
Business terms
BT-131, BT-129, BT-146, BT-149, BT-136, BT-141

What does PEPPOL-EN16931-R120 mean?

For each line, the net amount (BT-131) must equal quantity (BT-129) times net price (BT-146) divided by the price base quantity (BT-149), plus line charges (BT-141) minus line allowances (BT-136). A difference of up to 0.02 is tolerated. The line allowances and charges are summed and rounded to two decimals; a missing quantity or base quantity counts as 1, a missing price as 0.

Usual causes

  • The price is per 100 or per 1,000 units but the base quantity (cbc:BaseQuantity) was left out, so the checker treats it as per 1.
  • The net amount was computed with a more precise unit price than the one written in the file.
  • A line discount was taken off the price and also given as a line allowance, so it counts twice.
  • The quantity is negative on a credit and the price or amount sign does not follow.

How do I fix PEPPOL-EN16931-R120?

  1. Recalculate: quantity x price / base quantity + line charges - line allowances, rounded to two decimals, and write that as cbc:LineExtensionAmount.
  2. If the price is per 100 units, give cbc:BaseQuantity 100 with the same unit code as the quantity.
  3. Write the unit price with enough decimals that the product works out, or apply each discount only once (in the price or as a line allowance).

Corrected XML (minimal example we wrote)

UBL

<cac:InvoiceLine>  <cbc:ID>1</cbc:ID>  <cbc:InvoicedQuantity unitCode="C62">10</cbc:InvoicedQuantity>  <cbc:LineExtensionAmount currencyID="EUR">245.00</cbc:LineExtensionAmount>  <cac:AllowanceCharge>    <cbc:ChargeIndicator>false</cbc:ChargeIndicator>    <cbc:AllowanceChargeReason>Discount</cbc:AllowanceChargeReason>    <cbc:Amount currencyID="EUR">5.00</cbc:Amount>  </cac:AllowanceCharge>  <cac:Item>    <cbc:Name>Widget</cbc:Name>    <cac:ClassifiedTaxCategory>      <cbc:ID>S</cbc:ID>      <cbc:Percent>19</cbc:Percent>      <cac:TaxScheme>        <cbc:ID>VAT</cbc:ID>      </cac:TaxScheme>    </cac:ClassifiedTaxCategory>  </cac:Item>  <cac:Price>    <cbc:PriceAmount currencyID="EUR">25.00</cbc:PriceAmount>    <cbc:BaseQuantity unitCode="C62">1</cbc:BaseQuantity>  </cac:Price></cac:InvoiceLine>

Example values only. Put the elements in place of, or beside, what your file has; element order matters in both syntaxes.
