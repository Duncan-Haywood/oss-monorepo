Kazakhstan: IS ESF

Kazakhstan has issued e-invoices through the State Revenue Committee's IS ESF for years. 2026 changed the rules around it. Below: what advisory firms report and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelElectronic invoices (ESF) issued in the IS ESF portal and registered there. Not EN 16931.
TimelineSources disagree on the start: one says 2017, another says all VAT payers from 2019.
2026 changesTax Code No. 214-VIII applies from 1 January 2026, keeps the IS ESF and the 15-day issuance deadline, and adds automated control of ESF issuance. Order No. 629 (from 1 January 2026) replaced Order No. 370 of 2019. One source says Order No. 646 later restated it; that is unverified.
VAT balance controlUnder Order No. 611, covered taxpayers reportedly get an ESF registration number only if the invoice VAT is within their VAT balance; otherwise the invoice is blocked until funds are added.
CorrectionsCorrected, additional and cancelled invoices reportedly need confirmation by a VAT-paying recipient.

Written from web search summaries and vendor guides (EY Kazakhstan, Moore Kazakhstan, Thomson Reuters, VATupdate, Sovos, e-invoice.app), not from State Revenue Committee or Ministry of Finance texts, none of which were read. Dates and thresholds may have changed.

Common questions

What is IS ESF?

The State Revenue Committee's information system for electronic invoices (ESF), where Kazakh e-invoices are issued and registered.

What changed in 2026?

Guides report Tax Code No. 214-VIII from 1 January 2026, Order No. 629 replacing Order No. 370, and automated control of ESF issuance against the issuer's VAT balance.

Can an invoice be blocked?

Reportedly yes: if the invoice VAT exceeds the VAT balance, it is held in the IS ESF until the balance covers it. Confirm against current orders.

Can this checker validate a Kazakh ESF?

No. It has no Kazakh rules and does not call the IS ESF. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not IS ESF registration.
