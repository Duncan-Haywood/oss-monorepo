Indonesia: faktur pajak through Coretax

In Indonesia, per the sources read, VAT invoices (faktur pajak) are issued electronically, and since 1 January 2025 through the tax authority’s Coretax system under PMK 81/2024. Below: how it works as secondary summaries report it, where the sources are thin, and a local check of an NPWP’s shape.

How it works (as secondary summaries report)

StepWhat happens
IdentifyThe seller is a taxable entrepreneur (PKP) with an NPWP. In Coretax it has 16 digits: an individual citizen’s population ID (NIK), or a company’s old 15-digit number with a 0 in front (the tax authority’s own article).
IssueThe invoice is created and uploaded in Coretax and approved there; approval assigns the serial number, and an unapproved e-Faktur is not a tax invoice (vendor guides). Vendor guides give a deadline of the 20th of the following month for upload and approval.
CodesTen transaction codes, such as 01 for general supplies, 02 for government treasurers and 04 for other tax base values, and a 17-digit serial number (vendor guides; unconfirmed against the tax authority).
CorrectA replacement invoice or a cancelled invoice, depending on the error (vendor guides).
EnforcePMK 81/2024 reportedly allows the tax authority to deactivate a non-compliant entrepreneur’s access to create tax invoices; input VAT on a defective invoice may not be creditable.

Reported by pajak.go.id (search excerpts), DDTC News, fiscal-requirements.com and vendor guides, read through web search only: the hosts were unreachable for a full read, and PMK 81/2024 and the later director general regulations were not read. Where it is unsettled: the serial number length and structure (sources differ), the upload deadline, the transition end date for large taxpayers, and every penalty amount for invoices (none found, so none is stated). Check an NPWP

Tests the shape only: 15 digits (the old form) or 16 digits; dots, dashes and spaces are ignored. No check digit rule was found in a source, so none is applied. It does not look anything up, so it cannot say whether the number is registered, matched to an NIK in Coretax, or belongs to the name on the invoice.

Common questions

What is an Indonesian e-invoice?

In Indonesia the VAT invoice is the faktur pajak. Taxable entrepreneurs (PKP) issue it electronically, and since 1 January 2025 through the tax authority's Coretax system, which replaced the older e-Faktur application (PMK 81/2024; secondary summaries say the move was to be complete by 31 December 2025, with large taxpayers allowed the desktop or host-to-host route). Reported rule: an invoice is uploaded and approved in Coretax, which assigns the serial number, and an unapproved one is not a tax invoice. Confirm with the tax authority (DJP) or your accountant.

What is the NPWP format in Coretax?

Per the tax authority's own article on Coretax, the NPWP has 16 digits. A citizen who is an individual uses the population ID number (NIK) as the NPWP; a company or government body uses its old 15-digit NPWP with a 0 added in front. An NIK cannot be used for tax transactions such as creating an e-Faktur until it is matched and activated in Coretax. The test on this page checks length only: no check digit rule was found in a source.

What are the transaction codes and the number format?

Vendor guides say Coretax has ten transaction codes (for example 01 for general supplies, 02 for supplies to government treasurers and 04 for other tax base values) and that the serial number changed from 16 to 17 digits, structured AA.BB.CC.XXXXXXXXX with the transaction code, a replacement code and the year. No official source for this was read, so treat it as unconfirmed.

What happens if an invoice is wrong or not issued?

Vendor guides describe two ways to correct: a replacement invoice or a cancelled invoice, and say using the wrong one is a common cause of trouble. Reported consequences of not issuing a proper invoice are input VAT that cannot be credited and administrative penalties; PMK 81/2024 reportedly lets the director general deactivate access to create tax invoices for entrepreneurs who break the rules (DDTC News). No penalty amount for invoices was found, so none is stated.

Can this checker validate an Indonesian invoice?

No. It does not read the Coretax XML or upload format, cannot obtain an approval or serial number from the tax authority, and has no Indonesian rules. It tests an NPWP's shape on this page, and a clean result is not an approval by the tax authority.
