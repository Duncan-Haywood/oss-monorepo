What this checks

The same local rules as the CLI: required fields, ISO dates, currency shape, party identifiers, line arithmetic, totals, optional prepaid versus amount due (INV-028 and INV-029), optional VAT, IBAN, and BIC typo catches (INV-022 through INV-027), remittance matching (INV-030 and INV-031), credit-note document type plus original-invoice reference (INV-032 through INV-034), 0% or exempt lines that omit a tax reason (INV-035 and INV-036), mixed tax rates without a per-rate breakdown (INV-037 and INV-038), and optional buyer or seller electronic addresses (INV-039 through INV-041), and an optional invoice period that is incomplete or ends before it starts (INV-042 and INV-043), optional payment means versus IBAN (INV-044 through INV-046), optional buyer or purchase-order references (INV-047 through INV-049), optional header allowances or charges (INV-050 through INV-052), and optional payment terms days versus due date (INV-053 and INV-054). UBL XML, UN/CEFACT CII, and Factur-X / ZUGFeRD PDFs (the embedded XML) are mapped into that shape. A PDF result says Unwrapped factur-x.xml (CII) from the PDF when the standard attachment was opened; custom attachment names are not shown. UBL mapping includes CreditNote, BillingReference, payee account, branch, PaymentID, PaymentMeansCode, TaxExemptionReason, TaxSubtotal, EndpointID, InvoicePeriod, BuyerReference, OrderReference, invoice-level AllowanceCharge, and PaymentTerms SettlementPeriod DurationMeasure. It is not a Peppol, EN 16931, or tax-law validator and it does not submit anything.

Common questions

Does this submit an e-invoice?

No. The page only reads files or pasted JSON in your browser and reports observations. It never contacts a tax authority or submission network.

How do I check an e-invoice before submission?

Drop a JSON, UBL, or CII (XRechnung / Factur-X) XML invoice or credit note, a Factur-X / ZUGFeRD / XRechnung PDF with an embedded invoice, or paste the JSON shape. The checker unwraps the PDF in the browser when needed and reports missing fields, date-shape mistakes, a header allowance or charge whose amount does not match its percentage, a purchase-order reference that repeats the invoice number or is a placeholder, payment terms days that do not match the due date, a SEPA or credit-transfer payment means without an IBAN, an invoice period that is incomplete or ends before it starts, totals that do not match the line items, mixed tax rates without a per-rate breakdown, a buyer electronic address that fails its GLN or EAS scheme, a credit sent as a commercial invoice, a missing original-invoice reference, a 0% or exempt line without a tax reason, prepaid versus amount-due mismatches, a missing remittance on an IBAN invoice, and optional VAT, IBAN, BIC, and RF typo catches, with a rule ID and a suggested fix. It does not decide tax treatment or legal validity.

How do you calculate a mixed-VAT-rate tax breakdown?

Group the lines by rate, sum each rate's net, multiply by that rate, and round to the cent — see the worked example the INV-037/INV-038 checks are built from.

What does this e-invoice error code mean?

Every rule this checker reports has an INV- code naming one specific defect and the field it was found in — the rule reference lists all of them with a fix for each. They are this tool's own identifiers, not EN 16931 BR- rules or a submission network's rejection codes.
