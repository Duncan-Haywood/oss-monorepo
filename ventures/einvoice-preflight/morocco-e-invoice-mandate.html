Morocco's e-invoice rules: a DGI pre-clearance system, with no binding dates found

Morocco's tax authority, the Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), has been preparing mandatory e-invoicing with clearance by a central platform before an invoice is issued. Vendor guides give 2026 start dates; the most recent source read says the decree that sets them has not been published. Below: what guides report, where they conflict, and how to test a file first.

The timeline (as vendor guides report it, and where they conflict)

DateWhat is reported
Oct 2024Public consultations announced by the DGI (one guide).
Oct 2025Pilot phase announced; reported as completed.
1 Jan 2026Wave 1 per some vendor guides: large companies (turnover above MAD 200 million) and public-sector suppliers. Not confirmed by the latest source.
1 Jul 2026Wave 2 per the same guides: medium-sized companies. Not confirmed.
2027–2028Small and very small businesses, per one guide.
TodayNo binding date found. A guide dated about 28 September 2026 says no decree or order is published in the Official Bulletin and the 2026 start has not materialised.

Where sources disagree: whether the mandate is already live (some vendors say so) or waiting for a decree (the most recent source). The legal basis is cited as Article 145-9 or 145-IX of the General Tax Code (CGI), rolled out through the 2026 Finance Bill. These are vendor and advisory pages, not the DGI or the Official Bulletin, which could not be read from this build. Confirm with the DGI before acting on any date. A summary, not tax advice.

Which file counts, and how it travels

Guides describe a pre-clearance model: the invoice is submitted to a DGI platform, reported as Simpl-TVA, and validated before it is issued. The schema and format were not read from a primary source, so this page does not say which syntax is used.

What this checker does and does not cover

It applies EN 16931 arithmetic and tax-breakdown rules to UBL, CII and Factur-X files. It does not apply Moroccan rules and does not contact the DGI, so it cannot clear an invoice. Treat a clean result as “the numbers and structure are sound”, not “cleared in Morocco”.

Common questions

Does Morocco have an e-invoicing mandate?

Not yet in force on the latest source read. Vendor guides describe a DGI pre-clearance system (Simpl-TVA) and give wave dates in 2026, but a guide dated about 28 September 2026 says the decree setting effective dates and categories has not been published and the announced 2026 start has not materialised. These are not the DGI or the Official Bulletin, so confirm there. This is a summary, not advice.

What format does Morocco use?

Guides describe a pre-clearance model: the invoice is sent to a DGI platform for validation before it is issued. The platform is reported as Simpl-TVA. The schema and format were not read from a primary source, and nothing here says it is Peppol or EN 16931.

What does this checker cover for Morocco?

EN 16931 arithmetic and tax-breakdown rules for UBL, CII and Factur-X files. It does not apply Moroccan rules and does not talk to the DGI, so it cannot clear an invoice. A clean result is not DGI acceptance.

Drop the file into the checker. It tests totals, tax breakdown arithmetic, mandatory fields and VAT, IBAN and BIC typos, each with a rule ID and a fix. Nothing is uploaded. It is an offline observation, not the tax authority's acceptance and not legal advice.
