E-invoicing deadlines to the end of 2027

21 dated milestones in 17 countries, from 2 November 2026 on, in date order. Pulled from the same table as the mandates hub, so the two cannot disagree.

Track them: subscribe to the calendar (.ics) or read the JSON. Only full day-month-year dates are listed. Rules that start in a month or a year without a day are in the hub table and on each country page.
