Uruguay: the CFE is mandatory for VAT taxpayers

In Uruguay, per the sources read, tax receipts are electronic (CFE, comprobante fiscal electrónico) in the format the tax authority, DGI, specifies, and recent DGI resolutions reportedly extend the duty to all VAT taxpayers. Below: how it works as vendor guides report it, where the sources are thin, and a local check of a RUT’s check digit.

How it works (as vendor guides and the authority report it)

StepWhat happens
IdentifyThe issuer has a RUT (12 digits: a two-digit office, a six-digit number, a three-digit branch and a check digit, as one validator describes it).
Issue a CFEThe issuer produces the receipt as XML in DGI’s schema, signed and sent to DGI, which accepts or rejects it (vendor guides; the technical documentation is unread).
DeliverThe buyer receives the CFE; a vendor guide says manual documents are allowed only in justified contingencies.
VerifyDGI has a public RUT lookup at dgi.gub.uy; this page does not use it.

Where it is unsettled: the resolutions and the dates for each taxpayer group, which I did not read; the legal basis (sources cite Resolution 798/012 and Decree 36/015); the date of the 2026 format versions (a vendor says 3 March 2026). Sources are a KPMG bulletin and vendor guides read through search excerpts, not DGI’s resolutions. No penalty is stated because none was read. A summary, not tax advice.

Check a RUT

Tests the shape (12 digits; spaces, dots and hyphens are ignored) and the check digit. It does not look anything up, so it cannot say whether the RUT is registered, active, or belongs to the name on the invoice.

The sample is a RUT published in a public forum thread and a vendor validator. The rule (the first eleven digits weighted 4,3,2,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2; 11 minus the remainder mod 11, with 11 read as 0; a result of 10 has no digit) reproduces that one RUT only; it was not read from DGI’s specification.

Common questions

What is a CFE?

The comprobante fiscal electrónico: the electronic tax receipt (e-factura, e-ticket and related documents) issued in the format DGI specifies. A vendor guide says issuers must send the data to DGI and receive an authorisation. This is a summary from vendor guides, not advice; confirm with DGI or your accountant.

Who has to issue one?

A December 2023 KPMG bulletin reports that DGI resolutions made the CFE mandatory for all VAT taxpayers, including those on the minimum VAT regime; a 2026 vendor guide says almost every VAT taxpayer is covered from 2025, with few exceptions. The resolutions were not read, so dates and exceptions are unsettled; check dgi.gub.uy.

What is a valid RUT?

Twelve digits whose last digit follows from the first eleven by a mod 11 rule. A matching digit does not mean the RUT is registered or active; DGI’s public register answers that. The test on this page catches typos only.

Can this checker validate a Uruguayan invoice?

No. It does not read DGI’s XML schema and cannot obtain or verify a CFE authorisation. It does test a RUT’s shape and check digit on this page, and a clean result is not an authorisation by DGI.
