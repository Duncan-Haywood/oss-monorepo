Pakistan: FBR Digital Invoicing

Pakistan's Federal Board of Revenue is moving sellers onto real-time invoice reporting, starting with Tier-1 retailers. Most sources are vendor blogs. Below: what they say, where they differ, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelSales are reported to the FBR in real time through an API integration of the seller's POS or ERP. Registration is through the IRIS portal, followed by test integration in a sandbox.
WhoTier-1 retailers first; sources say it is expanding to large restaurants and certain service providers. Sectors and thresholds differ between sources.
Legal basisOne source cites Section 3(9A) of the Sales Tax Act; another cites Rules 150ZEA and 150ZQZQ of the Sales Tax Rules 2006.
DeadlinesOne source reports an FBR notification dated 9 March 2026 extending integration deadlines. Not confirmed here.
PenaltiesReported as including a 60 percent input tax cut, fines and sealing of premises. Not confirmed here.
UptakeA news-style page reports 13,731 integrated retailers and 37,770 branches up to 31 August 2026, citing the FBR.
EffortOne vendor estimates 4 to 8 weeks to connect an existing custom POS or ERP.

What this checker does and does not cover

The main checker reads UBL, CII and Factur-X and applies EN 16931 and Peppol rules. It does not apply FBR rules or call the FBR API. Common questions

What does the FBR require for invoices?

Real-time reporting of sales through the Digital Invoicing and POS integration, registered through the IRIS portal and tested in a sandbox first, as vendor guides describe. The first group is Tier-1 retailers, with other sectors being added.

Who has to integrate?

Tier-1 retailers are the core group in every source; some add large restaurants and certain service providers. The exact thresholds and sectors differ between sources, so check the FBR notification for your category.

What are the penalties?

Vendor guides report a 60 percent input tax cut, fines and sealing for non-integration. These were not confirmed against FBR or Sales Tax Act text.

Can this checker validate an FBR submission?

No. It has no FBR rules and does not call the FBR API. For a UBL or CII file it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not an FBR acceptance.
