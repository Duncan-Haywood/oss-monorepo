Pakistan: FBR Digital Invoicing

Pakistan's Federal Board of Revenue requires every sales tax registered person to issue electronic sales tax invoices through a system integrated with the FBR. Below: what the FBR's own notifications say (read 14 November 2026), what is only reported, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

What the FBR's texts say

TopicWhat the texts say
RulesChapter XIV of the Sales Tax Rules 2006, substituted by S.R.O. 69(I)/2025 of 29 January 2025 (rules 150Q onward). It applies to the registered persons the Board notifies in the official Gazette (rule 150Q(2)); persons whose point of sale was already integrated count as integrated.
Who and whenS.R.O. 1413(I)/2025 of 1 August 2025, superseding S.R.O. 709(I)/2025 of 22 April 2025, notifies all sales tax registered persons in seven categories, each with a date for registration, testing and issuing electronic invoices: public companies, companies with sales above PKR 1 billion in the last twelve months and all importers by 1 September 2025; other companies above PKR 100 million, and individuals and associations of persons above PKR 100 million, by 1 October 2025; companies up to PKR 100 million by 1 November 2025; everyone else by 1 December 2025. Reported, not read: S.R.O. 1852(I)/2025 of 24 September 2025 moved these dates to between 1 November and 31 December 2025.
What the system must doRule 150R(4): generate and store invoice data; issue the invoice in the prescribed format with a digital signature; send it to the FBR and receive a unique FBR invoice number; print a QR code (7 x 7 mm) built on that number; close the day, week and month; log every adjustment, modification and cancellation. No supply may be made except through an integrated outlet or machine (150R(3)); exempt supplies are invoiced the same way (150R(9)).
Invoice particularsRule 150R(13) lists 26: the FBR invoice number (XXXXXX-DDMMYYHHMMSS-0001), the QR code, the software registration number, the FBR logo, seller and buyer name, address and registration number, date, tax period, description, quantity, value excluding tax, sales tax rate and amount, tax withheld at source, extra tax, further tax, federal excise duty in sales tax mode, total discount, invoice reference number, HS code, unit of measurement and the SRO and serial number. Four of them do not apply to a retailer selling to the public.
Credit and debit notesIssued electronically through the integrated system too, and kept with the invoices for six years (150S).
CorrectionsSales Tax General Order 01 of 2026 (30 March 2026): a valid invoice issued by bona fide mistake may be cancelled, deleted or edited through the FBR system within 72 hours of generation; after that only with the Commissioner Inland Revenue's prior approval. A registered person may engage more than one licensed integrator.
OutagesInvoices issued while the software, internet or power is down are marked as offline and uploaded within 24 hours of restoration (150XC). The Commissioner may extend a deadline by up to 60 days in all, in 15-day steps, with paper invoices meanwhile (150V).
IntegratorsOnly a licensed integrator may integrate a notified person's software; PRAL acts as one free of cost (150XE, 150XF). A licence runs five years.
PenaltiesRule 150X: tampering, selling outside the system or breaking the Chapter draws the penalty under section 33 of the Sales Tax Act. The section 33 amounts were not read here; the 60 percent input tax cut that vendor guides mention is not in the rules read.

Read on 14 November 2026 from download1.fbr.gov.pk: S.R.O. 69(I)/2025, S.R.O. 1413(I)/2025 and Sales Tax General Order 01 of 2026 (sources and quotes: eval/mandate-primary-sources-pk-ng-jo-th-2026-11-14.md in the repository). S.R.O. 1852(I)/2025 and anything later were not found on the FBR's download host; those dates are from news reports, not from FBR notifications. The FBR's Digital Invoicing API documentation was not read.

Common questions

What does the FBR require for invoices?

Every sales tax registered person must issue a real-time, verifiable electronic sales tax invoice for every taxable supply through hardware and software integrated with the FBR's computerised system, which returns a unique FBR invoice number for a QR code on the invoice (Sales Tax Rules 2006, Chapter XIV, as substituted by S.R.O. 69(I)/2025, rules 150R and 150S). Debit and credit notes go through the same system.

Who has to integrate, and by when?

All sales tax registered persons, under S.R.O. 1413(I)/2025 of 1 August 2025, in seven categories with deadlines from 1 September 2025 (public companies, companies above PKR 1 billion, importers) to 1 December 2025 (everyone not listed). News reports say S.R.O. 1852(I)/2025 moved the dates to between 1 November and 31 December 2025; that notification was not read here.

Can an e-invoice be cancelled or corrected?

Sales Tax General Order 01 of 2026 (30 March 2026) lets a valid invoice issued by bona fide mistake be cancelled, deleted or edited through the FBR system within 72 hours of generation; after that the Commissioner Inland Revenue must approve first.

What are the penalties?

Rule 150X sends a person who tampers with the system, sells outside it or breaks the Chapter to the penalty under section 33 of the Sales Tax Act. The section 33 amounts were not read here, and the 60 percent input tax cut that vendor guides mention is not in the rules read.

Can this checker validate an FBR submission?

No. It has no FBR rules and does not call the FBR API. For a UBL or CII file it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not an FBR acceptance.
