Jordan: JoFotara

Jordan clears invoices through JoFotara, a national system run by the Income and Sales Tax Department. Sources agree on the 2025 start and disagree on who is covered next. Below: what they say and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelClearance: the invoice is sent in a structured format, validated by JoFotara, which returns a QR code and signed document. One source says an invoice has no legal standing until it is returned.
TimelineLaunched December 2022 as voluntary. Universal registration was due by 31 May 2024. Mandatory issuance of all invoices through JoFotara or integrated systems from April 2025, with the Amended Billing and Control Regulation No. 2 of 2025 cited as the legal basis.
ScopeSources disagree: some say all taxpayers, others say VAT-registered large and medium taxpayers. One says the Department confirmed no sector or entity type is exempt. Dates for later SME onboarding were not found.
FormatUBL 2.1 XML, as reported. Jordan has its own rules on top; not EN 16931.
Buyer detailsOne source says purchaser details are not required on invoices below JOD 10,000.

What this checker does and does not cover

The main checker reads UBL, CII and Factur-X and applies EN 16931 and Peppol rules. It does not apply Jordanian rules, sign an invoice or call JoFotara. Common questions

What is JoFotara?

Jordan's national e-invoicing system, run by the Income and Sales Tax Department with the Ministry of Digital Economy. Invoices are sent in a structured format, validated, and returned with a QR code and signature.

When did e-invoicing become mandatory in Jordan?

Sources report mandatory issuance through JoFotara or integrated systems from April 2025, under the Amended Billing and Control Regulation No. 2 of 2025, after a voluntary launch in December 2022. Who is covered in 2026 differs between sources.

Does the invoice have to be UBL?

Sources describe UBL 2.1 XML as the standard, with Jordanian rules in addition. Confirm the current specification with the Department or your integration provider.

Can this checker validate a JoFotara invoice?

No. It has no Jordanian rules and does not call JoFotara. For a UBL file it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not a JoFotara clearance.
