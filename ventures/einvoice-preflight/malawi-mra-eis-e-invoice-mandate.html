Malawi: MRA Electronic Invoicing System

Malawi is moving VAT-registered businesses from electronic fiscal devices to the Malawi Revenue Authority's Electronic Invoicing System (EIS). The start date was pushed back more than once. Below: what guides report, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelInvoices are issued through the MRA's Electronic Invoicing System and reported to the authority, replacing electronic fiscal devices (EFDs) over time. A Malawian system, not EN 16931.
LaunchThe EIS is reported live from 2 August 2025 with an initial three-month transition period.
First extensionThe MRA is reported to have moved the mandatory start from 1 November 2025 to 1 February 2026 (notice of 3 or 4 November 2025, sources differ).
PilotA notice of 30 January 2026 is reported to set a pilot from 1 February to 30 April 2026, with the EIS running beside EFDs.
Mandatory dateTwo guides (Edicom, Shared Services Link) say the MRA confirmed 1 May 2026 for all VAT-registered businesses. A compliance tracker (e-invoice.app) lists mandatory use from 1 February 2026 instead. No MRA notice was read, so which date applies is unconfirmed.

What this checker does and does not cover

The main checker reads UBL, CII and Factur-X and applies EN 16931 and Peppol rules. It does not apply local rules, sign an invoice or call the Malawi Revenue Authority system. Common questions

What is the Malawi EIS?

The Malawi Revenue Authority's Electronic Invoicing System, through which VAT-registered businesses issue and report invoices, replacing electronic fiscal devices.

When is it mandatory?

Reports disagree: 1 May 2026 after a pilot from 1 February to 30 April 2026 (Edicom, Shared Services Link), or 1 February 2026 (e-invoice.app). Check the MRA's notices.

Do I still need my fiscal device?

During the pilot the EIS was reported to run beside EFDs, with the devices phased out afterwards. Ask the MRA when yours may be retired.

Can this checker validate a Malawi EIS invoice?

No. It has no Malawian rules and does not connect to the MRA. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not MRA acceptance.
