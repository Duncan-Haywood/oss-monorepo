Brazil's NF-e: the state authorises the invoice, and CNPJs now may contain letters

In Brazil, per the notices read, a goods invoice is an NF-e: XML that the state tax authority (SEFAZ) validates and authorises before it counts. From July 2026 the 14-character CNPJ that identifies a company can contain letters in its first 12 characters for new registrations, and the NF-e schemas were changed to accept that. Below: how it works as these notices report it, where sources differ, and a local check of a CNPJ or CPF check digit.

How it works (as notices and vendor guides report it)

StepWhat happens
IssueThe seller builds the NF-e XML, with a 44-character access key, and signs it with a digital certificate.
AuthoriseSEFAZ validates the XML against the schema and its rules and returns an authorisation protocol. Without it the goods should not move.
DeliverThe seller sends the authorised XML to the buyer; the printed DANFE accompanies the goods.
ChangeMistakes are handled by cancellation within a time limit or a correction letter for limited fields, not by editing the XML.

Where it is unsettled: the start of the test environment for the alphanumeric CNPJ (notices gave 1 June and 15 June 2026; production 1 July 2026), the dates of the separate IBS and CBS tax-reform layout (NT 2025.002-RTC; one source gave testing from 1 July and production from 3 August 2026), and cancellation and correction limits, which were not read. Whether production went live as planned was not confirmed from the sources. These are search excerpts, not the Portal da NF-e. A summary, not tax advice.

Check a CNPJ or CPF

Accepts a 14-character CNPJ (digits or letters, with or without punctuation) or an 11-digit CPF, and tests the shape and both check digits. It does not look anything up, so it cannot say whether the number is registered or matches a name. Measured: on a sample numeric CNPJ, swapping any one of the first 12 characters for another digit is always caught, but allowing letters, 24 of 420 single-character substitutions (5.7%) leave both digits unchanged, so a pass is not a confirmation.

The sample is the alphanumeric example I remember from the announcement. The rule (value = ASCII code minus 48, weights 2 to 9 from the right, mod 11) also reproduces the classic numeric examples 11.222.333/0001-81 and 00.000.000/0001-91; it was not read from the Receita Federal specification.

Check an NF-e or NFC-e XML file

Drop an NF-e (model 55) or NFC-e (model 65) 4.00 file, signed or not, or the authorised nfeProc your system stored. It runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Checks: the NF-e 4.00 schema (PL_010_V1.30, the tax-reform layout, which also reads files without the reform groups), the access key and its check digit against ide and emit, CNPJ and CPF check digits, each item's qCom × vUnCom, every ICMSTot total against the items, vNF against its parts, an NFC-e's payments, the IBS/CBS reform sums (each item's vIBS as vIBSUF + vIBSMun, every IBSCBSTot and ISTot total against the items, vNFTot against the items' vItem), each item's CST and cClassTrib against the published IBS/CBS classification table (the code exists, belongs to the CST, is allowed on this model and on the issue date, the item's groups are ones the CST takes, and pRedAliq and pAliqEfet carry the code's rate reduction), the state codes and the CFOP's first digit. Where a finding matches a SEFAZ rejection it names the cStat (for example 610 for a vNF that does not add up).

Measured on 2026-11-12: against xmllint on the real PL_010_V1.30 schemas, 4,987 of 4,987 single changes to six valid files got the same valid-or-invalid verdict from this page, 2,424 of them inside the IBS/CBS reform groups. Not checked: the digital signature, whether a CNPJ or state registration exists, the IBS/CBS rates themselves and the cCredPres presumed-credit table (the cClassTrib table is read: 164 codes as published on 2026-06-22), NCM/CEST lists or SEFAZ's state-specific rules; no SEFAZ rejection code for the reform sums was read, so those findings name none. The sample is made up.

Check a national NFS-e (services) XML file

Services are invoiced on an NFS-e, not an NF-e. Drop a file in the national layout (the Padrão Nacional of the Sistema Nacional NFS-e, namespace http://www.sped.fazenda.gov.br/nfse): the DPS your system sends, or the NFSe the national system returns with the DPS inside it. It runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Checks: the schema of the 2026-10-20 package (leiaute 1.04.01, with the IBS/CBS groups), and where a file from an earlier layout fails it, what moved (finNFSe now required in infDPS, xDescServ after cNBS, CST and cClassTrib out of gIBSCBS); the DPS Id against cLocEmi, the issuer, serie and nDPS; the NFS-e access key's check digit and parts; CNPJ and CPF check digits; and the sums the schema's own field notes state: the ISSQN base, vISSQN = vBC × pAliqAplic, vTotalRet, vLiq, the IBS/CBS base (net of ISSQN, PIS and COFINS up to 2026), each IBS and CBS amount, each effective rate as the rate less its stated reduction, vIBSTot and vTotNF; and the DPS's CST and cClassTrib against the published IBS/CBS classification table, with the NFS-e's pRedAliqUF, pRedAliqMun and pRedAliqCBS against the code's reduction.

Measured on 2026-11-12: against xmllint on the same schemas, 7,843 of 7,843 single changes to eight valid files (a made-up NFS-e and DPS, two library samples, three real issued NFS-e moved to the current layout) got the same valid-or-invalid verdict from this page, and 1,439 of 1,439 inside the IBS/CBS groups. The three real NFS-e, issued in December 2025 by the national system, pass every sum and their access keys' check digits. No rejection code of the national system was read, so findings name none. Not checked: the signature, municipal rates and benefits, the cClassTrib and NBS tables, municipal (ABRASF) layouts. The sample is made up.

Check a municipal NFS-e (ABRASF layout) XML file

Most city halls that have not moved to the national system take NFS-e through web services built on the ABRASF model (namespace http://www.abrasf.org.br/nfse.xsd). Drop an RPS your system sends (GerarNfseEnvio, EnviarLoteRpsEnvio), an issued CompNfse, or a city's reply that carries them, even escaped inside a SOAP envelope. It runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Checks: the ABRASF 2.00, 2.01, 2.02, 2.03 and 2.04 schemas (element order, required fields, the LC 116/2003 item codes, lengths and formats), a dropped or mistyped namespace, CNPJ and CPF check digits, every IBGE city code against IBGE's list of 5,571 codes and the state beside it, and the values: BaseCalculo = ValorServicos − ValorDeducoes − DescontoIncondicionado, ValorIss = base × Aliquota, and ValorLiquidoNfse net of the federal retentions, the retained ISS and both discounts. Where an element is a known provider's extension (AVMB's Token, Fintel's DataPagamento, the tax-reform IBSCBS and CodigoNBS some providers added), the finding says whose it is, so you don't delete a field your city's system wants. Files in layout 1.00 get the identifier and value checks without the schema.

Measured on 2026-11-13: against xmllint on the same schemas, 3,447 of 3,447 single changes to 34 valid ABRASF files got the same valid-or-invalid verdict from this page, and 132 of 134 files as published did too (the two others differ only inside the digital signature, which is not read). For layouts 2.00 to 2.02 (added the same day): 48 of 48 published files and 3,059 of 3,059 single changes to the 21 valid ones. On 15 real files returned by six city halls (95 issued NFS-e) the value checks raised nothing. Not checked: the signature, city-specific variants and rates, and a clean result is not an acceptance by the city.

Common questions

What is an NF-e?

An NF-e (Nota Fiscal Eletrônica) is Brazil's electronic invoice for goods: an XML file the seller sends to the state tax authority (SEFAZ), which validates it and returns an authorisation protocol before the goods move. The printed DANFE is only a representation of the XML. This is a summary from vendor and professional-body notices, not advice; confirm with your accountant and the Portal da NF-e.

What is the alphanumeric CNPJ?

Per the notices read, the CNPJ stays 14 characters, but for new registrations the first 12 may be digits or capital letters; the last two remain numeric check digits. Existing CNPJs do not change. Technical note NT 2025.001 set the general rules and NT 2026.004 changed the NF-e and NFC-e schemas, including the access key, to accept letters, with production reported for 1 July 2026. Sources differ on the start of the test environment.

How is the CNPJ check digit calculated?

Each character is worth its ASCII code minus 48, so digits are 0 to 9 and A to Z are 17 to 42. Weights 2 to 9 are applied from the right and repeat; the digit is 0 if the sum modulo 11 is below 2, otherwise 11 minus the remainder. The second digit repeats the sum over the first 13 characters. This page implements that rule and was pinned on examples I know, not read from the Receita Federal specification.

Can this checker validate a Brazilian NF-e?

Partly, since 2026-11-12. Drop an NF-e or NFC-e 4.00 XML file on this page or on the main checker and it is read against the NF-e 4.00 schema (package PL_010_V1.30, which includes the IBS, CBS and IS tax-reform groups: element order, required fields, codes, patterns and lengths), the access key and its check digit, the CNPJ and CPF check digits, each item's quantity times unit price, the totals against the items and vNF against its parts, the IBS and CBS totals against the items (since 2026-11-12) and vNFTot against the items' vItem, each item's CST and cClassTrib against the published classification table with its rate reduction (since 2026-11-13), and CFOP against the direction of the operation. Where a finding matches a SEFAZ rejection, it names the cStat. It does not check the signature, the state's registration records, tax rates (including the IBS and CBS rates themselves), NCM or CEST lists, and a clean result is not an authorisation by SEFAZ.

Can this checker validate a Brazilian NFS-e?

Yes for the national layout, since 2026-11-12. Drop a DPS or an issued NFS-e in the national standard (Padrão Nacional) and it is read against the schema of the 2026-10-20 package, with the DPS Id, the access key and its check digit, CNPJ and CPF check digits, and the ISSQN and IBS/CBS sums as the schema's own field notes state them, and the CST and cClassTrib against the published classification table (since 2026-11-13). It names what moved when a file from an earlier layout fails. Since 2026-11-13 it also reads municipal NFS-e in the ABRASF layout (2.00 to 2.04 schemas; identifier and value checks for 1.00). It does not check the signature or municipal rates, and a clean result is not an acceptance by the national system or the city.
