Namibia: NamRA e-invoicing (planned)

Namibia has planned mandatory e-invoicing for VAT-registered businesses, run by the Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA). The 2026/27 budget removed the earlier April 2026 date. Below: what guides report, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

StatusNot in force as far as guides report: no binding date and no confirmed phasing by business size as of the latest summary seen (September 2026).

Revised planKPMG (June 2026) and others report a phased rollout over the next three years (the 2026/27 to 2028/29 medium-term framework). One publisher reads the budget as pointing to 2028; others call the window a planning period, not a schedule.
ScopeVAT-registered businesses are expected to come first, with B2B transactions the main focus. Thresholds and phasing are unconfirmed.

Common questions

Is e-invoicing mandatory in Namibia?

Not yet, as far as guides report. The 2026/27 budget removed the April 2026 start and places e-invoicing in a phased, multi-year plan with no binding date.

When will it start?

Unknown. KPMG reports a phased rollout over three years; one publisher reads that as 2028 at the earliest for most businesses. Check NamRA and the Ministry of Finance.

Who will it apply to first?

Guides expect VAT-registered businesses, mainly B2B, with phasing by size possible. None of this is confirmed.

Can this checker validate a Namibian invoice?

No. It has no Namibian rules and does not connect to NamRA. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not NamRA acceptance.
