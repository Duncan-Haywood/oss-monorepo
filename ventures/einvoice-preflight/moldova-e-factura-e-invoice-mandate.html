Moldova: e-Factura

Moldova's Serviciul Fiscal de Stat (SFS) runs the e-Factura system. Below: what guides report about the 2026 move from voluntary to mandatory B2B use, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

Common questions

What is e-Factura in Moldova?

The electronic invoicing system run by the Serviciul Fiscal de Stat (SFS), the State Tax Service. Suppliers to public entities have used it for years; secondary guides report B2B use becoming mandatory in 2026.

When does B2B e-invoicing become mandatory?

Vendor and advisory guides report 1 October 2026, after a pilot in January 2026, tied to amendments under Law 214/2024. Older guides still describe B2B use as voluntary, and no final SFS announcement was read. Check the SFS and the Official Gazette.

What happened to the buyer's VAT deduction rule?

One source reports that Tax Code Article 102(18), which tied a buyer's VAT deduction to holding an e-invoice from listed suppliers, was repealed from 1 January 2026. Unconfirmed against the law itself.

Can this checker validate a Moldovan invoice?

No. It has no Moldovan rules, does not know the e-Factura format and does not connect to the SFS. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not SFS acceptance.
