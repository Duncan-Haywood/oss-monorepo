El Salvador: DTE electronic tax documents

El Salvador's Ministerio de Hacienda requires electronic tax documents (Documentos Tributarios Electrónicos, DTE) transmitted through its own system. Below: what guides report about the version 2.0 rules due on 1 December 2026, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelThe DTE system run by the Ministerio de Hacienda: invoices and other tax documents are issued as structured files, transmitted to Hacienda's system and returned with a reception stamp. It is a national format, not EN 16931 and not UBL or CII.
Version 2.0Guides report that the Normativa de Cumplimiento de los DTE version 2.0 and its technical manual (MTIST 2.0) replace version 1.2, adding new fiscal events, revised validations and longer invalidation periods, with the deadline to adapt billing software set at 1 December 2026. One tracker dates the publication to May 2026.
Other dates in guidesOne 2026 guide also mentions 1 October 2026 for newly obliged taxpayers and 9 October 2026 for a control-number and catalogue change; other sources do not repeat either date, so treat both as unconfirmed.
InvalidationA news report says consumers can ask for an electronic invoice to be invalidated within 90 days from February 2026.
Who is obligedHacienda offers a portal where a taxpayer can look up whether and from when they must issue DTE; guides point to it rather than to a single published date.

Written from web search summaries (Edicom, VAT Update, LookupTax, e-invoice.app, news sites and billing vendors), not from Hacienda's normative texts or portal, none of which were read. Dates and the contents of version 2.0 may be wrong or superseded.

Common questions

What is the DTE in El Salvador?

The Documento Tributario Electrónico: the structured electronic invoice and tax-document system of the Ministerio de Hacienda, transmitted to Hacienda's system.

What changes on 1 December 2026?

Guides report that version 2.0 of the DTE compliance rules replaces version 1.2, with new fiscal events, revised validations and longer invalidation periods. Hacienda's own notice was not read, so confirm the date and details on its portal.

How do I know if I must issue DTE?

Reports say Hacienda has a portal where each taxpayer can check whether, and from when, they are obliged. Use it rather than a guide's date.

Can this checker validate a Salvadoran DTE?

No. It has no El Salvador rules and does not connect to Hacienda. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not Hacienda acceptance.
