Georgia: rs.ge e-invoices

Georgia's Revenue Service runs electronic VAT invoicing on its rs.ge portal. Below: what guides report about when it became mandatory, where they disagree, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelTax invoices are issued and received on the Revenue Service portal (rs.ge). This is a state platform, not a Peppol or EN 16931 exchange; no source read confirms UBL or CII.
Start of mandatory usee-invoice.app: B2B and B2G mandatory since 1 January 2012. A different guide describes a phase-in: 2022 for large taxpayers, 2023 for medium, 2024 for all enrolled taxpayers. The two may describe different things; the sources do not reconcile them.
B2CSales to consumers are reported through fiscalised cash registers connected to the Revenue Service, per e-invoice.app, not through rs.ge invoices.
2026 changeA lookuptax item reports that from October 2026 VAT taxpayers must confirm or reject received tax invoices on the portal by the 5th of the following month, or lose access to other e-services. Unconfirmed against Revenue Service texts.

Common questions

How does e-invoicing work in Georgia?

VAT tax invoices are issued and received on the Revenue Service portal at rs.ge, according to secondary guides.

When did electronic invoicing become mandatory?

Guides disagree: e-invoice.app gives 1 January 2012 for B2B and B2G, while another guide describes a phase-in from 2022 (large taxpayers) to 2024 (all enrolled taxpayers). Check the Revenue Service and the Tax Code.

Must received invoices be confirmed?

One item reports that from October 2026 VAT taxpayers must confirm or reject received tax invoices by the 5th day of the next month. Unconfirmed against the Tax Code.

Can this checker validate a Georgian invoice?

No. It has no Georgian rules, does not know the rs.ge format and does not connect to the Revenue Service. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not Revenue Service acceptance.
