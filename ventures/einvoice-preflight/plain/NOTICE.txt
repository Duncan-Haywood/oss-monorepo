# Rules in this folder

The `*.json` files are the official e-invoicing Schematron rules, compiled to JSON
by `../build-plain-rules.mjs`. They are derived works of:

- `en16931-*.json`: ConnectingEurope/eInvoicing-EN16931, EUPL-1.2.
- `xrechnung-*.json`: itplr-kosit/xrechnung-schematron, Apache-2.0.
- `peppol-*.json`: OpenPEPPOL/peppol-bis-invoice-3, MPL-2.0.

`manifest.json` has the upstream commit each file was built from.
`fontoxpath.esm.js` is fontoxpath (MIT, `fontoxpath.LICENSE.txt`) bundled with its
dependencies xspattern and prsc (both MIT).

The pint-*.json sets are OpenPeppol PINT Schematron (Singapore, Australia/New Zealand; MPL-2.0), read from the compiled XSLT in com.helger.phive.rules:phive-rules-peppol (Apache-2.0) on Maven Central by build-pint-rules.mjs.

The zatca-*.json sets are ZATCA's e-invoice validation rules (rules version 3.4.6; the ZATCA e-invoicing toolkit SDK, LGPL-3.0), read from the compiled XSLT in com.helger.phive.rules:phive-rules-zatca (Apache-2.0) on Maven Central by build-zatca-rules.mjs. They are converted data files; the LGPL text is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt.
