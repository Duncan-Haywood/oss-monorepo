Slovenia's e-invoice mandate: domestic B2B from 2028

Slovenia has set 1 January 2028 for mandatory domestic B2B e-invoicing, after earlier target dates in 2026 and 2027 slipped. PDFs will not count. Below: the dates as sources give them, where they disagree, and how to test a file before it goes to a customer.

Which file counts, and how it travels

Guides say accepted formats are the national e-SLOG standard and EN 16931, and that a PDF does not qualify as an e-invoice. An EN 16931 UBL or CII file is therefore the safe common ground, and emailing a PDF would not satisfy the mandate for domestic B2B sales.

Common questions

When does Slovenia's B2B e-invoicing mandate start?

Per vendor and advisory sources, 1 January 2028 for domestic business-to-business invoices between entities in the Slovenian Business Register, after the National Assembly adopted the law on 23 October 2025. Earlier target dates in 2026 and 2027 were dropped. The final law reportedly has no e-reporting duty. The sources are not the statute, so confirm against the law and the Financial Administration. This is a summary, not advice.

Which invoice format does Slovenia use?

Sources name the national e-SLOG standard and EN 16931 as accepted, and say a PDF is not a valid e-invoice. This checker reads JSON, UBL, CII and Factur-X PDFs against EN 16931 and has a Peppol BIS 3.0 option. It does not read e-SLOG.

What does this checker not cover for Slovenia?

The e-SLOG format, any Slovenian national rules beyond EN 16931, and delivery through a provider. A clean result means the invoice passes EN 16931 and the Peppol rules it knows, not that it will be accepted in Slovenia.

How do I check a Slovenian invoice before sending it?

Drop the file into the checker and tick the Peppol option. It tests the Peppol identifiers, electronic addresses, totals and tax breakdown arithmetic, and VAT, IBAN and BIC typos, each with a rule ID and a fix. Nothing is uploaded when you use the page. It is an offline observation, not an access point's acceptance and not legal advice.
