Oman: Fawtara

Oman's Tax Authority is building a national e-invoicing system called Fawtara on the Peppol network. Guides disagree on the dates because a decision reportedly replaced the first timetable. Below: what the sources say, where they differ, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelFive corners: seller, the seller's accredited service provider, the buyer's accredited service provider, the buyer, and the Tax Authority, which receives the data in near real time. The Tax Authority became a Peppol Authority in January 2026, as reported.
FormatPINT-OM, an Omani adaptation of Peppol PINT, as reported. Since PINT builds on UBL, a UBL file is the likely shape; confirm with your provider.
Current dates (newer sources)Decision 189/2026, issued 3 August 2026 and published in the Official Gazette on 9 August 2026 as reported: electronic tax invoices compulsory from 1 April 2027 for taxable persons with annual supplies above OMR 5 million, and from 1 October 2027 for those at or below.
Older scheduleEarlier guides, some still online, give phases from August 2026, February 2027 and August 2027. One source says it is superseded, while the Tax Authority portal was still showing it in mid-August 2026.
PilotReported: a mandatory pilot for about 100 selected large VAT-registered taxpayers from 1 August 2026.
Government salesA B2G phase is reported as planned with no confirmed date.

Written from web search summaries and adviser guides, not from the Official Gazette text of Decision 189/2026 or the Tax Authority portal, none of which were read for this page. The sources disagree on dates, so check the Gazette before acting.

Common questions

When does Oman e-invoicing become mandatory?

Newer sources say Decision 189/2026 sets 1 April 2027 for taxable persons with annual supplies above OMR 5 million and 1 October 2027 for the rest. Older guides give August 2026, February 2027 and August 2027. The Official Gazette text was not read for this page, so confirm which applies to you.

What is Fawtara?

Oman's national e-invoicing system, run by the Oman Tax Authority on the Peppol network in a five-corner model: your accredited service provider exchanges the invoice with the buyer's provider, and the Tax Authority receives the data in near real time.

What format does Oman use?

Reported as PINT-OM, an Omani adaptation of the international Peppol PINT standard. Not confirmed from a primary source here.

Can this checker validate an Omani invoice?

No. It has no PINT-OM rules and does not connect to Peppol. For a UBL or CII file it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not an acceptance by your provider or the Tax Authority.
