Panama: SFEP

Panama's tax authority, the DGI, runs the Sistema de Facturación Electrónica de Panamá (SFEP). Larger issuers must use an authorised provider (PAC). Below: what advisers report and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works (as secondary guides report)

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelSFEP: electronic invoices authorised by the DGI, through its free tool for small issuers or through a Proveedor de Autorización Calificado (PAC). Not EN 16931.
DutiesResolution 201-6299 of 29 July 2025 updated the duties of SFEP users and is reported in force from 1 January 2026.
PAC thresholdSources differ slightly: legal persons with gross income above B/. 36,000 a year, or more than 100 documents a month, must use a PAC (one source says from B/. 36,001). Confirm the wording.
Who is inNew taxpayer numbers (RUC) from 1 January 2022 are listed as obliged; one source says most businesses from 2023.
PhasesThe phase calendar found concerned banks and financial institutions (Resolution 201-0717 of 26 January 2023, per EY). Dates not found.

Common questions

What is the SFEP?

The Sistema de Facturación Electrónica de Panamá, the DGI's e-invoicing system.

What is a PAC?

A Proveedor de Autorización Calificado, a qualified provider through which larger issuers authorise their invoices.

What changed in 2026?

Advisers report DGI Resolution 201-6299 of 29 July 2025, updating SFEP user duties and the PAC requirement from 1 January 2026.

Can this checker validate a Panamanian invoice?

No. It has no Panamanian rules and does not call the DGI or a PAC. For UBL or CII it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not DGI authorisation.
