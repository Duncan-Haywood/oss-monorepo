Denmark's e-invoicing rules: the Bookkeeping Act, NemHandel and 2027

Denmark has no single "everyone must e-invoice from date X" rule. The Bookkeeping Act pushes businesses onto registered digital systems that can handle structured invoices, and NemHandel, the Danish Peppol network, is how they arrive. Below: the dates as sources give them, where they disagree, and how to test a file before it goes to a customer.

The timeline (dates from the orders themselves where read)

DateWhat changes
Jul 2024 to Jan 2026The Bookkeeping Act's digital-system requirement is phased in over four stages. Covered businesses must keep books in a registered digital bookkeeping system that can send and receive structured e-invoices.
29 Sep 2026BEK nr 811 (22 September 2026) takes effect. Its new annex 2 requires registered systems to send and receive e-invoices and credit notes over the common digital infrastructure (Act No. 1764 of 28 December 2023), send and show response documents (receipt, delivery, acceptance, rejection), look the recipient up in the NemHandel register and tell the user when a manually issued invoice could go as an e-invoice. The consultation draft's 30 November 2026 date is gone.
28 Oct 2026BEK nr 915 (13 October 2026) rewrites only the restore-the-records duty (§ 10).
1 Jan 2027 (reported)Registered systems must be able to produce SAF-T 2.0 files, per Erhvervsstyrelsen's guidance as excerpted. Not in BEK nr 811 or 915: the orders only require a CSV or SAF-T file for the Regnskab Basis annual report.
1 Mar 2027Systems must confirm new and existing customers registered in CVR with MitID at first login (§ 5 a, BEK nr 811 § 2(2)). Annex 2 then has the system check whether the confirmed customer is enrolled for e-invoicing and enrol them by default, unless they opt out within four weeks.
1 Jan 2028MitID for users who change bank or other supplementary details (§ 5 b, § 2(3)); documents a system sends must carry the sender's locked CVR master data (annex 3, points 1 and 2).
About 2028 to 2029Move from OIOUBL to a Peppol-based NemHandel BIS 4 profile, with OIOUBL retired.

Read on retsinformation.dk on 2026-11-14 (rendered in a browser; the site is a JavaScript app): BEK nr 97 of 26 January 2023 on requirements for digital standard bookkeeping systems, as amended by BEK nr 811 of 22 September 2026 and BEK nr 915 of 13 October 2026. The dates above marked without "reported" come from those texts. The orders bind providers of standard systems; a business's own duty comes from the Bookkeeping Act. Still from secondary sources: the 2024 to 2026 phase-in, SAF-T 2.0 from 1 January 2027, and the BIS 4 move. Where sources disagree: one says default registration began on 1 July 2026 (the order ties it to the MitID check from 1 March 2027); one says every business over DKK 300,000 turnover must e-invoice from 1 January 2026, which the orders do not say; OIOUBL 3.0 is said to have been cancelled in favour of going straight to BIS 4. No penalty is set in the orders. A summary, not tax advice.

Which file counts, and how it travels

Danish public buyers have long required structured invoices through NemHandel, in OIOUBL or Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (UBL 2.1, on EN 16931). For private buyers the practical effect of the Bookkeeping Act is that your customer's system can receive a structured invoice and reads it from NemHandel, so a PDF by email is increasingly the exception.

Common questions

When does Denmark's e-invoicing requirement start?

Denmark has no general date on which every B2B invoice must be a structured e-invoice. The Bookkeeping Act instead requires covered businesses to keep books in a registered digital system, phased in between July 2024 and January 2026. The order on those systems, as amended by BEK nr 811 of 22 September 2026 (read on retsinformation.dk on 2026-11-14), makes registered systems send and receive e-invoices and credit notes over the common infrastructure from 29 September 2026, confirm customers with MitID from 1 March 2027, and enrol each confirmed customer in e-invoicing unless they opt out within four weeks. From 1 January 2028 documents a system sends must carry the sender's master data from the CVR register. The duties fall on system providers, not directly on the businesses. This is a summary, not advice.

Which invoice format does Denmark use?

Public buyers receive OIOUBL or Peppol BIS 3.0 through NemHandel, Denmark's Peppol-based network. Guides describe a move to a NemHandel BIS 4 profile on Peppol, with OIOUBL retired around 2029, though sources disagree on the dates. This checker reads JSON, UBL, CII and Factur-X PDFs and has a Peppol BIS 3.0 option. It does not check OIOUBL or the NemHandel profile.

What does this checker not cover for Denmark?

OIOUBL schema rules, the NemHandel BIS 4 profile, SAF-T 2.0 files and your bookkeeping system's registration. A clean result means the invoice passes EN 16931 and the Peppol rules it knows, not that Denmark will accept it.
