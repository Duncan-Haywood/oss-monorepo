The check result, as a document you can file

The preflight finds what an access point or tax portal would reject: arithmetic that does not add up, missing mandatory fields, wrong country rules. The audit pack turns a batch run into one evidence document on your letterhead: a verdict, a row per file, the findings grouped by rule with the recommended fix, your notes, and a written statement of what a preflight does and does not show. Print it or save it as a PDF for a client file or an audit trail.

What costs money and what does not

Every check, the repair tools, the CSV and JSON downloads and the command line are free and stay free. Only the branded audit pack is a planned paid extra, a one-off price rather than a monthly plan. It is not on sale yet: there is no checkout, so nothing here can charge you. The sample uses bundled made-up example invoices and an invented firm.

Who it is for

Bookkeepers, accountants and finance teams who send e-invoices for clients under the EU, Peppol, XRechnung, Factur-X, FatturaPA, ZATCA and other mandates and want a dated record of what was checked before sending. Nothing you drop is uploaded; the pack is built in your browser. The code is MIT licensed.

Does a PASS mean the invoices are legally valid

No. A PASS means none of the published checks found a defect. Acceptance by a tax authority or access point rests on their own validation at submission, and the pack says so in a fixed paragraph.

Is my invoice data uploaded

No. The checks and the pack are built in your browser. Only a licence key, once one exists, would be sent to the licence server.
