# Greenhouse Bench Planner

Free, private browser tool for greenhouse growers, plant nurseries and market gardeners who sell transplants on fixed dates: list each crop with its sale date, quantity, germination and growing weeks, and bench density. It counts back the sow dates, checks germination and bench space week by week, holds or slips orders within limits you set, shows what does not fit, ranks crops by margin per square foot-week and prices extra bench space. Exports a plan CSV and a sowing list.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/greenhouse-bench-planner/

After using this, you know when to sow each crop and which spring weeks the bench overflows, which used to take a wall calendar and a lot of guessing.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/greenhouse-bench-planner/**/*.test.mjs'`.
