SELLER NET SHEET

Home seller transfer tax, state by state.

Transfer tax moves a seller's net more from one state to the next than most closing lines: nothing in Texas, $2,975 on a $425,000 sale in Florida. The table shows the statewide amount a seller customarily pays, as a percent of price, and what that is on $425,000. These are the starting points behind the state menu in the seller net sheet calculator.

Common questions

Who pays the transfer tax when selling a house?

It depends on the state and the contract. In most of the states listed here the seller customarily pays the state tax, in Pennsylvania it is customarily split, and the buyer and seller can agree to move it. The purchase contract and the title company decide, not a general rule.

Why does the calculator have a state menu for transfer tax?

A single default rate is wrong for most states. Picking a state fills the transfer tax percentage with the statewide rate the seller customarily pays, so the net-to-seller figure starts closer. City and county taxes are not included and you can edit the rate.
