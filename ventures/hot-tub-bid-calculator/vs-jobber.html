This calculator vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber is account-based field-service software for running a home-service business across clients, scheduling, and crews. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this hot-tub install cost, given its size, access path, and circuit and crane needs, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Jobber is field-service management software founded in 2011 and headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, used by home-service businesses across HVAC, plumbing, electrical, lawn care, and cleaning trades. It combines client management, quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payment collection into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every crew, and it says it serves over 200,000 users. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Jobber's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — home-service field-service management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Jobber appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage clients, schedule crews, send invoices, or collect payments, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes the tub size, access path (clear driveway drop versus a backyard carry versus a crane set), circuit footage, pad condition, haul-away of an old tub, and a cover lift, plus loaded wage, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the walk-away price below which the job loses money and where the bid's hours are actually going — plus a printable customer proposal with scope lines and a signature block. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
