# Section 1256 Futures Tax Calculator

Free, private browser tool for futures and index-option traders: enter Form 1099-B boxes 8, 9 and 10, and see the aggregate (box 11), the 60% long-term / 40% short-term split, the blended tax and what 60/40 saves against short-term treatment.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/section-1256-futures-tax-calculator/

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/section-1256-futures-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

Not tax advice. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
