Common questions

    
What is the Annual Investment Allowance?

    
A 100% deduction from profit for equipment you buy, up to £1,000,000 a year (scaled for periods shorter than 12 months). It covers most plant and machinery including vans, but not cars.

    
What is full expensing?

    
For companies only: a 100% first-year allowance on new, unused main-rate equipment with no cap, and a 50% allowance on new special-rate items. It does not cover second-hand or leased assets, or cars.

    
Where should the AIA go first?

    
On the items that would otherwise get the least relief: second-hand equipment and special-rate items such as integral features. A company's new main-rate kit gets 100% from full expensing anyway, so spending the AIA there wastes it.

    
Why do cars get less?

    
Cars are excluded from the AIA and go into a pool that is written down by 18% a year, or 6% for higher-emission cars. Some zero-emission cars qualify for a first-year allowance, so check GOV.UK for your accounting period.

    
Do I have to claim the maximum?

    
No. You can claim less, for example to avoid creating a loss or to keep more written-down value for a later year when your tax rate is higher.
