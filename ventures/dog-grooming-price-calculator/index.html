Common questions

Is this a market rate or professional advice?

No. Every price, minute and percentage is a number you typed. It does not look up what other groomers charge. It does the arithmetic on your own time so you can see what each breed class earns you per hour.

Why does a big dog earn less per hour than a small one?

Often the price rises more slowly than the minutes. A giant breed can take over three times the table time of a small smooth coat for under three times the price, and matting or de-shedding minutes are frequently covered by a surcharge that is too small. The calculator shows each class plain and with each extra.

Does it upload my appointments?

No. The browser page works on the text you enter, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

How is the effective hourly calculated?

(Price - supplies) x (1 - no-show rate) / ((minutes + drive minutes) / 60). The price for target solves that for the price. The walk-away price does the same at a floor rate, a percent of your target.
