Booking assumptions

How to use the result

The minimum booking price recovers the asset's allocated ownership cost plus booking-specific costs. Break-even utilization is the share of available rental days needed to cover monthly ownership cost at this booking's contribution. These are arithmetic scenarios, not a guarantee of profit.
