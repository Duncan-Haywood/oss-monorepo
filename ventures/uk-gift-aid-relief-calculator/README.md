# UK Gift Aid Relief Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your taxable income and a charity donation to see what the charity receives with Gift Aid, the higher-rate or additional-rate relief you can claim back, the effect on the £100,000 personal allowance taper, and the net cost to you.

After using this, you know what a donation really costs you and how much to claim back on Self Assessment.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-gift-aid-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rates are 2026/27 and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
