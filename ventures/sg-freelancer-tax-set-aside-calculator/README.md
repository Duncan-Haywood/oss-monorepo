# Singapore Freelancer Tax Set-Aside Calculator (YA 2027)

Free, private browser tool: enter business income, expenses, age and other reliefs and get chargeable income, resident income tax and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags MediSave, Form B filing and GST registration.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/sg-freelancer-tax-set-aside-calculator/

After using this, a Singapore freelancer knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill after 18 April.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/sg-freelancer-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
