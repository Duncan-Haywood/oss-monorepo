This calculator vs. QuoteIQ

Two different shapes: QuoteIQ is a paid, ongoing field-service app for running a concrete-leveling business's whole quoting, scheduling, and job-costing pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one mudjacking or polyjacking job cost, given its void volume, method, and access, before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

QuoteIQ is a field-service business app marketed at concrete-leveling and general contracting crews, covering on-site quote building (with a built-in measurement tool), scheduling, job costing against the quote once work starts, invoicing, and customer follow-up across a whole business. It is a paid, account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across every job you run, not a one-off calculator. QuoteIQ's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a subscription field-service app aimed in part at concrete-leveling contractors — is stated, and no dollar amount for QuoteIQ appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what QuoteIQ does. It does not build a quote from a phone-camera measurement, schedule a crew, or track job costs after the fact, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one lift's slab area and void depth, method — mudjacking or polyurethane — access path — street-side, backyard, or interior — extra holes, joint seal, and occupied- building containment, plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per square foot, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a copyable customer-facing bid summary. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Should I use this calculator or QuoteIQ?

They fit different points in the job.
