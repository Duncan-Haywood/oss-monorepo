Free cold email sequence checker

Paste the whole cadence, not just the first email. Start each step with a line like --- day 4 (days after the first touch), then an optional Subject: line, then the body. You get what to fix on each step. Nothing is uploaded or sent.

What it checks

  • Every step on its own: opt-out line and postal address on each follow-up, spam phrases, placeholders.
  • Spacing: days must increase; under 2 days apart or over 21 warns.
  • Bare bumps ("just following up") and follow-ups that mostly repeat the last email.
  • The same subject twice, and more than four touches.

Questions

How many follow-ups should a cold email sequence have?

A common rule of thumb is four touches in total, spaced several days apart, each adding something new. More than that tends to raise complaints. This is a heuristic, not a measured threshold.

Does every follow-up need an unsubscribe line?

Under CAN-SPAM each commercial message needs a way to opt out and a postal address, so this checker requires them on every step, not only the first.

Is my sequence uploaded?

No. The check runs in your browser and nothing is sent.
