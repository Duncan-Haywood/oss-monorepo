Questions

How many cold emails can one mailbox send per day?

There is no single number. A common conservative habit is to start near 10 a day on a fresh mailbox and stay under about 40 to 50 a day once warm, adding mailboxes instead of volume. Your provider's published limits are higher than what filters tolerate; check them.

Does this guarantee inbox placement?

No. Placement depends on your list quality, content, domain reputation and authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). This only plans the volume.
