Free cold email checker

Paste your first-touch email before it goes out. You get a list of what to fix: the opt-out line, the postal address, a deceptive subject, spam phrases and, for the recipient's country, the consent rule to look up. Nothing is uploaded or sent.

What it checks

  • A working opt-out line, and a postal address (required in the US).
  • First-touch subjects that start with Re: or Fwd:, or pose as an invoice or alert.
  • Spam phrases, fake urgency, absolute claims, all-caps subjects, link shorteners, too many links.
  • Unfilled placeholders such as [Company], which are the most common embarrassing mistake.
  • No ask, too many asks, a generic greeting, no mention of the reader.
  • The broad consent rule for the recipient's country: US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Switzerland, France, Germany, Austria and the other EU states.

After you send, the inbox agent handles the replies: unsubscribes become a suppression record, out-of-office replies pause the prospect.

Questions

What does a cold email need under CAN-SPAM?

An honest from line and subject, a clear way to opt out that you honour within 10 business days, and a valid physical postal address. B2B cold email is allowed without prior consent in the US, but the message must still say who sent it.

Is cold email legal in Germany or Canada?

Germany generally requires prior consent even for business recipients, and Canada's CASL requires consent with narrow implied-consent exceptions. This checker flags both and does not decide your case; ask a qualified professional.

Does this tool send or store my email?

No. The check runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
