SPF, DKIM and DMARC record checker for cold email

Bad email authentication sends cold email to spam before anyone reads it. Paste your records, one per line, and see what is wrong in plain English. It only reads the text you paste: no DNS lookups, nothing uploaded.

Questions

Where do I find these records?

In your DNS host, or look them up with dig TXT yourdomain, dig TXT _dmarc.yourdomain and dig TXT selector._domainkey.yourdomain. Your mail provider tells you the DKIM selector.

Does a clean result mean my mail reaches the inbox?

No. It means the text parses and avoids common mistakes. Placement also depends on list quality, content and sending volume; see the warm-up planner and the cold email checker.

Why does SPF stop at 10 lookups?

RFC 7208 caps DNS-lookup terms (include, a, mx, ptr, exists, redirect) at 10 per check. Included records count too, and this tool cannot see inside them, so treat 8 or more as close to the limit.
