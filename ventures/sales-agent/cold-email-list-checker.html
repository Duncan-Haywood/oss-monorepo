Cold email list cleaner

Bounces hurt your sender reputation. Paste one address per line (or a CSV with an email column) and see which to send, which to look at and which to drop. Nothing is uploaded and no mail server is contacted.

What it cannot do

It cannot tell whether a mailbox exists. Only a mail server can, and verifying without sending is something paid verifiers do with their own reputation at stake. Treat this as the free first pass that removes the obvious problems.

Questions

Why are role accounts such as info@ flagged?

They are shared inboxes, are filtered more often and rarely reach the person who decides. They go to "review", not "drop"; keep one if you know it is the right door.

Is the disposable-domain list complete?

No. It holds the common services only. Treat a clean result as "no obvious problem".
