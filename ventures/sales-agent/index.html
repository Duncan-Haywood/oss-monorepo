      Optional: let an AI model read the replies and polish the drafts, on your own key
      
Off by default. With a key, each reply and draft goes from this browser straight to the provider you pick, never to us. The model's reading is merged under one rule: it may lower how much contact follows (turn "unclear" into "unsubscribe"), never raise it (it cannot turn a "no" into a meeting). Its drafts are thrown away if they state a number you did not give or make a promise. The key is kept only in this browser if you tick the box.

      
To keep everything on your own computer, run a model with Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp and pick it below: no key, no cost per use. The local server must allow this page: start Ollama with OLLAMA_ORIGINS=https://duncan-haywood.github.io, or switch on CORS in LM Studio. Your browser may ask whether this site may reach devices on your network.

      

        

        

        

        

      

      

    

  

  

  

    
How well it reads replies (measured, not claimed)

    
The rules were tuned on 120 hand-written replies until they read all of them correctly. Then 40 new replies were written and scored once, without further tuning. On those 40:

    

      
WhatResult

      
        
Replies the rules named a category for24 of 40; right on 17 (71%)

        
Replies handed to you as "unclear"16; the word model's guess was right on 9

        
Unsubscribes the rules caught3 of 5 (the other 2 were worded unusually and went to you, paused)

        
Guesses that would have drafted to someone who wanted less contact5 of 16 (guesses never act; you approve every draft)

      
    

    
So: the rules are a floor, not a reader. Read every "unclear" reply yourself, or add a model on your own key. All three test sets, the scoring script and these numbers are in the source; the replies were written by the same author as the rules, so real inboxes will score differently.

  

  

    
Questions

    
What does an AI sales agent do with replies?

    
It reads each reply to your outreach and decides what it means: unsubscribe, bounce, out of office, left the company, not now, not interested, an objection, a question, or interested. Then it does the bookkeeping a sales rep does by hand: adds unsubscribes to the suppression list with their words as the record, pauses the out-of-office and not-now prospects until a date, stops the cadence for anyone who replied, and drafts an answer for the ones worth answering.

    
Does it send emails for me?

    
No. It never sends, and it never will without you. The agent may always do less on its own, such as stopping or pausing outreach, but anything that would contact a person waits in a queue for you to read, edit and approve, and you send it from your own mailbox.

    
How accurate is it at reading replies?

    
On 40 replies written after its rules were finished, the built-in rules named a category for 24 and were right on 17, and handed the other 16 to you with a guess that was right on 9. They caught only 3 of 5 unsubscribes worded unusually. That is why every reply it is not sure of comes to you, and why an AI model on your own key can read the reply too, under the rule that it may only ever lower how much contact follows.

    
What happens when someone asks to unsubscribe?

    
Their address goes into a suppression file with the date, the reason and the sentence they wrote, every cadence to them stops, and no reply is drafted. No AI model can override that. Load the suppression file into your sending tool and the sales cadence builder so they are never contacted again.

    
Does it use AI, and is my data sent anywhere?

    
Only if you choose. With no key it runs on rules and a small word model inside your browser, and nothing is uploaded. If you add your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or point it at a model on your own computer, each reply and draft is sent from your browser straight to that provider. Model drafts that state a number you did not give are thrown away.
