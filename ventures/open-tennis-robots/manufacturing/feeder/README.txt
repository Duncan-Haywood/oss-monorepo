Open Tennis Robots: feeder + catch net manufacturing package (generator v0.2.0)

Generated from parameters in manifest.json. Rebuild after any change:
    python -m otr_cad feeder --out dist/feeder

ORDER
1. laser/      Upload the DXFs to a laser or waterjet cutting service.
               Material and thickness per part are in laser_parts.csv.
               Units are millimetres. No bends or tapped holes.
2. print/      Print the STLs in PETG (settings in print_parts.csv).
3. cut_list.csv  Cut the 2040 / 2020 extrusion and the poles to length;
               tap M5 into the extrusion ends where noted.
4. bom.csv     Buy the rest. Check bought-in dimensions against params.py.
5. guide/ASSEMBLY.md  Put it together, step by step, one drawing a step.

NOT IN THIS PACKAGE YET: the netting's cut and lacing, the take-up spring's
bracket. See hardware/feeder-catcher.md. The elevation actuator is
bought to the window in the elevation check below (any make whose retracted and
extended lengths span it).

- regen: the firmware lowers a wheel's target at 100 W of shaft power, so ESCs that brake put up to 170 W on the bus (6.2 A at 27.6 V; assumed 85% back). Into the pack that is 5.4 A of charge (BMS's assumed 15 A) and the highest cell reaches 3.58 V (cut 3.65 V); braking at a full 29.2 V pack would lift it to 3.78 V, so the firmware coasts instead of braking above 27.6 V (REGEN_BRAKE_MAX_MV). Slowing a wheel 20% from 6425 rpm: 1.3 s braking, at least 4.7 s coasting; after a stop or an e-stop the wheels (351 J each) coast for at least 23 s. Simulated: no power at switch-on, none after an e-stop or fob release, none with RESET held or taped down, none from a CLEAR_ESTOP alone; RESET pressed while running drops the bus. K0's hold contact and RESET's NC carry 0.32 A of coil current (assumed: precharge relay 0.04 A, timer 0.03 A, contactor coil 0.25 A; RESET rated 1 A DC).
