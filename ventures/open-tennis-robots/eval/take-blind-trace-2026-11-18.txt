lag 0.45 s, seed 11), captured at sub 1 and sub 8, every take-survey wheel refitted at every candidate as the board
fits it through a copy of drop_from_commands_steps that also returns t0. Per candidate: rms in ppm ("=" when within

Spread candidates on 44 wheels: whole take inside one revolution 231, of them tied with the step (rms within 0.1%) 143; straddling an edge 33, of them tied 0.

So: a candidate whose parts all land in one revolution never fits better than the step by more than the search's own
scatter, and 143 of its 231 cases tie the step to 0.1%, with t0 + its mean offset within 1-3 us of the step's t0 (a
revolution's mean speed sees a take inside it only through its parts' mean time, to first order in p / tau, and every
later revolution through the sum of e^(t_i / tau)). The other 88 differ by a few ppm either way, at t0s 2-16 us off
the step's equivalent: the t0 grid's scatter, not the take. Every one of the 33 straddling cases differs from the step,
and in both captures the informative wheels are the same four (9, 10, 14, 18: the half-sine 6 ms candidate 31-76 ppm
against the step's 123-438). Even the right candidate fits those wheels 10-20 times worse than a take-blind wheel (the
four-part take is not the simulator's continuous profile), so their best rms is the drill's highest.

## Pre-registration (written and committed before the fresh held-out capture runs)

Candidate: the take survey sums every wheel (`controller.TAKE_SURVEY_TRIM = 0`, no trim; the ESC-lag survey keeps
SURVEY_TRIM 5, which this does not retest). Fresh held-out set no run has used: the same 72 builds at wheel lag
0.38 s, seed 13, capture on, captured at sub 8 and at sub 1.

PREDICTION: (a) at sub 8, trim 5 drops at least 30 wheels and no trim names and advises more spread takes than trim
5; (b) at sub 1, trim 5 drops at most 5 wheels and the two trims name and advise within 2 of each other; (c) with no
trim, instant builds advised at sub 8 are no more than at sub 1 plus 1.

DECISION RULE: TAKE_SURVEY_TRIM = 0 ships if (a) and (b) hold. config.TACH_CAPTURE_SUB moves to 8 only if, with no
trim, sub 8 names and advises at least as many spread takes as sub 1 on this set AND advises no instant build;
otherwise it stays 1 (the earlier rule: a finer clock must not invent takes).

## Pre-registration 2 (written and committed before the third capture runs)

A non-inferiority test on a third set no run has used: the same 72 builds at wheel lag 0.52 s, seed 17, capture on,
at sub 8 and sub 1. PREDICTION: (i) at sub 8, no trim names and advises at least as many spread takes as trim 5, and
advises no more instant builds; (ii) at sub 1, the two trims are within 2; (iii) at sub 8 with no trim, at least as many
named and advised as at sub 1 with no trim, and no instant build advised.

DECISION RULE: TAKE_SURVEY_TRIM = 0 ships if (i) and (ii) hold (it then has one set where it mattered, 14 to 42, and
three where it cost nothing). TACH_CAPTURE_SUB moves to 8 only if TAKE_SURVEY_TRIM = 0 ships and (iii) holds;
otherwise both stay as they are and this file reports the miss.
