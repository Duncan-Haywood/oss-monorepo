# tests/take_survey_jitter.py --tach-capture, 2026-11-18 (firmware/esp32/README.md "A tach timed in hardware")

The same 72 survey drills as eval/take-survey-jitter-2026-11-18.txt, with the edge times taken from the simulated
MCPWM capture register (feeder_sim tach_capture=True, firmware/esp32/capture.py and controller.CaptureClock on the
board's own Tach) instead of the handler's ticks_us(). The jitter column is now the handler's read error only: it
moves ticks_us() (the tach's `since`, LaunchCheck's tick log) but not the periods.

## Pre-registration (written and committed before the held-out capture ran)

Held out: the same 72 builds at wheel lag 0.45 s, seed 11, capture on. PREDICTION: (a) at 1.5, 2 and 3 us of handler
jitter the survey names and advises at least 3 of the 9 spread takes at each (the interrupt path advised 0 at each,
both sets); (b) no instant build is advised at any jitter; (c) the mean share on spread takes at 3 us is within 0.1 of
its value at 0 us; (d) the count named and advised varies by at most 2 across the six jitters. DECISION RULE: if (a) and
(b) hold, the README says the capture keeps the survey's voice through handler jitter and `tennisbots tach-jitter`
names TACH_CAPTURE when a log reads 0.55 us or more; TACH_CAPTURE stays off by default either way (never run on a
board). If (a) fails, the README reports the miss and the advice line is not added.
