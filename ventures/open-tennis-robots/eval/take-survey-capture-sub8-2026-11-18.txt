# tests/take_survey_jitter.py --tach-capture --sub 8, 2026-11-18 (firmware/esp32/README.md "Periods finer than a microsecond")

The same 72 survey drills as eval/take-survey-capture-2026-11-18.txt (the MCPWM capture on, handler jitter 0 to 3 us),
with the capture's periods handed to PeriodTach in 1/8 us (controller.CaptureClock(sub=8), PeriodTach(sub=8),
config.TACH_CAPTURE_SUB) instead of floored to whole microseconds. The handler's jitter never reaches a capture's
periods, so the six jitter columns are near-repeats of one plant (they differ only through ticks_us() in the tach's
`since` and LaunchCheck's tick log): read the per-jitter counts as about one draw per set, not six.

Compared against (whole-microsecond capture, the same builds): design 4, 4, 4, 4, 4, 5 named and advised of 9 (25 of
54), mean share 0.56-0.60; held out 7, 7, 6, 7, 8, 8 (43 of 54), mean share 0.43-0.56.

## Pre-registration (written and committed before the held-out run)

Held out: the same 72 builds at wheel lag 0.45 s, seed 11, capture on, sub 8. PREDICTION: (a) named and advised, summed
over the six jitters, more than the whole-microsecond capture's 43 of 54 on the same builds; (b) no instant build
advised at any jitter; (c) the mean share on spread takes lower than the whole-microsecond capture's at 4 or more of
the six jitters (0.50, 0.48, 0.56, 0.54, 0.45, 0.43). DECISION RULE: if (b) fails, config.TACH_CAPTURE_SUB goes back to
1 (a finer clock must not invent takes). If (a) and (b) hold, TACH_CAPTURE_SUB = 8 stays the capture's default and
`tennisbots tach-jitter`'s clean reference names the finer floor; TACH_CAPTURE itself stays off (never run on a board).
If (a) fails with (b) holding, the README reports no measured gain and TACH_CAPTURE_SUB stays 8 only for the finer
jitter reading.
