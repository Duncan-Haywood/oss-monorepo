Interview Content Studio · free · runs in your browser

Turn one interview into a month of content for every platform

Answer a guided interview about your brand, business or topic: who you serve, the problem you solve, your real stories, opinions, advice and results. The studio sizes a posting plan to the hours you actually have, lays out a 4-week calendar, and drafts every piece in the shape each platform wants: posts for X, Threads, Bluesky, LinkedIn and Facebook, Instagram captions and carousels, scripts for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, YouTube outlines, newsletter issues, blog articles, Reddit and Pinterest posts, and podcast clips.

Drafts are built only from your own answers, so nothing is made up. Your answers stay in this browser and nothing is uploaded. AI rewriting is optional and runs on your own key or a model on your own computer. The studio never posts anything for you.

    4. Export

    This tool does not post anywhere. Review every draft, follow each platform's rules, label AI-generated content where a platform requires it (TikTok, YouTube and Meta ask creators to disclose realistic AI-generated or altered media), and do not use it for spam, fake accounts or fake reviews.

    The CSV has one row per draft with the columns date, platform, format, title and text, which most schedulers and spreadsheets can import (map the columns on import).

    Questions people ask

    How do I turn one interview into content for every platform?

    Answer the guided interview: who you are, who you serve, their problem, three to five real stories, your opinions, your advice, results you can show, and your offer. The studio turns each answer into a different angle and reshapes it for each platform: a short post for X, Threads and Bluesky, a longer post for LinkedIn, a script for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, slides for a carousel, a newsletter issue and a blog article.

    How often should I post on each platform?

    There is no single right number. The studio starts from a floor and an ideal rhythm for each format, then fits them to the hours a week you say you have, cheapest formats first, and tells you which platforms your time cannot cover. Treat the numbers as a starting point for a month, then adjust to what you can keep up and what gets replies.

    Does it make up facts or results?

    No. The built-in drafts are assembled only from your own answers, with short connecting phrases. If you turn on AI, it is told to use only facts from your interview, and any AI draft containing a number that is not in your interview is thrown away and the original draft kept.

    Do I need an AI key?

    No. Every draft works without one. Optionally, add your own Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenAI-compatible key, or point it at a model on your own computer (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp), to rewrite drafts in your voice. The key stays in your browser and goes only to the provider you pick.

    Does it post for me?

    No. It never posts anywhere. Copy drafts one by one, or export a CSV with date, platform, format, title and text to import into the scheduler you already use, then review and post yourself.
