How often to post on each platform, and how to fill the calendar from one interview

"Post every day everywhere" is advice almost nobody can follow for long. A better question is how much you can keep up, on which platforms, with the hours you really have, and where the ideas come from. This page sets out the starting rhythms the studio uses, how it fits them to your time, and how one interview supplies a month of material.

Fitting the plan to your hours

The studio turns your hours a week into a minutes budget for four weeks and spends it in three passes: first one piece of each platform's main format (cheapest platforms first, so more of them get covered), then every format up to its floor, then up towards its ideal, one piece at a time, cheapest first in each round. Whatever the budget cannot reach is listed as "not this month", not silently dropped, so you can choose between adding time and dropping a platform.

Where a month of ideas comes from: one interview

The interview asks for raw material, not posts: who you serve and their problem in their words, three to five real stories with what you learned, opinions people would argue with, advice you give again and again, results you can show, and the one thing you want people to do next. Each answer becomes an angle. The calendar gives every day one theme, so the same story appears as a hook on X, a short post with its lesson on LinkedIn, a 30 to 60 second script for Reels and TikTok, and a fuller version in the newsletter, each in the shape that platform expects. Roughly one piece in five carries your offer; the rest give value with no ask. Reddit drafts never carry one.

Because the drafts are assembled only from your answers, they cannot contain a result you did not give. If you add AI on your own key, the model is held to the same rule: a rewrite that adds a number not in your interview is thrown away.

Questions

How many times a week should a small business post?

As many times as it can keep up for months, on the platforms where its customers are. A rhythm you can hold beats a burst you abandon. The studio starts each format at a floor and an ideal rhythm, then fits them to your hours and tells you which platforms your time cannot cover.

How do I repurpose one piece of content across platforms?

Start from one idea, such as a story, an opinion or a tip, and reshape it rather than copy it: a one-line hook for X, Threads and Bluesky, a short story with a lesson for LinkedIn, a 30 to 60 second script for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, slides for a carousel, and a fuller version for a newsletter or article. The studio gives each day one theme and drafts it for every platform posting that day.
