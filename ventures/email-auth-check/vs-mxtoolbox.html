Email Authentication Checker vs. MXToolbox

Two different shapes: MXToolbox is a broad network- and mail-diagnostic platform — MX records, blacklist status, SMTP diagnostics, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC among many other tools — with ongoing monitoring and alerting sold as an account-based product. This page is a free, no-account checker focused specifically on SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and BIMI, with a recursive RFC 7208 SPF lookup count and a matching CI-gateable CLI.

What each tool is for

MXToolbox is a network- and mail-diagnostic platform used by network and mail administrators, covering MX record lookups, blacklist status across many block-list providers, SMTP banner and connectivity diagnostics, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC lookups among a large catalog of other tools. Its ongoing monitoring, alerting, and blacklist-watch features are sold through an account-based product. MXToolbox's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names, monitoring limits, or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This checker does one narrower thing in more depth. It recursively walks every include:/redirect= mechanism in your SPF record and sums the total against RFC 7208's 10-DNS-lookup limit across the whole chain, not just the top-level record — the single most common real-world SPF bug. It checks DKIM at nine common selector names and flags a revoked (empty p=) key, reads DMARC's policy, pct=, reporting addresses, and alignment mode, and checks whether BIMI is honored by DMARC enforcement. Every finding names the exact record and tag it read, not a bare pass/fail score. It runs free in any browser, and the identical logic ships as a zero-dependency Node CLI you can run as a CI gate against your own domain. No account, no monitoring dashboard, nothing stored beyond an anonymous local counter.
