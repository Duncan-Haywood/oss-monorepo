How much does Mercari take per sale?

Mercari changed its US seller fee structure for listings created or updated on or after January 6, 2025. The current schedule is a single 10% selling fee, charged on the item price plus any shipping the buyer paid, with no separate payment processing fee. A listing made before that date keeps the old schedule — 10% on the item price only, plus a payment processing fee of 2.9% of the combined total plus a flat $0.50 — until you edit it.

The current-schedule formula

fee = 10% × (item price + buyer-paid shipping)

payout = item price + buyer-paid shipping − fee − your own shipping-label cost

Mercari also charges the buyer a separate 3.6% Buyer Protection Fee, on top of what they pay — it never reduces the seller's payout, and is not part of this formula at all.

Worked example 2: the fee reaches shipping too

LineAmount

Buyer-paid shipping$6.00
Combined$26.00
Selling fee (10% of $26.00)$2.60
Payout$23.40

Not $2.00 (10% of the $20 item price alone) — the fee is on the combined $26.00. This is the detail sellers most often get wrong on a listing where the buyer pays shipping separately.

The legacy schedule: 10% + 2.9% + $0.50

A listing created before January 6, 2025 that has not since been edited still runs the old schedule: 10% of the item price only, plus a payment processing fee of 2.9% of the combined item-plus-shipping total, plus a flat $0.50 per order. On the same $50 item with no shipping, that is $5.00 selling fee + ($50 × 2.9% + $0.50) = $1.95 processing fee = $6.95 total, for a $43.05 payout — $1.95 less than the current schedule pays on the same sale, because the current schedule dropped the processing fee entirely.

The comparison flips when most of the price is shipping: the legacy schedule only taxes shipping at 2.9%, while the current schedule taxes it at the full 10%, so a listing with a large buyer-paid shipping charge can net more under the old schedule. Use the calculator's "Compare both schedules" button to check a specific sale rather than assuming either direction.

Common questions

How much does Mercari take on a $50 sale?

Under the current schedule (listings created or updated on or after January 6, 2025): 10% of $50, which is $5.00, leaving a $45.00 payout, assuming no buyer-paid shipping.

How much does Mercari take when the buyer pays for shipping?

The 10% fee applies to the item price plus the buyer-paid shipping combined. A $20 item with $6 buyer-paid shipping pays 10% of $26, which is $2.60 — not 10% of the $20 item price alone.

What was Mercari's fee before January 6, 2025?

A 10% selling fee on the item price only, plus a separate payment processing fee of 2.9% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, plus a flat $0.50 per order. A listing made before that date keeps this schedule until it is edited.
