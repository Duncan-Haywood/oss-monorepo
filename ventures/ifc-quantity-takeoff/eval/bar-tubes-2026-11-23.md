# Swept-disk bars drawn in the model view, 2026-11-23

**Question.** A reinforcing bar is almost always an `IfcSweptDiskSolid`: a
circle swept along a path of lines and arcs. The takeoff measures it
exactly (pi r^2 L, and the bar schedule reads the path), but the body has no
faces, so the 3D view drew nothing for it and it had no world box. Every
bar on every public rebar model was invisible, could not be clicked from
a schedule line, and failed the box check against IfcOpenShell. Can the
view draw it from the same path, closed and facing out, at a cost a browser
can carry on a model with thousands of stirrups?

**What changed** (`view-mesh.mjs` `tubeTriangles`, `tubeDetail`;
`geom.mjs` `barPolyline`, `barExtent`; `qto.mjs` `worldBox`).

- A swept disk's bar is marked `tube` (an extruded round bar, which already
  has faces, is not) and the mark survives mapped items and placements.
- The view draws it as a polygon tube along its centreline: arcs cut into
  chords of at most 22.5 degrees, the ring of points carried along by
  parallel transport (no twist), a mitre on the bisecting plane at each
  bend (so a straight run keeps its full section and the tube's volume is
  exactly section x centreline length), two square rings instead where a
  bend turns more than 120 degrees (a hairpin's mitre would reach far out),
  and caps at both ends. The inner radius of a hollow tube is not drawn.
- How many sides: 8, unless the model's bars would need more than 600,000
  triangles at that detail; then 6, then 4 sides with 45-degree chords, then
  3. The quantities are untouched: the volume, length and bar schedule still
  come from the exact path.
- The world box of a swept disk is the centreline's reach widened by the
  disk, r sqrt(1 - (t.n)^2) along each axis for each piece (arcs read as
  their chords).

**Checks.**

- Unit tests (`view-mesh.test.mjs`, `rebar.test.mjs`): a straight tube
  encloses the 8-gon section x length to 1e-12; bent bars at 30, 90 and 119
  degrees enclose section x centreline length to 1e-9 (the mitre); a
  hairpin stays inside the bar's reach; a quarter-circle bar encloses section
  x chord length; Allplan's faceted L-bar gets a box to 1e-6 m and a closed
  tube.
- buildingSMART's IFC4.3 samples against IfcOpenShell 0.9.0 (rerun of
  [bsi-samples-2026-11-22.md](bsi-samples-2026-11-22.md); raw:
  [bsi-samples-2026-11-23-tubes.txt](bsi-samples-2026-11-23-tubes.txt)):
  world boxes within 2 mm **77 to 112 of 115** elements; the two bar
  files **1 of 36 to 36 of 36** (all 35 bars, 0 before; the other element is
  the assembly's concrete beam). Volumes unchanged, 110 of 115.
- Nine public Tekla Structures 2025 exports of a real plant's concrete
  (agustdiez/tekla-structures-hyt, `docs/ref/Ejemplo Hormigon/areas/`;
  seven hold bars) and the two buildingSMART bar samples:
  `node eval/bar-tubes.mjs <files>`, raw:
  [bar-tubes-2026-11-23.txt](bar-tubes-2026-11-23.txt).

SENDA-E-O is the one file over budget: 6,478 stirrups and bars would take
2.7M triangles at 8 sides, 2.0M at 6, so it is drawn at 3 sides with
45-degree chords (725k triangles, enclosing 0.41 of the round bar). Every
other file is drawn at 8 sides.

**On the page.** The stirrup sample before and after
([before](bar-tubes-stirrup-before-2026-11-23.png),
[after](bar-tubes-stirrup-2026-11-23.png)): before, an empty view; after,
the closed link with its two hooks. In the Tekla and buildingSMART models
the bars sit inside their concrete, so they show with the section plane or
when the concrete is hidden.

**Left.** A tube whose rings could share points with the next element's
(instancing identical bars in a Tekla group would cut SENDA-E-O's memory
roughly 20-fold and let it draw at 8 sides); swept disks in MEP models
(handrails, pipes written as swept disks) were not looked for; a hollow
tube's bore is not drawn.
