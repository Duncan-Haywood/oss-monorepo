# Rooms measured from their solids, 13 public IFC models, 2026-11-21

**Question.** An estimator prices finishes and fit-out room by room: floor
finish by floor area, skirting by perimeter, wall finish by perimeter x
height, loose items by the room they stand in. Exporters store some of this
on each IfcSpace (`Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities`). Can the takeoff measure each
room from its own solid, does the result match what is stored, and can it put
the elements the file leaves outside any room into the right one?

**Data.** `ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc`, `tests/ifcfiles/public/` (blob-less
sparse clone, not committed): every file there with an IfcSpace. ARK_NUS
skolebygg, dental_clinic, schependomlaan, Office_A_20110811, C20-Institute-Var-2,
FM_ARC_DigitalHub, kleine_Wohnung_R22, duplex, ISSUE_005_haus, AC20-FZK-Haus,
ISSUE_034_HouseZ, example, ISSUE_171.

**Method.** `node eval/rooms.mjs <files> --json eval/rooms-2026-11-21.json`
(output: `rooms-2026-11-21.txt`). `rooms.mjs` cuts each room's solid 1 cm
above its lowest point for floor area and perimeter (the outline of the room,
not of its convex pieces: edges two pieces share cancel along their line),
takes height as lowest to highest point and volume from the solid. A room with
no body but a `FootPrint` polyline and a `Box` is the outline extruded through
the box. Each figure is compared with the stored one (GrossFloorArea, then
NetFloorArea; GrossPerimeter; Height; NetVolume, then GrossVolume) within 2%.

991 rooms; 989 measured (895 from a body, 94 from an outline and box); one had
no extent and one (ISSUE_171) a body this reader does not take
(an intersection with a faceted operand).

Findings, in the order they were met:

- **ArchiCAD's NetFloorArea is not the floor's area.** On AC20-FZK-Haus and
  ISSUE_005 the computed floor area matched the stored NetFloorArea on 1 of 7
  rooms and the GrossFloorArea on 7 of 7. Net = gross x 0.97 on six rooms
  (the room's "Reduzierte Fläche" 0.970: a plaster allowance), and on the
  Galerie net = gross less a 32.65 m2 low-headroom zone. A space's solid is
  its gross floor, so the check now reads GrossFloorArea first; the CSV keeps
  both stored figures so the deduction stays visible.
- **Rooms with no body.** schependomlaan's 94 of 100 rooms carry only a
  `Box` and a `FootPrint` polyline. Read from those: 100 of 100 floor areas,
  perimeters and heights agree. Eleven top-floor volumes are stored 3-9% under
  the prism: the rooms sit under the sloped roof, and the outline through the
  box's full height holds more than they do (`outline-prism`, use the stored
  volume).
- **ARK_NUS writes room volumes in litres** (241 rooms, exactly x 1000), and
  57 more are floor area x the stated 2.9 m height, in litres, over ceilings
  lowered to 2.64-2.65 m (wcs, corridors, stairs). The heights say the same:
  30 rooms state 2.9 m over a 2.6-2.65 m solid.
- **HouseZ writes areas in mm2 and volumes in mm3** under m2 and m3 units (5
  of 5 rooms).
- **kleine_Wohnung and C20** agree throughout (28 of 28, 82 of 82 on every
  figure but C20's two attic heights).

Left unexplained: ARK_NUS 01109 lærerarbeid (stored 50.0 m2, solid 30.2),
01170 Utebod (18.7 vs 4.4), the two M&H wcs' perimeters (35.4 vs 42.3) and
02002 kantine upper part (128.4 vs 135.9), and eight ARK_NUS volumes 1-2% off
after the litre factor. Each room's solid would need drawing to say which
side is wrong.

## Elements placed in rooms

The files place 1,652 elements in rooms themselves (ARK_NUS 1,268 pieces of
furniture and fittings, dental_clinic 219, duplex 61). For the rest, the
middle of the element's plan box is tested at 10 cm above its bottom, at half
height and 10 cm under its top, against each room's solid; the smallest room
holding it wins. Placed this way: 1,024 elements.

| model | by the file | by where they stand |
|---|---:|---:|
| ARK_NUS | 1,268 | 195 (174 coverings, 19 proxies, 2 flow segments) |
| C20-Institute | 0 | 253 furnishings |
| dental_clinic | 219 | 241 (240 coverings) |
| schependomlaan | 12 | 198 coverings |
| Office_A | 38 | 83 ceilings |
| FM_ARC_DigitalHub | 0 | 39 |
| duplex | 61 | 13 ceilings |

Two rules came from the first run:

- **Elements that bound rooms are not placed.** ARK_NUS draws its rooms to
  wall centrelines, so a first pass put 901 walls, 306 doors, 266 windows and
  852 curtain-wall members inside a room, each in whichever room was tested
  first. Walls, curtain walls, slabs, roofs, doors, windows, plates, members,
  beams, columns, footings, piles, stairs, ramps, railings and chimneys are
  now placed only where the file says.
- **A ceiling belongs to the room under it.** Revit rooms stop at the
  ceiling's underside, so none of Office_A's 83 ceilings had a test point
  inside a room. A covering typed CEILING is also tested just under itself
  (a FLOORING one just over): 83 of 83 placed, and duplex 13 of 13,
  DigitalHub 38 of 38.

Not placed: schependomlaan's 1,016 untyped coverings are facade cladding and
insulation (outside every room, as they should be); 10 dental_clinic ceilings
sit over corridors with no IfcSpace.

## What it costs

The room pass adds nothing measurable: ARK_NUS takes 12-15 s with or without
it (the takeoff dominates).
