# Cant: IfcSegmentedReferenceCurve, 2026-11-23

**Question.** Rail alignments in IFC 4.3 carry their cant (superelevation) as
an `IfcSegmentedReferenceCurve` laid over the gradient curve. Parts placed on
it by `IfcLinearPlacement` (sleepers, slab track, rail fastenings) should sit
raised by the deviating elevation and rolled by the cross slope. Until today
such a part was "not read". Can the takeoff read the curve, and does its
reading agree with buildingSMART's text and with IfcOpenShell?

**Test file.** buildingSMART's IFC4.3.x-sample-models,
`models/alignment-geometries-and-linear-positioning/segmented-reference-curve`
(commit ef22440). A 100 m cosine transition spiral in plan (radius 300 to
1000 m, `IfcCosineSpiral`), level, and a cant that runs out on a cosine curve
from 0.16 m on the right rail to 0 (railhead distance 1.5 m). The README next
to the file works one station through by hand: D(s) = L/A0 + L/A1
cos(pi s / L), D(50) = 0.04 m, slope -0.0012567, and the Axis angle theta
moving linearly with D between the two segment placements. No element is
placed on the curve; the file is the reference for the curve alone.

**Method.** `eval/cant-ifcopenshell.py` (IfcOpenShell 0.9.0.post1) places two
`IfcLinearPlacement`s in memory every 2.5 m from 0 to 100 m on the plan curve
(#65) and on the cant curve (#112), one on the curve and one 0.75 m to the
left, and writes their world origins. `node eval/cant.mjs file.ifc ios65.json
ios112.json` compares those with `alignment.mjs`. Output:
[cant-2026-11-23.txt](cant-2026-11-23.txt).

## Results (41 stations)

| What | Here vs | Largest difference |
|---|---|---|
| plan point | IfcOpenShell | 5.0e-7 m |
| plan end (100 m) | the file's own next horizontal segment placement | **2.4e-9 m** here, 5.0e-7 m IfcOpenShell |
| deviating elevation | the README's formula | 6.9e-18 m |
| its slope | the README's derivative | 2.2e-19 |
| theta | the README's interpolation | 2.2e-16 rad |
| roll (from the point 0.75 m left) | IfcOpenShell | under 0.0001 degree |
| deviating elevation | IfcOpenShell | **7.92 m** |

The semantic cant agrees at both ends by hand: at the start the
`IfcAlignmentCantSegment` says right rail 0.16 m, left 0, so the centreline
is raised 0.08 m (the placement's Location y) and the roll is
atan(0.16 / 1.5) = 0.10627 rad (the placement's Axis, atan2(0.10606,
0.99436) = 0.10626 rad).

**IfcOpenShell's elevation.** IfcOpenShell 0.9 starts at 0.08 m and ends at
-7.92 m, where the file's EndPoint (and the semantic cant, 0 on both rails)
says 0. At every station its change from 0.08 m is exactly 100.000000 times
the README's: L, the segment length, applied once too often. Its roll agrees
with the text, and its plan with ours to 5e-7 m. So the reading here follows
the sample's text, and the elevation is the one place the two tools differ.
Reporting it upstream is an outside step and is left to the owner.

## What was added (`alignment.mjs`)

- `IfcCosineSpiral` as a parent curve: curvature 1/A1 cos(pi s / L) + 1/A0,
  with L the curve segment's length. The sample's plan ends where the file
  puts the next segment, 2.4e-9 m off, which confirms that L is the segment
  length.
- `IfcSegmentedReferenceCurve` as a basis curve over a gradient or composite
  curve. Each segment's placement gives (distance along, deviating
  elevation) and its Axis the cross slope (roll = atan2(Axis y, Axis z),
  positive with the right rail high). Between one placement and the next (or
  the EndPoint), elevation and theta move by w(s) = (f(s) - f(0)) / (f(L) -
  f(0)), where f is the parent's function, L times its curvature. For the
  cosine parent this is the README's formula exactly. A clothoid parent gives
  a linear run-out. A line (constant cant) holds, or moves linearly if the
  file's two placements differ. A 2D placement (the sample's own zero-length
  closing segment is one) is read as level.
- The station frame: raised by D, with the elevation's slope added to the
  gradient, and its lateral and up axes rolled about the tangent.
  `IfcLinearPlacement` offsets and axes and `IfcSectionedSolidHorizontal`
  cross-sections use the rolled frame. `IfcFixedReferenceSweptAreaSolid`
  keeps its FixedReference, as its definition says, and gets only the raised
  path.

Tests (`alignment.test.mjs`) cover a made-up 200 m track: a cosine run-in to
0.15 m, a constant 40 m stretch and a linear run-out. They check elevation,
slope and roll at each stretch, a lateral offset dropping by 0.75 sin(roll),
and a 2.6 m sleeper's world box tilted with its low end at the left rail.

## Left

- No public rail model with cant and parts placed on it has been found yet:
  the bSI samples hold the curve alone. That is the next real test.
- The sine and polynomial spirals (`IfcSineSpiral`,
  `IfcSecondOrderPolynomialSpiral` and others), both in plan and as cant
  parents, are not read.
- The curve has been checked against the semantic `IfcAlignmentCant` at the
  two ends only, by hand. Checking every station is not done.
