# Stored figures in doubt: what the bill of quantities sums, 2026-11-23

**Question.** The 2026-11-22 quality audit
([audit](../../../portfolio/quality/audits/2026-11-22-ifc-quantity-takeoff.json))
found that a stored NetVolume that disagrees with the geometry *for no named
cause* is still the figure the bill of quantities sums (`rowQuantities`), with
nothing on the line to say so. How much of a real BoQ rests on such figures,
and what should the page do with them?

**Data.** 15 public models: `opensourceBIM/IFC-files` (HHS Office architect
and construction) and `ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc` `tests/ifcfiles/public/`
(the 13 of [stored-vs-computed-2026-11-19.md](stored-vs-computed-2026-11-19.md)
plus FM_ARC_DigitalHub and ISSUE_102_M3D-CON-CD), blob-less sparse clones, not
committed.

**Method.** `node eval/in-doubt.mjs <files> --json eval/in-doubt-2026-11-23.json`
(output: [in-doubt-2026-11-23.txt](in-doubt-2026-11-23.txt)). A row is *in
doubt* when the very figure the BoQ sums (NetVolume if stored, else
GrossVolume; the first stored net or gross area) disagrees with the geometry
by more than 2% and `explainChecks` names no cause. For each model: the BoQ's
total volume and area (class, type lines), the rows in doubt, and what their
stored and geometry figures sum to.

On 11 of 15 models nothing the BoQ sums is in doubt. On ARK_NUS, two slabs
("Floor:Betongdekke 250 m fall", #1186582 and #1483037) store 5,618.50 and
802.35 m3 against bodies of 5.29 and 0.78 m3: 6,415 m3 of concrete that is
not in the model, 43% of the whole bill. The 2026-11-21 cause search
([stored-vs-computed-2026-11-21.md](stored-vs-computed-2026-11-21.md)) had seen
them (ratios 1,061.6 and 1,034.2) and left them unexplained because neither is
a power of ten within the unit-slip tolerance.

## The cause: a nominal-thickness volume, written in litres

Both slabs also store GrossArea and Width. Their stored GrossVolume is
exactly GrossArea x Width x 1000:

| | GrossArea m2 | Width m | GrossArea x Width x 1000 | stored GrossVolume | body m3 | body / (area x width) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| #1186582 | 22.4740007609 | 0.25 | 5,618.50019022 | 5,618.50019022 | 5.2924 | 0.942 |
| #1483037 | 3.2094104574 | 0.25 | 802.35261435 | 802.35261435 | 0.7758 | 0.967 |

Two faults at once: the volume is written in litres under a cubic-metre unit
(as 96 other slabs in this corpus are), and it is the nominal 250 mm times the
area, while the body is a slab laid to falls ("m fall", sloped to drain) whose
mean thickness is 236 and 242 mm. The cut plan area of each body equals the
stored GrossArea to 1e-12, so the area is the body's own.

`explainChecks` now names this `nominal-slip` (`nominalSlip` in `qto.mjs`):
the stored volume equals the element's own stored area x its stored width (or
depth, thickness) x 10^k, k from -9 to 9 and not 0, **to 1e-6 of the figure**,
and the body is within 15% of area x width. Six-digit agreement of a product
of two stored figures is the exporter's own arithmetic, not chance; the 15%
keeps it to the same element. The BoQ then sums the geometry.

Rerunning [stored-vs-computed](stored-vs-computed-2026-11-23-nominal.txt) on
the 13 models: exactly those two pairs move (ARK_NUS GrossVolume unexplained
3 to 1, nominal-slip 2); unexplained GrossVolumes over the corpus 12 to 10.
ARK_NUS's BoQ volume goes from 14,783.34 to 8,368.56 m3.

## What is left in doubt, and what the page does with it

After the fix, 3 volumes and 13 areas over 15 models are in doubt:

- HHS construction #1405, #4407 (Revit "STB 20.0", "STB 30.0" walls): stored
  about twice the body (5.25 against 2.66, 16.84 against 8.26 m3): 11.18 m3,
  0.47% of the model's BoQ volume.
- ARK_NUS #1167770: 0.4705 stored against 0.4609, 2.1% (just past the tolerance).
- M3D-CON-CD's 7 Tekla plates store an area 2 to 3 times the plan cut
  (28.30 against 12.88 m2 for each "JD-1"): 32.6 m2 of swing, 6.6% of that
  model's small BoQ area.
- schependomlaan's three zinc copings and ARK_NUS's three walls, already listed
  in [curved-and-faces-2026-11-22.md](curved-and-faces-2026-11-22.md), "The 14 left".

No rule picks the right figure for these: in the cause search so far, most
named disagreements were the stored figure's fault, but a few were the
geometry reader's, and an unexplained one could be either. So the page does
not choose silently:

- every BoQ line shows "N in doubt: stored X, geometry Y" for its volume and its
  area, and a sentence under the table totals them; the CSV carries six columns
  (count, stored sum and geometry sum for volumes and for areas) and which
  figure was summed;
- a control ("Where stored and geometry disagree for no known cause") sums the
  stored figure (the default, as before) or the geometry's; the CLI's
  `--unexplained geometry` does the same.

The default stays the stored figure, the model author's own, because nothing
here shows the geometry is right more often for the unexplained rest; the
range is shown so an estimator can price either.

## What this does not show

- Whether the two HHS walls' stored figures or their bodies are right (a
  wall drawn half as thick as its type, or a volume doubled?) needs the model
  opened in Revit.
- The 15% shape window comes from two slabs of one model; a slab laid to
  steeper falls than 15% of its thickness would stay unexplained (and in doubt,
  so shown, not hidden).
- The rule reads only GrossArea or NetArea x Width/Depth/Thickness; an
  exporter writing Length x Width x Height x 10^k is the existing
  `own-dimensions` test's case only when within 2%.
