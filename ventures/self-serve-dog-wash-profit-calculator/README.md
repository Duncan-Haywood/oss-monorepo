# Self-Serve Dog Wash Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/self-serve-dog-wash-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people planning a self-serve (and optionally full-service) dog wash: tubs, wash price, washes per tub by weekday, weekend and season, water, supplies, labor, rent, build-out and a loan become break-even washes per day, operating profit, yield on start-up cost, a month-by-month ramp with the lowest cash point, the payback month and an equity view.

After using this, you know how many dogs a day the shop must wash to cover its costs and loan, whether the tubs can physically do it, and how much cash to hold while traffic builds.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/self-serve-dog-wash-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
