    Common questions

    Why does a screen roof (lanai style) cost so much more than screening under an existing roof?

    “Existing roof” only screens the walls of a porch that's already covered, while a screen roof frames and mesh-panels the entire top of the enclosure — a separate structure with its own hardware and span limits. The roof dropdown prices these as distinct scopes so a pool-cage-style quote isn't built off wall-only numbers.

    Does upgrading to hurricane or pet-resistant mesh actually change the bid much?

    Yes, proportionally to your total screen square footage — both cost more per panel than standard fiberglass, and hurricane mesh is heavier and slower to install and tension correctly. Switch the mesh dropdown to see the material and labor difference on your actual dimensions rather than guessing at a flat upcharge.

    Does this tell me if my knee wall height or screen enclosure meets code?

    No. Knee wall height, structural attachment, and enclosure permitting are set by local building code and vary by jurisdiction — this calculator prices the wall height and roof type you select, it does not verify compliance. Confirm requirements with your local building department before framing.
