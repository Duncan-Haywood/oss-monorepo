# UK Marriage Allowance Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-marriage-allowance-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter both partners' incomes and see, for 2025/26 and 2026/27, whether you qualify for Marriage Allowance, the tax you save each year (up to £252), any cost to the lower earner, and what a claim backdated up to four earlier tax years is worth.

After using this, you know whether to claim and how much you can get back, which used to mean reading the eligibility rules and working the allowance transfer out by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-marriage-allowance-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
