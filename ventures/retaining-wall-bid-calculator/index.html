FOR HARDSCAPE AND LANDSCAPE CONTRACTORS

Bid the height and the drain, not the linear foot.

A flat per-foot quote treats a 40 ft, 3 ft garden wall as the same job as a 40 ft, 6 ft engineered wall. Face area, drain-stone volume, and geogrid layers grow with height. An engineer stamp does not shrink on a short, tall wall. Price those extras separately — then see the price, the dollars per foot, and the number below which the job loses money.

The wall

Start from a common residential wall, then replace length and height with the job you actually walked.

Height, drainage, and extras

Height multiplies face labor. Drain stone grows with face area. Geogrid layers and engineer fees are a step, not a foot. Corners are extra cuts that do not get cheaper on a short wall.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Matching a cheaper per-foot quote after leaving it out is how a sold job becomes a loss.
