# Craft Brewery Batch Margin Calculator

Free, private browser tool for craft brewers: enter your beers and sales channels and it costs each batch after yield loss, labour, ingredients, federal and state excise and a share of overhead, shows profit per barrel in the taproom, as self-distributed kegs and as distributor cans, flags any channel that loses money (with its break-even price), and ranks beers by profit per barrel of fermenter per day. Exports a CSV and a text summary.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/craft-brewery-batch-margin-calculator/

After using this, you know which beer and which channel deserve the tank, which used to take a spreadsheet nobody trusted.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/craft-brewery-batch-margin-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
