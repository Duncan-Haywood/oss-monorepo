# Employment Contract Checker

Free, private browser tool: paste a job offer or employment agreement and see the clauses that most often cost the signer (non-compete and non-solicit, invention assignment with no carve-out, signing-bonus repayment, forfeited bonus or commissions, equity buyback and short exercise windows, arbitration, long notice, salary that does not match what you were told), each with the change to ask for.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/employment-contract-checker/

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/employment-contract-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.

Not legal advice. It reads wording with fixed rules and does not decide enforceability in any state. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
