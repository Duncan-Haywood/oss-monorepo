A free pay application template for Excel, with the retainage worked out.

Two sheets: an Application summary (contract sum, change orders, completed and stored, retainage, previous certificates, payment due, balance to finish) and a Continuation sheet with one row per line of your schedule of values. Type into the shaded cells; every total is a formula.

Filling it in, month by month

  1. First application. On the Continuation sheet type the retainage rates from your contract and each line of the schedule of values: item, description and scheduled value. Put CO in column C of each approved change order. On the Application sheet type the project and leave line 7, previous certificates, at zero.
  2. Each month. Type the work done this period on each line, and any materials delivered but not installed under Materials presently stored. Read the payment due from line 8.
  3. Next month. Save a copy. Add this period (F) into from previous applications (E) on each line, clear F, and type last month's line 6 into line 7 (unless the owner certified a different amount). Or let the pay application generator roll it forward: work out this month's application there, use Next month, and download the result as Excel.

Common questions

What is in the pay application template?

Two sheets. The Application sheet has the project details and the nine summary lines: original contract sum, net change by change orders, contract sum to date, total completed and stored, retainage, total earned less retainage, previous certificates, current payment due, and balance to finish including retainage. The Continuation sheet has the retainage rates and one row per line of the schedule of values, in lettered columns A to L: item, description, type, scheduled value, from previous applications, this period, materials presently stored, completed and stored to date, percent complete, balance to finish, the line's own retainage rate, and retainage. The blank template has 30 lines.

Is this the AIA G702 and G703 form?

No. It is our own layout covering the same kind of summary and continuation sheet. The AIA documents are copyrighted and this template is not affiliated with the American Institute of Architects. If your owner or general contractor requires the AIA form or their own portal, use the numbers from this workbook to fill it in.

How is retainage worked out in the spreadsheet?

Each line's retainage is the work rate times previous plus this period, plus the stored-materials rate times materials presently stored, each rounded to the cent. The two rates are typed once at the top of the Continuation sheet. A line with a different rate takes it in column K, which then applies to both its work and its stored materials.

How do I add change orders?

Add each approved change order as its own row with CO in the Type column (C) and its scheduled value, negative for a deductive change order. The Application sheet sums CO rows into line 2 and everything else into line 1.

How do I start next month's application?

Save a copy of the file. On each line add This period into Previous applications and clear This period. Leave stored materials where they are until they are installed. Type last month's line 6 (total earned less retainage) into line 7, previous certificates, unless the owner certified a different amount. The free pay application generator can do this for you: work out this month there, use Next month, enter the new period's work, and download the result as Excel.

Does it work in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice?

It should. It is a standard .xlsx file with ordinary formulas (SUM, SUMIF, ROUND, IF). A test opens it in LibreOffice with every cached value stripped, so the formulas have to be worked out from scratch, and checks the summary and each line's retainage against the generator's own arithmetic to the cent. It has not been tested in Excel or Google Sheets by us; both read standard .xlsx formulas.
