Common questions

Does it submit applications for me?

No. It prepares every answer and you paste and submit them yourself. Many job boards forbid automated applying in their terms, and an application sent without your review can say something untrue in your name. Keep track of what you sent with the job application tracker.

Will it take interviews for me?

No. Software posing as you in a live interview misleads the employer, and the job is yours to do afterwards. It prepares you instead: likely questions, each paired with a story from your own answers, and the gaps where you still need one.

Where is my information stored?

Only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded. Download your profile to keep a copy or move it to another device.

Can the AI make things up?

It is told to use only your profile, and every draft is checked: any number or employer that is not in your profile or the job description is flagged before you copy it. That is a guard, not a guarantee, so read every draft. Check your resume's format with the ATS resume checker and compare offers with the total compensation calculator.
