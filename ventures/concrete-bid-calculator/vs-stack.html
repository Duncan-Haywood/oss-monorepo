This calculator vs. STACK

Two different shapes: STACK is an account-based takeoff and estimating platform for running a contractor's whole preconstruction workflow, across trades and across many jobs. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this driveway, patio, or slab cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

STACK is a cloud-based construction takeoff and estimating platform used by contractors across trades — including concrete, but also framing, roofing, electrical, and general contracting — to turn digital plans into a measured takeoff, build estimates and proposals from it, and manage the broader preconstruction workflow, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every project. STACK's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that takeoff and estimating software describe measuring plan sets and building estimates from them across a portfolio of jobs and trades; none describe working out what one concrete pour should cost from its own area, thickness, forms, and finish labor before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what STACK does. It does not read a plan set, manage a takeoff library, or track a bid across a sales pipeline. It takes one slab's area and thickness for cubic yards, a waste percentage rounded up to the ready-mix plant's billed order increment, form footage, reinforcement, finish labor by the surface you pick, access cost for a pump or wheelbarrow pour, a short-load fee if the order falls under your threshold, and demo and haul of any existing slab, and turns that into the recommended price, the walk-away number, the price per cubic yard, the price per square foot, and a check of whether a price you're already considering still clears your own margin — plus a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or STACK?

They do different jobs, and a concrete or general contractor of any size will likely end up touching both. STACK is a cloud-based construction takeoff and estimating platform used by contractors across trades to digitize plans into a takeoff, generate estimates and proposals, and manage the broader preconstruction workflow, on an ongoing paid account. This calculator does one narrower thing: it takes a slab's area, thickness, and waste percentage to get ordered cubic yards, plus form footage, reinforcement, finish labor by surface, access for a pump or wheelbarrow, a short-load fee, and demo and haul of an existing slab, and turns that into the recommended price, the walk-away number, price per cubic yard, and price per square foot, before you have quoted anything. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A contractor already running digitized takeoffs and estimates across many projects and trades is better served by STACK or a comparable takeoff platform; someone who needs an instant, private answer to what one driveway, patio, or slab should cost before it is offered is in the right place here.
