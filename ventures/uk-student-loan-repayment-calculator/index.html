Common questions

    
How much of my pay goes to student loan repayments?

    
9% of everything you earn above your plan's threshold for Plan 1, 2, 4 and 5, and a further 6% above the Postgraduate Loan threshold if you have one. It is taken from pay, not from the balance, so a bigger balance does not change the monthly deduction.

    
Will my student loan be written off?

    
Many borrowers on Plan 2 and Plan 5 never clear the loan from pay alone, and the remaining balance is cancelled after 30 years (Plan 2, Plan 4, Postgraduate) or 40 years (Plan 5); Plan 1 is 25 years. This calculator projects the write-off from your pay, growth and interest assumptions.

    
Should I overpay my student loan?

    
Only if you would otherwise clear it before write-off. If part of the balance will be cancelled anyway, extra payments just turn into cash you did not have to spend. Enter a yearly extra amount and the tool shows the change in total cost.

    
Which interest rate and thresholds does this use?

    
Prefilled with the 2025/26 thresholds and a 6% annual rate. The real Plan 2 rate varies with income and RPI and every figure is reset by government each year, so edit them from GOV.UK.

    
Is a student loan like normal debt?

    
It is repaid as a percentage of income, is cancelled after a fixed term and does not appear as a normal debt on most credit reports. That is why the monthly cost rises and falls with pay.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent.
