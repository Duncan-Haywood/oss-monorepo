# UK Student Loan Repayment Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your plan (1, 2, 4 or 5), balance, pay and any Postgraduate Loan, and see the monthly deduction, the year the loan clears or is written off, and whether overpaying saves anything.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-student-loan-repayment-calculator/

After using this, you know whether your loan is a debt you will clear or a graduate tax that gets cancelled, which used to mean building a spreadsheet of thresholds and interest by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-student-loan-repayment-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
