FOR DRAINAGE AND EXCAVATION CONTRACTORS

Bid the trench, the outlet, and the soil.

Price an exterior French drain the way the trade actually loses money: trench depth, pipe size, clay versus dirt, daylight versus a drywell, catch basins, downspout ties, and a driveway crossing. You get the bid, the hours the first deep clay run actually consumes, and the walk-away number below which the job loses money.
