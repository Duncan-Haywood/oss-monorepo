How to choose a log management tool by GB ingested

Put your GB of logs per month and growth rate into the calculator to price Datadog Logs, Grafana Cloud Logs, Axiom, Better Stack and Papertrail side by side. These are the questions to settle before you trust the answer.

Why do log-management prices differ so much at the same volume?

Some vendors bill per GB ingested and charge separately for indexing and retention (Datadog), others sell a flat plan with a pool of included GB (Axiom, Papertrail), and the per-GB rate varies by more than an order of magnitude. At 100 GB a month the cheapest and most expensive list prices in the calculator differ by hundreds of dollars.

What matters more than the per-GB price?

GB you do not send. Dropping debug logs in production, sampling noisy health checks, trimming verbose payloads and routing cold logs to cheap archive storage usually cut a logging bill more than switching vendor. Also check retention and search speed: a cheap tool that cannot answer a query during an incident costs more than it saves.

When should I switch log-management vendors?

When the 12-month spend under your growth rate differs enough to pay for the migration, which means re-pointing shippers or agents, rebuilding dashboards and alerts and re-learning a query language. Run the calculator with your growth rate before deciding, and remember retention and indexing fees can change the real figure.
