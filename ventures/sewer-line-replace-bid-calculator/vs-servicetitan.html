It is sold as an ongoing account for the company rather than as a single-job tool. This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not hold customer records, dispatch trucks, run a technician app, send invoices, or collect payments, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes the replacement method (open-cut excavation, pipe bursting, or CIPP lining), the run footage and how much of it sits under hard surface, bury depth, pipe diameter, the number of laterals, cleanouts, and access pits, whether the house stays occupied and needs a bypass, plus loaded wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the crew hours the job actually consumes, the price per foot, the share of the bid that is surface restoration rather than pipe, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Does this calculator decide whether a line can be burst or lined?

No. It prices the method you pick; it does not pick the method. Whether a given run can be pipe burst or lined at all depends on the existing pipe material and condition, bellies and offsets, the number and position of laterals, separation from other utilities, local code, and what the permit and inspection require, and that is a judgment call for the licensed plumber on site with a camera in the line. The calculator's value is showing what each method costs on the same run once that call has been made, including the surface restoration that often decides which one keeps the margin.
