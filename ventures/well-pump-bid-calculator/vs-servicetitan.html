This calculator vs. ServiceTitan

Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is an account-based field service management platform for running a well-service or plumbing company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one well-pump job cost, given its depth and its extras, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan, headquartered in Glendale, California. It serves HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other trades with scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, reporting, and job costing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. ServiceTitan's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software serving HVAC and plumbing among other trades, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceTitan appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not schedule techs, dispatch crews, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one well-pump job's setting depth past the included run, submersible-versus-jet hours, pump horsepower, a good versus a seized pitless adapter, a stuck-pump pull, extra well cable past the included run, a new pressure tank, and a constant-pressure drive, plus your crew wage, payroll burden, travel, overhead, and target margin. It turns that into the price, the price per foot of depth, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or ServiceTitan?

They do different jobs, and a well-service or plumbing shop of any size will likely end up touching both eventually. A shop that already schedules techs, dispatches crews, and invoices across every job is better served by ServiceTitan or a comparable field service platform; a shop that wants an instant, private answer to what one well-pump job should cost from its depth and its extras is in the right place here.

Does this calculator size the pump, the pressure tank, or the wire gauge for me?

No. Hours and material rates are ballpark shop figures you can edit, not a well-yield calculation, a voltage-drop determination, or a code review. Whether a given horsepower and cable gauge suit the well's depth and flow, and whether the installation meets local well-code requirements, are still a judgment call for the licensed tech on site. Verify every figure against your own takeoff and a licensed professional before you submit a bid or pull a permit.
