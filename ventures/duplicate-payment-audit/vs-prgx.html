Common questions

Should I use this tool or hire PRGX?

They fit different sizes of business. PRGX Global is a publicly traded recovery-audit firm: it works with large enterprises, plugging into their AP systems to review very high transaction volumes across many vendors and years, and it is paid a contingency fee out of whatever it recovers — so an engagement only makes sense once the expected recovery is large enough to be worth a percentage of it. This page is a free, one-time, self-serve check of a CSV export you already have, aimed at small businesses and bookkeepers a contingency firm has no reason to take on. It flags candidate duplicates for you to review yourself; it does not investigate, negotiate a recovery, or take a cut of anything.

Does this tool do the same forensic audit PRGX does?

No. PRGX's audits combine software with the firm's own auditors reviewing findings, contacting vendors, and pursuing recovery of confirmed overpayments across a client's full AP history. This page runs three pattern checks locally in your browser — an exact same-vendor/same-invoice-number/same-amount match, a same-vendor/same-invoice-number different-amount match, and a same-vendor/same-amount near-date match when invoice numbers are missing or nearly identical — and hands you a list to verify against your own records. It does not contact vendors, file disputes, or confirm that a flagged pair is an actual overpayment.

Is PRGX free?

No. PRGX and similar recovery-audit firms are typically paid on a contingency-fee basis, taking a percentage of whatever they recover, which is why they focus on large clients where that percentage is worth the engagement. This tool has no fee of any kind: it is a free static page, and the CSV you upload never leaves your browser.
