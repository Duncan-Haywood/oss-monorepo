Access, packing, and drive

Stairs and a long carry add hours from cubic feet — more cubes, more trips. Elevator wait is flat: the crew sits through it together. Minutes per 200 cu ft are starting points; time your own crew and replace them.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, and paid time off — the part of a mover’s cost that never shows up on their pay rate. Matching a cheap “3-bedroom for $800” quote without seeing stairs is the most common way a Saturday loses money.

Questions movers actually type

Why can’t I bid by bedroom count?

Two 3-bedroom houses can be a first-floor short carry or a third-floor walk-up with an 80-foot carry. Bedroom count cannot see the extra trips. That is how a Saturday that looked like $900 becomes a loss.

How much extra should stairs add?

Stairs add trips, so the extra hours should scale with how much you are moving, not a flat surcharge. Start from minutes per flight per 200 cubic feet, then replace it with a time from your own crew.

    Common questions

    How do movers price a local job?

    From clock hours: load and unload time for the inventory, plus extra hours for stairs and a long carry that scale with cubic feet, plus packing and drive time. Hours times crew size times loaded wage, then truck, fuel, materials, overhead, and margin.
