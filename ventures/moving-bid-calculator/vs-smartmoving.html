This calculator vs. SmartMoving

Two different shapes: SmartMoving is all-in-one CRM, dispatch, estimating, and billing software for running a moving company. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this local move cost, given its inventory, stairs, and long carry, before you open a business-management system.

What each tool is for

SmartMoving is all-in-one software built specifically for moving companies, founded in 2018 and headquartered in Dallas, Texas. It combines CRM and lead management, online booking, dispatch and job scheduling, estimating, digital signatures, storage management, billing, reporting, and a customer portal into one subscription system, with integrations into QuickBooks and Mailchimp for accounting and marketing. SmartMoving says thousands of moving companies run their business on it, and it made the 2024 Inc. 5000 list of America's fastest-growing private companies. It also took a growth investment from Mainsail Partners, a growth-equity firm. SmartMoving's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — all-in-one moving-company business software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for SmartMoving appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what SmartMoving does. It does not manage leads, online booking, dispatch across a fleet, storage, or billing, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one local move's room-by-room inventory — cubic feet and clock hours per room or specialty item — plus stairs, a long carry, elevator wait, packing and drive time, crew wage, payroll burden, truck, fuel, materials, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the crew hours the job actually consumes, the price per cubic foot, the effective hourly rate per mover, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a bedroom-count quote on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. It prices one local move; it does not handle long-distance tariffs or storage-in-transit.
