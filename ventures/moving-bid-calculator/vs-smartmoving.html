This calculator vs. SmartMoving

Two different shapes: SmartMoving is CRM and operations software for running a moving company. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this local move cost, given its stairs, long carry, and drive time, before you open a company-wide system.

What each tool is for

SmartMoving is business-management software built specifically for moving companies. It was founded in 2018 by Tobe Thompson and is headquartered in Dallas, Texas. The platform covers lead management and sales, binding, non-binding, and binding-not-to-exceed estimates, crew dispatch and scheduling, an inventory workflow with customer- and crew-facing apps, storage management, digital signatures, and text and email follow-up, and it integrates with QuickBooks. It was the only software built for the moving industry named to the Inc. 5000 in 2024. It is sold as an ongoing account for the company, not a per-job tool. SmartMoving's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — moving-industry CRM and operations software, sold as a company account — are stated, and no dollar amount for SmartMoving appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what SmartMoving does. It does not manage leads, dispatch, storage, or crew scheduling across a company, and it stores no customer or job record anywhere but the mover's own browser. It takes one job's inventory — room or specialty items with cubic feet and clock hours — plus stairs, long carry, elevator wait, packing, and drive time, plus crew wage, payroll burden, and target margin, and turns that into the crew clock hours the job actually consumes, the price per cubic foot, the effective hourly rate per mover, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if a bedroom-count quote is already on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Should I use this calculator or SmartMoving?

They do different jobs, and a moving company of any size will likely end up touching both eventually.
