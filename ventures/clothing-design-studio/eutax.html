Have you crossed the EU 10,000 euro VAT line?

If you ship clothing from one EU country to shoppers in other EU countries, you may charge your own country's VAT on those sales until they pass 10,000 euros a year (before VAT, all other countries added together). After that, each shopper's own country's VAT applies, and you register for the One-Stop Shop (OSS) in your home country to file it in one quarterly return. Type your sales below.

The standard rates are typed from memory and were not checked against the European Commission's table when this page was built; several countries changed rates in 2025. Every rate can be overridden. Reduced and zero rates for children's clothing (Ireland zero-rates them) are not applied. Selling from outside the EU? There is no 10,000 euro line: see the note at the bottom.
