Sell it before you make it.

No cash for a first run? Take pre-orders, and place the factory order only if the minimum is reached; refund everyone if not. Type your numbers to see how many orders and visitors that takes, how likely your traffic is to get there, what you make if it funds and what you lose if it does not.

A forecast from numbers you type, not a promise. Orders are modelled as independent visitors each with the same small chance of ordering, which real traffic is not (a good post is lumpy). Pre-order rules (clear delivery dates, refund times, what you may take payment for) differ by country: check them before you start.
