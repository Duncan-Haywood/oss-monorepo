Is your product copy a green claim problem?

Paste the words from a product page, hangtag or ad. The checker lists phrases that EU consumer law now bans or wants proof for, from eco-friendly and carbon neutral to a badge you drew yourself, and offers wording that says the same true thing safely.

A list of phrases, not a lawyer. EU Directive 2024/825 applies from 27 September 2026, each country wrote its own version, and a regulator decides a case. A clean result means no listed phrase was found, not that the copy is clear. English only. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.
