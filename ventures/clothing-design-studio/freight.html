What will freight add to each garment?

Carriers bill the larger of what a carton weighs and the space it takes, so a carton of folded hoodies is billed on size. Give the folded piece, the carton and the rates from your forwarder's quote: this counts cartons, finds the billable weight and shows sea, air and express per garment, then whether the days air saves are worth the extra.

Rates are yours to type from a real quote; none here is a price list. Volume-weight divisors are the usual conventions (air 6000, express 5000) and your carrier's may differ. Not logistics or customs advice.
