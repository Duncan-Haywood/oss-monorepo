What a first order really costs once it lands.

Duty on a single online order (EU flat 3 euro duty, US, UK) · Worked example: 500 tees into the US, EU and UK

The factory's price is not your cost. Duty is charged on a customs value that differs by destination (US: the factory price only; EU and UK: factory price plus freight and insurance), and an extra tariff or a zero-duty origin changes it again. Type one shipment and see the landed cost per garment in the US, EU and UK side by side.

The duty rates here are typed by hand from memory for knitted tees and sweatshirts and were not checked against the USITC, EU or UK tariff when this page was built. Check your garment's code on the official site (links below) and type the rate in the override box. US extra tariffs change often: the default is 0, so type today's figure for your origin.
