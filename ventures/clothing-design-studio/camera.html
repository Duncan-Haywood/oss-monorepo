Clothing Design Studio · Size finder

Try it on from a photo or your camera

Stand square to the camera, arms down, a few metres back, and take a front photo (or use the live camera). Tap your left shoulder as it appears on the picture's left, then the right, or press Find my shoulders. The garment outline in your size is drawn to scale on you. Your picture stays in your browser.

"Find my shoulders" runs an open-source pose model (MediaPipe, about 6 MB, fetched once from cdn.jsdelivr.net and storage.googleapis.com when you click it) inside this page. Your picture is not sent to them or anyone. You can still tap the shoulders by hand.

Choose a photo to start.

Not a fit guarantee: a flat outline scaled from your shoulder span and height, with no drape. Tape measurements remain the better guide; this shows the garment's length and width against you.
