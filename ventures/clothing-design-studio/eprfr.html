What do you owe per piece sold in France?

Brands that put clothing on the French market pay an eco-contribution per piece to the producer-responsibility organisation Refashion, set by product line and target (adult or child, women or men). Online sellers shipping to French shoppers are in scope. Type your pieces below; see the fee on the detailed per-line rate and on the flat simplified rate.

The per-piece rates are a handful of lines seen in search extracts of Refashion's 2025/2026 guides on 2026-11-08 (2026 guide), not the full schedule, and the simplified rate is the 2025 figure. Overwrite every rate with the current official schedule before relying on a number. Penalties, eco-modulation bonuses and the fast-fashion law are not modelled.
