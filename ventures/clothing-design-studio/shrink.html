What size should you cut so the washed garment fits?

Cotton jersey and many knits shrink in the first wash, more along the length than the width. Mark a swatch, wash it the way a customer would, measure again, and this works out how much bigger to cut each piece, and how much extra cloth that buys.

Your readings are the input; no test method or limit is assumed. If your buyer or contract names a method and a tolerance, use theirs. Not QC or legal advice.

Wash-test readings

One swatch per line: marked distance before, then after, same unit (cm). Mark at least 3 swatches, 35 to 50 cm apart, away from the edge.

Finished (washed) measurements

One per line: name, length or width, size in cm.
