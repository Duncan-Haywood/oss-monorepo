What the border adds to one online order.

Selling to customers abroad means one small parcel at a time, and the rules for parcels differ from those for a factory shipment. Since 1 July 2026 the EU charges a flat 3 euro customs duty for each item category in a parcel worth under 150 euro (until July 2028, starting with sellers registered for IOSS). The US duty-free threshold is suspended for all countries, so every parcel pays duty. The UK threshold below is typed from memory.
