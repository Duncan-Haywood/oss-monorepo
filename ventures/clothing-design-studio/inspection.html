How many pieces should the inspector check?

Before you pay the balance, an inspector pulls some pieces from the cartons and the order ships if they find no more than a set number of defects. This works out the sample that makes that rule fair to you and to the factory, the chance a lot passes at each defect rate, and what a result really tells you. It uses exact sampling without replacement, so small orders are right too, and needs no standards table.

Your contract may name a published plan (ISO 2859-1 or ANSI Z1.4, AQL 2.5 for major defects is common). Use the numbers here to understand it; check the plan itself against the standard. Not QC or legal advice.
