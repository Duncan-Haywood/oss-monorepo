Does the sample match the spec?

A factory sews to a table of measurements with a tolerance on each. Pick the garment and cut, get the table for every size (the same one goes in the printable tech pack), then type what you measured on the sample, laid flat, to see which points pass and what to tell the factory.

Tolerances and the shoulder and neck figures are typical, not a standard or your buyer's table. Agree them with the factory in writing; replace the sleeve with theirs.
