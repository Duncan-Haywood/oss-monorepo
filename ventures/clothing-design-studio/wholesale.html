Selling to shops: what do you keep?

A boutique buys at a wholesale price and marks it up, usually about double. Type your retail price and landed cost to see the wholesale price, what is left after fees, returns and waiting to be paid, how that compares with selling online yourself, and the retail price that makes stockists worth it.

Rules of thumb, not laws: shops expect about half the tax-free retail price and a brand wants wholesale at 2x landed cost or more. Change every number to match your own terms.
