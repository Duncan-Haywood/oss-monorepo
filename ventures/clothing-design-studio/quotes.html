Questions

How do I compare quotes from different clothing manufacturers?

Cost the same garment on each quote with the same quantity, then compare landed cost per garment (fabric, cut and sew, decoration with setup spread over the run, trims, duty and freight), cash needed up front, minimum order and lead time. The lowest unit price is often not the cheapest factory once setup and minimum order are counted.

Why does the cheapest factory change with the order size?

Setup fees such as screens and embroidery digitising are paid once per run. A factory with a low unit price and a high setup loses on small runs and wins on large ones. The run-size table shows where it flips.
