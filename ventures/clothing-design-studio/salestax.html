Which US states must you collect sales tax in?

Since the 2018 Wayfair decision a state can require an online seller anywhere to collect its sales tax once the seller's sales into the state pass a threshold, usually 100,000 USD or 200 orders in a year. Type your sales and orders on your own store for each state you sell into. The page says where you are over, where your plan crosses, and what the tax on the later sales comes to at the state base rate.

The thresholds, base rates and clothing exemptions are typed from memory for all 46 states that tax sales, and DC, and were not checked against the states' sites when this page was built; states change them often. Every figure can be overridden; states with no sales tax (Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, Oregon) are listed apart. Local city and county taxes are not included. Check with the state, or your cart's tax service, before you register or stop collecting.
