How many in each colour and size?

Factories set a minimum per colour. 300 tees in four colours is four orders of 75, and one under the minimum can sink the quote. Type the total, the colour and size shares and the minimum: get a whole-piece table whose cells add up exactly, the colours that miss, and a CSV for the purchase order.

Arithmetic on typed shares, not a forecast of what sells. Sizes use the same shares inside every colour; the size totals can drift a piece or two from rounding.
