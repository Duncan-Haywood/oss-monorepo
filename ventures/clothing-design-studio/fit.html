How many of each size should a new clothing brand order?

Not an even split. Adult chest sizes follow a bell curve around the audience average, so the middle sizes sell most. The size run above splits a quantity from the audience's average chest and spread, and moves it toward bigger sizes when buyers want a looser look than the garment is cut for. Replace the typical chart with your factory's.

What is ease?

How much bigger the finished garment is than the body, around the chest. About 6 cm is slim, 12 regular, 18 relaxed, 28 oversized for a t-shirt; sweatshirts and hoodies add 4 to 8 cm. These are typical trade values, not a standard.

Is this a virtual try-on?

Part of one. The try-on panel draws the garment over a body built from your tape measurements, to scale in width, and compares the chest, waist and hem. It is geometry: no camera, no drape or stretch, so it shows where the room is, not how the fabric hangs.
