Is the factory's colour close enough to approve?

Before a run, a factory sends a lab dip (dyed cloth) or a printed swatch. Type your target colour and the colour measured on the swatch, one row per colour, and get the CIEDE2000 colour difference (ΔE) and a pass or fail against your limit, plus the words to send back: "2 darker, 5 yellower".

Use measured Lab from a spectrophotometer, under the same illuminant and observer for both (write lab:52.1,-3.2,18.4). Hex colours are screen colours, not measurements of cloth: a phone photo will not do. Limits are typical choices, not a standard; agree yours with the factory in writing.
