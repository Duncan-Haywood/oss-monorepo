What does 52□18-145 on sunglasses mean?

    
Lens width 52 mm, bridge 18 mm, temple (arm) length 145 mm. The total frame width is about two lens widths plus the bridge plus a few millimetres of rim and hinge each side.

    
What do I need to sell my own sunglasses in the EU?

    
In the EU and UK sunglasses are personal protective equipment, category I. Ask the factory for an EN ISO 12312-1 test report, the filter category to mark on the temple, and a declaration of conformity. In the US, sunglasses need the FDA impact-resistance (drop-ball) test. Check the current rules for your market; this is not legal advice.
