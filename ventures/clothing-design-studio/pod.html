Order a factory run, or print each order on demand?

A factory run is cheap per piece but needs a minimum order and leaves leftovers if you guess too high. Print-on-demand costs more per piece and risks nothing. Type your costs and how many you expect to sell: see which earns more, how big the run should be, and the demand at which the factory wins.

The numbers below are placeholders, not any supplier's prices. Demand is your own guess, and real demand comes in bursts; the result is an average over a Poisson spread.
