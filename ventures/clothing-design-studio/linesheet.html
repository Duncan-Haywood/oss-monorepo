Line sheet for shops

A boutique buyer wants one page: each style, its colours and sizes, the wholesale price, the minimums and an order form. Type your styles, one per line, and download a printable page and a CSV order form to send.

Wholesale price = tax-free retail price less the shop margin (about half is usual). Check every price and term before you send it.
