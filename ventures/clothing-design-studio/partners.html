Other brands on your shop? Know what each sale pays them.

Commission is taken on the price without VAT, card fees fall on whoever you choose, and a refund takes its commission back. Type a price to see the split, then paste a period's sales for a statement per partner brand. Nothing is sent anywhere and no money moves here.

Who is the seller of record, and so who owes VAT, depends on your contract and country. This page assumes your shop sells and pays the VAT: check with an accountant.
