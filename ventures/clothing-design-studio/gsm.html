Fabric quoted by the kilo, the metre or by gsm: what does it come to?

Knit mills quote by the kilo, factories and shops by the metre, and a sample says "180 gsm". Type the weight and width to see metres per kilo, a price in the other unit, and how many kilos a garment run needs, rounded up to the mill's minimum.

Arithmetic on numbers you type. Use the cut width (a tube of knit is double its flat width) and ask the mill whether its weight is before or after finishing.
