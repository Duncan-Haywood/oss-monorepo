Start early enough.

A first run is a chain of waits: sample rounds, the factory's making time, freight, customs. Pick the date you want stock to ship by and see the latest day you can start; or type the day you could start and see when it is on sale. Change any stage to your factory's own numbers.

Defaults are a rough shape for a cut-and-sew tee or hoodie, not a quote. The factory clock stops during closure windows; the Lunar New Year window is typical, not checked: ask your factory for its dates. Freight and customs times vary by route and season.
