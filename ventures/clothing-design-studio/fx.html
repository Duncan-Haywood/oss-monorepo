What if the exchange rate moves before you pay the factory?

Factories often quote in dollars or yuan and want a deposit up front and the balance before shipping. Your shelf prices are in euros, pounds or something else. Between the deposit and the balance the rate can move. This shows what that does to your cost and profit, how big a move wipes you out, and what a forward rate would lock in.

Moves are scenarios you pick, not a forecast. Forward rates and fees are what you type from a bank or payment provider's quote. Not financial advice.
