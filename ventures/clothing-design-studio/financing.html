What does it cost to borrow for a factory run?

A cash advance says "1.3 factor", a loan says "2% fee", a card says "APR", a supplier says "2/10 net 30". None of them are comparable as quoted. Type each offer and get the yearly rate that makes what you receive equal what you repay, the dollars it costs, and what it takes from each garment's profit.

Arithmetic on terms you type, not a quote or advice. Read the contract's own total; daily-sales repayment, late fees and personal guarantees are not modelled.
