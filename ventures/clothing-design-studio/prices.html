One margin target. The right shelf price in each market.

EU and UK shoppers see prices with VAT; US shoppers see them before sales tax. Type your landed cost per garment and the margin you want, and get the shelf price in each market, rounded to a price ending, with the margin that price really gives.

VAT rates are standard rates typed in by hand and not checked against the official table when this page was built: confirm them (link below) and edit any that are wrong. Children's clothing is zero-rated in the UK and Ireland only if it meets the age and size conditions (check with HMRC or Revenue); other reduced rates, thresholds, OSS registration and US state sales tax are not applied.
