Sunglasses made for 20 euros, sold for 65: what is left?

A designer planning a branded sunglasses line told us the rough numbers: about 20 euros to make, 60 to 70 to sell. That sounds like a 3x markup. After VAT, duty, card fees, shipping and returns, it is less than half of that, and what remains has to pay for every customer you win. Here is the arithmetic for one pair.

Every figure is an assumption typed for illustration, not a quote or a benchmark: the 20 euros is the designer's estimate, the freight, duty rate (2.9%, from memory and unchecked: look up your tariff code), return rate and shipping are placeholders. No real order. Not tax or financial advice.

The example

Factory price 20.00 including the case, freight 1.50 per pair, import duty 2.9% on 21.50 (0.62): landed 22.12. Sold in the EU at 21% VAT included, card fee 2.9% plus 0.30, 4.00 postage paid by the seller, and returns: 90% come back resaleable, the rest sell for 20% of landed cost, a return label of 5.00 and 1.50 handling.

At 65.00 and 12% returns the profit is 20.96 per order (39.0% of the net price). The 21% VAT takes 11.28 of the shelf price before anything else.

What this means

  • The profit is the advertising budget. Paying more than about 21 euros to win a customer loses money on that order. Paid social at 25 euros a sale loses 4.04 on every pair.
  • Returns matter less than VAT and price. Moving from 5% to 20% returns costs 5.98 per order at 65.00; moving the shelf price from 60 to 70 adds 7.03 at 12%.
  • Price for a target. To earn 10.00 per order at 12% returns the shelf price is 49.42, so the brand has room, if the customer is found without paying for them.
  • Not counted: samples, tooling for the logo, the EN ISO 12312-1 test report and the FDA impact test, photos, the shop. The start-up budget adds them.

Questions

Can sunglasses made for 20 euros be sold for 65 euros at a profit?

On these assumptions yes: about 21 euros per order at a 12% return rate, before any advertising. The profit is 39% of the net price, and it is the whole budget for finding the customer.

How much can I pay to win a customer for sunglasses?

No more than the profit per order, about 21 euros at 65 euros and 12% returns. Paying 25 euros per sale from ads loses about 4 euros on every order, before the cost of samples and certification.

Do sunglasses need testing to be sold in the EU and the US?

The studio's sunglasses page lists the EN ISO 12312-1 report and declaration of conformity for the EU and UK, and the FDA impact test for the US, as things to ask the factory for. This page does not price them and gives no legal advice.
