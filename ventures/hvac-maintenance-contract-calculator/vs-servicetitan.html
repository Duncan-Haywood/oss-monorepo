This calculator vs. ServiceTitan

Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is an account-based platform for running field service operations, including scheduling, billing and renewing a maintenance agreement once it exists. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should a new preventive-maintenance agreement cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan, headquartered in Glendale, California. It serves HVAC, plumbing, electrical and other trades with scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, reporting and a service agreement / membership module that documents a maintenance plan, builds out its recurring visits for the contract term, and automates billing and renewal reminders. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole business, not a one-off calculator. ServiceTitan's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity, category and publicly described service-agreement features are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that service-agreement software describe managing, scheduling and billing a plan once its terms and price already exist; none describe a step that works out what a new agreement should cost from technician time, callback risk, parts reserve and target margin before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not schedule technicians, send renewal reminders, or invoice a customer. It takes one maintenance plan's preventive visits, technician hours per visit, loaded labor rate, travel, parts reserve, callback risk, annual overhead and target margin, and turns that into a break-even monthly floor, a target-margin monthly and annual price, technician hours reserved, and effective revenue per technician hour — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or ServiceTitan?

They do different jobs, and most HVAC contractors who run maintenance agreements at any scale end up touching both. ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan and headquartered in Glendale, California, used by HVAC, plumbing, electrical and other trades to run scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing and reporting across a business. Its service agreement and membership software documents, schedules and bills a maintenance plan on an ongoing basis once the plan's terms and price already exist — automating renewal reminders and recurring visit scheduling. It is an account-based platform you sign up for and use continuously. This calculator does one narrower thing: it takes a plan's preventive visits, technician hours, loaded labor rate, callback reserve, parts, travel, overhead and target margin, and turns that into a break-even floor, a target-margin price and an effective revenue per technician hour, before you have quoted anything. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A contractor who already has a priced agreement and needs to schedule, bill and renew it across many customers is better served by ServiceTitan or a comparable field service platform; a contractor who needs an instant, private answer to what a new agreement should cost before it is offered is in the right place here.
