Common questions

    
What is the National Living Wage in 2026/27?

    
From 1 April 2026 the rate for workers aged 21 and over is £12.71 an hour, £10.85 for ages 18 to 20, and £8.00 for under-18s and apprentices. Check GOV.UK for the current rates.

    
Do unpaid hours count towards minimum wage?

    
Yes. Working time includes travel between jobs, mandatory training, waiting at work and some on-call time, so an employee paid for 35 hours who works 40 is measured over 40.

    
Can an employer deduct uniform or tool costs?

    
Deductions for the employer's benefit, such as uniform, tools or cash shortages, reduce the pay that counts, and can push an employee below the minimum even when the contract rate is above it.

    
What happens if I have underpaid?

    
You must repay arrears, using the higher of the rate then and now, and HMRC can add a penalty of 200% of arrears up to £20,000 per worker, halved if paid within 14 days, and can publicly name the employer.
