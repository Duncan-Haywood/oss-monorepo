This calculator vs. BinWise

Two different shapes: BinWise links to your point-of-sale system and tracks pour cost automatically across your whole bar, under an ongoing subscription account. This page is a free, private worksheet for one recipe — its pour cost against your target, and what a small over-pour is quietly costing you across a year — with nothing to set up.

What each tool is for

BinWise is bar and beverage inventory software, sold under an ongoing subscription account to bars, restaurants, hotels, and restaurant groups running a full beverage program. It links to your point-of-sale system, updates inventory depletion in real time as drinks sell, and reports pour cost percentage and variance per product across your whole bar without anyone re-typing a recipe. Setting it up means an account, POS integration, and stocking your inventory into the system — not a one-off calculation. BinWise's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names, feature tiers, or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not link to a POS, track inventory depletion, or report variance across a bar's whole beverage program. It takes one recipe — a primary spirit plus an optional modifier and mixer, each with its own bottle cost and pour size — and works out the pour cost percentage against your own target, the menu price that exact recipe would need to hit it, and the weekly and annual cost of a stated over-pour on the primary spirit across your own drink volume. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and nothing you type is uploaded.
