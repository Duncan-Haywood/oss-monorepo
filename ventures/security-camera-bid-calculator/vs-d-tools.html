This calculator vs. D-Tools

Two different shapes: D-Tools System Integrator is business-management software for running an AV, security, or low-voltage integration firm's whole design-to-delivery pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one camera install cost, and do the storage, PoE, and cable-run numbers on the quote actually hold up — before you send it.

What each tool is for

D-Tools makes System Integrator (SI), founded in 1998 by Adam Stone and headquartered in Concord, California. SI is business- management software purpose-built for AV, security, and low-voltage integrators: it connects system design (through an AutoCAD or Visio integration for engineered drawings), the bill of materials, procurement, project management, and financial visibility across a whole firm, with an on-premises edition and, since 2019, a cloud edition aimed at smaller projects. It is an account-based platform: a firm signs up and runs its design-to-delivery pipeline through it on an ongoing basis, not a one-off bid worksheet. D-Tools' own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — business-management and system-design software for the AV, security, and low-voltage integration trade — are stated, and no dollar amount for D-Tools appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what D-Tools does. It draws no CAD layout, tracks no procurement, and stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one camera job's count and construction type (accessible attic, drop ceiling, masonry, or a lift job), resolution, codec, frame rate and the motion-only share of the day, the retention days you are quoting and the drives you actually plan to buy, the PoE switch's rated budget and each camera's draw, run length, and your loaded installer wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the storage the quoted retention needs versus the days the fitted drives will actually hold, the PoE headroom against an 80% planning ceiling, any run past the 328 ft Ethernet limit, the installed price, the price per camera, the walk-away floor, and a printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Should I use this calculator or D-Tools?
