Questions people ask

How much should a u-pick farm charge for admission?

Work backward from the day, not from the farm down the road: crew, rent, insurance and setup are paid whether or not people come. This planner solves for the admission that reaches break-even, and your profit goal, at your own attendance and weather.

How many visitors does a pumpkin patch or u-pick farm need to break even?

It depends on what each visitor leaves after crop cost, food cost and fees, and on how many days the weather takes. Enter your numbers and the planner solves for the visitors per open day you need.

Do u-pick farms make money from the crop or from the extras?

Often from the extras: food, rides and the shop usually keep more per visitor than the picking does. The planner shows what you keep from each so you know which one carries the season.

Is this business or financial advice?

No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in and the assumptions listed on the page. Check the output against your own costs before you change a price.

Not decided

Weekday versus weekend attendance, group and school-trip pricing, season passes, crop failure beyond the shrink you enter, permits and liability rules for agritourism in your state, sales tax, and what neighboring farms charge. The model assumes every visitor pays the same admission, picks the same pounds and spends the same on extras, and that crew and season costs do not change with attendance.
