    Common questions

    How much should I charge per window?

    A flat per-window price hides travel and glass type. Time each pane type, add the drive and setup, load the wage, then divide the visit price by pane count. Ten French panes both sides are a different job from ten ground-floor plates.

    Why do small window cleaning jobs lose money?

    Travel and setup do not shrink when the pane count does. A 25-minute drive plus hose-up on an 8-pane ranch is mostly windshield time. A minimum job charge exists to cover that block, not to pad profit.

    How do I price interior and exterior together?

    Count sides. One side is exterior-only or interior-only. Two sides is in and out. Minutes per pane stay per side, so a French pane at 4 minutes both sides is 8 minutes of glass before you have moved to the next one.

    How much more should I charge for second-story, lift, or hard-water windows?

    The calculator’s own starting figures show the jump: ground-floor exterior on a water-fed pole starts at 1.5 minutes per pane. A second-story ladder job starts at 3 minutes — double. Third-story, bosun-chair, or lift work starts at 6 minutes, four times the ground-floor figure, because rigging and safety add time before the glass gets touched. Hard-water or restoration glass and skylights both start at 8 minutes per pane — over five times the base rate — since mineral deposits need a chemical pass or a second wipe regardless of how reachable the glass is. Pick the preset closest to the access and glass condition in front of you, then replace it with a time from your own stopwatch; these are starting points for the group, not a published rate for the trade.
