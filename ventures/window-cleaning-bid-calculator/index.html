FOR WINDOW CLEANING OPERATORS

Price the stop, not the pane count.

A flat “per window” quote cannot see that ten French panes both sides are a different job from ten ground-floor plates, or that a 25-minute drive does not shrink when the house is small. Time each glass type, add travel and setup, load the wage, and see the visit price, the price per pane, and the number below which the stop loses money.

One row per glass type. Minutes are per pane on one side — pick a type to start from a common figure, then replace it with a time from your own stops. Sides: 1 is in-only or out-only; 2 is both.

Your costs

Travel and setup is the block that sinks small jobs: drive, park, hose-up, and pack-out happen once whether the house has 8 panes or 80. The minimum job charge exists to cover that block. Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, and insurance — underestimating it is the other common miss.
