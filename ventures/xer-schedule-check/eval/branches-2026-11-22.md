# Probabilistic branches in the risk run, checked on six public P6 files (2026-11-22)

`node eval/branches-check.mjs DIR --json eval/branches-2026-11-22.json`
(the six public XERs from `bash eval/corpus-fetch.sh DIR`; seed 1, 120 runs per
risk run, 4 picks per file; 4.8 minutes, 4 of them on the CCA hospital file).

No public P6 job publishes a branch register, so the branches are planted on
each file's own driving path: seeded picks of not-started activities with work
left. With zero-width ranges every run's finish is one of a few dates, each
reached a second way (cpm.mjs scheduled directly with the dropped activity's
remaining duration set to 0, or with 5 days added for the event check).

The checks (the script's header has the detail):

1. **One branch at 50%, the remainder dropping it**: every run finishes on the
   forecast or on the finish with the activity at 0 (24 of 24); P10 is the
   latter and P90 the former wherever the two differ (11 of 11); the drawn
   share is within 4 standard errors of 50% (24 of 24).
2. **Two branches at 50% each**: every run drops exactly one, so every finish
   is one of the two "dropped" finishes (12 of 12), and no remainder is
   reported (12 of 12).
3. **Draws untouched**: on the default 90/100/125 range, a branch group naming
   only a finished activity or a milestone gives the same percentiles, date
   for date, as the run with no branches (6 of 6).
4. **A sure 5-day risk event on branch work**: runs that keep the activity
   finish as it with 5 days added, runs that drop it as it at 0; the event
   adds nothing to work that does not happen (24 of 24).

## What the planted branches showed

Dropping a driving activity moved the finish in only 11 of 24 picks. On the
other 13 (3 on CCA, 3 on Expressway, 1 on example_xer_file, 3 on the hotel
file, 3 on the terminal file) the finish stayed put: another path, or a constraint, drives
the same finish, so the activity is on a driving path but not the only one.
For a branch register this matters: a "this work may not happen" branch on an
activity the finish does not hang on alone is worth nothing, and the branch
table shows it as a shift of 0.0 rather than letting the single CPM date imply
otherwise.

## Not checked

- A real job's branch register (none is public), and the dates Primavera
  Risk Analysis or Safran Risk give for the same branches on the same file.
- Branches on activities with SS or FF links only: the dropped activity keeps
  its logic at zero duration, which is how those tools describe it, but the
  dates were checked here only against this tool's own CPM engine.
