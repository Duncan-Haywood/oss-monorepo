# Time impact analysis on six public P6 files, 2026-11-20

**Question.** When a delay event's fragnet is inserted into a P6 update
(`tia.mjs`), does the recomputed finish move by what it should? No public
job publishes its fragnets, so fragnets were planted (seed 1, 20 per check per
file) and each result checked against an answer reached another way.

**Data.** The six public XER files of `eval/cpm-vs-p6-2026-11-19.md` (fetched
from their repositories for the run, not committed), each file's main project.

**Method.** `node eval/tia-check.mjs <dir> --plants 20 --json eval/tia-check-2026-11-20.json`

1. *Null fragnet.* A zero-day milestone between two open activities already
   linked finish-to-start with no lag, all on the project calendar: the
   finish must not move and no activity may lose float.
2. *Float.* An open activity on the project calendar made d working days
   longer (d 1-40): the finish must move by max(0, d - relative float), the
   relative float being the activity's float against the activity the finish
   rests on (`floatpaths.mjs`: a backward pass from it alone, no constraints,
   no other finish). Left out: milestones (check 2b), activities with an FF
   or SF predecessor (added days move their start earlier) and activities
   whose successors are all tied to their start (SS/SF: nothing after them
   waits for their finish). The tool warns about both.
3. *Two routes.* A new d-day activity inserted between A and B, where B is
   A's only successor in the network by a finish-to-start link with no lag:
   the finish, every milestone and B's start must equal those from making A
   d days longer.
4. *Impacted XER.* Each fragnet of check 3 written with `xerText()` (the
   download), read back and scheduled again: the same finish.

(sample.xer has 15 pairs that qualify for checks 3 and 4.)

**The 8 misses of check 2.** Each has a reason the oracle leaves out, not a
fault in the run:

- 7 have activities on the driving path after the event on a calendar other
  than the project's (`why.pathOtherCalendar` 1-3): the delay crosses a
  non-work day, holiday or shorter week on that calendar, so the finish
  moves a day or two more (Expressway: 7 for 6, 28 for 27, 21 for 19) or less
  (hotel: 17.5 for 18.5, 11 for 12) than the project-calendar arithmetic. On
  CCA, T10P106101C0 (19 days, 15.8 of relative float) moved nothing where 3.3
  was expected: its path also holds a constraint, which the relative float
  leaves out.
- 1 (A10510 on the P6 24.12 example file, 31 days) moved the finish by 0
  where 21.9 was expected: downstream activity A10590 has an expected finish
  date and the project uses expected end dates, so P6 shortens A10590 to keep
  the date and the delay is absorbed. `timeImpact` names it in `absorbed`
  and in a warning, and `expectedFinish: false` (CLI
  `--no-expected-finish`, page switch) runs both schedules without them.

**What the run changed in the tool.** Three behaviours were found by the
planted checks and are now handled:

- Days added to a milestone were silently dropped (a milestone has no
  duration in P6 or the engine). They are now refused with a warning (2b).
- Expected finish dates absorbing the delay are named (above).
- Activities whose successors are all start-to-start, and activities with an
  FF or SF predecessor, are warned about: added days on them do not reach
  their successors (terminal file: BA-P4-20, BA-P1-80, BA-P1-90 are SS-only,
  39 days moved the finish by 0, which is right).
- An activity ID ending in "-1" (A10390-1) was read as A10390 with a lag of
  -1 day and closed a loop; an ID the file or fragnet holds is now taken
  whole.

**Time.** About 1.5 s per fragnet on the 16,000-activity CCA file (124 s for 83 runs and 20 read-backs) (two full
schedules, plus one forward pass per named event), under 0.1 s on the small
files.

**Not checked.** No real TIA exhibit with its P6 dates was available to
compare with; the impacted XER is the way to check one in P6.
