Common questions

Does this read my video account?

No. It uses only the minute volumes you type and rate cards you can edit. It cannot see your library, resolutions, viewers or invoice. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate public list prices as best known in 2025 for on-demand 720p-1080p video, shown with an as-of line, and every number is editable. Bunny and the DIY AWS pipeline bill by GB, so they are converted at roughly 2.5 Mbps. Platforms change packaging often, so check each pricing page before deciding.

What is not included?

Live streaming, DRM, captions and AI features, 4K ladders, minimum commitments, enterprise discounts, other regions and each platform's fair-use terms. Per-GB platforms cost more per minute at high bitrates and less at low ones, so check the conversion against your own encodes.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local files and makes no network call.
