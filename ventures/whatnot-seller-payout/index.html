      Common questions

      How much does Whatnot take per sale?

      On a typical US lot Whatnot takes a commission on the item's final sale price (often 8%) plus payment processing of 2.9% + $0.30 on the checkout total — the hammer plus buyer-paid shipping and tax. Whatnot's own $50 example is $4.00 commission + $1.75 processing = $5.75, leaving $44.25. This page does the arithmetic from the rates you type; it is not Whatnot's live schedule.

      Is processing charged on shipping?

      Yes. Processing is a percentage of what the buyer paid at checkout, not just the hammer. Buyer-paid postage and tax sit in that total. Offering free shipping drops that slice and puts the label on leftover instead — the cheaper path depends on the hammer and the label, which is why the page compares both.

      Does Whatnot take 8% on a $2,400 graded card?

      Not on the published US TCG / comics / sports / toys row. That row commissions 8% only up to $1,500 of the hammer; the portion above is 0%. A $2,400 lot is $120 of commission, not $192. The cap is labelled limited-time on Whatnot's help page — confirm it on the lot before you treat it as permanent.
