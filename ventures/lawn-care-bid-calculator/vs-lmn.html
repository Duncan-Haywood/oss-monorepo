This calculator vs. LMN by Granum

Two different shapes: LMN by Granum is an account-based platform for running a landscaping company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one recurring stop — whether the price actually clears your production hours, travel, and margin, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

LMN by Granum is business management software built specifically for landscaping and lawn-care companies — not a generic construction or field-service tool. Long known as Landscape Management Network (LMN), it was brought together with SingleOps and Greenius under the shared Granum brand in 2025, while continuing under its own product name. It bundles estimating and budgeting, job costing, crew scheduling and time tracking, and payroll integration, used by a company's office staff and crews together across every property on the book, on an ongoing account. LMN's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not hold a customer list, schedule a crew across properties, or run payroll. It takes one recurring stop — each service split out with its own quantity and production rate (turf mowing, edging and blowing, bed weeding, hedge trim, leaf cleanup), visits per week, service weeks per year, round-trip travel time, loaded labor, fuel, dump, overhead, and the margin you intend to keep — and turns that into the price per visit, the in-season monthly, an honest annual at the growing-season week count rather than monthly × 12, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the stop loses money, plus a printable customer proposal with scope, deposit split, and a signature line. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
