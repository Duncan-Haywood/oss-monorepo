FOR INDEPENDENT LAWN CARE OPERATORS

Bid the stop, not the lot size.

Price a recurring lawn-care stop the way a crew actually runs it: turf, beds, and edging each at their own production rate, then windshield time, fuel, dump, and the margin you intend to keep. You get the price per visit, the in-season monthly, an annual that counts growing-season weeks rather than inventing winter, and the number below which the stop loses money.

Background: how to price lawn mowing without a per-acre guess.

The property

One row per service on the visit. Production rate is how much one person covers per hour in that row’s unit. Pick a service to start from a common figure, then replace it with a rate timed from your own crew.

Route and season

Round-trip travel is loaded labor. Service weeks are the growing season you actually bill — 32 is a common temperate-US figure, not a recommendation. Annual is visit price × these weeks, not monthly × 12.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, and paid time off. Equipment share is this stop’s piece of the mower payment, trailer, and shop — not the whole fleet.
