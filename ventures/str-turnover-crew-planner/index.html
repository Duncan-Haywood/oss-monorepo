    Common questions

    Does it upload my turnover list?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    How is the best day found?

    For one crew it searches over which turnovers have been done and which was last, keeping every way to get there that is not both later and less profitable than another. It then tries every way to split the queue across your crews. That finds the exact best plan for the numbers you give, and the tests compare it with trying every assignment and every order.

    Why would it turn down a turnover I would normally take?

    Because a crew can only be in one place. A clean that pays well but drives you across the map, or sits in a window that collides with two better cleans, can cost more than the two it displaces. The tool says what the turnover would need to pay to earn its place.

    Where do the costs come from?

    From your own books: fuel and wear per mile, and what an hour of one crew costs you in wages and payroll cost. They are your estimates, so treat the plan as a decision aid, not a forecast.

    Who is it for?

    Vacation-rental cleaning companies and property managers with one to three crews choosing which of the day's turnovers to take and who does them.
