    Common questions

    How much should I charge to install a standby generator?

    There is no flat installed price. A typical 22 kW natural-gas hang beside the house is a different job from a far-yard propane set with a 400 A automatic transfer switch. This calculator prices the feeder, the ATS, the pad, the fuel line, and the setback from your own wage and material inputs.

    Is this a load calculation or a code ruling?

    No. Outputs are arithmetic on the numbers you enter. The recommended-kW hint is a starting point, not a NEC 220 or manufacturer load calculation you can stamp or submit.

    How much more does propane cost than natural gas for a standby install?

    On a 15-foot fuel line at this calculator's default wage, burden, overhead, and margin, switching from natural gas to propane adds about 2.1 labor hours and $475 in materials (the LP regulator kit, the tank hookup, and pricier pipe) -- roughly $974 more on the bid ($6,106.00 to $7,080.12). That is on top of the tank itself, which this calculator does not price.
