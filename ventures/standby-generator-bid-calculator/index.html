FOR ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS AND GENERATOR DEALERS

Price the run, the switch, and the fuel — not a flat $8,000 hang.

Homeowners search “how much to install a standby generator” and come back with one number. A 22 kW natural-gas hang beside the house is not a far-yard propane set with a 400 A automatic transfer switch. Enter the machine, the service, the pad, and the setback.
