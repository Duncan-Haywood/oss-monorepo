Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is an account-based field service management platform for running an electrical or generator-dealer company's whole operation. What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan, headquartered in Glendale, California. It serves HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other trades with scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, reporting, and job costing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. ServiceTitan's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software serving the electrical trade among others, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceTitan appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not schedule technicians, dispatch crews, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one job's generator size, load coverage (whole-house or essential circuits), fuel (natural gas or propane), service amps, transfer switch type and amps, pad type, location/setback, and gas meter, with feeder length, fuel-line length, and trench footage as their own line items, plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, permit fee, generator hardware and other materials, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the installed price, dollars per kW, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
