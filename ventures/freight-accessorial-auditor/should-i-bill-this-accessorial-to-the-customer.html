Should I bill this accessorial to the customer?

Shippers search this after the carrier invoices detention, a liftgate, or a reweigh the quote never showed. AP can eat it, add a line on the customer invoice, or pay first and ask for a credit. Those are different cash.

On the default prepaid collect — $180.00 already collected, a $300.00 detention against $75.00 supported — the gap is $225.00. Eating the line keeps −$120.00. Billing the customer $300.00 keeps $180.00. A 1.5-hour review at $42/hr ($63.00 labor) that recovers the gap keeps $42.00. The same review with no credit keeps −$183.00.

The $225.00 gap buys 5.36 hours of review at that rate. A $50 gap against ten hours at $125/hr ($1,250.00) finishes $1,200.00 behind eating. Type the confirmation amount, not a portal default.

When does reviewing a freight extra cost more than eating it?

When the gap is crumbs and the review is expensive. An $80 line against $30 supported is a $50 gap. Ten hours at $125/hr is $1,250.00 of labor. Eating that line on $80 already collected keeps $0.00. A credit still finishes $1,200.00 behind eating.
