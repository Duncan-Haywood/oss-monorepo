# UK Child Benefit Charge Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-child-benefit-charge-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter the higher earner's income, pension and Gift Aid payments and the children you claim for, and see the High Income Child Benefit Charge, the pension payment that would remove it, and whether to pay it or opt out of payments.

After using this, you know what the £60,000 line costs your household and what to do about it, which used to mean reading HMRC's taper rules and grossing up pension payments by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-child-benefit-charge-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
