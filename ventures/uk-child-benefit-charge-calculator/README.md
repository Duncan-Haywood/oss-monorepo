# UK Child Benefit Charge Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter the higher earner's income, pension and Gift Aid and the children you claim Child Benefit for, and see the High Income Child Benefit Charge, what you keep, the pension or Gift Aid contribution that removes it, and your Self Assessment dates.

After using this, you know what going over £60,000 costs your household and what a pension contribution saves, which used to mean working it out from three GOV.UK pages.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-child-benefit-charge-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rules were written from memory and must be verified against the source each result names. 2026/27 weekly rates are not built in: type them. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
