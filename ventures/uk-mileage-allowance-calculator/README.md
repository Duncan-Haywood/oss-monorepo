# UK Mileage Allowance Calculator (2026/27)

Free, private browser tool: enter your business miles and what your employer pays per mile. It prices the HMRC approved amount (45p/25p for cars, 24p motorcycles, 20p bicycles, 5p passengers), the tax relief on any shortfall, and for the self-employed compares simplified mileage with apportioned actual vehicle costs.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-mileage-allowance-calculator/

After using this, an employee knows whether their employer under-pays and how much tax relief to claim (P87 or Self Assessment), which used to mean guessing from GOV.UK pages.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-mileage-allowance-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
