Bounce house rental calculator vs. Booqable

Different jobs: one prices a single booking's real cost in your browser right now; the other runs a rental company's whole calendar, catalog and invoicing as a paid subscription.

Price a real booking →

Booqable is rental-business management software, described on its own site (booqable.com) as covering inventory and availability tracking, an online booking page, quotes, invoices and rental contracts, and a website builder — with a dedicated page for inflatable and bounce house rental companies (booqable.com/industries/inflatable-rental-software). It is the kind of system a rental company runs its whole booking calendar and customer list on once it has a catalog of units, repeat customers, and payments to process continuously — set up, configured with the company's own inventory, and paid for every month it runs.

That is a different job from the one this page does. This calculator has no account, no inventory list, no invoice, and nothing to configure ahead of time. It exists for the narrower question that comes up every time a specific booking is being priced, often before it is ever entered into a booking system at all: given this fleet, this event's mileage and labor, whether a generator or a wet-unit attendant is needed, and insurance sized to what's actually being deployed, what does this booking actually cost to run — and what is the walk-away price?

It needs no account and no sign-in: it is a static page, the arithmetic runs in your browser, and once it has loaded it keeps working with the connection off.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or a platform like Booqable?

They serve different needs and an operator running Booqable for bookings and invoicing still has to decide what to charge for a specific event before it goes on the calendar. Booqable is a paid rental-business platform — inventory tracking, an online booking page, quotes, invoices and a website builder — built to run a rental company's ongoing operations, described on its own site including a dedicated page for inflatable and bounce house rental businesses. Getting a booking onto it means an account, a configured catalog, and an active subscription. This calculator needs none of that: type your fleet, your labor and delivery costs, whether a generator or a wet-unit attendant is needed, and your insurance rate, and it shows the walk-away price, the price per unit, and a margin check against a price you're already considering — in your browser, with no account, before the booking is ever entered anywhere.
