This calculator vs. InflatableOffice

Two different shapes: InflatableOffice is an account-based platform for running a party rental company's whole operation, including booking, routing and billing a job once it is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this booking cost, given the truck's two round trips, the crew, the generator, the attendant and the insurance, before you send the quote.

What each tool is for

InflatableOffice is a rental management platform used by inflatable and party rental businesses, from single-truck operators to multi-location companies, to run an online booking storefront, a customer and lead database, delivery routing and driver sheets, contracts and liability waivers, payment processing, weather tracking, and equipment or employee scheduling, tied to one account used on an ongoing paid basis across every booking the company takes. InflatableOffice's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that booking and route software describe taking, scheduling and dispatching a job once its price already exists; none describe working out what a bounce house or inflatable booking should cost from delivery mileage, setup labor, a generator, a wet-unit attendant and fleet-value insurance before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what InflatableOffice does. It does not store a customer record, run an online booking storefront, or route a driver's day. It takes one booking's fleet and replacement value, event days, setup and teardown minutes, crew size and labor rate, delivery miles and fees, a generator when the site has no outlet, wet-unit attendant hours, and an insurance rate against the deployed fleet's replacement value, and turns that into the walk-away price, the quoted price at your own target margin, a full cost breakdown, and — if you already have a price on the table — a check against that walk-away floor. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or InflatableOffice?

They do different jobs, and a party rental operator of any size will likely end up touching both. InflatableOffice is a rental management platform built for inflatable and party rental businesses, used to run an online booking storefront, a customer and lead database, delivery routing and driver sheets, contracts and liability waivers, payment processing, weather tracking, and equipment or employee scheduling across a whole operation. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from the fleet deployed and its replacement value, event days, setup and teardown minutes, crew size and labor rate, delivery miles and fees, a generator, wet-unit attendant hours, and an insurance rate against fleet value, it turns that into the walk-away price, the quoted price at a target margin, a full cost breakdown, and a margin check against a price already on the table, before any of it touches a business-management system. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. An operator already taking online bookings, routing drivers, and billing customers across a season is better served by InflatableOffice or a comparable rental management platform; an operator who wants an instant, private answer to what one booking should cost from its truck trips, labor, generator, attendant and insurance before opening a business system is in the right place here.
