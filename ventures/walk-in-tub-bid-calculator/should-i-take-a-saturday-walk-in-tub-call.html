Should I take a Saturday walk-in tub call?

Families call after a fall: “Can you start Saturday? Same $3,499 — she is not climbing that tub again.” That sounds like the same alcove. It is not the same crew day. After-hours is time-and-a-half. You cannot leave the alcove open overnight in the only bath they still use, so it is a second evening trip. Working around the only tub on a weekday is the same wage on a slower set.

Saturday is not the weekday bid

A weekday soaker still carries the wage you already timed. After-hours raises that wage to time-and-a-half and adds a starting second trip. On a typical cheap special — $42 wage, 30% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, eight miles, acrylic soaker, alcove swap, inward door — the weekday bid is $3,757.32. After-hours is $4,407.98. The walk-away jumps from $3,005.86 to $3,526.39. Taking $3,499 to “just do Saturday” covers the weekday and loses $27.39 after hours.

This is not a customer-supplied tub or a tiled shower

Customer-supplied tub is their Amazon box and a labor-only number — see should I install a customer-supplied walk-in tub. A tiled walk-in shower is a wet-area system, not a tub with a door. A toilet is a commode swap. After-hours is when you can stand in the only bath. This prices a walk-in tub.

Common questions

Should I install a walk-in tub after hours or on Saturday?

Only after you price time-and-a-half plus a second evening trip. You cannot leave the alcove open overnight in the only bath they still use. A $3,499 Saturday special on a cheap acrylic soaker often covers a weekday and sits under the after-hours walk-away. A $5,800 Saturday number on a whirlpool often loses even on a weekday.

Does working around the only tub change the bid?

Yes. Same wage, slower set and punch-list. On a typical acrylic soaker alcove swap the only-bath-still-in-use bid is $3,928.03 versus $3,757.32 when they leave.

Can I take the weekday $3,499 number on Saturday?

Usually not on the job they actually want. On a cheap soaker the after-hours walk-away is $3,526.39. $3,499 covers a weekday and loses $27.39 after hours. On a typical aging-in-place whirlpool, $5,800 still loses money — even on the weekday. Walk-away after hours is $7,652.95.
