FOR BATH REMODELERS AND ACCESSIBILITY CONTRACTORS

Bid the alcove, the unit, and the extras.

Price a walk-in tub the way the trade actually loses money: an acrylic soaker into an existing alcove with an inward door versus an extra-wide whirlpool with a drain relocate, a tile surround, a cast-iron demo, and a sistered floor — not the published $3,499 installed special. You get the job price, what it works out to per tub, and the number below which the install loses money.
