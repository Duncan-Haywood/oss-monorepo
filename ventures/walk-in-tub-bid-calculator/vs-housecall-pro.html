This calculator vs. Housecall Pro

Two different shapes: Housecall Pro is account-based field-service software for running a home-service business across clients, scheduling, and crews. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this walk-in tub install cost, given its alcove, unit, and access, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Housecall Pro is a field-service management platform marketed to HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other home-service companies. It is used to run CRM and customer records, scheduling and dispatch, online booking, invoicing and payments, and a mobile app for technicians in the field, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Housecall Pro's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — home-service field-service management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Housecall Pro appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Housecall Pro does. It does not manage clients, schedule crews, send invoices, or collect payments, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes alcove versus freestanding unit, door style, drain and valve rough-in, tile or surround repair, a customer-supplied unit, access, and the permit allowance, plus loaded wage, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the walk-away price below which the job loses money and a copyable customer-facing bid summary — plus a printable customer proposal with scope lines and a signature block. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
