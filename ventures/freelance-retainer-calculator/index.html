Why this is different from a basic hourly-rate calculator

A $75 rate means little if only 65% of your month is billable, your tools and taxes cost $1,800, and a vague retainer absorbs unplanned revisions. This calculator makes each assumption visible so you can change it before making a promise.

Common question: how many hours should a retainer include?

Start with a scope you can deliver inside your billable capacity, then leave the buffer intact. A retainer is a commitment of access and work, not permission for unlimited requests. Put included hours, response time, and out-of-scope pricing in your agreement.
