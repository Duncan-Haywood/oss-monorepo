# UK EMI Share Option Tax Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-emi-share-option-tax-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for UK startup employees and founders holding EMI options: enter the option terms and a sale price to see income tax on any discount at exercise, Capital Gains Tax with or without Business Asset Disposal Relief (14% for 2025/26, 18% for 2026/27), the cash you keep, the cost of missing the two-year mark, outcomes at other sale prices, and a comparison with an unapproved option.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-emi-share-option-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rates were written from memory and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
