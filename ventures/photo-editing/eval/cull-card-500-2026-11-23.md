# The cull page on a 520-file card (2026-11-23)

`site/cull.html` was checked on 65 files the same morning
(`eval/cull-page-previews-2026-11-23.md`). This run loaded 520, to see what
holds up at the size of a real shoot, and fixed the three things that did not.

## The card

The 65 public raws of github.com/drewnoakes/metadata-extractor-images (sparse
clone as in the earlier note), hard-linked eight times under distinct names
(`c1_` to `c8_`): 520 files, 8.7 GB, 61 of 65 with a preview. It is a stand-in,
not a real card: the copies share capture times, so they form eight-frame
"bursts", and a real card has one camera, not 50. A real card of 500+ raws is
still wanted.

## What was slow

1. **Every file was read whole.** `loadOne` called `file.arrayBuffer()` on each
   raw (7-38 MB here, 25-60 MB on current bodies) to find a preview whose
   structure is in the first few hundred kB. 520 files meant 8.7 GB off the
   card, plus each preview read a second time to decode it.
2. **Every grid refresh copied every thumbnail.** The grid was rebuilt every 12
   files, drawing each thumbnail into a new canvas: about 43 rebuilds of up to
   520 copies.
3. **The full-size JPEG was decoded for a 360 px tile** even where the raw
   carries a ~1,620 px one too.

## The fixes

- `readRawFile(file)` in `site/rawpreview.mjs`: the parser notes every byte
  range it looks at (IFDs, tag values, CR3 boxes, the Olympus maker note, each
  candidate JPEG's marker segments). The reader fetches a 256 kB head into a
  zero-filled buffer the size of the file, runs `readRaw`, fetches the 64 kB
  blocks it asked for and did not have, and repeats until it asks for nothing
  new (1-4 passes here). When that ends with no pointer preview of 640 px or
  more, the case where `readRaw` byte-scans, it reads the file whole, so the
  answer is always `readRaw`'s. Files under 4 MB are read whole.
- The grid moves each tile's thumbnail canvas into the new tile instead of
  copying it, and redraws at most twice a second while loading.
- The grid decodes the smallest embedded preview with a side of 1,024 px or
  more (the detail score works at 1,024); the large view still opens the
  largest. 8 of the 61 have one: the five CR3s (1620 x 1080 next to 6000 x
  4000), the Sony A7 IV and the Nikon D800E and D810. Pixels decoded for the
  grid: 629 to 418 MP per 61 files.
- Four files are read at once instead of two (capped by
  `navigator.hardwareConcurrency`).

## Results

Same parse, checked file by file (`node eval/cull-sparse-read.mjs mei`,
`eval/cull-sparse-read-2026-11-23.tsv`): **65 of 65 identical** to `readRaw` on
the whole file (format, every metadata field, preview offset, length, size and
source). 53 read sparsely, at **2.1% of their bytes** (256 kB for most TIFF
raws; 1.7-1.8 MB for CR3s, whose `moov` and preview boxes are larger); 12 read
whole: the four 2005-2008 Olympus compacts and the LG Nexus 5X DNG (no pointer
preview, so the byte scan runs), and seven files under 4 MB.

In the page (headless Chromium 1280 x 900, 4 cores, files in the OS page cache;
`eval/cull-card-run.mjs`):

| page | 520 files read in | read from disk | no page errors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before (main at 58d6531bf) | 45.6 s | 8.7 GB (+ previews again) | yes |
| sparse reader + canvas move | 37.7 s | 1.3 GB | yes |
| + grid-size previews | 32.1 s | 1.2 GB | yes |
| + four at once | 25.8 s | 1.2 GB | yes |

The 1.2 GB is mostly previews (decoding a JPEG needs all of it) and the five
whole reads per copy (537 MB of it). Every tile's thumbnail has the old page's
size and orientation on the 65 files, except two of 360 x 240 that became
360 x 241 and 360 x 238 (the camera's mid-size preview has a slightly different
aspect). 50 arrow-key moves then took 1.1 s, against 1.6 s before.

**What a card reader would make of it (arithmetic, not measured):** at the
~90 MB/s a UHS-I reader sustains, 8.7 GB takes about 97 s and 1.2 GB about
13 s, so on a real card the bytes, not the decoding, set the time, and the
sparse reader is most of the gain. These timings were from the page cache;
the container has no card reader.

## Not done
