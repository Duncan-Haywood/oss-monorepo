# Mid-Term Rental Calculator

Free, private browser tool for landlords and short-term-rental hosts: compare furnishing a unit for 30-day-plus stays (travel nurses, relocations, insurance displacement) with a plain long-term lease and with nightly renting. It shows the monthly rate, the empty-day limit and the furnishing payback that decide it, the nightly occupancy that would match, and whether your rate fits a guest's fixed housing budget.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/midterm-rental-calculator/

After using this, you know whether a furniture outlay of thousands of dollars is worth it and how many empty days between stays you can afford, which used to take a napkin that left out utilities and vacancy.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/midterm-rental-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
