Let your happiest customers sell for you, and pay them on your own books

Referral Keeper is a partner program for Stripe businesses that doesn't take a cut of your revenue or hold your customer list. Give each partner a link. It remembers who sent each customer, works out commission on what they really paid (after tax, refunds and chargebacks), and hands you a payout file when a balance is ready. Plan it below, then run the open-source server with your own webhook secret. No account with us, no monthly fee.

Run it yourself

git clone https://github.com/Duncan-Haywood/oss-monorepo && cd oss-monorepo/ventures/referral-keeper
STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_... ADMIN_KEY=$(openssl rand -hex 16) LINK_SECRET=$(openssl rand -hex 16) \
PUBLIC_URL=https://partners.example.com LANDING_URL=https://example.com/pricing \
COMPANY_NAME=Acme node server.mjs

# add a partner (or set OPEN_SIGNUP=true and share /join)
curl -s -X POST $PUBLIC_URL/v1/affiliates -H "authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_KEY" \
  -d '{"name":"Ada","email":"ada@example.com"}'

# on your site, once:  <script src="https://partners.example.com/rk.js" defer></script>

Send checkout.session.completed, customer.created, invoice.paid, charge.refunded and charge.dispute.created to /webhook/stripe. One-click Render deploy and Docker are in the README.

Questions

Does it pay my affiliates?

No. It never moves money. It keeps the ledger and gives you a payout file once balances are past the refund hold and your minimum. You pay by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer and record it, so a bug in this software cannot send money.

Does it need write access to my Stripe account?

No. It works from webhooks alone. An optional restricted key with Invoices read access is used only to look up payment ids on newer API versions. It never creates or changes anything in Stripe.

How is a sale attributed?

A partner link sends visitors to your site with ?ref=code. A one-line script remembers it in a first-party cookie and tags your Stripe Payment Links, buy buttons and pricing tables with client_reference_id. The first partner to bring a customer keeps them.

What happens on a refund or chargeback?

Commission is recalculated from the cumulative refund. A full refund or a dispute removes it. If the partner was already paid, a negative adjustment is netted against their next payout.
