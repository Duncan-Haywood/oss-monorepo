Should I tile a walk-in shower after hours?

Owners call with a weekday number: “Can you start Saturday? Same $45 a square foot — we only have one shower.” That sounds like the same wet area. It is not the same crew day. After-hours is time-and-a-half. You cannot leave mud overnight in the only bath they still use, so it is a second evening trip. Working around a shower on a weekday is the same wage on a slower set.

Saturday is not the weekday bid

An empty weekday curbless walk-in still carries the wage you already timed. After-hours raises that wage to time-and-a-half and adds a starting second trip. On a typical 5x5 curbless walk-in — $38 wage, 32% burden, 16% overhead, 28% target margin, linear drain, valve relocate, two niches, masonry bench, frameless glass — the empty-weekday bid is $12,821.25. After-hours is $15,363.84. The walk-away jumps from $9,231.30 to $11,061.97. Taking $5,175 ($45 / SF) to “just do Saturday” still loses money — even on the weekday.

Common questions

Should I tile a walk-in shower after hours or on Saturday?

Only after you price time-and-a-half plus a second evening trip. You cannot leave mud overnight in the only bath they still use. A $45 / SF Saturday special on a 5x5 curbless walk-in often sits under that walk-away number even on an empty weekday.

Does working around the only shower change the bid?

Yes. Same wage, slower set and punch-list. On a typical 5x3 alcove tub-to-shower the only-bath-still-in-use bid is $7,824.60 versus $7,418.51 when they leave.

Can I take the weekday $5,500 number on Saturday?

Usually not on the job they actually want. On a 5x3 alcove the after-hours walk-away is $6,403.94. $5,500 covers an empty weekday and loses $903.94 after hours. On a 5x5 curbless walk-in, $5,175 ($45 / SF) still loses money — even on the weekday.
