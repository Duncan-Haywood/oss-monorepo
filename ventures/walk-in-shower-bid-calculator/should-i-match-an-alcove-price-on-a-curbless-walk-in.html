Common questions

Should I match an alcove price on a curbless walk-in shower?

Only after you price both bathrooms from the same wage, burden, and overhead. A 5x3 alcove tub-to-shower with the existing valve and a framed door is not the same job as a 5x5 curbless walk-in with a linear drain, a valve relocate, a masonry bench, and frameless glass. The tile counts are close. Matching the alcove’s price per square foot of tile on the walk-in is how that job becomes a loss.

Why does a similar tile count still cost more on a walk-in?

Linear drains, curbless slope, a valve relocate, niches, a masonry bench, and frameless glass do not scale with tile square footage. On the sample rates a 5x3 alcove prices around $7,419 ($78 per tile sq ft). Matching that $78 / SF on 115 sq ft of walk-in tile is about $8,980 — under the walk-in’s walk-away. Steam is a third job, not an alcove plus 20%.

Is a steam shower just a markup on the alcove?

No. A sealed enclosure and a generator do not live in a published per-square-foot number. On the sample rates a 4x6 steam shower prices above the curbless walk-in even when wall tile is only modestly higher. Treat steam as its own bathroom, not a blended add-on.
