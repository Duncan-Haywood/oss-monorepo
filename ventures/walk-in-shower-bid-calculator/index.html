    Where the cost goes

      Transparent arithmetic from your own inputs. Starting hours and material rates are common ballpark figures, not an industry standard, a waterproofing warranty, or a building-code determination — replace them with your own takeoff.

      Common questions

      Why does a curbless shower cost so much more than a standard framed one?

      Curbless floors need the subfloor recessed or built up to create a barrier-free slope to the drain, which is a different framing and waterproofing detail than a standard curbed pan — that extra work, plus a linear drain if selected, adds hours a curbed shower never needs. Toggle curbless off to see how much of your bid that detail actually accounts for.

      The customer already bought the tile — does that mean labor is cheap?

      No. Zeroing the tile invoice is not a free set: unpacking, sorting, and setting owner-supplied tile still takes the same hours, and a short order or dye-lot mismatch becomes your second trip and a retail fill-in the customer expects you to cover, not a cost you caused. Price the labor the same way regardless of who bought the material.

      Does relocating the valve or adding a bench get priced automatically from the square footage?

      No, they're separate toggles because they don't scale with tile area — a valve relocate is plumbing work behind the wall, and a masonry bench is its own framed and waterproofed structure, both add fixed hours on top of whatever the wall and floor tile square footage already costs.

      Does this verify my waterproofing method meets code?

      No. It prices the scope you describe from your own hour and material rates; it is not an industry standard, a waterproofing warranty, or a building-code determination. Confirm your waterproofing assembly and any inspection requirements with your local building department.
