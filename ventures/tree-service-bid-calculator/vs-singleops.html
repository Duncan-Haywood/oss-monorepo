This calculator vs. SingleOps

Two different shapes: SingleOps is an account-based platform for running a tree-care or landscape company's whole operation, including scheduling and invoicing a job once it is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this removal stop cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

SingleOps is a business management platform built for tree care, landscaping and other green-industry service companies. It is used to run CRM and lead tracking, estimating and proposal generation, scheduling and crew routing, invoicing and payments, and QuickBooks integration across a whole operation, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. SingleOps's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that estimating and scheduling software describe building, tracking and billing a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a new removal stop should cost from tree-by-tree wood volume, access, haul and crew cost before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what SingleOps does. It does not track a lead, schedule a crew across a season, or invoice a customer. It takes one stop's trees — each one's diameter, height, species, access, targets and haul choice — plus stump grinding, travel, loaded crew cost, equipment, a crane day if one tree needs it, overhead and target margin, and turns that into the recommended stop price, the walk-away floor, the price per inch of DBH, the effective crew hourly rate, and which tree on the stop is actually carrying the job — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Should I use this calculator or SingleOps?
