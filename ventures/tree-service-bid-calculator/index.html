FOR TREE SERVICE OWNERS

Bid the tree, the access, and the wood.

A 24-inch backyard oak is four times the wood of a 12-inch street pine — not twice. Price a removal stop from diameter, height, species, access, targets, stump grinding, and haul, then add the drive, the loaded crew, and the margin you intend to keep. You get the bid, the walk-away number, and which tree is actually carrying the job.

How tree services actually bid a removal

The stop

One row per tree. Hours grow with the square of DBH because that is how wood volume grows. Access and targets change the hours more than species does.

Your costs

Crew hourly is what you pay the whole crew for an hour on the clock — climber, ground, and chipper operator together. Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation (expensive in this trade), and insurance. Travel is billed once for the stop, not once per tree. A crane day is charged only if any tree is marked crane-required.
