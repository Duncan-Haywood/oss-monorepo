Privacy

Site privacy notice

These pages have no forms, set no cookies and load no analytics or third-party scripts, so they store nothing about you.

The app has its own policy

When you sign up, Job Shop Quote stores your account, your shop's rates, RFQs, files and quotes. What it keeps, why and for how long is in its privacy policy, alongside the terms of service.

Hosting
