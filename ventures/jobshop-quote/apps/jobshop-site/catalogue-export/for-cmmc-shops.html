For defense and CMMC-conscious shops

Quoting help, without putting controlled drawings at risk

Many machine shops quote for defense primes and their suppliers. If some of your RFQs carry Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or export-controlled technical data, here is exactly where Job Shop Quote stands.

During early access: don't send CUI or ITAR/EAR drawings

Job Shop Quote early access is not set up to hold CUI or export-controlled technical data. Contractors that store covered defense information with a cloud service are expected to use one that meets security requirements equivalent to the FedRAMP Moderate baseline (DFARS 252.204-7012), and export-controlled drawings have their own rules. We don't claim either, so please use early access only for commercial RFQs and drawings with no CUI or export-control markings.

If you're not sure whether a drawing is controlled, treat it as controlled and leave it out. Signing up asks for your name, email and shop, never for drawings.

Why we're building for these shops anyway

CMMC requirements started appearing in Department of Defense contracts on 10 November 2025 (MeriTalk); check the current rollout schedule for what applies to your contracts. The shops that quote defense work are often the same small CNC and fabrication shops that Job Shop Quote is for, and they need the paperwork side handled too.

Coming: a CMMC workspace for small shops

Alongside Job Shop Quote we're building a separate workspace for CMMC Level 1 and Level 2 self-assessments: the System Security Plan, the plan of action and milestones, and the SPRS score across the NIST SP 800-171 controls. It is not available yet, it will not certify anyone (only an authorized assessor can), and its site isn't live yet.
