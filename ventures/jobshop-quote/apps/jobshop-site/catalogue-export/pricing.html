How these prices were set

Each plan is priced from what it costs us to run: the AI reading each drawing and checking itself, hosting and storing drawings, payment fees, a refund reserve and support time, for a shop that uses its whole allowance. The price is that cost plus a margin of roughly one and a half to two and a half times, and RFQ allowances are set so no plan sells below that.

What counts as an RFQ

One customer request, with any number of parts, up to 10 drawing pages. Bigger packages count once per 10 pages. Re-reading, editing and re-pricing the same RFQ doesn't count again.
