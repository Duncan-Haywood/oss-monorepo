FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Job Shop Quote today?

Yes. Sign up free at https://jobshop-quote.marisstella.app/sign-up. The Early access plan covers up to 40 RFQs a month for 3 people, with no card. It prices the parts you enter, and what it measures from STEP and DXF files, from your rate card, builds the quote PDF, emails it when your estimator sends it, and keeps the won/lost log. Reading RFQ emails and PDF drawings with AI is built but not switched on in the hosted app yet; until it is, you enter those values yourself.

Does the AI decide my prices?

No. Prices come only from the rates, minimums and markups you set. The AI reads the RFQ and drawings and proposes setups and cycle times with its reasoning shown; your estimator accepts or corrects them, and the dollar math is plain arithmetic on your rate table.

How does it price material: by weight, by the bar or by the foot?

Whichever way you buy it. Each material on your rate table is priced by weight (your price per lb or per cwt and density, plus scrap), by the stock piece (a sheet or a cut blank), or by length for bar, tube and extrusion: your price per foot or per metre, a cut allowance for kerf and facing, and, if you buy in fixed bars, the bar length and remnant, so a line is charged the whole bars its parts need. You can import these from your supplier's price list as a spreadsheet: before you save, each row shows its price change and the open quotes priced at the old price, and the supplier and the list's date are kept in each price's history. A price per cwt or per metre is kept exactly as your supplier quotes it, so $412.50/cwt prices at $4.125/lb, not a rounded $4.13.

What does it read?

The STEP and DXF measurements work today; reading email text and PDF drawings with AI is not switched on in the hosted app yet. RFQ email text and PDF drawings, including scans, plus STEP and DXF files. A STEP solid gives the blank size and, with your machine's measured removal rate, a roughing time; with your seconds per hole and, if you enter one, your drill feed for each hole's depth, a drilling time that counts a counterbored hole once, flags holes deeper than 3× their diameter and leaves bores over 1 in to you; tick which hole sizes are threaded and, with your seconds per tapped hole, it adds a tapping time. A DXF flat pattern gives a laser, waterjet or plasma cutting time from its cut length and pierces at your cut speed (one per machine, or your own speed for each material and thickness from your cut charts), and a press-brake time from its bend lines; on a material sold by the sheet, with your sheet size and nest gap, it counts how many flat blanks fit on one sheet (a rectangular nest, so a nesting program may fit more, never fewer). IGES, STL and native SolidWorks files are not measured yet.

What happens when a drawing is unclear?

The value is flagged for your estimator with the drawing page it came from. It is never filled in with a guess.

Will it send quotes to my customers?

No. Your estimator reviews and approves every quote and sends it themselves: by email from the app, as a PDF, or copied into your own email or ERP.

Will you use my drawings to train AI models?

No. Your drawings, rates and quotes are used only to quote for your shop. They are never used to train AI models and never shared with another shop.

Can I send CUI or export-controlled (ITAR/EAR) drawings?

Not during early access: it isn't set up for controlled data. Use it only for commercial RFQs. The page for CMMC-conscious shops explains why and what's coming.

Does it connect to my ERP or quoting system?

Not yet in the hosted app: download the quote PDF or copy it across. A QuickBooks Online connection, which turns a won quote into an invoice, is built and switches on once Intuit approves it. Tell us which system you use at hello@marisstella.app; that decides what gets connected next.

What does it cost?

The Early access plan is free, with no card. Paid plans start at $199 a month, priced from what it costs to run; they are not on sale yet. Nobody is moved onto a paid plan without choosing it.
