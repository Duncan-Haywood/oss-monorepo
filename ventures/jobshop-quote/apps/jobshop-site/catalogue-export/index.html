The quoting pile is the bottleneck nobody hired for

In a job shop, quoting usually lands on the owner or one estimator. Every RFQ means opening the email, reading each drawing, working out stock, setups and cycle times, looking up material and outside processing, and applying your rates. That work happens between running the floor and answering the phone, and while RFQs wait, the customers who sent them wait too.

Job Shop Quote does the arithmetic and the paperwork, so your estimator's time goes on the judgment calls: the tricky features, the quantity breaks, and whether you want the job.

What it does, and what stays yours

Job Shop Quote does the legwork

  • Reads RFQ emails and PDF drawings: parts, quantities, material, finish, tolerances and notes (AI reading: not switched on yet)
  • Checks what it read a second time against the drawing and flags anything it can't confirm (AI reading: not switched on yet)
  • Measures STEP, IGES, STL and DXF files: blank size, holes, cut length, pierces and bends, for roughing, drilling, cutting and bending times you can correct
  • Applies your rates, minimums and markups with plain arithmetic: same inputs, same price
  • Lists the sent quotes due for a follow-up each morning and keeps a log of every call, so no quote goes quiet
  • Dates every material price, warns when one is past your limit, and lists the open quotes priced before your supplier's price moved
  • Puts your won jobs on your machines by due date, shows which ones run past their date, and gives each new quote a lead time from your real backlog
  • Imports your supplier's price sheet showing each price change and the open quotes it touches before you save, and keeps the supplier and date in each price's history

Your shop keeps control

  • Your rate table sets every price; corrections your estimator makes become shop rules
  • Your estimator reviews every line and decides what gets quoted
  • Quotes are never sent automatically
  • Your drawings and rates are never used to train AI models

Where it stands

Job Shop Quote is open: any shop can sign up and use it on the free Early access plan. It is new. There are no customers yet and no results to quote, so you won't find testimonials or time-saved figures here. The first shops on it tell us where it gets things wrong, and that decides what gets built next.

Working today: the rate card (imported from your spreadsheet or entered by hand), parts and routings, STEP and DXF measurements, quantity breaks, quote PDFs and quote email, customer links to accept or decline, follow-up and expiry reminders, job travelers, and the won/lost log.

Not switched on yet: reading RFQ emails and drawings with AI, RFQs forwarded by email, the paid plans, the QuickBooks Online connection. Each is built; the hosted app turns it on when it is ready to run safely.
