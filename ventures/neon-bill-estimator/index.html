Common questions

Does this read my Neon account or invoice?

No. It reads only the usage numbers you type or paste (copy them from the Billing and Usage pages of the Neon console) and multiplies them by list prices you can edit. It cannot see your plan, region, projects, contract or real invoice. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate public usage-based list prices from Neon's pricing page as best known in 2025, shown with an as-of date, and every one is editable, including the included allowances and the monthly minimum. Neon changes prices and packaging, so check neon.com/pricing before relying on a number.

How is the saving for each lever calculated?

Each lever re-runs the estimate with one change applied under an assumption shown beside it (for example, scale-to-zero that removes 35% of compute hours) and reports the difference. The assumptions are editable. Levers overlap, so the savings are not additive.

Is my usage data uploaded anywhere?

No. The browser page calculates in your own browser and never sends anything. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
