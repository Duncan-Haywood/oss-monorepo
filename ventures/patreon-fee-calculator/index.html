FOR PATREON CREATORS

The pledge total isn't your payout. Take-home is.

Patreon takes a platform fee that depends on which plan your page is on (Standard is a flat 10%; a page published on or before August 4, 2025 may still carry a legacy Lite/Pro/Premium rate of 5/8/12%), then payment processing on top — and processing gets sharply worse, not just smaller, on any single pledge of $3 or less. This free calculator finds your real monthly take-home and shows what switching plans would change.

Common questions

Why do I keep less from a $1 pledge than a $10 pledge, proportionally?

Because Patreon charges a worse payment-processing rate on any charge of $3 or less: 5% + $0.10 instead of the standard 2.9% + $0.30. On a $1 pledge that micropayment rate alone is 15% of the pledge before the platform fee is even applied, versus about 6% on a $10 pledge. See the worked breakdown for the exact numbers.

What's the difference between Standard and the legacy plans (Lite, Pro, Premium)?

Standard is a single flat 10% platform fee that applies to every Patreon page created after August 4, 2025. A page published on or before that date keeps its old plan instead: Lite at 5%, Pro at 8%, or Premium at 12%. Standard replaced the old Pro/Premium tiers with one number, so a legacy Lite creator pays half the platform fee a new creator pays today.

Can I switch from my legacy plan to Standard, or back?

You can move to Standard, but not back. Unpublishing and republishing a legacy page forces it onto Standard (10%) and that cannot be undone from the creator's side — there is no menu option to return to a legacy rate once it's lost. Read your own current plan off your Patreon payout settings rather than assuming; the plan table above shows what that one-way move would cost at your own patron count.

Does this estimate payout timing, currency conversion, or my actual bank deposit?

No. This prices one month's pledges against the platform fee and processing fee only. It does not model when Patreon actually deposits funds, currency-conversion spread for a non-USD patron, refunds, or failed/declined charges — fold any of those into your own records separately.

Does anything about my numbers get uploaded?

No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server and there is no account. Your inputs are saved locally on this device so you don't retype them, and a shareable link can carry them to another device. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
