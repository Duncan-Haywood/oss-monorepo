    Common questions

    What does this deck estimate include?

    The price adds up each surface’s framing and decking by material and height, railing by the lineal foot, stairs by risers and width, estimated footings, demo disposal, a trip fee, a permit line, overhead, and your target margin — all built from the rates you type in, not a fixed price list.

    Why does height above grade change the price so much?

    Once a surface is around 30 inches off grade a guard is typically expected, and past about 6 feet the framing, posts, and access time increase again. The calculator applies extra framing and post-set hours at those two thresholds automatically, which is exactly what a flat per-square-foot rate cannot see.

    Does this check building codes or guardrail requirements?

    No. The height thresholds used here are typical residential starting points, not an IRC or local code table, and footing counts are a span estimate from surface size and height, not an engineered layout. Permit cost is a single dollar figure you enter yourself.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No account and no network requests — the calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your inputs can be kept in this browser’s local storage so the page remembers them, and a share link encodes only the quote fields in the page’s URL hash rather than sending anything to a server.

    What’s the difference between the job price and the walk-away price?

    The job price already includes your target margin. The walk-away price is the break-even total cost underneath it — labor, materials, footings, disposal, trip fee, permit, and overhead — the number below which the job stops being profitable at all.

    Can I price a job with stairs but no railing, or railing but no stairs?

    Yes. Railing lineal feet, stair runs, and demo square footage are each optional and priced independently, so a low deck with stairs and no railing, or an elevated deck with railing and no stairs, only adds the costs that actually apply to it.

    How is this different from a paid app like QuoteIQ?

    QuoteIQ is a paid subscription app for quoting and tracking every job an ongoing deck business runs. This calculator connects to nothing: you price one job in your browser, with no account to make, cancel, or pay for. See this calculator vs. QuoteIQ for which job fits which situation.
