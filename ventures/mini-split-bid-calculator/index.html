FOR MINI-SPLIT INSTALLERS

Bid the run and the circuit, not the head.

A flat per-head quote treats a first-floor 12k wall-mount with 15 ft of line-set as the same job as a second-story cassette with a 50 ft run, a condensate pump, and a new 240 V circuit. Price the heads, the line-set extras, the height, and the electrical extras separately — then see the price, the dollars per head, and the number below which the job loses money.

The system

Start from a common residential layout, then replace head count and BTU with the equipment you actually quoted.

The run and the service

Head type and story multiply indoor labor. Extra line-set hours also climb with height. Electrical extras are a fixed adder — they do not get cheaper on a single-zone job.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Matching a cheaper per-head quote after leaving it out is how a sold job becomes a loss.
