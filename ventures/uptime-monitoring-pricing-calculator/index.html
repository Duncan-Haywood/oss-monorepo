Common questions

Does this check my sites?

No. It uses only the monitor count, interval and status-page need you type, and plan tables you can edit. It never contacts your sites or the vendors. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate public list prices as best known in 2025 on monthly billing, shown with an as-of line, and every plan is editable. Vendors change plans often, so check each pricing page before deciding.

What is not included?

SMS and phone-call alert credits, extra seats, multi-location or transaction (browser) checks, annual-billing discounts, taxes, and each vendor's fair-use terms. Alerting and seats often cost more than the monitors themselves, so use this to compare the monitor lines, not to forecast the whole invoice.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local files and makes no network call.
