# EU ESPR Readiness Checker

Free, private browser tool for brands, importers and retailers selling consumer goods in the EU: pick your product, role and size. It shows whether the ban on destroying unsold apparel and footwear (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation, (EU) 2024/1781) binds you, drafts the annual unsold-goods disclosure table from your own numbers, and lists the Digital Product Passport data to start collecting.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/eu-espr-readiness-checker/

After using this, you know whether you may still destroy unsold stock, hold a ready-to-publish disclosure table (downloadable as CSV) and have a passport data to-do list, which used to take reading the Regulation and the Commission's working plan.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-espr-readiness-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
