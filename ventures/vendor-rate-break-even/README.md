# Vendor Rate Break-Even Calculator

Choosing a supplier discount, subcontractor rate, or equipment lease? Compare the real monthly cost at your expected job volume and see when the switch pays back. Everything runs in your browser; no account or API key is needed.

## Use it

Open `index.html`, enter current costs, revenue per job, and up to three alternatives, then compare cost per job, profit per job, margin, and break-even volume. Drafts are saved only in your browser and restored the next time you open the page. Use "Copy share link" to hand the same comparison to a partner, accountant, or the vendor themselves — the link carries only the numbers in the form, nothing is uploaded.

## Privacy and usage

Everything runs in your browser. Your data is never uploaded, and we use no accounts, tracking cookies, or analytics vendor. We count only anonymous local feature events such as a comparison or export, never people, IPs, inputs, or content. Use the opt-out checkbox in the page to turn counting off.

## Opportunity assessment

The target user is an owner-operator of a cleaning, landscaping, maintenance, delivery, or trade business who receives vendor pricing with a mix of monthly minimums and per-job charges. The value is a defensible, repeatable decision before accepting a rate card: the operator can see the cost at their actual volume rather than trusting a headline discount. Existing spreadsheets and generic break-even calculators rarely model both a current baseline and multiple vendor scenarios with fixed-cost payback. Search terms include “supplier discount break even calculator”, “subcontractor rate comparison”, and “equipment lease cost per job”. A future paid model could add saved team templates, importable rate cards, and optional customer-owned AI extraction, but the free arithmetic remains complete. It costs $0 per user, has no sensitive data requirement, and produces observations rather than compliance, tax, legal, or financial determinations.
