This calculator vs. My Music Staff

Two different jobs: My Music Staff is a paid subscription that runs a studio's scheduling, billing, and attendance. This page is a free, private calculator for one narrower question — what should the lesson price be, once cancellations, prep, travel, and monthly costs are counted — with nothing to sign up for.

What each tool is for

My Music Staff is a well-known paid subscription platform for independent music teachers and studios, built to run the operational side of teaching: online lesson scheduling, automated billing and payment collection, attendance and makeup-lesson tracking, and parent or student communication, described on its own site. It is the kind of tool a teacher reaches for once they already have a lesson price and need the calendar, invoices, and reminders built around a growing roster of students. My Music Staff's own pricing and plan terms are not repeated here, since this session cannot verify today's figures against its current terms; only its well-documented scope as a studio-management platform is described.

This calculator does none of what a studio-management platform does. It has no scheduling, no billing, no student roster, and no account. It takes the annual pay target, teaching weeks, cancellation rate, prep and travel minutes, and monthly costs a teacher already has in mind, and returns the lesson price needed to hit that target and the real effective hourly rate once the invisible time is counted — instantly, in the browser, with nothing uploaded and nothing to configure first.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or a studio-management platform like My Music Staff?

They answer different questions. My Music Staff is a paid subscription platform built to run an independent music studio's day-to-day operations: online scheduling, automated billing and payment collection, attendance tracking, and parent or student communication, described on its own site. It is the kind of tool a teacher reaches for once they already have a lesson price and need the calendar, invoices, and reminders built around it. This calculator does none of that. It has no scheduling, no billing, no student records, and no account. It answers the narrower question that comes first: from your annual pay target, real teaching weeks, cancellation rate, prep and travel time, and monthly costs, what should the lesson price actually be.
