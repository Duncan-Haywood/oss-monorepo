Questions federal employees ask

How is a FERS pension calculated?

Multiplier times high-3 times years of service. The multiplier is 1.0%, or 1.1% if you retire at 62 or later with at least 20 years. High-3 is your highest average basic pay over any three consecutive years. Unused sick leave is converted to months of service and added to the years used in the formula, but it does not help you qualify.

When can I retire?

Immediately at your minimum retirement age (55 to 57 depending on birth year) with 30 years, at 60 with 20 years, or at 62 with 5 years. At your minimum retirement age with 10 to 29 years you can retire at once too (called MRA+10), but the annuity is cut 5% for every year you are under 62.

What is the Special Retirement Supplement?

A bridge payment that stands in for the Social Security you earned in federal service, paid until 62. It goes to people retiring with 30 years at their minimum retirement age or with 20 years at 60. It is not paid with MRA+10, and it is reduced if you earn above the Social Security earnings limit.

Why is there a jump at 62?

Two rules meet there: the 1.1% multiplier for people with 20 years, and the start of cost-of-living adjustments. The table shows the effect in dollars so you can weigh a year of work against it.
