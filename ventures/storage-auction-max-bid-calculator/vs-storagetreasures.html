This calculator vs. StorageTreasures

Two different jobs: StorageTreasures is where facilities list lien-sale units and run the auction itself. This page is a free, private worksheet for the question that comes before you bid anywhere — what does the real cost stack say your bid should be.

What each tool is for

StorageTreasures is an online marketplace where self-storage facilities across the United States list units that have gone to lien sale and run public auctions for the contents, including online and live-online formats a remote bidder can join without visiting the facility. Bidding is done through a registered account, and, as on other lien-auction platforms, the winning bid is not the final price — a buyer's premium is charged on top of the hammer price. StorageTreasures' own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific fee percentages, deposit terms, or plan details, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure or percentage for it appears on this page. What StorageTreasures does not do is tell a bidder what to bid: it lists the unit, runs the clock, and takes the winning number.

This calculator does none of what StorageTreasures does. It does not list units, run an auction, or take a bid. It takes the numbers a bidder already has standing in front of a unit — an estimated retail value, a realistic resale recovery rate, blended resale channel fees, the buyer's premium, and the full cleanout cost stack (dump loads, transport, labor, and holding cost while reselling) — and turns them into the hammer bid that leaves a target profit margin, the break-even bid past which every extra dollar is a guaranteed loss, and the gap against the flat percent-of-value rule most auction forums repeat. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
