# UK Mortgage Overpayment Calculator

Free, private browser tool for UK homeowners: enter the mortgage balance, rate and term, then a monthly overpayment and/or a lump sum to see the interest saved, the years cut off, whether overpaying beats saving the same money, whether you stay inside the usual 10% free allowance, and the overpayment needed to be mortgage-free by a target date.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-mortgage-overpayment-calculator/

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-mortgage-overpayment-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not financial advice. Estimate for a repayment mortgage at one rate. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
