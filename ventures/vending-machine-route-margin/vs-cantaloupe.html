This calculator vs. Cantaloupe Seed

Cantaloupe's Seed products are a full vending management platform built around its own cashless card-reader hardware: real-time telemetry from every machine, remote price changes, inventory and depletion alerts, restock predictions, and route-scheduling tools across a whole fleet. Per Cantaloupe's own published pricing page, base subscription plans start around $18.95/month, with operators typically budgeting roughly $8-12 per machine per month in telemetry and software fees on top of the hardware itself. Seed Cashless+ and Seed Pro are paid software tiers quoted per fleet on top of that. This calculator charges nothing, needs no hardware, and reads no machine directly -- it is a static page you type numbers into.

That makes Cantaloupe's Seed the right tool for running cashless payments and telemetry across a fleet of machines. This calculator answers a narrower question, for an operator who already has sales and cost figures for a location (from a DEX report, a cashless dashboard, or a manual count): is this specific location actually profitable, and does the whole route add up to my income goal?

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Cantaloupe Seed?

They solve different problems at different scales. Cantaloupe's Seed products (Seed Cashless+ and Seed Pro) are a vending management platform: cashless card readers with built-in telemetry, remote price changes, inventory and restock-prediction alerts, and route-scheduling tools, sold as an ongoing software subscription on top of hardware you buy or lease. This calculator has no hardware, no telemetry, and no account at all -- it is a free one-page arithmetic check for whether a single location's numbers, that you type in yourself, actually add up to a profit once commission, card fees, shrink, drive time, and the machine's own cost are counted.

How much does Cantaloupe's software cost?

Per Cantaloupe's own published pricing page for Cantaloupe One, base subscription plans start around $18.95/month, and operators should plan for roughly $8-12 per machine per month in telemetry and software fees on top of that, plus the cashless reader hardware itself. Seed Cashless+ is described as a paid software subscription layered on top; Cantaloupe does not publish a flat price for it or for Seed Pro, both of which are quoted per fleet. This calculator has no subscription, no hardware requirement, and no per-machine fee -- it is a free static page.

Does this replace Cantaloupe's telemetry or cashless payment hardware?

No, and it is not trying to. This tool cannot read a machine's actual sales, cannot take a cashless payment, and cannot alert you when a slot is empty -- all of that requires the sensors and card readers Cantaloupe's hardware provides. This calculator only takes the numbers you already have (from a DEX report, a cashless dashboard, or your own count) and turns them into a profit-per-visit and a monthly route rollup. Plenty of operators running Cantaloupe or a competing telemetry platform still do this specific gut-check by hand or in a spreadsheet; this replaces the spreadsheet, not the telemetry.
