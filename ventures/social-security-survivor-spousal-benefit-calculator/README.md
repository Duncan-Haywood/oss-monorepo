# Social Security Survivor and Spousal Benefit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/social-security-survivor-spousal-benefit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for widows, widowers and married couples: enter your own and your spouse's benefit amounts to see the survivor benefit at every claiming age from 60, your own benefit by age, every survivor-first or own-first combination compared by lifetime total, and the spousal top-up.

After using this, you know whether to take the survivor benefit now and switch to your own at 70 (or the reverse), which usually takes an SSA appointment or a paid planner.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/social-security-survivor-spousal-benefit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
