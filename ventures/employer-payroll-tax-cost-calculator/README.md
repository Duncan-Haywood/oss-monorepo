# Employer Payroll Tax Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/employer-payroll-tax-cost-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter a salary and a state to see employer Social Security, Medicare, FUTA, state unemployment, workers' comp, retirement match and health cost, with the wage-base caps applied, and the all-in cost of a hire.

After using this, you know what a hire really costs on top of pay, and why part-year and low-wage hires cost proportionally more.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/employer-payroll-tax-cost-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. State unemployment wage bases and rates are editable starting points written from memory: replace them with your state's rate notice. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
