Common questions

    
When do I have to register for GST/HST in Canada?

    
When your worldwide taxable sales are more than CAD 30,000 in a single calendar quarter, or in total over four consecutive calendar quarters. Taxi and ride-share drivers must register from day one.

    
What happens if I pass 30,000 in one quarter?

    
You become a registrant on the sale that took you over the line, must charge GST/HST on that sale, and have 29 days to register.

    
What happens if I pass 30,000 over four quarters?

    
You stop being a small supplier at the end of the month after the quarter in which the total passed 30,000, and charge tax from the next day.

    
Does the CAD 30,000 include GST?

    
The test uses your taxable sales before tax, worldwide, including those of associated businesses. Check the CRA guide for items that are excluded.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent.
