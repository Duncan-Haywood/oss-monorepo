# EU Product Liability Directive Checker

Free, private browser tool for makers, importers, distributors and platforms selling software, AI systems, digital manufacturing files or hardware into the EU: pick your role and product and see whether the new Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853 applies from 9 December 2026, who is liable for a defect, which harm is covered, the time limits, update duties, and what to prepare.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/eu-product-liability-directive-checker/

After using this, you know whether strict liability reaches your software or product and what to line up (documentation, insurance, supplier recourse), which used to take reading the Directive and its recitals.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-product-liability-directive-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
