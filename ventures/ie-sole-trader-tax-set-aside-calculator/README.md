# Ireland Self-Employed Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2025)

Free, private browser tool: enter business income and expenses and get income tax, USC, PRSI (Class S), optional pension relief and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags preliminary tax and the €42,500 VAT line.

After using this, an Irish sole trader knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a bill at the 31 October preliminary tax date.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/ie-sole-trader-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
