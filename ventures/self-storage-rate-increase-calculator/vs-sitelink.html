This calculator vs. SiteLink

Two different shapes: SiteLink is an account-based platform for running a self-storage facility's whole operation, including automated rate rules once an increase policy is set. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — is this specific rate increase worth the move-outs it will cause.

What each tool is for

SiteLink, sold by Storable, is a self-storage facility management platform used by operators from single-site owners to national multi-facility companies. It runs tenant records, online rentals, billing and payments, and a Price Optimizer feature that helps set street rates and automate existing-customer rate-increase rules, tied to one account used on an ongoing paid basis across the whole facility. SiteLink's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that revenue-management software describe setting and automating rate rules across a facility; none describe working out, for one proposed increase, how many move-outs it can absorb before it loses money — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what SiteLink does. It does not store tenant records, take online rentals, or automate a rule across a portfolio. It takes one proposed existing-customer rate increase — the tenants it applies to, the street rate, the move-in concession a replacement tenant gets, the turnover cost, and a horizon — and turns that into the induced move-out rate at which the increase breaks even, the units that represents, the margin of safety in percentage points, and what the increase is worth across the group at every step from 5% to 25%, with a flag on any rung that prices the raised rent above the facility's own street rate. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
