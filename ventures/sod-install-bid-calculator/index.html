FOR LANDSCAPERS AND LAWN-INSTALL CREWS

Bid the pallets, the dirt, and the extra work.

Price a sod install the way the trade actually loses money: whole pallets after waste, then soil prep, delivery, slope, and watering — not the published $0.55-a-foot special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot and per pallet, and the number below which the lawn loses money.

The lawn

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with your farm ticket and a crew you have timed. Changing grass, prep, or delivery fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and farm rates

These start from the grass, prep, and delivery you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a farm catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a per-foot special that looked profitable was not.
