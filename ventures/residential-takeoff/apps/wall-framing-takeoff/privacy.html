Privacy notice

Version 2026-11-28 · Maris Stella Labs LLC

This notice covers the Residential Takeoff page and its paid features. The site-wide Privacy Policy covers everything else on marisstella.app, including your rights and how to reach us.

Your plans stay on your device

The Tool runs in your browser. Walls, openings, sketches, traced plan images or PDFs, IFC and DXF files and price lists are never uploaded. The last plan, the sketch, the price list, a saved licence key and the usage counters below are kept in your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time in the browser's settings.

Usage counters on your device

The page counts which features are used (for example "material list downloaded") in local storage on your device only, never with anything you typed, and never sends them. The "Turn usage counting off" button on the page removes them, and a browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal stops them.

Visit counts

Our host, Cloudflare, adds cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics to every page on marisstella.app, this one included. It reports the page address, referrer, browser type, country and load timings as aggregate statistics. It sets no cookie and no identifier that follows you to other sites. A content blocker that blocks static.cloudflareinsights.com stops it. Cloudflare also sees the request data any web host sees (IP address, browser, the page asked for); the site-wide Privacy Policy says what is kept and for how long.

When you buy (once checkout opens)

  • Pressing Buy loads Paddle's checkout from cdn.paddle.com. Paddle, our merchant of record, collects your name, email, billing address and payment details to take the payment, issue the receipt and handle tax. Paddle's own privacy notice applies to that data.
  • Your browser makes a random claim code and passes it to Paddle, so only your browser can collect the key. Our billing service keeps a hash of that code and the key it signed (the product, Paddle's transaction id and the time issued). It stores no name, email or address.
  • The key is checked in your browser against a public key; it is not sent to us to unlock features.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share personal data.
