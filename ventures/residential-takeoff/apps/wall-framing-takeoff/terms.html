2. What the Tool does

The Tool runs in your browser. From a house you type, draw or open (IFC, DXF or a vector plan PDF), it lays out studs, headers, plates and cuts, chooses a board order, and works out wall and roof sheathing, roofs, siding and trim quantities. A Job report adds the supplier material list (CSV) and the branded bid package.
