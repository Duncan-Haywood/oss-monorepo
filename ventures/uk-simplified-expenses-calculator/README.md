# UK Simplified Expenses Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-simplified-expenses-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for UK sole traders and partnerships: enter business miles and the hours you work at home, add your actual costs, and see whether HMRC's simplified expenses (45p/25p mileage, £10/£18/£26 use-of-home flat rate) or actual costs give the bigger claim, and the tax each saves.

After using this, you know which method to put on your Self Assessment for each expense.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-simplified-expenses-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
