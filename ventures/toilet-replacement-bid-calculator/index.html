Bid the closet, the bowl, and the extras.

Price a toilet replacement the way the trade actually loses money: a two-piece 12-inch swap on a good flange versus a one-piece on a 10-inch rough-in with a rotten closet flange, a new shutoff, and a bidet seat — not the published $249 installed special. You get the job price, what it works out to per toilet, and the number below which the install loses money.

The closet

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a fixture invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing scope, bowl, or rough-in fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the scope, bowl, and rough-in you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a fixture catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $249 swap that looked profitable was not.
