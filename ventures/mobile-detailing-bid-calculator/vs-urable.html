This calculator vs. Urable

Two different shapes: Urable is account-based work management software for running an automotive detailing shop's whole vehicle history, photo, and job record across technicians. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one mobile detail cost, given its travel, labor, and supplies, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Urable is account-based work management software built for automotive detailing, paint protection film, vinyl wrap, tint, and ceramic coating businesses. It scans a vehicle's VIN to pull its data, reports the services performed into a two-way Carfax history for that vehicle, documents jobs with before/after photos, and offers a 3D visualizer that lets a customer preview PPF or vinyl colors on their own vehicle model before committing to a job. Every plan includes unlimited users, which independent comparison write-ups describe as its main advantage over competitors that charge per technician. Urable's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and feature set — automotive-detailing work management software with VIN/Carfax integration, sold as an ongoing subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Urable appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Urable does. It does not scan a VIN, hold a vehicle's service history, store before/after photos, or share a record across technicians. It takes one mobile detail — hands-on labor hours, travel, setup, and cleanup time, round-trip mileage at your own vehicle rate, hourly wage and payroll burden, supplies and chemicals, job overhead, payment processing fee, and target margin — and turns that into the contribution left after costs, the break-even quote, the price that hits your target margin, the effective hourly rate on hands-on time and on the whole stop, plus a printable customer proposal with scope, deposit split, and a signature line. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Urable?

They do different jobs. Urable is account-based work management software built for automotive detailing, paint protection film, vinyl wrap, tint, and ceramic coating businesses. It scans a vehicle's VIN to pull its data, builds a two-way Carfax service history for the vehicle, documents jobs with before/after photos, offers a 3D visualizer for PPF and vinyl colors on a customer's exact vehicle, and includes unlimited users on every plan, sold as an ongoing subscription account for running a shop's whole customer and vehicle record. This calculator does none of that. It takes one mobile detail — hands-on labor, travel, setup, and cleanup time, round-trip mileage, loaded wage, supplies, overhead, and payment fees — and turns that into the contribution after costs, the break-even quote, the price needed to hit a target margin, the effective hourly rate on hands-on time and on the whole stop, plus a printable customer proposal, instantly, in the browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A detailing business that wants a running vehicle history, photo documentation, and a shared record across technicians is better served by Urable or a comparable platform; a detailer who wants an instant, private, correctly-sized price for one job before it's quoted is in the right place here.
