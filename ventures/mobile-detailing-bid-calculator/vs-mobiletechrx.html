This calculator vs. Mobile Tech RX

Two different shapes: Mobile Tech RX is account-based business management software for running a reconditioning shop's whole marketing-to-invoicing workflow. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one mobile detail cost, given its travel, labor, and supplies, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Mobile Tech RX is web-based business management software built for the auto reconditioning industry — used by businesses across detailing, paintless dent repair, glass repair, paint touch-up, wheel and rim repair, window tint, interior repair, and paint protection film and vinyl. It covers text-message marketing, employee scheduling, estimates, invoicing, and credit-card payment processing, with a QuickBooks integration, and a mobile app for use in the field. It was founded in 2014 by a former paintless-dent-repair technician, later took a Series A funding round led by Silverton Partners, and in 2021 was acquired by AsTech, with an independent trade publication reporting it served roughly six thousand users across the reconditioning industry at the time. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Mobile Tech RX's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — auto-reconditioning-industry business management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Mobile Tech RX appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Mobile Tech RX does. It does not run marketing campaigns, hold a customer or job record on a server, or manage a schedule across technicians. It takes one mobile detail — hands-on labor hours, travel, setup, and cleanup time, round-trip mileage at your own vehicle rate, hourly wage and payroll burden, supplies and chemicals, job overhead, payment processing fee, and target margin — and turns that into the contribution left after costs, the break-even quote, the price that hits your target margin, the effective hourly rate on hands-on time and on the whole stop, plus a printable customer proposal with scope, deposit split, and a signature line. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
