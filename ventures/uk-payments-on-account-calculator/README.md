# UK Payments on Account Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your Self Assessment bill and the tax already collected at source and see what you owe on 31 January and 31 July, whether payments on account apply, the first-year double hit, and whether a reduction claim would save cash.

After using this, you know exactly how much cash to set aside for each HMRC date, which used to mean decoding a bill that mixes a balancing payment with an advance on next year's tax.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-payments-on-account-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
