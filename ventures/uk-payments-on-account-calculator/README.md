# UK Payments on Account Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your Self Assessment bill and see exactly what HMRC will ask for on 31 January (balancing payment plus first payment on account) and 31 July, whether payments on account apply at all, and what reducing them could save or cost in interest.

After using this, you know how much cash to hold back for January and July, which used to mean reading HMRC's guidance and working out 150% of a first-year bill by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-payments-on-account-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
