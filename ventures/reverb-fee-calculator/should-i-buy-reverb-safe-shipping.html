Common questions

Should I buy Reverb Safe Shipping or use my own label?

On the worked example — $800 list, $75 shipping charged, $65 own label, $80 Safe Shipping quote, $450 gear cost as the unprotected claim, 2% claim rate, 5% marketplace, 3.19%+$0.49 payment — your own label leaves $281.60 if nothing goes wrong and $272.60 after the $9.00 expected claim. Safe Shipping leaves $266.60. The extra $15.00 label is worth it only if you lose more than 3.33% of shipments at a $450.00 claim.

Does Reverb Safe Shipping change what the buyer pays?

No. Safe Shipping changes the label you buy and whether you eat a damaged or lost shipment. Marketplace and payment fees stay the same when the buyer still pays the same shipping charged. Who pays postage — buyer, included, or pickup — is a different question.

When does Reverb Safe Shipping beat my own label?

When the expected unprotected claim (claim rate times cash you lose on a damaged or lost shipment) is larger than the extra label cost. On the worked example that crossover is 3.33%. A $0 Safe Shipping quote is illustrated at your own label plus $15.00 so the table is never empty.
