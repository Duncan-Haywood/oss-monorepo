Buyer-pays, include shipping, absorb the label, or pickup

Path Buyer checkout Fees Net cash vs buyer-pays

Including shipping folds postage into the item so marketplace fee rises. Absorbing the label keeps your typed list and leaves the postage on you — a cheaper checkout for the buyer and less cash for you. Local pickup uses your list with no shipping charged; add gas or parking as the meetup cost if pickup is not free. This is not a recommendation — it is the arithmetic on the rates you typed. Path Label you pay Expected claim Expected net vs own after claims

Safe Shipping does not change what the buyer pays. It only changes the label you buy and whether you eat a damaged or lost shipment. Expected keep subtracts (claim rate × cash you lose on an unprotected claim) from own-label profit. A $0 Safe Shipping quote is illustrated at your own label plus $15 so the table is never empty. List it yourself, consign at the shop, or take their cash

Consignment is a percent of the shop's sale, not of your Reverb list. They ship and they collect, so this path does not pay Reverb marketplace, payment processing, a bump, or a label. Their cash offer is a buy today — you walk with that number minus what you paid for the gear. Pay for a bump, or take the offer

Path Sale price Fees Net cash vs offer, no bump

A bump is a fixed cash out of this sale. Paying one and still taking the same offer always leaves less by exactly the bump. It only pays if it gets you to list or to a higher offer that covers the bump after percentage fees. A $0 bump is illustrated at $3 so the table is not the same sale twice — overwrite it from your listing. This is not a recommendation to buy a bump. Worked example.
