Including shipping folds postage into the item so marketplace fee rises. Absorbing the label keeps your typed list and leaves the postage on you — a cheaper checkout for the buyer and less cash for you. Local pickup uses your list with no shipping charged; add gas or parking as the meetup cost if pickup is not free. This is not a recommendation — it is the arithmetic on the rates you typed.
