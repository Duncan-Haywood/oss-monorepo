Consignment is a percent of the shop's sale. They ship and they collect, so you do not pay Reverb marketplace, payment processing, a bump, or a label. Their cash offer is a buy today. Listing it yourself keeps the Reverb checkout and the fees that come with it.

Should I consign a guitar or list it myself on Reverb?

On the worked example — $800 Reverb list, $75 shipping charged, $65 postage you pay, $450 gear cost, 5% marketplace, 3.19%+$0.49 payment, $800 shop sale, 30% shop cut, $520 cash offer — listing it yourself leaves $281.60. Their cash offer would have to be $731.60.

Does a shop consignment pay Reverb fees?

No. The shop ships and collects. You receive their sale price minus their cut, then subtract what you paid for the gear. Marketplace, payment processing, a bump, and a label stay on the self-list path.

When does taking the shop's cash beat listing on Reverb?

When their cash offer minus what you paid for the gear is larger than Reverb list profit on the rates you typed. On the worked example that crossover is $731.60. A $520 cash offer leaves $70.00 versus $281.60 on Reverb.
