This calculator vs. HoodClean Pro

Two different shapes: HoodClean Pro is a subscription field platform for running a hood-cleaning company's compliance reports, scheduling, and invoicing. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one job cost, given its duct, fan, and access, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

HoodClean Pro is an all-in-one field platform built specifically for commercial kitchen exhaust and fire protection professionals. It generates NFPA 96 hood cleaning reports and NFPA 17A fire suppression inspection records, runs CRM, scheduling, invoicing, payments, and QuickBooks sync, and produces the compliance certificates an insurer or fire marshal asks for. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. HoodClean Pro's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — kitchen-exhaust and fire-protection field software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for HoodClean Pro appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what HoodClean Pro does. It does not file a compliance report, schedule a crew, or send an invoice, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one restaurant hood and exhaust system — canopy and filter square footage, horizontal and vertical duct length, the fan, a grease-load multiplier, an access multiplier, after-hours labor, travel, chemicals, and disposal — and turns that into the job price, the price per the annual contract, and the walk-away price below which the stop loses money, plus a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
