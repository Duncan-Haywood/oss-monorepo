This calculator vs. HoodCleaningDispatch

Two different shapes: HoodCleaningDispatch is a subscription dispatch system for running a kitchen-exhaust cleaning company's call intake, night-crew scheduling, and recurring follow-up. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one hood-and-duct system cost, given its canopy, duct run, and fans, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

HoodCleaningDispatch is a vertical dispatch system built specifically for commercial kitchen exhaust-cleaning companies. It offers 24/7 call intake, access-aware scheduling that books overnight work around a restaurant's kitchen-close hours, job photos, service records, access notes, and recurring follow-up so the next service date queues automatically. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. HoodCleaningDispatch's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a dispatch platform for kitchen-exhaust cleaning companies, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for HoodCleaningDispatch appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what HoodCleaningDispatch does. It does not take calls, schedule crews, or queue follow-up visits, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes the canopy hood area, hood filters, horizontal and vertical duct runs, clean-out door work, rooftop or wall exhaust fans, and makeup-air units that make up one system, prices them by grease load and access difficulty rather than a flat per-hood guess, and adds loaded wage, after-hours premium, travel, consumables, disposal, overhead, and target margin to turn that into the per-job price, the price per hood, the annual contract value at your cleaning frequency, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
