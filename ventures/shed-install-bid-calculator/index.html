FOR SHED DEALERS AND BACKYARD-BUILDING CREWS

Bid the pad, the delivery, and the extras.

Price a backyard shed the way the trade actually loses money: existing pad versus new gravel or a slab, delivery miles, crane versus forklift, then ramp, loft, windows, and haul-away — not the published $2,499 drop-and-set special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot, and the number below which the shed loses money.

The shed

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with your kit ticket and a crew you have timed. Changing fulfillment, pad, access, or electrical fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.
