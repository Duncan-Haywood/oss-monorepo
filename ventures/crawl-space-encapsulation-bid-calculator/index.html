Bid the liner, the access, and the extras.

Price crawl space encapsulation the way the trade actually loses money: a dry hatch with 6-mil liner-only versus a sealed encapsulate, belly-crawl versus standing water, then a dehumidifier, a dedicated circuit, and a clean-out — not the published $4 / sq ft special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot of dirt floor, and the number below which the crawl loses money.
