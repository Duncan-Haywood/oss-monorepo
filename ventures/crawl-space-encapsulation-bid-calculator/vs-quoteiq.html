This calculator vs. QuoteIQ

Two different shapes: QuoteIQ is a subscription CRM for running a waterproofing company's leads, estimates, and job costing. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one crawl space cost, given its floor area, liner type, and access, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

QuoteIQ is CRM and estimating software marketed specifically to crawl space encapsulation and waterproofing businesses, alongside basement waterproofing and related trades. Its own marketing describes tiered pricing presets — basic vapor barrier, full encapsulation, and premium conditioned crawl space — satellite-based measurement of a home's footprint and perimeter, job costing, and lead and follow-up tracking. It is sold as an ongoing subscription. QuoteIQ's own current pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — waterproofing-industry CRM and estimating software, sold as a subscription — are stated, and no dollar amount for QuoteIQ appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what QuoteIQ does. It does not track leads, manage a sales pipeline, or measure a home from satellite imagery, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one crawl space's floor area, a choice between liner-only and sealed encapsulation, hatch versus belly-crawl versus wet access, dehumidifier and sump-pump extras, loaded wage, payroll burden, materials, overhead, target margin, and travel, and turns that into the per-job price, the extra-work share, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
