Common questions

Is this an asbestos inspection or a substitute for my certification?

No. This is a pricing worksheet only. It does not perform an asbestos inspection, bulk-sample identification, or air-quality analysis, does not check any federal, state, or local regulatory requirement, and does not replace your own certification, competent-person determination, or professional judgment about scope and liability on a given job.

Why does the calculator ask for a containment class instead of just square footage?

Class I friable material needs full containment, a negative-air machine, and a decon chamber, and crews move far slower doing wet removal in full PPE. Class III minor, incidental disturbance of non-friable material can be glovebagged in an afternoon. The labor hours per square foot between the two differ by five to six times, so a flat rate that only sees area gets one of them wrong.

Why not just quote a flat rate per square foot like a lot of the trade does?

A flat rate does not see containment class, how many separate work areas need their own setup, or how much air monitoring and waste the job produces. A small, simple Class III job can end up overpriced by a rate calibrated to an average job mix, and a large Class I job can end up badly underpriced. Does anything about my job or client get uploaded?

No. Every number on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and no job address, client name, or price is ever saved or transmitted. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off below.

What if I don't know the exact air monitoring sample count or waste volume before I bid?

Use your best estimate from a walkthrough, the project specification, or a similar past job of the same containment class and size — the same way you would size up any job. This tool prices the numbers you enter; it does not verify them against a site inspection.
