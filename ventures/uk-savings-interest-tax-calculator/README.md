# UK Savings Interest Tax Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your other taxable income and your savings interest to see the tax due after the personal allowance, the 0% starting rate for savings and the Personal Savings Allowance, how much interest you can earn tax-free, and what a cash ISA would save.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-savings-interest-tax-calculator/

After using this, you know whether your bank interest is taxable and how much of your savings should move into an ISA.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-savings-interest-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
