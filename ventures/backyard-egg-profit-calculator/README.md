# Backyard Egg Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/backyard-egg-profit-calculator/>

**Find what a dozen eggs costs you in cash and hours, your profit per dozen and per hour, the price per dozen you need to pay yourself a wage, and how feed cost and winter lay rate move it.** Browser page, no install.

After using this, you know whether your egg money beats the feed bill, the price you need for the wage you want, and how much a dark winter costs you.

## Your numbers, not advice

Every value is a number you type. Prices, costs and times are yours to check. Check your state's rules on selling eggs. Not affiliated with any poultry association or marketplace.

## How it computes

Cash cost per year = feed (hens x lb a day x 365 x price per lb) + other cost x 12 + hens x pullet cost / laying years + dozens sold x carton cost. Dozens sold = hens x lay rate x 365 / 12 x share not lost. Price for your wage = (cash cost + wage x hours a year) / dozens sold.

## What it is not

Not financial, tax, legal, food-safety or business advice, and no warranty. It ignores sales tax, licences, egg-grading rules, mortality and the value of manure and pest control.

## Privacy

No network call, no account, no analytics vendor. Anonymous feature counts (`calculate_run`, `example_loaded`) live in local storage only and can be switched off on the page; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured.
