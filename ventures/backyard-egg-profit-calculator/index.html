Common questions

What does it cost to produce a dozen eggs at home?

On the default numbers $3.57 in cash per dozen sold ($0.30 an egg): $821.25 of feed a year, $360.00 of bedding and other costs, $80.00 to replace hens and $137.12 of cartons, plus 0.53 hours of your time per dozen. Feed is the largest cash line at 58.7%. Enter your own figures; the defaults are invented.

How much should I charge for a dozen eggs?

$11.53 a dozen to pay yourself $15 an hour on those numbers, against a cash breakeven of $3.57. Selling at $5.00 leaves $1.43 a dozen and $2.69 an hour before any wage is deducted.

What happens when feed costs more or the hens lay less?

Doubling the feed price takes the profit per hour from $2.69 to -$1.25 and lifts the price you need to $13.63. Hens laying at half the rate (35 percent in the dark of winter) cost $6.79 per dozen and need $22.72.
