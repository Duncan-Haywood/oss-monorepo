This calculator vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber is an account-based platform for running a service company's whole operation, including scheduling a drop-off or pickup and invoicing the customer once a rental is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this roll-off rental cost, given its included weight allowance, tipping fee and overage rate, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Jobber is a field service management platform used by home and trade service businesses — lawn care, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, junk removal and general contracting among them — to run client management and online quoting, scheduling and crew or driver dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client-facing hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Jobber's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that kind of field service software describe scheduling a job, dispatching a driver, and invoicing and collecting payment once a price already exists; none describe working out whether a container's overage rate actually covers what an extra ton of disposal costs, or reconciling a rental's price against the scale ticket that comes back once the job is done — which is what this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage a customer list, schedule a driver's route, or collect a payment. It takes a container size, the included weight allowance and rental days, the tipping fee you pay and the overage rate you charge, driver labor, round-trip mileage, a flat dump/gate fee, equipment cost, overhead and target margin, and turns that into the rental price, the breakeven at the allowance, and the profit if the job goes as expected — plus, once the container is back and weighed, a check of the real scale ticket against that same breakeven, so an overage rate priced below the tipping fee shows up as a loss before it repeats across a fleet of containers. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Jobber?

They do different jobs, and a roll-off dumpster rental operator of any size will likely end up touching both eventually. Jobber is a field service management platform used across home and trade service businesses — including hauling and waste-adjacent trades — to run client management and quoting, scheduling and dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. This calculator does one narrower thing: it takes a rental's included weight allowance, tipping fee, overage rate, rental days, driver labor, mileage and equipment cost, and turns that into the rental price, the breakeven at the allowance, and — once a scale ticket comes back — the real profit and margin the job actually left. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. An operator already scheduling drop-offs and pickups, invoicing customers, and taking payments across a fleet of containers is better served by Jobber or a comparable field service platform; an operator who wants an instant, private answer to what one rental should cost before it is quoted, and whether the overage rate even covers the tipping fee, is in the right place here.
