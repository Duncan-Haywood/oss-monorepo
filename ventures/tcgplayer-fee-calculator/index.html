      Common questions

      How much does TCGplayer take per sale?

      Two fees. A marketplace commission of 10.75% of the order subtotal (cards plus the shipping the buyer pays), capped at $75 per product, and a transaction fee of 2.5% of the order total plus $0.30 per order. On a single $50 card that is $5.38 + $1.55, so you keep about $43.08 before postage and supplies, an effective 13.8%. There is no listing fee.

      Why does a $1 card lose so much to fees?

      Because the $0.30 transaction fee is charged once per order, not per card. A one-card $1.00 order pays $0.43 in fees, 43.3% of the sale. The same $1 card inside a 20-card $20 order pays $2.95 in total, 14.8%, so the order size matters more than the card price.

      Does TCGplayer charge commission on shipping?

      Yes. The commission is taken on the order subtotal, which includes the shipping the buyer pays, and the 2.5% transaction fee is charged on the whole order total including shipping and sales tax. A $20 card with $1.31 of buyer-paid shipping pays commission on $21.31, not $20.00.

      What is the TCGplayer commission cap?

      The 10.75% commission stops at $75 per product sold, which it reaches at a subtotal of about $697.67. On a single $1,000 card the commission is $75.00 instead of $107.50, so total fees are $100.30 (10.0%). Below roughly $700 the cap changes nothing.

      Did TCGplayer raise its fees in 2026?

      Yes. According to TCGplayer's fee table, the Marketplace Level 1-4 commission rose from 10.25% to 10.75% on 10 February 2026 and the per-product cap rose from $50 to $75. On a $100 card that is $0.50 more commission than the old rate. Many older calculators still show 10.25%.
