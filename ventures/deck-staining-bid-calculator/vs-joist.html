This calculator vs. Joist

Two different shapes: Joist is an account-based estimating, invoicing, and payment app for running a contractor's whole business. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one deck-staining job cost, given its stainable surface, rail, stairs, prep, and coats, before you write the estimate.

What each tool is for

Joist is estimating, invoicing, and payment-collection software, founded in 2011 and headquartered in Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada, later acquired by ServiceTitan in 2021. It is built for small contractors across trades — painters, remodelers, landscapers, deck builders — covering line-item estimates, branded proposals, signed approvals, invoicing, and in-app payment collection across a whole business, with a mobile app for use on site. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across every job, not a one-off calculator. Joist's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — estimating and invoicing software serving small contractors including deck-staining crews, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Joist appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Joist does. It does not build a branded proposal, collect a signature, or take a card payment, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one deck-staining job's walking-surface square feet, picket-rail lineal feet, stair runs, prep level — wash-and-brighten, wash-and-sand, or strip peeling stain — finish — water, oil, or composite clean-and-seal — coat count, second-story access, and a furniture move, plus loaded painter wage, payroll burden, travel and equipment, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per walking square foot, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
