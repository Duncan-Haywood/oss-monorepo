# Appliance Repair Quote Calculator

The free appliance repair quote calculator helps an independent technician price a job before sending the customer a number. Enter the diagnostic and repair hours, loaded labor cost, trip cost, parts, parts markup, callback reserve, overhead, and target margin. It shows a quote target, walk-away floor, effective hourly rate, and a quick price sensitivity table.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It works without an account, network request, API key, or paid service. All arithmetic is deterministic and the inputs remain in the browser.

## Why this niche

Appliance repair technicians search for repair pricing and service-call fee guidance, but flat hourly-rate calculators commonly omit windshield time, parts handling, warranty callbacks, and the difference between diagnostic and repair labor. This tool makes those assumptions visible so a technician can revise them from their own books. It does not tell a technician what a customer must pay or certify that a quote is profitable.

The product is distinct from the portfolio's janitorial and trade bid calculators: its unit is a single service call with parts risk and callback reserve, not a recurring contract or construction job.

## Privacy and usage

Everything runs in your browser. Your prices, customer details, and repair notes are never uploaded. There are no accounts, cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics vendor. Unless you opt out, an anonymous local counter records only feature names and the day: quote calculations after an edited input and copied summaries. It never records inputs or customer content. Turn it off with the checkbox in the footer; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control also disable it.
