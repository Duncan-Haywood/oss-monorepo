This calculator vs. Housecall Pro

Two different shapes: Housecall Pro runs a home-service business — scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, payments. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this repair cost, and does finishing it still beat telling the customer to replace.

What each tool is for

Housecall Pro is field service management software for home service businesses, used as a central hub for the work around a job rather than the pricing of one. It covers a drag-and-drop scheduling calendar, real-time dispatch, automated on-my-way texts with GPS tracking, online booking, recurring service plans, a mobile app that lets a technician pull up the work order and record repair notes without returning to the office, estimates and invoicing with automated payment reminders, card payments, and a QuickBooks integration. It is sold as an ongoing account for the business, and it publishes that it is used by over 200,000 field service professionals across HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, and other trades. Housecall Pro's current plan names and dollar figures are not verified from this page, so none are repeated here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software, sold as an ongoing account — are stated, and no price for Housecall Pro appears anywhere on this page.

This calculator does none of what Housecall Pro does. It does not schedule a job, dispatch a van, send an invoice, or take a payment, and it holds no customer record between visits. It takes one service call — diagnostic and labor hours at your loaded rate, parts at cost with your markup applied to the parts themselves before anything else, trip cost, a callback and warranty reserve charged as a percentage of that combined direct cost, overhead, and target margin — and turns that into the walk-away floor below which the job loses money, the target-margin quote, the effective hourly rate you actually earn, and a repair-versus-replace verdict measured from both sides at once: the customer's price per remaining year of service, and what you keep if they decline and you sell the replacement instead. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

They do different jobs, and most working technicians end up wanting both. Does Housecall Pro price an appliance repair for me?

Field service platforms are built to move a job through scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and payment, and they hold whatever price book or flat-rate pricing you put into them. What they do not do is derive that number from your own cost structure for one specific repair. Reviewers also note that appliance repair contractors get comparatively little trade-specific tooling in Housecall Pro next to HVAC and plumbing, where equipment tracking and service-plan features are more developed. This calculator is the step before the price book: it works out what a repair actually costs you once parts markup, trip cost, callback reserve and overhead compound, so the number you load into a platform — or say at the door — has a floor under it.
