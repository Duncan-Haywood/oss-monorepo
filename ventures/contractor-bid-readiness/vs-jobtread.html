This checklist vs. JobTread

Two different jobs: JobTread is an account-based construction management platform for running a contractor's whole sales, estimating, and scheduling pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one bid's missing artifacts before its submission deadline.

What each tool is for

JobTread is a construction management platform built for residential and remodeling contractors: a client-facing CRM, estimating and proposal tools, budgeting, scheduling, and job financials, run through an ongoing account you and your team sign into. JobTread's own pricing and current feature list are not reachable from this session to confirm, so no dollar figure or plan name for JobTread is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — an account-based construction management platform covering sales, estimating, and job operations — are stated.

This checklist does none of what JobTread does. It has no CRM, no estimating engine, no job schedule, and no connection to any account — it stores and uploads nothing. It takes the requirements one solicitation lists, lets you assign an owner and a status to each, tracks the submission deadline as whole days remaining, and produces a downloadable Markdown report. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this checklist or JobTread?

Both, for different jobs, and a contractor who already runs client and job data through JobTread can still use this checklist for the narrow moment before a bid goes out the door. JobTread is a construction management platform built for residential and remodeling contractors: a client-facing CRM, estimating and proposal tools, budgeting, scheduling, and job financials, run as an ongoing account you and your team sign into. This checklist does none of that. It takes the requirements one solicitation lists — documents, forms, signatures, confirmations — and turns them into an owner-by-owner status board with a days-remaining count and a downloadable Markdown report, entirely in your browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A contractor who wants estimating, scheduling, and job costing run through one system is better served by JobTread or a comparable platform; a contractor who wants an instant, private, no-login check of what is still missing before a specific bid's deadline is in the right place here.

Can JobTread track the bid-readiness items this checklist covers?

JobTread's proposal and job tools can carry a bid through its own workflow once you are set up in that account. This checklist is not a substitute for that workflow — it is a fast, account-free pass at one question: which requirements on this solicitation are ready, which need attention, and which are blocked, before the clock runs out. Some contractors will use this checklist once per unfamiliar solicitation and run everything else through JobTread or a similar platform; neither tool replaces the other.
