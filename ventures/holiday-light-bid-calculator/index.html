FOR HOLIDAY LIGHTING CONTRACTORS

Bid the hang, the take-down, and the extras.

Price Christmas lights the way the trade actually loses money: a 1-story ranch C9 hang-only versus hang-and-takedown, two-story lift versus 3-story lift, then peaks, tree wraps, and bushes — not the published $3 / LF hang special. You get the job price, what it works out to per lineal foot of eave, and the number below which the hang loses money.

The house

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a light invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing scope, stories, or light type fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the scope, stories, and light type you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a light catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $3 / LF hang that looked profitable was not.
