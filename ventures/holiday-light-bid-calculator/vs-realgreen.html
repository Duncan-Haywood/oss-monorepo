Two different shapes: RealGreen is business-management software for running a holiday lighting company's marketing, inventory, scheduling, and job history across a season. What each tool is for

RealGreen sells business management software with a product line aimed specifically at holiday light installation companies. Its published description of that line covers marketing services, inventory management, estimating and scheduling, and job tracking — the operational spine of a company that installs lights for a few hundred properties between October and January. It is sold as an ongoing account for a business, not a single-job tool. RealGreen's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's plan names or dollar figures, so none is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — business-management software for the holiday lighting trade — are stated, and no dollar amount for RealGreen appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what RealGreen does. It does not market your services, hold your inventory, route your crews, or keep a customer record on a server. It takes one property's eave linear feet, tree, bush and column counts, storeys and roof pitch, an access premium for the runs that need a ladder set three times, material cost per linear foot, the takedown-to-hang labour ratio, off-season storage, loaded wage and payroll burden, overhead, travel, and target margin, and turns that into the per-job price and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears that floor, and a printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or RealGreen?

They do different jobs, and a holiday lighting business of any size may end up using both. RealGreen sells business management software with a product line aimed at holiday light installation companies: marketing services, inventory management, estimating and scheduling, and job tracking, run as an ongoing account for the whole company. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from eave linear feet, tree and bush and column counts, the number of stories and whether the roof is steep, an access premium for hard runs, material cost per linear foot, the takedown-to-hang labour ratio, off-season storage, loaded wage and payroll burden, overhead, travel, and target margin, it turns that into the per-job price and the walk-away price below which the job loses money. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A lighting company already running marketing, inventory, scheduling and job history across a full crew and a full season is better served by RealGreen or a comparable platform; an installer who wants an instant, private gut-check on what one house should cost before opening a business system is in the right place here.

Does this calculator handle takedown and storage?

Yes, and they are usually where a holiday lighting bid goes wrong. Takedown is entered as a ratio of the hang labour rather than a flat guess, because a crew that took eight hours to hang a roofline does not take eight hours to pull it down. Storage is entered as its own line so that the months the product sits in your building between seasons are priced rather than absorbed. Both feed the same walk-away price as the hang labour, so a bid that looks profitable on hang day and loses money in January shows up before you send it.
