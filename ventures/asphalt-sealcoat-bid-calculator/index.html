FOR SEALCOATING CONTRACTORS

Bid the coats and the cracks, not the driveway special.

Price a sealcoating job the way the lot actually costs: each surface times coats and coverage, then oil-spot primer, crack fill, striping, the round trip, and the margin you intend to keep. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot and per gallon, and the number below which the job loses money.

Condition and prep

Oxidized asphalt drinks the first coat. Oil spots need primer, not more sealer. Cracks are linear feet. A cents-per-foot special that “includes” those lines is unpaid work.
