This calculator vs. Bitumio

Two different shapes: Bitumio is a subscription platform for running a paving company's estimating, scheduling, and job costing. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one lot or driveway cost, given its square footage, coats, and condition, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Bitumio is asphalt estimation and scheduling software built for the paving industry, aimed at asphalt maintenance contractors who focus on sealcoating, crack filling, striping, and repair work rather than new construction. It combines job estimating, scheduling, customer management, and job costing with integrated map measurement tools and QuickBooks integration. Its own marketing describes automated proposal generation and material-quantity calculations that factor in crack sizes, application method — spray versus squeegee — coverage rate, and waste. It is sold as an ongoing subscription with a free tier. Bitumio's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — paving-industry estimating and scheduling software, sold as a subscription with a free tier — are stated, and no dollar amount for Bitumio appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Bitumio does. It does not manage customer records, schedule crews, or integrate with accounting software, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one job's square footage, coat count, pavement condition, oil-spot priming, crack fill, sealer coverage rate and waste, loaded wage, payroll burden, materials, overhead, target margin, and travel, and turns that into the per-job price, the price per square foot, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
