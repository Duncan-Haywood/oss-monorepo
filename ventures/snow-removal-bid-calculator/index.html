Questions contractors actually ask

How do I price a snow removal contract?

Time each stop, price those minutes at a loaded operator-and-truck hourly, add salt, then multiply by the storms and ice events you expect. Do not start from a neighbor’s driveway price.

Per push or seasonal?

Offer both. Seasonal only works if the quote survives a long winter plus ice. Paste a number you are considering and the tool will say how many storms it can absorb.

What about unlimited ice?

Ice is usually salt and sidewalks, not a full plow — and there are often more ice events than snow events. Price them as their own line or the “included” clause eats the season.

    Common questions

    How do I price a snow removal contract?

    Time each stop (plow + sidewalk + drive time), price those minutes at a loaded operator-and-truck hourly, add salt, then multiply by the storms and ice events you actually expect. Margin is a share of the seasonal price, not a markup on one driveway.

    Is a seasonal snow removal contract better than per push?

    Seasonal is only better for the contractor if the quote covers a long winter plus ice events. A $350 driveway seasonal that looked fine at eight storms often loses money at fourteen, especially with unlimited ice.

    How many snow events should I estimate?

    Use your own trigger depth and last few winters, not a national average. In many Northeast and Midwest metros a 1-inch trigger fires more often than a 2-inch trigger. This tool does not forecast weather — it prices the event count you enter.
