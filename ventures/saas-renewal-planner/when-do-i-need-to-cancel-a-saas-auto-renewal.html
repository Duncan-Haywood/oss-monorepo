When do I need to cancel a SaaS auto-renewal? Notice windows and seat right-sizing

Short answer: the last day is the renewal date minus the notice days in your order form, and the time to start talking is a month before that. After it, most auto-renewing contracts have renewed for a full term at the vendor's proposed price, so the conversation that could have saved money turns into a request for a favour.

A worked list

Eight subscriptions costing $228,600.00 a year, seen on 2 October 2026, with a 10% spare-seat buffer, a 10% extra ask and talks starting 30 days before each notice date.

  • Salesforce renews on 15 December with 60 days' notice, so the last day is 2026-10-16, 14 days away. Only 51 of 80 seats are used. Renewing 57 seats at today's price instead of all 80 at the proposed 9% increase turns $104,640.00 into $68,400.00: $36,240.00 saved, or $43,080.00 with the extra ask.
  • Slack's notice date is 2026-10-31, 29 days away, and is worth $8,352.00 to $10,598.40.
  • Figma, Zoom and DocuSign are already past their notice dates. They are probably committed, so the move is to ask for fewer seats or a credit at the next anniversary and to diarise next year's date.
  • Asana does not auto-renew, so there is no lock-in date; it simply lapses unless someone renews it.
  • Across the list, negotiating the live renewals is worth about $65,070.00 (right-size and no uplift) to $83,250.00 (with the extra ask).

What to feed it

  1. From each order form: the renewal date, the notice period, whether it auto-renews and the proposed or capped increase.
  2. From each admin console: seats paid and seats used in the last term, not the headcount.
  3. Your own choices: how many spare seats to keep, how much extra to ask for, and how early to start.

Read the contract itself before relying on a date: some require written notice to a named address, count days differently, or give a right to reduce seats mid-term.

Questions

When do I need to cancel a SaaS auto-renewal?

Before the notice date in your order form: the renewal date minus the notice days, commonly 30, 60 or 90. After it, an auto-renewing contract usually renews for a full term at the proposed price. The planner prints that date for every subscription.

How many seats should I renew?

The seats people actually used in the last term plus a buffer for hiring, rounded up and never above the seats you pay for. A 10% buffer is a common starting point; the planner lets you change it.

How much should I ask for?

Ask first to renew right-sized at today's per-seat price with no increase. That is the floor target. Anything beyond it, such as a multi-year commitment in return for a further discount, is the stretch target, and what the vendor agrees to is theirs to decide.
