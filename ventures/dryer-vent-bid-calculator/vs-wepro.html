This calculator vs. WePro

Two different shapes: WePro is a subscription field-service app for running a dryer-vent cleaning company's scheduling, dispatch, and invoicing. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one job cost, given its stories, run length, and extras, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

WePro is a field-service app built for dryer vent and duct cleaning companies. It schedules cleanings, dispatches the right tech with job details and notes, tracks work in progress with real-time status updates from the field, sends customers appointment reminders and on-the-way messages, and turns completed jobs into invoices and customer-ready service reports. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. WePro's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a field-service platform for dryer-vent and duct cleaning companies, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for WePro appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what WePro does. It does not schedule techs, dispatch jobs, or send customer messages, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes the job type — 1-story wall cap, 2-story wall or soffit, roof-cap termination, or a multi-dryer duplex laundry — the stories and run feet beyond a base setup, extra dryers, a camera pass, crushed-flex replacement, a nest or blockage, occupied-home access, loaded wage, brush kit and rod material, camera and flex kit fees, nest disposal, a roof safety kit on roof jobs, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per dryer, the price per foot, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a copyable customer-facing bid summary. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
