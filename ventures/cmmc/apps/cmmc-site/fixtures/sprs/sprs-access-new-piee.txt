Roles control what the user can see and do in SPRS. To monitor company data, scoring, and cyber information, request the “Contractor/Vendor (Support Role)” role. To enter, edit, or affirm cyber information (NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC) the “SPRS Cyber Vendor User” role is required. Both roles allow the user to enter or edit an Industrial Based Survey.
