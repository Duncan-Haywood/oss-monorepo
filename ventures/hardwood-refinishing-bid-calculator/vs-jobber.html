This calculator vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber is an account-based platform for running a home service company's whole operation, including scheduling the crew and invoicing the customer once a job is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this hardwood refinish cost, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Jobber is a field service management platform used by home service businesses — flooring, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, and lawn care among them — to run client management and online quoting, scheduling and crew dispatch, invoicing and online payments, and a client-facing hub, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Jobber's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that kind of field service software describe scheduling, invoicing, and collecting payment for a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a hardwood refinish should cost from field square footage, stair treads, and closets before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage a client list, dispatch a route across a season, or collect a payment. It takes field square footage, stair treads, closets, a stain change, and extra coats as their own line items, plus loaded refinisher wage, payroll burden, travel, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per square foot, the share of hours eaten by stairs, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
