FOR INDEPENDENT JUNK HAULERS

Price the dump ticket, not the truck.

A half-truck of couches and a half-truck of concrete look the same in the driveway. The landfill bills the weight. This calculator turns each pile into estimated tons, prices the ticket (minimum, per-ton, surcharge, appliance fees), then adds crew time, stairs, travel, and the dump run so you see the walk-away number before you quote the job.

The load

One row per material. Pounds per unit is a starting density — replace it with a figure from your own scale tickets. Quantity is cubic yards for bulk, or a count for appliances, mattresses, and tires.

Dump and truck

Transfer stations bill the greater of the per-ton rate and a minimum ticket, then often add an environmental or host-community surcharge. Appliance, mattress, and tire fees are on each row because many landfills will not take those on the regular ticket.

Labor and costs

On-site hours are clock time. Stairs, a long carry, round-trip drive, and each dump run are added, then multiplied by crew size so windshield time is loaded labor. A posted minimum lifts the customer’s number; it does not change the walk-away cost.
