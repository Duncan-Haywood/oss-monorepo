    Common questions

    What is the difference between markup and margin?

    Markup is profit as a percentage of your cost; margin is profit as a percentage of the price. A 25% markup is only a 20% margin, so a contractor who adds 25% to cost and believes they earn a 25% margin is under-pricing every job.

    What is a walk-away price?

    It is your full cost: labor, payroll burden, materials, subcontractors, other costs and a share of overhead. Any price below it loses money before you earn a dollar of profit.

    How do I price a job at a target margin?

    Divide the full cost by one minus the margin. A job costing $2,750 at a 20% margin is $2,750 / 0.8 = $3,437.50, not $2,750 x 1.20.

    Where do the trade starting rates come from?

    Each trade's takeoff is taken from that trade's own calculator in this collection: its production rates, coverage and default prices, with the source of every figure recorded in the open-source preset file. They are examples so the form is not blank, not industry standards and not a statement of what your crew does. Replace them with your own hours and supplier prices.

    Is my pricing uploaded anywhere?

    No. The page runs in your browser with no account and no network requests.
