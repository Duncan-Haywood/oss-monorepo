The model, and what it leaves out

  • Sales are what each file records: eBay and Etsy include shipping the buyer paid; Shopify includes it too, with sales tax taken out when the Orders export is given; Depop includes the shipping the buyer paid, since Depop credits it to you and deducts the label, and its net is built from the fee, label, boosting and refund columns because the export carries no payout per sale (buyer-paid sales tax and the Buyer Marketplace Fee are reported beside the books); Poshmark, Mercari and Whatnot are read at item price (Mercari books a sale when the order completes; in-progress and canceled orders are listed, not booked, and the shipping, sales tax and fees buyers paid on top are reported beside the books). The share a channel keeps is therefore not perfectly like-for-like, and a heavy-item channel pays more in postage that is not its fee.
  • Net payout is what the marketplace reports you kept: sales, less refunds, fees, postage bought there, ads and anything else it deducts. Reconciles. Every month, every channel and the total satisfy sales + refunds + deductions = net payout, and the months add up to the totals.
  • Profit is net payout minus the cost you gave. A channel with any sale left uncosted shows no profit; the total shows no profit unless every channel has one.
  • Worksheet. Totals are grouped under the Schedule C lines they usually belong on. Postage bought through a marketplace is often reported in cost of goods and sometimes as an other expense; the row says so.
  • Inventory and Part III. With an inventory log and one tax year of reports, each unit sold takes the oldest matching purchase still on hand (first in, first out), matched by the SKU or title the marketplace shows; a returned unit goes back on the shelf. Line 35 is last year's closing inventory if you enter it, otherwise the log's earlier purchases not marked sold before the year. Line 36 is the year's purchases less anything marked personal or kept. Line 41 is what the log still holds at December 31; rows marked donated, lost or damaged leave it, and rows marked sold in the year that no report sale took are counted as sold elsewhere. Line 42 is line 40 less line 41, carried to line 4, valued at cost (line 33a). Labor, materials and other costs (lines 37 to 39) are not in a sourcing sheet. A sold unit with no purchase in the log is named, because line 42 is short by its cost. A small business taxpayer can choose not to keep an inventory at all (Schedule C instructions, Part III); that choice is yours and your preparer's.
  • Mileage. Business trips in a trip log are priced at the IRS standard mileage rate for each trip's date (70¢ in 2025; 72.5¢ to June 30 2026 and 76¢ from July 1 2026, IRS Notice 2026-10 and Announcement 2026-11), plus business tolls and parking, and shown on Schedule C line 9, with business, commuting and other miles for Part IV. A log with no category column is read as all business; with one, anything not marked business is not deducted. The standard rate replaces actual car costs. Trips before 2025 are left out, not guessed at.
  • Not modelled: lower of cost or market, specific lots chosen by you rather than first in first out, sales tax and marketplace facilitator rules, income and self-employment tax, 1099-K differences, actual-expense car costs, packaging, your own time, off-platform sales, currency conversion, and anything not in the files.

Common questions

How do I see my real profit across eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Whatnot and Shopify together?

Download each marketplace's report (eBay Transaction report from Seller Hub, Etsy Payment Account statement, Poshmark My Sales Report, Mercari Gross Sales Report, Depop sales export, Whatnot orders export, Shopify Payments Transactions export), drop them all on this page and click Build my books. Each file is read in your browser, put into the same shape, and added into one monthly table, one comparison of what each channel keeps and one tax-preparer worksheet.

What does each marketplace's report leave out?

eBay's report carries sales, fees, shipping labels and ads but not what you paid for the item. Etsy's statement has fees but not your costs, which is why the Sold Order Items file helps. Poshmark's and Whatnot's reports show a fee and a payout. None of them carries your cost of goods, so profit needs the costs you add.

Why is profit blank for some channels?

Profit is only shown for a channel when every sale on it has a cost. Counting an uncosted sale as free would overstate profit, so the page leaves it blank and says which channel is missing costs.

Is my sales data uploaded anywhere?

No. The files are read in your browser tab and are never sent to a server. The page has no accounts and only cookieless page analytics, and a test in the repository fails if a network call is added.

Can I use the worksheet for my taxes?

It groups the totals the way a Schedule C asks for them (gross receipts, returns, advertising, commissions and fees, postage, cost of goods), so a preparer starts from organised numbers. It is arithmetic on your files, not tax advice, and where a line depends on how you treat an item the row says so.

What is in Mercari's Gross Sales Report, and what counts as my sales?

Mercari's help centre lists 20 fields, among them Item Price, Seller Shipping Fee, Mercari Selling Fee, Payment Processing Fee, Shipping Adjustment Fee and Net Seller Proceeds, plus the shipping, sales tax and fees charged to the buyer. This page books the item price of completed orders as sales and the seller-paid shipping and fees as deductions, so they add up to Net Seller Proceeds. What the buyer paid on top never reaches you and is shown separately, because a 1099-K can include some of it. Orders still in progress are left out, since their fees are estimates.

What is in Depop's sales export, and what counts as my sales?

Depop's Download sales file (Selling Hub, up to three months a file) has a row per sale with Item price, Buyer shipping cost, USPS Cost, Depop fee, Depop Payments fee, Boosting fee, Refunded to buyer amount and Fees refunded to seller, plus the sales tax and Buyer Marketplace Fee the buyer paid. This page books the item price plus buyer shipping as sales and the label, fees and boosting as deductions; refunds are returns, and fees Depop gave back reduce fees. The file has no payout per sale, so the net is computed from those columns: check it against your Depop payouts. Buyer names and addresses in the file are never read.

How do I work out opening and closing inventory for Schedule C Part III as a reseller?

Export your sourcing spreadsheet as a CSV with the item or SKU, quantity, cost and date bought, and drop it here with one tax year of marketplace reports. Each sale takes the oldest matching purchase still on hand, so what is left on December 31 is your closing inventory (line 41). Purchases in the year are line 36, less anything you marked personal; opening inventory (line 35) is last year's line 41 if you enter it, or the earlier purchases in the log. Line 42, cost of goods sold, is line 35 plus line 36 less line 41. Sales the log has no purchase for are listed, because they would make line 42 too low.

Why do eBay and Whatnot show different sales definitions?

eBay, Etsy and Shopify files record what the buyer paid, including shipping you charged. Poshmark, Mercari and Whatnot files are read at item price. Compare the share each channel keeps with that in mind; the model section lists it.
