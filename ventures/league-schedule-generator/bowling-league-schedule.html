Bowling league schedule maker: every team rotated evenly across the lane pairs

A bowling league bowls the same night every week on the same lanes, and the round robin repeats until the season ends: a 30-week season of 10 teams plays every opponent three times and a third. The part a hand-made sheet gets wrong is the lanes. Bowlers notice when one team keeps getting the pair by the wall or the pair with the dead approach, so each team should bowl on each lane pair about as often as every other pair, and not on the same pair two weeks running.

This page builds that schedule in your browser: the round robin, real dates (skipping holidays), a lane pair for every match chosen so the counts come out even, weeks held for position rounds, and a vacancy for an odd league. Nothing is uploaded.

How the lanes are chosen, and how even it gets

Who bowls whom each week is the circle-method round robin. Each week's matches are then put on pairs as an assignment problem: the price of a pair for a match is how much it would push its two teams' counts away from even, plus a little for bowling the same pair as last week. The Hungarian method solves each week exactly given every other week, the page sweeps the season until no week improves, and then re-deals a few weeks at random and sweeps again, keeping the most even season it finds.

We measured it on 36 seasons (6 to 24 teams, 26 to 34 weeks). Every team got the most even split possible in 30 of the 36; in the other 6 no team was more than two weeks off between its most- and least-used pair. A hand rotation (move every match one pair along each week) got every team even in 1 of the 36 and left some team 13 weeks off. In the 6 misses a SAT solver shows the perfect split does exist, so a better search could still close them. The numbers, and the script that reproduces them, are in the project's eval/bowling-lanes.txt and eval/bowling-lane-sat.txt.

When a schedule shows a team two off, "Try another lane deal" runs the search from a different start; the round robin itself stays the same.

Questions

How do you make a bowling league schedule?

List the teams, pick the first league night and the number of weeks, and say where your lanes start. The page plays a round robin, repeats it until the weeks run out, and puts every match on a lane pair so each team bowls on each pair as evenly as the season allows. Download it as a CSV or copy the text for the league sheet.

How many lane pairs does a bowling league need?

One pair for every two teams: an 8-team league bowls on 4 pairs (8 lanes) every week, and a 12-team league on 6 pairs. An odd league adds a vacancy, so 9 teams still use 5 pairs and one team bowls against the vacancy each week.

How do you rotate teams across lanes in a bowling league?

Count, for every team, how many weeks it has bowled on each pair, and give each week's matches the pairs that even those counts out. A team that bowls 30 weeks on 5 pairs can have each pair exactly 6 times; the page shows each team's counts and says when a team is one or two off that.

What is a position round in bowling?

A week where the pairings come from the standings that night: 1st bowls 2nd, 3rd bowls 4th, and so on. Type the week numbers and the page holds those weeks for position rounds, shows which places bowl on which pair, and carries on the round robin after them.

What happens with an odd number of teams?

The league bowls against a vacancy (also called a blind). The team drawn against it still bowls on its pair that week, and the vacancy moves round the schedule like any other team, so every team meets it equally often.

Is my team list uploaded?

No. The schedule is built in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
