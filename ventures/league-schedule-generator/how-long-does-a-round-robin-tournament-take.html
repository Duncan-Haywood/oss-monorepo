Questions

How long does a round robin tournament take?

Count the match slots, then multiply by the length of one slot. Six teams play 15 games; on 2 courts that is 5 rounds of 2 slots, so 10 slots, or 10 hours at 60 minutes a slot and 7.5 hours at 45. The table gives the slot count for 4 to 16 teams on 1 to 4 courts.

How many courts do I need to finish a round robin in one day?

Divide the slot count by the number of slots you can fit in the day. With 8-hour days of 60-minute slots, 8 teams need 28 slots on 1 court (too long), 14 on 2, 7 on 4. Adding courts helps until a round fits in one slot, which takes half the teams in courts; after that more courts change nothing.

Why is the slot count sometimes higher than games divided by courts?

Each round is played to completion before the next starts, as in the generator, so a round of 3 games on 2 courts takes 2 slots with one court idle in the second. That keeps the schedule easy to run and every team resting between rounds; a looser packing can save a slot or two but lets teams play back to back.
