Bracket size is the next power of two at or above the number of teams; each empty slot is a first-round bye, given to the top seeds. Double elimination counts the games before the grand final reset; add one game if the losers-bracket team wins the grand final. Games per team vary: with a bye a team plays one fewer game.

Questions

How many games are in a single-elimination tournament?

One fewer than the number of teams, because every game eliminates exactly one team and all but the champion go out: 12 teams, 11 games. Byes do not add games.

How many rounds does a bracket with byes have?

The number of rounds is the exponent of the next power of two: 5 to 8 teams is 3 rounds, 9 to 16 is 4, 17 to 32 is 5. The extra slots become first-round byes for the top seeds.

How many games in a double-elimination bracket?

Two fewer than twice the number of teams (12 teams: 22), plus one more game only if the team from the losers bracket wins the grand final and forces the reset.
