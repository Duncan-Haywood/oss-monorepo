Questions

What is a round robin schedule template?

A fixture list for a set number of teams, labelled by team number, in which every team plays every other team once. Replace the numbers with your team names: Team 1 is the first name on your list, Team 2 the second, and so on. Home is listed first.

Why does the 8 team round robin have 7 rounds?

In each round every team plays once, and each team has 7 opponents, so 7 rounds. With 8 teams there are 4 games per round and 28 games in total.

What do I do with an odd number of teams?

One team sits out each round (a bye), so the schedule has as many rounds as teams. The 5, 7, 9 and 11 team tables below show the team with the bye in each round; every team gets exactly one.

Can I use these for a double round robin?

Yes. Play the same rounds again with home and away reversed. The generator does this for you with the double round robin option, and also puts real dates, courts and time slots on the rounds.
