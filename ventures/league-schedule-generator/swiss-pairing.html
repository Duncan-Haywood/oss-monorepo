Free Swiss pairing program, in your browser

Pair a chess club's Swiss tournament from a phone or laptop: FIDE Dutch-system pairings round by round, results entered board by board, standings with Buchholz and Sonneborn-Berger, and FIDE TRF files in and out. Nothing to install and nothing uploaded. Also works for draughts, Scrabble, go, card and board-game clubs that play Swiss.

Other lines it reads: Ben forfeits to Ana, Cy half bye (asked for, written before pairing the round), Cy absent, Dee out (left the event).

Check a round an arbiter paired

Open a TRF that includes the round (from Swiss-Manager, Vega, chess-results or this page). The round is re-paired from the rounds before it by the Dutch rules and compared board by board. If they differ, each earlier result near the difference is read the other two ways in turn; when one of them gives the arbiter's boards exactly, the round was probably paired before that result was entered or corrected. Read on this device, never uploaded.

Why use it

Most clubs pair a Swiss with one of these. Each is good at something; this page is for the club night where the organiser has a phone, a list of names and twenty minutes.

Questions

How close is it to Swiss-Manager?

Six real over-the-board events published on chess-results.com (among them the Aeroflot Open and the Prague festival) were replayed round by round through this pairer. Given the players who actually played each round, it produced exactly the arbiters' pairings in 51 of 54 rounds, and 99.2% of the boards from round 2 on. Four of the six events match in all nine rounds. Two of the three misses are round 1, which those arbiters paired on an earlier rating list than the file carries; the third (Graz, round 4) was paired before one round-3 result was entered as it now stands: read that game as the draw the arbiters apparently had, and all 137 boards match. The replay, its code and every number are in the source (eval/swiss-vs-published.mjs, eval/swiss-big-brackets.txt).

Which pairing rules does it follow?

FIDE's Dutch system (C.04.3): score groups paired from the top, players who cannot be paired in their group float down, the top half of each group meets the bottom half, no rematches, no board where both players are owed the same colour absolutely unless every round needs one, then strong and mild colour preferences, then players who floated last round or two rounds ago are not floated the same way again. The pairing bye goes to the lowest-ranked player who can take it, once per player. Every round is checked before it is shown: everyone placed once, no rematch, no second bye.

Can I carry on an event from Swiss-Manager or Vega?

Yes. Open the event's FIDE TRF file (TRF-16) and the players and results so far are read on your device. The page can also write a TRF of its own event for a rating officer or an endorsed program.

What if a player is late, leaves or asks for a bye?

Add a line to the results box before pairing: Cy half bye for a requested half-point bye this round, Cy absent to sit out for nothing, Dee out for a withdrawal. A late joiner is added to the player list; they start on zero points.

How are ties in the standings broken?

Points, then Buchholz (the sum of your opponents' scores), then Sonneborn-Berger (full score of each opponent you beat plus half of each you drew), then starting rank.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Names, ratings and results stay on the device that typed them, saved in its own storage so a closed tab does not lose the event.
