How to put a league schedule on your website

Most clubs want the schedule on their own site, where new players and parents look first. The usual ways are a pasted table or a PDF that goes stale at the first rainout, a screenshot nobody can search, or the widget a league platform gives you on a paid plan. The hard part is not showing the schedule once. It is keeping the club page right after every result and every moved game.

This guide describes the league page in this tool's source code (server.mjs). It is no longer offered for you to run on your own server, and no hosted version is live yet, so read the steps below as how the feature works rather than something you can switch on today.

How it works

  1. Build and publish the season on your league page as usual.
  2. Copy the embed code. The organiser page has a section called "On your club's website". It holds one code for the whole league and one for each team, for teams with their own page.
  3. Paste it into your site. Wix: Add, Embed code. Squarespace: a Code block. WordPress: a Custom HTML block. Google Sites: Insert, Embed, Embed code.
  4. Post results as usual. Scores you approve, captain reports that match, rainouts and moved games all reach the club page by themselves.

What visitors see

SectionWhat it shows
Next gamesThe next 3 match days (up to 20), with times, both teams and the court; a team's bye nights when it shows one team
Latest resultsThe newest 5 results (up to 20), winners in bold, forfeits marked
StandingsThe full table: played, won, drawn, lost, difference and points, the followed team highlighted
Season (optional)Every round of the season, past and future

Add options to the address in the code: &show=next,results,table,season picks the sections and their order, &team=Hawks follows one team, &accent=c8102e uses your club colour, &theme=dark matches a dark site, and &title=Spring%20League adds a heading. The embed grows to fit its content, so there is no box inside a box to scroll on a phone.

Why it has its own key

The player link can send things: scores for the organiser to approve, who is in for Thursday, sub offers, waiver signatures. A website's source is readable by anyone, so the embed uses a separate key that can only read. Pasting it on a public page shows the schedule, which is the point, and nothing more. The embed page itself sends nothing, loads nothing from other sites and contains no forms.

What it does not do

It does not host your league: the embed needs your league server running. It does not show availability, rosters, contacts, sportsmanship comments or anything else the organiser page keeps private. If you want registration, payments and an app for every team in one paid product, a league platform such as TeamSnap, LeagueApps or SportsEngine is the right tool.

Questions

Can I put my league schedule on my club website for free?

Not with this tool today: its league page (server.mjs) does this, but it is no longer offered to run yourself and no hosted version is live yet. In that page: The organiser page gives you a short embed code: an iframe and four lines of script. Paste it into your site builder's embed block and the schedule, latest results and standings show on your own page.

Does the embedded schedule update by itself?

Yes. The embed is served by your league server, so a result you post or a game you move shows on the club website within a minute, with no re-pasting. A schedule pasted as a table or an image would have to be redone after every change.

Is it safe to put the embed code in public page source?

The embed carries its own read-only key, separate from the player link. Anyone who copies it can read the schedule, which the embed already shows, but cannot send a score, mark availability or sign anything. Changing ADMIN_KEY retires every pasted copy.

Which website builders does it work with?

Any builder with an HTML or embed block: Wix (Embed code), Squarespace (Code block), WordPress (Custom HTML), Google Sites (Embed) and hand-made sites. Builders that strip scripts still show the schedule, in a fixed 600 pixel box that scrolls.
