Practice slots: practice.mjs's search against the least possible score (CP-SAT, OR-Tools 9.15,
20 s and 4 workers a case). 2026-11-21.

  node eval/practice-exact.mjs 60 1 tight > tight.json    # demand 75-105% of field capacity
  node eval/practice-exact.mjs 40 2 loose > loose.json    # demand 40-70%
  python3 eval/practice-exact.py tight.json 20            # pip install ortools

Random leagues: 2-4 fields (1, 2 or 4 units), Mon-Thu windows with some Fri and Sat mornings,
8-40 teams (1, 2 or 4 units up to the biggest field; 1 or 2 practices a week), half with preferred
days, 40% a preferred time range, 30% a no-day, 30% Saturday games, early finishes for half the
small teams, a quarter of teams sharing a coach with the team before. Both sides use the options
practiceOptions builds, so the rules are identical; the score is sum over teams of
10000 x unplaced + misses^2.

== How the search got there (same rules, earlier generators)

- First version (single eviction, one cycle, ~60x fewer iterations), first generator's 40 tight
  cases: 25 of 35 proved optima matched, 14 cases worse (gaps 1 to 10,000: a practice left out
  that could be placed). Search time median 19 ms.
- Occupancy lists, eviction chains, four reheats, 60 tight cases of the shipped generator: 54 of
  55. The miss (case 14) left a half-field team out because one eviction freed one quarter.
- Evicting holders until the block fits fixed it in one of four seeds; three restarts: 55 of 55.
- Large overloaded leagues (eval-style 200 teams on 13 parks): 10.5 s with three restarts at the
  old iteration cap, 1.8 s with one restart above 80 practices and an 800,000 cap. The page runs
  the search in a worker (practice-worker.mjs) so the tab stays responsive.

== Every other week (2026-11-21): teams that take a slot on A weeks or B weeks

  node eval/practice-exact.mjs 60 1 tight alt > alt-tight.json
  node eval/practice-exact.mjs 40 2 loose alt > alt-loose.json
  python3 eval/practice-exact.py alt-tight.json 20

Same generator, plus: a third of the teams alternate (the search picks A or B), 3% fixed to A weeks
and 2% to B weeks; an alternating practice counts as half in the demand, and up to 60 teams. CP-SAT
model: capacity per block in A weeks and in B weeks, a team's practices all in one set of weeks (a
boolean per alternating team), coach clashes only between options whose weeks meet. Search as shipped
(the whole-team flip move 1 in 10 for alternating teams with 2+ practices).

83 of 84 proved optima matched with alternating teams. The miss (case 0: 19 teams, 6 alternating,
35 practices, one practice unplaced either way) is a 9 vs 4 miss on one weekly team whose coach also
coaches an alternating team: the better grid moves that alternating team from B weeks to A weeks and
to different blocks at the same time. Seeds 2, 6 and 7 find it (10064), seeds 1, 3, 4, 5, 8 do not;
six restarts or 2,000,000 iterations find it from seed 1. Tried and not kept (no change across 10
seeds): a flip that rehouses each practice at its best open block in the other weeks, and flip rates
of 0.05, 0.25 and 0.4. Next for this: a parity swap between two alternating teams that share blocks,
judged on fresh cases.
