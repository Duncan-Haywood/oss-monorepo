The earlier 41-team attempt (two seeds sharing cores) sat at 1 of 14 nights for its whole run with no
step accepted. Two changes to the script came first:
  - A repair keeps the layouts the solver found for the nights it marked good. Before, each freed night
    was solved again by night_rows under a 20 s limit, and a step was thrown away when that second solve
    timed out; at 41 teams (60 matches a night) it often did.
  - --save STATE writes the round robin and its good nights' layouts after every accepted step, and
    --resume STATE starts from that file, re-checking each stored layout match by match (night_ok).
    A night with no stored layout gets a longer first check (--resume-check, 180 s). The first resumes
    in this session had neither (the state file then held rounds only) and re-checked nights under
    20 s: 11 good nights came back as 9 or 10. With the 180 s check, 12 came back as 12.
