Referee crews for a weekend tournament: tournament-referees.mjs against the least possible number
of empty places (CP-SAT, OR-Tools 9.15, 20 s and 4 workers a case). 2026-11-20.

  node eval/referee-exact.mjs 60 1 > cases.json            # short-handed: 3 to 6 places an official
  node eval/referee-exact.mjs 60 2 staffed > cases.json    # well staffed: 2 to 3.5 places an official
  python3 eval/referee-exact.py cases.json                 # pip install ortools

Random weekends: 2 days, 3-5 fields, 2-4 divisions of 5-9 teams (pools of 3 or 4, top 2 or 4),
60% with a 3-official crew in one division. Random officials: half with a club, hours (all
weekend, Saturday morning + Sunday, Saturday afternoon, or Sunday only), 40% a daily cap, 30% a
cap in a row, 20% assistant only, some limited to two divisions, 15% playing for a team.
Rules in the exact model: per-slot eligibility from validateReferees (hours, divisions, roles,
own club, own team playing), no overlap, the turnover between fields, daily and weekend caps,
games in a row (a gap under 30 minutes keeps a run going; every chain of cap+1 games enumerated).
A case counts as proved when CP-SAT proves it in 20 s, or when its best answer leaves no place
empty (nothing can be lower). Knockout club risk is a preference, not a rule, so it is not in the
objective; it is counted.
