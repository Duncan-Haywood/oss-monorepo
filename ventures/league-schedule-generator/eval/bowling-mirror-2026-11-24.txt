# The symmetry. roundRobin(n) round r plays {r+k, r-k} (mod n-1) for k = 1..P-1, and {r, n-1}. The map
# t -> W-1-t (mod n-1), n-1 fixed, sends round r to round W-1-r (and, past n-1 weeks, week w to week
# W-1-w, since rounds repeat mod n-1), match k to match k. --mirror asks for answers that map onto
# themselves with pair p sent to pi(p) = p xor 1 (P odd: the last pair fixed). It replaces fixing week 1,
# which would clash with a fixed pi (with both, 22 x 10 was INFEASIBLE in 0.0 s).
# Odd W cannot use it: the middle round is its own image, which forces pi = identity, and the fixed team
# then has an even count on every pair but one. 22 x 33 (every pair 3 times): INFEASIBLE in 0.0 s.
#
