How to run an Americano: rotating partners for padel, pickleball and badminton nights

An Americano is a social doubles format where everyone plays with and against as many different people as possible. Nobody needs a fixed partner, so it suits club nights, open mornings and mixed-level groups. Each round you get a new partner, and your own points count towards your own total.

A worked example: 8 players, 2 courts, 7 rounds

Eight invented players on two courts play 7 rounds. Everyone plays every round, so there are no sit-outs. Every player partners each of the other seven players exactly once and plays against each of them exactly twice, so nobody spends the night facing the same strong pair.

Partner everyone once, face everyone twice

A rotation where every pair partners exactly once and meets as opponents exactly twice is called a whist tournament (the card-game clubs worked them out first). They exist for every group of 4k or 4k+1 players, and the builder carries a stored one for 4, 5, 8, 9, 12, 13, 16, 17, 20, 21, 24, 25, 28, 29, 32, 33, 36, 37, 40, 41, 44, 45, 48, 49, 52, 53, 56, 57, 60, 61 and 64 players (every size of that form up to 64). With one court per four players, that is the plan you get: 16 players on 4 courts play 15 rounds and everyone partners all 15 others once.

For 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27 and most larger 4k+3 groups (up to 63, except 51) the builder plays the next design up with one empty seat: the table holding the empty seat sits out that round. Partners still never repeat, everyone plays the same number of games, and every pair meets as opponents once or twice. For 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26 and up (4k+2 players) a full whist design is impossible: the number of pairs is odd, and every game uses two partnerships. The builder plays the closest thing instead, a near-whist design: nobody partners anyone twice, each player misses exactly one partner and faces that player twice, and every other pair meets as opponents once or twice. Two players sit out each round and everyone sits out exactly twice. It is stored for 6 to 54 players (every 4k+2 size) and 62. With 10 players on 2 courts the round-by-round search repeated 2 partnerships and left some pairs never meeting; the near-whist design repeats none and every pair meets.

With fewer courts than tables (say 20 players on 3 courts) the whole design still works, it just takes more rounds: 32 instead of 19. The builder tells you that number; type it into Rounds to get it. A shorter night uses the round-by-round search, which on a short night does as well.

Questions

How many rounds does an Americano have?

With 8 players and 2 courts, 7 rounds lets every player partner every other player exactly once. In general, with n players the full cycle is n - 1 rounds when n is a multiple of 4; shorter nights simply stop earlier, and the tool lets you pick any number of rounds.

Can everyone partner everyone once and play against everyone twice?

Yes, for 4k or 4k+1 players (8, 9, 12, 13, 16, 17 and so on): that rotation is called a whist tournament, and the builder uses a stored one (every such size up to 64) when there is a court for every four players. With 20 players on 5 courts the round-by-round search repeats 2 partners and some pairs meet four times; the whist design repeats none and every pair meets exactly twice.

What if the number of players is not a multiple of 4?

Each court needs 4 players, so the extras sit out. The tool shares sit-outs so nobody sits out more than one round more than anyone else, and it shares games the same way.

How do you score an Americano?

Each player keeps the points their side scored in every game, and the night's winner is the player with the most points. Games are usually played to a fixed score such as 21 or 24, or for a fixed time. The rotation builder makes the sheet; the Americano scorer page keeps the score on the night, with a live leaderboard and sit-out credit. Neither rates anyone.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

In an Americano the pairings are fixed in advance so everyone partners as many different people as possible. In a Mexicano, each round after the first is drawn from the standings, so players of similar level meet. The tool has both: Build rotation for Americano and Draw next round for Mexicano.
