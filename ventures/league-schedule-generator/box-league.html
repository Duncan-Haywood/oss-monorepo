How it works

    
What is a box league?

    
Players are ranked and split into small boxes of four to six. Everyone in a box plays everyone else once over a few weeks, arranging the matches themselves. When the round closes, the top of each box moves up a box and the bottom moves down, and a new round starts. Over a season the boxes sort themselves by level, so most matches are close.

    
How are box league points scored?

    
The page defaults to 3 points for a win and 1 for playing and losing, with a walkover worth 3 to the winner and 0 to the other player. You can change each value and add a bonus point per game won. Ties on points go to the head-to-head result when two players are level, then game difference, then games won, then the order the box started in. When a tie sits on a promotion or relegation line, the page says how it was decided so you can overrule it.

    
What happens when players join or leave?

    
Before closing a round, list who is leaving and who is joining. A new player goes to the foot of the bottom box, or of any box you name. The page then re-splits the ranked list so every box stays within one player of the target size. Every move is listed with its reason, ready for the group chat.

    
Is anything uploaded?

    
No. Names and results stay on the device that typed them. A share link carries the league inside the link itself; nothing is stored on a server.
