Common questions

How do I schedule several divisions on the same courts?

List each division on its own line as Division: team, team, team. Name the shared courts and time slots, pick the start date and play days, and click Build. Each division keeps its own fair round robin; the page then decides which divisions play on which night and gives every match a court and a time without booking any court twice.

What if the same coach or player is on two teams?

Add a link such as U10/Hawks, U12/Hawks. Linked teams are kept out of the same time slot on a night, and out of back-to-back slots at different venues. If a clash cannot be avoided, it is listed under Conflicts rather than hidden.

Can courts be at different venues?

Yes. Write a court as Field 1 @ Park. Each team plays at the venue it used last time where it can, so families make fewer trips between the park and the school gym.

Does it handle a closed court or a holiday?

Yes. Skip dates remove a night for everyone, and court closures such as Gym A: 2026-12-03 take one court out on that date; the matches move to the other courts.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser, nothing is sent to a server, and there are no accounts.
