How it is built

Pairings: each division keeps its own circle-method round robin, so every pair meets the right number of times and home and away stay balanced. Only the dates, courts and times are decided across divisions.

Shared courts: one pass goes night by night, choosing which divisions play and giving each match a court and time. No court is booked twice and no closed court is used.

Linked teams: teams that share a coach or player are kept out of the same time slot. If that cannot be done, the clash is listed above the schedule, never hidden.

Checked: a separate validator re-reads the finished schedule and confirms every pair meets the right number of times, no court is double-booked and the conflict list matches a recount. It cannot tell whether the schedule suits your league's own rules.

Common questions

How do I schedule several divisions that share the same courts?

Type one division per line as Name: team, team, team, list the shared courts and time slots, and pick the first date and weekdays. One pass decides which divisions play on which night and gives every match a court and a time, so no court is booked twice and no closed court is used.

What if one coach or player is on two teams?

List them under teams that share a coach or players, for example U10/Hawks, U12/Hawks. The scheduler keeps those teams out of the same time slot. If it cannot, it still builds the schedule and lists every unavoidable clash instead of hiding it.

Can courts be at different venues?

Yes. Write a court as Field 1 @ Park. Teams are kept at the same venue from one night to the next where they can be, which cuts the number of venue changes.

Is my team list uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser from what you type, nothing is sent to a server, there are no accounts, and it works offline once the page has loaded.
