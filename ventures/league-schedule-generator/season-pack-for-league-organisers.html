A season pack you can hand out

Most leagues send a spreadsheet link and hope. The season pack turns the schedule you already built into something that looks like your league: a cover page with your name, logo and colour, the full schedule, one page per team with their own games and home or away marked, and a notes page for rules. Print it or save it as a PDF.

What costs money and what does not

The schedule, every export and the calendar files are free and stay free. Only the branded pack is a paid extra, planned as a one-off price per season rather than a monthly plan. It is not on sale yet: there is no checkout, so nothing here can charge you. The sample is an invented league.

Who it is for

Volunteer organisers, rec departments and club secretaries who run one or several divisions and want a clean handout. Nothing you type is uploaded; the pack is built in your browser. The code is MIT licensed.
