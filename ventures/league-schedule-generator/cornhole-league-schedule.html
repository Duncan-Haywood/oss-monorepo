Cornhole league schedule: lanes, start times and holiday weeks

Bar and backyard cornhole leagues run on a few boards and a few start times, and the weeks around a holiday are always short. A round robin settles who plays whom; the lanes, start times and skipped nights settle when. This guide works one season through.

A worked example: 8 teams, 2 lanes, 2 start times, 1 skipped week

Questions

How do you make a cornhole league schedule?

List the teams, pick the weeknight, list the lanes and start times, and add any nights the bar or park is unavailable as skip dates. The tool plays a round robin so every team meets every other team once, and gives each game a lane and start time.

How many lanes does an 8-team cornhole league need?

Eight teams play 4 games a round. Two lanes at two start times (18:30 and 19:30) give exactly four slots, so every team plays once each night.

What happens on a holiday week with no play?

Add the date as a skip date. The season simply runs on the following Wednesday. In the example 1 July is skipped, so Round 5 is on 8 July and no game is lost.

Does an 8-team cornhole league need byes?

No. An even number of teams all play every round. With seven or nine teams one team sits out each round.

Is my team list uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser; team names are never sent to a server.
