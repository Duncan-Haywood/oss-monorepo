Split a league into divisions by location

For regional leagues whose teams play at their own grounds: youth soccer across towns, a county darts or pool league, a school conference. Every team visits every division rival, so who is in which division decides most of a season's driving. Paste each team's home ground, choose how many divisions, and get divisions of even size with the least total distance between rivals, a club's teams kept in different divisions, and the saving against the split you use now. Then send the divisions straight to the multi-division scheduler. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

    
The page measures the straight-line (great-circle) distance between every pair of grounds and looks for the split into divisions of even size, within one team of each other, where the distances between teams in the same division add up to the least. That total is what changes from one split to another: in a single round robin each pair meets once, so one side drives there and back; in a double, both do. Road distance is roughly 1.2 to 1.4 times the straight line, so the saving in percent carries over.

    
Finding the very best split is a hard search (there are about 59 billion billion ways to split 32 teams into 8 divisions of 4). The page runs a local search from 64 starts, each improved by swapping teams between divisions until no swap helps, then shaken and improved again, and tells you how many starts reached the split it shows. It was checked against every possible split of 60 random leagues of 8 to 12 teams and matched each one, and against every split of each NFL conference (2.6 million each).

    
Where do I get the coordinates?

    
In most map apps, right-click (or long-press) the ground and copy the two numbers it shows, latitude first. A town centre is close enough if the ground is in town.

    
What it does not do

    
It does not balance team strength or use drive times from a road map; if one ground is across a river with one bridge, list it in the keep-apart box against the teams it should not join, or adjust by hand. Use it as a starting proposal for the league meeting.
