Chess club round robin schedule for seven players: boards, byes and pairings

A club ladder or ratings-free round robin is the simplest fair format for a chess club: everyone plays everyone once, nobody is knocked out, and the final table needs no tie-break tournament. The snag is the odd number. Seven players means one person sits out each week, and the organiser has to make sure it is a different person each time. This guide works one season through: 7 players, Monday evenings, 3 boards, 7 weeks.

Questions

How do you make a round robin for seven chess players?

List the seven players, pick the club night and give the number of boards. With an odd number of players one is paired with nobody each week, so the tool plays seven weeks of three games and gives every player exactly one bye.

How many weeks does a chess club round robin take?

For an odd number of players it takes as many weeks as there are players, so seven players need seven weeks. For an even number it takes one week fewer than the number of players.

Who gets White in a round robin?

The tool alternates colours so each player has White and Black the same number of times, give or take one. In the seven-player example everyone has three games with White and three with Black.

What if a player cannot come one week?

Add the player and the date under blackout dates. Only that game moves to a makeup night after the main rounds, and the rest of the evening goes ahead.

How do you rank a chess club round robin?

Score 1 for a win, one half for a draw and 0 for a loss. In the tool you can use 2-1-0 points and break ties on head-to-head results, and type the results in to see the standings update.
