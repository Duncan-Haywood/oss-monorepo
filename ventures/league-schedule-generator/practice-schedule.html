Practice schedule maker for shared fields

For youth soccer, baseball, softball, flag football and rec basketball leagues that hand out weekly practice slots. List your fields with the hours you have them and how many teams each holds at once, list your teams with what they need, and get a weekly practice grid that overfills no field, double-books no coach and shares the good slots fairly. Then a calendar for every team. No accounts, nothing uploaded.

Opened from a share link. This is the same timetable the sender built; change anything and build again to make your own.

"Teams at once" is the field's capacity in units: a field split into quarters is 4, into halves 2, a gym or a diamond 1. Separate the parts with |, tabs (paste from a spreadsheet) or commas.

How it works

How do I share practice fields between teams?

List each field with the days and hours you have it and how many teams it holds at once (a field split into quarters holds 4). List each team with how much of a field it needs and how many practices a week. The page cuts the hours into practice-length blocks and gives every team its slots without overfilling a field, double-booking a coach, using a team's game day, or keeping the youngest out past their finish time. A team with two practices gets them on different days, and on days apart where it can.

How does it decide who gets the good slots?

Each team counts a miss for a practice off its preferred days, one outside its preferred times, and a pair of practices on consecutive days. The search keeps the sum of the squared misses as low as it can, so two teams missing once is better than one team missing twice. Nobody gets the good slots for asking first, and the same lists always give the same timetable ("Try another timetable" draws a different one of equal or near-equal quality).

What does "proved" mean?

For each team the page also works out the best week it could have with the fields to itself. When every team gets that, no other timetable could be better, and the page says so. Otherwise it lists which teams missed what, and for a team it could not place, why: no window that fits its hours, too few days, or full slots and who holds them.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything is worked out in your browser. A share link carries your lists inside the link itself and rebuilds the same timetable on the other device; nothing is stored on a server.
