How to schedule a round robin with an odd number of teams, with a worked example

With 6 or 8 teams everyone plays every round. With 5, 7 or 9 someone has to sit out each round, and doing that by hand usually ends with one team idle twice while another never rests. The fix is the circle method: add an imaginary extra team, schedule as if it were real, and whoever is paired with it has the bye.

A worked example: 5 teams, 5 rounds, 10 games

Five invented teams play a single round robin. Each round has two games and one team on a bye, and every team has its bye in a different round. Home team is listed first.

Questions

How many rounds does a round robin with an odd number of teams have?

As many as there are teams: 5 teams need 5 rounds, 7 teams need 7. An even number of teams needs one fewer round than teams, because nobody sits out; an odd number adds a dummy team, and whoever is paired with it has the bye.

How many byes does each team get?

Exactly one in a single round robin, whatever the size. With 5 teams that is one idle round each across 5 rounds, with two games played in every round.

How many games are played in total?

n × (n − 1) ÷ 2, odd or even. Five teams play 10 games and seven teams play 21, because each team plays every other team once: 4 games each for 5 teams.

Is a bye fair when teams play on different dates?

Only if the bye falls in a different round for each team, which the circle method guarantees. If a team needs a particular week off, give them the round where they have the bye by choosing which team takes which line of the list before dates are set.

What do I do for a double round robin with an odd number of teams?

Repeat the same rounds with home and away reversed. Each team then has two byes, one in each half, and meets every opponent twice.
