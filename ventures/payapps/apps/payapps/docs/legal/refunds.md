# Refund Policy

**Version 2026-11-26** · Maris Stella Labs LLC

## Try it free first

PayApps has a free plan for one active job, with no card required, so you can build a real pay application before you pay.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

If a paid plan is not right for your company, you can request a **full refund within 30 days** of your first paid subscription payment.

## How to request a refund

- Contact us, or reply to the receipt from our merchant of record (Paddle). Tell us which company the subscription is for.
- Refunds are issued through the merchant of record to the original payment method, including any sales tax charged.
- Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

## After the first 30 days

- You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep the paid plan until the end of the period you paid for, then the free plan's limits apply. Everything you created stays readable and printable.
- Renewals after the first 30 days are generally not refundable, except where required by law or in cases of billing error or an extended outage, which we will review in good faith.

## Plan changes

When you change plans, any credit or charge is handled as shown at checkout.
