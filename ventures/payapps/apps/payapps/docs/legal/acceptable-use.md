# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 2026-11-26** · Maris Stella Labs LLC

PayApps exists to help contractors bill accurately and get paid for the work they did. You agree not to use it to do any of the following.

## Honest billing

- Prepare applications, change orders or waivers that are **false or inflated**, such as billing for work not performed or materials not stored, or misstating retainage or payments received.
- Prepare a document for a company you are not authorized to represent, or impersonate another person or company.
- Present our application summary or continuation sheet as an AIA Contract Documents® form.

## Other people's data

- Enter personal information about others unless you have the right to share it with us for this purpose.
- Invite people to your company who should not see its billing records.

## The Service

- Attempt to access another company's data, probe for vulnerabilities, or bypass access controls or plan limits.
- Overload the Service, or use it to send spam (including invitation emails to people who did not ask for them).
- Resell or share access outside your company without our agreement.

## Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy, with notice where practical. Report misuse or security issues to us.
