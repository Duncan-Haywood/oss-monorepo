What billing day looks like for a lot of subs

If you bill general contractors on a schedule of values, this list will be familiar. Every item is a place for a number to go wrong, and a wrong number can mean the pay app comes back and payment slips to the next cycle.

Schedule of values

Percent complete on every line, work completed this period, materials stored, and totals that must match the GC's copy of your SOV to the cent.

Retainage

Withheld on every line, sometimes at a different rate on stored materials or after a project milestone, depending on the contract. Tracking what is owed back across jobs is its own spreadsheet.

Change orders

Approved changes become new SOV lines; pending ones usually can't be billed yet. Keeping both lists straight with the GC is a monthly back-and-forth.

Lien waivers

A conditional waiver for this payment, an unconditional one for the last, sometimes on a form your state prescribes, sometimes notarized, and the same again from your suppliers.

GC portals

Every general contractor has its own portal, cut-off date, required attachments and format. The numbers get typed in again, once per GC.

QuickBooks

Then the same amounts go into your accounting system as invoices, with retainage receivable tracked separately, so the books match what you billed.

How PayApps works

  1. Set up a job once. Import the schedule of values from your own spreadsheet or the PDF bid breakdown, with the contract sum, retainage rate and pay terms. Already billing the job? Start from your last application.
  2. Update progress each month. Work this period and stored materials per line; the continuation-sheet math, retainage and "less previous certificates" are calculated for you.
  3. Send it with its backup. The application and continuation sheet as PDF and CSV for the GC's portal or email, the waiver it needs, and the backup attached.
  4. Get paid and keep the books straight. Record each check, see what is past due across every job, send the invoice and payment to QuickBooks Online, and bill the retainage when the work is done, with the punch items and close-out documents it still waits on named job by job.

All four steps work in the app today. The features page marks what is built and what comes next.

Who is PayApps for?

Subcontractors and specialty trades who bill general contractors every month with a schedule of values, retainage and lien waivers: electrical, mechanical, plumbing, framing, drywall, concrete, roofing and similar trades.

Does it replace my GC's billing portal?

No. Your GC decides how pay apps are submitted. It keeps your numbers and backup in one place and gives you the PDF and CSV, so the portal step is copying finished figures, not redoing them. For Procore, Oracle Textura, GCPay and Trimble Pay it also fills the SOV and billing files those portals take; you still submit them in the portal yourself.
