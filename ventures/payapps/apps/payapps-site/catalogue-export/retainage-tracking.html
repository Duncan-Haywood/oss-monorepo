Retainage tracking, held and released

Set each job's retainage rate from your subcontract, separately for work and stored materials. Every pay app applies it, the cash report totals what is held across jobs and since when, and a finished job still holding retainage is flagged.

Start free on one job

What it does

Retainage terms come from your contract, not from us. You set the rate per job, separately for work and for stored materials, and every pay app applies it.

  • Release a share of what is held (say 50% at substantial completion, or 100% on the final billing) or the dollar amount the GC approved, across all lines, the finished lines or the ones you pick; it is split by what each line holds and totals to the cent
  • Retainage held to date, per job and across all jobs, and since when
  • Jobs whose work is complete with retainage still held, flagged so you bill it

Plans

Every plan, including Free on one job. See pricing; every module works on the same jobs, so nothing is entered twice.
