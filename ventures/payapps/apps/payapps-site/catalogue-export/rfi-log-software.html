RFIs, and the extra work they lead to

Number and log each request for information you send the GC, print it as a PDF with a response box, and record the answer. An answered RFI that changes the price or the time with no change order linked is flagged, so the extra work gets billed.

What it does

Log each request for information you send the GC on a job: the question, the sheet or spec section it is about, your proposed solution, the date sent and the date you need an answer by. Each one prints as a PDF to attach, with a blank box for the response, and the answer is recorded with who gave it and when.

  • Numbered per job, with open, overdue and answered at a glance, how many days each has waited and how long answers take on that job
  • Mark whether an answer changes the price or the contract time; an answered RFI that does, with no change order linked, is flagged so the extra work gets priced and billed
  • An email draft to the GC's project manager to send an RFI, or to chase one past its response date; you send it from your own mail
  • The whole log as a CSV, every plan
