What this site stores

Nothing that identifies you. There is no form on these pages, and they set no cookies and run no advertising scripts. They are served from the catalogue at marisstella.app, whose script counts page loads without cookies: one number per page per day, and, when you arrive from another site, which kind of site it was (a search engine, an AI assistant, a forum), worked out in your browser. It stores no IP address, browser details, referring address or identifier. The host that delivers the pages (Cloudflare, in the United States) handles your IP address to send them, as every web host does.
