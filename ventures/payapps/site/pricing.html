Questions about pricing

Can I buy a paid plan today?

Not yet. The Free plan is open now with no card and no time limit. These are the prices the app is set up to charge when paid plans go on sale; if they change before then we will say so here first, and you choose whether to subscribe.

What is an active job?

A job you haven't closed. When a job is finished, close it and its slot frees up; its pay apps, waivers and files stay in your account to read and download on every plan.

What happens if I downgrade or stop paying?

Nothing is hidden or deleted. You can still read, export and download everything on every job. Starting new pay apps or jobs past the Free limits needs a paid plan again.

Why price by jobs and not by contract value?

Our costs grow with the number of jobs we help you set up and bill, not with their dollar value, and a flat monthly price is easier to plan for than a percentage of what you bill.

Is there an annual price?

Yes: each paid plan is also offered yearly at 10% off twelve months.

How were these prices set?

Cost-plus: each paid plan is priced at 1.8 to 2 times what it costs us to serve a customer who uses all of it. Most of that cost is support time, helping set up schedules of values and answering why a total doesn't match the GC's; the rest is hosting, file storage and payment fees.
