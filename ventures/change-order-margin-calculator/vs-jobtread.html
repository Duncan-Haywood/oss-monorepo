This calculator vs. JobTread

Two different shapes: JobTread is an account-based platform for running a whole construction project, change orders included. This page is a free, private, no-account worksheet for one number — whether a single change order's proposed price actually clears your cost floor and target margin.

What each tool is for

JobTread is a construction estimating and project-management platform headquartered in Dallas, Texas. It is built around running a job end to end — proposals and estimating, budgets, purchase orders, client communication, financial tracking, and change orders, all tied to one project record that a builder, remodeler, or specialty contractor and their client both work from over the life of the job. JobTread's own site states its platform is used by thousands of construction businesses; that is a claim from JobTread, not something this session can independently confirm. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across projects, not a one-off calculator. JobTread's own pricing and current plan details are not reachable from this session to confirm, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not estimate a whole job, track a budget across trades, or manage a client portal. It takes one change order's added labor hours, loaded wage, materials, subcontractor cost, schedule delay, overhead, target margin, and contract markup cap, and turns that into a walk-away cost floor, a target price, and — if you have one — a check of whether your proposed price clears both, plus a plain-text approval summary. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or JobTread?

They do different jobs. JobTread is a construction estimating and project-management platform headquartered in Dallas, Texas, used by contractors to run a job end to end: proposals, budgets, purchase orders, client communication, financial tracking, and change orders, all tied to one project record across the life of the job. It is an account-based platform you sign up for and use on an ongoing basis. This calculator does none of that. It takes one change order's added labor, materials, subcontractor cost, schedule delay, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into a walk-away cost floor, a target price, and a check against your contract's markup cap, instantly, in the browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A contractor who needs to run an entire job's estimating, budget, and change-order workflow in one system is better served by JobTread or a comparable platform; a contractor who wants an instant, private read on whether one change order's proposed price actually clears cost and margin, plus an approval summary to send, is in the right place here.
