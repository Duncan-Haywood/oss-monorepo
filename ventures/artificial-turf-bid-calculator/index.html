FOR TURF AND LANDSCAPE CONTRACTORS

Bid the type, the waste, and the extra work.

Price an artificial turf install the way the trade actually loses money: landscape versus pet versus putting-green, turf you have to buy after 15-foot-roll waste, crushed-rock base tons, sod demo, infill, seams, and tree wells. 600 square feet of landscape turf is not the same bid as 800 square feet of pet turf that has to come out of living grass. You get the price, the rolls, the extra-work share, and the walk-away number below which the job loses money.
