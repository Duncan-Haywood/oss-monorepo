# Ice Cream Shop Profit Calculator

Free, private browser tool for people planning or running a scoop shop or a seasonal ice cream stand: scoops per day by weekday and weekend, a hot season and an off season, a ticket built from cones, cups, shakes, toppings and pints, cost of goods, crew, rent, build-out and a loan become break-even scoops per day, cost of goods, labor and prime cost, operating profit, yield on start-up cost, the scoop price for a target yield, a month-by-month cash table with the lowest point and payback month, and an equity view.

After using this, you know how many scoops a day the shop must sell to cover its costs and loan, what share of sales goes to cost of goods and labor, and how much cash to hold through ramp-up and the off season.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/ice-cream-shop-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
