# Grant Indirect Cost Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/grant-indirect-cost-calculator/>

**Find your modified total direct costs (MTDC), the indirect cost you can charge, and the direct budget that fits under a funder's cap.** Enter salaries, fringe, supplies, travel, services, equipment and subawards. The tool applies the U.S. federal Uniform Guidance exclusions (equipment of $10,000 or more per unit, tuition remission, participant support, rental, patient care, and each subaward above its first $50,000), then charges 15% de minimis or your own rate on MTDC or total direct costs. Browser page, no install.

After using this, you can state your effective indirect rate on direct costs and trim a budget to a funder's total cap without guessing, which used to take a spreadsheet and a re-read of 2 CFR 200.

## Your numbers, not grant advice

Every amount is a number you type. Thresholds are editable because foundations, states and agencies set their own bases and caps. Your funder's notice, your negotiated rate agreement and your grants office decide what is allowed.

## How it computes

MTDC = included direct costs (salaries, fringe, supplies, travel, services, other, equipment under the threshold, first $50,000 of each subaward). Indirect = rate x base (MTDC or total direct costs). Award-cap fit: max direct = cap / (1 + rate x base share). The effective rate on direct costs is indirect / total direct costs.

## What it is not

Not grant, accounting, tax or legal advice, and no warranty. Federal definition as of the October 1, 2024 revision of 2 CFR 200.

## Privacy

No network call, no account, no analytics vendor. Anonymous feature counts (`calculate_run`, `example_loaded`) live in local storage only and can be switched off on the page; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured.
