Common questions

What is MTDC and why is it not just my direct costs?

Modified total direct costs are your direct costs minus items the federal Uniform Guidance excludes from the indirect-cost base: equipment of $10,000 or more per unit, tuition remission, participant support costs, rental costs, patient care charges, scholarships and fellowships, and the part of each subaward above the first $50,000. Indirect is a percent of that smaller base, so your effective rate on total direct costs is lower than the nominal rate.

What is the de minimis indirect cost rate?

An organisation that has never had a negotiated indirect cost rate agreement may elect a de minimis rate of up to 15 percent of MTDC under 2 CFR 200.414 (raised from 10 percent for awards on or after October 1, 2024). Funders such as foundations often set their own, lower caps.

Does the $50,000 subaward limit apply to each subaward or the total?

Each subaward. The first $50,000 of every subaward is in the MTDC base for the life of the award; only the excess is excluded. Two $80,000 subawards put $100,000 in the base, not $50,000.

Does this give grant or accounting advice?

No. It does arithmetic on the numbers you type using the federal definition, with every threshold editable. Your funder's notice of funding, your negotiated rate agreement and your institution's grants office decide what is allowed. Nothing is uploaded.
