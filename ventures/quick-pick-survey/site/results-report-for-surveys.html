Hand your survey result to a client without overselling it

You ran a pairwise ranking survey for a committee, a club or a client. The free engine gives you the ranking. The branded report puts it on one printable page with your logo, and says plainly which places are real and which are ties.

A sample, made by the real code

This is the report function run on an invented team-offsite vote with simulated respondents. It is not a real survey.

Open the sample on its own page (it prints cleanly).

What is on the page

  • Your name, logo (https or an embedded png, jpeg or webp) and one accent colour.
  • A place per option, with a 95% range bar. Options the picks cannot separate show as a range such as 2–5, never a false 1-2-3.
  • A warning naming options too few people rated, so a thin option is not read as a loss.
  • A note when respondents clicked the same screen position (top or bottom) at least 70% of the time over 30 or more answers. Positions are shuffled per pair, so those picks say nothing about the options and are left out of the ranking.
  • A note when some respondents' order barely matches everyone else's. That is what careless clicking looks like, but so is a genuinely different taste, so they are counted, not dropped, and you decide.
  • The note that this is an estimate from limited picks, not a fact about any person.

Questions

Is the branded results report on sale?

Not yet. The code is built and switched off until a checkout link is set. The ranking, intervals and CSV are free and stay free.

If it goes on sale, the price (a one-off in the $9 to $29 range) is a guess nobody has tested.

Why does the report show a place as 2-4?

Because the picks cannot separate those options. Ranges that overlap are shown as a shared range instead of being ordered by noise.

Do I need to host anything?

Yes: Quick Pick is a Next.js and Postgres app you run yourself. See the main page for how.
