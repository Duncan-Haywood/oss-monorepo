# 000N. Title, as a statement of the decision

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by `[000M](000M-....md)`

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. Include the constraint that made the
obvious option unattractive — that is the part nobody can reconstruct later.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one or two sentences, in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it rules out, and what someone should look at before
reversing it. Be specific about the failure mode the decision avoids;
"cleaner" is not a consequence.
