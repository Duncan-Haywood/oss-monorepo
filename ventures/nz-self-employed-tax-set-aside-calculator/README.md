# New Zealand Self-Employed Tax Set-Aside Calculator (2025-26)

Free, private browser tool: enter business revenue and expenses and get income tax, the ACC earners' levy, your ACC work levy, optional student loan repayments and the share of every invoice to set aside. Flags provisional tax and the $60,000 GST registration line.

After using this, a New Zealand freelancer or sole trader knows how much to move out of each payment instead of finding a tax bill after 31 March.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/nz-self-employed-tax-set-aside-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
