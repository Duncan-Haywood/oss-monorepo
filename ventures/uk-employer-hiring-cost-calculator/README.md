# UK Cost of Hiring an Employee Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-employer-hiring-cost-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for UK small employers: enter a salary and see the true yearly, monthly and first-year cost of a hire, including employer National Insurance (15% above £5,000 from April 2025), the Employment Allowance, the automatic-enrolment pension, the under-21 and apprentice relief, and your own extras. It also shows how much the April 2025 NIC change added and what salary a total budget can fund.

After using this, you know what a first hire will actually cost you per month, which used to mean a spreadsheet of thresholds and percentages.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-employer-hiring-cost-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
