Bid the base and the waste, not the square footage.

A flat per-square-foot quote treats a 12 × 16 running-bond patio as the same job as 12 × 16 of 45° herringbone on a 10-inch driveway pad. Pattern waste, excavation volume, haul-away, and edge restraint do not scale with the face the homeowner measures. Price those extras separately, then see the walk-away number.
