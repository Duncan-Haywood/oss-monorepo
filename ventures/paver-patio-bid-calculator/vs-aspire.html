This calculator vs. Aspire

Two different shapes: Aspire is an account-based business-management platform for running a hardscape or landscape company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this paver patio cost, given its face, pattern, base, and edge — before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

Aspire is a cloud business-management platform, founded in 2013 by Kevin Kehoe and Mark Tipton, headquartered in Chesterfield, Missouri. It is built for landscape maintenance and construction contractors — including hardscape and paver installation crews — as well as irrigation, snow and ice management, and commercial cleaning companies, and has been part of ServiceTitan since ServiceTitan's 2021 acquisition of it. It bundles CRM and lead tracking, estimating, scheduling, purchasing, job costing, mobile crew time tracking, invoicing, and accounting and payroll integration into one ongoing subscription account, with a mobile app for crews in the field. Its own plans are tiered by a contractor's annual revenue rather than sold at one flat price, and no dollar figure for any plan is published on its site — pricing is reached by requesting a demo. Aspire's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact tier names or any dollar figure, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a business-management platform serving landscape construction, irrigation, snow and ice, and cleaning contractors, sold as a revenue-tiered subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Aspire appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Aspire does. It does not track a lead, schedule a crew, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one patio — length and width, laying pattern (running bond through 45° herringbone), base depth, bedding sand, edge restraint footage, and whether excavated spoils are hauled off site — plus loaded crew cost, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the install price, the price per finished square foot, the effective crew hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one patio. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
