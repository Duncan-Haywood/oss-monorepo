    InputHandling

    Common questions

    Does it upload my lot list?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    Why would I sell some items in bulk instead of listing them?

    Low-value items can cost more in fees, shipping and your time than they bring. A $4 jar that takes three minutes to list and ship nets less listed than sold on to a liquidator. The tool compares the two for every line.

    Is the answer guaranteed to be best?

    It is the exact best list-or-bulk choice for the numbers you typed, checked in the tests against every possible combination. Prices and sell-through are your estimates, so it is a decision aid, not a forecast.

    What is the break-even hourly rate?

    The value of an hour of your time at which the whole-lot offer and your best split earn the same. If your time is worth more than that, take the offer.
