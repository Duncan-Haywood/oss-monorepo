Common questions

How much should I charge for a no-show or late cancellation?

There's no single right number, but a common starting point is charging close to the full service price for a true no-show (the slot usually can't be rebooked) and a partial fee for a late cancellation with some notice. This calculator turns your own price, notice window, and fee percentage into a specific number.

Is a cancellation or no-show fee legal?

Charging a disclosed cancellation fee is common practice across many service industries and is generally enforceable when clients agree to it in advance, but rules vary by location, payment processor, and industry — some regions treat fees for medical or dental appointments differently, for example. This is not legal advice; check the requirements for your business type and location.

How much notice should I require?

24 hours is the most common default — usually enough time to fill the slot from a waitlist. Businesses with longer or harder-to-fill sessions often use 48 hours instead.

Usage
