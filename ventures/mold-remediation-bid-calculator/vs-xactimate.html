This calculator vs. Xactimate

Two different shapes: Xactimate prices an insurance claim against a standardized, shared regional line-item list. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this remediation job cost, given its zones, demo, dry-out days, and access, before you quote it directly.

What each tool is for

Xactimate is line-item estimating software from Xactware, a company founded in 1986 and headquartered in Orem, Utah, acquired by ISO / Verisk in 2006. Insurance adjusters and restoration contractors use it to scope and price a covered loss — water, fire, mold, and similar claims — against a shared, regionally-priced list of standardized line items, so both sides of a claim can negotiate from the same reference prices. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account, and Xactware does not publish a fixed public price for it, so no dollar figure for Xactimate is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — insurance-claim estimating software built around a shared regional price list — are stated, and no dollar amount for Xactimate appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Xactimate does. It has no regional price list, produces no carrier-facing claim estimate, and stores no customer or claim record on a server. It takes job type (closet, bedroom, basement, or sewage-affected), affected square footage, extra containment zones over the included setup, demolition square feet, HEPA and dehumidifier days, third-party clearance testing, HVAC cleaning, a dumpster, occupied-home access, loaded wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the walk-away price below which the job loses money, the price per square foot of affected area, and where the crew hours are actually going — plus a printable customer proposal with scope lines, a deposit split, a price-held date, and a signature block. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
