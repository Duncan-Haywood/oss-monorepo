This calculator vs. CoatingOS

Two different shapes: CoatingOS is a subscription business-management app for running a coating company's estimates, scheduling, invoicing, and customer records. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one slab cost, given its grind hours, flake, and kit, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

CoatingOS is an all-in-one business management app built specifically for floor coating contractors — epoxy, polyurea, polyaspartic, and concrete coating professionals. It lets a contractor create and send estimates, convert an accepted estimate into a work order, generate invoices and track payments, schedule jobs, store customer records, send two-way SMS to customers, document jobs with photos, and take deposits through a Square integration. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account with a free trial offered on its own site. CoatingOS's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — coating-industry business-management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for CoatingOS appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what CoatingOS does. It does not manage customer records, schedule crews, or send invoices, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one slab's square footage, grind hours, flake load and coverage, coat count, a choice between full-broadcast epoxy and 1-day polyaspartic kits, waste, crack repair, moisture-barrier treatment, loaded wage, payroll burden, overhead, target margin, and travel, and turns that into the per-job price, the price per square foot, and the walk-away price below which a cut-rate special loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
