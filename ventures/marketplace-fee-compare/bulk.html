Read this first

Headline rates, not a statement. Fee rates change and the defaults are inputs from the single-item tool; confirm against your own statement. The winner is the best channel that brings buyers: your own store tops almost any table on arithmetic but sends nobody, so it is never recommended here. Monthly gain assumes each item's whole monthly order count moves with it, which a real channel switch rarely does; read it as an upper bound.
