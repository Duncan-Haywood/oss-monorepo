FOR ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS

Bid the run and the panel, not the charger.

A Level 2 EVSE is a continuous load. Size the breaker at 125%, pick copper that holds a 3% voltage drop for the actual run, then add trench hours and panel work. A 25-foot indoor hang on a spare slot is one job. The same 48 A charger on a 70-foot trench to a detached garage that also needs a service upgrade is several times that price — and a flat “EV charger install” quote cannot see the difference.

Questions electricians actually type

How much should I charge to install an EV charger?

There is no flat hang fee. Use this page: a short indoor 48 A job on a spare slot often lands near $1,200–$1,400 at the default rates. Switch the location to a detached garage, add a 60-foot trench, and pick a service upgrade — the same charger becomes a several-thousand-dollar job. That gap is the whole product.

What size breaker and wire does a 48 A charger need?

48 A continuous × 1.25 = 60 A breaker. Copper #6 usually carries that ampacity; a long run may upsize to hold 3% voltage drop at 240 V. The result panel shows the gauge and the calculated drop for the feet you entered.

Do I have to include a service upgrade?

Only if the existing service does not have the headroom. Enter the service rating and a honest existing-load estimate. If the new breaker is larger than the unused capacity, the tool flags it — it does not decide the upgrade for you, and it is not a permit load calculation.
