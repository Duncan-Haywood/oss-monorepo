    Common questions

    Why does this price liner square feet instead of water surface area?

    A published “$8/sf of water” special counts only the floor's footprint, but vinyl is sold and stretched over floor plus walls plus any hopper extra plus stairs — a hopper deep-end box or a set of wedding-cake stairs adds real liner square feet the water-surface number never sees. That's why the same water surface area can price very differently depending on depth style and stairs.

    Why does an overlap or uni-bead conversion cost more than a standard bead replacement?

    Standard bead / J-hook reuses the existing track, but overlap (coping-over, no track) and uni-bead/Fast-Bead conversions change how the liner attaches at the top and often require extra prep or hardware the direct swap doesn't need. Pick the bead type that matches what's actually at your pool's coping, not just the cheapest option, since the wrong choice can mean redoing the top edge.

    Does a thicker mil liner (27 or 30) actually need more labor, or just cost more material?

    Both — a heavier liner is stiffer to handle, stretch, and set wrinkle-free, so the calculator adds labor hours as well as the higher material cost as you move up the mil presets. It is not simply the 20-mil price times a material multiplier.

    Does this check whether my pool's structure or bead track is sound?

    No. It prices the liner replacement scope you describe; it does not inspect the shell, verify the bead track or coping condition, or make a structural or code determination. Confirm the receiver track and wall condition in person before quoting a bead type or ordering vinyl.
