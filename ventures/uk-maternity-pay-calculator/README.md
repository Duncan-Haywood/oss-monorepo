# UK Maternity Pay Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your due date, start date and average weekly pay to see whether you qualify for Statutory Maternity Pay (or Statutory Paternity Pay), the week-by-week pay for the 39 weeks, the total, and how far it falls below your normal pay.

After using this, you know what statutory pay you will actually receive each week and can plan the drop, which used to mean working out the qualifying week and the 90%-then-flat-rate split by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-maternity-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
