Common questions

    
How much is Statutory Maternity Pay?

    
For up to 39 weeks: 90% of your average weekly earnings for the first 6 weeks, then the lower of the flat rate (£187.18 a week in 2025/26) and 90% of your average earnings for the remaining 33 weeks.

    
Who qualifies for Statutory Maternity Pay?

    
Employees who have worked for the same employer for at least 26 weeks up to the qualifying week (the 15th week before the week the baby is due) and who earn at least the lower earnings limit on average (£125 a week in 2025/26). If you do not qualify you may get Maternity Allowance instead.

    
When can maternity pay start?

    
The earliest is the Sunday 11 weeks before the week the baby is due. It starts the day after the birth at the latest if the baby arrives before you start.

    
How much is Statutory Paternity Pay?

    
One or two weeks at the lower of the flat rate and 90% of your average weekly earnings, if you meet the same 26-week and earnings tests.

    
Is statutory maternity pay taxed?

    
Yes. It is taxed and subject to National Insurance like wages, so take-home pay is lower than the gross figures here.
