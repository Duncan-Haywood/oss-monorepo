Common questions

Can't I just click through to the pinned commit and read its uses: lines myself?

For one reference, yes — open the reusable workflow or composite action at the pinned commit on GitHub, read its action.yml or workflow file, and see whether its own uses: lines are pinned to a SHA or a mutable tag. This tool does exactly that fetch and that read, for every pinned reference in the workflow you paste, in one pass, and reports which ones load something unpinned underneath — rather than you repeating the click-and-read for each line by hand.

What does checking by hand actually miss?

Not correctness — a careful read of one reference by hand is as accurate as the tool's read of that same reference. What it misses is coverage: a workflow with a dozen pinned steps means a dozen separate page loads and reads, and a repository with many workflow files multiplies that by every file, every time a dependency is bumped. The check that gets skipped because it takes ten minutes is the check that misses the one reference that changed.
