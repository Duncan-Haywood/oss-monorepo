Common questions

Why does PPE cost scale with decon cycles instead of just one set per technician?

Because a technician who leaves the contaminated area — for a break, to run equipment out, or across a shift change — has to doff the PPE they were wearing before re-entering. Used PPE cannot be re-donned, so re-entry needs a fresh set. A count that assumes one set per technician undercounts every job with more than one decon cycle per tech.

Why is disposal billed per container instead of per pound?

Because that is how a licensed regulated-waste hauler actually invoices: a per-container pickup charge, not a per-pound rate. A container that is 10% full costs the same as one that is completely full, up to its rated capacity. This calculator rounds your waste estimate up to the next whole container before pricing it, the way your hauler's invoice will, instead of multiplying pounds by a linear per-pound rate that undercounts whenever a container isn't full.

What does the minimum job fee do?

It floors the quoted price at your company's stated minimum. A very small scope — one room, a short on-site window, little waste — can price below a sensible minimum on cost-plus-margin arithmetic alone, even though the job still needs a full truck roll, a hauler pickup, and travel regardless of size.
