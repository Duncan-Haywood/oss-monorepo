# UK Take-Home Pay Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-take-home-pay-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter a gross salary, pension and student loan and see income tax, National Insurance and loan deductions for England, Wales and Northern Ireland (2025/26 rates), take-home pay per year, month and week, your marginal rate, and what the same pension costs under salary sacrifice, net pay and relief at source.

After using this, you know what lands in your bank account and which pension method costs you least, without a payslip or a spreadsheet.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-take-home-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
