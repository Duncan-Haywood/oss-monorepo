FOR DENTAL PRACTICE OWNERS AND OFFICE MANAGERS

Your fee schedule is not what you collect.

Every practice knows its total write-off. Almost none knows it per plan — and per plan is the only form in which two contracts can be compared. The adjustment is posted the moment the claim is, in a column no report turns into a rate, so the contract taking the most out of the practice is rarely the one that feels worst. Put one month in below and see what each carrier actually pays, which of them buy chair time for less than the chair costs, and what a fee increase would really collect.

      What this is, and is not

      No warranty. This free tool is not legal, tax, accounting, insurance, employment or contract advice, and it does not tell you what to charge, which networks to join or leave, how to code or record any adjustment, or how any agreement should be read. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type. Verify every figure against your own practice management system, payroll records and accounts, and against a qualified professional, before relying on it or acting on a contract with it. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the authors accept no liability for loss or damage from its use.

      Private by construction. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and there is deliberately no shareable link — a practice's production, payroll and overhead should not ride in a URL. No patient-level information is an input. Saving a month writes the figures to this browser's own storage; clearing your browser data clears them.
