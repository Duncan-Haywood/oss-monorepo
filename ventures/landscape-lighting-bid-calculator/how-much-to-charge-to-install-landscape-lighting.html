Price the fixtures and the trench, not the $150 dump

Landscape lighting is a fixture mix, then a scope, then how you get power there. Path lights are a stake. Uplights are aim and a spike. Well lights are a dig and a drain. Fixtures-only hangs lights on a transformer that is already there. A new system is trench, low-voltage wire, and a transformer. An add-on is a home-run back to what is already on the house.

Fixtures-only is not a new system

The cheap special is almost always path lights on an existing transformer, no trench, no wells, no saw-cut. A new-system architectural job is a different crew day. Matching a per-light number on a new-system yard is how a sold lighting job becomes a loss when the home-run and the well lights eat the margin.

This is not holiday lights or an electrical panel

Holiday lights are a seasonal C9 hang and January take-down. An electrical panel is service amps. Irrigation install is water zones and a backflow. Exterior painting is a coat on elevations. Walk-away price

Load the wage with payroll burden. Add overhead as a share of direct cost. Then take margin as a share of the customer’s price, not a markup on cost. The walk-away number is total cost. A proposed price under that number loses money before you take anything out of it.

Common questions

How much should I charge to install landscape lighting?

Charge from fixture mix, scope, trench, soil, and extras — not a published $150 / light special. Six path lights on an existing transformer is a different job from an 18-fixture architectural install with well lights, a new transformer, irrigated-turf trench, and a driveway saw-cut.

Is fixtures-only the same bid as a new landscape lighting system?

No. Fixtures-only hangs lights on a transformer and wire that are already there. A new system is a trench, low-voltage wire, and a transformer. An add-on is a home-run back to what is already on the house. Matching a per-light special on a new-system job is how a sold lighting job becomes a loss when the trench eats the margin.

How do well lights and trench change a landscape lighting price?

A path light is a stake. A well light is a dig and a drain. Trench lineal feet, irrigated turf repair, rocky soil, a photocell, a driveway saw-cut, and a second transformer do not live in a published per-light dump.
