FOR LANDSCAPE LIGHTING CONTRACTORS

Bid the fixtures, the trench, and the extras.

Price low-voltage landscape lighting the way the trade actually loses money: six path lights on an existing transformer versus a new-system architectural job with well lights, trench through irrigated turf, and a driveway saw-cut — not the published $150 / light special. You get the job price, what it works out to per fixture, and the number below which the install loses money.

The yard

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a light invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing scope, soil, or transformer fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the scope, soil, and transformer you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a light catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $150 / light install that looked profitable was not.
