# Concrete maturity calculator

Estimate in-place concrete strength from a temperature logger's record, using
the maturity method described in ASTM C1074. Drop in the logger's CSV and the
lab breaks for the mix; the tool fits the mix's strength-maturity curve and
shows each sensor's estimated strength now and when it should reach a
form-stripping or stressing target.

**Live:** https://marisstella.app/concrete-maturity/ ·
[The maturity method explained](https://marisstella.app/concrete-maturity/maturity-method-explained.html)

## Who it is for

Concrete contractors, post-tensioning crews, QC technicians and site engineers
who already log concrete temperatures and want the strength arithmetic without
a spreadsheet or a vendor account.

## What it does

- Reads logger CSV exports: a timestamp column (ISO, US `m/d/yyyy h:mm AM`,
  day-first, `02-Nov-2026`) or elapsed time (hours, or minutes/days/seconds
  when the header says so); one or more temperature columns in °C or °F;
  semicolon files with decimal commas. Humidity, battery and index columns are
  skipped.
- Maturity index per reading: Nurse-Saul temperature-time factor (datum
  temperature) or Arrhenius equivalent age (activation energy, reference
  temperature). Interval mean temperatures; gaps over 6 h are flagged.
- Strength-maturity fit from lab specimens (age at a constant lab temperature,
  or a logged maturity): logarithmic, hyperbolic and exponential curves, with
  R², RMSE and AICc; automatic choice by AICc, or pick one.
- Per sensor: estimated strength now, time the target was reached, or a
  projection at an assumed temperature (default: the last 24 h average); the
  lowest sensor is marked. Warnings for freezing, high early temperatures and
  extrapolation past the calibration.
- Charts, CSV export, and a CLI:

```
node cli.mjs logger.csv --cal breaks.txt --target 3000 [--method equivalent-age] [--json] [--csv out.csv]
node cli.mjs --example --target 3000
```

## Files

- `maturity.mjs`: the engine (pure functions, no dependencies).
- `index.html`: the tool. `maturity-method-explained.html`: the marketing and
  explainer page with a worked example.
- `cli.mjs`: command line. `usage.mjs`: the anonymous local usage counter.
- Tests: `node --test maturity.test.mjs page.test.mjs` (23 tests).

The example data (the "Load a made-up example" button and `--example`) is
generated by `exampleCsv()` and is made up; no real project produced it.

## Limits

The estimate is only as good as the calibration: one curve per mix, specimens
of that mix, concrete kept moist enough to hydrate. ASTM C1074 asks for the
estimate to be confirmed by other tests before critical operations such as form
removal or post-tensioning. Datum temperature and activation energy defaults
are common starting values, not measured for your mix.

uploaded, and there are no accounts, tracking cookies or analytics vendor. An
anonymous counter in the browser's local storage (`concrete-maturity-usage`)
records which features were used and the day; the checkbox in the footer turns
it off.
