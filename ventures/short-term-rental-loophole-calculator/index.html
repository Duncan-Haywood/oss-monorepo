Questions people ask

What is the short-term rental tax loophole?

If the average guest stay is 7 days or fewer, the rental is not a rental activity under the passive-loss rules. If you also materially participate, depreciation losses (often boosted by a cost-segregation study and bonus depreciation) are non-passive and can offset W-2 or business income instead of being suspended. It is a real, widely used reading of the regulations, and also a frequent audit target, so records matter.

How many hours do I need to materially participate?

More than 500 hours in the year, or more than 100 hours and more than any other single person who works on the property, cleaners and property managers included. A manager who logs more hours than you can defeat the second test. Keep a contemporaneous time log.

Does cost segregation matter?

It moves part of the building into 5, 7 and 15 year property that bonus depreciation can write off in year one. The tool shows the year-one tax saved with and without it, and the recapture you owe later. Depreciation is a deferral, not forgiveness.

Is there a limit on how much loss I can use?

A non-passive business loss is capped by the excess business loss limit (section 461(l)), an indexed threshold you type in. Anything above carries forward as a net operating loss.

What happens when I sell?

Depreciation on the cost-segregated share is recaptured at ordinary rates and building depreciation at up to 25%. The tool estimates this for the years you plan to hold. A 1031 exchange or holding until death changes the picture; this tool does not model either.

No. It is arithmetic on figures you type. Whether your stays, services and hours qualify is a facts-and-circumstances question for a qualified tax professional.
