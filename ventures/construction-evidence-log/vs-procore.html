This evidence pack vs. Procore

Two different shapes: Procore is account-based project-management software for running a whole company's projects. This page is a free, private worksheet that turns one site day — or several combined — into a dated evidence record.

What each tool is for

Procore is construction project-management software founded in 2002 and headquartered in Carpinteria, California. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account and covers daily logs, RFIs, submittals, drawings, photos, quality and safety, and financials across a company's whole project portfolio, used by general contractors, owners, and specialty contractors mostly on commercial and larger-scale work. Procore's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — account-based, subscription construction project-management software — are stated, and no dollar amount for Procore appears on this page.

This tool does none of what Procore does. It does not manage RFIs or submittals, track drawings, run financials, or store any project record on a server. It takes one site day — crew, weather or site conditions, work completed, and any change or delay, with who requested it and its time or cost impact — flags what is missing before a thin record is all you have, and turns it into a dated JSON evidence pack. Every entry you submit is saved in your own browser, so at the end of a job you can download one combined, multi-day pack instead of hunting a week of separate files. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
