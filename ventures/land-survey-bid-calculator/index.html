Common questions

Is this a legal boundary determination or a substitute for my professional judgment?

No. This is a pricing worksheet only. It does not perform a legal boundary determination, does not check any state's minimum technical standards, and does not replace your own professional judgment about scope, liability, or errors-and-omissions coverage on a given job.

Why does the calculator separate corners to locate from corners to set?

Locating an existing, recoverable monument is a confirmation against a physical marker. Setting a corner with no recoverable monument means resolving the position from the deed and record research and then running and holding a traverse to it with nothing to check it against on the ground — field time this calculator prices noticeably higher per corner, because it usually is.

Why not just quote per acre like most of the trade does?

Acreage does not see corner count, whether corners must be set or just located, or how tangled the title chain is. A small parcel with several lost corners and a messy deed history can cost more to survey than a large, clean, open one — so a flat per-acre number gets one of them wrong. Does anything about my job or client get uploaded?

No. Every number on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and no parcel address, client name, or price is ever saved or transmitted. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off below.

What if I don't know the exact corner count or research hours before I bid?

Use your best estimate from the plat, prior surveys of adjoining parcels, or a quick record-search pass before quoting — the same way you would size up any job. This tool prices the numbers you enter; it does not verify them against the deed itself.
