Bid the wall, the pattern, and the extras.

Price a kitchen tile backsplash the way the trade actually loses money: 30 SF of subway running-bond on painted drywall versus a herringbone demo with outlets, a cooktop cutout, and Schluter — not the published $35 / SF special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot, and the number below which the install loses money.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $35 / SF splash that looked profitable was not.

Leftover boxes and the next wall

Tile is sold by the box. A 33 SF buy is not 3.3 boxes — it is four boxes at 10 SF each, and leftover after the kitchen may or may not cover a laundry return. Leftover already on the van is already-paid stock unless you decide to credit it; this does not change the headline bid.
