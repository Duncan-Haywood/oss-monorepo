Should I tile a backsplash after hours?

Owners call with a weekday number: “Can you do Saturday so I can cook? Same $35 a square foot?” That sounds like the same wall. It is not the same crew day. After-hours is time-and-a-half. You cannot leave mud overnight in a kitchen they still use, so it is a second evening trip. Working around a cook on a weekday is the same wage on a slower set.

Saturday is not the weekday bid

An empty weekday herringbone still carries the wage you already timed. After-hours raises that wage to time-and-a-half and adds a starting second trip. On a typical 42 SF herringbone demo — $38 wage, 30% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, 16 miles, six outlets, cooktop, Schluter — the empty-weekday bid is $3,858.00. After-hours is $5,251.26. The walk-away jumps from $3,086.40 to $4,201.01. Taking $1,470 ($35 / SF) to “just do Saturday” still loses money — even on the weekday.
