Common questions

How many days do I need for the physical presence test?

330 full days in a foreign country within any 12 consecutive months. A full day is 24 hours from midnight to midnight, so a day you fly in or out does not count. On the sample dates the best 12-month span holds 347 full foreign days, 17 more than needed, so up to 35 days of that span could be spent anywhere else.

Can the 12 months start on any day?

Yes: the 12-month period does not have to be a calendar year and can start on any day, so the calculator slides a 12-month window across your dates and reports the one with the most foreign days. It also reports the first date a window holds 330 days (2025-09-04 on the sample).

How much income can I exclude?

Up to the annual limit ($130,000 for 2025, entered as an editable figure), prorated by the days of the tax year that fall in your qualifying period and never more than your foreign earned income. On the sample, $95,000 of income is fully excluded. Check the current limit and your eligibility with the IRS or a tax professional.
