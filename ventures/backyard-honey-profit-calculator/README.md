# Backyard Honey Profit Calculator

**Find what a pound of honey costs you in cash and hours, your profit per pound and per hour, the price per pound you need to pay yourself a wage, and how a poor honey year and winter hive losses move it.** Browser page, no install.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/backyard-honey-profit-calculator/

After using this, you know whether your honey money beats the cost of keeping bees, the price you need for the wage you want, and how much a bad year or a bad winter costs you.

## Your numbers, not advice

Every value is a number you type. Prices, costs and times are yours to check. Check your state's rules on selling honey. Not affiliated with any beekeeping association or marketplace.

## How it computes

Cash cost per year = hives x winter loss x replacement colony cost + equipment + hives x treatment and feed + pounds sold x jar cost + other. Pounds sold = hives x honey per hive x share not lost. Price for your wage = (cash cost + wage x hours a year) / pounds sold.

## What it is not

Not financial, tax, legal, food-safety or business advice, and no warranty. It ignores sales tax, licences, honey-grading and labelling rules, the value of pollination and the resale value of your colonies.

## Privacy

No network call, no account, no analytics vendor. Anonymous feature counts (`calculate_run`, `example_loaded`) live in local storage only and can be switched off on the page; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured.
