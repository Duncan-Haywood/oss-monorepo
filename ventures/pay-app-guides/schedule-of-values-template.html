Free schedule of values template, with the continuation sheet math done

A free Excel workbook for a construction pay application: one sheet for the schedule of values / continuation sheet (one row per line, 40 lines) and one for the summary (the nine usual lines from contract sum to current payment due). Type your scheduled values and this month's work in the shaded cells; total completed and stored, percent complete, balance to finish, retainage and payment due fill themselves in, and a check column flags any line billed over its value.

What is in the continuation sheet

ColumnYou type, or it works outFormula
A ItemYou type: the SOV line number; CO-1, CO-2 for change orders
B Description of workYou type: the line as it reads on the approved SOV
C Scheduled valueYou type: the line's value; a deductive change order is negative
D From previous applicationYou type: billed on this line in all earlier applications
E This periodYou type: work installed this period, in dollars
F Materials presently storedYou type: delivered, not yet installed, not in D or E
G Total completed and storedWorks outD + E + F
H % completeWorks outG ÷ C
I Balance to finishWorks outC − G
J RetainageWorks out(D + E) × work rate + F × stored rate, rounded to the cent
K CheckWorks out"Over-billed" if G is more than C; "No scheduled value" if work is billed on a line with none

What is in the summary

  1. Original contract sum: you type it.
  2. Net change by change orders: line 3 less line 1, so it is the total of your CO lines.
  3. Contract sum to date: the scheduled value total.
  4. Total completed and stored to date: the column G total.
  5. Retainage: the column J total.
  6. Total earned less retainage: line 4 less line 5.
  7. Less previous certificates for payment: you type it: what was certified on earlier applications, not what you were paid.
  8. Current payment due: line 6 less line 7.
  9. Balance to finish, including retainage: line 3 less line 6.

Under the summary, a checks line warns when the original contract sum is missing, when retainage is more than the work completed, when the payment due is negative, and how many continuation-sheet lines are flagged.

Next month, and what the template leaves to you

To roll forward: save a copy, set each line's from previous to this month's previous plus this period, clear this period, leave uninstalled materials in stored (move them into this period when they go in), and add this month's certified payment to line 7. The calculator on this site and the pay application generator do the same arithmetic in the browser if you would rather not keep a spreadsheet.

The template does arithmetic only. Your contract decides the retainage rate, whether stored materials can be billed, and when retainage drops or is released; state law may cap retainage (public caps for 12 states). It does not know whether a change order is approved, and it does not handle sales tax.

Common questions

Is this the AIA G703 continuation sheet?

No. It is our own layout with the same columns and arithmetic that a continuation sheet uses. The AIA G702 and G703 are copyrighted forms sold by the AIA, and this template is not affiliated with the American Institute of Architects. If your owner requires the AIA forms, use the numbers from this sheet to fill them in.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets, or open it in Excel or LibreOffice Calc. It contains only ordinary formulas: no macros, no links to other files, and no add-ins.

How is retainage calculated in the template?

Each line's retainage is the work rate times work completed (previous plus this period) plus the stored-materials rate times materials presently stored, rounded to the cent per line. Both rates sit at the top of the sheet. The summary's retainage line is the total of the line retainage.

How do I start next month's application from this one?

Save a copy. On each line, set From previous application to this month's previous plus this period, clear This period, and leave stored materials that are still uninstalled in the stored column. On the summary, add this month's current payment due, as certified, to Less previous certificates.
