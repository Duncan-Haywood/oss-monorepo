Arizona lien waiver forms

Arizona sets the wording of lien waivers in statute (A.R.S. § 33-1008). How strict: Unenforceable "unless it follows substantially the following forms". Below are all four statutory forms, word for word, with a blank line wherever the statute leaves one. Print the one you need, fill it in by hand, and keep a copy.

What Arizona asks of you

  • A progress waiver covers work "through" a date you fill in "only and does not cover any retention, pending modifications and changes or items furnished after that date".
  • The unconditional forms carry a notice: "This document is enforceable against you if you sign it, even if you have not been paid. If you have not been paid, use a conditional release form."
  • No statement waiving lien rights binds you unless it is one of these waivers or you "had actually received payment in full for the claim" (§ 33-1008(B)).

Notes on the forms

  • Arizona: a waiver and release is effective only if it follows substantially one of the forms in Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 33-1008(D) and is signed by the claimant or its authorized agent; a conditional release also needs evidence of payment (§ 33-1008(A)).
  • Arizona requires the unconditional-waiver notice in type at least as large as the largest type otherwise on the document (§ 33-1008(D)(2) and (4)).

Common questions

Does Arizona have a statutory lien waiver form?

Yes. A.R.S. § 33-1008 sets 4 forms: conditional and unconditional, progress and final. How strict: Unenforceable "unless it follows substantially the following forms". The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-25.

Which Arizona lien waiver form goes with which payment?

Conditional waiver and release on progress payment: conditional progress waivers. Unconditional waiver and release on progress payment: unconditional progress waivers. Conditional waiver and release on final payment: conditional final waivers. Unconditional waiver and release on final payment: unconditional final waivers.

When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Arizona?

Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if Arizona has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver.
