Nevada lien waiver forms

Nevada sets the wording of lien waivers in statute (NRS 108.2457). How strict: Unenforceable "unless it is in the following forms": no "substantially". Below are all four statutory forms, word for word, with a blank line wherever the statute leaves one. Print the one you need, fill it in by hand, and keep a copy.

What Nevada asks of you

  • The strictest wording rule in the list: the waiver must be "in the following forms", not substantially like them.
  • No through date. The blanks are Property Name, Property Location, Undersigned's Customer, Invoice/Payment Application Number and Payment Amount, so a progress waiver is tied to one pay application.
  • A joint check counts as payment to you only up to the smallest of the check, the amount meant for you, or your balance on that work (§ 108.2457(3)).

Notes on the forms

  • Nevada: a waiver and release is unenforceable unless it is in the forms of Nev. Rev. Stat. § 108.2457(5), signed by the lien claimant or its authorized agent; a conditional one needs payment of the amount it names (§ 108.2457(1)).
  • Nevada requires the unconditional-waiver notice in type at least as large as the largest type otherwise on the document (§ 108.2457(5)(b) and (d)).
  • If the payment is a check that fails to clear, the waiver is void (§ 108.2457(5)(e)).

Property Location: Property Location

Payment Amount: Payment Amount

Upon receipt by the undersigned of a check in the above-referenced Payment Amount payable to the undersigned, and when the check has been properly endorsed and has been paid by the bank on which it is drawn, this document becomes effective to release and the undersigned shall be deemed to waive any notice of lien, any private bond right, any claim for payment and any rights under any similar ordinance, rule or statute related to payment rights that the undersigned has on the above-described Property to the following extent:

This release covers a progress payment for the work, materials or equipment furnished by the undersigned to the Property or to the Undersigned’s Customer which are the subject of the Invoice or Payment Application, but only to the extent of the Payment Amount or such portion of the Payment Amount as the undersigned is actually paid, and does not cover any retention withheld, any items, modifications or changes pending approval, disputed items and claims, or items furnished that are not paid. Before any recipient of this document relies on it, the recipient should verify evidence of payment to the undersigned. The undersigned warrants that he or she either has already paid or will use the money received from this progress payment promptly to pay in full all laborers, subcontractors, materialmen and suppliers for all work, materials or equipment that are the subject of this waiver and release.

Common questions

Does Nevada have a statutory lien waiver form?

Yes. NRS 108.2457 sets 4 forms: conditional and unconditional, progress and final. How strict: Unenforceable "unless it is in the following forms": no "substantially". The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-25.

Which Nevada lien waiver form goes with which payment?

Conditional Waiver and Release upon Progress Payment: conditional progress waivers. Unconditional Waiver and Release upon Progress Payment: unconditional progress waivers. Conditional Waiver and Release upon Final Payment: conditional final waivers. Unconditional Waiver and Release upon Final Payment: unconditional final waivers.

When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Nevada?

Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if Nevada has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver.
