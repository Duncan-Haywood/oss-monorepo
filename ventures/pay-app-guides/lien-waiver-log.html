Lien waiver log for subcontractors

Paste your lien waiver log, or start from the example. The page matches each conditional waiver to its unconditional, flags unconditional waivers you gave before the money cleared or for more than you were paid, lists the unconditional waivers you still need from suppliers and lower-tier subs you have paid, and writes out the waivers to collect for the next pay application.

Your log stays in this browser. The page checks the log against itself; which waiver form your state requires, and what it may say, is set by the state and your contract.

Summary


  
  

  
Waivers to request from your suppliers and lower-tier subs

  
Unconditional waivers for payments you have made, and, when you give the next pay app's through date, a conditional waiver through that date from each lower tier on the log that has not given a final waiver. Use the form your state or the job requires; see waiver forms by state.

  

  





  
How the log is checked

  
    
    
  • Matching: a conditional waiver is matched to an unconditional with the same direction, party (ignoring case), progress or final, and through date.
    • 
    
  • Waivers you gave: an unconditional with no cleared-payment date, signed before the payment cleared, or for more than the paid amount is flagged; so is a paid conditional with no unconditional yet, as the GC will usually ask for it next.
    • 
    
  • Waivers you collected: a conditional you have paid on with no matching unconditional is listed; past the chase days it is flagged, because until you hold it that supplier can still claim a lien for that amount.
    • 
    
  • Log checks: repeated waivers, progress waivers with no through date, an unconditional through a date after it was signed, and progress waivers signed after a final one.
    • 
    
  • Not decided here: whether a waiver is valid in your state, whether your state requires a statutory form, and what exceptions it allows. Read conditional vs unconditional lien waivers, and to check the wording of a waiver you were sent, the lien waiver checker.
    • 
  






  
Common questions

  
What is a lien waiver log?

  
A list of every lien waiver on a job, both the ones you sign for the GC or owner and the ones you collect from your own suppliers and lower-tier subs: who, conditional or unconditional, progress or final, the through date, the amount, when it was signed and when the payment it covers cleared.

  
When is it safe to sign an unconditional lien waiver?

  
After the payment it covers has actually cleared your bank, and only for the amount you received. Before that, give a conditional waiver, which takes effect only when you are paid.

  
Why collect waivers from my own suppliers?

  
A supplier or lower-tier sub you paid can still claim a lien on the job until it signs an unconditional waiver for that payment, and most GCs ask for those waivers with your next pay application.
