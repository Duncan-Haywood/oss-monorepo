Michigan lien waiver forms

Michigan sets the wording of lien waivers in statute (MCL 570.1115). How strict: "Shall be used in substantially the following format". Below are all four statutory forms, word for word, with a blank line wherever the statute leaves one. Print the one you need, fill it in by hand, and keep a copy.

What Michigan asks of you

  • Michigan's words are "partial" and "full" rather than progress and final. Conditional waivers take effect "upon payment of the amount indicated".
  • Once fully paid you "shall provide" a full unconditional waiver; when partly paid, a partial unconditional waiver if the owner asks (§ 570.1115(2)–(3)).
  • Each form ends: "DO NOT SIGN BLANK OR INCOMPLETE FORMS. RETAIN A COPY."
  • On residential work, an owner may not rely on a waiver handed over by someone other than you, once you have sent a notice of furnishing, without first checking it with you.

Notes on the forms

  • Michigan: waivers of construction liens are executed on the forms of Mich. Comp. Laws § 570.1115(9), used in substantially that format. A partial waiver is the progress kind here and a full waiver the final kind.
  • A claimant paid in full must give the owner, lessee or designee a full unconditional waiver; one paid in part must give a partial unconditional waiver for the amount received if asked (§ 570.1115(2), (3)).
  • A partial or full conditional waiver is effective upon payment of the amount it names (§ 570.1115(4)). A waiver is effective when someone pays relying on it unless they had written notice that its consideration failed (§ 570.1115(6)).
  • Requiring a lien waiver in advance of the work, as part of the contract, is invalid except to the extent payment was actually made (§ 570.1115(1)).

Common questions

Does Michigan have a statutory lien waiver form?

Yes. MCL 570.1115 sets 4 forms: partial and full, conditional and unconditional. How strict: "Shall be used in substantially the following format". The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-25.

Which Michigan lien waiver form goes with which payment?

Partial Conditional Waiver: conditional progress waivers. Partial Unconditional Waiver: unconditional progress waivers. Full Conditional Waiver: conditional final waivers. Full Unconditional Waiver: unconditional final waivers.

When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Michigan?

Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if Michigan has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver.
