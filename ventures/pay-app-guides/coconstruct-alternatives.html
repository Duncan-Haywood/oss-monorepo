What changes, and when

What CoConstruct's own migration page says, read on 27 November 2026 (coconstruct.com/migration):

  • Until 31 March 2027: you can keep adding projects to CoConstruct and keep running active ones.
  • After that: new projects can no longer be added. CoConstruct says no project data will be lost and you keep access to see historical project information.
  • The offered path: a Buildertrend account (created after a credit card verification, with no charge at that point, by the page's description), onboarding and training with an account manager, and finishing active jobs in CoConstruct while new ones start in Buildertrend.

So nothing forces an active job to move mid-stream. The real decisions are where jobs that start after the cut-off will live, and whether to move a long job that will still be running in mid-2027.

Moving a job that is already partly billed

A job that ends within a few months is usually simpler to finish where it is. For a long job, the new tool has to start where the old one left off, or the next application will be wrong. Before you move it, pull these from CoConstruct and keep a copy outside any software:

  1. The contract: the signed contract sum, the retainage rate, and the pay terms (when applications are due and how long the payer has).
  2. The schedule of values or draw schedule: each line and its scheduled value. A spreadsheet is fine; see our free template for the columns.
  3. Approved change orders: number, amount and date approved, and which line each one changed. Pending ones separately: they are not billable yet (change order log).
  4. Billed to date, by line: work completed and stored materials through the last application, and that application's number and period end.
  5. Retainage held to date, and any retainage already released.
  6. Payments received: each check or transfer, its date and reference, so what is still owed carries over.
  7. Lien waivers given and received for each payment, and anything a lender or owner still has to sign.
  8. Documents you will need for warranty or a dispute: plans, the signed change orders, photos, daily logs and the homeowner or GC correspondence.

Check the moved job by its last application: the new tool's "previous applications" total, retainage held and balance to finish should match the last application you sent from CoConstruct to the cent. If they do not, find the difference before you bill again. Our pay app checker compares this month's application against last month's.

Where PayApps fits, and where it does not

PayApps is pay-app billing for subcontractors and small GCs who bill on a schedule of values. For a CoConstruct user it covers:

  • Starting mid-job: a job already billed elsewhere starts from its last application's number, period end and work completed to date per line, so the next application continues the sequence.
  • Bringing the schedule of values in: import it from your own .xlsx or CSV, a PDF of the SOV or continuation sheet, or rows pasted from Excel; the file is read in your browser.
  • Monthly or draw billing: work this period, stored materials, retainage held and released, change orders that add to lines or create new ones, and the application summary and continuation sheet as PDF and CSV.
  • Getting paid: partial payments recorded per application, and a cash and retainage report across jobs.

It does not do selections or allowances, a homeowner portal, cost-plus billing, or residential scheduling with clients. If those are why you used CoConstruct, a builder suite is the better choice.

Common questions

When does CoConstruct stop taking new projects?

CoConstruct's migration page, read on 27 November 2026, says projects can still be added through 31 March 2027. After that, new projects can no longer be added. Existing projects stay readable, so you can finish active jobs and look back at old ones.

Will I lose my CoConstruct project data?

CoConstruct says no project data will be lost and that you keep access to see historical project information. Keep your own copy of anything you need for warranty, lien or tax records anyway: contracts, approved change orders, the billing history and payments received.

Do I have to move to Buildertrend?

No. Buildertrend is the path CoConstruct offers, with a migration specialist and training, but you can choose any software, or split the work between a builder suite and a billing tool. Pick by what your jobs need: selections and a client portal, cost-plus budgets, or fixed-price draw and pay-app billing.

Can I move a job that is half billed?

Yes, if the new tool can start mid-job. You need the contract sum, the schedule of values or draw schedule, approved change orders, what has been billed to date per line, retainage held and payments received. Finishing the job in CoConstruct is the simpler choice when it ends within a few months.
