Billing through a GC's portal: Procore, Textura and GCPay

More general contractors now ask subs to bill inside their payment portal instead of emailing a pay application. The numbers are the same ones on your continuation sheet (the G703-style page: scheduled value, work from previous applications, this period, materials stored), but each portal wants them in its own place and its own file. This page puts the three portals side by side, quotes the rules their vendors publish, and turns one line's figures into what each one asks for.

Procore

The schedule of values (SSOV)

A GC's Procore commitment has its own schedule of values, the SOV positions. Your detailed schedule, the Subcontractor SOV, hangs underneath them. The GC downloads a template from the commitment (Edit SSOV, then Import CSV, then Download CSV Template) with one row per SOV position, and the detail rows go under each position. Procore's help page "Import a Subcontractor Schedule of Values from a CSV" (read 2026-11-25) sets the rules:

  • Do not add, move or rename columns.
  • Every row needs the SOV Position Number and the SOV Amount.
  • The Subcontractor SOV Amount is a plain number such as 15000: no commas or dollar signs, and 0.00 rather than a blank cell.

Each month's invoice

The invoice is typed in by hand. The invoice contact opens the commitment, clicks Create Invoice and enters, line by line, an amount for the current billing period in Work Completed This Period. Procore's help pages cover exporting an invoice to CSV and importing only the Subcontractor SOV; we found no import for the invoice itself. Stored materials go in one of two ways (Procore, "Create a New Subcontractor Invoice as an Invoice Contact", searched 2026-11-26):

  • The older form's Materials Presently Stored: "the amount of the materials being stored during the current billing period".
  • The newer form's New Materials Stored: "the amount of any new materials being stored for the current billing period. Your entry should exclude any costs entered in the 'Work Completed this Period' column". It appears only when the GC turned on "Enable Billing Separately for Stored Materials".

Approved commitment change orders are listed as their own invoice lines, apart from the SOV positions.

Oracle Textura Payment Management

A subcontractor's invoice can be filled from a file. In the billing workflow, the Progress tab's More menu has Upload Billing Amounts. The dialog offers This Period Billing Amounts or To Date Billing Amounts and a Download Template button, which Oracle describes as "a pre-formatted Excel spreadsheet for uploading. The template includes phase codes, description, budget amounts, and the current billing amounts for the line items in your contract." You "update the file with desired billing and retention values", save it as CSV and upload it. And: "If your retention amounts do not meet the retention settings, the system will override the amounts in the file." (Oracle, "Importing a Payment Application from a File for Subcontractors" and the Subcontractor Billing Workflow Guide, North America; read 2026-11-26.)

  • This Period upload: your column E goes in Work This Period, and materials newly stored this month in Material Stored This Period.
  • To Date upload: Completed to Date is, in Oracle's words, "the total monetary value ... for the current and previous periods", so it takes your column G (work completed plus materials presently stored). Where the template has a stored-to-date column, it takes column F. (Oracle, "Progress and Retention Tab Fields Overview" and "Recording Progress Billing for Subcontractors", read 2026-11-26.)
  • Oracle does not publish the template's column headings, so work in the file the GC's project gives you: keep its header rows, columns and their order, and change only the billing cells. Typing amounts as plain numbers (1234.56, no commas or $) is the safe choice for a CSV.

GCPay

When the GC has entered your contract amount (a "constrained" schedule of values), you enter the breakdown by hand or with the Line Items section's Upload button. From GCPay's Subcontractor Training Guide (Rev. 12/19, "Creating a Schedule of Values" and "Uploading a Schedule of Values via Excel Upload", as published by a GC for its subs; read 2026-11-26):

  • "A standard Schedule of Values will be 3 columns: Item Number, Description, Value while a Unit Price Schedule of Values will be 5 Columns: Item Number, Description, Units, Unit Description, Unit Value".
  • Use the GCPay Excel template: "the system will not upload a document saved as any other file format", and remove "any and all information outside of the following: item number, description, & value."
  • "The sum of values entered should equal your Original Contract Value. You will not be able to submit if you contract totals do not match the amount entered by your General Contractor."
  • "Change orders will be added by your General Contractor AFTER your base contract value has been approved in GCPay."

Billing is typed into the $ This Period (or % Work to Date) column; the guide describes no billing upload, and it does not say where stored materials go, so ask the GC how they want them shown. The check below catches the usual reasons an upload is refused.

Other portals

Trimble Pay (formerly Flashtract) and Siteline are also used to bill GCs; this page does not cover their screens yet. If a GC sends you to a portal not listed here, ask for its subcontractor guide and the template it uses before your first billing date, since a schedule of values that is refused usually holds up the first payment.

Common questions

Can I upload my pay application to Procore?

Not the monthly invoice, as far as Procore's help pages go: they cover exporting an invoice to CSV and importing only the Subcontractor Schedule of Values. The invoice contact types each line's Work Completed This Period and stored materials into the invoice by hand. The SOV detail can be imported from the CSV template the GC downloads from the commitment.

How do I upload billing amounts to Textura?

In the billing workflow, open the Progress tab, choose More, then Upload Billing Amounts. Pick This Period Billing Amounts or To Date Billing Amounts, download the template, fill in the billing and retention values, save it as CSV and upload it. Oracle says that if the retention amounts do not meet the draw's retention settings, the system overrides the amounts in the file.

Why won't GCPay let me submit my schedule of values?

GCPay's subcontractor guide says the values must add up to the Original Contract Value the general contractor entered, or you cannot submit. Leave change orders out: the GC adds them after the base schedule is approved. The upload takes the GCPay Excel template with three columns: Item Number, Description and Value.

What goes in Procore's New Materials Stored column?

Procore asks for the amount of any new materials stored in the current billing period, excluding anything already entered as Work Completed This Period. It only appears when the GC has turned on billing stored materials separately. If your stored balance only rose, the new amount is the rise; if it fell because material was installed, take the new deliveries from your delivery tickets.
