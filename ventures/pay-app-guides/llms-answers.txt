# Pay app guides: schedule of values calculator and pay application guides: answers

The questions each page answers in its "Common questions" section, with the answer as the page states it. Each link opens that section; the page has the sources.

- [What is a schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/#faq): A schedule of values (SOV) splits the contract sum into line items, each with a dollar value. Every pay application bills against those lines, so the owner can see how much of each part of the work is done.
- [How is retainage calculated on a continuation sheet?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/#faq): Retainage is a percentage of the work completed to date, and often of stored materials too, at the rate your contract sets. This page multiplies each line's completed work by the work rate and its stored materials by the stored rate, rounds each line to the cent, adds them, and subtracts any retainage already released.
- [What is balance to finish?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/#faq): Balance to finish is the scheduled value less the total completed and stored to date. Balance to finish including retainage adds back the retainage still held, because that money is also still to be paid.
- [Is this the AIA G702 or G703 form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/#faq): No. The page does the same kind of arithmetic in its own layout. The AIA forms are copyrighted and this page is not affiliated with the American Institute of Architects. If your owner requires the AIA form, copy the numbers into your licensed copy.
- [Does Arizona have a statutory lien waiver form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/arizona-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Yes. A.R.S. § 33-1008 sets 4 forms: conditional and unconditional, progress and final. How strict: Unenforceable "unless it follows substantially the following forms". The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-25.
- [Which Arizona lien waiver form goes with which payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/arizona-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Conditional waiver and release on progress payment: conditional progress waivers. Unconditional waiver and release on progress payment: unconditional progress waivers. Conditional waiver and release on final payment: conditional final waivers. Unconditional waiver and release on final payment: unconditional final waivers.
- [When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Arizona?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/arizona-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if Arizona has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver. This page is not legal advice; ask a Arizona construction attorney before signing anything you are unsure of.
- [Does California have a statutory lien waiver form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Yes. Civ. Code §§ 8132–8138 sets 4 forms: conditional and unconditional, progress and final. How strict: "Null, void, and unenforceable unless it is in substantially the following form". The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-25.
- [Which California lien waiver form goes with which payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Conditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment: conditional progress waivers. Unconditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment: unconditional progress waivers. Conditional Waiver and Release on Final Payment: conditional final waivers. Unconditional Waiver and Release on Final Payment: unconditional final waivers.
- [When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in California?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if California has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver. This page is not legal advice; ask a California construction attorney before signing anything you are unsure of.
- [Who has to send a California preliminary notice?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-preliminary-notice.html#faq): Before recording a lien claim, giving a stop payment notice or claiming on a payment bond, a claimant gives preliminary notice to the owner, the direct contractor and the construction lender, if any (Cal. Civ. Code § 8200(a)). A laborer does not have to, and a claimant with a direct contract with the owner gives it only to the construction lender, if any (§ 8200(e)).
- [When is the California preliminary notice due?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-preliminary-notice.html#faq): Not later than 20 days after you first furnished work on the job (§ 8204(a)). By personal delivery, certified or registered mail, express mail or an overnight carrier the notice is given the day it is delivered or deposited; left and mailed under Code Civ. Proc. § 415.20 it is given five days after mailing (§ 8116).
- [What if I send the preliminary notice late?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-preliminary-notice.html#faq): You can still give it, but a lien, stop payment notice or bond claim then covers only work performed within the 20 days before the notice and after (§ 8204(a)). Work before that window is not covered.
- [Is there an official California preliminary notice form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/california-preliminary-notice.html#faq): No. The statute sets the contents, not a form: the names and addresses of the owner, direct contractor and lender, the site, who you are and your relationship to the parties, the work, who hired you, an estimate of the total price, and the NOTICE TO PROPERTY OWNER statement in boldface (§§ 8102, 8202).
- [Does listing the GC as additional insured on the certificate make them one?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/certificate-of-insurance-for-subcontractors.html#faq): No. The ACORD 25 says it is issued for information only and confers no rights on the certificate holder, and that an additional insured needs the policy itself to provide for it or be endorsed. Send the additional insured endorsement page with the certificate.
- [What is the difference between waiver of subrogation and primary and noncontributory?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/certificate-of-insurance-for-subcontractors.html#faq): Primary and noncontributory decides whose policy pays first: yours pays before the GC's own policy and does not ask it to share. Waiver of subrogation decides what your insurer can do after it pays: it gives up the right to recover that payment from the parties named. A subcontract can ask for both, and each usually needs its own endorsement.
- [Can a GC hold my pay application because my certificate expired?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/certificate-of-insurance-for-subcontractors.html#faq): Many subcontracts make current certificates a condition of payment, so check yours. Either way, a lapsed certificate on file is a common reason a pay application stalls, so send the renewal to every GC holding the old one before the expiry date.
- [Who issues the certificate of insurance?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/certificate-of-insurance-for-subcontractors.html#faq): Your insurance agent or broker, the producer named in the top box of the ACORD 25. Ask them for one per GC, with that GC (and anyone else the exhibit names) as certificate holder, and for the endorsement pages the exhibit asks for.
- [What goes in a change order log?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-order-log.html#faq): One row per change: its number, a description, its status (pending, approved, rejected or void), the amount, any days of schedule time it asks for, the date it was submitted, the date it was approved, and what has been billed against it.
- [Can I bill a pending change order on my pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-order-log.html#faq): Usually not. Most pay applications only carry executed change orders, so pending work is tracked in the log and billed the month it is signed. Some subcontracts allow billing work approved in principle; check yours.
- [How do approved change orders get onto the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-order-log.html#faq): Each approved change order becomes its own line, CO-1, CO-2 and so on, with its full amount as the scheduled value; a deductive change order is a negative line. This page writes those lines for you in the columns the free pay application generator reads. Worked example: change orders and the schedule of values.
- [How do I follow up on a change order the GC has not signed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-order-log.html#faq): In writing, to the person who approves change orders on the job (usually the GC's project manager, not the billing contact), restating the number, the scope, the price and any time it asks for, and asking what is needed to sign it. Note the date of each follow-up next to the date submitted, so the log shows how long it has waited and how often you asked. Your subcontract may set how long the GC has to answer, a notice period for claims, or what happens if nobody answers; check it, because this page does not.
- [How do I add a change order to the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-orders-schedule-of-values.html#faq): Give each approved change order its own line, CO-1, CO-2 and so on, with its full value as the scheduled value. Do not raise the value of an original line: the original contract sum stays as it was bid.
- [Can I bill a pending change order on my pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-orders-schedule-of-values.html#faq): Usually not. A change order request, a pending change order or a signed T&M ticket with no executed change order is usually not billable yet: track it separately and bill it the month it is signed. Some subcontracts allow billing approved-in-principle work, so check yours.
- [How do I bill a deductive change order?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/change-orders-schedule-of-values.html#faq): As a negative line that reduces the contract sum to date. Bill it as completed in the period the credit applies, so the balance to finish on that line reaches zero. Retainage applies to change-order work too, unless the change order says otherwise.
- [When does CoConstruct stop taking new projects?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/coconstruct-alternatives.html#faq): CoConstruct's migration page, read on 27 November 2026, says projects can still be added through 31 March 2027. After that, new projects can no longer be added. Existing projects stay readable, so you can finish active jobs and look back at old ones.
- [Will I lose my CoConstruct project data?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/coconstruct-alternatives.html#faq): CoConstruct says no project data will be lost and that you keep access to see historical project information. Keep your own copy of anything you need for warranty, lien or tax records anyway: contracts, approved change orders, the billing history and payments received.
- [Do I have to move to Buildertrend?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/coconstruct-alternatives.html#faq): No. Buildertrend is the path CoConstruct offers, with a migration specialist and training, but you can choose any software, or split the work between a builder suite and a billing tool. Pick by what your jobs need: selections and a client portal, cost-plus budgets, or fixed-price draw and pay-app billing.
- [Can I move a job that is half billed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/coconstruct-alternatives.html#faq): Yes, if the new tool can start mid-job. You need the contract sum, the schedule of values or draw schedule, approved change orders, what has been billed to date per line, retainage held and payments received. Finishing the job in CoConstruct is the simpler choice when it ends within a few months.
- [How do you bill a unit-price contract on a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/concrete-contractor-billing.html#faq): Each line carries a unit, a unit price and a contract quantity. Each month you report the quantity placed this period; the amount is that quantity times the unit price, and the to-date quantity shows how much of the contract quantity is used.
- [What happens when concrete quantities run over the contract quantity?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/concrete-contractor-billing.html#faq): Under most subcontracts the extra quantity needs an approved change order before it is paid. Track quantity to date against the contract quantity and ask for the change before the overrun.
- [How should pump time and standby be billed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/concrete-contractor-billing.html#faq): As extra work on a T&M ticket signed the same day, showing the pump, crew hours, equipment and any material, unless your subcontract already prices it.
- [Can lump-sum concrete work be billed by percent complete?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/concrete-contractor-billing.html#faq): Yes. Split the schedule of values by element and area (footings, walls, slabs, decks, flatwork) and bill each line's percent complete.
- [What is the difference between a conditional and an unconditional lien waiver?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/conditional-vs-unconditional-lien-waivers.html#faq): A conditional waiver gives up your lien rights only once the stated payment is received and clears. An unconditional waiver gives them up whether or not you were paid, so sign one only after the money is in your account.
- [Which lien waivers do I send with a monthly pay app?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/conditional-vs-unconditional-lien-waivers.html#faq): A common, safe routine is a conditional progress waiver for this month's amount and an unconditional progress waiver for last month's payment, which has now cleared.
- [What should a progress lien waiver exclude?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/conditional-vs-unconditional-lien-waivers.html#faq): List what you are not waiving: retainage, disputed or unapproved change orders, extra work billed but unpaid, and earlier unpaid progress payments. The waiver should cover work through a stated date and a stated amount, not "all work to date".
- [How should a drywall contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/drywall-contractor-billing.html#faq): By floor or area and by phase (framing, board, tape and finish, ceilings, punch), with stocked material on its own line. Each month's percent then comes from what is done on each floor.
- [Can I bill for drywall board delivered to the floor but not hung?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/drywall-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often yes, as stored materials, if your subcontract allows it; attach delivery tickets and photos. Move it to work completed as it is hung.
- [How do I dispute a back charge on a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/drywall-contractor-billing.html#faq): Ask for it in writing with its basis (who, what, when, cost), compare it with your subcontract's back-charge clause, and answer in writing. Keep it on your log so it is not forgotten when the next check arrives.
- [How do I get paid for extra work caused by other trades?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/drywall-contractor-billing.html#faq): Write a T&M ticket or change order request the day it happens and get it signed by the GC's superintendent, then bill it once approved.
- [Can I bill for switchgear before it is installed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/electrical-contractor-billing.html#faq): Usually, as stored materials, if your subcontract allows it and you attach what it asks for (invoices, delivery proof, photos, and for off-site storage often insurance and title papers). Move it from stored to work completed in the month it is installed so it is billed once.
- [How should an electrical contractor break down the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/electrical-contractor-billing.html#faq): By area or floor and by phase (underground, rough-in, gear, fixtures, trim, low voltage, testing and close-out), with expensive material on its own lines. Small lines make each month's percent easy to check and keep late work funded.
- [Do electrical subs need certified payroll?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/electrical-contractor-billing.html#faq): On federal and federally assisted construction covered by Davis-Bacon, yes: every contractor and subcontractor submits a weekly certified payroll for the weeks it has workers on site. Several states require the same on state public works, some in their own online systems.
- [How do I get paid for added circuits and devices?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/electrical-contractor-billing.html#faq): Write a T&M ticket or change order request and get it signed before or on the day of the work, then bill it once the change order is approved. Bills for unsigned extras are the ones most often refused.
- [How should a millwork contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/finish-carpentry-contractor-billing.html#faq): By floor or area and by type of work (casework, countertops, trim and paneling, doors and hardware), with shop drawings, attic stock and punch on small lines of their own. Each month's percent then comes from what is installed on each floor.
- [Can I bill for casework built in my shop before it is delivered?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/finish-carpentry-contractor-billing.html#faq): Only if your subcontract allows stored materials off site. Expect to show invoices and photos, and often a bill of sale, insurance on the stored goods and a right for the GC to inspect them. Move them to work completed as they are installed, so they are billed once.
- [Should shop drawings be their own line on the pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/finish-carpentry-contractor-billing.html#faq): Yes, if the subcontract allows it, as a small line billed when the drawings are approved. A large line billed before approval reads as front-loading.
- [What happens when field dimensions do not match the drawings?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/finish-carpentry-contractor-billing.html#faq): Send an RFI before you build, with the dimension you measured and a proposed answer. If the answer adds work, price it as a change order before you do it.
- [How should a flooring contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/flooring-contractor-billing.html#faq): By floor or area and by material (carpet, resilient, tile, base), with moisture testing, floor prep and attic stock on lines of their own. Each month's percent then comes from what is laid on each floor.
- [Who pays for slab moisture mitigation?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/flooring-contractor-billing.html#faq): Whoever your subcontract and the specifications say. If it is not in your price, report the test results, ask for a change order before you apply anything and keep the results with it.
- [Can I bill for flooring delivered but not installed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/flooring-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often yes, as stored materials, if your subcontract allows it; attach invoices, delivery tickets and photos. Move it to work completed as it is installed, so it is billed once.
- [What is attic stock, and when is it billed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/flooring-contractor-billing.html#faq): Spare flooring the specifications ask you to leave with the owner for future repairs. Bill it when it is handed over, and get the hand-over signed: it is usually on the close-out list final retainage waits on.
- [Does Florida have a statutory lien waiver form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Yes. Fla. Stat. § 713.20 sets 2 forms: progress and final. How strict: Forms "may" be used, but no one may require a different form. The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-25.
- [Which Florida lien waiver form goes with which payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Waiver and Release of Lien upon Progress Payment: unconditional progress waivers. Waiver and Release of Lien upon Final Payment: unconditional final waivers. Florida's statute has no separate conditional waiver form. Fla. Stat. § 713.20(4) and (5) give one form for a progress payment and one for the final payment, each a waiver and release given in consideration of the payment, with no condition in its text; this page lists those two as the unconditional waivers. § 713.20(7) lets a lienor who signs one in exchange for a check condition it on payment of the check, but the statute gives no wording for that condition, and this page does not supply any. Before signing a Florida waiver ahead of payment, ask counsel.
- [When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Florida?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if Florida has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver. This page is not legal advice; ask a Florida construction attorney before signing anything you are unsure of.
- [Who has to send a Florida Notice to Owner?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-notice-to-owner.html#faq): Subcontractors, sub-subcontractors and suppliers who did not contract with the owner. Laborers are excepted, and a contractor who contracted with the owner does not serve one (Fla. Stat. § 713.06(2)(a)). Sub-subcontractors and suppliers to a subcontractor also serve a copy on the contractor, and everyone serves a copy on anyone the notice of commencement names to receive one.
- [What is the deadline for a Florida Notice to Owner?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-notice-to-owner.html#faq): Before you start, or not later than 45 days after you first furnish labor, services or materials, and in any event before the owner's final payment to the contractor after the contractor's final affidavit (§ 713.06(2)(a)). Sent by certified, registered or Global Express Guaranteed mail within 40 days, with a mail log or USPS tracking, it counts as served on the day it is mailed (§ 713.18(2)).
- [What happens if I serve the Notice to Owner late?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-notice-to-owner.html#faq): Florida does not save part of the work the way some states do. Failing to serve the notice, or to serve it on time, is a complete defense to enforcement of the lien by any person, and the time requirements must be strictly complied with (§ 713.06(2)(a), (f)). Small errors in the contents can be forgiven; the date cannot.
- [Is there an official Florida Notice to Owner form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-notice-to-owner.html#faq): Yes. Section 713.06(2)(c) prints the form, and the notice may be in substantially that form but must include its information and its warning. The draft on this page is that form word for word with your details in the blanks.
- [How long do I have to record a claim of lien in Florida?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/florida-notice-to-owner.html#faq): Not later than 90 days after your final furnishing of labor, services or materials (§ 713.08(5)), and the claim of lien must be served on the owner before recording or within 15 days after (§ 713.08(4)(c)). Serving the Notice to Owner does not replace recording the claim of lien.
- [What is a front-loaded schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/front-loading-schedule-of-values.html#faq): One where the lines billed early in the job (mobilization, submittals, early rough-in) carry more than their share of the contract's markup, and the lines billed late (testing, closeout, punch list) carry less. The sub collects cash ahead of cost; the owner pays for work before it is done.
- [Is front-loading a schedule of values allowed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/front-loading-schedule-of-values.html#faq): Contracts rarely ban it by name, but most let the GC, architect or owner's reviewer reject a schedule of values they think is unbalanced, and many cap mobilization. A reviewer who finds front-loading can send the SOV back, rebalance it, or hold the early pay apps. Read your contract's schedule of values and payment articles.
- [How do I check whether my SOV is front-loaded?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/front-loading-schedule-of-values.html#faq): Put your estimated cost next to each line's scheduled value. If every line carries about the same markup on cost as the whole job, the SOV is balanced. Lines well above the job's markup that bill early, and lines below it (or below cost) that bill late, are the front-loading.
- [What is a balanced schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/front-loading-schedule-of-values.html#faq): One where each line's scheduled value is its cost times the same factor, the contract sum divided by total cost, so every line carries the same markup and billing tracks cost as the work goes.
- [Can I upload my pay application to Procore?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/gc-portal-billing.html#faq): Not the monthly invoice, as far as Procore's help pages go: they cover exporting an invoice to CSV and importing only the Subcontractor Schedule of Values. The invoice contact types each line's Work Completed This Period and stored materials into the invoice by hand. The SOV detail can be imported from the CSV template the GC downloads from the commitment.
- [How do I upload billing amounts to Textura?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/gc-portal-billing.html#faq): In the billing workflow, open the Progress tab, choose More, then Upload Billing Amounts. Pick This Period Billing Amounts or To Date Billing Amounts, download the template, fill in the billing and retention values, save it as CSV and upload it. Oracle says that if the retention amounts do not meet the draw's retention settings, the system overrides the amounts in the file.
- [Why won't GCPay let me submit my schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/gc-portal-billing.html#faq): GCPay's subcontractor guide says the values must add up to the Original Contract Value the general contractor entered, or you cannot submit. Leave change orders out: the GC adds them after the base schedule is approved. The upload takes the GCPay Excel template with three columns: Item Number, Description and Value.
- [What goes in Procore's New Materials Stored column?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/gc-portal-billing.html#faq): Procore asks for the amount of any new materials stored in the current billing period, excluding anything already entered as Work Completed This Period. It only appears when the GC has turned on billing stored materials separately. If your stored balance only rose, the new amount is the rise; if it fell because material was installed, take the new deliveries from your delivery tickets.
- [Does Georgia have a statutory lien waiver form?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/georgia-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Yes. O.C.G.A. § 44-14-366 sets 2 forms: interim payment and final payment, each waiving "upon the receipt of the sum". How strict: "Shall substantially follow the language of the following form", "in at least 12 point font"; no statement waives lien or bond rights unless it is one of these forms and you have been paid. The forms on this page are printed word for word from the statute as read on 2026-11-26.
- [Which Georgia lien waiver form goes with which payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/georgia-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Waiver and Release of Lien and Payment Bond Rights upon Interim Payment: progress payments, before or after the money clears (the form itself is neither conditional nor unconditional). Waiver and Release of Lien and Payment Bond Rights upon Final Payment: final payments, before or after the money clears (the form itself is neither conditional nor unconditional).
- [When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Georgia?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/georgia-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. Before that, give the conditional form if Georgia has one, and keep retainage, unapproved change orders and disputed amounts out of a progress waiver. This page is not legal advice; ask a Georgia construction attorney before signing anything you are unsure of.
- [How should a glazing contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/glazing-contractor-billing.html#faq): By elevation or area and by system (curtain wall, storefront, windows, entrances, interior glass), with engineering, the mockup and testing on lines of their own, and material split from install where the subcontract allows stored materials.
- [Can I bill for curtain wall or glass stored at the fabricator?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/glazing-contractor-billing.html#faq): Only if your subcontract allows stored materials off site. Expect to show invoices and photos, and often a bill of sale, insurance on the stored goods and a right for the GC to inspect them. Move them to work completed as they are set, so they are billed once.
- [Should the mockup and water testing be their own lines?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/glazing-contractor-billing.html#faq): Yes, when the specifications ask for them: they are real work billed when done and passed, and separate lines keep them from being argued into the wall price. A retest caused by another trade is a change order.
- [Why is my final glazing retainage held?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/glazing-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often because close-out is not complete: the punch list, final cleaning, the warranties or a final lien waiver. Ask the GC in writing which items are open, and send them together.
- [What does "less previous certificates for payment" mean on a pay app?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-fill-out-a-pay-application.html#faq): It is the sum of the payment-due amounts certified on earlier applications: not what you were actually paid, and not what you billed.
- [How do I fill in "work completed from previous application"?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-fill-out-a-pay-application.html#faq): It is the total billed on the line on all earlier applications: last month's total completed and stored for the line minus last month's materials presently stored. - [Why do pay applications get rejected?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-fill-out-a-pay-application.html#faq): Common reasons are a line billed over its scheduled value, "from previous" not matching last month's approved numbers, billing a change order that is not yet signed, retainage at the wrong rate, stored materials without backup, and missing waivers.
- [What is an RFI in construction?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-write-an-rfi.html#faq): A request for information: a written question to the GC, and through it the architect or engineer, when the drawings or specs conflict, leave something out or cannot be built as shown. The answer becomes part of the job record, so it should be in writing, numbered and dated.
- [Does an RFI answer authorize extra work?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-write-an-rfi.html#faq): Not by itself, on most subcontracts. An answer that changes your scope, cost or time usually still needs a change order (or a written directive) under your subcontract's changes clause. Price it, send a change order request that names the RFI, and keep the notice deadline in your subcontract.
- [Who should a subcontractor send an RFI to?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-write-an-rfi.html#faq): Usually the GC's project manager, or whoever your subcontract names, not the architect directly. The GC forwards it to the design team, keeps its own log and sends you the answer.
- [How long should an RFI take to answer?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-write-an-rfi.html#faq): Your subcontract, the prime contract or the job's procedures may set a response time; read them. Otherwise ask for a date tied to when the work needs the answer, say what is waiting on it, and log the date so a late answer can back a time claim.
- [Should one RFI ask more than one question?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/how-to-write-an-rfi.html#faq): No. One question per RFI gets each answered, tracked and closed on its own. - [What is a joint check in construction?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/joint-checks.html#faq): A payment from the GC or owner made out to a subcontractor and its supplier or lower-tier sub, so the supplier has to endorse before the money moves. - [What is the joint check rule?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/joint-checks.html#faq): In states that follow it, a supplier that endorses a joint check with no written split can be treated as paid what it was owed, up to the check amount, even if the sub kept the money. - [What is overbilling in construction?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/overbilling-underbilling.html#faq): Billing ahead of the work: billed to date is more than earned revenue, where earned revenue is percent complete (cost to date divided by estimated total cost) times the contract sum. On the balance sheet it is a liability, often called billings in excess of costs and estimated earnings.
- [What causes underbilling?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/overbilling-underbilling.html#faq): Most often work on an unapproved change order, whose cost is in the job cost report while the billing waits for a signature. A cost overrun on an estimate that is not updated, a GC cutting your percent complete, and materials charged to the job before they are billed can cause it too.
- [Why do sureties care about overbilling and underbilling?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/overbilling-underbilling.html#faq): A large overbilling means the cash in the bank is partly borrowed from the end of the job. Underbilling is a soft asset that counts profit you have not billed and may never collect; underwriters often discount it, and a pattern of underbillings across jobs is a sign they read as estimates going wrong.
- [Why did the GC pay less than my pay application asked for?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/paid-short.html#faq): Usually one of three causes: the application was certified for less than you applied for, the GC deducted a backcharge, or the GC is holding money without saying why, often for a missing document such as a lien waiver, certificate of insurance, certified payroll or sworn statement.
- [What lien waiver should I sign after a short payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/paid-short.html#faq): One for each payment you actually received, for that amount, once it has cleared. Keep the unpaid balance under a conditional waiver or out of the waiver altogether, and check whether your state fixes the waiver wording by statute.
- [How do I record a short payment in my books?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/paid-short.html#faq): As what it is: a backcharge you accept reduces the job's contract income, a balance you will not collect is a write-off, and a balance you still dispute is a claim you track until it is settled. In QuickBooks that is usually a credit memo against the pay application's invoice; ask your accountant which account it should hit.
- [How should a painting contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/painting-contractor-billing.html#faq): By floor or area and by phase (prime, finish coats, wallcovering, special coatings, exterior), with touch-up and punch on a small line of its own. Each month's percent then comes from what is done on each floor.
- [How do I get paid for repainting damage caused by other trades?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/painting-contractor-billing.html#faq): Photograph the damage, write a T&M ticket or change order request naming it and get it signed by the GC's superintendent before the repair, then bill it once approved.
- [Can a painting contractor bill for wallcovering before it is hung?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/painting-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often yes, as stored materials, if your subcontract allows it; attach the invoices. Move it to work completed as it is hung, so it is billed once.
- [When is a painter's retainage released?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/painting-contractor-billing.html#faq): When your subcontract says, which is often after the GC is paid its own retainage and the punch list is accepted. Ask which items are open in writing, and mark any that are other trades' damage.
- [When will I get paid on a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-app-cash-forecast.html#faq): Add up the chain: the date you submit (the GC's cutoff day), the days the owner takes to pay the GC after it receives the application, and the days the GC takes to pay you after the owner pays it. A 25th cutoff with 30-day owner terms and a GC that pays 10 days later puts the money about 40 days after the cutoff. Your subcontract, and on some jobs a prompt payment statute, set the GC's part.
- [Why do subcontractors run short of cash on profitable jobs?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-app-cash-forecast.html#faq): Payroll and suppliers are paid every week or two, but pay applications go in once a month and are paid 30 to 60 days later, less retainage. Work done just after a cutoff can wait more than two months to become cash. A week-by-week forecast shows the gap before it arrives.
- [Why do pay applications get sent back?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-application-checker.html#faq): Most often because this month's sheet does not match last month's approved one: a "from previous" column copied from the submitted file instead of the approved one, an edited scheduled value, a new line with no change order behind it, previous certificates typed as last month's payment, or stored materials that vanish or are billed twice.
- [What goes in previous certificates for payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-application-checker.html#faq): Last month's total earned less retainage, which is the amount certified to date. - [What is the difference between pay-if-paid and pay-when-paid?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-if-paid-clauses.html#faq): Pay-when-paid sets the time for payment: the GC pays after the owner pays, or within a reasonable time. - [Is a pay-if-paid clause enforceable?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-if-paid-clauses.html#faq): It depends on the state. North Carolina and South Carolina by statute, and California and New York by their highest courts, will not let the owner's payment be a condition of yours. - [What wording should I ask for instead of pay-if-paid?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/pay-if-paid-clauses.html#faq): Ask for timing wording with an outside date, such as payment within 7 days after the contractor receives payment and in any event within 45 days after the pay application is approved, and strike "condition precedent" and "assumes the risk".
- [What is progress billing in construction?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/progress-billing.html#faq): Billing a job in parts as the work gets done, instead of one invoice at the end. - [How is a deposit handled on progress invoices?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/progress-billing.html#faq): A deposit is money for work not done yet, so it is credited back against the invoices as the work is billed. The common ways are to spread it over every stage in proportion to its value, or to take it all off the first invoice. Either way, the credits should add up to the deposit by the final invoice.
- [Is progress billing the same as a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/progress-billing.html#faq): A pay application is one form of progress billing: the percent-complete method written out line by line on a schedule of values, with retainage and the previous certificates, usually monthly. Homeowner and small commercial jobs often use a simpler draw schedule instead.
- [Does QuickBooks Online do progress billing?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/progress-billing.html#faq): Yes, from an accepted estimate, once progress invoicing is switched on in the Sales settings: each invoice bills a percent or an amount of each line, and the estimate keeps track of what is left. - [Does prompt payment interest stop my lien deadline?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/prompt-payment-laws.html#faq): No. Prompt payment interest does not stop the clock on mechanics lien or bond claim deadlines, so watch those dates at the same time.
- [What should I do first when a GC pays late?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/prompt-payment-laws.html#faq): Find out when the owner paid, since the deadline counts from that date, then put it in writing: name the pay application, the amount, the statute and the interest so far. Several of these laws make suspending work or claiming interest depend on written notice.
- [What is a punch list in construction?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/punch-list.html#faq): The list of small items still to fix or finish near the end of a job, written at a walk-through by the GC, architect, owner or an inspector. Each item is fixed, marked done, then back-checked and accepted by whoever listed it.
- [Who is responsible for punch list items?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/punch-list.html#faq): The trade whose work the item is. An item that is another trade's work, or work built as the drawings show, should be raised with the GC in writing straight away, so it is reassigned instead of staying on your list.
- [Does the punch list hold up retainage?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/punch-list.html#faq): Often. Many subcontracts tie retainage release to completion and acceptance of your scope, and an open punch item is the most common reason given for holding the balance. Your subcontract says what release actually depends on.
- [How is retainage calculated on a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-explained.html#faq): Retainage held is the work completed to date times the work rate, plus stored materials times the stored rate, minus retainage already released. It is a percentage of everything completed and stored to date, not of this month's bill alone.
- [Can a GC hold more retainage from a sub than the owner holds from the GC?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-explained.html#faq): Several state statutes say the prime contractor cannot hold back a higher percentage from a subcontractor than the owner holds from the prime; California and Texas say so directly. A 10% sub retainage on a 5% public job is worth a question.
- [When is retainage released?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-explained.html#faq): The balance is released at substantial or final completion, usually against a final lien waiver, closeout documents and sometimes a punch list. Many contracts, and some statutes, cut the rate once the job is 50% complete.
- [Does QuickBooks Online track retainage?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-in-quickbooks.html#faq): Not as a built-in field. The usual workaround is a Retainage Receivable account (Other Current Assets, detail type Retainage) and a Retainage item: a negative line holds the money back on each pay app invoice, and a positive line on a later invoice bills it when it is released.
- [How do I bill released retainage in QuickBooks Online?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-in-quickbooks.html#faq): Add the Retainage item as a positive line for the amount released. No new income is booked, because it was booked when the work was billed: the amount moves from Retainage Receivable to accounts receivable, and the customer's payment clears it like any other invoice.
- [Why is retainage missing from my A/R aging report?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-in-quickbooks.html#faq): Because retainage sits in an asset account rather than accounts receivable, the standard A/R aging report does not show it. Open Retainage Receivable from the chart of accounts, or run Transaction Detail by Account filtered to that account, and group by customer and project to see it per job.
- [When is retainage due to a subcontractor?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-release.html#faq): Your subcontract usually ties release to an event (substantial completion of the project, final acceptance, or the GC being paid its own retainage) plus a number of days. A statute may set an outer limit, mostly on public work.
- [What holds up a retainage release?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-release.html#faq): An open punch list item is the most common reason given. A missing final pay application, final conditional lien waiver, lower-tier waivers, consent of surety on bonded jobs, or closeout documents such as warranties, O&M manuals and as-builts can hold it up too.
- [How do I get retainage released sooner?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-release.html#faq): Negotiate the rate and release terms before you sign, bill retainage as its own line, collect closeout documents as you go, and ask in writing on a schedule. Watch your lien and bond claim deadlines while you wait, so they do not pass.
- [How do I track retainage across jobs?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-tracker.html#faq): Keep one row per job with the retainage held to date, the retainage released, the completion date, the number of days your subcontract allows for release after completion, and the date you billed the release. Outstanding retainage is held minus released; the release is due on the completion date plus the release days.
- [Why does my A/R aging report not show retainage?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-tracker.html#faq): Retainage is usually not invoiced until it is released, so in many setups (QuickBooks Online among them) it sits in a separate retainage receivable account and never ages like an open invoice. A retainage tracker ages it from the date the release fell due instead. See retainage in QuickBooks.
- [When is retainage overdue?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/retainage-tracker.html#faq): When the date your subcontract (or, on some public work, a statute) sets for release has passed and it has not been paid. That date is usually a number of days after substantial or final completion, often conditional on closeout documents and a final lien waiver. This page uses the days you enter; it does not decide what your contract or state law says.
- [How should a roofing contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/roofing-contractor-billing.html#faq): By roof area or building and by system (tear-off, insulation, membrane, flashing and sheet metal), with close-out and the warranty on a small line of its own. Each month's percent then comes from what is done on each roof.
- [Can I bill for roofing materials loaded on the roof but not installed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/roofing-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often yes, as stored materials, if your subcontract allows it; attach invoices, delivery tickets and photos. Move them to work completed as they are installed, so they are billed once.
- [How do I bill deck replacement found during tear-off?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/roofing-contractor-billing.html#faq): At the unit price your subcontract sets for it, if it has one, else as a change order. Measure and photograph the area before it is covered and get the quantity signed by the GC that day.
- [Why is my final roofing retainage held?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/roofing-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often because close-out is not complete: the manufacturer's warranty, the punch list, as-builts or a final lien waiver. Ask the GC in writing which items are open, and send them together.
- [How do you bill unit-price excavation on a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/site-work-contractor-billing.html#faq): Each line carries a unit, a unit price and a contract quantity. Each month you report the quantity installed, measured by survey, count or tickets; the amount is that quantity times the unit price.
- [How do I get paid for rock excavation?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/site-work-contractor-billing.html#faq): Bill it on its own line at the rock unit price if your subcontract has one, else as a change order. Measure it, get the quantity signed in the field before or while it is removed, and keep the measurement as backup.
- [Can I bill for pipe and structures delivered but not installed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/site-work-contractor-billing.html#faq): Often yes, as stored materials, if your subcontract allows it; attach invoices and delivery tickets. Move them to work completed as they are installed, so they are billed once.
- [Are weather days paid on a site work job?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/site-work-contractor-billing.html#faq): Usually as extra time, not money, unless your subcontract says otherwise. Record each day lost in a daily report and ask for the time in writing, so it is there if a delay claim is needed later.
- [How should a steel contractor set up the schedule of values?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/steel-contractor-billing.html#faq): By sequence or area, with detailing, fabrication and erection on separate lines, embeds and anchor bolts on their own line, and miscellaneous metals (stairs, rails, lintels) apart from the frame. Each month's percent then comes from what is approved, fabricated and standing.
- [Can I bill for steel fabricated in my shop before it is delivered?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/steel-contractor-billing.html#faq): Only if your subcontract allows stored materials off site. Expect to show invoices, photos or a fabrication list, and often a bill of sale, insurance on the stored steel and a right for the GC to inspect it. Move it to work completed as it is erected, so it is billed once.
- [Can a steel pay application be billed by the ton?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/steel-contractor-billing.html#faq): Yes, when the subcontract prices it that way: each line carries tons and a unit price, and each month you report the tons fabricated or erected, backed by the shop's fabrication list or the erection sequence.
- [How do I get paid for rework after a design change?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/steel-contractor-billing.html#faq): Send an RFI as soon as the change arrives, naming the pieces already fabricated or erected, and price the rework and extra steel as a change order before you do it.
- [Where do stored materials go on a pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/stored-materials-billing.html#faq): On the continuation sheet they go in their own column, materials presently stored, which holds material bought for the job and not yet installed. It is added to work completed to give total completed and stored to date, and it is not part of the work-completed columns.
- [What happens to stored materials when they are installed?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/stored-materials-billing.html#faq): The installed value leaves the stored column and is billed as work completed this period. Stored now equals stored on the last pay app plus deliveries this period minus what was installed. If installed material is added to work completed without being taken out of stored, it is billed twice.
- [Can I bill for materials stored off site?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/stored-materials-billing.html#faq): Only if your contract allows it. The federal construction payment clause, FAR 52.232-5, counts off-site material only when the contract specifically authorizes it and the contractor shows evidence of title and that the material will be used on that contract. Private contracts vary; read the payment article of yours.
- [Can I bill my markup on stored materials?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/stored-materials-billing.html#faq): It depends on the contract. Some pay stored material at invoice cost and release the rest of the line's value as it is installed. If yours is silent, ask the GC's project accountant before the first pay app rather than after a rejection.
- [What is a contractor's sworn statement?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/sworn-statement.html#faq): A list of everyone who furnished labor or materials to your part of a job, with what each was contracted for, has been paid and is owed now, signed under oath, usually before a notary, and sent with the pay application. Illinois (770 ILCS 60/5) and Michigan (MCL 570.1110) write it into their lien laws.
- [What happens without a sworn statement in Michigan?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/sworn-statement.html#faq): The lien survives, but a contractor is not entitled to payment, and nobody can file to enforce the lien, until the statement is provided. A false statement made knowingly with intent to defraud is a crime.
- [Does a sworn statement have to add up to my contract?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/sworn-statement.html#faq): Yes. All rows together, including one for your own forces, equal your contract sum, and the owing column together equals what this pay application asks for. For each party, completed to date less retainage equals paid plus owing.
- [When is the Texas notice of claim for unpaid labor or materials due?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-notice.html#faq): On a commercial job, by the 15th day of the third month after the month the labor or materials were provided; on a residential construction project, the 15th day of the second month (Tex. Prop. Code § 53.056(a-1)). Each month of unpaid work has its own date: March work is due 15 June on a commercial job and 15 May on a residential one.
- [Who has to send the Texas notice of claim, and to whom?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-notice.html#faq): Every claimant other than the original contractor: subcontractors and suppliers at any tier on an original contract entered into on or after 1 January 2022. The notice goes to the owner or reputed owner and to the original contractor (§ 53.056(a), (a-1)).
- [What if the Texas notice deadline falls on a weekend or holiday?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-notice.html#faq): Section 53.003(e) extends a deadline that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next day that is not one. This page plans on the unmoved date and shows the extended date separately, counting the legal holidays of Gov't Code § 662.021 (the national holidays and the state holidays of § 662.003(b)(1)-(6), not 24 or 26 December).
- [Do I need a separate notice for retainage in Texas?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-notice.html#faq): Yes, for retainage not already included in a monthly notice, if your contract provides for retainage. The notice of claim for unpaid retainage is due by the earlier of the 30th day after your contract is completed, terminated or abandoned, or the 30th day after the original contract is terminated or abandoned (§ 53.057(a), (a-1)).
- [When do I file the Texas lien affidavit?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-notice.html#faq): A subcontractor or supplier files with the county clerk by the 15th day of the fourth month after the month it last provided labor or materials (third month on a residential project), and by the 15th day of the third month after the original contract ended for a retainage lien (§ 53.052(b)-(d)). Send a copy to the owner and the original contractor within five days of filing (§ 53.055).

- [Which Texas lien waiver form goes with which payment?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Conditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment: conditional progress waivers. Unconditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment: unconditional progress waivers. Conditional Waiver and Release on Final Payment: conditional final waivers. Unconditional Waiver and Release on Final Payment: unconditional final waivers.
- [When should I sign an unconditional lien waiver in Texas?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/texas-lien-waiver-forms.html#faq): Only once the payment it covers has cleared your bank. - [Can I bill a T&M ticket on my pay application?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/tm-tickets.html#faq): On most jobs, not until the ticket becomes an executed change order, which then becomes a new line on the schedule of values. - [What should a T&M ticket include?](https://marisstella.app/pay-app-guides/tm-tickets.html#faq): The ticket number and date of the work, where and why it was done, each worker or trade with regular and overtime hours apart, material with quantities, equipment with hours or days, and the GC's signature, printed name and date, signed on the day.

A signature confirms the time and material were spent; it usually does not agree the price or that the work is extra, so the change order still matters. Whether a ticket counts as written notice of extra work is for your subcontract to decide.
