How the numbers are worked out

    
    
      
  • Total completed and stored = from previous applications + this period + materials presently stored.
    • 
      
  • Percent = completed and stored ÷ scheduled value. Balance to finish = scheduled value − completed and stored.
    • 
      
  • Retainage = (previous + this period) × work rate + stored × stored rate, rounded to the cent on each line, less any retainage released.
    • 
      
  • Payment due = completed and stored − retainage held − previous certificates.
    • 
      
  • Not decided here: what your contract or state law allows for retainage and stored materials, whether a change order is approved, sales tax, and whether the owner will certify the amount. See retainage explained for the state caps.

Common questions

    
What is a schedule of values?

    
A schedule of values (SOV) splits the contract sum into line items, each with a dollar value. Every pay application bills against those lines, so the owner can see how much of each part of the work is done.

    
How is retainage calculated on a continuation sheet?

    
Retainage is a percentage of the work completed to date, and often of stored materials too, at the rate your contract sets. This page multiplies each line's completed work by the work rate and its stored materials by the stored rate, rounds each line to the cent, adds them, and subtracts any retainage already released.

    
What is balance to finish?

    
Balance to finish is the scheduled value less the total completed and stored to date. Balance to finish including retainage adds back the retainage still held, because that money is also still to be paid.

    
Is this the AIA G702 or G703 form?

    
No. The page does the same kind of arithmetic in its own layout. The AIA forms are copyrighted and this page is not affiliated with the American Institute of Architects. If your owner requires the AIA form, copy the numbers into your licensed copy.
