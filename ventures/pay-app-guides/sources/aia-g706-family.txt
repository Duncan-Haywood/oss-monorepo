Sources for final-payment-affidavit.html, read 2026-11-28.

These are short excerpts quoted for commentary, not copies of the forms. The forms are
copyrighted by The American Institute of Architects; buy a licensed copy from AIA Contract
Documents to use one. page.test.mjs-style checks in finalpay.test.mjs require every phrase the
page quotes to appear below.

1. AIA Contract Documents preview of G706-1994
   https://contractdocs.aia.org/PreviewFiles/Preview_G706-1994.pdf
   Title: "Contractor's Affidavit of Payment of Debts and Claims"
   Certification (excerpt): "except as listed below, payment has been made in full and all
   obligations have otherwise been satisfied" ... "for all known indebtedness and claims against
   the Contractor"
   Box: "EXCEPTIONS:"
   Supporting documents: "Whenever Surety is involved, Consent of Surety is required."
   "if required by the Owner" ... "Contractor's Release or Waiver of Liens, conditional upon
   receipt of final payment" ... "Separate Releases or Waivers of Liens from Subcontractors and
   material and equipment suppliers, to the extent required by the Owner, accompanied by a list
   thereof" ... "Contractor's Affidavit of Release of Liens (AIA Document G706A)"
   Execution: "Subscribed and sworn to before me on this date:" "Notary Public:"

2. AIA Contract Documents preview of G706A-1994
   https://contractdocs.aia.org/PreviewFiles/Preview_G706A-1994.pdf
   Title: "Contractor's Affidavit of Release of Liens"
   Certification (excerpt): "to the best of the undersigned's knowledge, information and belief,
   except as listed below, the Releases or Waivers of Lien attached hereto include the
   Contractor, all Subcontractors, all suppliers of materials and equipment, and all performers
   of Work, labor or services who have or may have liens"

3. AIA Contract Documents preview of G707-1994
   https://contractdocs.aia.org/PreviewFiles/Preview_G707-1994.pdf
   Title: "Consent of Surety to Final Payment"
   Excerpt: "hereby approves of the final payment to the Contractor, and agrees that final
   payment to the Contractor shall not relieve the Surety of any of its obligations"

4. AIA help centre, "Summary: G706-1994"
   https://help.aiacontracts.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009446741
   "typically executed as a condition of final payment"
   "The contractor may also be required to furnish a lien bond or indemnity bond to protect the
   owner with respect to each exception."
   (This summary also calls G707 "Consent of Surety to Reduction in or Release of Retainage";
   the G707 preview's own title, item 3, is "Consent of Surety to Final Payment", and the page
   uses that.)

5. AIA help centre, "Instructions: G706-1994"
   https://help.aiacontracts.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009309042
   "The contractor submits this affidavit with the final request for payment"
   "If there are any EXCEPTIONS to the statement, these should be listed in the space provided;
   otherwise enter as "None.""
   "The affidavit requires notarization and must be signed by the contractor or authorized
   representative."
   "Indicate the state and county where the Affidavit is made. This is not necessarily the same
   location as the Project"

6. AIA help centre, "Summary: G706A-1994"
   https://help.aiacontracts.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009446521
   "it is normal for the contractor to submit AIA Documents G706-1994 and G706A-1994 along with
   attached releases or waivers of liens for the contractor, all subcontractors, and others who
   may have lien rights against the owner's property"
