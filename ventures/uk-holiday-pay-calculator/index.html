Common questions

    
How much holiday am I entitled to by law?

    
Most workers get 5.6 weeks a year. For someone working 5 days a week that is 28 days, which can include bank holidays. The 28-day cap means working 6 or 7 days a week does not give more.

    
How is holiday worked out if I work part-time?

    
Multiply the days you work in a week by 5.6. Three days a week gives 16.8 days. Employers should round up to the next half day, not down.

    
What if I start or leave part-way through the year?

    
Holiday builds up in proportion to the part of the leave year you are employed. If you leave with unused holiday your employer must pay for it in your final pay.

    
How does the 12.07% method work?

    
For irregular-hours and part-year workers whose leave year starts on or after 1 April 2024, holiday is 12.07% of the hours worked. Employers may pay it as rolled-up holiday pay, shown as a separate line on each payslip.

    
How much is a day of holiday pay?

    
One normal day's pay. If your pay varies, it is based on your average over the last 52 weeks in which you were paid.
