# Mini-Golf Course Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/mini-golf-course-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people building or buying a mini-golf, putt-putt, batting-cage or small family-fun-center business: build cost per hole and shared build cost, rounds per day and price by season, parties, concession and arcade spend, staff, fixed costs, an upkeep reserve, a rain-out allowance and a loan become operating income, debt coverage, cash after the loan, payback in years (whole investment and your own money), break-even rounds per day, the price change that hits your payback target, a price-by-attendance grid and a weather table.

After using this, you know whether the course pays back, how many rounds a day cover the loan, and how bad a wet season can get before the loan is in trouble.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/mini-golf-course-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
