FOR CABINET PAINTERS / REFINISHERS

Bid the doors, the drawers, and the degrease — not the door count.

A flat per-door quote treats a 10-door galley with eight drawer fronts, a greasy range wall, and a dark-to-light extra coat as the same job as ten shop-sprayed doors. Drawer fronts, face frames, hardware remove-and-rehang, and an extra coat do not scale with the door count the homeowner counts. Price those extras separately, then see the walk-away number.
