This calculator vs. Kickserv

Two different shapes: Kickserv is an account-based platform for running a home service company's whole operation, including scheduling the crew and invoicing the customer once a job is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this kitchen's refinish cost, before you offer it.

What each tool is for

Kickserv, founded in 2006, is a field service management platform used by home service businesses — painting, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing and general contracting among them — to run client and job management, online estimates and invoicing, scheduling and crew dispatch, and online payments, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Kickserv's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that kind of field service software describe scheduling, invoicing and collecting payment for a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a cabinet refinish should cost from door and drawer counts, hardware, finish method and degrease before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Kickserv does. It does not manage a client list, dispatch a crew across a season, or collect a payment. It takes one kitchen — doors, drawer fronts, face-frame openings, hardware pieces, finish method (shop spray, on-site spray, or brush), color change, and degrease hours — plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, primer and finish material, supplies, travel and equipment, overhead and target margin, and turns that into the refinish price, the price per door, the price per painted piece, the effective crew hourly rate and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
