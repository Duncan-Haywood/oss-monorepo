What this checks

Generic “Amazon FBA calculator” pages often stop at a referral percentage. The decision sellers actually face is FBA vs FBM on the same SKU: fulfillment, inbound, and storage versus the label you buy yourself — and whether the buy cost still works. FBA vs FBM: which path leaves more? walks through that in search-query words. On a cheap SKU the next decision is whether staying under Low-Price FBA still beats a real raise — a one-cent move over $10 usually does not.

A coupon is a second decision on the same SKU: the buyer pays less, referral falls with that price, and a per-redemption fee is deducted from proceeds. Fulfillment does not get cheaper. Does this Amazon coupon still pay? shows the lift you would need to match this month’s list-price profit.

Subscribe & Save is the standing version of that discount: same referral cut, usually no redemption fee, and only some units enroll. Does Subscribe & Save still pay? shows the mixed month and the new S&S-only sales that would offset converted buyers.

A website order is a further decision on the same SKU sitting in FBA: sell it on Amazon, let Amazon ship the website order (MCF), or ship it yourself. Should I use Amazon MCF for website orders? shows when skipping the referral still covers the higher pick fee and the card.

A supplier MOQ is a sourcing decision on the same SKU: a cheaper unit that sits longer and ties up more cash. Does this Amazon supplier MOQ still pay? scales the warehouse stay by pile size and shows the MOQ cost that still matches your usual purchase order.

Paying Amazon to label, bag, or wrap inbound is a prep decision on the same SKU: you-prep is already in the listing; Amazon’s inbound prep fees replace that line. Should I pay Amazon to prep inbound? shows leftover profit, the monthly gap, and the Amazon fee that still matches you-prep.

The selling plan is a further account decision on the same SKU: Individual charges per item sold; Professional is a flat month. Individual vs Professional: when does the monthly plan pay? applies both to leftover profit and max buy.

A Vine invite is a launch decision on the same SKU: gift leftover units, gift units that would have sold, or skip. Is Amazon Vine worth it? shows enrollment cost after the pick fee, leftover over the typed months, and the conversion lift that still ties.

It does not recommend a price, a category, a fulfillment method, a coupon, Subscribe & Save enrollment, a purchase-order size, a prep path, an account type, or a Vine enrollment, and it is not tax or financial advice. The gap between paths is an arithmetic comparison of the rates you entered.
