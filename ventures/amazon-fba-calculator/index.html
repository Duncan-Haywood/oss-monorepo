What this checks

Generic “Amazon FBA calculator” pages often stop at a referral percentage. The decision sellers actually face is FBA vs FBM on the same SKU: fulfillment, inbound, and storage versus the label you buy yourself — and whether the buy cost still works. FBA vs FBM: which path leaves more? walks through that in search-query words.

A second decision is whether thin FBA stock is cheaper than sending more units. Low-inventory-level fee: restock or stay thin? compares the surcharge on each sale against inbound and storage on the extra units that would get you to 28 days of supply.

It does not recommend a price, a category, or a fulfillment method, and it is not tax or financial advice. The gap between paths is an arithmetic comparison of the rates you entered.
