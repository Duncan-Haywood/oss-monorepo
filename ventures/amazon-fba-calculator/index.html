What this checks

Generic “Amazon FBA calculator” pages often stop at a referral percentage. The decision sellers actually face is FBA vs FBM on the same SKU: fulfillment, inbound, and storage versus the label you buy yourself — and whether the buy cost still works. FBA vs FBM: which path leaves more? walks through that in search-query words. After the path is chosen, a smaller carton can still drop a size-tier cliff.

It does not recommend a price, a category, or a fulfillment method, and it is not tax or financial advice. The gap between paths is an arithmetic comparison of the rates you entered.
