Individual vs Professional: when does Amazon’s monthly plan pay?

Sellers search for “Amazon Professional vs Individual” because the account fee is not a rounding error. Individual charges a per-item selling fee on every order. Professional charges a flat monthly subscription and drops that per-item fee. At the illustrative US defaults — $0.99 per item versus $39.99 a month — the subscription matches the per-item fee at about 40.4 units a month.

Volume alone is not the whole decision. The allocated account fee sits in the same cash stack as cost of goods, inbound, and storage. Switching plans changes unit profit and the maximum you can pay a supplier on both FBA and FBM. Open the Amazon FBA vs FBM calculator, enter the Individual per-item fee and Professional monthly fee from your current fee preview, and compare both plans on one listing. The calculator’s volume ladder shows that flip at 10, 20, 40, 80, and 200 units so you can see when to switch as sales grow.

Common questions

Is Amazon Professional worth it vs Individual?

Professional pays for itself on the account fee when monthly units exceed the monthly plan price divided by the Individual per-item fee. At the illustrative US defaults that is about 40.4 units a month. This page also applies each plan to the same FBA or FBM listing so you can see profit and max buy cost, not only the subscription math. It does not recommend an account type.

What is the Amazon Individual selling fee?

Individual charges a per-item selling fee on every order in addition to referral and fulfillment. The illustrative US default on this page is $0.99 per item sold. Enter the per-item fee from your current Amazon fee preview.

Does the seller plan change my max buy cost?

Yes. The allocated account fee sits in the same cash stack as cost of goods, inbound, and storage. Switching plans changes unit profit and the maximum you can pay a supplier on both FBA and FBM. The page does the arithmetic from the rates you enter; it does not recommend an account type.
