Individual vs Professional: when does Amazon’s monthly plan pay?

Sellers search for “Amazon Professional vs Individual” because the account fee is not a rounding error. Individual charges a per-item selling fee on every order. Professional charges a flat monthly subscription and drops that per-item fee. At the illustrative US defaults — $0.99 per item versus $39.99 a month — the subscription matches the per-item fee at about 40.4 units a month.

Volume alone is not the whole decision. The allocated account fee sits in the same cash stack as cost of goods, inbound, and storage. Switching plans changes unit profit and the maximum you can pay a supplier on both FBA and FBM. A seller doing 10 units a month keeps more on Individual; a seller doing 200 units a month keeps more on Professional — on the same listing, not just on the subscription line.

Rates change by marketplace and account. This page does not publish Amazon’s live schedule, and it does not recommend an account type.
