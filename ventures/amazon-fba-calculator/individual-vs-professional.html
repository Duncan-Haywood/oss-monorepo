Individual vs Professional: when does Amazon’s monthly plan pay?

Sellers search for “Amazon Professional vs Individual” because the account fee is not a rounding error. Individual charges a per-item selling fee on every order. Professional charges a flat monthly subscription and drops that per-item fee. At the illustrative US defaults — $0.99 per item versus $39.99 a month — the subscription matches the per-item fee at about 40.4 units a month.

Volume alone is not the whole decision. The allocated account fee sits in the same cash stack as cost of goods, inbound, and storage. Switching plans changes unit profit and the maximum you can pay a supplier on both FBA and FBM. Open the Amazon FBA vs FBM calculator, enter the Individual per-item fee and Professional monthly fee from your current fee preview, and compare both plans on one listing.

The table below uses the page defaults: $29.99, 15% referral, large-standard FBA at $4.15, $8.00 cost of goods, 80 units a month, and a 30% target margin.

At 10 units the ranking flips: Individual keeps $10.50 and Professional keeps $7.49. Rates change by marketplace and account.
