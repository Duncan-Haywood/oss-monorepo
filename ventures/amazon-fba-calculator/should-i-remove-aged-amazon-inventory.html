Common questions

Should I remove aged Amazon FBA inventory?

On the calculator defaults ($29.99, 15% referral, $4.15 pick, 80 leftover units, 5% monthly sell-through, $1.50 aged-inventory surcharge) keeping over 3 months is −$109.82. A return removal is −$83.20. Disposing is −$25.60. Keeping loses $84.22 more than disposing. Keeping needs about 6.7% monthly sell-through to match dispose. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to create a removal order.

Does Amazon charge aged inventory surcharge on leftover FBA units?

Amazon can add an aged-inventory surcharge on units that have sat past an age band. The amount is a line on your fee preview, not a published live schedule this page fetches. Type the per-unit monthly surcharge from that preview. Storage still sits on top.

Is disposing leftover FBA cheaper than a return removal?

A dispose order is usually a cheaper per-unit fee because the units do not come back. Cost of goods is already sunk — both paths spend a fee, and only a return gives you the units. Q4 storage is a different question: that is a seasonal rate, not an age surcharge.
