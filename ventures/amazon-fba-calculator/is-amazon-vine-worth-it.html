Common questions

Is Amazon Vine worth it on an FBA listing?

Vine gifts units. You still pay cost of goods, inbound, prep, and the FBA pick fee. On the calculator defaults ($29.99, 15% referral, $4.15 pick, $8 cost, 80 units / month) a 30-unit leftover enrollment costs $419.95. A 12% lift over 6 months keeps $213.26 more than skipping. Vine on units that would have sold keeps $116.54 less. Leftover Vine needs about 8.0% lift to match. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to enroll.

Does Amazon charge referral on Vine units?

Vine gifts are usually $0 checkout, so referral is $0 unless you type a rate. The pick fee, inbound, and cost of goods still leave. Replace the referral and pick fee with the lines on your Vine / fee preview.

Should I enroll a hero SKU in Vine?

Gifting a unit that would have sold also gives up that unit’s leftover profit. On the defaults that extra $10.99 × 30 wipes the lift. Coupons are a different question — they discount paid sales, they do not gift the unit.
