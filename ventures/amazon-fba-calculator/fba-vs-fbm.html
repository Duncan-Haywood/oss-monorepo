FBA vs FBM: which fulfillment path leaves more on the same listing?

Sellers search for “FBA vs FBM” because the referral fee is not the decision. Both fulfillment-by-Amazon and merchant-fulfilled listings usually pay the same category referral percentage. The gap is everything around it.

FBA adds a size-tier fulfillment fee, inbound to an Amazon warehouse, and monthly storage while the unit sits. Prime shipping is Amazon’s problem after that. FBM skips those Amazon fulfillment costs and replaces them with the label, packaging, and time you pay to ship the order yourself.

On a typical mid-price example — a $29.99 home-and-kitchen unit, 15% referral, a large-standard fulfillment default, $1.20 inbound, one month of storage, and a $4.75 merchant label — one path can keep several dollars more than the other. That difference is often larger than the referral-rate gap between categories.

Rates change by size tier, season, category, and account. This page does not publish Amazon’s live schedule.
