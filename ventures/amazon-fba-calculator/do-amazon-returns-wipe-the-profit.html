Do Amazon returns wipe FBA profit?

Sellers search for “Amazon FBA return rate profit” because the number on a completed sale is not the number that hits the bank account. A slice of orders come back. Amazon typically refunds the buyer and the referral. The outbound pick fee, inbound, and storage do not come back. Cost of goods comes back only when the unit can be sold again.

On the $29.99 home listing — 15% referral, large-standard FBA at $4.15, $8.00 cost of goods, $1.20 inbound, one month of storage, and the professional plan spread over 80 units — a completed sale keeps $10.99. At a 15% return rate, $2.50 handling, and 20% of returns unsellable, expected profit is $7.75: $3.24 of drag. The return rate that wipes expected profit is about 50.9%. FBM on the same mix keeps $8.40 because there is no Amazon pick fee left spent on the returned unit.

What costs stay when an Amazon customer returns an FBA order?

Amazon typically refunds the buyer and the referral fee. The outbound fulfillment fee, inbound, storage, prep, ads, and the allocated plan fee usually stay spent. A return-processing fee is often added. Cost of goods comes back only when the unit can be listed again.
