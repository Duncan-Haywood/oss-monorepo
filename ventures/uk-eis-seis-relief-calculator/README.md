# UK EIS and SEIS Tax Relief Calculator

Free, private browser tool for UK angel investors: enter the amount you plan to put into an EIS or SEIS company and the income tax you owe to see the relief you can actually use, the CGT deferred or saved, how much you are out of pocket if the company fails after loss relief, and the sale price that gets you back to even.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-eis-seis-relief-calculator/

After using this, you know the real downside of an EIS or SEIS cheque before you write it.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-eis-seis-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
