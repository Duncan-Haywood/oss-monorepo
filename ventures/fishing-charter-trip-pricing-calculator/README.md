# Fishing Charter Trip Pricing Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/fishing-charter-trip-pricing-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for charter captains: fuel, deckhand, bait, yearly fixed costs, expected trips and weather cancellations become a break-even price, the price that pays your target income, a per-angler price, and a check on the flat rate you charge today.

After using this, you can publish a trip price that still pays you after the weather takes a fifth of your bookings, which used to mean guessing from what the dock next door charges.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/fishing-charter-trip-pricing-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
