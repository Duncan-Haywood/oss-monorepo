Questions truck owners ask

How many portions of each menu item should I prep?

Rank items by contribution (price after card fees, minus food cost), then prep the best one until a limit stops you: prep labor minutes, grill or fryer minutes, ingredient cash, or the demand you expect. When a limit binds, the answer changes, because a lower-priced item can earn more per scarce minute. The tool solves this exactly as a linear program.

What is a shadow price here?

It is what one more unit of a limit is worth: one more prep minute, one more grill or fryer minute, or one more dollar of ingredient cash. If prep time shows $3.75 a minute, paying a helper less than $225 an hour for that time raises profit that service.

Why does a popular item get zero portions?

Because minutes or dollars are better spent elsewhere. The tool shows the contribution per portion that item would need, everything else unchanged, before it earns a place.

Does it handle waste, spoilage or leftovers?

Only through the ceiling you set per item. Enter the portions you expect to sell, not the most you could make. Leftover value, spoilage, queue length and weather are yours to judge.

Is this food-safety, legal or financial advice?

No. It is arithmetic on prices, costs and times you type. Food-safety rules, permits and taxes vary by place; check them with the right authority or a qualified advisor.

Not decided

Whether your demand estimates will hold, waste and leftovers, food safety and holding times, permits, queue length, weather, taxes and financing. Each item is treated as one uniform portion with a fixed contribution.
