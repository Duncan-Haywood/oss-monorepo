# UK Unfair Dismissal Compensation Calculator (2026/27)

Free, private browser tool: enter your dates and pay. It estimates the basic award (age-banded weeks, weekly pay capped), a compensatory award from lost earnings with Polkey, ACAS Code and contributory-fault adjustments, the 52-week and fixed cap, and the "three months less a day" claim deadline.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-unfair-dismissal-compensation-calculator/

After using this, a dismissed employee knows roughly what a claim could be worth and the date to contact ACAS by, which used to mean reading three GOV.UK pages and a solicitor's blog.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-unfair-dismissal-compensation-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
