# Kombucha Brewery Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/kombucha-brewery-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people planning or running a kombucha brewery: fermenter capacity, batch fill, brew days, wholesale and taproom prices, packaging, labour, rent, fit-out and a loan become gallons a week, monthly profit at full production, break-even gallons, the price change that reaches a target margin, how an electricity-price swing moves profit, a sixty-month ramp-up with the cash reserve and payback, and loan coverage.

After using this, you know how many gallons a week the brewery has to sell to pay its rent, labour and chilling, how much cash to hold while output ramps up, and how exposed the plan is to packaging and power costs.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/kombucha-brewery-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not legal, tax or financial advice. Every figure is your own input. Check your buyers, food-safety licensing, alcohol-content testing and labelling rules (kombucha above 0.5% ABV is regulated as alcohol), zoning and power capacity before you build. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
