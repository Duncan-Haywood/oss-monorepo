# Ice Cream Shop Seasonal Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/ice-cream-shop-seasonal-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people who run or plan a seasonal ice cream, frozen-dessert or snow-cone shop: enter days open and customers per day for each of the twelve months and see profit by month, break-even customers per day, an open, close or closed verdict for every month, loan coverage, the cash reserve the slow months need and payback on the start-up cost, plus what moving prices or traffic does to the year.

After using this, you know whether the season carries the loan and the lease, which shoulder months are losing money to stay open, and how much cash to hold for winter.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/ice-cream-shop-seasonal-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
