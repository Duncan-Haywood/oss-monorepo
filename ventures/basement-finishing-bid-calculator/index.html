FOR BASEMENT REMODELERS

Bid the finished SF, the scope, and the extras.

Price a basement finish the way the trade actually loses money: 800 SF of rec-room paint, drop ceiling, and carpet versus a legal-living suite with a full bath, two egress windows, and HVAC — not the published $30 / SF special. You get the job price, what it works out to per square foot, and the number below which the finish loses money.

The basement

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with an invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing scope, ceiling, floor, or bath fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the scope, ceiling, floor, and bath you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $30 / SF basement that looked profitable was not.
