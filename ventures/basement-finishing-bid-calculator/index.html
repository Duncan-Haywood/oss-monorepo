Where the hours go

    Transparent arithmetic from your own inputs. The starting hours and material rates are common ballpark figures, not an industry standard, a benchmark, or a guarantee — time your own crews and replace them.

    Common questions

    What does this basement-finish estimate include?

    The price adds up shell labor and material (framing, insulation, drywall, or paint depending on scope), a ceiling plane, a floor with waste, a bathroom kit when you add one, egress windows, bedroom extras, a stair rebuild, wet bar, HVAC extend, and permit coordination when checked, plus travel, overhead, and your target margin — all from the finished square footage, scope, and rates you enter, not a flat per-square-foot price list.

    Why does a legal-living suite cost so much more than a rec room the same size?

    Rec-room, framed-finish, and legal-living each start from their own shell hours and material rates — legal-living adds framing, insulation, and drywall hours per square foot that a rec-room paint-and-carpet job skips, plus egress-window and bedroom hours and material that do not apply to a rec room at all. The “Same basement, three scopes” comparison prices your same finished square footage under each scope’s starting kit so you can see where a cheap rec-room rate would lose money on a bedroom suite.

    Why is the floor I buy bigger than the basement I measured?

    Flooring waste — cuts, transitions, and pattern loss — is applied to the finished square footage to get the floor material you actually buy, which is why the floor line in the breakdown uses a larger number than the finished square feet everywhere else.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No account and no network requests — the calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your bid is saved in this browser’s local storage between visits, and a bid link you copy carries those same numbers in the page’s URL hash rather than sending anything to a server. Your business details for the printed proposal are saved separately on this device; the customer’s details are never saved.

    What’s the difference between the bid price and the walk-away price?

    The bid price already includes your target margin on top of labor, materials, and overhead. The walk-away price is the total cost underneath it — shell, ceiling, floor, bath, egress, and every other line item — the point below which the finish stops being profitable at all.

    Does this check building code, egress, or habitable-space requirements?

    No. Hours and material rates are ballpark shop figures you can edit, not a building-code determination, an egress design, or a habitable-space certificate. It also does not price basement waterproofing, a walk-in shower remodel, a drywall-only job, or a standalone flooring install — those are separate jobs with their own calculators. Whether a specific basement meets code for bedrooms and egress is still a judgment call for the inspector.
