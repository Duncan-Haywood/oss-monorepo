This calculator vs. JobTread

Two different shapes: JobTread is account-based project-management and job-costing software for running a remodeling business across clients, crews, and jobs. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this basement finish cost, given its scope, ceiling, floor, and extras, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

JobTread is project-management and job-costing software marketed to residential home builders and remodelers, including basement and whole-home remodeling shops. It is used to run budgets and estimates, a client communication portal, scheduling, purchase orders and change orders, and job costing across a whole company's pipeline, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job. JobTread's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — construction project-management and job-costing software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for JobTread appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what JobTread does. It does not manage clients, schedule crews, track purchase orders, or run job costing across a pipeline, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes finished square feet, rec-room versus framed-finish versus legal-living scope, a drop ceiling or drywall ceiling plane, carpet or LVP flooring with waste, a bathroom, egress windows, bedroom extras, a stair rebuild, a wet bar, an HVAC extend, and permit coordination, plus loaded wage, travel, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the job price, the extra-work share over a flat per-square-foot rate, and the walk-away price below which the finish loses money, plus a printable customer proposal with a deposit split and a price-held date. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
