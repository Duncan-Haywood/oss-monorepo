This calculator vs. Buildxact

Two different shapes: Buildxact is account-based estimating and project-management software for running a construction business across trades and jobs. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this basement finish cost, given its scope, ceiling, floor, and extras, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Buildxact is construction estimating and project-management software founded in Australia in 2011, with North American headquarters in Austin, Texas. It serves builders, remodelers, and contractors across trades — including carpentry, painting, roofing, and flooring — combining material takeoffs, quoting, job costing, scheduling, and client communication into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every trade on the crew. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Buildxact's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — multi-trade construction estimating and project-management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Buildxact appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Buildxact does. It does not manage takeoffs across trades, schedule crews, track job costs over time, or store any project record on a server. It takes one basement finish — finished square feet, rec-room versus framed-finish versus legal-living scope, a drop ceiling or drywall ceiling plane, carpet or LVP flooring with waste, a bathroom, egress windows, bedroom extras, a stair rebuild, a wet bar, an HVAC extend, and permit coordination, plus loaded wage, travel, overhead, and target margin — and turns that into the job price, the extra-work share over a flat per-square-foot rate, and the walk-away price below which the finish loses money, plus a printable customer proposal with a deposit split and a price-held date. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
