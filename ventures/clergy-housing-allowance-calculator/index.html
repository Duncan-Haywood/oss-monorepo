The arithmetic, and what it leaves out

  • Own or rent: the income-tax exclusion is the smallest of the amount designated in advance, the housing costs you actually paid, and the fair rental value of the home furnished with utilities.
  • Parsonage: the fair rental value of the church-owned home is excluded, plus designated utilities up to what you paid.
  • Income tax saved is the exclusion times your federal and state rate.
  • Self-employment tax: 92.35% of salary plus the exclusion, minus unreimbursed ministry expenses, then 12.4% up to the wage base and 2.9% on all of it. Additional Medicare tax is not included.
  • Not modelled: exemption from SE tax by Form 4361, the half-of-SE-tax deduction, retirement plans, state rules that differ from federal, the Additional Medicare tax, and any year-specific limits. Wage base default is a planning figure; check the current year.

Common questions

How is the minister's housing allowance calculated?

The income-tax exclusion is the smallest of three numbers: the amount the church designated in advance, the housing costs you actually paid, and the fair rental value of the home furnished and with utilities. Anything designated above that smallest number is taxable wages.

Does the housing allowance reduce self-employment tax?

No. The allowance is excluded from income tax only. A minister who is treated as self-employed pays Social Security and Medicare tax on salary plus the housing exclusion, so a bigger designation does not lower that bill.

When must the church designate the housing allowance?

In advance, by an official action of the church such as a board resolution or minutes. It cannot be set retroactively, but it can be changed going forward if costs change. Use the resolution template below as a starting point and have your adviser review it.

Can I get a housing allowance if the church gives me a parsonage?

Yes, in a different form. The fair rental value of a church-owned parsonage is excluded from income tax, and the church can also designate an allowance for the utilities you pay yourself.

What housing costs count?

Rent or mortgage payments, property taxes, homeowners or renters insurance, utilities, repairs and maintenance, and furnishings and appliances. Food and household help do not count. Check the IRS guidance for ministers or ask your tax adviser about anything unusual.

Can I still deduct mortgage interest and property tax?

Generally yes. A minister may deduct mortgage interest and real estate taxes even when the housing allowance paid for them. Confirm with your adviser.
