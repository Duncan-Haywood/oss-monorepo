Common questions

Why not just track StockX payouts in a spreadsheet?

Plenty of resellers do, and a spreadsheet is a fine tool for tracking a flip list over a season. The failure mode is not the spreadsheet itself — it is that your seller fee rate is not a constant. It changes when your Seller Level changes and again if you enroll in or drop Fast Shipping, and a formula copied down a column keeps using whatever rate was true the day you typed it, silently understating or overstating profit on every sale after your level moves.

Does this calculator replace my flip-tracking spreadsheet?

Not necessarily. This tool prices one sale at a time and does not save a running list across sessions the way a spreadsheet's rows do. Many resellers use both: this calculator to get one sale's arithmetic right whenever their seller level or shipping enrollment changes, and their own spreadsheet to log the result alongside item, size, and purchase date for every flip over a season.
