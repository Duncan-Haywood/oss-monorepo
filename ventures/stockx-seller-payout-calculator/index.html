Common questions

Why isn't the seller fee just one fixed percentage?

Because StockX's seller (transaction) fee percentage drops as your own Seller Level rises — more completed sales and a clean history earn a lower rate — and Fast Shipping enrollment knocks a further percentage off on top of that. Two sellers moving the identical item at the identical price can keep different amounts. This calculator treats both as numbers you supply from your own seller dashboard, not a fixed published rate.

Why does shipping cost matter more on a cheap item than an expensive one?

The seller fee and payment processing fee both scale with the sale price, but the cost to ship an item to a verification center is closer to a flat dollar amount regardless of what it sold for. That flat cost eats a much bigger share of a $40 sale than a $400 one, which is easy to miss if you only think in percentages.

Does this tool verify authenticity or track my inventory?

No. StockX runs its own authentication process, and this calculator never touches it. This tool only prices the payout and profit arithmetic of a sale you already made or are considering, from numbers you supply — it is not an inventory system, a sourcing tool, or a substitute for reading your own payout statement.

Does anything about my sales get uploaded?

No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and the sales list you type into the month section is never saved anywhere — it exists only in the page while it is open. The sale inputs above and the target monthly profit setting are different: those are saved locally on this device, so you don't retype them, and a shareable link can carry them to another device. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
