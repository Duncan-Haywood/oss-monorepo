    What's recognised

    InputHandling

    The plan is exact for the numbers you type (a search over every set and order, up to 14 jobs).     Common questions

    Does it upload my job list?

    No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    How is the best day found?

    The tool tries the jobs as a search over which have been done and which was last, keeping every way to get there that is not both later and less profitable than another. That finds the exact best set and order for the numbers you give, and the tests compare it with trying every ordering of every subset.

    Why would it turn down a job I would normally take?

    Because a van can only be in one place. A job that pays well but drives you across the map, or sits in a window that collides with two better jobs, can cost more than the two it displaces. The tool says what the job would need to pay to earn its place.

    Where do the costs come from?

    From your own books: fuel and wear per mile, and the hourly cost of the van and the technician. They are your estimates, so treat the plan as a decision aid, not a forecast.
