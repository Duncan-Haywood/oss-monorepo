Common questions

How much does S3 egress cost?

Egress is billed per gigabyte of outbound transfer at the rate on your current price sheet. On the default 500 GB stored, 2 million requests, and 100 GB outbound example, a $0.023 / $0.40 / $0.09 sheet is $21.30 per month. An alternative at $0.006 / $0.36 / $0.00 is $3.72. The $17.58 gap is $210.96 per year. Paste your quoted rates; this page does not fetch provider prices.

Does a cheaper storage rate still win after egress?

Only when outbound transfer is low enough. If cheap storage has a higher egress rate, the page shows the break-even outbound volume. Above that volume the other option is cheaper. The comparison is arithmetic from the rates you enter, not a migration recommendation.

What outbound volume is the break-even point?

The volume where storage-and-request savings and the egress-rate gap cancel out: below it the cheaper-storage, pricier-egress option wins; above it, the other one does. Example: 500 GB stored and 2 million requests at $0.006/GB-month storage, $0.36/million requests, and $0.09/GB egress totals $9.12/month at 60 GB outbound — cheaper than $0.023/$0.40/$0 storage's $12.30. Push outbound to 150 GB and the same cheap-egress sheet totals $17.22, more than the $12.30 alternative. The crossover on this pair is 95.33 GB/month; the page computes it for whatever rates and workload you enter.

Does this include migration cost or contract minimums?

No. The monthly total is storage, request, and egress charges only — it does not add a one-time data-transfer-out fee for the move itself, a minimum monthly commitment your contract may require, or the engineering time to actually migrate. Check your price sheet and contract for those before deciding on this number alone.
