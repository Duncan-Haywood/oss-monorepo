# EU E-Invoicing Mandate Checker

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/eu-einvoicing-mandate-checker/>

Free, private browser tool for businesses invoicing from Germany, France, Belgium, Poland, Croatia, Greece, Italy or Spain: pick your country and size band and see when you must receive and issue structured e-invoices, the required format, the penalties, and whether paper or PDF invoicing is still enough. Also names the EU-wide ViDA date (1 July 2030) for intra-EU B2B supplies.

After using this, you know which e-invoicing deadline applies to you and what to change, which used to take reading six tax authorities' announcements.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-einvoicing-mandate-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
