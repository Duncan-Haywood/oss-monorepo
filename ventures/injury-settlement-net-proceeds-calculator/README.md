# Injury Settlement Net Proceeds Calculator

Free, private browser tool for people weighing a personal-injury settlement offer: gross amount to what the client actually keeps after the attorney fee, case costs, medical liens, the Medicare pro-rata procurement-cost reduction and tax on any taxable slice, with the net at other offer sizes.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/injury-settlement-net-proceeds-calculator/

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/injury-settlement-net-proceeds-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

Not legal or tax advice. The fee agreement, the lienholders and the closing statement decide. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
