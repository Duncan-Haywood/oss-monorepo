Permit packet preflight

Cities bounce incomplete residential permit packets, and each bounce costs weeks. Paste your city's published checklist (one item per line), choose the files in your packet, and see which lines have no matching file.



An estimate from file names, not a plan review and not legal, engineering or professional advice. Only the city decides whether a packet is complete. No checklist is built in because requirements differ by city and change; use the one the city publishes. Free, MIT licensed.
