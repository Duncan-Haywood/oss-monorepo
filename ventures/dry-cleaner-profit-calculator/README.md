# Dry Cleaner Profit Calculator

Free, private browser tool for dry cleaner owners and buyers: dry-clean and laundered pieces, prices, supplies per piece, claims and card fees, labor, fixed costs and loan payments become monthly operating profit, contribution per piece, break-even pieces, the average price change that reaches zero profit, debt coverage, and how price and volume changes move profit.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/dry-cleaner-profit-calculator/

After using this, you know how many pieces a month pay the plant's labor, rent and loan, and what a price increase per piece is worth.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/dry-cleaner-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
