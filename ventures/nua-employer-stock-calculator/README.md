# NUA Employer Stock Calculator

Free, private browser tool for people holding employer stock in a 401(k): compares taking the shares out in kind with net unrealized appreciation (NUA) treatment against rolling everything to an IRA, after tax, with the 10% additional tax, a holding period and a basis-share sensitivity table.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/nua-employer-stock-calculator/

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/nua-employer-stock-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

Not tax or financial advice. Plan rules and your own tax situation decide; check with a tax professional. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
