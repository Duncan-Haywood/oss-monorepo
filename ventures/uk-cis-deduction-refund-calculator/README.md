# UK CIS Deduction and Refund Calculator

Free, private browser tool for UK construction subcontractors: see the 20% / 30% / 0% Construction Industry Scheme deduction on an invoice, estimate your yearly income tax and Class 4 NI, and find out how much of the CIS held back you can claim back through Self Assessment.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-cis-deduction-refund-calculator/

After using this, you know what your contractors should be holding back and roughly how much tax you will get back (or owe), which used to mean waiting for the Self Assessment return to find out.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-cis-deduction-refund-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
