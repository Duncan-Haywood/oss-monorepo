# UK VAT Flat Rate Scheme Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter your sales and costs to compare the VAT you would pay under the Flat Rate Scheme with standard VAT accounting, including the 16.5% limited cost trader trap, the 1% first-year discount and the single capital asset exception.

After using this, you know whether to join the scheme before you register.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-vat-flat-rate-scheme-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rates are 2026/27 and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
