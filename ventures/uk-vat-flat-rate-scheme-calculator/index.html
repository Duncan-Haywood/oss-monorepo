Common questions

    
Who can use the VAT Flat Rate Scheme?

    
Businesses expecting taxable turnover of £150,000 or less excluding VAT over the next 12 months. You must leave when turnover goes above £230,000 including VAT-exempt and zero-rated sales.

    
What is a limited cost trader?

    
If your spend on goods (not services, capital assets, food and drink, or vehicle fuel for most businesses) is under 2% of your VAT-inclusive turnover, or under £1,000 a year, you pay 16.5% instead of your sector rate. Many consultants and freelancers fall into it, which often makes the scheme worse than standard VAT.

    
Do I still charge 20% VAT to customers?

    
Yes. You charge and show VAT as normal. The flat rate only changes how much you pay HMRC, which is a percentage of your VAT-inclusive turnover.

    
Do I get a discount in my first year?

    
Yes. In the first year after VAT registration you take 1 percentage point off your flat rate.

    
Can I reclaim VAT on purchases?

    
Not under the Flat Rate Scheme, except one capital asset costing more than £2,000 including VAT.
