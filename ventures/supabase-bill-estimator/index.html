Common questions

Does this read my Supabase account or invoice?

No. It reads only the usage numbers you type or paste (copy them from the Usage page of your Supabase dashboard) and multiplies them by list prices you can edit. It cannot see your plan, regions, spend limits, enterprise contract or real invoice. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate public Pro-plan list prices from Supabase's pricing documentation as best known in 2025 (Pro is $25 per organization per month), shown with an as-of date, and every one is editable, including the included allowances (the bundled $10 compute credit is modeled as the compute line's allowance). Supabase changes prices and packaging, so check supabase.com/pricing before relying on a number.

How is the saving for each lever calculated?

Each lever re-runs the estimate with one change applied under an assumption shown beside it (for example, rightsizing that removes 30% of compute spend) and reports the difference. The assumptions are editable. Levers overlap, so the savings are not additive.

Is my usage data uploaded anywhere?

No. The browser page calculates in your own browser and never sends anything. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
