How to reduce your Supabase bill: levers ranked by dollar impact

A Supabase Pro invoice is the plan fee plus compute per project, minus a monthly compute credit, plus separately metered usage: monthly active users, database disk, egress, file storage, Edge Function invocations and Realtime. The useful question is not "how do I spend less" but "which line, changed by how much, saves the most". That is arithmetic you can do before the invoice, from the numbers on your dashboard's Usage page.

1. Pause or downsize compute

Each project is billed for its compute size every month, whether or not anyone uses it. Staging, preview and abandoned side projects on Small or Medium instances add up: a Medium is $60 a month against $10 for a Micro. Pause what is idle and size production to measured CPU and memory.

2. Cut egress

Egress beyond the included 250 GB is billed per GB. CDN caching of storage files, Cache-Control headers, narrower select lists and smaller images cut it directly: 30% of 2,250 GB is 675 GB, about $60 a month at $0.09.

3. Trim storage and database disk

Lifecycle rules that delete temporary uploads, compression and de-duplication reduce file storage. For the database, archive old rows, drop unused indexes and fix table bloat, because disk is billed by size reached, not by data you still need.

4. Tame Realtime and Edge Functions

Realtime bills messages and peak connections. Batch and throttle broadcasts, use narrower channels and close idle sockets. For Edge Functions, cache results and move hot paths that only touch your data into Postgres functions.

5. Clean the MAU count

Monthly active users beyond the included 100,000 are billed per user. Bot signups, test accounts and anonymous sessions minted on every page load all count, so block abuse at signup and reuse anonymous sessions.

Run it on your numbers

Enter your usage in the estimator or from the CLI, and gate a budget in CI:

node cli.mjs estimate usage.csv --budget 300

What this cannot see

Plans, add-ons, spend cap, enterprise contracts and price changes are outside it. Prices here are approximate Pro-plan list prices and editable. The estimate is arithmetic on your inputs, never a bill reader.

Common questions

Which Supabase costs usually dominate?

For most Pro teams it is compute (especially several projects), then egress and MAU once the app grows. Estimate your own mix with the estimator rather than assuming, because an auth-heavy consumer app and a storage-heavy media app have different top levers.

Does the Pro compute credit cover everything?

The credit offsets compute charges, not the plan fee or metered usage, and it only goes as far as your compute spend. The estimator lets you edit the credit and every included allowance.

Do the savings add up?

No. Levers can touch the same traffic, so each is calculated alone and the list is a ranking, not a plan total.
