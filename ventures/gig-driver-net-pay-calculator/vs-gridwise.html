This calculator vs. the Gridwise app

Gridwise is a phone app built for ongoing tracking: create a driver account, link your Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, or other gig-platform account so it can auto-import earnings, and it runs background GPS tracking to log mileage over days and weeks. Its effective-hourly figure is built from that tracked history — gross earnings minus logged mileage and expenses — and deeper analytics sit behind a paid Gridwise Plus tier that starts with a 14-day trial and then bills automatically unless cancelled.

That makes Gridwise the right choice for tracking pay and mileage automatically across every trip, every app, over time. This page answers a narrower, faster question: for one session you already have numbers for, right now, what did the car actually cost, and what's really left — with no account, no install, and nothing uploaded.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or the Gridwise app?

Both answer different questions. Gridwise is a phone app: you create a driver account, link your Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, or Instacart account so it can auto-import earnings, and it runs background GPS tracking to log mileage over days and weeks, building an ongoing effective-hourly and expense history. This page is a single static form: no account, no download, no linking a gig platform. You type one session's gross pay, miles, hours, gas price, MPG, and a maintenance-per-mile reserve, and it computes that session's net pay per hour and per mile, a self-employment tax reserve, and the gross pay needed to hit a take-home goal — right now, with nothing installed and nothing uploaded.

Does Gridwise compute the same vehicle-cost breakdown this page does?

Not the same way. Gridwise's own material describes its effective-hourly figure as gross earnings minus logged mileage and expenses, drawn from its background GPS tracking and whatever expenses a driver has entered in the app — a rolling average built from tracked data, not a single session priced against a specific gas price and MPG you choose. This page asks for your own gas price, MPG, and a maintenance-per-mile reserve for one session, plus monthly insurance and other fixed costs prorated across your typical monthly mileage, so the figure is arithmetic you can check against a fuel receipt and your own odometer for that one session.

Is Gridwise free?

The core tracking is free to use, but a driver account is required and deeper analytics sit behind Gridwise Plus, a paid tier that starts with a 14-day trial and then bills automatically unless cancelled. This calculator has no account, no trial, and nothing that bills — it is a free static page with no server behind the numbers you type.
