Common questions

    
Can I claim tax relief on business mileage if I use my own car?

    
Yes. If your employer pays you less than the HMRC approved rate of 45p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25p after, you can claim tax relief on the difference. If your employer pays nothing, relief is on the full approved amount.

    
What counts as business mileage?

    
Travel in your own vehicle for work, such as visiting clients or driving between two workplaces or to a temporary workplace. Ordinary commuting between home and your usual workplace does not count.

    
How far back can I claim?

    
Four tax years after the end of each year, so 2022/23 can be claimed until 5 April 2027. Each year is a separate claim.

    
How much will I get back?

    
Relief is your income tax rate on the shortfall. If you drove 8,000 business miles and your employer paid 20p, the shortfall is £2,000 and a basic rate taxpayer gets £400 back for that year.

    
What if my employer pays more than 45p a mile?

    
The part above the approved rate is taxable earnings and should be reported through payroll. This tool shows the extra tax so you can check your payslip.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent.
