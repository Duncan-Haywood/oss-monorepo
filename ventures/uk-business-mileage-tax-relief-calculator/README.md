# UK Business Mileage Tax Relief Calculator

Free, private browser tool for UK employees who use their own vehicle for work: compares what your employer pays per business mile with the HMRC approved rate (45p, then 25p after 10,000 miles) and shows the tax refund per year, the claim deadline for each and what the deadline has already cost.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-business-mileage-tax-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax advice. Rules were checked against adviser summaries, not HMRC's own pages; verify against the source each result names. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
