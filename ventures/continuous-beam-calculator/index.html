Questions

What is pattern live loading and why does it matter?

On a beam over three or more supports, loading every span at once does not give the largest moments. The biggest sagging moment in a span comes with live load on that span and every other span, the biggest moment over a support with live load on the two spans beside it, and an overhang loaded on its own can lift the far end. Live load (people, furniture, snow) can be anywhere, so a check should cover every arrangement. This calculator does that exactly: it solves each span's live load on its own and adds, at every point, the spans that make that effect worse.

How exact is it?

It is an elastic Euler-Bernoulli analysis with no approximation beyond that theory. The beam is cut at every support and load point, each piece is solved with the exact stiffness method, and moment, shear and deflection inside each piece are integrated in closed form. An independent Macaulay (singularity function) solution in exact fractions agrees to about one part in 10^11 on 60 random beams.

Which supports can I use?

Pin (holds the beam up but lets it rotate: a post, a wall, a hanger), fixed (holds it up and stops it rotating: a beam built solidly into a wall or a moment connection) and free (an end with nothing under it, which makes an overhang or cantilever). The beam must be stable: an overhang needs at least two supports or a fixed end.

Does it size the beam for me?

No. You type the section and the allowable stresses, and it tells you how hard the beam is working. Allowables are used exactly as typed, so adjust them yourself for load duration, size, wet use or repetitive members. Lateral-torsional buckling, bearing, notches and connections are not checked. Anything that carries people should be checked by a licensed engineer or against your local span tables.

Why is a cantilever judged on twice its length?

Building codes commonly allow a cantilever's tip deflection to be judged against twice the overhang, so an L/360 limit on a 2 ft overhang allows 4 ft divided by 360. You can change both limits.
