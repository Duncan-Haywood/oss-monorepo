Questions

How is the cut length worked out?

Dimensions on a bar bending schedule are outside dimensions. Each bend uses the minimum scheduling radius for the bar size (2d up to 16 mm, 3.5d from 20 mm), and the length lost at each bend is subtracted. Shapes 00, 11, 21 and 51 use the total-length formulas printed on BS 8666 shape-code charts, and the tool also checks them against the exact centreline geometry. The result is rounded up to 25 mm by default.

How does the cutting plan save steel?

It solves the cutting-stock problem: which combinations of pieces to cut from each stock bar so every piece is cut and the least stock is bought. The linear relaxation is solved exactly by column generation, which gives a lower bound no plan can beat. Whole-bar plans are then built several ways and the cheapest is kept. When the plan meets the bound, it is proven optimal; otherwise the page shows how many bars it could at most be over.

How much does it save against cutting by hand?

On 200 generated schedules cut from 12 m bars, first-fit decreasing (longest piece first into the first bar it fits) bought about 1% more steel than the optimiser and more bars in 122 of the 200. The plan met the proven lower bound in 197 of 200 and was at most one bar over in the rest. These are generated schedules, not real jobs; the page shows the comparison for your own schedule.

What schedule format does it read?

Comma, tab or semicolon separated text, such as cells copied from a spreadsheet. A header row is read by name (mark, dia or size, shape, members, each, A, B, C, D, E). Without a header, columns are read as mark, size, shape, members, bars in each, then A, B, C, D, E. Sizes may be written 12, H12, T12 or B500B 12.

Is my schedule uploaded?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser and your schedule is never sent anywhere. The hosted page counts one anonymous page view a day (the page, the day and the referring channel, never what you type; off under Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and described in the site footer). An optional anonymous counter in this browser's local storage records only which features were used and the day.
