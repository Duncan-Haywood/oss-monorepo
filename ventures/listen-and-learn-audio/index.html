Questions

Can you learn a language just by listening?

Listening to material you mostly understand builds vocabulary and an ear for the language, and it fits time when your hands and eyes are busy. It works best alongside speaking practice; the sibling Hands-Free Language Coach does that part.

What level are the stories?

The built-in stories are beginner level (CEFR A1 and A2). The AI skill adapts stories and episodes to whatever level you tell it.

Is the audio recorded by people?

No. The built-in stories are read by your device's own text-to-speech voices, so quality depends on the voices your device has installed. The stories themselves were written for this project.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The player runs entirely in your browser with your device's voices. Nothing you do is sent to us.

Looking for something to fall asleep to? Saints by Candlelight is a free bedtime podcast of saints, angels and Bible stories, 30 to 60 minutes an episode.

Privacy. Everything runs in your browser with your device's own voices. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, tracking cookies or analytics vendor. We count anonymous events in this browser only (stories started and finished, nothing about you).
