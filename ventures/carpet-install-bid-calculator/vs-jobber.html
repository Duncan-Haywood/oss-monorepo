This calculator vs. Jobber

Two different shapes: Jobber runs a home-service business's clients, quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payments. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one carpet install cost, given its floor yards, stairs, and scope, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Jobber is field-service management software founded in 2011 and headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, used by home-service businesses across HVAC, plumbing, electrical, lawn care, flooring, and cleaning trades. It combines client management, quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payment collection into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every crew, and it says it serves over 200,000 users. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Jobber's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — home-service field-service management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Jobber appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Jobber does. It does not manage clients, schedule crews, send invoices, or collect payments, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one carpet install — floor square feet converted to yards, stair count priced per step, a choice between stretch-in, glue-down, double-stick, and carpet tile, solid-versus-patterned waste, tear-out, a furniture move, and haul-away — and turns that into the job price, the price per yard, and the walk-away price below which a flat per-yard special loses money, plus a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
