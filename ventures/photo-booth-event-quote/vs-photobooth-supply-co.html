This calculator vs. Photobooth Supply Co's Profit Calculator

One tool asks whether a photo booth business could work in your market. This one prices the booking you already have. They are useful at different points in the business, not competing answers to the same question.

What each one is for

Photobooth Supply Co's Profit Calculator is a free lead tool published by Photobooth Supply Co, a photo booth equipment and business-management-software vendor. It estimates how profitable a photo booth business could be for you from local search volume and an assumed booking rate for your area, with sensible defaults if you are not sure of your own numbers, and emails the projection to the address you provide. It is aimed at someone deciding whether to start a photo booth business, or how much a new market could be worth, not at pricing a specific event that is already on the calendar.

This calculator does the other job: it prices one booking you already have. It takes the booked hours, the setup and teardown time on either side of them, travel, attendant labor, the props and equipment cost, and the print-volume risk an unlimited-print package carries, and turns that into a real price at your own target margin — then checks a number you are already considering against the breakeven. A market-sizing average has no field for any of that, because it is not trying to answer that question.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Photobooth Supply Co's Profit Calculator?

They answer different questions. Photobooth Supply Co, a photo booth equipment and business-software vendor, publishes a free Profit Calculator that estimates how profitable a photo booth business could be in your area from local search volume and assumed booking rates, and emails you the projection — a market-sizing tool aimed at someone deciding whether to start or grow a booth business. This calculator instead prices one booking you already have: the hours, setup and teardown time, travel, attendant labor, print cost, and equipment cost behind that specific event, checked against your target margin. If you are deciding whether the business is worth starting, their market estimate is the right tool. If you already have a date and a venue and need a real number to quote, this one is.

Does this calculator need my email or an account?

No. Photobooth Supply Co's Profit Calculator asks for an email address to send you the projection. This tool needs neither an email nor an account — it is a static page, the arithmetic runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved when you close the tab.

Does a business-level profit estimate already cover one booking's real cost?

No, and it is not built to. A market-sizing estimate works from an assumed average booking rate and volume for an area — it has no field for one event's specific setup and teardown time, drive distance, attendant labor, or how much of an unlimited-print package's paper and ink a busy open bar will actually burn through versus a quiet dinner. Those are exactly the numbers this calculator asks for, because they are what determine whether one booking, priced at a given number, actually clears its costs.
