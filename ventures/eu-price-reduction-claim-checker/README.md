# EU Price Reduction Checker

Free, private browser tool for webshops and marketplace sellers in the EU: paste a price history, enter the sale price and the crossed-out price or percentage off you want to advertise, and see the prior price the 30-day lowest price rule (Article 6a, Price Indication Directive) lets you show, the true discount, and whether your claim holds up.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/eu-price-reduction-claim-checker/

After using this, you can check a sale banner against your own price log in a minute, instead of counting back 30 days across a spreadsheet by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-price-reduction-claim-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
