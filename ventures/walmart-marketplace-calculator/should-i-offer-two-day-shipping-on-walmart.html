Should I offer 2-day shipping on Walmart Marketplace?

Sellers search this after Walmart weights the Buy Box toward two-day delivery. WFS includes that promise after inbound. Buying a 2-day label yourself keeps the listing merchant-fulfilled and eats the faster postage plus pack time. Shipping standard is cheaper on a completed sale and usually converts fewer sessions. The main calculator’s seller-shipping line is one label, so it cannot show whether the 2-day promise is cheaper than WFS.

On the default $24.99 home unit, WFS keeps $4.83 more per session than seller-fulfilled 2-day ($6.92 vs $2.09), $361.94 on 60 WFS units. The $8.95 2-day label and 6 minutes at $18 leave $2.99 on a completed sale — not enough to catch WFS even at 100% take. A $4.50 standard label keeps $9.24 when it sells, but 35% take leaves $3.23. The 2-day label has to fall to about $2.06 before it matches WFS. A $4.50 2-day label with no pack time and matched 80% take flips ahead by $0.47.

Common questions

Should I use WFS or buy a 2-day label myself on Walmart Marketplace?

On the calculator defaults — a $24.99 home unit, 15% referral, standard WFS fulfillment, $1.10 inbound, a $4.50 standard label, an $8.95 2-day label, 6 minutes at $18, 80% WFS take, 70% 2-day take, and 35% standard take — WFS keeps $4.83 more per session than seller-fulfilled 2-day ($6.92 vs $2.09), $361.94 on 60 WFS units. The 2-day label has to fall to about $2.06 before it matches. Standard shipping keeps $3.23 because the cheaper label cannot make up for losing the Buy Box. This page does the arithmetic from the rates you enter; it does not recommend a fulfillment method.

Is Walmart 2-day the same as the WFS versus seller-fulfilled table?

No. The main calculator compares a completed sale on both paths and assumes the offer already won. This page mixes take rate with the faster label and pack time so a cheaper completed-sale leftover does not hide a 2-day Buy Box gap. Price match is a different squeeze: it holds take fixed and drops the ticket.

When does seller-fulfilled 2-day beat WFS?

When the 2-day label plus pack time is cheaper than WFS fulfillment, inbound, and storage, and take stays close.
