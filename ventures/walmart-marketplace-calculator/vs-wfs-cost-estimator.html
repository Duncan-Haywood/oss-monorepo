This calculator vs. Walmart's own WFS Cost Estimator

Walmart publishes its own free fee-estimation tool for sellers inside Seller Center, generally called the WFS Cost Estimator (also shown as an estimated-item-revenue calculator on the inventory page). Walmart's own guide to it is documented at marketplacelearn.walmart.com, and the tool itself is reachable, once signed into a seller account, from seller.walmart.com/wfs/cost-calculator or the calculator icon on the Inventory page's "Manage item" row. It estimates the fulfillment fee, the category referral fee, optional Prep Services, and storage for one WFS item.

That makes it the right tool for one job: the fee Walmart will actually charge to fulfil a specific WFS item right now. It reads Walmart's live schedule, so it is authoritative in a way no third-party page, including this one, can be — referral rates, size-tier fulfillment, and storage vary by category, size, and season, and this site does not publish Walmart's current numbers or claim to. Walmart's own guide also states the estimate does not include inbound shipping or return-processing fees, and that it is an estimate, not a guarantee of actual costs. If you need the current fee for a real listing, that is the tool to open, and this page says so.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Walmart's own WFS Cost Estimator?

Most sellers get more use out of both. Walmart's own WFS Cost Estimator, inside Seller Center, is the authoritative source for the fulfillment, referral, prep, and storage fees Walmart will charge on a specific WFS item, because it reads Walmart's live fee schedule for that listing. This page does not try to replace that lookup. What it adds is the decision Walmart's tool is not built around: whether WFS or shipping it yourself nets more on your own cost of goods, whether matching a lower competing price still leaves a profit, and around half a dozen follow-on calls on the same listing, from returns to Walmart Connect ad spend, none of which are fee lookups.
