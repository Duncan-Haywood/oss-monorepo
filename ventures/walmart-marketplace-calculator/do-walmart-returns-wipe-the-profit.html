Do Walmart returns wipe WFS profit?

Sellers search for “Walmart return rate profit” because the number on a completed sale is not the number that hits the bank account. A slice of orders come back. Walmart typically refunds the buyer and the referral. The outbound WFS pick, inbound, and storage do not come back. Cost of goods comes back only when the unit can be sold again.

On the $24.99 home listing — 15% referral, standard WFS fulfillment, $7.00 cost of goods, $1.10 inbound, and one month of storage — a completed sale keeps $8.65. At an 18% return rate, $2.75 handling, and 20% of returns unsellable, expected leftover is $5.34: $3.31 of drag. The return rate that wipes expected leftover is about 47.0%. Seller-fulfilled on the same mix keeps $5.93 because there is no WFS pick left spent on the returned unit.
