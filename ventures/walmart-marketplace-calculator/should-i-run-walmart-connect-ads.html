Should I run Walmart Connect ads?

Sellers search this after Walmart Connect Sponsored Products starts billing last-click sales they already had. The main calculator’s ads-per-unit line is one leftover on a completed sale, so it cannot show whether extra units cover that bill. Skipping ads keeps the organic leftover. Running ads adds extra units and charges ACOS on last-click billed units — a share of organic plus the extras. Spending harder at a higher ACOS buys more extras and usually a larger bill.

On the default $24.99 home unit on WFS, skipping ads keeps $31.15 more than Sponsored Products ($518.99 vs $487.84) on 60 organic units. Twelve extra units at 15% ACOS cannot cover last-click billing on 40% of organic (24 units). Ads match if ACOS falls to about 11.5%, extra units rise to about 18.4, or organic attribution falls to about 26.2%. An 8% ACOS flips ahead by $31.83. A 25% ACOS campaign with 22 extra units and 50% organic attribution leaves $384.42.

Common questions

Should I run Walmart Connect Sponsored Products ads?

On the calculator defaults — a $24.99 home unit on WFS, 15% referral, 60 organic units, 12 extra ad units, 15% ACOS, and 40% last-click attribution on organic — skipping ads keeps $31.15 more than Sponsored Products ($518.99 vs $487.84). Ads match if ACOS falls to about 11.5%, extra units rise to about 18.4, or organic attribution falls to about 26.2%. An 8% ACOS flips ahead by $31.83. This page does the arithmetic from the rates you enter; it does not recommend a campaign.

Is Walmart Connect the same as the ads-per-unit line on the main calculator?

No. The main calculator subtracts a flat ads-per-unit leftover from a completed sale and assumes the offer already sold. This page mixes extra units ads buy with last-click billing on a share of the organic sales you already had. A completed-sale WFS versus seller table, a 2-day take-rate mix, a refund mix, and aged leftover are different squeezes.

When do Walmart Connect ads still beat skipping them?

When extra units cover the ACOS bill on last-click attributed organic plus those extra units. On these defaults that is about 11.5% ACOS, 18.4 extra units, or 26.2% organic attribution.
