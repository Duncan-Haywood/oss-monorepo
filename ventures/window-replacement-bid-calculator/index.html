FOR WINDOW REPLACEMENT CONTRACTORS

Bid the opening, not the window.

A 3040 double-hung is not a 6048 picture window, a second-floor bedroom is not a first-floor insert, and a rotten sill is a carpentry job hiding inside a “window” line. A flat price per window cannot see any of that. Price United Inches, the story, and the tear-out — then see the walk-away number before a $550-a-window special eats the week.

The job

Unit prices and hours follow common ballpark figures as a time-and-material budget you can edit — not a manufacturer size chart, an Energy Star list, or a finding that any opening is permitted, structurally sound, or lead-safe. Scaffolding is charged only when a priced opening sits on the second story or higher.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. An installer paid $38 often costs more than $50 on the wall. Underestimating that is how a $550-a-window special loses money.
