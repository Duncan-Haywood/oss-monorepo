Common questions

Does this transcribe my audio?

No. It uses only the hours and needs you type, and plan tables you can edit. It never receives audio and never contacts the vendors. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate public list prices as best known in 2025 on pay-as-you-go billing, shown with an as-of line, and every plan is editable. Vendors change prices often, so check each pricing page before deciding.

What is not included?

Accuracy and language differences between models, word timestamps, redaction and other add-ons, volume or committed-use discounts, free credits, billing-increment rounding, taxes and, for self-hosting, engineering and on-call time. Accuracy on your own audio often matters more than a few cents an hour, so test two or three vendors on a sample.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local files and makes no network call.
