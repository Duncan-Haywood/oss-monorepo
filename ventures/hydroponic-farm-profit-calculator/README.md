# Hydroponic Farm Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/hydroponic-farm-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people planning or running a hydroponic, vertical or indoor farm: growing area, plant density, crop cycle, wholesale and retail prices, lights, labour, rent, fit-out and a loan become heads a week, monthly profit at full production, break-even heads, the price change that reaches a target margin, how a power-price swing moves profit, a sixty-month ramp-up with the cash reserve and payback, and loan coverage.

After using this, you know how many heads a week the farm has to sell to pay its lights, rent and labour, how much cash to hold while output ramps up, and how exposed the plan is to electricity prices.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/hydroponic-farm-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
