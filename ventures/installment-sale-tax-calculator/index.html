Common questions

    
What is an installment sale?

    
When you sell a business, rental property, land or other investment asset and take a promissory note from the buyer, the IRS lets you report the gain as you collect the principal instead of all in the year of sale (IRC section 453, Form 6252). Each principal payment carries a share of taxable gain equal to your gross profit divided by the contract price.

    
What is taxed in the first year no matter what?

    
Depreciation recapture. Under IRC 453(i), recapture on depreciation you claimed is recognised in full in the year of sale even if you collect nothing, and only the remaining gain is spread over the note. Interest on the note is ordinary income each year.

    
Why would spreading the gain save tax?

    
Spreading can keep each year's gain in a lower capital gains bracket (0, 15 or 20 percent) and can keep income below thresholds for the 3.8 percent net investment income tax and Medicare premium surcharges. You enter the rate you expect with and without spreading, because it depends on your other income.

    
What can go wrong?

    
The buyer can default, and tax already paid on payments received is not refunded. Notes over $5 million can trigger an interest charge on the deferred tax (IRC 453A). Sales to relatives, sales of inventory and publicly traded securities, and many depreciable-property sales to related parties are restricted. A note that pays less than the IRS minimum rate can be recharacterised in part as interest.

    
Does this decide whether I qualify?

    
No. It is a worksheet that does arithmetic on the figures you enter, using a level yearly payment on the note, payments at year end, and no assumed debt. It does not model assumed mortgages above basis, a pledged note, state differences beyond the single extra rate you enter, or the alternative minimum tax. Check with a CPA before you sign.

    
Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    
No. Everything is worked out in your browser and nothing is sent; a test in the code asserts there are no network calls.
