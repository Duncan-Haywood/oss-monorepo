This calculator vs. Janitorial Manager

Two different shapes: Janitorial Manager is an account-based platform for running a cleaning company's bidding, inspections, and work orders. This page is a free, private worksheet for one narrower question — what does the ready-to-use gallon I am about to pour actually cost — before that number becomes a line in a bid.

What each tool is for

Janitorial Manager is a cloud-based janitorial business management platform, described on its own site as covering bidding, inspections, work order management, and staff and supply tracking for commercial cleaning companies. It is an account-based platform: a cleaning company signs up and runs its bidding, inspections, and day-to-day operations through it as an ongoing subscription, not a one-off calculator. Janitorial Manager's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — janitorial business management software with a bidding module, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Janitorial Manager appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Janitorial Manager does. It does not bid a whole job, schedule inspections, manage work orders, or store a customer or staff record on a server. It takes the concentrate ratio printed on the product label, the container's price and size, the bucket or job size you need, and optional mixing labor minutes and a waste allowance, and turns that into the cost per ready-to-use gallon, per bucket, and per job — plus, since concentrate is only sold in whole containers, how many containers a job actually needs to buy, the real purchase cost of those containers, and how much concentrate is left over for the next job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Janitorial Manager?

They do different jobs, and a janitorial or commercial cleaning company of any size may end up touching both. Janitorial Manager is a cloud-based, account-based janitorial business management platform used to bid jobs, run inspections, manage work orders, and track staff and supplies across a cleaning operation, sold as an ongoing subscription. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from the concentrate ratio on the product label, the supplier's container price, and optional mixing labor and a waste allowance, it turns that into the cost per ready-to-use gallon, per bucket, and per job, plus how many whole containers of concentrate a job actually needs to buy and what is left over. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A company that already bids, inspects, and dispatches through one system is better served by Janitorial Manager or a comparable janitorial management platform; someone who wants an instant, private answer to what a diluted gallon costs before that figure goes into a bid line is in the right place here.

Does this calculator help me price a whole janitorial bid, not just the chemical?

No. It prices one input to a bid — the ready-to-use cost of a diluted cleaning chemical, including mixing labor and a waste allowance — not labor hours for the whole job, square footage, frequency, supplies beyond the chemical itself, or overhead and margin on the full contract. Those still belong in a full bid or in janitorial bidding software built for that job. This tool answers the narrower question a flat percentage for "chemical cost" usually gets wrong.
