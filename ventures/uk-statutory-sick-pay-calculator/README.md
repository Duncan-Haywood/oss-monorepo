# UK Statutory Sick Pay calculator (2026/27)

Free, private browser tool: enter average weekly earnings, qualifying days and sickness dates to see the weekly and daily SSP rate, waiting days, days paid, the 28-week limit and the total due, with the 6 April 2026 change (SSP from day one, lower of the flat rate and 80% of earnings, no lower earnings limit) applied day by day.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-statutory-sick-pay-calculator/

After using this, a small employer or bookkeeper knows exactly how much SSP to pay for an absence, which used to mean reading three GOV.UK pages and a spreadsheet.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-statutory-sick-pay-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
