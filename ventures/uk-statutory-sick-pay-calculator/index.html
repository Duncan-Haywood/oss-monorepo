Common questions

    
How much is Statutory Sick Pay in 2026/27?

    
The flat weekly rate is £123.25 from 6 April 2026, or 80% of the employee's average weekly earnings if that is lower. Check GOV.UK for the current figure.

    
Are there still waiting days?

    
Not for sickness from 6 April 2026: SSP is payable from the first qualifying day. Before that date the first three qualifying days of a period of incapacity were unpaid.

    
Does the employee need to earn the lower earnings limit?

    
Not from 6 April 2026. Before that date, average weekly earnings had to be at least £125.

    
How long does SSP last?

    
Up to 28 weeks per entitlement. Linked spells within 8 weeks of each other add together.
