# UK Pension Withdrawal Tax Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-pension-withdrawal-tax-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool: enter a pension withdrawal and your other income to see the tax-free share, the tax for the year, the emergency tax likely taken at source (and the refund to claim), the money purchase annual allowance warning, and the saving from spreading the withdrawal over 2, 3 or 5 tax years.

After using this, you know what you will keep and whether to wait for next April.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-pension-withdrawal-tax-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax or financial advice. Rates are 2026/27 and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
