# UK Tax-Free Childcare & 30 Hours Calculator

Free, private browser tool for England: enter your childcare hours and rates and each parent's income to see whether you qualify for the 15/30 free hours and Tax-Free Childcare, the value of the free hours, the 20% top-up (capped at £2,000 a child, £4,000 if disabled), what you pay a month, and the Universal Credit childcare alternative.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-tax-free-childcare-calculator/

After using this, you know what your childcare really costs and which support route to take.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-tax-free-childcare-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
