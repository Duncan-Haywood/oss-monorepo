Common questions

Does this read my CI account?

No. It uses only the monthly build-minute volume you type and rate cards you can edit. It cannot see your plan, add-ons, contract or invoice. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

Where do the prices come from?

They are approximate public list prices as best known in 2025, shown with an as-of line, and every number is editable. Vendors change packaging often and bill macOS, Windows and larger runners at multiples of Linux minutes, so check each vendor's pricing page before deciding.

What is not included?

Extra seats, larger and macOS/Windows runners, concurrency limits, caching and artifact storage, support plans and committed-use discounts. These are often the real bill, so use this to compare the per-minute line, not to forecast the whole invoice. Build speed and queue time are not priced here and often matter more than a few dollars.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local files and makes no network call.
