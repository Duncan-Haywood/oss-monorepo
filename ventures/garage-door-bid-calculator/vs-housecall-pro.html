This calculator vs. Housecall Pro

Two different shapes: Housecall Pro is an account-based platform for running a garage-door (or HVAC, plumbing, electrical) company's whole operation, including scheduling and invoicing a job once it is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this door or spring ticket cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

Housecall Pro is a field-service management platform marketed to garage door, HVAC, plumbing, electrical and other home-service businesses. It is used to run CRM and customer records, scheduling and dispatch, online booking, invoicing and payments, and a mobile app for technicians in the field, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. Housecall Pro's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that scheduling and invoicing software describe running and billing a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a new garage-door ticket should cost from door size, parts, shop hours and drive time before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what Housecall Pro does. It does not store a customer record, dispatch a technician, or invoice anyone. It takes one ticket — job type, door size and construction, parts (door, spring kit, opener, hardware, disposal, permit), shop labor hours, and round-trip travel — and turns that into the ticket price, the price per square foot of door, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
