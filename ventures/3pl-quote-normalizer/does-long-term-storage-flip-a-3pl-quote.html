Does long-term 3PL storage flip a fulfillment quote?

A rate card prices every pallet at the fresh-storage line. After leftover sits past 30–90 days, most 3PLs move those pallets onto a higher long-term rate. Hold the rest of the quote still, put leftover on that surcharge, and compare it with liquidating leftover now. The quote flips when the aged storage line, on top of the other monthly fees, exceeds your in-house cost per order. Months to eat a liquidation recovery is leftover recovery divided by leftover times the long-term rate.
