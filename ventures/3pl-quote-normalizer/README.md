# 3PL Quote Normalizer

Compare a third-party logistics (3PL) fulfillment quote with in-house costs before signing. Enter the recurring fees, order volume, storage, receiving, pick/pack, shipping, returns, and minimum monthly spend to get a comparable monthly total, line-by-line breakdown, cost per order, and break-even volume.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The calculator is deterministic, free, and works without an account, upload, API key, or paid service. Use **Copy summary** to paste a normalized quote into a team review.

## Privacy and usage

Everything runs in your browser. Your inputs are never uploaded; there are no accounts, tracking cookies, or analytics vendors. The optional local counter records only feature names such as `quote_normalized` and `summary_copied`, never people or quote contents. Use **Turn usage counting off** to delete the local counter.

The output is a planning comparison, not a carrier quote, accounting result, or recommendation. Verify every rate and contract term with the 3PL and your own records.
