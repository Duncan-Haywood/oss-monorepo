Common questions

How much does an Upwork hire really cost in Connects?

Connects spent per proposal times the price per Connect, divided by your win rate. At 12 Connects at $0.15 and a 5 percent win rate that is $36.00 in Connects per hire. Your own time is usually the larger cost: at 15 minutes a proposal and $50 an hour it is $250.00 per hire.

What win rate do I need for Connects to pay off?

Connects alone pay off at a very low win rate, 0.1 percent on the default numbers. Once you count the time you spend writing, the break-even rises to 1.1 percent, which is the number that matters.

Is it worth paying more Connects to boost a proposal?

Only if the boost raises your win rate by more than it adds to cost. The calculator shows the most Connects a proposal can spend at your current win rate before it loses money, and the scenarios table shows how the month changes if the win rate moves.
