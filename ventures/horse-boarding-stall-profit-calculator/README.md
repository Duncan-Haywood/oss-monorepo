# Horse Boarding Stall Profit Calculator

Free, private browser tool for horse boarding barn owners: stalls, occupancy, board price, feed, bedding, your own care time and fixed costs become monthly profit, break-even occupancy, the board price that breaks even or hits your target, and a table by occupancy.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/horse-boarding-stall-profit-calculator/

After using this, you know whether the barn pays you for your labour or only looks profitable because your time is counted as free, and what to charge for board to fix it.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/horse-boarding-stall-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
