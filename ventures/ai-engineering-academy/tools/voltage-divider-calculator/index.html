Voltage Divider Calculator

What does R1/R2 actually give you at Vout — and how much does connecting a real load pull that down? Everything runs in this page — nothing is uploaded.

Divider output

A real load in parallel with R2 pulls Vout down from the unloaded value — the higher R1/R2 are relative to the load, the worse the sag. Fill this in to see the real-world number, not just the textbook one.

Design for a target output

Pick R1, say what Vout you want, and get the R2 you need — exact, and the nearest resistor you can actually buy.
