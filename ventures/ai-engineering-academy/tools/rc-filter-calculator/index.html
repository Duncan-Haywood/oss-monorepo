What "cutoff" actually means

The corner frequency is not where the signal stops. At exactly fc a first-order RC filter still passes about 70.7% of the amplitude — that is what −3 dB means — and it has already rotated the phase by 45°. Past the corner the response falls at roughly 20 dB per decade, so a tone ten times above the cutoff is attenuated by about a factor of ten, not removed.

If you need a signal genuinely gone rather than merely reduced, one RC stage is usually not enough — that is a job for a higher-order filter, and this calculator will tell you honestly that it is not doing it.
