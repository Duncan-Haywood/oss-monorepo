      Common questions

      Why does entering a 1,200 square foot footprint add nothing to the price?

      Because 1,200 sq ft is the included footprint for a single-point system, and one suction point is also included. Neither adds a charge until you exceed it. A larger slab, or a floor plan that needs a second suction point to draw properly, is where the price starts moving — the square footage on its own does not.

      Is $0.93 per square foot a useful number?

      Only as a comparison against someone else’s per-square-foot quote. It is $1,121.98 divided by 1,200 sq ft, and it is dominated by fixed items: a $185 fan, $145 of pipe, $85 of travel, and five hours of loaded labor. Double the footprint without adding a suction point and the per-foot figure nearly halves while the work is virtually identical. Radon systems price by suction points and discharge routing, not by area.

      What does the default system actually include?

      A basement foundation, one suction point, a standard fan, and sidewall discharge — five hours of work all in, including one hour of site time. Roof discharge, a high-suction fan, a crawlspace membrane, a dedicated electrical circuit, firestopping, and a permit are all off by default. Sidewall discharge in particular is not permitted everywhere; check your local code before quoting it.

      Is a post-mitigation radon test included?

      No — it is an option and it is off on the defaults. That matters because a mitigation job is not finished until a follow-up test shows the level actually came down, and many jurisdictions and real-estate contracts require that document. If your quote is being compared against one that includes the retest, turn it on so you are comparing the same scope.
