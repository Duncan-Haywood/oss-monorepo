Bid the opening, the leaf, and the extras.

Price an interior door replacement the way the trade actually loses money: a hollow-core slab hung on an existing jamb versus a solid-core prehung in a new opening with paint-grade casing, new privacy hardware, and a haul-away — not the published $150-a-door installed special. You get the job price, what it works out to per door, and the number below which the hang loses money.

The opening

Pick a common job to start, then replace every rate with a millwork invoice and a crew you have timed. Changing scope, leaf, or size fills starting hours and material only when you have not already edited that field.

Hours and material rates

These start from the scope, leaf, and size you picked. They are ballpark crew figures, not a millwork catalog or a guarantee.

Your costs

Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, liability insurance, and paid time off. Underestimating it is how a $150 hang that looked profitable was not.
