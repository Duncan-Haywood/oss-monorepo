# UK Rent a Room Relief Calculator

Free, private browser tool: enter the rent from a lodger, your expenses for the room and your other income, and see for 2025/26 and 2026/27 whether the rent is tax-free under Rent a Room Relief, and above the £7,500 limit which of the relief or the normal expenses rules costs less tax.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-rent-a-room-relief-calculator/

After using this, you know which route to take on your Self Assessment return, which used to mean working out the tax twice by hand.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-rent-a-room-relief-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
