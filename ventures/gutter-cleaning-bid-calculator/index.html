FOR GUTTER CLEANING CONTRACTORS

Bid the stories and the downspouts, not the linear feet.

Price a gutter cleaning the way the job actually takes time: each run of trough, the story it sits on, the pitch, whether guards are in the way, what is in it, and every downspout you have to flush. You get the visit price, what that works out to per linear foot, the walk-away number, and an optional spring-and-fall contract.

New to two-story pricing? Why a second story is not twice the footage.

The house
