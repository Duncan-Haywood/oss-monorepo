Questions people ask

How do I price a sauna or cold plunge install?

Add up the hours on site and the drive, crew pay, tools and consumables, the truck cost per mile, the heater or plunge unit, lumber and the electrician, and a share of your fixed monthly costs, then divide by what is left after the card fee and the margin you want. The calculator does that and shows each line so you can see what the customer is paying for.

Should I charge for delivery and travel?

The drive costs you hours you cannot bill elsewhere plus truck cost per mile, so the calculator prices both and shows what share of the quote is the drive. The distance table shows how the price climbs from 0 to 80 miles out, which is where a flat delivery fee stops covering the trip.

What does it cost me when an install runs over?

With the price fixed, extra hours on site are pure cost. The planner shows profit and margin if build, prep and helper time run over by the percentage you choose. The drive does not stretch, so only on-site time is affected.

How many installs a month do I need to break even?

Each job leaves a contribution after variable costs and card fees. Fixed monthly costs divided by that contribution is the number of installs needed, and the planner also warns if your installs per month need more hours than you said you have.

Is this a quote, a code or a legal document?

No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in. It does not decide electrical or building code, permits, clearances, inspection, licensing, insurance or tax treatment, and the defaults are placeholders, not market rates.
