# UK Lifetime ISA vs Pension Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/uk-lifetime-isa-vs-pension-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for UK savers aged 18 to 39: per £1 of take-home pay, which is worth more, a Lifetime ISA or a pension, at 60, for a first home or after an early withdrawal, and the retirement tax rate or employer match where the answer flips.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/uk-lifetime-isa-vs-pension-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.

**No warranty.** Free and as-is; not tax or financial advice. Rates were written from memory and must be verified on GOV.UK. See `VENTURE.yaml`.
