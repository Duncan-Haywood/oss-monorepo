# 200 simulated Texas homes on the example plans

Homes: 200 drawn (seed 2026) from 14934 occupied, no-PV ResStock AMY2018 homes in Harris County, Fort Bend County, Dallas County, Tarrant County, Collin County. Simulated, not metered. Plans: the worked example's A, B, C and a free-nights plan D, all invented terms with the same placeholder TDU charges.

Annual use: median 15,004 kWh, 10th-90th percentile 8,263-24,989 kWh.
Home-months between 1,000 and 2,000 kWh (plan B's credit band): 901 of 2400 (37.5%); under 1,000: 44.9%; over 2,000: 17.5%.

By the label's 1,000 kWh price the order is B (8.1 cents), C (14.2), A (16.0).

Choosing B by its label price cost a home, against the cheapest of A, B and C on its own year: median $241, 90th percentile $649, most $1,812.

## Free nights

Share of each home's year used 9 p.m.-6 a.m.: median 30.8%, 10th-90th percentile 26.9%-35.0%.
For a free-nights plan to beat A's flat 9.1 cents, with the same base and TDU charges, its daytime rate must be under 9.1 / (1 - night share): 13.2 cents for the median home (10th-90th percentile 12.4-14.0).
D beats A when more than 57.7% of use falls in the free hours (1 - 9.1 / 21.5); that held for 0 of 200 homes. D was cheapest of all four for 0.

Simulated homes, invented plans: this measures how much the label's three prices leave out for a realistic spread of Texas usage, not what any real plan costs.
