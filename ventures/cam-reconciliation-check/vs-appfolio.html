This calculator vs. AppFolio CAM reconciliation

Two different shapes: AppFolio runs expense recovery on top of a configured property-management and accounting platform, built for a company running its leases, tenant ledgers, and bookkeeping through one system. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — does a single year's CAM statement match what the area-share math and credits say it should, before you raise it with the other side.

What each tool is for

AppFolio Property Manager is a cloud property-management and accounting platform aimed at small-to-midsize residential and commercial portfolios. Expense recovery and CAM reporting sit on top of the same lease and general-ledger data as rent collection, maintenance, and owner reporting: a reconciliation reflects every transaction the platform has recorded for the property over the year. It is accurate to the extent every charge, credit, and lease term was entered correctly, and it requires the platform itself, a configured chart of accounts, and ongoing bookkeeping to produce a reconciliation at all. AppFolio's own pricing and plan terms are not repeated here, since this session cannot verify today's figures against AppFolio's current terms; only its well-documented scope as a cloud property-management platform is described.

This calculator does none of what AppFolio's expense-recovery reporting does. It has no ledger, no lease database, and no memory between visits. It takes the figures already on one year's CAM statement or lease exhibit — annual expenses, total rentable area, tenant area, billed amount, credits, and optionally the variable-expense share and occupancy percentages for a gross-up clause — and returns the area share, the modeled tenant share, the net billed amount, and the variance between them, plus a review checklist. It runs free in any browser, needs no account and no property set up in advance, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or AppFolio?

They solve different problems. AppFolio Property Manager is a cloud property-management and accounting platform built for small-to-midsize residential and commercial portfolios: it tracks leases, tenant ledgers, and recurring charges, and its expense-recovery reporting is generated from transactions already recorded in the platform for the property over the year. Setting it up means configuring the property, the chart of accounts, and the ongoing bookkeeping underneath it. This calculator does none of that. It takes the five or eight numbers already on a CAM statement or in a lease exhibit — annual expenses, total rentable area, tenant area, billed amount, credits, and an optional occupancy gross-up — and returns the area share, modeled tenant share, net billed amount, and variance, instantly, with no account, no ledger, and nothing uploaded. A property manager already running AppFolio gets recovery reporting from the platform it already operates in; a landlord, tenant, or manager checking one statement by hand — or without access to the property's AppFolio account at all — is in the right place here.
