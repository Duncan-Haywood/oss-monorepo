    Common questions

    Does a nonzero variance mean I'm being overcharged or undercharged?

    Not by itself. Variance is just modeled share minus net billed; a mismatch is exactly what you'd expect when a landlord bills estimated CAM through the year and reconciles later, or when credits were applied differently than you expected. Use the review checklist below the result to decide whether the gap is normal timing or something to raise with the other party.

    Does this apply my lease's caps, gross-ups, or base-year exclusions?

    No. Annual CAM expenses is a single number you type in — this tool does not know your lease's controllable-expense cap, administrative gross-up, or base-year deduction. Back those adjustments out of the statement yourself before entering the figure, or the modeled share will be wrong.

    Is this a legal or lease-interpretation conclusion?

    No. It only checks the arithmetic — area share, modeled share, and the net billed amount after credits — against what the statement claims. It does not interpret lease language, decide what counts as a common-area expense, or tell you who owes whom.

    Does my lease or expense data leave my browser?

    No. The reconciliation runs locally; nothing is uploaded. Only an anonymous local count of which buttons you used is kept, and you can turn that off with the checkbox in “Use it safely.”
