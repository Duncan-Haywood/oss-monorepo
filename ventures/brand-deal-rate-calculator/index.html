What this does and doesn't do

Common questions

How much should I charge for a brand deal?

It depends on your follower count, engagement rate, platform, and which deliverable you're selling. This calculator applies commonly cited market CPM-per-1000-engaged-followers ranges to your own numbers to give a starting price range for a feed post, a Story, a Reel or short-form video, or a dedicated long-form video.

Why does this calculate price from engaged followers instead of raw follower count?

Brands are paying for people who actually see and react to the post, not for a number on a profile. A 100,000-follower account with 1% engagement reaches fewer real, active people than a 10,000-follower account with 8% engagement — pricing against engaged followers rewards the account brands actually want.

Is this price a guarantee of what a brand will pay?

No. It is a starting point for negotiation based on general market ranges, not a quote, valuation, or offer performed on your behalf. Actual rates vary by audience quality, past brand-deal history, niche demand, and how much a specific brand wants your specific audience that week.

What counts as a Story versus a Reel or a dedicated video?

A feed post is a single photo or carousel in your main feed. A Story is a single ephemeral frame, usually gone after about 24 hours — it commands the lowest price of the visible-content deliverables. A Reel or short-form video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) sits in between. A dedicated long-form video — mainly a YouTube deliverable — is an entire video about the brand, which commands the highest price because it has no competing content.
