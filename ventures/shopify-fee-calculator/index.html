What this checks

Generic “Shopify fee calculator” pages often stop at one processing rate. The decision most merchants actually face is Shopify Payments vs PayPal or Stripe: the third-party path can add Shopify’s extra transaction fee on top of the processor. Why PayPal can cost more on Shopify walks through that in search-query words.

It does not recommend a price, a plan, or a processor, and it is not tax or financial advice. The gap between paths, and the volume at which a higher plan’s lower rates cover its extra monthly fee, are arithmetic comparisons of the rates you entered.
