What this checks

Generic “Shopify fee calculator” pages often stop at one processing rate. The decision most merchants actually face is Shopify Payments vs PayPal or Stripe: the third-party path can add Shopify’s extra transaction fee on top of the processor. Why PayPal can cost more on Shopify walks through that in search-query words. Selling the same SKU at a market or retail counter is a different question — Shopify POS vs online fees compares the cheaper tap with checkout after postage. Buying the label is a third question — Shopify Shipping vs your own label compares admin postage with a Pirate Ship, Shippo, or carrier-account quote. Shop Pay Installments is a fourth — card vs Shop Pay Installments shows the extra fee, the blended month, and the conversion lift that covers it. A requested return is a fifth — store credit vs a cash refund compares sending cash, issuing credit, and exchanging on the same order. A Capital offer is a sixth — Shopify Capital vs waiting shows the fee, days of holdback, simple annualized cost, and the extra monthly profit that covers the fee. A bank-transfer invoice is a seventh — bank transfer vs the card shows the fee you skip, the leftover if invoices pay, and the collection rate that wipes the save. A chargeback is an eighth — refund vs fight vs accept shows the $15 fee, the leftover on each path, and the win rate that makes representment beat a refund. Local delivery is a ninth — local delivery vs the carrier label shows driver time, mileage, and the run cost that still beats postage. A gift card is a tenth — gift card vs selling the SKU now shows sale-day fees, the second swipe after the face, and the mix that only looks better because of breakage. International duties are an eleventh — DDP vs DAP vs skipping international shows the card fee on the collected duty, the door-refuse leftover, and the take rate that flips collecting duties at checkout. An abandoned checkout is a twelfth — leave vs recovery emails vs a coupon shows the recover rate, allocated send cost, cheaper ticket, and the lift that still beats leaving it. A subscription is a thirteenth — one-time vs monthly vs a prepaid pack shows the subscribe-and-save leftover, billing fee, skip rate, and the stay that still beats a one-time checkout. In-store pickup is a fourteenth — pickup vs the carrier label shows staff time, the bag, no-show refunds, and the label cost that still beats a customer who comes in.

It does not recommend a price, a plan, or a processor, and it is not tax or financial advice. The gap between paths is an arithmetic comparison of the rates you entered.
