What this checks

Merchants search “should I offer subscriptions on Shopify” because a 10% subscribe-and-save looks cheap next to a $54 ticket they already wanted. The $4.80 still comes out of leftover, and a 1% billing fee sits on every cycle. Skip and cancel cut the stay. A prepaid pack collects more now.

On the defaults — $48 item, $6 shipping charged, $5.00 outbound, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $14 cost, $1 pack, $39 / 100 plan, 10% monthly off, 15% prepaid off, 1% billing fee, 5 months stay, 20% skip, 25% enroll, 80 buyers — one-time keeps $31.74. Monthly keeps $26.59 a paid cycle and $106.36 across 4.0 paid cycles ($74.62 more). The prepaid 3-month pack keeps $74.23 ($32.13 less than monthly, $42.49 more than one-time). Monthly has to land about 1.2 paid cycles before it matches one-time. Skip can rise to about 76.1% before monthly matches one-time. The billing fee can rise to about 38.9% before monthly matches one-time. A 25% enroll mix across 80 buyers keeps $1492.42 more than selling every buyer one-time. After one paid cycle with no skip, monthly keeps $26.59 against $31.74 one-time; a 20% one-month prepaid keeps $21.98 and one-time wins. Raise skip to 80% and the prepaid pack keeps $74.23 against $26.59 monthly.

Common questions

Should I offer subscriptions on Shopify?

A subscriber who stays is worth more than one checkout even after a subscribe-and-save discount and a billing fee. On the calculator defaults ($48 item, $6 shipping, 2.9%+$0.30, $14 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% monthly off, 15% prepaid off, 1% billing fee, 5 months stay, 20% skip) one-time keeps $31.74, monthly expects $106.36 across 4.0 paid cycles, and a prepaid 3-month pack keeps $74.23. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn Recharge, Skio, or Shopify Selling Plans on.

When does a Shopify subscription lose to a one-time checkout?

When they do not stay long enough to cover the cheaper ticket. Monthly has to land about 1.2 paid cycles before it matches a $31.74 one-time leftover, and skip can rise to about 76.1% before monthly matches one-time. After one paid cycle with no skip, monthly keeps $26.59 against $31.74 one-time.

When does a prepaid Shopify subscription pack beat monthly?

When skip or cancel cuts monthly down to about one paid cycle. At an 80% skip rate monthly keeps $26.59 and the 3-month prepaid pack still keeps $74.23. The billing fee can rise to about 38.9% before monthly matches one-time on the default stay.
