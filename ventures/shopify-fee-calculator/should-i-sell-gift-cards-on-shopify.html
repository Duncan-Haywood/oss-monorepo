What this checks

Merchants search “should I sell gift cards on Shopify” because a $50 card looks like $50 in the bank. Sale-day fees on $50 are $1.75. When they spend it on a $54 ticket they swipe $4 more — another $0.30 plus 2.9%. You still ship the item.

On the defaults — $50 face they pay $50, $48 item, $6 shipping charged, $5.00 label, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $14 cost, $1 pack, $39 / 100 plan, 80% redeem, 20 cards — selling the SKU now keeps $31.74. A later redeem keeps $31.44 ($0.30 less, $6.00 / month). Cash after sale-day fees is $47.86; that is still a $50 liability. Cut the card to $45 and a later redeem keeps $26.59 ($5.16 less). An 80% mix shows $34.73 only because 20% never come back.

It does not tell you to turn gift cards on or off, and it is not tax or financial advice. A requested return that you close with store credit is a different question — store credit vs a cash refund. Charging a card in installments is on Shop Pay Installments. Collecting duties at checkout is a different question — DDP vs DAP vs skipping international. Common questions

Should I sell gift cards on Shopify?

A gift card is cash today and fulfillment later. Card fees hit what they pay for the card. When they redeem, leftover ticket after the face is a second card swipe — another fixed fee. On the calculator defaults ($50 face they pay $50, $48 item + $6 shipping, 2.9%+$0.30, $14 cost, $5 label, $1 pack) selling the SKU now keeps $31.74 and a later redeem keeps $31.44. The $0.30 gap is the second $0.30 swipe on the $4 leftover. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn gift cards on or off.

Does discounting a Shopify gift card still pay?

Usually not on the same SKU. Selling a $50 card for $45 and redeeming it on the default $54 ticket keeps $26.59 — $5.16 less than selling the item now. The $5.00 discount plus the second swipe is the gap.

Do unredeemed Shopify gift cards count as profit?

Unredeemed cards leave cash after the sale-day fee, which is why an 80% redemption mix on the defaults shows $34.73. That extra is breakage — leftover liability that never shipped — not a forecast you should plan on. This page shows the mix so you can see the temptation; the winner is sell-now versus they-redeem.
