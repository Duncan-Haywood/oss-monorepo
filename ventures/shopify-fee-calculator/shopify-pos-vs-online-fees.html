Merchants search “Shopify POS vs online fees” because the in-person rate looks cheaper and the card reader looks free after a few markets. The missing piece is postage. If checkout charges more to ship than the label costs, that markup can beat the cheaper tap.

Common questions

Are Shopify POS fees lower than online Shopify Payments?

In-person Shopify Payments rates are often a lower percentage and a smaller fixed fee than online rates on the same plan. That is only the card side. An online order that marks up postage can still keep more cash than a pickup tap. Enter the rates from your statements — this page does not publish Shopify's live schedule.

Does a Shopify card reader pay for itself?

Only if in-person tickets keep more than the same SKU sold online. This calculator divides the reader cost by the extra cash kept on the winning in-person path (pickup when pickup wins, otherwise the same-ticket rate gap). It is arithmetic from your inputs, not a forecast of market-table volume.
