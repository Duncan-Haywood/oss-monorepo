What this checks

Merchants search “should I collect duties on Shopify” because a $54 checkout looks cheaper than a $62.10 DDP total. The $8.10 still has to be paid. DAP puts it on the door. DDP puts it on the card — and Shopify Payments takes 2.9% of it.

On the defaults — $48 item, $6 shipping charged, 15% duty, $5.00 outbound, $8.00 return, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $14 cost, $1 pack, $39 / 100 plan, 55% DAP take / 18% refuse, 42% DDP take / 6% refuse, 20 visitors — DAP expects $12.59 per visitor. DDP expects $12.00 ($0.59 less, $11.72 / month). A delivered DAP order keeps $31.74; DDP keeps $31.51. A refuse leaves −$17.49. DAP refuses have to reach about 20.2% before DDP matches. Common questions

Should I collect duties on Shopify?

Collecting duties at checkout (DDP) adds the duty to the card swipe — Shopify Payments charges its percentage on that extra amount — and usually lowers conversion. Letting the buyer pay at the door (DAP) keeps checkout cheaper and raises door-refuses. On the calculator defaults ($48 item + $6 shipping, 15% duty, 2.9%+$0.30, $14 cost, $5 outbound, $8 return, 55% DAP take / 18% refuse, 42% DDP take / 6% refuse) DAP expects $12.59 per international-interested visitor and DDP expects $12.00. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn Shopify Markets duties on or off.

Does Shopify charge fees on duties I collect?

Card fees sit on the checkout total. If you collect $8.10 of duty on a $54 ticket, Shopify Payments at 2.9% + $0.30 charges $2.10 instead of $1.87 — $0.23 extra on the duty. You then remit the $8.10. A delivered DDP order on the defaults keeps $31.51 versus $31.74 on DAP.

When does DAP lose to DDP on Shopify?

When enough DAP buyers refuse the package at the door. A refuse refunds the checkout, leaves the leftover $0.30 fixed fee, and eats outbound plus the return label. On the defaults DAP refuses have to reach about 20.2% before DDP matches, or DDP take has to reach about 44.1%. At a 25% DAP refuse rate DDP keeps more ($12.00 vs $10.69).
