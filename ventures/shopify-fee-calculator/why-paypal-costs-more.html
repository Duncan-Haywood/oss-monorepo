Why PayPal can cost more than Shopify Payments on the same order

Merchants search for “Shopify PayPal fees” because a checkout that uses PayPal, Stripe, or another processor is not just that processor’s rate. Shopify may also charge a third-party transaction fee on the order total. Shopify Payments replaces that extra Shopify fee with the Shopify Payments processing rate.

On a typical Basic-plan example — a $50 item, 2% Shopify third-party fee, and a 3.49% + $0.49 processor — the third-party path pays both the processor and Shopify. The Shopify Payments path pays only the Payments rate (for example 2.9% + $0.30). The difference is cash the merchant keeps or gives away on every order.

Rates change by country, plan, card type, and contract. This page does not publish Shopify’s live schedule. Open the Shopify fee calculator, enter the percentages from your current statements, and compare both paths on one order — including shipping, cost of goods, and a share of the monthly plan.

The calculator is arithmetic, not advice. It does not recommend a processor or claim a store is more profitable after switching.
