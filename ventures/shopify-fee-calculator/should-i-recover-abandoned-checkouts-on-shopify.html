What this checks

Merchants search “should I recover abandoned checkouts on Shopify” because a 10% code looks cheap next to a $54 ticket that already walked away. The $4.80 still comes out of leftover. Emails without a code keep the list. Leaving it keeps only the people who bounce back on their own.

On the defaults — $48 item, $6 shipping charged, $5.00 outbound, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $14 cost, $1 pack, $39 / 100 plan, 6% organic bounce-back, 12% email recover, 18% coupon recover, 10% off the item, $0.08 send cost, 80 abandoned checkouts — leaving it expects $1.90. Emails expect $3.73 ($1.82 more). Emails plus the coupon expect $4.79 ($2.89 more than leaving it, $231.23 / month). A recovered list order keeps $31.74; the coupon keeps $27.08. Emails have to recover about 6.3% before they match leaving it. The coupon has to recover about 14.1% before it matches emails. Send cost can rise to about $1.90 before emails match leaving it. Raise send cost to $3.00 and leaving it expects $1.90 versus $0.81 for emails and $1.87 for the coupon. Match recoveries at 12% and emails expect $3.73 versus $3.17 for the coupon.

Common questions

Should I recover abandoned checkouts on Shopify?

Recovery emails convert some of the checkouts that already left. A coupon converts more of them on a cheaper ticket. On the calculator defaults ($48 item, $6 shipping, 2.9%+$0.30, $14 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 6% organic bounce-back, 12% email recover, 18% coupon recover, 10% off the item, $0.08 allocated send cost) leaving it expects $1.90, emails expect $3.73, and emails plus the coupon expect $4.79. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn Shopify Email on or send a code.

When do recovery emails lose to leaving a Shopify checkout?

When the allocated send cost eats the extra recoveries. Emails have to recover about 6.3% before they match a 6% organic bounce-back on the defaults, and send cost can rise to about $1.90 before emails match leaving it. At a $3 SMS-adjacent cost, leaving it expects $1.90 against $0.81 for emails and $1.87 for the coupon.

When does a recovery coupon lose on Shopify?

When it does not lift recoveries enough to cover the cheaper ticket. A recovered list order keeps $31.74; the 10% item coupon keeps $27.08. The coupon has to recover about 14.1% before it matches emails alone. At a matched 12% recover rate emails keep $3.73 against $3.17 for the coupon.
