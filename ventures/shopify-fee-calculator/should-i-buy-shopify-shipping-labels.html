What this checks

Merchants search “Shopify Shipping vs Pirate Ship” because admin labels look convenient and a third-party quote often looks cheaper. The missing piece is checkout. If you already charge a flat shipping rate, buying the cheaper label keeps the spread. If you drop checkout shipping to match the Shopify quote, card fees fall with it — so the leftover is smaller than the raw label gap.

Common questions

Is Shopify Shipping cheaper than Pirate Ship?

Often not on USPS. Shopify Shipping is a discounted label inside admin. A Pirate Ship, Shippo, or carrier-account quote on the same weight and service is frequently lower. Enter both quotes from a live preview — this page does not publish Shopify's or USPS rates.

Can I charge Shopify Shipping and buy a cheaper label?

Yes, if checkout shipping is a rate you set. Charging Shopify's quote and buying a cheaper own label is not the raw label spread: Shopify Payments (or PayPal) takes a percentage of shipping charged, so lowering what the buyer pays also lowers the card fee. This calculator shows that leftover next to buying the Shopify label and buying your own label while checkout stays put.
