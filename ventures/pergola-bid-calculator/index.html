    Common questions

    Why does an attached pergola cost more than the same footprint freestanding?

    An attached cover ties into a ledger on the house, which means flashing the ledger correctly and framing to it — work a freestanding frame on four independent posts never needs. The calculator prices attachment as its own choice so a 12×16 ledger-mounted cover is not quoted off the freestanding number.

    Does this tell me if my pergola needs a permit or an engineered post footing?

    No. Permit requirements and footing engineering depend on your jurisdiction, wind load, and span — the “Include permit” toggle only adds a placeholder cost line for your own paperwork time, it is not a determination that a permit is or isn’t required. Confirm with your local building department before you frame it.

    Why is louvered or polycarbonate shade so much more than an open frame?

    An open rafter frame is mostly post-and-beam labor; louvered and polycarbonate roofs add a second material system, fasteners, and often a drainage detail the open frame doesn’t have. Switch the shade dropdown to see the dollar and hour gap directly rather than guessing at a flat per-square-foot upcharge.

    Where do the starting spans and hours come from?

    They're common residential ballpark figures for post spacing and shade install time, not a building-code opinion or an engineering stamp. Time your own crew on a job or two and replace the defaults in calc.mjs so the bid matches how your shop actually builds.
