FOR RESIDENTIAL ROOFING CONTRACTORS

Bid the roof, not the floor plan.

Price a reroof the way the trade actually does: each section’s footprint converted to roof squares by its pitch, plus waste, tear-off layers, dump, loaded labor, and the margin you intend to keep. You get the job price, what it works out to per square, and the number below which the job loses money. Why house square footage is the wrong unit.

The roof

One row per section. Footprint is the area on the ground, not the shingle surface — pitch turns it into squares. Tear-off layers is how many existing roofs come off (0 for a new deck).

Material and waste

Picking a covering fills in material and install hours, but never overwrites a number you have already changed. Replace them with your current supplier invoice and a rate timed from your own crew.

Your costs

Steepness is applied automatically: 7/12–9/12 adds 25% labor, 10/12–12/12 adds 50%, steeper than 12/12 doubles it. Those steps are starting points, not a safety rating.
