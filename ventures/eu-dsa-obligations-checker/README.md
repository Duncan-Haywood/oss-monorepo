# EU DSA Obligations Checker

Free, private browser tool: say what kind of online service you run (conduit, cache, host, public platform, marketplace, search engine), your size and your monthly EU users, and get the Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065) duties that apply, each with its Article, the micro and small enterprise exemption, the 45 million very-large threshold and the penalty ceiling.

**Live tool:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/eu-dsa-obligations-checker/

After using this, you know which DSA Articles to build for (notice and action, statements of reasons, complaints, trader checks) and which you can skip, which used to take a law-firm memo.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/eu-dsa-obligations-checker/**/*.test.mjs'`.
