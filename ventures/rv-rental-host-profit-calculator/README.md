# RV Rental Host Profit Calculator

**Live tool:** <https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/rv-rental-host-profit-calculator/>

Free, private browser tool for people who rent out an RV, camper or trailer: nightly rate, nights rented, platform fee, cleaning, wear, loan, insurance, storage and depreciation become a cash result, a result after depreciation, break-even nights and the nightly rate that pays the profit you want.

After using this, you can tell whether renting your camper pays for owning it, and what rate or how many nights it takes, which used to mean guessing from a listing site's earnings estimate.

Open `index.html`. Tests: `node --test 'ventures/rv-rental-host-profit-calculator/**/*.test.mjs'`.
