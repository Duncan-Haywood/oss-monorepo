This calculator vs. Fence Cloud

Two different shapes: Fence Cloud is an account-based CRM and estimating platform for running a fence company's whole lead-to-job pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one fence install cost, given its posts, gates, and tear-out, before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

Fence Cloud is a cloud-based CRM and estimating platform built specifically for fence contractors. It covers lead and project tracking with contact management, activity logs, and advanced search; material-breakdown estimating across chain link, wood, vinyl, ornamental, and agricultural/split-rail fence, including mixing suppliers or materials and marking up drawings; and auto-filled, customizable paperwork — contracts, packing lists, labor worksheets, gate cut sheets, work orders, and foreman reports — meant to take a job from lead through completion inside one account. It is sold as an ongoing subscription. Fence Cloud's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — fence-specific CRM plus estimating, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Fence Cloud appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Fence Cloud does. It does not track a lead, generate a cut sheet, or hold a record across jobs. It takes one fence install — posts counted from run length and spacing, heavier terminals at ends, corners, and gates, panel infill after gate openings come out, tear-out, and clay, rock, or slope conditions — plus loaded labor, other direct cost, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the job price, the price per linear foot, the effective hourly rate, the post and gate counts, the material split, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, plus a copyable plain-text bid summary. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Fence Cloud?

They do different jobs. Fence Cloud is a cloud-based CRM and estimating platform built specifically for fence contractors, covering lead and project tracking, material-breakdown estimates across chain link, wood, vinyl, ornamental, and agricultural/split-rail fence, and auto-filled paperwork such as contracts, packing lists, labor worksheets, gate cut sheets, work orders, and foreman reports. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account for running a fence company's whole lead-to-job pipeline. This calculator does none of that. It takes one fence install — posts from run length and spacing, heavier terminals at ends, corners, and gates, tear-out, and digging conditions — and turns that into the job price, price per linear foot, effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, plus a copyable bid summary, instantly, in the browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A fence company running leads and paperwork across every job at once is better served by Fence Cloud or a comparable platform; a contractor who wants an instant, private, correctly-sized price for one job before a bid goes out is in the right place here.
