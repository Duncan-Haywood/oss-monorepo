Moses and Aaron at the Mountain of God

An old man in Egypt is told to go out into the wilderness to meet a brother he has not seen in forty years, and he goes, and finds him at the mountain of God, and kisses him; and then the two of them walk back together to tell their people that the Lord has seen. Told from the Book of Exodus, chapter four.
