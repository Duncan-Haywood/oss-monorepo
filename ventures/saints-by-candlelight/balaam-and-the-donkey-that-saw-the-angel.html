Balaam and the Donkey That Saw the Angel

The king of Moab hires a famous seer from far beyond the desert to curse the people of Israel camped on his border, but on the road an angel blocks the way, a donkey sees what her master cannot, and every curse Balaam opens his mouth to speak comes out a blessing, ending with a star rising out of Jacob. Told from the Book of Numbers, chapters twenty-two to twenty-four.

the story is Scripture, the Book of Numbers, chapters twenty-two to twenty-four, with Numbers twenty-five and thirty-one, Deuteronomy twenty-three, Micah six, Second Peter two, Jude and Revelation two, told straight in a plain rendering; a plaster inscription found at Tell Deir Alla in Jordan in nineteen sixty-seven names a seer called Balaam son of Beor
