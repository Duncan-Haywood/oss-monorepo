Paul and the Speech on the Steps of the Fortress

Saint Paul reaches Jerusalem at last, tells Saint James and the elders everything God has done among the nations, agrees to pay for four men finishing a vow in the temple, and is seized by a crowd, carried up the steps of a Roman fortress in two chains, and asks the commander if he may say a few words.
