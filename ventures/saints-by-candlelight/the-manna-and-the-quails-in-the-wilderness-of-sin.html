That is why Christians have always loved this story. Saint Ambrose, the bishop of Milan, explaining the Eucharist to the newly baptised in the fourth century, told them about the manna. It was a great and wonderful thing, he said, that God rained manna on the fathers, and they were fed every day with food from heaven. And yet, he said, those who ate it in the wilderness died.
