The Wise Woman of Tekoa and the Last Coal

King David's son Absalom has been in exile for three years, and the king will neither forgive him nor forget him, so the general Joab fetches a wise woman from the hill town of Tekoa to tell the king a story about a widow, two sons and the last coal in her hearth; the king rules on the story, and then finds out whose story it was.
