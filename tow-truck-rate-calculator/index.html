      The model, and what it leaves out

      • Truck hourly cost = (truck payment + insurance) / billable hours per month + driver cost per hour. Per-mile cost = fuel price / mpg + wear per mile.
      • True cost = job hours x truck hourly cost + (deadhead + loaded miles) x per-mile cost + storage days x your storage cost. Job hours are driving time plus on-scene and recovery minutes.
      • Price = hook-up + loaded miles beyond the included distance x rate + surcharges + storage days x daily fee. Break-even = true cost. Target price = true cost / (1 - target margin).
      • Levers are fixed-size steps (hook-up +$10, rate +$0.50 per mile, 25% less deadhead, 10 minutes saved, storage +$5 a day) so they can be compared; the sizes are illustrative, not recommendations.
      • Not modelled: idle time between jobs beyond your billable-hours figure, tolls, impound lot and gate fees, labour for dollies or special equipment, motor-club and rotation-list payout schedules, taxes, and any rate cap that applies to you.

      Common questions

      How do I price a tow job?

      Add up what the job really costs you: truck time at your hourly cost (payment, insurance and driver spread over billable hours), fuel and wear on every mile including the empty deadhead, and any storage cost. Then price above that by the margin you need. This tool does the arithmetic from your own figures.

      What is deadhead in towing?

      Deadhead miles are the miles you drive empty to reach the pickup. They cost fuel, wear and truck time like any other mile but are often not billed separately, so they are folded into the true cost of the job here.

      What is my break-even per tow?

      The break-even price is the job's true cost: truck time at your hourly cost, per-mile fuel and wear for loaded and deadhead miles, and any storage cost. Below it every job loses money.

      Can I charge whatever this calculator suggests?

      Not necessarily. Many states and cities regulate or cap towing rates, rotation-list contracts and private-property tows set their own schedules, and storage fees are often limited. This tool does not know your local rules. Check them before you publish a rate.
