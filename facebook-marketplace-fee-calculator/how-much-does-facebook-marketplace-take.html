How much does Facebook Marketplace take per sale?

Facebook Marketplace charges a 10% selling fee on a shipped checkout order, applied to the buyer-paid total -- the item price plus any buyer-paid shipping, and sales tax where Facebook collects it -- with an $0.80 minimum fee per sale. A sale arranged for local pickup instead carries no selling fee at all. The rate has changed more than once in the last two years, so a number quoted from an old blog post or forum thread may already be stale.

The shipped-order formula

fee = max(10% × (item price + buyer-paid shipping), $0.80)

payout = item price + buyer-paid shipping − fee − your own shipping-label cost

Local pickup skips the formula entirely: the fee is $0, so the payout is simply the item price minus whatever you spent on the item and packaging.

Worked example 1: $50 item, shipped, no buyer-paid shipping

Worked example 2: the $0.80 minimum takes over on a small sale

LineAmount
Item price$5.00
10% of $5.00 would be$0.50
Selling fee actually charged ($0.80 minimum)$0.80
Payout$4.20

Not $4.50 (item price minus 10%) -- once the buyer-paid total falls under $8, the $0.80 minimum is the binding fee, not the 10% rate. This is the detail sellers who mentally calculate "just take 10% off" get wrong on small sales.

Shipping counts toward the fee base too

A $50 item with $10 in buyer-paid shipping pays 10% of the combined $60, which is $6.00 -- not 10% of the $50 item price alone. The payout is $54.00 ($60.00 collected minus the $6.00 fee), assuming you did not also pay for your own shipping label.

Local pickup: genuinely $0

LineAmount
Item price$50.00
Selling fee$0.00
Payout$50.00

No selling fee, listing fee, or subscription cost applies to a sale arranged for local pickup rather than Facebook's shipped checkout.

Common questions

How much does Facebook Marketplace take on a $50 sale?

For a shipped checkout order with no buyer-paid shipping: 10% of $50, which is $5.00, leaving a $45.00 payout.

How much does Facebook Marketplace take on a small $5 sale?

10% of $5 would be $0.50, but Facebook charges an $0.80 minimum fee on every shipped order, so the fee is $0.80, not $0.50 -- leaving a $4.20 payout instead of the $4.50 a flat 10% calculation would suggest.

How much does Facebook Marketplace take on a local pickup sale?

Nothing. Local pickup sales carry no selling fee, listing fee, or subscription cost -- the full item price is the payout, minus whatever you spent on the item and packaging yourself.
