        Common questions

        How much does Facebook Marketplace take per sale?

        For a shipped checkout order, Facebook charges a 10% selling fee on the buyer-paid total -- the item price plus any buyer-paid shipping, and sales tax where Facebook collects it -- with an $0.80 minimum fee per sale. A locally arranged pickup sale has no selling fee at all.

        Is local pickup really free on Facebook Marketplace?

        Yes. Facebook does not charge a selling fee, listing fee, or subscription cost on a sale arranged for local pickup. The 10% fee only applies to orders that go through Facebook's shipped checkout.

        What is the $0.80 minimum fee?

        On a shipped order, if 10% of the buyer-paid total would come to less than $0.80, Facebook charges the $0.80 minimum instead. That only changes the fee on sales where the item price, shipping, and any tax collected add up to less than $8.

        What happens if a shipped order ends in a dispute?

        If a payment dispute on a shipped order is ruled against the seller, Facebook deducts a flat $20 chargeback penalty from the seller's payout ledger, on top of losing the sale itself. This tool models that as an optional toggle rather than assuming it by default.
