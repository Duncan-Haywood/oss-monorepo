Should I hire a grant writer to file this report on time?

Coordinators search this because a progress report is due in weeks — or already late — and the remaining draw is larger than the weekend they have left. The temptation is to treat the report as paperwork. The funder treats it as the gate on the next check.

A late report usually stops cash, not just a PDF. Unspent award stays encumbered. The next installment does not ship. A renewal can be refused. On a typical $24,000 community food program — $8,500 spent, $15,500 still unspent, a $6,000 next installment, report due 15 September 2026 — that is $21,500.00 at risk as of 26 August 2026 (20 days away).

A writer still pays when the leftover is real. Filing it yourself at 14 hours and $42/hr costs $588.00 and keeps $20,912.00. A contract writer at 8 hours and $85/hr costs $680.00 and still keeps $20,820.00. Missing the report keeps $0.00 — a $21,500.00 hole.

A writer does not pay when only crumbs remain. A $300 leftover against a $1,250 writer finishes $950 worse than missing the report. Writing that leftover yourself in 3 hours at $42/hr ($126) still keeps $174. Type only the installment or renewal your award letter actually withholds.
