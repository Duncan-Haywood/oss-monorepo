Skill: pick an object, retry on failure.  Twin: each attempt succeeds independently with p=0.60.  Real: each object has a latent
success probability ~ Beta(a,b) with the same mean p (so the single-attempt success rate, which a twin is calibrated on, matches)
and intra-object correlation rho = 1/(a+b+1) between two attempts' outcomes.
