# sensing-ghostring: handling the edge ring of a moving subject in a burst merge (synthetic, Poisson-Gaussian noise)
48x48 px scenes, 12 scenes per cell, still camera, one disc (radius 4 px) moving along x at v px per frame.
A = 4, read = 3 e-, P = 300 e- per unit radiance in one large frame (small frame: 75). Truth = noise-free frame 0. Display PSNR, border cropped.
ring = pixels within 1 px outside to 2 px inside the frame-0 footprint edge; disc = footprint eroded by 2 px; static = never within 2 px of the subject.
band/median/ring=aligned/ring=reject: aligned+rej inside the estimated subject mask, refguided outside it, and in the 1-px band around the mask edge: frame 0 / temporal median / aligned+rej / refguided.
