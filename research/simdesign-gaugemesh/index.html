Is the narrow 2 mm laser-cut bracket region a mesh artefact?

research/simdesign-gaugemesh

The narrow 2 mm laser-cut bracket region is a mesh effect: all 39 designs feasible at h = 2.5 mm fail the stress limit at h ≤ 1.5 mm (peak 1.09–1.27× higher, still rising at 0.75 mm); stylised, negative for the search mesh.

4 tests

What it found

A negative result for the search mesh (stylised). All 39 gauge-2 bracket designs that were feasible on the 2.5 mm search mesh (19 under laser rules, 20 for the no-rules twin) are infeasible on finer meshes (1.5, 1.0, 0.75 mm): the peak von Mises stress is 1.09–1.27× higher at 0.75 mm and is still rising, at the point where the fillet meets the arm. Tip deflection is unchanged and the fillet facet count is not the cause. Earlier mass prices in the bracket chain are therefore conditional on the coarse mesh; no fine-mesh optimum was searched.

  • Does the peak stress at the fillet tangent point of the L-bracket converge under graded mesh refinement?Does the L-bracket's peak stress at the fillet tangent point converge under graded mesh refinement? Yes for four designs: it flattens at 1.09–1.14× its 1.5 mm value (0.0–1.1% change over the last 4× refinement down to 0.025 mm), so the 2 mm region's mesh dependence is a resolution effect; the 16/15/2 design is marginally over 100 MPa when converged; stylised 2D, no reference solution.
  • Is the least-mass laser-cut bracket the same on coarse and fine meshes under a patch-averaged stress rule?Under a 3 mm patch-averaged stress rule the least-mass laser-cut bracket is the same on a 2.5 mm and a 1.0 mm mesh (gauge 2: 32.31 vs 32.33 g), while the centroid-peak rule leaves no feasible gauge-2 design on the fine mesh; the patch value hardly sees the fillet radius; stylised, preliminary.
  • Do cheap beam and 2D models rank candidate cantilever designs like 3D finite elements?Do cheap beam and 2D models rank cantilever designs like 3D finite elements? Yes on 3 seeds of 80 designs: Kendall τ 0.987–0.996 and the 3D-best design always ranked first, even though single-design merit is off by up to 14%; stubby sections agree least; stylised, one load case.

Assumed material inputs. Stylised.
