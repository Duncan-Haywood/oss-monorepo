# religion-origins: how did religion develop? The evidence from cognitive science, archaeology, history and theology

**How did religion begin and change?** This project is a heavily cited synthesis of what the evidence shows, from the cognitive science of belief and the first burials through Göbekli Tepe, the first states, the Axial Age, Israelite monotheism and the "Big Gods" debate, to early Christianity, the East–West Schism and today's data. The evidence is read through several lenses: evolutionary and cognitive science, archaeology, historical criticism, sociology, and Catholic and Orthodox theology. Each lens is labelled, and where the evidence is contested we say so. Produced with AI assistance (Claude).

**Live page:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/monorepo-projects/research/religion-origins/

## Strands
1. Cognitive and evolutionary foundations
2. Prehistoric archaeology (burials, symbolic behaviour, Göbekli Tepe, Çatalhöyük)
3. Religion in the first states (Mesopotamia, Egypt, Shang, Indus, Vedic India, Mesoamerica, Andes, Ugarit)
4. The Axial Age and the emergence of monotheism
5. The quantitative Big Gods / moralizing gods debate and its datasets
6. Early Christianity to the East–West Schism
7. Theological lenses on religion's origin, Catholic and Orthodox among them
8. Modernity, secularisation and current data

## Integrity
No result here is new data analysis. Every citation should be checked against its source before it is quoted elsewhere: the notes mark which sources were opened this session and which were not.
