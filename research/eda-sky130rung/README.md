# eda-sky130rung: does generic-gate cell count rank accelerator designs like SKY130-mapped area and clock period?

Why it seemed worth doing: `eda-fidelity` built a ladder whose top rung was generic-gate synthesis and listed "a real cell library" as missing. Every `eda-*` result so far scores designs in *generic gates*, which have no area or delay. If that rung ranks designs differently from a real library, the Pareto fronts those projects report may be wrong about which design is best. This is the first rung with physical units. It asks a new question rather than another variant of the proposer comparisons. Produced with AI assistance (Claude). **Preliminary:** one design family, one ABC script, no place and route, so nothing here is sign-off or silicon.

**Live page:** https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/research/eda-sky130rung/

**Builds on.** `research/eda-harness` (design generator and its committed evaluation log of generic-gate cells, flip-flops and depth), `research/chip-accel` (the MAC engine), `research/chip-ppa-sky130mac` (liberty filtering of dont-use cells) and `research/eda-flowtune` (`fetch_pdk.sh`, the pinned SKY130 platform files). Tools: Yosys 0.33 and ABC (apt), the SKY130 HD `tt_025C_1v80` liberty from OpenROAD-flow-scripts commit `e5cc6e799f5d` (Apache-2.0, fetched, not vendored). The fidelity-ladder framing follows Peherstorfer, Willcox and Gunzburger (2018), *SIAM Review* 60(3):550-591. No CU Boulder lab's work is built on and no affiliation is implied.

## Method
- `src/eda_sky130rung/flow.py`: `read_verilog; synth -flatten -noabc; dfflibmap -liberty; abc -liberty -script "strash; dc2; map; topo; stime"; stat -liberty`. The existing `map_sky130` skips `dfflibmap`, which would leave the design's flip-flops (about a third of its cells) without area; this adds it. Chip area is Yosys's `stat -liberty` total, delay is ABC's `stime` critical path over combinational logic between registers.
- `experiments/run.py` regenerates the Verilog for all 40 `eda-harness` designs and maps each (about 2 s to 30 s each). `experiments/analyze.py` reads `mapped.json` and writes `results.txt` and `rungs.png`.
- Compared per design: generic cells vs mapped area, generic depth vs mapped delay, generic flip-flops vs mapped flip-flops, and the harness's objective cells × cycles vs area × latency (latency = cycles × mapped delay). Rank agreement is Spearman and Kendall tau-b (`src/eda_sky130rung/rank.py`, checked against the textbook IQ-versus-TV example for both), plus top-k overlap and Pareto-front membership.

## Reproduce
```bash
sudo apt-get install -y yosys && pip install numpy matplotlib
cd research/eda-sky130rung
PYTHONPATH=src python -m unittest discover -s tests
PYTHONPATH=src:../eda-harness/src:../chip-accel/src python experiments/run.py   # a few minutes; fetches the PDK to /tmp/pdk/p
cd experiments && PYTHONPATH=../src python analyze.py
```

## Results (`experiments/results.txt`, data `experiments/mapped.json`)
Yosys 0.33, SKY130 HD tt_025C_1v80 at ORFS `e5cc6e799f`, ABC `strash; dc2; map`.
- **Area: the generic rung is a very good proxy.** Spearman 0.995 and Kendall 0.954 between generic cells and mapped area over 40 designs; mapped area per generic cell stays within 6.4 to 8.4 µm². Flip-flop counts agree at Spearman 0.972 (the two flows count enable flip-flops differently, so the absolute counts differ).
- **Delay: it is not.** Generic logic depth vs ABC's mapped critical-path delay is Spearman 0.611, Kendall 0.465. Delay is not monotone in the lane count either (for 6-bit designs, p = 1 to 8: 5292, 5679, 7587, 6556, 7655, 7971, 10113, 10789 ps).
- **Consequence for the front.** The (cells, cycles) front (p = 8, 4, 2, 1 at 4 bits) equals the SKY130 (area, cycles) front, so every earlier `eda-*` front survives a switch to real area. Once the clock period enters (latency = cycles × delay), the front becomes p4w4, p2w4, p2w5, p1w4: three of four generic-front designs remain, p = 8 at 4 bits drops off because its clock is slow, and a 5-bit two-lane design joins. Top-3, top-5 and top-10 overlap on cells × cycles vs area × latency: 0.67, 0.60, 0.90. The best single design (p = 4, 4 bits) is the same under both.
- Figure: `experiments/rungs.png`.

## Limitations
- One design family generated by one script; the 5-bit design joining the front is a single point. A different family (the `chip-systolic-*` arrays, `eda-flowtune`'s eight designs) may behave differently.
- ABC `map` with no target period (`-D`) minimises delay then recovers area. Its delay has no wire load, no placement and no clock-tree or setup/clk-to-q terms, so the latency numbers are a model of a clock period, not a measurement. Sizing under a real constraint would change both area and delay.
- One ABC script (`dc2; map`). `eda-flowtune` shows the script matters; the cell-count-to-area agreement may be script-dependent.
- No place and route (OpenROAD is not installed here), so the question of how well synthesis ranks post-route results is still open. Open-flow numbers are estimates, not sign-off results, and say nothing about silicon.
- 40 points with many ties in cycles; rank correlations carry no confidence intervals here.

## Next steps
Install OpenROAD and add a post-route rung; rerun with a clock-constrained `abc -D` sweep; repeat on `chip-systolic` and on `eda-flowtune`'s designs to see whether delay is the generally unreliable cheap rung; feed the mapped objective back into `eda-fidelity`'s successive-halving replay.
