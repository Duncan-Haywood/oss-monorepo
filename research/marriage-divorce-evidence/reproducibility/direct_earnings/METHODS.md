Run `python3 analyze.py`. The helper independently reconstructs the existing jointmodel design from saved ../couple_profiles/couple_profile_input.csv, joint_model_coefficients.csv and joint_model_covariance.csv; no original script, fit or report is modified. It uses the entire saved coefficient covariance, not separate marginal confidence intervals. The target is the model's fixed baseline-weighted observed person-year distribution. Different rows are standardized settings over that common target, not observed crude subgroup rates.

The direct comparison is statistically unresolved at95%: do not claim husband-only is worse or husband-majority dual earning definitively better, and do not claim equivalence. These are observational conditional rates, not causal effects of a spouse stopping work. The global contrast is supported by broader model data and should not be substituted as a validated prediction for that sparse configuration.
