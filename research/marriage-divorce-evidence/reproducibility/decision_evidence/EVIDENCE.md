## Quotes and caveats

- **Stanley2010_PREP_Army_1yr** (Results): “This chi-square test indicated that the PREP for Strong Bonds group had a significantly lower divorce rate (5/246 = 2.03%) than the control group (14/226 = 6.20%), χ2 (1) = 4.26, p < .05. Fisher’s exact test for significance yielded a value of p = .03.” *Caveats:* Short follow-up; one site; superseded by the 2-year report on the full trial, where the effect held at this site but not at the second site.
- **Stanley2014_PREP_Army_2yr_pooled** (Results, Tests Hypothesis One: Divorce Outcomes): “Findings from an overall test of divorce likelihood (pooled across both sites) are consistent with the prediction that the intervention group would be less likely to divorce than the control group; 7.6% (26/342) of the intervention group divorced compared to 11.9% (38/318) of the control group, B = -0.51, Odds Ratio = 0.60, p = .06.” *Caveats:* Pooled effect is driven by Site 1; at Site 2 the intervention group divorced slightly more (6.3% vs 4.4%, OR 1.37, p = .60). No lasting effect on relationship quality.
- **Stanley2014_PREP_Army_2yr_site1** (Results, Tests Hypothesis One: Divorce Outcomes): “At Site 1, 8.1% (20/248) of the intervention group divorced compared to 14.9% (34/228) of the control group, B = -0.69, Odds Ratio = 0.50, p < .01.” *Caveats:* Subgroup of a two-site trial; the effect was concentrated in ethnic-minority couples (3.8% vs 15.7%), with little difference for non-minority couples (10.6% vs 13.3%).
- **Rhoades2015_PREP_x_precommitment_cohab** (Results (third hypothesis)): “A significant chi-square test in the control group showed that 18 out of 123 (14.6%) of those who cohabited before a mutual commitment to marry filed for divorce compared to 14 out of 186 (7.5%) of those who did not, χ2 (1, N = 309) = 4.03, p < .05. For the PREP group, there was not a significant difference in divorce by cohabitation history, with 11 out of 141 (7.8%) of those who cohabited before a mutual commitment to marry having filed for divorce compared to 14 out of 188 (7.4%) of those who did not, χ2 (1, N = 329) = 0.02, p = .90.” *Caveats:* Cohabitation history is self-selected and reported years after the fact; only the intervention is randomised. Subgroup contrast, not a test of cohabiting itself.
- **Rogge2013_PREP_CARE_RA_3yr** (Abstract): “Couples in the no-treatment condition dissolved their relationships at a higher rate (24%) than couples completing PREP, CARE, and RA, who did not differ on rates of dissolution (11%).” *Caveats:* Small trial; the cheap one-session arm did as well as 15 hours of skills training, so the active ingredient may not be skills; skills arms had some unintended effects. Abstract only read.
