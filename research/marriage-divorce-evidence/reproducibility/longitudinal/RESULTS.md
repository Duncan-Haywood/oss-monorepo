The masters split by sex is substantial within this retained cohort; it is not a comparison of husbands and wives married to one another. Doctoral and professional cells, especially women, are too small for precise rankings. Education is latest attained and may follow divorce. These estimates do not predict an individual marriage or establish what would happen if someone earned another degree.

Prospective income and work-hour results appear in `nlsy79_prospective_income_employment.csv`. Predictors refer to 1999 and divorce transitions to rounds 2002–2022, among 3,502 people married in 2000 and retained in 2022. The highest family-income band is associated with less subsequent divorce for men; intermediate income bands are not monotonically ordered. The pattern among women is weaker. These data do not establish a salary threshold or causal effect.
