A single husband-only interaction coefficient has an interval excluding1, but the interaction family as a whole is uncertain and shows no held-out improvement; selecting that coefficient would overstate the evidence.

All four broad own/partner-schooling cells contain59–372divorces. Yet only21of160sex×schoolingpair×totalwagequartile×earnings-role cells meet even the modest100-person/10-event screen. Thus the model cannot responsibly rank a detailed partner for every individual profile.

Given a fixed own salary, earnings-role comparisons at fixed total wages also change own salary. They are not the requested partner-salary comparison unless own and partner wages are jointly reconstructed while total/share categories are recomputed. The evidence currently supports population associations and uncertainty, not validated matching advice.
