What does a verifier's silence tell you?

research/abstention-scoring

Selective reporting under proper scoring: abstention iff |p−α| < √c (Brier), silence is evidence unless the anchor is a symmetric prior, ignoring it costs 30× the log-loss at n=12, and a stale anchor makes silence point at the wrong state.

12 tests

What it found

When reporting costs c, a truthful verifier paid the score gain over an anchor α abstains exactly when |p−α| < √c (Brier) or KL(p‖α) < c (log), so silence is an interval of signals and carries evidence unless the anchor is a symmetric prior. At prior 0.1 the outcome rate among reporters is 0.57; an aggregator that drops silent verifiers has log-loss 0.62 against 0.02 for one that uses silence at n=12 (it worsens as verifiers are added); a stale anchor makes silence point at the wrong state. Quadrature and seeded Monte Carlo. Stylised.

More in Proper scoring & elicitation

  • How do you price the tail of benign drift?Jointly eliciting VaR and expected shortfall of benign drift with a scale-free Rockafellar–Uryasev score: ES alone is not elicitable, misreport cost is exactly ln r + 1/r − 1, quantile-only tolerances leave a/(a−1) of the Pareto tail uncovered, and auditing tail reports breaks down at index ≤ 2.
  • Should you pay verifiers for their calibration?Paying verifiers for resolution, not calibration: raw Brier/log excess is exactly E[(r-p)²]/E[KL(p‖r)], Brier is bounded but log is not, log-score selection reverses for overconfident high-resolution verifiers (a ≥ 2.49), and n-sample recalibration costs tr(HI⁻¹)/2n (0.264/n).
  • Should a verifier's continuous drift report be scored by CRPS or log score?CRPS vs log score for continuous drift reports: exact Gaussian closed forms, overconfidence capped at 0.234σ under CRPS (unbounded under log), 1-Lipschitz outcome sensitivity, plug-in cost 5σ/(8√π n), and finite score variance for tails ν>2 vs ν>4.
  • Can a verifier be paid for reporting drift variance?Eliciting drift variance from two independent re-executions: D=(y1−y2)²/2 unbiased with Var=σ⁴(κ+1)/2, exact Brier and scale-free misreport costs (1/λ+ln λ−1), detection needs ≈2z²(κ+1)/(λ−1)² tasks, disjoint pairs 1.8× worse than the sample variance, heavy tails break m^{−1/2} concentration.
  • What can be elicited from several independent draws of benign drift?Eliciting drift *variance* from pairs of independent draws: exact pair score (excess loss (r−σ²)²), degree table (mean² 2, third moment 3, σ⁴ 4), audit cost ((z_α+z_β)/ρ)²(κ+1)/2 pairs, robust Gini score beats it for heavy tails, all-pairs pooling saves (κ+1)/(κ−1), correlated draws bias the target to σ²(1−ρ).
  • Can the log score be made payable?Bounded-payment log score by quadratic tangent extension: strictly proper, log-identical on [ε,1−ε], worst-case payment exactly ln((1−ε)/ε)+1/(1−ε), 63× Brier's curvature per range at p=10⁻³, crossover p(1−p)=1/(2R), best cutoff ε=p.
