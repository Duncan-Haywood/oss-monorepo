A twin whose wind gusts are matched to the logged variance each step: how wrong is the position variance of a PD-held vehicle, and does matching the spectrum instead fix it?

research/gust-twin

A twin whose wind gusts are iid per step with the logged variance: real position variance is s²(a+k_d)/(k_d k_p(a²+a k_d+k_p)), the twin's is 141× smaller at a=1, dt=0.01 and scales as dt, its 1e-3 margin is exceeded 78% of the time, and a spectrum-matched white twin is always conservative.

12 tests

What it found

Real gust: Ornstein–Uhlenbeck with rate a and variance s2; exact real variance s2(a+kd)/(kdkp(a2+akd+kp)). A twin drawing iid gusts per step has variance s2dt/(2kdkp): 141× too small at a=1, dt=0.01 (270× at a=0.05), a standard deviation scaling as √dt, and a 10−3 margin that the real system exceeds 78% of the time. A spectrum-matched white twin is always conservative, real/twin = a(a+kd)/(a2+akd+kp) < 1 (0.71 at a=1). Fitting the OU rate from logged wind recovers the real variance (within 1% at 105 samples). Stylised: linear loop, Gaussian gusts, no wind data.
