## 2026-11-14 · A start that has an AC power flow: dispatch homotopy from the file's dispatch to the DC point (plan, written before any homotopy or repair ran; milestone 6's Next (1), `research/grid-linopf`)
- Why: on 5 of the 24 held-out cases (case1888_rte TYP, API, SAD; case1951_rte TYP, SAD) and on case300 TYP in development, the AC power flow at the DC start does not converge, so the frozen repair stops at round 1 ("PF failed at point") with no AC point to linearise at. Milestone 6 read this as "the method's reach on the RTE networks is decided by the start, not by the repair". That is only true if the DC dispatch really has no power-flow solution; a Newton started from the file's bus voltages may simply miss one that exists.
- What I know before writing this: only the six "PF failed at point" verdicts above (from `results_heldout_noref.txt` and `results_converge.txt`). No power flow at any other dispatch, and no homotopy, has run on these cases.
- Method fixed now (`experiments/run_homotopy.py`): voltage setpoints held at the file schedule throughout (as every held-out arm). Path PG(&lambda;) = (1 &minus; &lambda;) PG<sub>file</sub> + &lambda; PG<sub>DC</sub>, where PG<sub>DC</sub> is milestone 6's start (DC OPF with angle limits, else without). &lambda; = 0 is a cold Newton (PYPOWER, 30 iterations, as `aclin.PFOPT`) from the file's voltages; each next point is a Newton warm-started at the previous solution's voltages, step 0.1, halved on failure down to 1/1024, after which the path stops. &lambda;* is the largest &lambda; reached. **Arm H**: the frozen J-m-a repair (20 rounds, margins, analytic, Clarabel, screened; no constant changes) started at PG(&lambda;*) with round 1 linearised at the path's last solution, and every later AC check warm-started at the current round's linearisation point (a Newton solution of the same power-flow equations, found from a nearby start; the default cold check stays unchanged for every earlier arm). H+R = the reactive stage on H's best point if it is not fully clean. Cost reference: PIPS's AC optimum for case300 TYP, PGLib's published IPOPT objective for the RTE cases (as milestone 6's diagnostic).
- Predictions, to be reported held or refuted:
  - **Q1:** the file dispatch (&lambda; = 0) has a cold power flow on all 6 cases. Prior 80 %.
  - **Q2:** the path reaches &lambda; = 1 (the DC dispatch has a power flow, reachable by continuation) on at least 3 of 6. Prior 55 %.
  - **Q3:** H is fully clean within 20 rounds on at least 2 of 6. Prior 35 %.
  - **Q4:** H or H+R is fully clean on at least 3 of 6. Prior 35 %.
  - **Q5:** every fully clean H or H+R point costs at most +1 % over its reference. Prior 60 %.
- Reported as found: &lambda;* and the number of power flows per case, which limit class the start breaks, how many of H's rounds go elastic or fail their power flow, and wall time.

## 2026-11-13 · Why does the repair finish, or not? Convergence diagnostics and a 30-round budget (plan, written before any instrumented run; milestone 7 of `research/grid-linopf`)
- Why: the proposal's H4 says the repair reaches feasibility in a number of rounds bounded by the initial violation over the minimum per-round decrease, under a checkable condition (nonsingular power-flow Jacobian, bounded curvature of the limit functions on the trust region), within a factor of 10 of the observed rounds, and is refuted if the condition holds on a case where the method cycles. Milestone 5 found the nominal counts most sensitive to the round budget, and 13 of 34 cases end round 10 not clean, 6 of them on hard (non-elastic) QPs in every round. Nothing so far says *why* a round fails.
- Theory to check (one-step finishing condition, Proposition 1). Let a round's hard QP return a step d from x<sub>0</sub>, so every linear row satisfies g<sub>i</sub>(x<sub>0</sub>) + &nabla;g<sub>i</sub> d &le; b<sub>i</sub> &minus; &mu;<sub>i</sub> (&mu; = `vlin.MARGIN`). With the remainder e<sub>i</sub> = g<sub>i</sub>(x<sub>0</sub>+d) &minus; g<sub>i</sub>(x<sub>0</sub>) &minus; &nabla;g<sub>i</sub> d, the trial point breaks row i only if e<sub>i</sub> &gt; &mu;<sub>i</sub>; where the power flow is smooth (nonsingular Jacobian), |e<sub>i</sub>| &le; &frac12; M<sub>i</sub> ||d||<sup>2</sup>, so ||d|| &le; min<sub>i</sub> (2&mu;<sub>i</sub>/M<sub>i</sub>)<sup>1/2</sup> finishes the repair in that round. An elastic round has no such guarantee (the linear model has no feasible point in the trust region). Every rejected round halves the trust region, so with hard QPs the step must eventually fall inside that radius; accepted not-clean rounds are bounded only by the merit decrease. Hence the form to test: rounds &le; 1 + k* + &phi;<sub>0</sub>/&delta;<sub>min</sub>, k* = ceil(log<sub>2</sub>(&Delta;<sub>1</sub>/r*)), r* = &kappa;<sub>max</sub><sup>&minus;1/2</sup>.
- Run (`experiments/run_converge.py`): the frozen J-m-a (`vlin.slp` logic, margin `vlin.MARGIN`, analytic sensitivities, Clarabel) on the 36 PGLib-OPF v23.07 TYP, API and SAD cases from the case-file start, re-implemented step for step with extra logging only, and a **30-round budget**. Reproduction gate: the first 10 rounds' statuses must equal `results_vlin_margin_analytic_clarabel.json` on every case (the loop is deterministic and stops at the first clean round, so a 30-round run's first 10 rounds are the 10-round run). Per round: QP kind (hard or elastic), merit &phi; at the point and the trial, accepted or not, the step d in pu (P over baseMVA, V in pu) and its 2-norm, the trust radii, the remainder of every tracked row normalised by that row's margin (signed towards the row's nearer bound; thermal by 10<sup>&minus;3</sup> of rating), &rho; = the largest of them, the curvature estimate &kappa; = max<sub>i</sub> |e<sub>i</sub>|/&mu;<sub>i</sub> / ||d||<sup>2</sup>, and the smallest singular value and condition number of the Newton power-flow Jacobian at the round's base point (dense SVD). One extra AC power flow per round at the trial point; nothing it computes feeds back into the repair.
- Failure classes for each not-clean trial: **elastic** (no hard QP), **curvature** (hard QP, some broken row has remainder above its margin), **other** (hard QP, a broken row with remainder at or below its margin: a tolerance edge or a bug; each is listed).
- Predictions, to be reported held or refuted:
  - **P7a:** at least half of the not-clean trials in the first 10 rounds are curvature failures (hard QP), not elastic ones.
  - **P7b (check of Proposition 1):** on hard rounds, no trial with &rho; &le; 1 is not clean, and every broken row has normalised remainder &gt; 1, apart from at most 2 % of hard not-clean trials (tolerance edges, listed).
  - **P7c:** per case, &kappa; varies by less than a factor of 10 across its hard rounds with ||d|| &ge; 10<sup>&minus;4</sup> pu on at least 75 % of cases with three or more such rounds (curvature is a usable per-case constant).
  - **P7d (H4's bound):** on cases that finish within 30 rounds, B = 1 + k* + &phi;<sub>0</sub>/&delta;<sub>min</sub> (k* from &kappa;<sub>max</sub> and the first round's step; &delta;<sub>min</sub> the smallest merit decrease over accepted rounds, or 0 accepted rounds giving &phi;<sub>0</sub>/&delta; = 0) is within a factor of 10 of the observed rounds on at least 75 % of them. My guess: refuted, because &delta;<sub>min</sub> can be tiny.
  - **P7e (H4's refutation clause):** at least one case meets the condition (Jacobian &sigma;<sub>min</sub> &ge; 10<sup>&minus;3</sup> on every round, finite &kappa;) and does not finish in 30 rounds with its merit falling by less than 1 % over its last 10 rounds (stalled). If so, H4 as written is refuted.
  - **P7f (budget):** a 30-round budget makes at most 3 more cases fully clean than the 10-round budget (21 of 34).
- Reported as found, not predicted: the round-by-round distribution of &rho;, whether stalled cases cycle between rejected steps or creep with accepted ones, and which row families (thermal, slack, angle, V, Q) the curvature failures sit on.
