Does the 2D hanger result survive in 3D, with front and back panels and a hanger arm of finite depth?

research/hanger-3d

Does the 2D hanger result survive in 3D? With an arm as deep as the garment a 3D cloth sheet matches the 2D drape (within 0.05 cm for round-ended hangers) and the best width is again 1 cm over the half shoulder; a shallow arm lets the front and back panels fall 5–8 cm and raises a wooden hanger's error from 0.4 to 2.0 cm. Stylised.

4 tests

What it found

A 3D cloth sheet (stretch, shear and bending in both directions, penalty contact with a swept arm) reproduces the 2D hanger-fit drape when the arm is as deep as the garment: shape error within 0.05 cm for the round-ended and wooden hangers and within 0.6 cm for thin wire (which is resolution-sensitive). The width finding holds: the best arm is 1 cm wider than the half shoulder for child, M and XXL tees, in 2D and 3D. What 2D cannot show is depth: on a 3 cm half-depth arm the wooden hanger's whole-sheet error rises from 0.4 to 2.0 cm, and on a rod to 3.8 cm, as the front and back panels fall 5–8 cm below the arm. Stylised: assumed fabric constants, one 20 cm strip, coarse grid (0.5 cm by 2.2 cm), no collar, sleeve seam or friction.

A stretch-set and crease-set model on five fabric archetypes: woven shirts and linen are better hung, tees, fleece and heavy knits better folded; shoulder width, mass and stretch when lifted pick the better action for 89–91% of 600 simulated garments (always fold: 59%). The split depends mostly on the assumed crease model. Stylised, assumed fabric constants.
  • How does a laundry robot get a hanger into a garment, and what does it cost the neck?How does a laundry robot get a hanger into a garment, and what does it cost the neck? A swept-section model of a rigid, telescoping or folding hanger passing a neck or hem loop: rigid hangers need 43–61 cm of loop, a tee's neck stretches up to 33% and a buttoned shirt collar cannot take them at all (shirts go in at the hem, 10 s against 6 s); a hanger that collapses to a 10 cm half width needs 31 cm. The round end hanger-fit wants costs about 3 cm more. Stylised, assumed constants.
