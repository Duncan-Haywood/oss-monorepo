# E18: our finite semantics against the AFP Isabelle/HOL formalisation of Fitting's variant

**Question.** Do the programme's finite-model results on Fitting's variant of Gödel's argument (E17)
agree with the published machine-checked formalisation? And what does that formalisation's
choice of axioms, A4a in particular, actually buy?

**Baseline.** D. Fuenmayor and C. Benzmüller, *Types, Tableaus and Gödel's God in Isabelle/HOL*,
Archive of Formal Proofs, 2017 (https://www.isa-afp.org/entries/Types_Tableaus_and_Goedels_God.html),
theory `FittingProof.thy`, read on 2026-11-12 from the AFP's GitHub mirror
(`isabelle-prover/mirror-afp-devel`, `thys/Types_Tableaus_and_Goedels_God/FittingProof.thy`).
It transcribes Fitting's *Types, Tableaus, and Gödel's God* (Kluwer 2002), chapter 11, and
numbers each axiom after the book. We transcribed every statement it proves (17) and every
statement it reports Nitpick countersatisfiable (4) into our semantics. We also added B&F 2020's
ultrafilter theorem U1 (arXiv:1910.08955, §6.1).

**What the file settles before any search.** It contains **A4a, "axiom 11.11": a positive
property is necessarily positive**. That answers E17's open question, whether positivity is
rigid in Fitting's book. Going by the AFP's citation of the book's numbering, it is (we have not
seen the book itself). B&F 2020's Fig. 6 leaves the axiom out. The file's "de re" conclusion
`GodNecExists_v2` is (λX. □∃ᴱX)↓G: at every accessible world some existent belongs to the
*actual* extension of G. That is weaker than what E17 called de re (one individual God-like at
every world and existing at every world). The AFP does not state E17's version.

## Method

`run.py` enumerates finite models. It covers every accessibility relation (modal logic K, as the
AFP uses until its last section; S5 = equivalence relations), every domain assignment including
empty domains, and every partition of the individuals (full semantics = singletons; otherwise a
coarse algebra). At each world it tries every positive set that A1a and A1b allow. The AFP's
stages are I = A1a, A1b, A2, T2; II = I + A4a; III = II + A5. **III-noA4** is I + A5, the reading
of B&F 2020's Fig. 6. A theorem is checked at every world of every model of its stage and of
every later stage. For each Nitpick claim, the script finds the smallest countermodel by
(worlds, individuals, blocks). T1b's quantifier over intensions is decided by an exact shortcut,
stated in the code (the intension's values away from the world are unconstrained). Q4 enumerates
every intension.

Sizes are every relation for worlds × individuals of 1×1–4, 2×1–4 and 3×1–3, plus S5 only at
3×4, 4×2 and 4×3. In total that is 2,070,148 frames. They give 9,741,406 stage-I models,
2,953,552 stage-II, 1,531,510 stage-III and 2,587,476 III-noA4. The run took 28 min. The full
table, with failure counts / models for K and S5, full and coarse, is in [table.md](table.md);
`results.json` has every count and the first smallest countermodel of each statement.

**Not predicted.**
- **A4a makes T1b true in S5.** In S5, T1b (an intensional positive property is possibly
  instantiated) holds in every stage-III and III-noA4 model, though the AFP lists it as
  countersatisfiable. The AFP states it at stage I, where it fails, so there is no conflict.
  The reason it holds later: every positive extension contains the G block, which A5 makes exist
  at every world.
- **T3_deDicto, countersatisfiable at stage I, holds from stage II on** in K, in 0 of 685,057
  full models. A4a and the ultrafilter property make P the same at every accessible world, so
  the de re and de dicto readings coincide. Without A4 and with A5 it fails again in K
  (38,676 full models).
- **With A4a, in K under full semantics, E17's strong de re holds over accessible worlds** (one
  individual God-like and existing at every accessible world): 0 failures in 247,191 models. It
  still fails in coarse algebras (235,354 of 1,284,319), as E17 found in S5.

## Proofs (Lean 4, `lean/TheismFormal/AFP.lean`)

At a world w, every condition of Fitting's variant in K quantifies over w's successors. So it is
E17's S5 condition with the worlds replaced by the subtype of w's successors, and the K results
are corollaries of E17's `de_dicto`:

- `afp_de_re_K`: without A4, in K, any algebra and any domains, AFP's de re `GodNecExists_v2`
  holds at every world where the five axioms hold.
- `afp_de_dicto_K_A4`: with A4a, AFP's de dicto `GodNecExists_v1` holds in K (via `pos_back`:
  A4a and A1 give the same positive set at accessible worlds).
- `cm_dedicto_noA4`: without A4 the de dicto reading fails in K. The model has two worlds that
  see each other and not themselves, two individuals and full semantics, and individual v exists
  only at world v. The search found the same model as its smallest countermodel.

Build: `lean/build.sh` (Lean 4.15.0, core library only), no `sorry`, no warnings. All three use
only Lean's standard axioms ([lean_build.txt](lean_build.txt)).

## Reading

Our finite semantics agrees with the published Isabelle/HOL formalisation on all 21 of its
claims, in K as well as S5, and the agreement extends to coarse algebras. So E10 to E17 rest on
an encoding that reproduces the field's reference formalisation.

The comparison also corrects two things in E17's write-up:
1. Fitting's book has A4, according to the AFP's numbering. E17's "Fitting's needs A4 as well"
   is therefore a remark about B&F 2020's Fig. 6 encoding, not about Fitting.
2. E17's de re gap concerns a stronger conclusion than any the AFP proves.

What A4 buys is now exact. In S5 it is not needed for either of the AFP's conclusions. In K it
is what makes the de dicto conclusion follow, while the de re one (in the AFP's sense) needs only
A5. It is also what gives one God-like being per model under full semantics (E17,
`de_re_full`).

## Limits

- The book itself was not read. The A4 attribution comes from the AFP's comment `axiom 11.11`.
- These are finite models up to the sizes listed. The Lean theorems cover every size, but only
  for the three statements above.
- Anderson's variant (`AndersonProof.thy`) was not cross-checked here.
- The AFP proves its theorems in general HOL models. A failure in our finite models would have
  indicated an encoding bug, and none occurred. Agreement does not show the encoding is the only
  correct one.
