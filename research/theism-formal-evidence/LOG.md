# Research log: theism-formal-evidence

## 2026-11-07 · M1: catalogue, checker, ledger (predictions written before each run; outcomes below)

- **Predictions (E1).** Every catalogue argument valid in its intended logic (H1); the modal ontological argument valid in B and S5 but not in T or S4, its reverse valid in T (H2); the Catholic and Orthodox positions each a maximal consistent subset of the eight-claim church set, differing in two claims per dispute (H3).
- **Outcome (E1, `experiments/e1_arguments/results.md`).** All three held. Ten arguments are valid as stated; the free-will-defence set is satisfiable (Mackie's argument is valid but needs "no morally sufficient reason exists", which Plantinga's set shows is not a logical truth). Ontological: K, T, S4 invalid with two-world countermodels; B, S5 valid. Reverse: valid from T up. The church set has exactly two minimal inconsistent sets (Honorius: c1–c4; Florence: c5–c8) and 16 maximal consistent positions; the Catholic position drops c4 (Honorius spoke ex cathedra) and c8 (the Filioque is false), the Orthodox drops c1 (papal infallibility) and c6 (Florence was ecumenical).
- A first version of the monadic checker enumerated predicate types and did not finish on the eight-claim set in 120 s; replaced by grounding over a #constants + #quantifiers domain (exact for unnested quantifiers) and backtracking search, 0.8 s for all of E1.
- **Predictions (E2).** Unwin's 67% reproduces from 50% and his six factors; the resurrection item carries over 90% of the variance of the Christianity-vs-rest log odds (H4); the prior carries more of the theism-vs-naturalism variance than any one item (H5).
- **Outcome (E2, `experiments/e2_ledger/results.md`).** Unwin: 0.6667. H4 held: resurrection 98.5% of the variance; the Bayes factor it needs for P(Christian) > 0.5 is 7.3 at P(theism) = P(Christian | theism) = 0.5 and ranges from 0.5 to 294 over the 3 × 3 prior grid, all far below the 10^44 reported for the McGrews and far above a sceptic's 1. H5 held: prior 54%, fine-tuning 31% (a swept range), every sourced item under 6%.
- **A design error caught before reporting.** The first E2 took midpoints of every range, so the resurrection item sat at 10^22 and gave P(naturalism) ~ 10^-21: an artefact of a uniform over 44 orders of magnitude. The second error was mine too: the first church-level sweeps leaned against Catholicism (three of five ranges negative). Both fixed: the resurrection item is now reported as a threshold, not averaged, and every church range is symmetric about zero until elicited (M3).
