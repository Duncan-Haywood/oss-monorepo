## 2026-11-12 · M2 (part): E17 against the published Isabelle/HOL formalisation of Fitting's variant (E18). Prediction written here before the check ran

- **Why.** The proposal's evaluation asks that our results agree with the machine-checked literature where they overlap. Fuenmayor & Benzmüller's AFP entry *Types, Tableaus and Gödel's God in Isabelle/HOL* (2017; `FittingProof.thy`, read from the AFP mirror on GitHub this session) formalises Fitting's variant with his book's numbering, and it **includes axiom 11.11, A4a: a positive property is necessarily positive**. That answers E17's open question (is positivity rigid in Fitting's book?) from a secondary source that cites the book's axiom number; B&F 2020's Fig. 6 leaves A4 out. The AFP file also states which readings it proves (Isabelle) and which it refutes (Nitpick), and it works in K until its last section.
- **What the AFP calls de re is not what E17 called de re.** AFP's `GodNecExists_v2` is (λX. □∃ᴱX) ↓G: at every accessible world some existent belongs to the *actual extension* of G. E17's de re was stronger: one individual God-like at every world and existing at every world. So E17's "de re gap" is not a gap in any theorem the AFP states.
- **Prediction (H22), reasoned on paper before any code.** Semantics as E17 but with any accessibility relation (K), possibly empty domains, the positive set per world, and AFP's staging: I = A1a, A1b, A2, T2; II = I + A4a; III = II + A5; S5 = equivalence relations. (a) Every AFP theorem holds in every finite model of its stage's axioms, in K, under full semantics (a failure would be a bug in our encoding, since Isabelle proved them). (b) The same holds in every coarse (block) algebra: the proofs only instantiate at ↓G, ↓NE and complements, which T2 and A5 put in the algebra. (c) Every Nitpick claim has a countermodel in our search: T1b (an intensional positive property need not be possibly instantiated), T3 de dicto, the non-equivalence of □∃ᴱQ and (λX. □∃ᴱX)↓Q, and modal collapse (smallest: 2 worlds, 1 individual, as the AFP reports); T3 de dicto's smallest countermodel has 2 worlds and 2 individuals (B&F 2020's stated size), in K and in S5. (d) The stage-I axioms force seriality (AFP's lemma D) and make P at each world a principal ultrafilter of the algebra generated by one block (B&F's U1). (e) Without A4a (B&F's Fig. 6), in S5 both conclusions (`GodNecExists_v1` de dicto and `_v2` de re) still hold given A5, by E17 (a); in K the de re `_v2` still holds but the de dicto `_v1` has a countermodel (A5 at w only speaks about w's successors' domains, not about G at those successors). (f) No model of stage III has a world with an empty domain. Refuted by any AFP theorem failing in a full-semantics model (a), any failing in a block algebra (b), a Nitpick claim with no countermodel up to our sizes or a smallest countermodel of other size (c), or any part of (d) to (f) failing.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e18_afp_crosscheck/results.md`).** (a) to (e) held. Part (f) was refuted in K and held in S5. The search covered 2,070,148 frames (every relation up to 3 worlds × 3 individuals and 2 × 4; S5 at 3 × 4, 4 × 2 and 4 × 3; empty domains included), with 9.7 million stage-I models and 1.5 million stage-III models; it ran in 28 min. All 17 AFP theorems and B&F's U1 have 0 failures, in K and S5, in full and coarse algebras. All 4 Nitpick claims have countermodels at the predicted smallest sizes: modal collapse 2 × 1, T3 de dicto 2 × 2. Without A4, in K, de dicto `GodNecExists_v1` fails in 196,502 models and de re `_v2` in none; in S5 neither fails. (f): in K a world that no world sees can have an empty domain (25,038 stage-III models). Proved in Lean 4 (`lean/TheismFormal/AFP.lean`, no `sorry`, no warnings): `afp_de_re_K` and `afp_de_dicto_K_A4` (both corollaries of E17's `de_dicto`, applied to the subtype of a world's successors), and the countermodel `cm_dedicto_noA4`, which is the search's smallest one. Not predicted: with A4 and A5, T1b and T3 de dicto (countersatisfiable at stage I) hold in S5, and T3 de dicto holds in K too; with A4 under full semantics, E17's strong de re holds over accessible worlds in K. Reading: our encoding reproduces the reference formalisation on all 21 claims. E17's two write-up points are corrected: Fitting has A4, and the de re gap is about a stronger conclusion than the AFP proves. A4 is not needed for either AFP conclusion in S5; in K it is what makes the de dicto conclusion follow.

## 2026-11-12 · M2 (part): Fitting's variant over block algebras with varying domains (E17). Prediction written here before the search ran

- **Setting.** Fitting's variant as Benzmüller & Fuenmayor (2020, arXiv:1910.08955, Fig. 6) encode it: positive properties are *extensions* (sets of individuals, the same at every world), not intensions. G x iff x is in every positive extension; A1a, A1b (exactly one of Y and its complement is positive); A2 with actualist entailment (P X and every existent anywhere in X is in Y gives P Y); T2 (the extension of G at the world is positive); essence E Y x iff x is in Y and Y's existents lie in every Z containing x; NE x iff every essence of x has an existent at every world; A5 (the extension of NE is positive). S5, varying non-empty domains, individuals that exist nowhere allowed. B&F's figure lists no A4 (positivity rigid), so positivity may differ between worlds; both readings are checked. Properties are the unions of the blocks of a partition of the individuals (the analogue of E8 to E16's block algebras); full semantics is the partition into singletons.
- **Prediction (H21), proof sketched on paper before any code.** (a) At each world the positive set is "contains b" for one block b that has an existent somewhere, and every such block gives one; G at that world is b. So without A4 a model is a choice of such a block per world, with A4 one block for all worlds. (b) Unlike Anderson's NE (E15), Fitting's NE is always in the algebra: it is the union of the blocks that have an existent at every world; A5 holds iff each chosen block is one of them. So models of all axioms number k with A4 and k^|W| without, k the number of such blocks. (c) De dicto (at every world some God-like individual exists) holds in every model with A5 and fails in some without it. (d) De re (one individual God-like at every world and existing at every world) fails in some models with A4 in coarse algebras, the de re gap E13 found in Anderson's variant (a block of two individuals that exist at complementary worlds), and holds in every full-semantics model with A4. Without A4 it fails even under full semantics whenever there are two worlds and two necessary existents (a different God at each world). (e) Under full semantics there is exactly one God-like individual at each world and it exists at every world; with A4 the models number the necessary existents, as in E12. (f) In a coarse algebra a world can have several God-like individuals, all existing everywhere, so the variant does not give uniqueness ("monotheism", which B&F leave as further work). Refuted if the definition-level search over every frame up to 4 individuals and 3 worlds (every partition, every non-empty domain assignment, every positive set at each world) gives any count different from (a) or (b), a model with A5 where de dicto fails, or no de re failure with A4 in a coarse algebra.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e17_fitting/results.md`).** H21 held in all six parts. The definition-level search (every partition up to 5 individuals, 1 to 3 worlds, 4 worlds at 3 individuals, every positive set A1 allows at each world; 119,890 frames, about 10 s) equals the closed form on every frame. With A5, de dicto never fails with or without A4; without A5 it fails in 149,846 A4 models. With A4, de re fails in 33,102 models, none under full semantics; without A4 it fails in 4,858 full-semantics models. Fitting's NE is a union of blocks in every frame. Proved in Lean 4 for every algebra and domains (`lean/TheismFormal/Fitting.lean`, no `sorry`, no warnings): `model_iff`, `model_a5_iff`, `ne_iff`, `ne_in_algebra` (no axioms), `de_dicto`, `god_unique_full`, `de_re_full`, and the countermodels `cm_de_re` and `cm_no_a4`. Not predicted: Fitting's NE contains individuals that do not exist at every world in 82,074 frames (anyone sharing a block with an existent at each world), so "necessary existent" in this variant is a property of blocks, not of beings, the same weakness E13 found in Anderson's NE. Reading: the de re gap is common to Anderson's and Fitting's repairs; both need a property space that separates individuals, and Fitting's needs A4 as well. The search was extended past the predicted sizes (5 individuals) because it ran in seconds.
- **Not done.** Hájek's variants; a check of Fitting's book for whether positivity is rigid there; the literature check for the de re gap; Swinburne's inputs.

## 2026-11-12 · M2 (part): E14's conjecture "A5₀ implies A5'" is false; when it holds (E15). Prediction written here before the census ran

- **How it started.** E14 left open why every A5₀ model it found also satisfied A5'. Working on a proof, the argument broke at one step: A5' needs Anderson's NE (the individuals y with hE(y) &cap; Ex meeting every world) to be a union of blocks, and nothing in A1, A2w, A3', A5₀ forces that. The paper construction: worlds {0, 1}; g exists at both, a only at 0, c and b only at 1; blocks {g's row}, k = {(0,a), (1,c), (0,b)}, k3 = {(1,b), (1,a), (0,c)}. Then a and c are Anderson-necessary (k meets both worlds through existents), b is not, yet (0,b) shares block k with (0,a), so NE is not in the algebra. Checked with E14's own `search` on that one frame before this entry: one model, A5₀ true, A5' false. It has 8 cells, outside E14's search (at most 6).
- **Prediction (H20), before the census.** (a) In a model of A1, A2w, A3', A5₀, A5' holds exactly when NE is a union of blocks (positivity then follows from A1, since NE₀ &sube; NE). (b) Under full property semantics A5₀ always implies A5'. (c) No counterexample has at most 7 cells, so the smallest has 8: one world gives NE = NE₀, one individual likewise, 7 cells is 1x7 or 7x1, and E14 covered the rest up to 6 except the full-semantics partition, settled by (b). (d) Two individuals are never enough at any number of worlds (g is necessary; the other is either in NE, making NE every cell, or not, making NE = NE₀). So 4 worlds x 2 individuals has no counterexample, 2 x 4 has some, and 3 x 3 has some (paper example: a at worlds 0, 1, b at 2, blocks {g}, {(0,a), (0,b)}, {(1,a), (2,b)}, {(2,a), (1,b)}). Refuted by any 4x2 counterexample, none at 2x4 or 3x3, or a census that disagrees with E14 at 6 cells.
- **Method.** Every model's positive set is {f : NecA f x} for any God-like x (the definition of G\*), and with A5₀ every God-like x exists at every world (E14, Lean), so the models of a frame are enumerated exactly by H = closure of a necessary existent's row; each candidate is checked against the axioms' definitions. Lean: (a), (b) and the 8-cell countermodel.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e15_a5z_not_a5/results.md`).** H20 held in all four parts. Census (exact, about 30 s): 4x2, 0 of 7,539 A5₀ models fail A5'; 2x4, 4,608 of 57,413; 3x3, 11,520 of 160,645. The enumeration agrees with E14's definition-level search frame by frame on all 17,440 of E14's frames. Proved in Lean 4 (`lean/TheismFormal/Closure.lean`, no `sorry`, no warnings): `nea_iff` (E13 (b), until now search-only), `a5a_iff_closed`, `full_a5z_imp_a5a` (all three use no axioms) and the countermodel `cm8` (`propext` only). Not predicted: the 2-block counterexamples at 2x4 use an individual that exists at no world; with every individual existing somewhere the minimum is 3 blocks (2,592 of the 4,608). Every frame with a counterexample has exactly one A5₀ model. Reading: A5₀ and A5' are independent premises in coarse algebras; A5₀ still gives de re, but it is a different axiom from Anderson's, not a strengthening. Correction added to E14's results and to the README.
- **Not done.** Lean proofs of E13 (a) and E14 (c); Hájek's and Fitting's variants; the literature check for A5₀ and the de re gap; Swinburne's inputs.

## 2026-11-11 · M2 (part): does plain necessary existence close the de re gap? (E14). Prediction written here before the search ran

- **Setting.** As E13 (S5, varying domains, rigid positive set, block algebras, Anderson's own A2, actualist entailment and actualist "x has f necessarily"), but A5' is replaced by **A5₀**: the property "exists at every world" (NE₀, the union of the full rows of the individuals that exist at every world) is positive. E13 traced the de re gap to Anderson's NE, which a coarse algebra lets someone else's cells satisfy; NE₀ cannot be met that way.

## 2026-11-10 · M2 (part): a theorem for every finite property algebra (E10). Prediction and proof sketch written before the check

- **Prediction (H15), from a pen-and-paper proof written before any code ran.** In the E7 to E9 semantics (S5, constant domain, positive set rigid, properties a finite Boolean algebra of world-by-individual cells, which is always the set of unions of the blocks of a partition), Anderson's A1, A2, A3', A5' have a model exactly when some block B is a union of whole individuals (every world's cell of each of them, and nothing else); each such block gives exactly one model (positive = "contains B"), so the number of models of an algebra equals its number of such blocks. Corollaries: A5' never binds; G* is the individuals in B, and they have no contingent property; and no property of the form "p holds" with p true at some worlds and not others is in the algebra, so every proposition the algebra can express is non-contingent. Refuted if the exhaustive search of E8/E9 gives a per-algebra model count that differs from the block count for any partition it covers (all partitions up to 5 cells; 6 cells up to 5 blocks), or if a model has an empty G*.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e10_theorem/results.md`).** H15 held. The search's model count equals the theorem's on all 563 partitions checked (every partition of up to 5 cells at 9 shapes, and 202 of 203 partitions each at 3x2 and 2x3; the remaining 6-block one is the full-powerset case, settled by the proof: no model). No model had an empty G*, and no model's algebra contained a contingent proposition. The proof covers every finite model, so the E7 and E8 one-individual nulls now hold at all sizes, not only up to 5 cells.
