## 2026-11-11 · M2 (part): Lean compiled; E10 proved in Lean; a correction, and Anderson's own A2 (E11). Prediction written here before the E11 search ran

- **Lean compiled for the first time.** The v4.15.0 release tarball downloads through the proxy (only the elan installer script had been refused before); `lean/build.sh` fetches it and builds. Modal.lean and Arguments.lean, written in M1 and never compiled, built unchanged.
- **E10's theorem in Lean** (`lean/TheismFormal/Anderson.lean`): forward and converse directions, uniqueness, corollaries (a), (b), (d) and step 4 (A5' never binds), for any block algebra (finite or not). No `sorry`; the classical results use only Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).
- **Correction found while checking related work.** Benzmüller & Fuenmayor (2020, arXiv:1910.08955) note that Anderson drops half of Scott's A2: he keeps "if f is positive its complement is not" and drops "if f is not positive its complement is". E7 to E10 kept both halves. So their results are about Anderson's definitions of G\* and essence *with Scott's A2*, a hybrid, and E7's reading "Anderson's repair collapses under full property semantics" does not apply to Anderson's own system; B&F's Nitpick finds a two-world, one-entity countermodel to collapse there.
- **Prediction (H16), proof sketched on paper before any code.** With Anderson's own A2 (not both f and f' positive) and A1, A3', A5' as before: for an individual y let hull(y) be the union of the blocks meeting its row. A model exists for a non-empty union of blocks H exactly when H is the union of the rows of the individuals y with hull(y) = H; each such H gives exactly one model, P = {f : H ⊆ f}, and every model is one of these. Corollaries: with full property semantics each individual gives exactly one model at every number of worlds (so collapse is avoided, matching B&F); with one individual every algebra has exactly one model; the God-like individual may have contingent properties, and they are simply not positive. Refuted if an exhaustive search over every up-closed positive set (A1) of every partition up to 5 cells gives a model count different from the number of such H for any partition.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e11_anderson_a2/results.md`).** H16 held: search count equals the predicted count on all 573 partitions checked (every partition up to 5 cells; 6-cell partitions with up to 5 blocks). Under full property semantics there is exactly one model per individual at 2 to 5 worlds; in 51 of 52 algebras at 5 worlds x 1 individual the God-like individual has a contingent property. The theorem and the full-semantics corollary are proved in Lean (`weak_model_form`, `weak_model_of`, `weak_full_semantics_model`; the last two use no axioms at all). Reading: Anderson's repair does avoid collapse, and the reason is precisely the half of A2 he drops, not the redefined God-likeness or essence on their own. Corrections added to E7's and E10's results.

## 2026-11-10 · M2 (part): a theorem for every finite property algebra (E10). Prediction and proof sketch written before the check

- **Prediction (H15), from a pen-and-paper proof written before any code ran.** In the E7 to E9 semantics (S5, constant domain, positive set rigid, properties a finite Boolean algebra of world-by-individual cells, which is always the set of unions of the blocks of a partition), Anderson's A1, A2, A3', A5' have a model exactly when some block B is a union of whole individuals (every world's cell of each of them, and nothing else); each such block gives exactly one model (positive = "contains B"), so the number of models of an algebra equals its number of such blocks. Corollaries: A5' never binds; G* is the individuals in B, and they have no contingent property; and no property of the form "p holds" with p true at some worlds and not others is in the algebra, so every proposition the algebra can express is non-contingent. Refuted if the exhaustive search of E8/E9 gives a per-algebra model count that differs from the block count for any partition it covers (all partitions up to 5 cells; 6 cells up to 5 blocks), or if a model has an empty G*.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e10_theorem/results.md`).** H15 held. The search's model count equals the theorem's on all 563 partitions checked (every partition of up to 5 cells at 9 shapes, and 202 of 203 partitions each at 3x2 and 2x3; the remaining 6-block one is the full-powerset case, settled by the proof: no model). No model had an empty G*, and no model's algebra contained a contingent proposition. The proof covers every finite model, so the E7 and E8 one-individual nulls now hold at all sizes, not only up to 5 cells. Reading: in finite models Anderson's repair avoids modal collapse only for propositions its property space cannot express. Not yet in Lean; literature check for a prior statement of this characterisation still open.

## 2026-11-07 · M2 (part): leave-one-out (E3) and Gödel–Scott modal collapse (E4) (predictions before running)

- **Predictions (E3).** Dropping any one sourced theism-level item moves the midpoint P(naturalism) by under 0.1 absolute (H6); dropping fine-tuning (a swept range, not a published figure) moves it most, and the sign of "is P(N) > 0.5 at the midpoints" does not change under any single drop except fine-tuning (H7). Refuted if any sourced item alone flips the verdict.
- **Predictions (E4), sharpened before the run.** Scott's axioms are A1 (positive properties are closed under necessary implication), A2 (exactly one of a property and its complement is positive), A3 (God-likeness G is positive), A4 (positivity is necessary, built in here by making the positive set rigid across worlds), A5 (necessary existence NE is positive), with G, ess and NE defined as in Scott/Sobel. Exhaustive search over S5 models with full property semantics (every function from worlds to subsets of the domain is a property; world x domain size at most 5): (H8) models exist, with every positive property possibly instantiated; (H9) every model has exactly one world, because with full property semantics the collapse p -> []p must hold for the constant property of every proposition, and a proposition true at one world and not another exists whenever there are two worlds. Refuted by a model of A1-A5 with two or more worlds.

- **Outcome (E3, `experiments/e3_leave_one_out/results.md`).** H6 and H7 both refuted. At the midpoints P(N) = 0.76; dropping natural evil gives 0.09, moral evil 0.42 (a *published* item) and hiddenness exactly 0.50 (boundary); dropping fine-tuning gives 0.99. So three items each decide the midpoint verdict, and the variance-share ranking (prior, fine-tuning first) does not predict this: variance measures spread over the ranges, leave-one-out measures what the central estimate leans on. Both views go in the paper. Church-level items do not touch the naturalism contrast (change 0.000), as they should.
- **Outcome (E4, `experiments/e4_godel_scott/results.md`).** H8 and H9 held within the sizes searched: one-world models number one per individual (1, 2, 3 for domains of 1, 2, 3, the principal ultrafilters, an analytic check), and none exist for 2, 3, 4 or 5 worlds (and 2 worlds with 2 individuals). Limits: exhaustive only up to 5 (world, individual) cells; this is a search, not a proof (Sobel's derivation is the proof; the Lean version is still to write).

## 2026-11-09 · M2 (part): Anderson's emendation of Goedel's argument (E7) (prediction before running)

- **Prediction (H12).** Anderson's (1990) emendation replaces Scott's God-likeness with G\*(x) iff for every property f, f is positive exactly when x has f necessarily, and replaces "ess" by the biconditional form (box psi(x) iff box forall y (f(y) -> psi(y))). Same search as E4 (S5, constant domain, full property semantics, positive set rigid, A2 by construction). I predict models with two or more worlds exist (the collapse is avoided, as Anderson claims), with every positive property possibly instantiated. Refuted if no multi-world model exists at any size searched (up to 5 cells). A null at this size is weak evidence, as in E4.
- **Outcome (E7, `experiments/e7_anderson/results.md`).** H12 refuted within the sizes searched. One-world models number one per individual (1, 2, 3 for domains of 1, 2, 3), as in E4, and there is none for 2, 3, 4 or 5 worlds (or 2 worlds with 2 individuals). So under full property semantics Anderson's repair collapses at these sizes just as Scott's does. This does not contradict Anderson: his consistency result uses a restricted (non-full) property space, which this search does not model, and the search is exhaustive only to 5 cells, not a proof. What it does say is that the repair needs a restricted property domain, and the choice of that domain is where a dispute would sit. ## 2026-11-10 · M2 (part): Anderson's repair over restricted property algebras (E8) (prediction before running)

- **Prediction (H13).** Restricting properties to a Boolean algebra (unions of blocks of a partition of the cells) lets some algebra with contingent properties carry a multi-world model of Anderson's axioms; refuted if none does at any size up to 5 cells.
- **Outcome (`experiments/e8_restricted/results.md`).** H13 held, narrowly. Every partition of the cells was tried. With one individual (2 to 5 worlds) no algebra that has a contingent property gives a model, so the collapse persists there. With 2 worlds and 2 individuals, 2 of 13 contingent algebras give a model (e.g. blocks {(w0,x0),(w1,x0)}, {(w0,x1)}, {(w1,x1)}: x0 gets only rigid properties, x1 is distinguished at each world). Algebras where every property is rigid were skipped, since they are one-world models in disguise. Reading: the repair survives only when the property space is cut so that the God-like individual has no contingent properties while others do; that choice is exactly what a critic can contest. Limits: ≤5 cells, constant domain, S5, partition algebras only; not a proof, and the examples are toy sizes. Next: the Lean version, and Swinburne's inputs.
