Grid h &isin; {1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 20, 30, 50}. Selection: step 1's rule on 2018 only (most 2018 detections at &le; 2 false alarms; ties to fewer false alarms, then larger h). 2019 scored once.

## 2026-11-12 · Step 1 plan, written before any detector was run
- **Data.** BattLeDIM (Zenodo record 4017659, CC-BY-4.0), fetched through the session proxy for the first time; `infra-waterloss` had to fall back to Net3 because it could not. Ground truth is the onset of non-zero flow in `<year>_Leakages.csv` (it matches the onsets in Melnikov 2025's table; the configuration file's start times differ for incipient leaks). 2019 has 23 leaks: 19 that start in 2019 and 4 carried over from 2018 (p257, p427, p654, p810).
- **Protocol.** 2018 is the tuning year, 2019 the test year, scored once with the settings 2018 picked. 2018 labels are used for tuning; BattLeDIM's competitors had only 2018's repair times, so this is more information than they had, and it is disclosed wherever a comparison is made.
- **Detector (D1).** Hourly means. For each pressure sensor and each day, a least-squares model of pressure on hour-of-week, tank level and (variant F) the three inlet flows, fitted on a trailing window that ends `g` days before the day scored; residuals standardised by the window's residual sd; a one-sided CUSUM on the negative residuals per sensor; an alarm resets all CUSUMs and holds off for `g` days. The trailing refit absorbs leaks already running, so each alarm is a claim that a *new* leak started.
- **Score (detection only).** An alarm is a true positive if it falls while an unmatched leak is running and within 30 days of its onset (the latest such onset wins); every other alarm is a false positive. Delay is alarm minus onset. The distance from the alarming sensor to the leak pipe is reported as a first localisation figure (BattLeDIM counted a location within 300 m). Not the competition's score, which needed a reported pipe.
- **Hypothesis H1-step1.** Tuned on 2018 to at most 2 false alarms, D1 detects at least 9 of the 19 leaks that start in 2019 with at most 4 false alarms in 2019. *Refuted if* it detects 8 or fewer, or needs 5 or more false alarms. The published best entries reach 15 to 17 of 23 with 1 to 4 false alarms *with localisation* (Melnikov 2025, Table 2), so this is a floor, not a claim to match them.
- **Expected.** Abrupt leaks above ~10 m³/h found within a day; small incipient ones (p123, p455, p762, p193) missed, as every published entry mostly misses them.
