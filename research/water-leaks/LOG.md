- **Design (injection into the real residual).** Eight sets (seeds 1 to 8) of eight leaks, 64 in all. Each leak resamples one of the 33 configured leaks with replacement and keeps its type, diameter d, rise R (start to peak) and duration (start to end; a leak that runs to the end of 2019 runs to the end of the run). Its start is drawn uniformly (5-minute grid) so that it falls on or after the first scored hour of steps 21 to 24 and its peak at least one day before the end of 2019. Its node is drawn uniformly among the junctions more than 300 m along the pipes from every real leak pipe's midpoint and from the set's other injected nodes, so no hit can come from a real leak's atom. The leak is an EPANET emitter at that node with coefficient C(t) = 0.75 &pi; d&sup2;/4 &radic;(2g) &times; 3600 (m&sup3;/h per m<sup>0.5</sup>, an orifice with discharge coefficient 0.75; not checked against BattLeDIM's generator, so the run reports the injected peak flows next to the 33 published ones) times the shape: 1 from start to end for an abrupt leak, ((t &minus; start)/R)&sup2; up to the peak and 1 after it for an incipient one. Each set is one continuous two-year run of `L-TOWN_Real.inp` (as step 14's), the coefficients updated every 5 minutes.
