# sensing-burst: small-sensor burst vs one frame of a larger sensor (synthetic scenes, Poisson-Gaussian noise)
48x48 px scenes, 16 scenes per cell; PSNR is display-referred (gamma 1/2.2, scene peak = 1), border cropped.
A = area ratio (large/small pixel); P = electrons per unit radiance in ONE large-sensor frame; read = read noise (e-).
Small sensor has P/A electrons per unit radiance per frame. Equal exposure time per frame (the burst takes N times longer).
