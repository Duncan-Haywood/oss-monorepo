== 7. Exact speed limit: the pole sum is -1/tau, so no (kp, kd) decays faster than 1/(3 tau) ==
tau = 0.05: bound 1/(3 tau) = 6.66667; best decay rate over 200000 log-uniform (kp, kd) draws = 6.66601
triple-pole gains wn = 1/(sqrt(27) tau) = 3.8490, zeta = sqrt(3)/2: decay rate 6.666636, poles -6.6666-5.2e-05j, -6.6667-8.8e-316j, -6.6666+5.3e-05j
twin rate zeta wn at those gains = 3.333; at zeta = 0.7 the twin's promised rate zeta wn exceeds the cap 1/(3 tau) once wn > 9.524
