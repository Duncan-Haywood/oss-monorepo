- **Method, fixed now (`sync.rate_lag_search`, `experiments/tracker/ratelag.py`).** Device 0's first 20 s (envelope-normalised) is cut into sub-windows (1 s; 2 s as a secondary); each is correlated, partially whitened (gamma 0.25), against device 1's first 20 s on a common lag axis (+-15 s); for each rate on -400..400 ppm in 5 ppm steps the curves are summed after shifting sub-window k by rate x (t<sub>k</sub> &minus; 10 s); the (lag, rate) with the largest sum gives the delay at 10 s and the rate. With gamma 0 and rate 0 the sum is the full-window correlation (up to each sub-window's own mean removal), so the only new freedom is the rate. Ablation `inc1`: 1 s curves summed at rate 0 only. - **Model** (`src/capture_sync/threshold.py`). Correlator weight W = (Gxx Gyy)^(-gamma/2) from smoothed spectra; mean output at the true lag mu = max over +-1 ms of the integral of W Re(Gxy e^{j2 pi f tau}); spreads sigma1^2 = (1/2L) int W^2 Gxx Gyy and sigma0^2 = (1/2L) int W^2 (Gxx Gyy + Re Gxy^2) (Knapp and Carter 1976); largest wrong-lag output over 2.93 s of lag from M = 2 B_eff T independent cells (Ianniello) or from Rice's upcrossing rate. Spectra: Welch, 512-sample non-overlapping Hann segments, of the two windows aligned at the true lag. Estimators: the smooth-weight GCC the model describes (`gcc_fixed`) and step 1's GCC, which divides each bin by its own raw |X Y*|^gamma.

Windows 0.5/1/2/4 s; gamma 1, 0.5, 0.25, 0; 250 trials per cell; prior +-1 s, search +-1.5 s, failure more than 33.3 ms off, as step 1. A cell is scored only if it has at least 10 observed failures; "agrees" means predicted/observed in [0.5, 2].
