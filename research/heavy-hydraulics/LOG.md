## 2026-10-28 · Milestone 2a: do the rankings survive a random cycle bounded by a sourced machine envelope? (planned before running)
- Why not the measured cycle yet: the two sources that would give one (Juza and Hermanek, *Research in Agricultural Engineering* 69(1):18-27, 2023, doi:10.17221/77/2021-RAE; Kubota KX018-4 dealer sheet) are on hosts this session cannot resolve (`rae.agriculturejournals.cz`, `kubota.com.au`), so the cycle itself stays unsourced. What a search result summary did give, not opened: KX018-4 pump 17.3 x 2 + 10.4 + 6.2 L/min, a 180 bar figure from a parts site (unverified as the relief setting).
- Plan: replace the one hand-written cycle with 4,000 random cycles per seed x 5 seeds: 4 phases, 1 to 3 simultaneous actuators, each actuator's flow drawn up to its section capacity (17.3, 17.3, 10.4 L/min), total flow under the 45 L/min pump, pressures uniform on 20 to 180 bar, phase times 2 to 6 s. Margins at the sourced-or-swept values of milestone 1 (dp_ls 14 to 30 bar, dp_spool 5 to 20, dp_m 3 to 10, eta 0.75 to 0.92) drawn uniformly. This tests whether the milestone 1 medians are an artefact of one cycle. It is still not a measured cycle.
- **Prediction (before running):** ordering open-centre >= load-sensing >= metered holds in 100% of cycles (structural). Median LS/OC will fall below milestone 1's 0.47 because open-centre runs at 45 L/min against a mean demand well under that. Metered/LS median between 0.75 and 0.9. H3 check per case: at equal actuator pressures, metered/LS stays under 0.85 in over 95% of cycles.

### Outcome (`experiments/envelope.py`, seeded, a few seconds; `experiments/envelope_results.txt`)
- 20,000 random cycles (5 seeds x 4,000): the ordering open-centre >= load-sensing >= metered held in 100% (structural, as predicted).
- Load-sensing/open-centre median 0.442 (5 to 95%: 0.26 to 0.64), per-seed medians 0.440 to 0.444: below milestone 1's 0.47, as predicted; the headline is not an artefact of the one hand-written cycle.
- Metered/load-sensing median 0.717 with random per-actuator pressures (5 to 95%: 0.57 to 0.85), outside my predicted 0.75 to 0.9: the wider 20 to 180 bar spread of random pressures gives metering more to remove than the reference cycle. With equal pressure inside each phase the median is 0.873 (predicted range held).
- **My H3 prediction was framed backwards.** I wrote "metered/LS under 0.85 in over 95% of equal-pressure cycles", which is the opposite of H3 (saving under 15%, ratio above 0.85). The result: ratio above 0.85 in 67.7% of equal-pressure cycles, so H3's "under 15% at zero spread" holds per case in about two thirds, and fails in about one third (ratio up to 0.77 at the 5th percentile). H3 as a per-case claim is refuted; as a median claim it held (0.873). The saving at zero spread comes from the margin difference alone (dp_ls 14 to 30 against dp_m 3 to 10 bar) and is largest in low-pressure phases where a fixed margin is a large fraction.
- Limits: cycles are random, not measured; the 45 L/min pump, 17.3/17.3/10.4 section flows and 180 bar are from a search-result summary of a Kubota KX018-4 listing and a parts site, neither opened; margins are the milestone 1 ranges. Randomly drawn cycles may be unlike real digging, which is exactly why a measured cycle remains the H4 test.
- Next: obtain a measured or physics-derived cycle (needs `rae.agriculturejournals.cz` and a manufacturer sheet host allowed, or a dataset via `add_repo`); until then milestone 2 is not done.

## 2026-10-28 · Milestone 1: do the three circuits order the way theory says, and by how much? (planned before running)
- Hypotheses and plan: see PROPOSAL H1 to H3. Grid: flow demand 3 x pressure spread 4 x pump flow 3 x dp_ls 3 x dp_spool 3 x dp_m 3 x pump efficiency 3 = 2,916 cases on one illustrative four-phase cycle (pressures and flows written from memory, not sourced).
- **Prediction (before running):** open-centre > load-sensing > metered in 100% of cases (this follows from the model's structure, so it is a check of the code, not a finding); load-sensing/open-centre falls as demand falls; metered/load-sensing falls as spread rises; at zero spread metered/LS is above 0.85.

### Outcome (`experiments/run.py`, under 5 s; `experiments/results.txt`; 6 tests pass)
- All three orderings held in 2,916 of 2,916 cases. Because each rung only removes a loss term from the one before it, this is a structural fact, not evidence about real machines.
- Median load-sensing/open-centre shaft energy 0.47 (0.22 to 0.81); by demand 0.31, 0.47, 0.63 for 0.5, 0.75 and 1.0 of the cycle flow: H2's direction held.
- Median metered/load-sensing 0.80 (0.68 to 0.97); by spread 0.88, 0.82, 0.78, 0.74 for 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5. At zero spread the median is 0.88 (prediction above 0.85 held), still a 12% saving from the smaller margin alone; H3's "under 15% at zero spread" held on medians, but no per-case check was made.
- Limits: the cycle is illustrative; no dynamics, no line losses, no leakage, no energy recovery; open-centre modelled as full pump flow at the highest load pressure, which is the pessimistic end (real open-centre bleed-off spools do better at partial lever); efficiency is one constant. These numbers say nothing yet about a real machine; H4 is the test.
