# sensing-ghostedge: a layered sub-pixel coverage model for the edge ring of a moving subject (synthetic, Poisson-Gaussian noise)
48x48 px scenes, 12 scenes per cell, still camera, one disc (radius 4 px, centre (24, 8) in frame 0) moving along x at v px per frame.
A = 4, read = 3 e-, P = 300 e- per unit radiance in one large frame (small frame: 75). Truth = noise-free frame 0. Display PSNR, border cropped.
ring = pixels within 1 px outside to 2 px inside the frame-0 footprint edge; disc = footprint eroded by 2 px; static = never within 2 px of the subject.
layered-fit: disc centre and radius fitted to the burst; layered-oracle: true geometry. Both use the true speed v. Layered estimates replace aligned+rej within 3 px of the frame-0 disc.
