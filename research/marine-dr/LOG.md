# Research log: marine-dr

## 2026-11-02 · Milestone 1: dead-reckoning drift per kilometre and the surfacing interval (hypotheses written before the sweep)
- Inputs: DVL bottom-track velocity accuracy +-0.4% +-0.2 cm/s (WHN 300) and +-0.2% +-0.1 cm/s (WHN 600/1200); single-ping sd 0.3 cm/s at 1 m/s (Teledyne Workhorse Navigator datasheet, via the BODC copy https://www.bodc.ac.uk/data/documents/nodb/pdf/rdi_workhorse_nav_ds_lr.pdf; read as search-result excerpt, PDF not opened here). Treated as a 1-sigma scale error and swept 0.06% to 0.4%. Heading bias 0.05 to 1 degree and gyro random walk are NOT sourced: swept. Surface GNSS fix sd 3 m: assumed.
- H1: simulated end error on a straight track equals L*sqrt(s^2 + (2 sin(b/2))^2) within 1% (tested).
- H2: at 1 deg heading bias the heading term dominates the 0.2% DVL scale error by more than 8x (analytic: 1.745% vs 0.2%); the DVL spec alone is not what sets drift unless the heading reference is better than about 0.11 degree.
- H3: holding a 10 m bound with the WHN 600 scale (0.2%) and 0.1 degree bias needs a surfacing fix every 2.2 km or sooner (analytic: 10/hypot(0.002, 0.001745)).
- Outcome (`experiments/results.txt`, `PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/run.py`, seconds). H1 held (tests, within 1%). H2 held: 1 deg bias gives 1.75% vs 0.2% DVL scale, 8.7x; break-even bias is 0.115 degree. H3 was wrong because I mis-did the arithmetic when writing it: hypot(0.002, 0.001745) = 0.00265 gives 3.8 km (table: 3.77 km), not 2.2 km. Logged as my error, not a model finding.
- Monte Carlo, 20 km, 200 runs. First run used a gyro random walk of 5e-4 rad/sqrt(s) (about 4 degrees over 20 km), which is not a credible heading reference and swamped everything (no-fix median max error 465 m); discarded and rerun at 5e-5 rad/sqrt(s) (about 0.17 deg/sqrt(h); unsourced, tactical-grade order). Rerun: no fix median max error 64 m (95th 178 m); fixing every 2 km 15 m (28 m); every 1 km 11 m (18 m); every 0.5 km 9.6 m, which is the 3 m fix noise floor (max over many fixes), so fixing more often than about 1 km buys nothing at this fix quality.
- Limits: straight track only (no turns, so heading bias is a pure cross-track chord); constant bias; no current-induced water-track error, no DVL dropout, no USBL; the 3 m fix and gyro noise are assumed; DVL spec read from a search excerpt, datasheet not opened. Surfacing energy and time cost not modelled, so "how often to surface" is the error side only.
