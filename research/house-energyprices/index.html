Does the cost-optimal home envelope change when invented fuel prices are replaced by EIA state prices?

research/house-energyprices

Does the cost-optimal home envelope change when invented fuel prices are replaced by EIA state prices? Optimum unchanged in 10 of 12 cases; 30-year saving moves by −$496 to +$549; advanced 2x6 walls still win and R-49 attics and U 0.27 windows still do not pay. Only fuel prices are sourced.

5 tests

What it found

Re-runs house-energy's exhaustive 640-package search in six climates with EIA state residential prices (2025 gas, Jan–Jul 2026 electricity) instead of invented ones. The optimum is unchanged in 10 of 12 cases; the 30-year saving over the baseline moves by −$496 to +$549; advanced 2x6 walls still win, and R-49 attics (27–40 y) and U 0.27 windows (74–107 y) still do not pay. Only fuel prices are sourced; component costs are still invented. Stylised.
