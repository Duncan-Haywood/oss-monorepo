(3) An affine map from SPMe to DFN fitted on the verified designs is worse (gaps up to 14.6% on Marquis2019@2x), because the verified designs are the salt-depleted ones, and the line does not extrapolate to the sound ones.

- Reading: whether SPMe can be trusted is a property of the operating point as much as of the cell. Doubling the current turns a cell where SPMe is mostly right into one where it fails on four designs in five. The screen half of the rule kept full recall at every condition. The decision to skip SPMe is cheap to get wrong (at most 13% here), so a designer can run the rule by default. The open problem is the answer: an SPMe bias in either direction breaks a mixed-fidelity comparison, and the fixes tried so far are either expensive or worse. The next step should pre-register a stopping rule that keeps verifying while unverified designs' SPMe scores are within the observed SPMe-DFN gap of the verified best.

## 2026-11-12 · Milestone 5d outcome: SPMe's early stops are the solver's (tight tolerances finish all 6, each within 0.3% of DFN); its outright failures are SPMe's (salt concentration below zero in 59 of 61, no setting rescues a usable score)
- Bench: 455 SPMe runs (67 census designs and 24 controls x 5 variants) plus 67 one-cycle diagnostics. V0 reproduces every committed cycle count (91 of 91). None killed. A first report keyed designs by (set, index) and merged OKane2022 index 1 of the `fresh` bench (a failure) with index 1 of the `cycles` bench (a control); the rows now carry their bench and the report keys on it, no run repeated.
- H38 supported: V1 (IDAKLU rtol 1e-6, atol 1e-8) and V3 (Casadi "safe", same tolerances) take all 6 Mohtat2020 stops to 100 cycles; V4 (mesh doubled) takes 4; V2 (more Newton iterations and convergence failures allowed) takes none and stops each at the same cycle as V0. So it is the error-test tolerance, not the nonlinear solve, that ends these runs.
- H39 supported: the 6 rescued designs score within 0.3% of DFN@100 (gaps -0.000 to +0.003); none is mis-scored. So on Mohtat2020 every mis-score came from the solver setting: the other 58 designs were already within 2.3% per cycle in 5c (only 8 of them were re-run here, as controls).
- H40 supported: no variant gives a usable score to any of the 61 outright failures. V3 returns 100 cycles on 4 NCA_Kim2011 designs and V1 on one of those, but three score 0 (no energy after cycle 1) and one 30.6 against DFN's 70.4 Wh/kg; all four stay mis-scored. The diagnostic puts SPMe's first-discharge salt concentration below zero on 59 of 61 (median -0.08 of initial, low -0.15), and on 57 the charge step comes back empty: the state the discharge ends in has left the reduced model's domain, so no feasible step follows and PyBaMM discards the finished cycle.
- H41 supported: all 6 stops keep positive salt in the first discharge (0.65 to 0.89 of initial).
- Controls: V1 and V3 move completed scores by at most 2.2%, V4 by up to 8.2% (one NCA_Kim2011 design at r_min -0.06), V2 not at all; no control breaks. Extra, after the verdicts: on Mohtat2020's controls tight tolerances halve SPMe's median distance to DFN@100 (1.01% to 0.50%); on NCA_Kim2011 (1.03 to 1.08%) and OKane2022 (5.46 to 5.43%) they change nothing. DFN was not re-solved at tight tolerance.
- Two failures do not fit: OKane2022 `cycles` design 16 and NCA_Kim2011 design 2 keep positive salt (0.05, 0.16) and finish their first charge, then fail later under every variant. Not diagnosed further.
- Reading: 5c's two kinds of mis-score are two different things. An SPMe run that *stops early* with healthy salt is PyBaMM's default tolerance (rtol 1e-4) losing the step; re-solve it once at rtol 1e-6 (about 2.4x the run time, 3.8 against 1.6 s) and it scores like DFN. A run that *returns nothing* has driven its linearised salt concentration negative in the first discharge; that is SPMe outside its validity, no solver setting helps, and it goes to DFN with the salt-depleted ones. So the practical two-part rule becomes: re-solve stops tightly; send failures and low-r_min designs to DFN bottom-up. 5c's refutations on Mohtat2020 (H33, H34, H37) stand as registered, but their cause was the solver setting, not the flag.

## 2026-11-12 · Milestone 3 (theory, part 2b): can SPMe's own salt-depletion output say *where* its error falls, well enough to plan the DFN budget and to correct the screen? (plan, written before the run; H21 to H24 are this entry's)

- Mechanism (to write out in `THEORY.md`, part 2b; a scaling argument, not a theorem): in PyBaMM 26.10's SPMe the concentration overpotential is chi RT/F times the difference of the x-averages of log c_e over the two electrodes (`electrolyte_conductivity.Composite`, log clipped at a small tolerance), with one reaction rate for the whole electrode. ## 2026-11-11 · Milestone 3 (theory, part 1): what kind of screen error costs DFN calls, and when does whole-pool screening lose? (plan, written before the run; H11 to H14 are this entry's)

Target: within 1% of the pool's best DFN@100. Primary metric T1: charged seconds until the best DFN@100 seen is within 1%. Every method continues until the whole pool is DFN@100-verified, so T1 is finite.
- Methods: (a) random order at DFN@100; (b) GP-EI at DFN@100 (5 random starts, isotropic RBF on the 4 inputs scaled to [0, 1], length scale chosen from a grid by marginal likelihood, standardised y); (c) screen-then-verify for SPM@2, SPM@100, SPMe@100, DFN@2, DFN@10 (screen the whole pool, then DFN@100 in descending screen score); (d) SPMe@100 plus milestone 1's flag: the top 5 by SPMe, then every design with whole-run `r_min <= 0.1` in SPMe order, then the rest; (e) successive halving over DFN at 2, 10, 30, 100 cycles with eta = 3 from the whole pool (Hyperband's most exploratory bracket), every rung charged a fresh run, then the remaining designs verified in order of their last rung's score; an incremental-cost variant (a resumed run pays only the extra cycles) is reported beside it; (f) GP-EI at DFN@100 with the DFN@10 or SPM@2 screen score as a fifth input (input-augmented multi-fidelity GP, after Kennedy and O'Hagan's autoregressive idea), the screen's top design as the start.
- H7 (proposal H3, in wall time): DFN@10 screen-then-verify reaches T1 at median at most a third of GP-EI on DFN's median T1. Refuted if its median regret at a budget of a third of GP-EI's median T1 exceeds 2%.
- H8: successive halving over DFN cycles does not beat the DFN@10 screen. Refuted if its T1 is lower in a paired Wilcoxon test at p < 0.05.
- H9: the cheapest screen, SPM@2 (0.3 s a design), has a lower median T1 than the DFN@10 screen. Refuted if higher at p < 0.05 (paired Wilcoxon).
- H10: the trust flag does not slow SPMe screening: SPMe@100 with the flag has median T1 no higher than SPMe@100 alone. Refuted if higher at p < 0.05.
- Also reported: median and 95% bootstrap interval of T1 per method, median regret at budgets of 60, 120, 240 and 480 s, the number of DFN@100 calls to T1, and the paired Wilcoxon p for every method against GP-EI on DFN.
- Limits fixed now: a pool replay, not a continuous search; costs from one design's timings; BOCA (continuous-fidelity BO) is not run in this step.
- Script: `experiments/search/run.py`; outcome logged above this entry.
