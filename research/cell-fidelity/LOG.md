# cell-fidelity research log

## 2026-11-11 · Milestone 2 outcome: a 10-cycle DFN run ranks designs far better than a 100-cycle SPMe run at the same cost, and SPM beats SPMe
- Benchmark: 128 Sobol designs x SPM, SPMe, DFN at 100 cycles, 384 runs, all committed per cycle (`experiments/cycles/bench.jsonl`). DFN completed all 128; SPMe failed on 7 (5%); SPM on none. Prefix check exact (relative difference 0 for all three models) and the baseline reproduces `energy-cell-dfn` exactly (SPMe 109.14, DFN 116.26 Wh/kg).
- H1 supported: Spearman with DFN@100 is 0.962 for DFN@10 against 0.641 for SPMe@100, a difference of +0.32 (paired bootstrap 95% +0.18 to +0.48).
- H2 supported, narrowly: DFN@10 takes 3.6 s and SPMe@100 3.7 s on the baseline (median of 3, idle machine), ratio 0.98.
- H3 supported: DFN@10 screen plus top-10 DFN@100 verify finds the DFN best of the 128 (162.3 Wh/kg, regret 0%; in fact from k = 1). The SPMe@100 screen misses it at every k up to 20 (regret 1.98%) because SPMe ranks the DFN-best design 41st, scoring it 121.0 (gap 0.25).
- Not in the plan, and the finding to follow up: the plain SPM, which has no electrolyte at all, ranks better than SPMe (Spearman 0.955 at 100 cycles), and still does over the 121 designs SPMe completed (0.984 against 0.769), so SPMe's problem is not only its solver failures. SPM over-scores by a median +7.1 Wh/kg and SPMe under-scores by -8.7, but SPM's error is closer to a uniform shift. SPM is not a safe screen for the single best design (top-1 regret 9.4%, it scores the DFN best at 182.7), only for a shortlist (regret 0 from k = 5).
- Also not in the plan: on this unbiased sample milestone 1's label marks 15 of 128 designs (12%) untrusted under SPMe, against 7 of 49 in the stratified sample; SPMe's minimum salt concentration over the 100-cycle run flags them with AUC 0.994, and `r_min <= 0.1` catches all 15 with 8 false flags. (Milestone 1's flag used a 2-cycle run; this one is the whole run's minimum, so it is a replication of the signal, not of the exact test.)
- SPMe's agreement falls with cycle count (0.736 at 2 cycles, 0.641 at 100): its error grows as degradation accumulates, while DFN's short runs converge to the 100-cycle ranking (0.942, 0.962, 0.980 at 2, 10, 30).
- Plan change: the milestone 4 search uses (model, cycles) as a two-axis fidelity space with DFN at short horizons and SPM as the cheapest rung; SPMe stays only as the source of the trust flag. Milestone 3's theory should explain why the SPMe correction hurts design ranking when it is meant to help voltage.
- Output: `experiments/cycles/results.txt`, `summary.json`, `cost.json`, `prefix.json`, `bench.jsonl`, `cycles.svg`.

## 2026-11-11 · Milestone 2: is the cycle count a better fidelity axis than the model? (plan, written before the run)
- Question: milestone 1 found the cost is in the cycle count (100-cycle DFN 27.9 s, 2-cycle DFN 1.3 s), not in the model. Does a short DFN run rank designs by their 100-cycle DFN score as well as a full 100-cycle SPMe run does, at lower cost? The answer decides which axis the multi-fidelity search of milestone 4 spends its budget on.
- Data: a new paired benchmark, 128 designs from a scrambled Sobol sequence over the `energy-cell` bounds (`scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol`, d = 4, seed 0, `random_base2(7)`), not chosen by any search, so it is not the stratified sample of milestone 1. Each design runs once per model (SPM, SPMe, DFN) for 100 cycles of the `energy-cell` protocol (OKane2022, 5 A discharge to 2.5 V, 5 A charge to 4.2 V, SEI and plating on), one fresh process per run. Each run stores the discharge energy of every cycle, so the score at cycle n (Wh/kg of cycle n, `energy_cell.cell.evaluate`'s rule: 0 when the run stops before cycle n) comes from the same run for n = 2, 10, 30, 100. Prefix validity (an n-cycle run equals the first n cycles of a 100-cycle run) is checked on the baseline before use. The baseline's 100-cycle SPMe and DFN scores must reproduce `energy-cell-dfn`'s 109.14 and 116.26 Wh/kg within 0.5%.
- Cost: wall time of an n-cycle run on the baseline for each model and n, fresh process, median of 3, on an otherwise idle machine (the benchmark runs three at a time, so its own timings are not used for cost).
- Target: DFN at cycle 100 (DFN@100). Designs DFN fails on (score 0) stay in, as ties at the bottom.
- H1: Spearman(DFN@10, DFN@100) is at least Spearman(SPMe@100, DFN@100). Refuted if it is lower by more than 0.05.
- H2 (cost): a 10-cycle DFN run costs no more than a 100-cycle SPMe run on the baseline. Refuted if it costs more than 1.5 times as much.
- H3 (screen then verify): screening all 128 with DFN@10 and DFN-verifying the top 10 at 100 cycles finds a design within 1% of the DFN@100 best of the 128; refuted if the regret exceeds 2%. The same for SPMe@100 as the screen is reported beside it.
- Also reported (no hypothesis): the full grid (SPM, SPMe, DFN) x (2, 10, 30, 100 cycles) of Spearman and top-10 overlap against DFN@100 with bootstrap intervals, and each cell's cost, so the cheapest cell that ranks as well as SPMe@100 can be read off.
- Script: `experiments/cycles/run.py`; outcome logged above this entry.

## 2026-11-11 · Outcome: the flag works (AUC 0.997), but cycle count, not model reduction, is where the cost is
- H1 supported: SPMe's 2-cycle minimum electrolyte concentration (`r_min`) separates the 7 untrusted designs from the 42 trusted with ROC AUC 0.997 (bootstrap 95% 0.980 to 1.000). The 3 solver failures count as `r_min = 0` by the plan; over the 46 designs SPMe solved, AUC is 0.994 (4 untrusted). A flag at `r_min <= 0.10` catches all 7 with 1 false flag (design 30, gap 0.17); at `r_min <= 0` it catches 6 with none.
- H2 supported: Spearman(`r_min`, relative gap) = -0.833 over 49.
- H3 refuted, and this is the finding that changes the plan: a 2-cycle run costs SPM 0.3 s, SPMe 1.2 s, DFN 1.3 s (median of 3, baseline design, fresh process, set-up included), so SPMe/DFN = 0.88. Extra run, not in the plan: at 100 cycles, SPM 1.0 s, SPMe 3.9 s, DFN 27.9 s (one run each), SPMe/DFN = 0.14. Going from 100 cycles to 2 saves 21x on DFN; going from DFN to SPMe at 100 cycles saves 7x. A 2-cycle *DFN* run costs no more than the 2-cycle SPMe flag, so the cheapest trustworthy screen may be short-horizon DFN, not SPMe.
- Replay: DFN-verifying the top 10 by SPMe already returns the DFN best of the 49 (163.0); the top 1 to 5 returns 160.9 (regret 2.1, 1.3%). Adding the flagged designs costs 8 DFN calls and finds nothing better, because the designs SPMe mis-scores are not the DFN optimum in this sample. The flag's value is in not pruning a region, not in the best design here.
- Plan change: milestone 2 adds the cycle count as a fidelity axis (2, 10, 30, 100 cycles x SPM, SPMe, DFN) and asks whether early-cycle DFN ranks 100-cycle DFN as well as 100-cycle SPMe does.
- Output: `experiments/trust/results.txt`, `probe.jsonl`, `cost.json`, `cost100.json`, `summary.json`, `trust.svg`.

## 2026-11-11 · Milestone 1: can the cheap model flag where it cannot be trusted? (plan, written before the run)
- Question: `energy-cell-dfn` found that SPMe and DFN agree on most of 49 `energy-cell` designs (median gap 9%) but disagree badly on a few (SPMe 0 to 27.6 Wh/kg where DFN gives 120 to 146), and traced the failures to electrolyte salt depletion. A multi-fidelity search needs to know *before* paying for DFN which cheap scores to distrust. The candidate indicator is free: SPMe's own minimum electrolyte concentration, read from a short SPMe run.
- Data: the 49 designs of `energy-cell-dfn/experiments/selected.json` with their committed 100-cycle SPMe and DFN scores (`dfn.jsonl`, not re-run). New runs: each design under SPMe for 2 cycles (5 A discharge to 2.5 V, 5 A charge to 4.2 V; the `energy-cell` protocol and OKane2022 parameters), recording the minimum electrolyte concentration over space and time divided by the initial 1000 mol/m3 (`r_min`), whether the solve failed, and wall time. A solver failure counts as `r_min = 0`.
- Label (fixed now): a design is *untrusted* when SPMe's cycle-100 score is below DFN's by more than 25% of DFN's (`(DFN - SPMe) / DFN > 0.25`), or SPMe scored 0.
- H1: `r_min` separates untrusted from trusted designs with ROC AUC at least 0.90. Refuted if AUC < 0.80.
- H2: across all 49, `r_min` and the relative gap `(DFN - SPMe) / DFN` have Spearman correlation at most -0.5. Refuted if above -0.3.
- H3 (cost): one 2-cycle SPMe run costs under a tenth of a 2-cycle DFN run on the baseline design (wall time, 3 repeats each, one process). Refuted if the ratio is above 0.25.
- Also reported (no hypothesis): the screen-then-verify replay on the committed scores (DFN on the top-k by SPMe, with and without the flagged designs added), and its regret to the DFN best of the 49.
- Script: `experiments/trust/run.py`; outcome logged above this entry.
