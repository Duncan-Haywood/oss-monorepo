Plaza replay

This is a simulation, not footage. Sixty invented walkers in a 30 m × 6 m corridor that wraps around. Blue discs are simulated people, orange discs are robots that always keep to the left of their heading. A short tick marks each walker’s direction of travel; a filled dot marks a person who currently prefers to pass on the left. Part of the research project society-plaza; settings are in the caption below.
