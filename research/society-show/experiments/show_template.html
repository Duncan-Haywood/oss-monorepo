Robot Town

This is a simulation and a work of fiction. The forty-eight townspeople are invented and the robots are a stylised fleet. Nothing here depicts a real person, place or event, and it is not footage. Each dot is a person; green is high trust in the town’s robots, red is low, and a ring marks someone who has stopped using robots at all. Part of the research project society-show, built on society-trust.
