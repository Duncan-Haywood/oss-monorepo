Newest first. Write the hypothesis and the planned analysis before running an experiment, then the outcome.

## 2026-10-24 · Milestone 1: harness, and a first H sweep (planned before running)
- Built `src/lowcomm/`: a one-block numpy transformer with hand-written backward (no torch: `download.pytorch.org` returned 403 through the proxy and the PyPI wheel carries gigabytes of CUDA libraries), a flat-vector DiLoCo trainer with AdamW or SGD inner and Nesterov or heavy-ball outer, and a synchronous reference. 7 tests: gradient vs finite differences; `M=1, H=1, lr=1, b=0` DiLoCo equals synchronous AdamW to 1e-12; heavy-ball outer equals a hand-written loop; and on a noisy quadratic the same trainer's stationary loss matches `noisy-local-sgd`'s exact floor within 8%.
- **Planned run** (`experiments/h_sweep.py`): Tiny Shakespeare (char level, 65 symbols, last 10% held out), model d=32, ff=64, ctx=32 (about 14k parameters, far below the 0.8M floor in the proposal), `M=4` workers, batch 16 per worker, total inner steps per worker fixed at 640, inner AdamW lr 3e-3, outer Nesterov lr 0.7 and momentum 0.9 (the DiLoCo defaults of Douillard et al., 2023), `H` in {1, 4, 16, 64, 160}, 3 seeds, versus synchronous AdamW with batch `4*16`.
- **Hypothesis (H2, weak form):** held-out loss at `H <= 16` is within the seed spread of `H = 4`, and `H = 160` is worse. This is a smoke test of the harness at a much smaller scale than H2 is stated for; it can neither confirm nor refute H2. Outer hyperparameters are not tuned per `H`, which biases against large `H`.
- **Outcome** (`experiments/h_sweep_results.txt`, 3 seeds, mean ± sd of held-out loss in nats/char; uniform guess is 4.174): synchronous AdamW 2.437 ± 0.007; DiLoCo `H=1` 2.976 ± 0.085; `H=4` 2.743 ± 0.068; `H=16` 2.316 ± 0.040; `H=64` 2.512 ± 0.038; `H=160` 4.05 ± 1.47 (one seed 5.75, a divergence).
- **The weak form of H2 failed as stated**: `H=4` is not within the seed spread of `H=16` (0.43 higher, about 10 sd). The curve is U-shaped, not flat then rising. Reading, not tested: with outer lr 0.7 and Nesterov momentum 0.9 the outer step is too aggressive at small `H` (few inner steps, so the pseudo-gradient is small and noisy) and too slow at large `H`, which is the `H* ~ C` and "optimal momentum falls with `H`" effect (H4) showing up as a confound. `H=16` beating the synchronous run is also not evidence that DiLoCo beats synchronous training: the model is under-trained (640 steps, loss still falling) and outer momentum acts as acceleration. It would need the outer step tuned per `H`, a longer budget and a larger model before saying anything about H2.
- Next: milestone 2 needs per-`H` outer tuning (a grid over lr and momentum) before any H2 claim, plus the `B_noise` measurement. The harness takes about 27 s per 640-step run on this machine's CPU, so a 4-size, 5-seed grid needs a GPU or a faster backend; torch is not installable here (see above).
