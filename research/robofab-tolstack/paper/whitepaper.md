# Clearance for a printed two-pin cover: Monte Carlo yield, a closed form, and the cost of worst-case stacking

*Produced with AI assistance (Claude). Preliminary, simulation only.*

## Abstract
A printed base with two pins and a cover with two holes, each centre with a Gaussian position error and each diameter a Gaussian size error, assembles by translation iff two disks intersect. With exact sizes the yield is `1 − exp(−c²/(8 sp²))`, which Monte Carlo reproduces. For position sd 0.10 mm and size sd 0.05 mm, 99% yield needs 0.62 mm nominal diametral clearance and 99.9% needs 0.77 mm, against 2.0 mm for a ±3σ worst-case stack (3.0 to 3.4 times the 99% requirement across six cases). A hole bias adds to the needed clearance one for one. The spreads are assumed, not measured.
