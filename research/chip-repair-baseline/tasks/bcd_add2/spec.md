Module `bcd_add2`: inputs `[7:0] a`, `[7:0] b` and `cin`; outputs `[7:0] s` and `cout`. Each input is two packed BCD digits (high nibble tens, low nibble ones), each digit in 0..9. `{cout, s}` is the BCD sum a + b + cin, so `cout` is the hundreds digit (0 or 1) and `s` holds the two low digits in BCD. Behaviour for digits above 9 is unspecified and is not tested. Purely combinational.
