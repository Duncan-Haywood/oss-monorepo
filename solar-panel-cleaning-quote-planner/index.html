    Questions people ask

    How much should I charge to clean solar panels?

    Work out your cost for the job: crew labour over set-up, travel and cleaning time, plus vehicle and consumables. Then divide by one minus your target margin. This tool does that for each job and shows the price per panel, so you can see whether a flat per-panel rate or a minimum call-out is doing the work.

    Why is a small array not worth a visit at a per-panel price?

    Set-up and travel cost the same for 6 panels as for 30. The price curve shows the panel count at which your per-panel price covers that fixed time, and that is where a minimum call-out should sit.

    Does cleaning solar panels pay for itself for the homeowner?

    Only if the output dirt costs them is worth more than the clean. Enter their system size, yearly yield, electricity rate and an honest soiling-loss guess; the tool shows the value recovered, the net, and the soiling loss at which a clean breaks even.

    Is this business advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in. Check access factors, roof safety, insurance and panel warranty terms before you rely on it.

    Not decided

    Roof safety and working-at-height rules, insurance, whether cleaning affects a panel warranty, water quality, weather and rain that cleans for free, panel tilt, and tax. Access factors are rough defaults, not measurements. The soiling loss is the customer's guess, not a reading from their inverter.
