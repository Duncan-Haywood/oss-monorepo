    Common questions

    How much is the federal excise tax on spirits?

    Federal law taxes distilled spirits at $13.50 per proof gallon, but the Craft Beverage Modernization Act lowers that to $2.70 on the first 100,000 proof gallons and $13.34 on proof gallons from 100,000 up to 22.13 million for a domestic producer. A proof gallon is one US gallon of liquid at 100 proof (50% alcohol by volume).

    How much federal excise tax is in a bottle of liquor?

    A 750 mL bottle at 80 proof holds about 0.1585 proof gallons, so it carries about 43 cents of federal tax at the $2.70 rate, about $2.11 at $13.34 and about $2.14 at $13.50. Higher proof means more tax: a 120 proof bottle carries 1.5 times as much as an 80 proof one.

    What is a proof gallon?

    Wine gallons multiplied by proof divided by 100. One gallon of 80 proof spirits is 0.8 proof gallons. Proof is twice the alcohol by volume, so 40% ABV is 80 proof.

    What happens when I pass 100,000 proof gallons?

    Only the proof gallons above 100,000 move to $13.34, about five times the reduced rate. The earlier ones keep $2.70. The calculator shows the effective rate and the next-gallon rate.

    Do distilleries under common ownership each get 100,000 proof gallons?

    No. Distilleries in a controlled group share one reduced-rate allotment. Enter your share in the allotment box if you split it.

    When is the tax due?

    Spirits are taxed when they are removed from bonded premises, such as a bottle leaving the warehouse or a tasting-room sale, not when they are distilled. Enter what you remove.
