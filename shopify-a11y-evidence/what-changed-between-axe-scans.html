    Common questions

    How do I compare accessibility scans before and after a Shopify fix?

    Export axe-core JSON from the same storefront URL before the change and after it. Paste the earlier export in Before and the later export in After, then choose Compare scans. The page lists rules that disappeared, rules that remain (with node-count change), and rules that appeared only in the later scan. That is a diff of two supplied results, not a compliance determination.

    Does a resolved rule mean the storefront is compliant?

    No. A resolved rule only means that rule id was in the earlier export and not in the later one. Manual review is still required. The comparison does not prove a fix was complete.

    Is my axe JSON uploaded?

    No. Both scans stay in your browser. The tool counts only anonymous feature events in localStorage, with an opt-out.
