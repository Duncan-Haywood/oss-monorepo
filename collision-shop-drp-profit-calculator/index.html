    Questions people ask

    Is a collision repair DRP worth signing?

    It depends on three numbers: what each program job gives up against the same job at retail (the labor rate cut, the parts discount, the materials cap and your extra paperwork), how many jobs you already do for that insurer that will be repriced, and how many new jobs they promise. The planner puts the month with the program next to the month without it.

    How many new repair orders do I need from an insurer to make a DRP pay?

    The planner finds the new volume from which the program stays at least as good as having none. It counts the repriced jobs you already do and the whole-technician steps the extra hours force, so a lucky month at a hiring step is not read as the answer.

    What labor rate can I accept from a DRP?

    The planner searches program labor rates, body and refinish together, and shows the lowest at which the program still earns at least what you make without it at today's promised volume. It is a floor for a negotiation, not a market rate.

    Why is my profit per hour so different on DRP work?

    Parts and materials margin shrink with the same labor, and the rate cut lands on every flagged hour. The planner shows what an hour earns each way.

    Is this legal, tax or financial advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in. Insurer contracts, steering rules, state regulations, tax and employment law are out of scope.

    Not decided
