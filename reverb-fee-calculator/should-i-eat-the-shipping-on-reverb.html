Should I eat the shipping on Reverb?

A used guitar is listed at $800. You would charge $75 for shipping and pay $65 for the label. “Free shipping” can mean two different sales. Folding the $75 into an $875 list is the path on should I include shipping. This page is the other path: keep the $800 list, offer free shipping, and still buy the label. Overwrite the rates on the calculator.

Absorbing the label keeps $208.99. That is −$68.86 versus including shipping in the price, because you stop collecting $75 and you still pay $65. Including shipping only costs the extra marketplace line on the folded postage ($3.75 here). If pickup is not free, add gas or parking as the meetup cost on the calculator — it comes out of pickup cash only.

None of those sentences is advice to raise the list or require a meetup — they are the arithmetic on the inputs above.

Should I eat the shipping on Reverb?

On the worked example — $800 list, $75 shipping charged, $65 label, 5% marketplace, 3.19%+$0.49 payment — keeping the $800 list and absorbing the label leaves $208.99. Folding the $75 into an $875 list leaves $277.85. Charging the buyer leaves $281.60. Absorbing the label is $68.86 worse than including shipping and $72.61 worse than charging the buyer. None of that is advice to raise the price — it is the arithmetic on those inputs.
