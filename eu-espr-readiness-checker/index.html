    Common questions
    
    
    Can a fashion brand still destroy unsold clothes in the EU?
    
    
    Not if it is a large enterprise. Under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (EU) 2024/1781, large enterprises may not destroy unsold apparel, clothing accessories or footwear from 19 July 2026. Medium-sized enterprises follow from 19 July 2030, and micro and small enterprises are exempt. Destroying means disposal or energy recovery; resale, donation, remanufacture and recycling stay open.
    
    
    What do large companies have to publish about unsold goods?
    
    
    Enterprises that are not SMEs must publish each year the number and weight of unsold consumer products they discard, the reasons, and the share sent to preparation for reuse, remanufacture, recycling, energy recovery and disposal. This page drafts that table from your own numbers.
    
    
    What is a Digital Product Passport?
    
    
    A structured record of a product's materials, origin, repair and end-of-life information, reached through a QR code or similar data carrier on the product. The ESPR creates it; product-specific delegated acts decide the data and the dates. Textiles are in the first wave of the Commission's working plan.
    
    
    Does this apply to me if I sell from outside the EU?
    
    
    The rules attach to products placed on the EU market, so a non-EU brand selling to EU consumers is covered, usually through an importer or authorised representative in the EU. Marketplaces have their own duties.
