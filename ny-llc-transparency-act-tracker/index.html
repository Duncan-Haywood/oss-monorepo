    Common questions
    
    
    Who actually has to file under the NY LLC Transparency Act?
    
    
    Per NYDOS guidance confirmed December 31, 2025, the reporting obligation (a full Beneficial Ownership Disclosure) applies only to an LLC formed outside the United States that is also formed in New York or authorized to transact business in New York. Every other LLC with a New York nexus — including an LLC formed in New York itself — is instead an "Exempt Company" that still must file an Attestation of Exemption. An LLC with no New York nexus at all is outside the Act's scope.
    
    
    When is the deadline?
    
    
    An LLC that already had a New York nexus before January 1, 2026 must file its initial disclosure or attestation by December 31, 2026. An LLC formed in New York, or authorized/registered to transact business in New York, on or after January 1, 2026 must file within 30 days of that formation or registration date. Every LLC subject to the Act also owes a recurring annual statement in later years.
    
    
    How do I actually file?
    
    
    As of this tool's last check, NYDOS had not yet launched an online filing portal — completed disclosures and exemption attestations, with the statutory filing fee, are submitted by email to doscorpboi@dos.ny.gov (case-insensitive). Confirm the current filing process directly with NYDOS before you send anything, since this can change once a portal launches.
