    Know the special assessment before it happens.

    A balance that looks healthy today can still be years from a roof, an elevator, or a repaving job it hasn't saved enough for. This free calculator projects your reserve fund against your actual capital components and names the first year you're headed for a special assessment or reserve loan — then solves the contribution that avoids it.

    Common questions

    Is this the state-mandated reserve study my association needs?

    No. This is a financial planning estimate based on your own assumptions, not a substitute for a state-mandated reserve study (e.g. Florida SB 4-D / SIRS) or advice from a licensed reserve specialist. Those studies involve a site inspection and a credentialed preparer; this tool only projects the numbers you type in.

    Why does a freshly replaced component start at 0% funded instead of 100%?

    Percent funded measures how much of a component's wear-based obligation the fund currently covers, not whether the component itself is in good shape. A component just replaced has no wear-based obligation yet — nothing is owed toward its next replacement on day one, so its prorated share of the obligation is zero, and it climbs back toward its full replacement cost as the years pass before the next cycle.

    Why does the calculator use two different triggers for a special assessment?

    A negative balance is the sharp trigger — the fund cannot pay a bill it owes. Falling below your target percent funded is the softer, earlier warning boards actually use in practice: most reserve studies flag anything under roughly 30% funded as a special-assessment risk long before the balance itself goes negative, because a large replacement due soon can wipe out a fund that still looks positive today.

    Does anything about my association's finances get uploaded?

    No. Every number on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and nothing about your balance, contribution, unit count, or component list is ever saved or uploaded. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button below.

    What if I don't know a component's exact replacement cost or remaining life?

    Use your best estimate — a contractor quote, a neighboring association's recent invoice, or a manufacturer's stated service life are reasonable starting points. This tool is for planning and does not verify any figure you enter; a formal reserve study is the credentialed version of the same exercise.
