    Questions people ask

    Does a PTET election always save federal tax?

    No. It only helps when your state and local taxes already exceed the SALT deduction cap. If they fit under the cap, moving the state tax to the entity changes nothing except a smaller 20% pass-through deduction, and the election costs you money. The calculator shows which side you are on and the income at which it starts to pay.

    How does a pass-through entity tax election work?

    The partnership or S corporation pays state income tax itself, deducts it in computing the income that flows to you, and the state gives you a credit or exclusion so you are not taxed again on the same income. Because the deduction is taken at the entity, it is not subject to the personal SALT cap.

    Why does the election lower my AGI and raise my cap?

    The entity-level deduction reduces the income reported to you. The SALT cap phases out as income rises, so lower income means a higher cap, which can restore some deduction for your other state and local taxes. The calculator includes that effect.

    Why does it reduce my 20% pass-through deduction?

    The qualified business income deduction is a percentage of the business income that reaches your return. A PTET payment deducted at the entity lowers that income, so the deduction base shrinks. The calculator shows the lost deduction as its own line.

    Is this tax advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in, with one flat federal rate. State rules differ on who may elect, the rate, the credit, the payment deadline and whether the election is binding, so confirm with a tax professional before electing. The defaults are placeholders.
