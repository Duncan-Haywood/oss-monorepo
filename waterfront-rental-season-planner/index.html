    Questions people ask

    How many days of bad weather can a kayak or boat rental absorb?

    Staff are only paid on open days, but the dock, insurance and marketing are paid for the whole season. The planner finds the share of lost days at which the season stops covering its cash costs, and shows profit at 0 to 50% of days lost so a wet summer is not a surprise.

    How many kayaks or boats should a rental business own?

    Another unit earns only from demand the current fleet is turning away. If your weekend demand is under the fleet's daily capacity, a new kayak earns nothing; if you turn people away every Saturday, it can pay back in a season or two. The planner shows both, using the weekday and weekend demand you type in.

    How long does a rental tie up a boat?

    Rental length plus the turnaround to refuel, clean and inspect it, except the last rental of the day. Four-hour pontoon rentals with a 30-minute turnaround fit two a day in nine open hours, which is the capacity ceiling the planner uses.

    What should kayak and paddleboard rentals cost?

    The best price is where extra revenue per rental stops outweighing the rentals you lose. The planner moves both fleets' prices together along a demand curve you set and shows profit at each step; test any change on a few weeks of real bookings.

    Is this business, tax or financial advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in and a demand assumption you choose. Insurance, licensing, safety equipment rules, liability waivers and tax are out of scope.
