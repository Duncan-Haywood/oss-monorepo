This checker vs. GovWin IQ

Two different jobs: GovWin IQ, from Deltek, is an account-based government-contracting intelligence platform for finding public-sector opportunities and tracking who else bids on them. This page is a free, private readiness pass on one tender you already have, before you spend a day pricing it.

Open the free bid readiness checker

What each tool is for

GovWin IQ is a government-contracting intelligence platform built by Deltek for contractors who sell into the public sector: opportunity discovery and forecasting, incumbent and competitor tracking, agency spending history, and market research, run as an ongoing account. GovWin IQ's own pricing and current feature list are not reachable from this session to confirm, so no dollar figure or plan name for GovWin IQ is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — an account-based public-sector opportunity and competitive-intelligence platform — are stated.

This checker does none of what GovWin IQ does. It does not find opportunities, forecast agency spending, or track competitors. It takes the text of one solicitation you paste in and scans it for seven readiness signals — deadline, bid or performance bond, insurance limits, licences or certifications, a mandatory pre-bid meeting or site visit, the submission channel, and open questions or addenda — entirely in your browser, with no account and nothing uploaded.

Common questions

Should I use this checker or GovWin IQ?

Both, for different jobs, and a contractor who already subscribes to GovWin IQ to find opportunities can still paste a found notice into this checker before spending a day pricing it. GovWin IQ, from Deltek, is a government-contracting intelligence platform: opportunity discovery and forecasting, incumbent and competitor tracking, and agency spending research, run as an ongoing account. This checker does none of that. It takes the text of one solicitation you already have and scans it for seven readiness signals — deadline, bond, insurance, certification, mandatory pre-bid meeting or site visit, submission channel, and questions or addenda — entirely in your browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. A contractor who needs to find and forecast public-sector opportunities is better served by GovWin IQ or a comparable platform; a contractor who already has a notice in hand and wants a fast, free, private first pass at its paperwork risk is in the right place here.

Does GovWin IQ check a tender for the readiness signals this tool looks for?

GovWin IQ's own research and alerts can surface an opportunity and its agency history, but reading one solicitation's own bond, insurance, and deadline requirements is not that platform's job any more than it is this checker's job to find the opportunity in the first place. Some contractors will use GovWin IQ or a comparable service to find and shortlist notices, then run each shortlisted notice through this free checker before committing estimating time; neither tool replaces the other.
