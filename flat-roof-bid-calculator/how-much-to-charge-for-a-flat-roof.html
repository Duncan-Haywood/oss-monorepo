How much to charge for a flat roof

A published “$650 a square” special prices the plan. A TPO or EPDM roof is not that number. It is a membrane mil that does not scale with squares, a recover or a tear-off, dry insulation or wet, and lineal feet of parapet the coupon never counted. Matching the coupon on an 80-mil TPO tear-off is how a sold job becomes unpaid welding time after the first parapet.

The number a per-square special cannot see

Take a 40×30 garage, TPO 45-mil, recover, two drains, no parapet. That is 1,200 plan square feet and 12 squares — 12.96 membrane squares after 8% waste. The coupon says about $7,800. Raise the same shop to an 80×50 two-story commercial deck at 1/2" per foot. The plan is 4,000 square feet, so the coupon says about $26,026 on 40.04 squares. The job did not scale that way: 80-mil membrane, a welder, a wet-insulation tear-off, two polyiso layers, 80 lineal feet of parapet, six drains, and four HVAC curbs the garage never had.

Put EPDM on that garage. Sheets now cost more per square the TPO-45 recover never priced. Two stories are not “the same squares plus a ladder.” A parapet is not in the square number at all.

How to price it

  1. Measure building length and width.
  2. Apply the slope: 1/4" per foot is 1.000, 1" per foot is 1.003. Roof squares are that area ÷ 100.
  3. Count membrane squares after kit waste and extra waste from parapet flashing. Recover tear-off is zero. A full tear-off covers the roof.
  4. Price membrane (TPO 45 / 60 / 80, EPDM, modified bitumen, PVC), insulation layers, parapet, drains, curbs, walk pads, cover board, and the permit allowance.
  5. Load the wage, add materials and mobilization, mark overhead on direct cost, then divide by one minus the target margin. The walk-away price is total cost.

What this is not

This is not a building-code opinion, a permit, a construction specification, a wind-uplift rating, or an engineering stamp. Confirm the jurisdiction and a qualified professional before sending a price. The output is arithmetic on the numbers you typed.

Common questions

How much to charge for a flat roof?

Start from the building plan, apply the low-slope factor to get roof squares, then count membrane squares after waste, tear-off, insulation layers, parapet flashing, drains, and the permit. A published $650 per square special only sees plan squares.

Why do TPO 80-mil and a tear-off change the price?

A 40-by-30 garage is 12 squares of TPO-45 recover. An 80-by-50 two-story commercial deck is 40.04 squares of 80-mil TPO, a wet-insulation tear-off, two polyiso layers, 80 lineal feet of parapet, and six drains. Matching the garage coupon is how the commercial job loses money.

Is this a building-code calculation?

No. Membrane rates, waste, and insulation figures here are common starting points on residential and light-commercial bids. They are not a building-code opinion, a permit, a wind-uplift rating, or an engineering stamp.
