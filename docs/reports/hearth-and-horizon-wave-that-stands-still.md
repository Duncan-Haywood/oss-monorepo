## Model used

Flat-bed textbook jump, 9.14 m crest, 2.4 m crest-to-bed fall, friction on
the face neglected. Toe depth from y₁ + q²/(2g·y₁²) = 2.4 + 1.5·y_c, solved
by fixed-point iteration y = q/√(2g(E₁ − y)). Bélanger for y₂, head loss
(y₂ − y₁)³/(4y₁y₂), power ρgqΔH, jump length ≈ 6·y₂.
