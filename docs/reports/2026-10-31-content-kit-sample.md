**Title:** What a bootstrap penalty does to 33 real time series

```
**TL;DR:** On 33 real series the changepoint-detector's --bootstrap penalty returns fewer changes on 22 and more on none, but it also drops one step known to be real.

The changepoint-detector's --bootstrap option sets the penalty from block-resampled residuals instead of a fixed BIC rule.

**What we found**

- A sweep over the 33 real series in the repository (default settings, no labels) shows it returns fewer changes on 22 and more on none.
- Series with no documented break lose their cuts: Annual sunspots go from 5 changes to 0, the lh hormone series (48 points) from 1 to 0, and daily Swiss market index returns from 7 to 1.
- UK driver deaths keeps its three, including the 1983 seat-belt step, and the Nile keeps its one break.
- The cost: US accidental deaths loses its single cut, a step in December 1973 that is believed real.

**Limits**

- It had only been tested on simulated noise.
- Without labels these are counts, not error rates.
```
