# Pay app guides (bet #1 front door): Miller Act payment bond claims

2026-11-25, ventures run. New page `ventures/pay-app-guides/miller-act-payment-bond-claims.html`
with `millerbond.mjs` (9 tests, hand-counted dates).

**Why.** Subs on federal jobs have no lien; the payment bond is their only security, and the
deadlines (90-day notice for second tier, one-year suit limit) are a common search ("Miller Act
notice", "payment bond claim deadline"). No pay-app-guides page covered it; only `joint-checks.html`
mentioned the Act. It is the same subcontractor buyer as the PayApps bet.

**What it does.** From the last day of labor or material: notice date (second tier only), earliest
suit day (day 91), one-year limit; who is not covered (prime; below a sub-subcontractor, J.W.
Bateson 1978); whether the contract value needs a payment bond (FAR 28.102-1, FAC 2026-01:
above $150,000, protections between $35,000 and $150,000); a draft notice to the prime naming the
amount and the party furnished to (the two things 3133(b)(2) asks for). Nothing legal beyond the
statute is in the draft; a test checks it never mentions interest, attorneys, suits or enclosures.

**Sources, read 2026-11-25.** 40 U.S.C. 3131 and 3133 fetched directly from govinfo.gov (2023
edition) and Cornell LII (no amendment since 2006); FAR 28.102-1 from acquisition.gov; Bateson
from Cornell LII (justia 403s through the proxy). All loaded directly from this cloud session.

**Choices.** 90 days counted from the day after the last day; a year as the same calendar date
(29 Feb -> 28 Feb, earlier). The page says courts differ and to act early. Federal only: state
"little Miller Acts" are a possible next page, one state at a time, each from the statute.
