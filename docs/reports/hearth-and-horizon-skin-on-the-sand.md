Filtration* (WHO, 1974) and later guides: head loss of about 1 m as the
cleaning trigger, 1 to 2 cm scraped, ripening judged by test, trenching on

centimetres" as a range across guides). No removal percentage is stated. The
story's counts are invented and labelled so.
