# Product Strategy

## Audience

Primary users are photographers, videographers, and small creative teams who
produce frequent social content but lose too much time to ingest, culling,
grading, editing, exporting, and file management. A second segment is creators
who already pay an editor but want faster turnaround, more output per shoot, and
better organization between shoots.

## Core promise

Drop in a shoot from SD cards, SSDs, phones, old footage folders, logs, RAWs,
JPEGs, video clips, audio, LUTs, and references. The app safely organizes the
media, generates a grading and edit plan, produces high-quality intermediate
assets, and assembles Instagram-ready reels, carousels, and photo posts that can
be reviewed, tweaked, and exported.

## Wedge

The first sellable wedge should not be "AI edits everything perfectly." It
should be:

1. Safe ingest from cards and drives.
2. Intelligent organization and duplicate/collision handling.
3. Fast high-quality first passes for color and social deliverables.
4. Human-reviewable outputs that preserve source media.

Creators will forgive an edit that needs small tweaks if the system saves hours
and never risks their originals. They will not forgive lost files.

## Differentiation

- Local-first media safety for large shoots instead of cloud-upload-first.
- Parallel job orchestration for tens of thousands of assets.
- VLM-assisted understanding of scenes, faces, motion, products, locations, and
  story beats.
- Color pipeline that preserves camera originals and produces editable
  intermediates, not only final social exports.
- Business workflow awareness: client selects, editor handoff, revision notes,
  and repeatable presets per creator.

## Business viability

Likely pricing paths:

- Personal/prosumer one-time Mac app with optional pro add-ons.
- Pro subscription for accelerated local workers, project libraries, batch
  automation, and editor collaboration.
- Studio tier for shared presets, brand kits, NAS/SSD ingest, and queue
  management.

Strong adoption signals to validate early:

- Time saved from card insert to organized project.
- Percent of generated reels/carousels that require only light edits.
- Repeat usage across multiple shoots.
- Willingness to trust the app with original media ingest.
- Willingness to pay compared with editor cost or lost creator time.

## Non-negotiables

- No automatic destructive source deletion.
- Clear manifests for every file operation.
- Preview before export or publish.
- Explainable edit decisions and easy override.
- Native Mac performance for heavy ingest and rendering; iPhone as capture,
  review, and light editing companion.

## Near-term build order

1. Safe ingest planner and manifests.
2. Drive/card detection shell around the ingest planner.
3. Asset database and project model.
4. Culling, scoring, and content brief generation.
5. Color pipeline with LUT/preset support and reference matching.
6. Reel/carousel assembly plans.
7. Native Mac UI for ingest, review, and export.
8. iPhone companion for review, annotations, and mobile exports.
