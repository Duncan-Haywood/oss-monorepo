Product vision

Optimize profit and sales in micro-format retail without surveillance capitalism

Problem

Small luxury retail (e.g. leather goods) has thin margins, high SKU storytelling needs, and limited staff. Operators lack quantitative feedback on:

  • Whether window and doorway presentation converts passers-by into entrants
  • Which items attract attention but fail to sell
  • Where upsell or bundling opportunities exist in the basket
  • Whether layout, lighting, or display changes actually move revenue

Solution

A store-local intelligence layer that:

  1. Runs computer vision and sensor fusion on premises, emitting only aggregated, non-identifying statistics
  2. Ingests POS “data capsule” events (line items, totals, timestamps — not card data)
  3. Joins engagement signals with sales for funnel and assortment analytics
  4. Suggests experiments (display A/B, placement, hours) and tracks outcomes with simple statistical rigor

Target pilot

  • Format: ~10 m² leather boutique, Italy
  • Hardware: High-resolution wide-angle camera at interior door; optional scanners/RFID on key fixtures; existing POS
  • Compute: Small form-factor edge PC (e.g. NUC / industrial mini-PC) on store LAN, no cloud requirement for raw video

Operator outcomes

  • Daily funnel: pass-by (later) → enter → engage → purchase
  • SKU-level “attention vs revenue” leaderboard
  • Alerts for high-touch / low-conversion items
  • Experiment cards: “move bestseller left”, measure 2-week uplift

Non-goals (v1)

  • Facial recognition, loyalty identification, or cross-store shopper tracking
  • Cloud upload of identifiable imagery
  • Replacing POS — we integrate, not compete
  • Full inventory WMS — focus on pilot metrics first
