Retail Pilot Intel

Edge analytics + POS intelligence for small-format retail pilots (Italy / GDPR-first)

Pilot context: A ~10 m² leather boutique running a technology pilot: door-mounted vision (aggregate only), in-store scanners or RFID where available, and POS sales data unified for profit-oriented recommendations and controlled experiments.

Documentation map

  • Vision — Why we’re building this, operator outcomes, non-goals
  • Architecture — On-device CV, POS bridge, local aggregation, dashboard
  • Metrics — Footfall funnel, product engagement, conversion, upsell signals
  • Privacy & GDPR — Legal constraints, technical controls, DPIA checklist
  • Roadmap — Phase 1 (camera + POS) through window/display analytics

Phase 1 deliverable

Combine indoor door camera aggregates with normalized POS events so operators can answer:

  • How many people entered vs walked away (bounce)?
  • Of those who entered, how many engaged with product (touch / pickup proxy) vs purchased?
  • Which SKUs get attention but under-convert relative to peers?
  • What simple layout or staffing experiments might lift margin?
