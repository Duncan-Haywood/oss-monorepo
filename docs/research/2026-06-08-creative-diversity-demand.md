# Creative Diversity Demand Notes — 2026-06-08

## Why it matters

Meta says creative fatigue happens when audiences are overexposed to the same creative and creative similarity happens when ad images are visually too alike, both of which can reduce engagement and raise cost per result [Demystifying Creative Diversification](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/demystifying-creative-diversification). Meta recommends building ads that differ in look, feel, storyline, and message rather than making minor tweaks to the same ad [Demystifying Creative Diversification](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/demystifying-creative-diversification).

Specialist paid-social guidance is consistent with that direction: The Performers argues that Meta's current delivery systems reward concept diversity over small headline or color changes and recommends testing multiple distinct concepts each week [Creative Testing Guide Post Andromeda](https://www.blog.theperformers.io/p/creative-testing-guide-post-andromeda). Logical Position similarly frames creative variety, hooks, formats, calls to action, metadata, and AI-assisted templates as part of a 2026 paid-social operating system [Mastering Meta Andromeda, Creative Strategy, and AI Attribution](https://www.logicalposition.com/blog/the-2026-paid-social-playbook).

For wall art demand, mockup and SEO guidance points to the same operating need. MOCKL says high-performing art listings combine high-intent keywords, realistic room mockups, and listing structure that answers buyer questions above the fold [Best Etsy Art Mockups in 2026](https://mockl.io/blog/etsy-art-mockup-seo-guide-for-artists-2026). Merchize says wall art SEO is becoming intent-driven around aesthetic, room type, mood, and lifestyle preference, and says customers need lifestyle mockups to understand how artwork fits a real space [Wall Art Trends 2026](https://merchize.com/wall-art-trends/).

## Product implication

Print Atelier should not treat "AI marketing" as generic copy generation. The sharper product wedge is a catalog-to-demand engine: score whether a print has proof approval, SEO tags, room mockups, and healthy margin; then generate distinct channel concepts and only scale spend when similarity risk is low.

This run ships the first Phase 1 version in `src/lib/marketplace.ts` and `/marketing-portal`: a creative readiness score, CAC-reduction estimate, blockers, and three channel-aware creative concepts per artwork.
