# Competitor Payout Snapshot — 2026-06-08

Light research for the catalog/economics run. Sources are directional, enough to shape product positioning and next backlog items.

## Findings

- Saatchi Art pays artists 60% of profit on open edition prints, where profit is final sale price minus production costs, and takes 40% commission on original works ([Commission Rate – Help Center](https://support.saatchiart.com/hc/en-us/articles/14567125883547-Commission-Rate)).
- Society6 is a curated POD marketplace where artists typically receive a 10% royalty on most products, while art prints and framed art can use artist markup controls ([Society6 Print on Demand Marketplace | POD Sellers](https://podsellers.com/pod-marketplaces/society6/)).
- Artfinder charges 45% commission on Core plans or 40% on Premium plans, with commission applied to artwork retail value and not shipping ([How much will I be paid? - Artfinder Seller Support](https://sellers.artfinder.com/article/481-how-much-will-i-be-paid)).
- Redbubble uses a base price plus artist margin model; artists set markup, but Standard and Premium accounts pay a 50% excess markup fee on markup above 20% ([How is my payment calculated? – Redbubble](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/202270799-How-is-my-payment-calculated), [What are Redbubble's account fees? – Redbubble](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412593541908-What-are-Redbubble-s-account-fees)).

## Implications

- Print Atelier should not position only on payout percentage. Saatchi can advertise a higher print profit share, but Print Atelier can differentiate on AI-first capture, mockup generation, catalog readiness, and automated demand generation.
- The 30% net-profit artist share needs transparent per-print breakdowns in every artist workflow. If artists see fulfillment, payment, AI cost, net profit, and their share, the lower nominal percentage can still feel predictable.
- Buyer-facing storefront pages should show artist benefit in plain language without overloading the purchase flow. "Artist earns $X per print" is stronger than generic marketplace claims.
- The next finance tool should compare artist take-home under Print Atelier, Society6-style royalty, Redbubble-style markup, and Saatchi-style print profit share.
