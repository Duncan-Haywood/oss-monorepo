# CAC + POD Benchmark Notes — 2026-06-08

Light research for the channel CAC simulator and next finance/GTM backlog.

## Findings

- Ecommerce CAC benchmarks vary widely, but multiple 2025 summaries cite broad online retail averages around the high double digits: LoyaltyLion references Shopify-style CAC ranges around `$68–$78`, while Retainful cites `$68–$84` across categories ([The Average CAC in Ecommerce (2025) | LoyaltyLion](https://loyaltylion.com/blog/blog-average-cac-ecommerce), [Customer Acquisition Cost: Ecommerce Benchmarks & CAC Guide](https://www.retainful.com/blog/customer-acquisition-cost-ecommerce)).
- CAC sustainability should be judged against lifetime gross profit, not first-order revenue; LoyaltyLion frames `3:1` CLV:CAC as a healthy ecommerce benchmark ([The Average CAC in Ecommerce (2025) | LoyaltyLion](https://loyaltylion.com/blog/blog-average-cac-ecommerce)).
- POD poster and art-print costs depend heavily on size and material; QP Prints lists a `16x20` print at `$17.00` before shipping, while Merch Titans frames standard poster production costs near `$7–$12` for `18x24` and canvas `16x20` around `$25–$35` ([Pricing for Print on Demand Prints](https://printondemand.qprints.com/pricing), [Print on Demand Posters: Sell Wall Art for Profit 2026 | Merch Titans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/print-on-demand-posters-guide)).
- Society6 standardized artist markups in 2025, with `10%` for posters and selected wall-art products and `5%` for other products; Redbubble lets artists set markup, but applies excess markup fees above `20%` for Standard and Premium accounts ([Updates to Pricing and Artist Earnings - Effective March 18, 2025](https://help.society6.com/en-US/updates-to-pricing-and-artist-earnings-effective-march-18-2025-1261599), [How is my payment calculated? – Redbubble](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/202270799-How-is-my-payment-calculated), [What are Redbubble's account fees? – Redbubble](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412593541908-What-are-Redbubble-s-account-fees)).

## Implications

- Print Atelier should model CAC by channel and repeat purchase rate because paid social CAC can exceed first-order platform gross profit even when per-print margins look healthy.
- Artist-led community loops are strategically important: they can lower CAC while reinforcing the dual-sided marketplace loop.
- Finance UI should expose payback orders, LTV:CAC, profit after CAC, and monthly contribution after marketing so the company can choose channels before spending.
