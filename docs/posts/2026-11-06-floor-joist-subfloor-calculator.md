Floor framing is a lumber order people still count on the back of an envelope: how many joists, how long, how many sheets of subfloor, and what goes in the dumpster. The floor engine inside wall-framing-takeoff already did this for the whole-house takeoff; this page puts it on its own.

Worked example from the page: a 24 by 14 ft floor with 2x10 joists at 16 in on centre, a rim board and one row of blocking needs 19 joist lines, 4 rim boards and 18 blocks, plus 14 sheets of 4x8 subfloor for 336 sq ft. The waste is 25.0% because the last course is only 24 in wide. At 24 in on centre the joists drop to 13 and the blocks to 12; the sheets stay at 14. A test pins both to the engine, and the page was loaded in headless Chromium.

Joist size and spacing are what you type. Nothing here checks them against a span table or code, and hangers, fasteners and bearing are not decided. Invented floors only; no real framer's takeoff has been compared. Free, in your browser, MIT licensed: `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/floor-calculator.html`.
