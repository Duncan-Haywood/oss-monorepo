The [AI bookkeeper](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/ai-bookkeeper/) files bank lines with built-in keyword rules before any learning happens. To find where they are thin, we write descriptors for kinds of business not yet covered and count what the rules do with them.

This round covered locksmiths, chimney sweeps, drone pilots, sign makers and brewers. Of 25 descriptors, the rules filed 16 right, 1 wrong and 8 not at all. The "wrong" one was our own expectation: an FAA Part 107 exam is training, not a licence, so the rule was right. The real gaps were chimney-sweep supply houses, software and trade associations, a sign-industry association, homebrew supplies, brewing software and locksmith lead fees.

After the rules, all 25 descriptors file correctly. That is tuned on the same list, so it counts coverage, not accuracy. The descriptors are made up from public company names; no real bank export was used. Labelled real books are still what the project needs most.
