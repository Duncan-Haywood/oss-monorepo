The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *Fourteen Watt-Hours Short*, is a back-of-envelope energy budget for a retired postman who wants to ride a 64 km parish pilgrimage on an e-bike.

The sum, for 104 kg of rider, bike and bag, over 64 km with 1,100 m of climbing:

- Climbing: 104 x 9.81 x 1,100 J, about 312 Wh at the wheel.
- Rolling resistance at 0.8% of weight (quoted from memory): about 145 Wh.
- Air drag with a guessed drag area of 0.6 m2 at 5 m/s: about 160 Wh.
- That is 617 Wh. A rider giving 80 W for 4.5 hours supplies about 350 Wh, so the motor must deliver 267 Wh, or 334 Wh from the battery at 80% efficiency.
- A 400 Wh battery used to 80% offers 320 Wh. The gap is 14 Wh, about 4%.

The point of the story is what the assistant does with that 4%: every input is good to ten percent or worse, so the honest finding is "no margin", not "it will fail" or "it will be fine". The group's fix is a 90-minute charge at a village hall, worth about 92 Wh. All the figures are illustrative, the setting and people are invented, and the story is text only for now because committed audio has passed its size cap.
