[The Quarter Percent a Sol](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-quarter-percent-a-sol.html) is a short story about an invented Martian settlement whose solar panels have gone a hundred sols without brushing.

The numbers: a loss of about 0.28% of output per sol (from memory of Pathfinder's early data) compounds as 0.9972^n, giving 92% after 30 sols, 77.7% after 90 and 75.6% after 100. With an invented 13.5 kWh per sol clean and a 9 kWh load, the margin runs out at sol 144.6. Brushing 40 m2 takes two people five hours at an assumed 4 m2/h each and recovers an assumed 97%, worth about 154 kWh over the next 44 sols. The 5 W sanctuary lamp uses 0.123 kWh a sol, 1.4% of the load, so switching it off would move the crossing by under a day. Waiting for a dust devil to clean the panels is a bet whose odds the assistant refuses to invent.
