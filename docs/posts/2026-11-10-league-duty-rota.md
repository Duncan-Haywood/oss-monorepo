Parents argue about who ran the snack stand last. The
[league schedule generator](https://marisstella.app/league-schedule-generator/multi-division-league-schedule.html)
now has a box for it. Type one duty per line with the teams you need per
playing night (`Snack stand: 2`, `Scoreboard: 1`), build the schedule, and
download the rota.

The rules are simple and checked. Only a team playing that night is picked, no
team gets two duties in a night, and the team with the fewest duties so far
goes first, then the one longest since its last. A night with too few teams
lists the empty slot instead of inventing a name. Before the download an
independent check re-reads the rota and refuses it if anything is off.

It runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Tested on a made-up league only,
so no real league's rota has been compared. Next is a per-team duty list in the
print pack.
