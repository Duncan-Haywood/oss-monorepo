The Denmark page in einvoice-preflight listed 1 July 2026 and 1 March 2027 as the start of default NemHandel registration, from vendor summaries. A search result for Erhvervsstyrelsen's own announcement of its consolidated provider guidance (5 October 2026, after the amendments published on 28 September) gives SAF-T from 1 January 2027 and e-invoicing duties from 1 March 2027 and 1 January 2028. That backs 1 March 2027.

It is still an excerpt: erhvervsstyrelsen.dk does not resolve from our build environment, so the order text is unread and the page says so. A mandate date should come from the regulator; the page now marks which part does.
