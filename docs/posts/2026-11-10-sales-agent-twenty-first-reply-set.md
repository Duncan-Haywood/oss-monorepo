Most rounds of sales-agent testing probe the dangerous end: opt-outs, bounces, false stop words. This round checked the ordinary middle: pricing and trial questions, "we already use Outreach", "security would never approve", and plain yes-let's-talk replies.

Scored once before any change: **17 of 20, no overreach.** Both objection misses ("we tried something similar and the data was a mess, so I'm sceptical", "I don't think this fits a business our size") went to a person instead of being named, the safe direction. Two narrow patches fixed them, giving 19 of 20. An older set gained one reply as a side effect, and nothing fell.

The remaining miss is a label dispute: "Looks relevant. Looping in my colleague Dan who owns this" is interest and a handover at once. The rules call it a wrong-person reply. We left it, because a person reads either category before anything is sent.

The caveat is the usual one: the agent wrote the replies and the labels, so 19 of 20 is a tuned score. Real replies from a user's mailbox are still the next step.
