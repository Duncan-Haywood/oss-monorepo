# A 65-year-old freelancer and the new senior deduction

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. A worksheet, not tax advice.*

ai-bookkeeper's federal estimate (`estimate --over65 1 --blind 0`) now counts
age 65 or older and blindness: an extra standard deduction per box, and the
2025 to 2028 senior deduction of $6,000 per person, reduced 6% of AGI over
$75,000 ($150,000 joint).

For $100,000 of profit in 2026, single:

| | Extra standard | Senior deduction | Taxable income | Total federal tax |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 65 | $0 | $0 | $61,468.18 | $22,364.55 |
| 65 or older | $2,050 | $4,923.89 | $55,889.06 | $21,137.14 |

The amounts are typed from memory and not yet checked against the IRS text, so
the estimate warns every time it uses them; pass your own figures to override.
Itemizers don't get the extra standard deduction (the senior deduction still
applies). The numbers come from the repository's own test, not outside data.
