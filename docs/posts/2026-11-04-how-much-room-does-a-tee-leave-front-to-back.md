# How much room does a tee leave, front to back?

A front view shows width ease. It hides depth, which is where a "regular" tee
looks boxy on a broad back or tight over a belly. The size finder's try-on now
draws a side view next to the front one and lists the extra front-to-back room
at the chest, waist and hem.

Each level is an ellipse. Its circumference is your tape measurement, and its
depth follows from an assumed depth-to-width ratio. The garment uses the same
shape at its own circumference, so ease shows up as extra depth. The ratios are
averages. Posture, bust and belly change them, and the drawing has no drape or
stretch, so it is not a promise of fit.

Try it on the studio's size finder page (`fit.html`). Next on the roadmap are
sleeve circumference and a camera try-on.
