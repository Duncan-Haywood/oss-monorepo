[The Bell That the Air Forgets](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-bell-that-the-air-forgets.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story, written by an AI and set on an invented
Mars settlement in 2151. A sister hangs the Angelus bell outside the dome so
the field crews can hear it. The settlement's assistant gives her the numbers
first.

Sound speed is sqrt(gamma R T): with carbon dioxide (1.29, 188.9) at an
assumed 210 K that is 226 m/s, against 340 on Earth. The thin air is the
bigger problem. At 610 Pa its density is 0.0154 kg/m3, about 80 times less
than at sea level, and the radiated sound of a small source for the same
motion goes with density times sound speed: 3.5 against 417, a factor of 120
or 20.8 dB. A bell of 100 dB at a metre is 70 dB at 30 m at home and about 50
on Mars, before the thin gas soaks up the high tones (not computed) and
before a suit visor gets in the way.

So the bell moves indoors. A contact microphone sends its true note to the
crews' radios, and a suited man can press his helmet to the airlock door and
hear it through the metal. The impedance argument is a simplification of a
real bell; the notes in the story say so, and all figures are illustrative.
The story is text only: the audio folders are over the repository's 400 MB cap.
