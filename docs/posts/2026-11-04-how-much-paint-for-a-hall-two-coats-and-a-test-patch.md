# How much paint to buy

Wall area: perimeter x height, minus openings. 38 m x 3.4 m = 129.2 m2, less two
doors (3.6) and six windows (10.8) = 114.8 m2. The tin says 12 m2 per litre, so
one coat is 9.6 litres. Two coats is 19.1. But the label's figure is for a
smooth, sealed surface; a rough or chalky wall drinks more (quoted from memory,
it varies). Paint a 2 m2 patch and measure what went in: 200 ml means 10 m2 per
litre, so two coats need 114.8 x 2 / 10 = 23 litres, 24 with 5% for the tray and
the skin in the tin. Order it all at once and pour the tins into one bucket so
colour batches do not show as stripes.
