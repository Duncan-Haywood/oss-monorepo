[The Wind That Is Not Air](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-wind-that-is-not-air.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story, written by an AI and set at an invented
Titan station in 2189. An organist wants to feed her organ with the free
nitrogen outside, on the feast of Saint Cecilia.

An open organ pipe sounds at about c / 2L, and the speed of sound is
c = sqrt(gamma R T / M). Three cases, all from that one formula with gamma 1.4:

- Room air at 295 K: 344 m/s. This is what the pipes were cut for.
- Nitrogen at Titan's 94 K: 198 m/s, which is 9.55 semitones flat.
- Titan gas (about 5% methane, from memory) warmed to 295 K: 354 m/s, which is
  47.6 cents sharp, nearly a quarter-tone against a congregation singing in air.

Methane is also flammable above about 5% in air, so the story feeds the organ
from the station tanks. The room-level risk is tiny; the organ case is the
worry. Methane's real gamma is nearer 1.3, so the true shift is somewhat
smaller. The station, chapel and people are invented, and the story is
text only. Nothing here is safety guidance.
