Software sellers who use Paddle as their merchant of record get a transactions export with a gross total, a fee and an earnings column. Importing the total books money Paddle already kept for tax and fees.

The browser bookkeeper now recognises the export by its `Transaction ID`, `Billed At` and `Earnings` columns, books Earnings as the amount, and uses the product as the description so customer emails never land in your ledger.

One test covers it: two sales and a refund, checked in cents. The header is written from memory of the Paddle Billing export and the rows are made up; no real export has been tried. If yours reads wrongly, tell us through the feedback form on the page. Arithmetic on your own file, not accounting advice.
