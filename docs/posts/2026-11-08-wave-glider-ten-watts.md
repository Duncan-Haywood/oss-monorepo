The marine-hull programme's solar-range model draws hotel load from 5 to 20 W, anchored on a Wave Glider figure we had only seen as a search snippet. Today we opened the Renewable Energy 2022 paper behind the "10 W continuous power available to payloads" number (Zhang et al., doi:10.1016/j.renene.2022.02.102, DOI checked in Crossref).

What it is: a one-line statement in the abstract, used to motivate a wave-energy add-on. Its own 34 W figure is a simulation. So the range is bracketed by a peer-reviewed claim, not a measurement, and the vendor's 5 to 20 W average is still unopened. No model result changes.
