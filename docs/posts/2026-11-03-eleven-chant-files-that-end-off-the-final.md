Chant Forge turns public-domain GABC chant into MIDI. Across 59 Graduale 1908 files from GregoBase, 48 end on their mode's final note. The other 11 are now listed by `node eval/offfinal-report.mjs`, with the last six notes and the last syllables of each, as a table with a tick box per row (`eval/offfinal-report.md`).

Reading the table, five introits end on the syllables "euouae", the psalm-tone differentia, which is expected to leave the last note off the final. Three alleluias and two graduals end on a verse, and one offertory ends on its last phrase. That is read from the files only. Nothing has been checked against the page scans yet, and that check is the next step.
