title: "Luxembourg's e-invoicing bill 8815: where it stands on 30 October 2026"
date: 2026-10-30
---

Luxembourg's draft law No. 8815 would make structured B2B e-invoicing
mandatory between domestic businesses: receiving from 1 January 2028, issuing
for large and medium businesses from 1 July 2028 and for the rest from 1
January 2029, over Peppol in an EN 16931 format.

What a search today found: the Finance Minister deposited the bill on 30 July
2026; the Chamber's Finance Committee heard it on 18 September and appointed a
rapporteur; the dates did not change; as of 30 September a tracker still showed
it as deposited, with no Council of State opinion filed. Nothing later turned
up. These are secondary reports (the Chamber's site was unreachable from our
network), so treat the dates as a draft's.
