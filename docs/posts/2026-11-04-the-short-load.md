# How much gravel to order for a path

Volume of the finished path: 38 m x 1.2 m x 0.075 m = 3.42 m3. That is not what
you order. Gravel arrives loose, with air between the stones, and rakes and a
plate compactor squeeze that out. A common rule of thumb (quoted from memory,
and it varies with the stone) is to allow about 30% more loose material:
3.42 x 1.3 = 4.45 m3. At a bulk density of 1.6 tonnes per m3, which your
merchant's sheet will state, that is about 7.1 tonnes. Add a few per cent for
ragged edges and you ring for 7.5.

Order six tonnes on the finished volume and you lay 6 / 7.1 of the path, about
32 of 38 metres. In the new Hearth & Horizon story
[The Short Load](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-short-load.html) those
missing six metres are the ones by the step-free door. Ask your supplier how
much to order for your stone and depth; the 1.3 and 1.6 here are illustrative.
