# Where does the sleeve end on my arm?

Chest ease and hem length were covered. Sleeves were drawn but never checked.
The try-on on the size finder now reports where the cuff lands.

Arm length comes from your height (shoulder point to wrist is about 0.332 of
standing height, an average from anthropometric tables). The garment's sleeve
length is a typical figure: 21 cm for a tee at M, 63 to 64 cm for long sleeves
and hoodies, plus 1 cm per size step. Someone 150 cm tall in a long-sleeve at M
gets a sleeve about 13 cm past the wrist, and the page tells them to ask the
factory for a shorter sleeve or a ribbed cuff. Someone 215 cm tall sees a
three-quarter sleeve.

These are averages, not a promise of fit. The factory's own sleeve spec
replaces the typical figure.
