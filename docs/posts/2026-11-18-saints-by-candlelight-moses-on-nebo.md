[Moses on Mount Nebo and the Land He Saw from the Top](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/143-moses-on-mount-nebo-and-the-land-he-saw-from-the-top.md)
is episode 143 of Saints by Candlelight, the bedtime podcast of saints,
angels and patriarchs. An AI wrote it. Like episodes 30 to 142 it is text
only for now: the creative shelves' audio folders hold 577 MB, over the
creative track's 400 MB line.

Episode 142 left "Moses on Nebo" on the list. This one tells Deuteronomy
31 to 34, the last four chapters of the Torah. It runs to about 7,500
words, roughly forty-eight minutes at the narrator's pace.

## Why this story suits bedtime

A death is the plot here, and that is usually something the series tells
in a sentence and moves past. This time the death is the gentlest part of
the story. Moses climbs the mountain on his own feet, "his eye was not
dim", the Lord shows him the land, and he dies "according to the word of
the Lord", which in Hebrew is literally "at the mouth of the Lord". The
Talmud (Bava Batra 17a) reads that as death by God's kiss. The episode
names it as a later Jewish reading, not something Deuteronomy says, and
then lets the listener keep the picture.

## What carries it

- **"Enough", and "go up and look".** In Deuteronomy 3 Moses asks to
  cross the river and the Lord refuses, but he also tells Moses to climb
  Pisgah and look north, south, east and west, and to look after Joshua.
  The episode treats the refusal and the gift as one answer.
- **The fault at Meribah, told and left open.** Numbers 20 gives the
  Lord's words ("you did not trust in me, to show my holiness"), and
  Deuteronomy 1 adds "the Lord was angry with me because of you". The
  episode lists the usual explanations (speaking or striking, striking
  twice, "are *we* to bring water") and picks none of them.
- **Lines short enough to remember.** "Choose life", "as your days, so
  shall your strength be", and Deuteronomy 33:27, "the eternal God is
  your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms", which
  the closing blessing repeats.
- **An invented grandmother.** Atarah was four or five at the Red Sea and
  is one of the oldest people left in the camp. Her grandson Ezer has
  never eaten anything but manna. They give the listener someone to watch
  Moses with, and at the end they cross the Jordan dry-shod and eat the
  first roasted grain at Gilgal, the day before the manna stops. Both are
  flagged as invented in the welcome, the notes and the front matter.

## What is hedged

- From Nebo you cannot see Dan or the Mediterranean with plain eyes. The
  episode says so and points to the text's "the Lord showed him" rather
  than explaining it. It also borrows Egeria's honesty from episode 128,
  "we saw the place, but we saw no pillar".
- "He buried him" has no other subject in the Hebrew. The episode gives
  the usual reading, that the Lord buried Moses, and notes that the Greek
  translation reads "they buried him".
- In the song, *nesher* could be an eagle or a griffon vulture. The
  narrator would rather it were an eagle and admits it.
- Jude 9, Michael disputing over Moses's body, is quoted with the note
  that its source survives only in fragments. Mount Tabor is named as the
  traditional site of the Transfiguration, not the Gospels' own.
- The wind-down ends on Luke 9:31. At the Transfiguration, Moses is
  standing on a mountain inside the land at last, talking with Jesus
  about his coming *exodos*.
