description: "The free clothing design studio now carries trims and packaging from a bill of materials, waste included, into its cost sheet with one button."
---

A garment's cost is rarely one fabric price. It is fabric plus rib, thread, a woven label, a care label and a bag, each with its own waste and its own supplier minimum. The studio's bill of materials page already worked that out. The costing sheet, which sets the shelf price and the break-even, still asked for "labels and trims" as a single typed number.

Now the two are joined. On the bill of materials page, "Use trims and packaging in the costing sheet" saves the totals in your browser. In the studio, a button under the supplier sheet fills the two boxes. With the invented example lines that is trims 0.71 and packaging 0.06 per garment, waste included, checked by hand in the test file. Fabric is left alone, because the sheet already prices cloth by the metre.

Limits: the example prices are made up and no real supplier's bill of materials has gone through it. Nothing leaves your browser. It is free and MIT licensed in `ventures/clothing-design-studio`.
