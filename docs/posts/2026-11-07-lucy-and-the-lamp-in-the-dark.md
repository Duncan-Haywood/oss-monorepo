The newest [Little Lamps](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *Lucy and the Lamp in the Dark*, is for ages 3 to 8 and sorts three layers that usually arrive tangled together.

The oldest layer is the old *Acts* telling that Lucy of Syracuse gave her dowry to the poor. Those *Acts* were written later and are weak as history, so the story says "the old accounts say". The second layer is the tale of a wreath of candles that freed her hands to carry bread to Christians hiding in the catacombs. It is a later legend, and the story says it was written down long after her life. The third is the Swedish Lucia procession, a custom that grew up in Sweden over the last few hundred years, and the story says it is newer than Lucy. Her martyrdom and the legend about her eyes are left out, and a note for grown-ups says so.
