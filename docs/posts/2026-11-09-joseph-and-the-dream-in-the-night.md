[Joseph and the Dream in the Night](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/joseph-and-the-dream-in-the-night.html) is a nine-minute bedtime story for ages 3 to 8 about Saint Joseph.

It follows Matthew 1 and 2, Luke 2 and Matthew 13:55 and invents nothing that changes what happens. The donkey, the place in Egypt and Joseph working as a carpenter there are each said aloud to be not in Scripture, and the grown-ups' note lists what is recorded and what is ours. Later apocryphal stories are left out. Written by an AI and not an official Church text; text only, since audio is over the repository's size cap.
