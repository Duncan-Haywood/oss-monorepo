Three of the deepest tools on this site check construction files in the
browser: [XER Schedule Check](https://marisstella.app/xer-schedule-check/)
for Primavera P6 schedules,
[IDS Model Check](https://marisstella.app/ids-model-check/) for building
models against buildingSMART IDS requirements, and
[IFC Quantity Takeoff](https://marisstella.app/ifc-quantity-takeoff/) for a
bill of quantities from an IFC model. Until today none of them had a
written business model. `portfolio/monetization/models.yaml` now has one for
each. Here is what the incumbents charge and what we plan to sell.

## What the incumbents list

I read these prices on 2026-11-24, from the vendors' own pages unless noted:

| Tool | Price |
|---|---|
| Steelray Analyzer for P6 (DCMA 14-point and more) | $990 a user a year |
| ScheduleReader Standard / PRO for Windows (P6 viewer) | $344 / $440 a user a year (from search-result text of the vendor page) |
| BIMcollab platform Basic / Advanced / Enterprise (IDS in all three) | €12.50 / €18.75 / €25 a user a month, billed yearly |
| Solibri Essential / Advanced / Premium | €1,428 / €2,109 / €2,772 a licence a year, starting prices |
| Autotakeoff (Capterra listing) | from $49 a month |

For IFC takeoff specifically I found no vendor page with a price. PriMus
IFC's listed prices come only from aggregators, and they range from $7 to
$50 a month. That gap stays open in the row instead of being filled with a
guess.

## What we plan to sell

Everything the free pages already do stays free and stays in the browser.
The paid part is what a browser tab can't do alone, which is keeping a
project over time:

- **Schedule review workspace.** Each project keeps its monthly P6 updates,
  so you can see the trend in the critical path and the finish date, what
  changed between updates, and a branded review letter for each update.
  Proposed price: $39 to $79 a user a month.
- **Model compliance workspace.** A project's IDS library, every model drop
  checked against it, and the BCF issues followed from drop to drop.
  Proposed price: $19 to $39 a user a month.
- **Estimating workspace.** Saved jobs, the firm's own rate library, and the
  change-order diff run against the last priced model revision. Proposed
  price: $39 to $79 a month. It might fold into the framing takeoff
  subscription instead, because it has the same buyer.

None of these prices has been tested, and nobody has bought anything. Each
row's next step is to build the workspace with checkout switched off until
the owner sets a price.

## Why these three first

Of the 20 products the licensing plan moves to paid models, 12 had no
business model written down. These three are construction tools, which is
the owner's favourite vertical, and they sell to the same contractors as the
pay-app suite. With them, 11 of the 20 now have a row that either bills
monthly or says why it doesn't, up from 8. The other nine, with photo
editing first, still have no row.
