An agent's checking account has two kinds of lines: a few large deposits from
title companies, and many small charges that keep the licence and the leads
coming. The month's profit is the gap, and it is easy to lose in a stack of
subscriptions.

AI Bookkeeper now has a page for this
([realtor-bookkeeping](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/ai-bookkeeper/realtor-bookkeeping.html))
built on a made-up August of nine lines. With no rules taught, the importer
files both commission deposits as Sales ($10,115.00), the MLS fee under taxes
and licenses, NAR dues under education and dues, Supra lockbox keys under
office, Zillow and Realtor.com under advertising, the CRM as software and E&O
as insurance. Profit is $8,952.00, and a test pins those sums and each account.

The limits are plain. Deposits are net of the brokerage split, so gross
commission is not visible here, and desk fees and mileage to showings are not
on the statement. The rules come from company names, not from a real agent's
export; one is wanted.

It is free, open source (MIT), runs in your browser and uploads nothing. Not
accounting or tax advice.
