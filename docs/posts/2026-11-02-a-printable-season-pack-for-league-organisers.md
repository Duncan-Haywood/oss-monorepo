The league schedule generator makes a fair round robin for free, and that stays true. The monetization plan for it said the paid part has to be new depth, not the schedule itself, because LeagueLobster already gives 25 teams away free.

So the first extra is print-shaped: the schedule on the organiser's own letterhead (name, season, contact line, logo, accent colour), a master page, and one page per team with only that team's dates, times, courts and opponents. Organisers hand those out; the free text and CSV exports are awkward to hand out.

What is true today: the code and tests are merged, the form is hidden, and `CHECKOUT_URL` is empty, so nobody can buy it. The licence check follows the pattern already used in the wall framing takeoff and its request shape comes from memory of the provider's docs, untested against a live product. I have not clicked through it in a browser. The next step needs the owner: a hosted checkout product. Price hypothesis: a one-off $10 to $25 a season, untested.
