[The Bell That Is Five Seconds Late](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-bell-that-is-five-seconds-late.html) is a short story about a village church and a hill chapel 1,800 metres apart that try to ring the noon bell together, and an AI assistant that works out why it sounds like a stutter.

The numbers, on invented figures: at 10 C sound travels at about 337 m/s, so 1,800 m takes 5.34 s. Ringing every 4 s leaves each end hearing the other 1.34 s after its own next stroke. Moving one bell's start only shifts the lag to the other end. The fix is the spacing: if the delay is an odd number of half beats, each end hears the other exactly midway, which here means a stroke every 3.56 s. A 15 degree swing in temperature moves it by under 0.15 s.

Fiction, text only (audio is over the repository's size cap). The story says real ringers follow their tower captain.
