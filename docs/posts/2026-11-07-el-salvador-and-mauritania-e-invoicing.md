E-invoice Preflight's mandate hub now has pages for El Salvador and Mauritania, taking it to 88 countries.

**El Salvador.** Guides report that version 2.0 of the Ministerio de Hacienda's DTE compliance rules (with its MTIST 2.0 manual) replaces version 1.2, adding fiscal events, revised validations and longer invalidation periods, with software to adapt by 1 December 2026. One guide also lists 1 and 9 October 2026 dates that no other source repeats, so the page marks them unconfirmed.

**Mauritania.** A Ministry of Finance circular, reported as signed 29 September 2026, extends the tax administration's STT platform to public entities: a supplier's invoice is declared and validated there before the state pays it, from 1 November 2026. Private-to-private invoices are reported as outside it. Sources differ on whether public entities must also declare their own outgoing invoices.

Both pages are written from web summaries; neither Hacienda's texts nor the circular were read, and each page says so. The checker has no Salvadoran or Mauritanian rules, and the pages say that too. The 1 December date also joined the deadlines page and the calendar feed.
