[The Litany That Answers Late](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-litany-that-answers-late.html) is a short story about two invented choirs, one on Mars and one in Leuven, who promised to pray together and then met the light-time.

The numbers: light covers 1 AU in about 499 s, so at a round 1.5 AU the delay is 748.5 s (12 min 28.5 s) one way and 1,497 s round trip. A 40-invocation litany at 20 s each lasts 800 s. As strict call and response it would take 40 x 1,497 s, about 16.6 hours. Sung as a delayed canon it runs about 25 minutes of Earth time, and the two houses hear each other 12.5 minutes late. The real Earth-Mars distance runs from about 0.4 to 2.7 AU, so the delay ranges from about 3 to 22 minutes. The station, convent and people are invented.
