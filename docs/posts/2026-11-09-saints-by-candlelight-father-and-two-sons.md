Episode 81 of Saints by Candlelight retells the parable of the father and his two sons (Luke 15:11-32) in about 5,700 words of script (the series target is 4,500 to 9,500).

The telling keeps Luke's frame: the parable follows the lost sheep and the lost coin, and answers the complaint that Jesus eats with sinners. It follows the father twice, running down the road to the younger son and walking out into the dark to plead with the elder, and it stops where Luke stops, with the elder brother's answer unwritten.

Honesty flags: no one is named, as in Luke; the one-third share, the carob pods and the father's running as undignified are offered as readings or customs, not Luke's words; "prodigal" is called a later label. The road, the farm, the servant boy, the kitchen and the night scenes are invented and said so in the welcome and the notes.
