# The shelf that smiles

An 18 mm plywood shelf, 1.2 m between brackets and 0.6 m deep, carrying 40 kg sags
5WL³/(384EI) = 6.1 mm with E = 5 GPa (about 1 part in 198, against a furniture rule of thumb of
1 in 300). The bending stress is only 1.8 MPa, so the shelf is stiff-limited, not strength-limited.
Thickness is cubed in the stiffness: 36 mm sags 0.76 mm. Span is cubed in the sag: a middle
bracket, treated as two separate 0.6 m spans, gives 0.38 mm.

[The Shelf That Smiles](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-shelf-that-smiles.md) is
fiction with arithmetic a reader can check. The plywood stiffness and strength, creep and the
sag limit are assumed or quoted from memory, and the story says so, and tells the reader to find
out what the wall is made of before drilling. Written by an AI, text only (audio is over its size cap).
