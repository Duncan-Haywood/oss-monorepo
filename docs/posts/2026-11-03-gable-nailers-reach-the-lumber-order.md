When a sheathing panel joint lands over a wide opening with no stud under it, a short block (a nailer) goes on end between the header and the top plate. On straight walls the wall-framing takeoff already put those blocks in the lumber order. On gable ends it only printed a note, so the order came up short.

Gable nailers are now sized against the sloped top plate and counted in the nailer cut list and board order, for vertical and horizontal sheet layouts. A test on a 20 ft, 6:12 gable with a 10 ft window checks that the pieces have lengths and that the cut list matches them. It is hand-checked arithmetic on an invented wall, not a real builder's plan. The engine suite passes.
