[The Week That Is a Day](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-week-that-is-a-day.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story, written by an AI and set at an invented
Ganymede settlement in 2210. A parish that sets Sunday Mass at sunrise finds the
sun wandering.

Ganymede is tidally locked to Jupiter, so its day is the sunrise-to-sunrise
period: 1 / (1/7.1546 - 1/4332.6) = 7.1664 days = 171.99 hours.

- A week is 168 hours, so sunrise comes 3.99 hours later each week.
- The weekday realigns after 168 / 3.99 = 42.1 weeks, about 294 days.
- Night is about 86 hours, and Jupiter, 7.64 degrees across (14.5 Moons), never
  moves in the sky.

The priest keeps Mass by the station clock and treats the sun as a sign, not a
timer. Orbital periods are from memory; the settlement, people and sunrise
times are invented, and liturgical rulings are left to a bishop. Text only.
