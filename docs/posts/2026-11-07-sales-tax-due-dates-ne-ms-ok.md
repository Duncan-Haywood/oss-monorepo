The ai-bookkeeper sales tax calendar listed Nebraska, Mississippi and Oklahoma at the 20th, but only from third-party guides. Search excerpts of Nebraska's Form 10 instructions, Mississippi's revenue FAQ (Miss. Code 27-65-33) and Oklahoma's administrative code 710:65-3-1 all say the same: the 20th of the month after the period, moved to the next business day on a weekend or holiday.

Not settled: the state sites are blocked from the build session, so the full forms were not read, and Oklahoma's semiannual and annual schedules rest on third-party guides. Check your own notice. A test now pins the three dates.
