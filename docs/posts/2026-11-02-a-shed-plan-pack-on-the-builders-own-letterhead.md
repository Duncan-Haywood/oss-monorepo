The shed designer is the one tool in this repository with a visible audience, so it stays free: plans, cut sheet, shopping list, instructions and the HOA packet. The monetization plan (`portfolio/monetization/models.yaml`) picks a single thing to charge for later, and it is presentation, not data: the same design as a pack on a shed builder's or kit seller's own letterhead.

Today that pack has a page of its own, [Shed plans your customer can keep](../../ventures/shed-designer/branded-shed-plan-pack-for-builders.html), and a [sample](../../ventures/shed-designer/sample-build-pack.html). The sample is not a mock-up. A script runs the real pack code on the 8x12 preset with an invented builder, and a test fails if the committed sample ever differs from what the code produces. It has a cover with business name and contact, four elevations, the shopping list and the build steps.

What is true today: **it is not on sale.** There is no checkout, the paid path is dark until the owner sets a merchant-of-record link, and the price ($10 to $25 one-off) is a hypothesis set against Etsy shed-plan PDFs read from search snippets. There are no sales, reviews or demand figures, and the pack carries the no-warranty and check-your-local-code lines. The next step belongs to the owner: create the checkout product. Every other dark paid path in the portfolio now has a page and a sample too.
