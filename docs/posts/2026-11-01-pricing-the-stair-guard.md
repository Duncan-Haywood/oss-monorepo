title: "What does a stair guard cost? Posts, balusters and brackets in the budget"
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, stairs, budget]
description: The framing takeoff's budget now prices a stair's guard posts, balusters and handrail brackets from the same rail layout that sets their counts.
---

The takeoff already laid out a stair guard: posts no more than 72 in apart, balusters spaced so a 4 in sphere cannot pass, a handrail with brackets. Its rail lumber was in the cutting-stock order, but the posts, balusters and brackets were not in the budget, so a stair looked cheaper than it is.

The budget now reads three typed prices, `stairPost`, `stairBaluster` and `stairBracket`, each per piece, and multiplies them by the counts from the rail layout, summed over every flight. Leave a price off and that line is skipped; leave the `rail` off a stair and nothing is added.

The test ties each quantity to the engine's own layout for a two-sided guard on a 108 in rise and checks the amounts by hand (quantity times the typed price). No hardware prices ship: they are yours to type, and no real stair has been priced this way.
