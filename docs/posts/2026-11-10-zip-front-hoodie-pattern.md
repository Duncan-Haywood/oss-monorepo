The [clothing design studio](https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/)
drafts a graded hoodie (hood, kangaroo pocket, optional rib bands). Today it
gained a zip-front option. The front becomes two halves, one mirrored, each
with a 1.5 cm centre-front allowance where the fold used to be. The check
panel gives the centre-front stitch length so you can order an open-end zip,
and the halves go through the cutting plan: for a two-size run of three
hoodies the plan fits all 24 pieces with no overlap at 1.37 m of 150 cm cloth
per hoodie, against 1.39 m for the fold front.

What it is not: the allowance is a typed drafting default, and no real hoodie
or zip tape has been compared. Cut a test garment first. Next is a placket or
facing and a comparison against a real zip hoodie.
