A new clothing brand with no cash can take pre-orders and only place the factory order if the minimum order quantity (MOQ) is reached. We added a worked example to the Clothing Design Studio, with every figure recomputed by a test from the pre-order planner.

Assumptions (illustrative, not measured): a 35.00 tee with 20% VAT, 12.00 landed at a 300-piece MOQ, 5% cancellations, 800 spent up front, 2% conversion, 30 days.

- 300 pieces needs 316 orders, about 15,800 visitors.
- At 150 visitors a day that is day 106, far outside the window. At 400 a day it is day 40, still outside.
- Buying the traffic at 0.40 a visit costs 6,320, so a funded run loses 2,375. The price would need to reach 44.85 to break even.

The point is the method: do this sum before you promise a campaign. The page is at `ventures/clothing-design-studio/clothing-minimum-order-quantity.html`, the planner at `preorder.html`.
