# What goes on a clothing label in the UK and Canada?

A new brand that sells to the EU and US often adds the UK and Canada next, and
the label is where it goes wrong first. The
[label checker](https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/labels.html)
now handles four markets.

- **Great Britain.** Same approved fibre names and 3% tolerance as the EU, in
  English, plus the maker's or importer's name and address and a product
  reference. Northern Ireland follows the EU rules, and the page says so.
- **Canada.** Fibre content in English and French on one line
  (`95% cotton, 5% spandex / 95% coton, 5% élasthanne`), a fibre under 5% grouped as
  "other fibres" while spandex keeps its own entry, and a dealer name and address or a CA number.

Type `92% cotton, 5% elastane, 3% viscose` and pick Canada: you get
`92% cotton, 5% spandex, 3% other fibres / 92% coton, 5% élasthanne, 3% autres fibres`.

This is a summary of published rules, not legal advice. The Canadian generic
names and the tolerance should be confirmed against the Regulations' schedule
before printing; the page says so beside the result. Wool, fur and leather
special rules are still not covered. Free, in the browser, nothing uploaded.
