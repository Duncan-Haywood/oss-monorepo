A brand that ships one tee to a customer in Lyon used to pay no EU duty on a parcel under 150 euro. From 1 July 2026 the EU charges a flat 3 euro customs duty for each item category in such a parcel, until July 2028. For a 45 euro tee that is 6.7% of the goods, and for a 20 euro sticker pack it is 15%. The rule started with sellers registered for the IOSS VAT scheme.

In the US the duty-free threshold for small parcels is suspended for every country, so a parcel pays the ordinary rate (16.5% for a knitted cotton tee, from memory) plus any extra tariff, and a carrier clearance fee on top. The UK page figure is typed from memory.

The Clothing Design Studio now has a parcel page that works the sum for one order: duty, clearance fee, share of the goods, the difference from the same garment in a bulk shipment, and the basket size at which the EU flat duty drops below 5%. Every figure is editable.

Sources are secondary web pages seen on 2026-11-06 (the official ones could not be opened from the build host), so confirm with your carrier before promising customers "duties paid". The page is at `ventures/clothing-design-studio/parcel.html`; tests recompute each case by hand.
