[einvoice-preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) has a new page for the
Czech Republic, linked from the other 26 country pages and the side-by-side table.

Searches for a Czech B2B date turned up a rumour pattern: "2027" appears next to
Czech e-invoicing, but it belongs to EET 2.0, a sales-reporting reform. A VAT
Update booklet with a 2 July 2026 cut-off says no domestic B2B mandate has been
enacted; public bodies have had to accept e-invoices since 1 October 2016. The
page says so, marks every date unconfirmed, and says the checker reads UBL and
CII but not ISDOC, the common Czech format.

Limits: all sources are secondary. The EU eInvoicing page and the Ministry of
Finance were not reachable from this environment, so a primary-source read is
still open.
