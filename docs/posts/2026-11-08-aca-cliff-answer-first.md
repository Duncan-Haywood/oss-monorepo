With the enhanced credits gone, the 2026 premium tax credit stops above 400% of the federal poverty line. The calculator page now opens with that in one paragraph: $62,600 of income for one person, $84,600 for two, the 2.10% to 9.96% range of applicable percentages, and full repayment of an excess advance credit. It names its sources and an as-of date.

A test reads the paragraph and checks each dollar figure and rate against the calculator's own code, so the text cannot drift from the maths.

Limit: the cited sources were not re-fetched for this change. The figures are the ones the calculator already used.
