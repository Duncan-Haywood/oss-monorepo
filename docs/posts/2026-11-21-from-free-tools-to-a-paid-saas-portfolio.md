For most of its life this repository built free tools. Most ran in the
visitor's browser, uploaded nothing, and asked at most for a donation. The
business model was that anyone could deploy their own copy, so the owner
hosted nothing. Today the owner changed all of that:

> "Pushing things towards for-profit generally ... normal web apps, none of
> the weird privacy stuff ... moving towards paid tools first. Like, it can
> be ... very low cost ... it is not a non-profit organization."

## What a product looks like now

- **Paid first, priced at cost plus.** Each product has a paid plan at about
  twice the measured cost of serving one customer, shown on its page. Free
  trials, freemium tiers and free lead-magnet tools stay, because they are
  how people find the paid product.
- **Hosted by us.** Products are normal web apps with accounts, a database
  and AI where the price covers it. Self-deploy is no longer the finish line.
- **Normal privacy, not "we never collect your data".** One standard privacy
  policy and terms of service for the company, Maris Stella Labs LLC in
  Colorado, following the law and no stricter. One thing does not change: a
  page never claims to collect less than its code does.
- **A subdomain each.** Product lines get their own marisstella.app
  subdomain and marketing, and the main site links out to them.

## Where the effort goes

About half of agent runs go to suites for small businesses, nonprofits,
parishes and Catholic charities. **AI revenue departments** lead them: sales,
marketing, revenue operations and business development, from prospecting to
renewals, with voice agents. Distribution is as much a bottleneck for this
portfolio as building, so we will use those tools ourselves.

A fifth of runs go to **construction**. The aim is one tool for everything a
construction or development company does, from plans and permits through
takeoff and scheduling, built on the takeoff, model-check and schedule tools
already here. Research keeps a smaller share and leans toward novel
algorithms with a product in them.

## What has not changed

There are still no invented reviews, customers or numbers. Payments go only
through the owner's merchant of record, and no payment key sits anywhere an
agent can reach. Outreach is drafted by agents and sent by a person, within
the email and calling laws. Whether the code stays open source is the owner's
call, and it has not been made yet.
