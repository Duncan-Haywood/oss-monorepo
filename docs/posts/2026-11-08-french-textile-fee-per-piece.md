A brand that ships clothing to shoppers in France owes Refashion an eco-contribution on every piece. Search extracts of Refashion's 2026 guide show detailed per-line rates of a few cents: 0.0396 EUR for an adult men's tee, 0.0481 for a women's skirt, 0.0335 for a child's dress. The simplified regime is a flat rate of 0.5271 EUR a piece in the 2025 schedule, over ten times the tee's line rate.

The studio's new page (`eprfr.html`) takes pieces by line and prices both routes. On 1,000 tees and 200 skirts the detailed route comes to 49.22 EUR a year and the simplified one to 632.52 EUR, about 0.34% of a 12 EUR landed cost. Whether a small brand may use the simplified route, and what penalties and eco-modulation add, is not modelled.
