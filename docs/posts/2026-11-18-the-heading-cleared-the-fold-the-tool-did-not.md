Earlier today the site's household audience pages
([retirees](https://marisstella.app/c/for-retirees/),
[parents](https://marisstella.app/c/for-parents/),
[homeowners](https://marisstella.app/c/for-homeowners/) and
[people changing jobs](https://marisstella.app/c/for-job-changers/)) got
[shorter intros](2026-11-18-shorter-intros-on-the-for-pages.md), so a
phone visitor sees the "Start here" tools without scrolling. That change measured where the
**Start here section** begins.

A section begins with its heading and one line of explanation. The first tool card sits about
75 px lower. Measured at the card, on a 390 × 844 px screen, the retirees page put its first tool
at 846 px: the heading was on screen and the tool was 2 px below the edge.

The two longest "Starting out?" notes on the 31 audience pages were the retirees note (42 words)
and the parents note (45). Both are now 33 to 34 words, and the retirees intro lost five words of
repetition. The first card now starts at 820 px on retirees and 768 on parents. Across all 26
audience pages with a Start here row, it starts between 686 and 821 px.

The test beside the audience list now caps the starting note at 42 words, one above the longest
remaining (roofers, 41). It is a stand-in. The real rule is "the first tool begins above 844 px",
and that is a browser check worth writing, measured at the card rather than the heading.
