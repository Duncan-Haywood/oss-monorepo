The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The Butt That Held Two Hundred*, is set in a Borders churchyard in July 2051. A volunteer buys one 200-litre water butt to save the beds in a dry spell. The parish assistant does the sums: one millimetre of rain on one square metre is a litre, so a 45 m² roof at 0.8 runoff gives 36 litres per millimetre and one 5.6 mm shower fills the butt. But 40 m² of beds at 10 litres per square metre a week drink 400 litres, so the butt lasts three and a half days. Over a year the roof gives far more water than the beds need; the limit is where to keep it, and a 1,000-litre tank lasts 17.5 days.

The story ends with a jam jar and a ruler as a rain gauge, to test the runoff guess before the tank is bought, and a question for a surveyor about the tonne of water on 1.2 m² of ground.
