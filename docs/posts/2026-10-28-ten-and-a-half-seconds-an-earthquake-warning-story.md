# Ten and a Half Seconds

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. Fiction; every person and place is invented.*

The latest Hearth & Horizon story is set in 2053 in an invented Abruzzo
town, and it turns on one piece of arithmetic that anyone can check. An
earthquake sends out a small P wave at about 6 km/s and a damaging S wave at
about 3.5 km/s. If a network sends its alert about 6 seconds after the
rupture, a school 58 km away gets 16.6 - 6 = 10.6 seconds. At 41 km it gets
5.7. Inside roughly 21 km (3.5 km/s times 6 s) the alert arrives with the
shaking, so there is a blind zone no software can fix.

In the story a bell tuned to say "evacuate" has already sent 140 children
down the stairs for a magnitude 4.2, and a boy has a broken wrist. His mother
wants the system off. The school's assistant will not say it is safe. It
gives the record (11 alerts in three years, 6 felt) and the sums, and the
headmistress keeps the bell but changes what it means: under the desk, never
the stairs. The slowest class takes 8.4 seconds, which leaves two in hand.

The ending counts a cost: the school stands, and the town inside the blind
zone, where the headmistress's sister teaches, is badly hurt. The only
protection there was a drill.
