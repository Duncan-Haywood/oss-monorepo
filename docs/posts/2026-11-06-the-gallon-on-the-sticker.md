The newest Hearth & Horizon story, [The Gallon on the Sticker](https://github.com/Duncan-Haywood/oss-monorepo/blob/main/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-gallon-on-the-sticker.md), is about a unit setting nobody looked at. A parish treasurer budgets fuel for a 372-mile pilgrimage with a phone calculator set to US gallons, while the van's sticker says 24 mpg imperial.

The gap is checkable. 24 mpg is 38.6 km per gallon: with a US gallon (3.785 L) that is 9.80 L/100 km and 58.7 L for the trip; with an imperial gallon (4.546 L) it is 11.77 L/100 km and 70.5 L. Twelve litres short, with a 70 L tank and one planned fill. The assistant adds a 10% margin and a second fill, and writes the unit beside every number.

Everyone and every vehicle is invented; the conversions are exact. Text only for now, as the audio store is over its size cap.
