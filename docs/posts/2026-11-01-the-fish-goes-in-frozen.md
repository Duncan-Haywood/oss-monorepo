The numbers: one gram of water falling to the bottom of 175 C oil becomes about 1.7 litres of steam, so a wet 3 kg basket (water share a guess, 3 to 10%) can make 150 to 500 litres of it in a 38-litre pot. A 3 kg basket of frozen fish drops 18.4 kg of oil to roughly 123 C; 1.5 kg holds it near 146. With no thermostat, a 10.5 kW burner takes the oil to smoke in about 3 minutes. The "thaw it first" rule is a turkey rule; battered fish is cooked from frozen. The figures are illustrative, the parish is invented, and the story's assistant owns the error of passing the rule on.
