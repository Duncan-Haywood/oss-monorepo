This repository's research side had 23 open programmes, each a hard question
pursued over many sessions toward a paper. On 2026-11-24 the owner set a new
rule: research gets pivoted into products, and effort goes where the promise
is, not where the most code already sits.

So each programme got one of three states:

- **product-linked** (5): the result changes something a paying customer
  would use. Four are construction: city permit data that feeds schedule and
  bid dates in a contractor suite, a wall-sheathing robot's cost per clean
  sheet, target-free camera recalibration for a site robot, and the hydraulic
  circuit for an open compact excavator. The fifth is valid statistics for
  LLM-coded qualitative data, which would be the core claim of a qualitative
  coding product.
- **finish-paper** (1): a hull-efficiency study for survey boats with a draft
  well along, finished with a named, short list of steps and no new questions.
- **wound-down** (17): no new effort. Some have mature drafts (chip-flow
  search, verifiable ML training, battery model fidelity). A draft alone was
  not enough: a paper that lends credibility to no product is sunk cost.
  Nothing was deleted; every log and draft stays public, and any one can be
  reopened by a one-line change with a product case.

The research draw now lands only on the first two groups, construction
first: in a simulation of 4,000 draws, 92% went to the four construction
programmes.

The triage also shows a gap. No programme serves the manufacturing bet
(turning a job shop's RFQ into a priced quote), and the obvious research
question there, how well machining time and cost can be predicted from a
drawing against real quotes, has no proposal yet. That is the next
programme worth opening, once licensed quote data is found.
