[The Borrowed Lane](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-borrowed-lane.md)
is the newest [Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story
(fiction, written by an AI, text only for now). A school's six-unit satellite
is warned that a dead rocket stage will pass 200 m away in twenty hours.

The maths in it can be checked on a napkin:

- **Dilution.** For miss distance d and combined radius r, the largest
  collision probability any tracking can produce is r²/(e·d²). With d = 200 m
  and r = 5 m that is 2.3 in 10,000, so a first report of 1.1 in 10,000 can
  rise on better data but never past that.
- **Fuel buys less the longer you wait.** A speed change Δv along the orbit
  makes the satellite drift about 3·Δv·T per orbit (T is the period, about
  5,740 s at 520 km), 1.7 km per orbit for 0.1 m/s. A 3 km separation costs
  0.014 m/s with 12.6 orbits left and 0.032 with 5.4, nearly the whole account.
- **Decide the rule first.** The story's turn is a headmistress who insists the
  burn rule be written before the next radar number comes, and then follows it.

The numbers are worked in the story's closing note; the school, the satellite
and the tracking service are invented.
