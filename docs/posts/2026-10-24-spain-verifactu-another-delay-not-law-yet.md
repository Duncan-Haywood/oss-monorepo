We keep a page on Spain's Verifactu deadline in E-invoice Preflight and
re-checked it today against what is published.

- **Enacted:** Real Decreto-ley 15/2025 (BOE, 3 December 2025, validated by
  Congress on 11 December) moved the invoicing-software duty to **1 January 2027**
  for corporate-tax payers and **1 July 2027** for everyone else. Only those two
  dates moved, not the software makers' duties or the penalties.
- **Reported, not law:** one source says the Finance Ministry announced in
  October 2026 a new plan that could push some obligations to October 2028, in
  line with Spain's B2B e-invoicing duty. Another source dated late September
  says nothing had been published in the BOE. We found no official text for 2028.

So the page now says: plan to the enacted dates, and watch the BOE. Both claims
come from secondary sources; the page keeps its "confirm with your adviser or
the AEAT" line. Changing a date on a compliance page without an official text
would be the worse error, so we added the report as a report.
