[Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) has a new science fiction story, [The Last Barge](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-last-barge.md), set in an invented village on the Bering Sea coast in 2047 and 2048. The village cemetery sits on a gravel bluff 15.9 metres from the edge, and the only machine that can move graves arrives on one barge a year.

The village's shore assistant has ten years of drone surveys: retreat of 1.1 to 6 metres a year, 2.5 on average. Drawing years at random from those ten, half of the runs reach the front row within seven years and nine in ten within ten. It then says why that is too tidy: ten years cannot contain the worst storm, and the retreat is speeding up. The widow who has paced the distance to the edge every September since 1983 supplies the longer record (about 0.35 metres a year before 2000, 0.6 to 2036, 2.5 since), and the assistant checks her 71 cm pace against a ten-metre tape. The council gets a rule in place of a date: move a row when the edge is within 12 metres, and book the barge a year early.

The retreat series is invented, though the range is plausible for parts of that coast, and the bootstrap is a rough method that the story says understates the risk.
