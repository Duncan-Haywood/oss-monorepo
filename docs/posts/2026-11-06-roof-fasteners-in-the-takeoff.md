A roof order usually lists shingles, felt and lumber. The small fasteners get a "a few boxes" line and a bad guess, and they are the items a crew runs out of on a Friday.

The wall-framing takeoff now counts several of them from the roof it already measured: drip edge nails along eave and rake, ridge vent nails down both flanges, underlayment cap nails per square, ridge cap and starter nails, and H-clips between sheathing panels. Each takes a spacing or rate you type, and a box size turns the count into boxes and, if you add a price, a budget line.

Nothing is assumed. The vent maker's nailing schedule, not a default in the tool, sets the ridge vent spacing. Leave the spacing blank and the line does not appear.

The tests are hand arithmetic on invented houses. No real roofer's order has been compared with these counts, so treat them as a starting list to check against the manufacturer's instructions and your own supplier, not as a quote.
