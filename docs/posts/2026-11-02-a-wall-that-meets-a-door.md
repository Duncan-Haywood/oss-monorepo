While probing the wall-framing takeoff with hand-made bad plans, one slipped through: a tee wall (an interior partition meeting an exterior wall) listed at a spot inside a door opening. The tool answered "ok" and priced backing studs where the door's king and jack studs already stand.

The check now looks at each tee against the rough width of every opening on the wall, and against other tees closer than one stud thickness. Either way it refuses the plan with the wall, the tee position and the opening named, so the fix is a measurement away.

It is a small rule, but a takeoff that says "ok" to an impossible plan is worse than one that says nothing. Invented plans only so far; a real framer's plan set is still the open test. The tool is free and MIT licensed under `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff`.
