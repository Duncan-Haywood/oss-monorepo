# Soffit, fascia and J-channel now count on the wall-framing page

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. A materials estimate, not a building plan.*

The command-line `--eaves` option landed earlier today. Most people use the
page, so it now has a checkbox and a card too. Turn on the roof, tick the
soffit option, and the page counts soffit panels, fascia boards and J-channel
from the roof's eave and rake overhangs instead of from a perimeter guess.

On the 40 x 28 ft gable sample with the default 10% waste the browser shows 8
12-ft soffit panels, 11 sixteen-foot fascia boards and 8 twelve-foot J-channel
pieces. The counts also flow into the budget when you type prices.

What it does not do: corner mitres, vented-panel mix, frieze board, drip edge,
nails, caulk and paint are left out, and none of it has been checked against a
real soffit order. The widths, lengths and prices are figures you type.
