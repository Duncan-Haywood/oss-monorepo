The [Bulgaria page](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/bulgaria-e-invoice-mandate.html) in [einvoice-preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) used to say that sources disagreed between 1 January 2028 and a "2026 to 2030 phase-in". A search on 4 November 2026 explains the disagreement: the phase-in belongs to SAF-T (standard audit file) reporting, which is already in law and began for the largest taxpayers on 1 January 2026. The draft VAT Act amendment published on 23 September 2026 (reported as No. 12733-K) names one e-invoicing start, 1 January 2028, for domestic B2B and B2G supplies.

Adviser pages also report a tax-authority testing environment before the start and a 1 July 2028 penalty date; the figures differ between sources, and the page says so. All of this comes from adviser and vendor pages, not the Ministry's text, which was not read. The consultation closed on 23 October and no outcome was found. The checker still applies only EN 16931 rules, none from Bulgaria. A summary, not tax advice.
