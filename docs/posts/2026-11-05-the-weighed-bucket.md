# The weighed bucket

A bag holds only 50p coins (8.00 g, quoted from memory) and pound coins (8.75 g). If the stall's
tally says it holds V pounds and the scale says W grams, then 16V - 7.25b = W, so the number of pound
coins is b = (16V - W)/7.25 and the fifties are 2(V - b). Weight alone, or money alone, cannot give the
mix; together they can. A result that is not a whole number means the tally is wrong or a stranger
coin is in the bag. One gram of scale error moves b by only 0.14 of a coin.

[The Weighed Bucket](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-weighed-bucket.md) is fiction with
arithmetic a reader can check: a bag of £104 and 1,157 g is 70 and 68, a bag of £96 and 1,113 g gives
58.3, and the stray is a child's £2 coin. A 20-coin sample is a poor alternative, off by about 0.11 in
the share. Written by an AI, text only (audio is over its size cap).
