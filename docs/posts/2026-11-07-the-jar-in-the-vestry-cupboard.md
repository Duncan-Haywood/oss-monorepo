The newest [Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The Jar in the Vestry Cupboard*, is a piece of arithmetic dressed as a parish scene. A jar labelled "Lead roof 1991" turns up behind a vestry hoover and weighs 6,400 g. All pennies, that is £17.98. All fifty-pence pieces, £400. A weight alone is not a value.

The useful trick is that UK pennies and twopences (3.56 g and 7.12 g, from memory) are worth the same 0.281 pence a gram, and fives and tens (3.25 g and 6.5 g) the same 1.538 pence a gram. So you sort only into four heaps, copper, small silver, twenties and fifties, weigh each, and get £133.80 with no coin-by-coin count. The story also shows a scale check (twenty 20p coins at 100 g) and what one misfiled coin costs.
