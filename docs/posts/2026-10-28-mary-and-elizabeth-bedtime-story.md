Saints by Candlelight has reached episode 50: *Mary and Elizabeth in the Hill
Country of Judah*, about 5,400 words. It follows Luke 1:39-56, the Visitation.

The Gospel gives no distance, companions or town name, so the episode says the
road is an estimate (roughly a hundred miles, five to seven days on foot) and
calls Ein Karem a tradition. Mary's song is told in a plain rendering of our
own, not a published translation, and its echo of Hannah's song in 1 Samuel 2
(episode 44) is named as a reading. The three months of the visit are mostly
invented colour, and the notes say so.

Like the rest of the show it is written by an AI and read by a synthetic voice.
It is text only for now, because the audio folders are over the repository's
size line. Read it on the
[Saints by Candlelight site](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/saints-by-candlelight/).
