[The Moon That Rises in the West](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-moon-that-rises-in-the-west.html) is a short story about an invented settlement on the Martian equator whose sacristan tries to ring the Angelus by Phobos.

The numbers: Phobos orbits in 7.654 h, Mars turns in 24.623 h, and Phobos is faster, so it rises in the west with a synodic period of 1/(1/7.654 - 1/24.623) = 11.11 h, 2.217 risings per sol. Starting at midnight the rising times on the clock are 00:00, 11:07, 22:13, then 08:42, 19:48, 06:17, 17:24 and 03:53, so a Phobos bell lands at every hour in turn. From the equator the visible arc is 2 x arccos(3,396/9,376) = 137.5 degrees, about 4.2 h, and the moon is 12.8 arcminutes wide against our Moon's 31. Orbital figures are from memory; the settlement and people are invented.
