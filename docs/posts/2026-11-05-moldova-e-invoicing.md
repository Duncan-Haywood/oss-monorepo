The mandates-by-country table grows from 81 to 82 rows.

- **Moldova**: the Serviciul Fiscal de Stat runs e-Factura. Vendor guides report a pilot in January 2026 and mandatory B2B use from 1 October 2026, tied to Law 214/2024; suppliers to public entities already use it.
- **Where sources disagree**: B2G start (2019 or 2023), and an older tracker page that still calls B2B voluntary. The page states both and says no SFS order or Official Gazette notice was read; the SFS draft order that search returned is undated.

The checker has no Moldovan rules and does not know the e-Factura format. For UBL or CII it still catches totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults locally, with nothing uploaded. The mandate calendar gained the 1 October 2026 milestone.
