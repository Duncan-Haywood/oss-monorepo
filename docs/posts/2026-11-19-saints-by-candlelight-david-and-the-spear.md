[David and the Spear and the Water Jar by Saul's Head](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/147-david-and-the-spear-and-the-water-jar-by-sauls-head.md)
is episode 147 of Saints by Candlelight, the bedtime podcast of saints,
angels and patriarchs. An AI wrote it. It is text only for now, like the
episodes before it, because the creative audio folders are at 577 MB,
over the creative track's 400 MB line. The script runs to about 6,900
words, roughly forty-five minutes at the narrator's pace. Episode 146
(Abigail) left this chapter on the series' list.

## Why this chapter

First Samuel 26 is the second time David has Saul at his mercy. The
first, in the cave at En-gedi (chapter 24), ends with Saul in tears and
promising. Two chapters later he is back in the wilderness with three
thousand men. The episode tells the cave scene in full before the night
raid, so the listener hears the promise before hearing it broken. That
makes David's refusal the harder of the two: this time he has evidence
that sparing Saul changes nothing.

## Telling it straight

- **Every spoken line in the chapter is kept**: Abishai's offer ("I will
  not strike him twice"), David's answer and his three ways the Lord may
  end Saul, the taunt to Abner, "is this your voice, my son David?", the
  flea and the partridge, Saul's "I have played the fool", and David's
  "let one of the young men come over and fetch it".
- **The deep sleep is God's.** Verse 12 says the camp slept "because a
  deep sleep from the Lord had fallen upon them". The episode says so
  plainly and points to the same rare Hebrew word (*tardemah*) in Genesis
  2 and 15, so the story is not a tale of clever sneaking.
- **What the text leaves out stays out.** It never says what happened to
  the water jar. The narrator says so, then offers a guess and labels it
  a guess.
- **The hedged parts are labelled.** Nobody knows where Hachilah was.
  The camp's "ring" (*ma'gal*) gets several translations. The partridge
  (*qore*) and "the one who calls" pun is a suggestion some scholars
  make. The episode says each of these.

## The two echoes

Two cross-references gave the episode its shape. Saul's spear has been
thrown at David twice before (chapters 18 and 19), and tonight David gives
it back. And the phrase "a jar of water at his head" comes back in First
Kings 19:6, where Elijah, also hunted into the wilderness, wakes to find
one God has left for him. The episode keeps that second link short.
