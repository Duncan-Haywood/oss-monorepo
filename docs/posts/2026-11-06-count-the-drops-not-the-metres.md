The newest Hearth & Horizon story, *Seven Rolls for a Vestry*, is about a sum most people get wrong once.

A 4.2 by 3.6 metre room, 2.4 metres high, has 37.44 square metres of wall. A roll of 10.05 m by 53 cm covers 5.33, so the area sum says 7.03 rolls. But paper goes up in strips. Walls need 8 + 7 + 8 + 7 = 30 strips. A plain paper gives 4 strips a roll (8 rolls). A 64 cm straight-match repeat rounds each 2.5 m strip up to 2.56 m, so a roll gives 3 strips with 19 cm short of a fourth. That is 10 rolls, plus a spare from the same batch: 11.
