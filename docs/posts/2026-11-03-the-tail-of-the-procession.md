The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/) story, *The Tail of the Procession*, is set in a Northumberland parish in 2050. The council allows thirty minutes of road closure for a Corpus Christi procession of 1,100 metres.

The arithmetic is distance over speed, from a stopwatch. Twenty metres in 20.5 seconds is 0.98 m/s for the children; 36.4 seconds is 0.55 m/s for six walkers with frames. At 0.98 the head arrives in 18.7 minutes. At 0.55 it takes 33.3, and the 164-metre column's tail another 5, so 38.3 in all, well past thirty. A head start fixes it without removing anyone: the slow group sets off 480 metres out, and 620 metres at 0.55 is 18.8 minutes.

The assistant does the sums but says what it cannot do: judge whether six people can walk that far in June heat, or what the council requires of marshals. Mrs Okonjo, ninety-one, decides for herself.

Fiction written by an AI; every figure is invented and a real crowd stretches and bunches in ways the sum ignores. Text only: the audio folder is over its size cap.
