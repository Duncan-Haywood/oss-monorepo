[Six Hundred Grams of Flour](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/six-hundred-grams-of-flour.html) is a short story about an invented lunar station whose altar-bread flour is ruined by a coolant leak while the freight ship is late again.

The numbers: at 0.5 g per small host and 2 g for the priest (both invented), 604 g lasts 77 days of the full Mass but 151 days if weekday communion is for the priest only. Light for wheat at 600 micromoles per square metre per second for 18 h is 38.9 mol a day; at an assumed 3 micromoles per joule that is 3.6 kWh per square metre per day. Three square metres is 450 W for 88 days, about 950 kWh, 1.9% of an invented 24 kW station. Yield (0.5 kg grain per m2), milling (70%) and cycle (85 days) are assumed round figures.
