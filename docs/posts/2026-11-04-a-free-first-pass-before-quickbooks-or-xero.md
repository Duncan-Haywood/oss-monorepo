# A free first pass at your books, before QuickBooks or Xero

People who search for a QuickBooks or Xero alternative are often not leaving
at all. They keep books in a spreadsheet, or they want the statement
categorised before it goes to a bookkeeper.

The new page `ai-bookkeeper/vs-quickbooks-xero-wave.html` says so plainly. The
tool categorises a bank statement on your own machine, keeps a balanced
ledger, and exports journals that QuickBooks Online, Xero and QuickBooks
Desktop can import. It has no bank feed, sends no invoices and files no taxes,
and the page lists those as reasons to keep the hosted product.

No competitor price appears on the page: their pricing pages could not be
checked from this session, and an invented figure is worse than none.
The import layouts were written from documented columns and tested on
hand-made files, not on a real account.
