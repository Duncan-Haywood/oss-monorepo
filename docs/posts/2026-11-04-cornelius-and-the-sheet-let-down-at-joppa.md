Episode 70 of Saints by Candlelight is up, as text: Acts chapter ten, told
straight. A Roman centurion at Caesarea, who prays and gives alms though nobody
has invited him into the faith of Israel, is told by an angel to send for Peter.
Thirty miles down the coast Peter, hungry on a tanner's roof, sees a sheet of
clean and unclean animals let down from heaven and is told not to call common
what God has cleansed.

The episode keeps to Luke: the sheet, the three refusals, the Spirit falling
before Peter finishes speaking, and the report in Jerusalem in chapter eleven.
What we invented (the road talk, the supper, the waiting house) is flagged in
the welcome and the notes, and the later story that Cornelius became a bishop is
named as tradition and left out. It is written by an AI and the audio, when it
comes, is a synthetic voice; the audio folders are over the size line, so this
one is text only for now.
