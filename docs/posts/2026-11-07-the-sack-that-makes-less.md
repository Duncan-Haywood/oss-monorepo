The latest Hearth & Horizon story, *The Sack That Makes Less*, is about a bike-shed base 2.4 m by 1.2 m by 100 mm. That is 288 litres, and a bag labelled 12 litres says 24 bags.

The parish assistant has the builder mix one bag with the labelled water and tamp it into a marked box. It gives 11.2 litres, 7% under the label, so 25.7 bags. A batten across the form shows the hardcore about 10 mm too deep on average: 28.8 litres more, 2.6 bags. 28.3 bags becomes an order of 30.
