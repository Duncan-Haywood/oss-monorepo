[The Water That Boils at Nought](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-water-that-boils-at-nought.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story, written by an AI and set in an invented
Hellas Planitia settlement in 2161. A sacristan wants holy water in the
airlock porch, and the lock technician runs the experiment.

Water boils when its vapour pressure equals the pressure around it. Mars's mean
surface pressure, about 610 Pa, is just under the triple point (611.657 Pa), so
liquid water there boils at about 0 C. On the Hellas floor, at an assumed
1,150 Pa, it boils at 9.1 C (Antoine equation). A 200 g beaker at 16 C boils
off only 2.3 g and stops; at 610 Pa, 13.8% (27.6 g, about 5.7 m3 of vapour)
must leave before the rest freezes. Ice takes 9.05% more room than water, so
an open clay bowl cracks overnight, while a sealed phial filled to 85% does
not (212 mL becomes 231 mL in a 250 mL vessel).

The pressure figures for Hellas are from memory, the heat leak from the porch
is not modelled, and the settlement and people are invented. Text only: the
audio folders are over the repository's 400 MB cap.
