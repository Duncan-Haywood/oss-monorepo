# Can a new clothing brand draw its own care label?

A care label has five slots in a fixed order: wash tub, bleach triangle, dryer
square, iron, and the professional-care circle. A cross through a symbol means
"do not". Dots or numbers inside say how hot.

The label page already turned a fibre content line into starting care text.
Now it also draws the five symbols that go with that text, and offers each as
an SVG file to drop into label artwork. Cotton gives a 40 degree tub, any
non-chlorine bleach, tumble dry low and a medium iron. Wool gives a hand-wash
tub, crossed-out bleach, dry flat, a low iron and a circle with a P and a bar.

What to check before printing: the shapes are drawn by the author from the
published symbol family, not copied from the standard, and the hand-in-tub is
crude. The starting text comes from the most delicate fibre only, so the
factory's wash test and the current ISO 3758 (or CAN/CGSB-86.1 for Canada) win.

Try it: https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/labels.html
