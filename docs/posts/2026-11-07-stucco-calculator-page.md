The stucco takeoff landed in wall-framing-takeoff earlier today as an engine and a CLI flag. It now has a page of its own: type the house length, width and wall height, a door and window count, the total coat thickness (7/8, 3/4 or a 1/2 in one-coat), rebound and trim length, and it counts the weather barrier, lath sheets and fasteners, plaster bags, and the weep screed, casing bead, corner bead and control joints packed into stock lengths.

For the default 40 x 30 ft house with 8 ft walls, a door and two windows: 1,076 sq ft net of openings, 78.5 cu ft of wet plaster at 7/8 in, 151 bags of 80 lb with 15% rebound, 73 lath sheets, 1,953 fasteners, 2 barrier rolls and 34 pieces of trim in all. Switching to a 1/2 in one-coat build drops the plaster to 86 bags.

Thicknesses, yields, laps and barrier layers are typical figures, not your plaster maker's or your code's; flashing, floor-line joints, color coat and scaffolding are not counted. A test pins the page's FAQ numbers to the engine, and the page was run in headless Chromium with no console errors.
