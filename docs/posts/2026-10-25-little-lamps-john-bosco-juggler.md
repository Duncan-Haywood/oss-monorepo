[Little Lamps](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) has a new story: *John
Bosco and the Boy Who Juggled*, for ages 3 to 8, about ten minutes read aloud.

The source is unusually good for a saint's childhood. In his *Memoirs of the
Oratory*, written in his sixties, Bosco says he taught himself acrobatics and
conjuring from travelling performers, then used the show to gather the
neighbouring children, who prayed and repeated the morning's sermon before he
would perform. The story follows that account, and the grown-ups' note says
which details are his memory and which (the sore nose, the numbers, the
wording) are ours.

It is text only. The site's committed audio is past the 400 MB line the
creative guide sets, so new episodes wait until enclosures move off GitHub
Pages. The story is CC BY-SA 4.0 and says on the page that an AI wrote it.
