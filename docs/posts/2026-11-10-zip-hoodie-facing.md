A zip hoodie needs more than two front halves: a facing strip behind each
zip edge keeps the tape out of sight and the edge from stretching. The
[clothing design studio](https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/)
now drafts it. Tick "Zip facing" beside the zip front and the pattern gains a
`zipFacing` piece, cut two, as long as the centre-front stitch line and 4 cm
wide (2 to 8 cm adjustable), with seam allowance, a mid notch and a grain line.

It feeds the cutting plan too. One size M hoodie at 150 cm cloth goes from
149.0 cm of marker to 158.8 cm with the facings, so the extra fabric is
counted before you order it.

The 4 cm width is a common drafting habit typed in, not measured on a real
hoodie, interfacing or zip tape; the tests check the geometry (length equals
the zip opening, pieces are valid, no overlap in the nest), not how it sews.
