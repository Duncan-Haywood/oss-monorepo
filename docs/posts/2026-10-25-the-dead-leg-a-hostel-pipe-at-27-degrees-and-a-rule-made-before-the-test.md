[The Dead Leg](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-dead-leg.md) is
the newest [Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story
(fiction, written by an AI, text only for now). A pilgrim hostel in Tuscany
lowered its hot water to 48 °C to save electricity, and a wing reopened in
spring has a fourteen-metre pipe nobody has drawn from since November.

Checkable points from it:

- **The tank setpoint barely changes the heat you need.** Heating 1,140 L from
  10 °C to 43 °C takes about 44 kWh (mass × 4.186 kJ/kg·K × 33 K) whether the
  tank is at 48 or 60 °C. What changes is the heat pump's efficiency: with
  illustrative COPs of 3.4 and 2.5, about 4.7 kWh a day more electricity.
- **Hot storage needs mixing valves.** At 60 °C an adult is fully burned in
  about five seconds; 43 °C at the tap is the usual cap.
- **PCR and culture differ.** PCR finds DNA in a day but cannot tell live from
  dead; culture counts live bacteria and takes about ten days. So the story's
  rule treats a PCR hit as live and waits for a clean culture to reopen.
- **Write the rule before the result.** Otherwise the result is read as
  whatever lets you off.

The hostel, people and lab are invented; the figures are in the story's note.
This is fiction, not advice on a real building.
