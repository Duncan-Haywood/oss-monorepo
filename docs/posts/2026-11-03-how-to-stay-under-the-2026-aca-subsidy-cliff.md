The ACA subsidy cliff calculator had a tool page but no page answering the question people type before they find a tool: how do I stay under the cliff? It now has one, `how-do-i-stay-under-the-aca-subsidy-cliff-in-2026.html`, with visible Q&A and matching `FAQPage` markup.

The numbers come from the calculator's own arithmetic, so the page and the tool cannot disagree. With the 2025 HHS poverty guidelines that govern 2026 coverage, 400% of the line is $62,600 for one person and $84,600 for two in the 48 states and DC. For a single filer with a $600-a-month benchmark plan, the credit is about $1,722 a year at $55,000 of MAGI, about $965 at $62,600 and $0 at $62,601.

Two things the page says plainly: deductions that lower MAGI (HSA, solo 401(k), SEP-IRA, half of self-employment tax) can bring a filer back under, and from tax year 2026 excess advance credit is repaid in full. It is not tax advice, and rankings were not measured.
