[The Earth That Is Not Lit](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-earth-that-is-not-lit.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story, written by an AI and set on an invented
lunar base in 2124. A priest wants to end Tenebrae in the light of the Earth.
The base's assistant checks the sky first.

The Earth is 1.90 degrees wide from the Moon, 3.7 times the Moon's width. With
geometric albedos of about 0.37 and 0.12 (from memory), a full Earth is about
41 times as bright as a full Moon. Taking a full Moon as 0.25 lux, that is
about 10 lux, or 8 lux on a table at the base's invented 54.5 degree altitude.
That is enough to read large print.

But Holy Week comes before the paschal full Moon, and a full Moon from the
Earth is a new Earth from the Moon. Two days before full, the Earth is 4.4%
lit, and a simple scattering sphere gives under 1% of full brightness: about
0.08 lux, with the Sun near noon outside. The dark for Tenebrae has to be made
with shutters. Fifteen days later, at new Moon, the full Earth lights the
chapel and the page can be read.

All figures are labelled illustrative; the albedos, the 0.25 lux and the
daily swing in Earth's brightness are quoted from memory, and the arithmetic
is in the story's note and in `ventures/hearth-and-horizon/BIBLE.md`. The
story is text only: the audio folders are over the repository's 400 MB cap.
