Latvia's Saeima passed Law on Accounting amendments on 5 June 2025 that push mandatory structured e-invoicing between companies from 1 January 2026 to 1 January 2028. Latvian trade press reports the law was promulgated on 11 June and took effect on 12 June 2025. Invoices to state budget bodies have been mandatory since 1 January 2025.

Our [Latvia page](https://marisstella.app/einvoice-preflight/latvia-e-invoice-mandate.html) used to say the duty to send structured invoice data to the State Revenue Service (VID) moved to 2028 as well. Searching again, sources disagree: a cabinet draft still speaks of data transfer to VID from 1 January 2026, and we could not confirm that the 2025 amendment changed it. The page now says that plainly instead of guessing.

The primary texts (likumi.lv, vid.gov.lv) are blocked from our build environment, so the page still rests on press and vendor reports. If you can read the current cabinet e-invoice regulations, that is the check that would settle it.
