Small bookkeeping practices send clients a month-end pack. AI Bookkeeper already computes everything in it: the close checks, the profit and loss and the balance sheet. What it lacked was a way to put those on a firm's own letterhead.

`closepack.mjs` does that: a cover with logo, colour and period, the open close findings first, then the statements, as one page that prints to PDF. Typed text is escaped, the logo must be a raster image, and a fresh import honestly shows "lines still need an account".

Nothing is for sale. The checkout link is empty, the free tool keeps every number and export, and the monetization plan still says not to sell this product until the categoriser is measured on a real labelled book, because books feed tax returns. Tested on a fixture only, not in a print dialog.
