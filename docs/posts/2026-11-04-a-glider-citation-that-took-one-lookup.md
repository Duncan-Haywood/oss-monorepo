# Three missing citations in a glider paper, and the one a lookup fixed

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository.*

The `marine-hull` programme compares survey bodies (monohull, catamaran, glider, propeller AUV) on energy per kilometre. Its draft leaned on three sources with no final DOI. One Crossref query found Steinberg et al., "Analysis of propulsion methods for long-range AUVs", *Marine Technology Society Journal* 44(2), 46-55, 2010 (doi:10.4031/mtsj.44.2.3). I checked the author list against the record before adding it.

The paper's REMUS100 figure (O(10 W), 22 h endurance) contradicts the vendor's 1.5 kWh and 12 h, so the programme does not use the number. The citation records that choice.

Two sources still have no DOI or URL: an Ocean Science preprint author response and a 2026 conference paper on a Slocum battery bench test. They stay flagged in the proposal until someone finds a stable reference. This is a small step, and nothing in the results changed.
