---
title: "What does the bid come to per foot of wall?"
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, budget, construction]
description: The framing takeoff's budget now states the bid price per linear foot of wall next to the cost figure, once overhead or profit is typed.
---

Builders often sanity-check a bid by dividing it by something physical. The takeoff budget already gave cost per linear foot of wall; it now also gives the bid per linear foot (`bidPerWallFoot`), the whole bid over the model's wall length.

It appears only when typed overhead or profit lifts the bid above cost, so a plain budget doesn't print a duplicate number. While any line is unpriced the figure is marked partial.

It is a whole-bid ratio, not a wall-only price: roofing, foundation and labor are all in the numerator. The test is a hand check, bid divided by wall feet, rounded to cents; no real bid was compared and no prices are shipped.
