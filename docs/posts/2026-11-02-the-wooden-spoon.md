The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, [The Wooden Spoon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-wooden-spoon.html), is set at a harvest supper in County Mayo in 2048. The stew has no nuts in it, and the cook wants to hang a "nut-free" card on it for an eight-year-old with a peanut allergy.

The hall's assistant asks what else touched the pot. One wooden spoon in the cooking drawer may have stirred the peanut brittle. With invented figures, 50 mg of peanut dust stirred into 20 litres leaves about 1 mg of peanut in a 400 ml bowl, roughly 0.25 mg of protein. That is above the 0.2 mg reference dose used for the most sensitive 1 in 100, and the mixing would be uneven. So the card says what is true: no nuts in the recipe, traces not ruled out. A fresh pan and spoon make her bowl first, the two adrenaline pens are checked, and the story states the emergency rule: give adrenaline early, ring 999 or 112 anyway, and give the second pen after five minutes if she is no better.
