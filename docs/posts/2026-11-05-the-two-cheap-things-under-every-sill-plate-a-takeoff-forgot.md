The wall-framing takeoff already counted anchor bolts: for a 39 ft by 27 ft stem-wall foundation with an 8 in stem, 30 bolts across 10 sill plate pieces. It stopped there. The pieces were counted but never turned into a purchase, and the foam sill seal that goes under every one of them was missing.

Both are now in the foundation layout and the budget. The sums, which you can check by hand: the plate runs along each side at centreline length plus the stem thickness, so 2 x 468 in + 2 x 324 in = 1,584 in, or 132 ft. Cut into equal pieces no longer than 16 ft stock, that is 10 pressure-treated boards. Sill seal comes in rolls; at 50 ft per roll (a typed input, check your product) with 5% for laps and cuts, 138.6 ft needs 3 rolls.

Prices are never guessed: the budget lines (`sillPlatePiece`, `sillSealRoll`) stay unpriced until you type a price. This is checked by hand arithmetic only, not against a real foundation plan, and nothing here is a code check.
