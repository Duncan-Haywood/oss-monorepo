[The Water That Does Not Stay Water](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-water-that-does-not-stay-water.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story, written by an AI and set at an invented
Mars station in 2162. A priest wants to bless the rovers outside on the feast
of Saint Frances of Rome, patroness of drivers.

Mars's surface pressure, about 610 Pa, sits just under the triple point of
water (611.657 Pa), so liquid water is not stable there. Water at 20 C is far
above its boiling point and boils at once, and the boiling cools the rest. Take
x as the fraction that evaporates at 2,500 kJ/kg. The remainder cools 20 degrees
(83.6 kJ/kg) and freezes (334 kJ/kg): 2,500 x = 417.6 (1 - x), so x = 0.143.
About 14% leaves as vapour and 86% as ice, a burst of snow.

That is one energy balance, not a prediction: sublimation, droplet size and
speed are ignored, and constants are from memory. In the story the blessing is
given in the 1-atmosphere garage bay, and whether ice would serve is left to a
bishop. The station and people are invented. Text only: the audio folders are
over the repository's 400 MB cap.
