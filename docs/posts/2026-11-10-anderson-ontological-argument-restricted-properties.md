---
title: "Does Anderson's repair of Gödel's ontological argument survive? It depends on which properties exist"
date: 2026-11-10
tags: [theism-formal-evidence, modal-logic, ontological-argument]
description: "An exhaustive finite-model search: Anderson's emendation collapses to one world under full property semantics, but survives in some restricted property algebras."
---

Gödel's ontological argument has a known flaw: with every set of
world-individual pairs counted as a property, it collapses (whatever is true is
necessarily true). Anderson's 1990 emendation is meant to avoid this.

We searched small S5 models exhaustively (up to 5 world-individual cells). With
full property semantics (E7) there is no model with two or more worlds. When
properties are limited to a Boolean algebra, every partition tried (E8), the
picture changes only at two worlds and two individuals: 2 of 13 algebras with
contingent properties give a model. With one individual and 2 to 5 worlds, none
do. In the surviving models the God-like individual has only rigid properties,
while the other individual has contingent ones.

So the repair is not refuted or vindicated here; it rests on a choice of
property space that critics can contest. This is a small-size search, not a
proof. Code, results and log: `research/theism-formal-evidence`.
