From today, most product, marketing and sales work in this portfolio targets
one audience: owners of small businesses, and the people they hand the work
to. The plan lives in [`portfolio/smb/README.md`](../../portfolio/smb/README.md).

The second half of that sentence is the important part. In a cafe, a salon or
a trade contractor's office, the owner decides and pays, but the weekly work
is often done by someone else: an office manager, a shift lead, a bookkeeper,
an outside accountant. Software sold only to the owner tends to fail in one of
two ways. The owner signs up, hands the password to someone, and loses sight
of what was done. Or the delegate finds the tool, likes it, and can't get the
owner to pay for something the owner never sees.

So every paid app gets the same layer:

- one business with many people in it: owner, staff and outside adviser roles,
  invited by email, no shared passwords;
- an adviser workspace, so a bookkeeper with many clients runs all of them
  from one login;
- a weekly owner digest of what was done and what is waiting for approval;
- delegates prepare, owners approve anything that moves money or goes to a
  customer or a regulator.

The adviser workspace is also the best sales channel we have found on paper.
One bookkeeper who adopts a tool brings it to every client. That is a single
conversation instead of many cold ones.

## How the paid products sort

We read the descriptions of the 20 products on the paid track and sorted
them. This is a reading of what they say they do, not of who uses them.
Usage is unmeasured.

- **Small-business back office, first in line:** Grant Autopilot, AI
  Bookkeeper, sales-export-to-journal (as the bookkeeper's import layer),
  e-invoice preflight, Shift Roster Builder, Local Site Studio and the
  babysitting business kit.
- **Small firms in a trade:** the framing, shed, IFC and P6 schedule tools for
  small builders and contractors, Listing Lens for solo agents and small
  brokerages, the photo and media pipelines for small studios, and the
  clothing and print tools for independent brands.
- **Not small-business first:** the changepoint detector, the pairwise survey
  engine and the league scheduler. They keep their users.

## How we will find them

There are four plays, all inside CAN-SPAM, GDPR and PECR, with a real sender
and a working unsubscribe:

1. pages by trade and by job that answer the owner's exact search;
2. outbound to one trade at a time, timed to a trigger the business made
   public (a hiring post, a new location, an open grant round);
3. partner seats for bookkeepers, accountants and VAs;
4. the Local Site Studio preview as the first thing an owner sees from us.

The file will record what each play returns, measured and dated. No reading
exists yet, and none will be made up.
