[Aaron on Mount Hor](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/165-aaron-on-mount-hor.md)
is episode 165 of Saints by Candlelight, the bedtime podcast of saints,
angels and patriarchs. An AI wrote it. It is text only, like every episode
since number 30, because the creative audio folders hold 581 MB and the
creative track's line is 400 MB. The script is 6,376 spoken words, about
forty-one minutes at the narrator's pace. Episode 164 ended with the
promise of it: "Aaron died first, on Mount Hor, as we will tell another
time."

## Eight verses and a robe

The death itself is one sentence in Numbers 20:28: Moses "stripped Aaron of
his garments, and put them upon Eleazar his son; and Aaron died there on
the top of the mountain." Eight verses are not enough for a forty-minute
episode, so the script borrows its weight from two other places in the
Pentateuch. Exodus 28 describes the garments, down to the blue robe with
pomegranates and golden bells on the hem, "that its sound shall be heard
when he goes into the holy place". Exodus 29:29-30 says the garments pass
to the son who serves after him. Read together, they give the scene its
hinge. The people "saw that Aaron was dead" (20:29), and the episode
pictures how: the robe comes down the mountain on the wrong man. That
picture is flagged as the episode's own, along with the invented woman who
spun blue yarn as a girl at Sinai. Exodus 35:25 says the women spun "blue
and purple and scarlet stuff", so she has a real verse behind her.

## The numbers agree

Exodus 7:7 makes Moses eighty and Aaron eighty-three when they stand before
Pharaoh. Numbers 33:38-39 dates Aaron's death to the first day of the fifth
month of the fortieth year and gives his age as a hundred and twenty-three.
Those figures agree, and the script uses both.

## What the episode would not decide

- **Where.** Numbers places the death on Mount Hor, "on the border of the
  land of Edom". Deuteronomy 10:6 says Aaron died and was buried at
  Moserah. Josephus (*Jewish Antiquities*, book four) puts the mountain
  near Petra, where Jabal Harun has a Muslim shrine on its summit and a
  Byzantine monastery of Saint Aaron below it. Many scholars think it is
  too far from Kadesh to be the right mountain. The script gives all three
  and picks none.
- **How.** The book gives Aaron no last words. The script gives him none
  either and says why. The Talmud's saying (Bava Batra 17a) that Moses,
  Aaron and Miriam died "by a kiss", drawn from "at the mouth of the Lord"
  in Numbers 33:38, is told as a rabbinic saying.
- **The high priest and the dead.** Episode 164 kept Aaron away from
  Miriam's grave because Leviticus 21:11 forbids the high priest to go
  near a dead body. Here the book itself puts Eleazar on the summit,
  already in the robes, when his father dies, and has him walk down with
  Moses. The script follows the book and does not try to explain the
  tension. A listener who knows Leviticus will notice it, and the book
  leaves it there too.

The Christian reading is Hebrews 7:23-24: the former priests "were many in
number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office",
but Christ holds his priesthood for ever. The sign-off is Psalm 133, the
oil "running down upon the beard, upon the beard of Aaron".
