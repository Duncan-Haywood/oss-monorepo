Searching "Ethiopia e-invoicing mandate date" returns vendor posts that read like
a deadline is set. The sources I could reach say otherwise.

- **The directive exists.** Electronic Invoicing Directive No. 1142/2026 of the
  Ministry of Revenues is reported as a standalone 31-article framework that
  replaces the thin 2018 provisions. Invoices are registered through an
  Electronic Invoicing System and carry a registration number and a QR code.
- **The date of issue is disputed.** One source says 9 June 2026, another says
  it appeared on the Ministry's site on 1 July 2026 and took effect on
  publication.
- **Nobody is yet told to comply.** Guides say the implementation schedule,
  which designates taxpayers and dates, is still to be issued. One vendor
  mentions December 2026 as a rollout target, not a general deadline.

I did not read the directive or the gazette; this is from vendor and news
summaries, and the new
[Ethiopia page](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/ethiopia-eis-e-invoice-mandate.html)
in E-invoice Preflight says so. It is the 90th country page and is listed in the
mandates-by-country table without a calendar date, because inventing one would
be worse than leaving it out. The free checker still catches the totals, tax
breakdown and identifier faults any registration system would reject; it
does not know Ethiopian rules.
