The site's "for" pages gather tools by who uses them. This week they grew past trades into
stages of life: [retirees](https://marisstella.app/c/for-retirees/),
[parents](https://marisstella.app/c/for-parents/) and
[people changing jobs](https://marisstella.app/c/for-job-changers/). Yesterday the household tools
also got their own section, which brought one more group into view. Homeowners had thirteen tools
on the site and no page that put them together.

[Homeowners & buyers](https://marisstella.app/c/for-homeowners/) lists them:

- **Before you buy:** the [rent vs buy calculator](https://marisstella.app/rent-vs-buy-breakeven-calculator/)
  (break-even year, with the down payment invested instead) and the
  [mortgage points calculator](https://marisstella.app/mortgage-points-break-even-calculator/)
  (how long you must keep the loan for the points to pay back).
- **While you own it:** the [escrow analysis checker](https://marisstella.app/mortgage-escrow-analysis-checker/),
  [extra payments vs investing](https://marisstella.app/mortgage-extra-payment-vs-invest-calculator/),
  the UK [mortgage overpayment calculator](https://marisstella.app/uk-mortgage-overpayment-calculator/),
  [HELOC vs cash-out refinance](https://marisstella.app/heloc-vs-cash-out-refinance-calculator/) and the
  [property tax appeal calculator](https://marisstella.app/property-tax-appeal-calculator/).
- **Upgrades:** [solar payback](https://marisstella.app/solar-payback-calculator/),
  [heat pump vs furnace](https://marisstella.app/heat-pump-vs-furnace-calculator/),
  [ADU payback](https://marisstella.app/adu-payback-calculator/), and an
  [HOA reserve fund projection](https://marisstella.app/hoa-reserve-fund-calculator/) for condo boards.
- **Selling:** [net proceeds](https://marisstella.app/home-sale-net-proceeds-calculator/) and the
  [home sale exclusion](https://marisstella.app/home-sale-exclusion-calculator/) (how much of the gain is taxed).

## What stayed off

The site also has contractor bid calculators for solar, furnaces and standby generators. Those are
for the installer pricing the job, not the person paying for it, so they stay on the trade pages.
Landlord tools such as house-hack and rental DSCR stay on
[Landlords & hosts](https://marisstella.app/c/for-landlords/). A test pins both lists, so a new
`solar-...-bid-calculator` does not land on the homeowner page by accident.

## Renters, not yet

A renters page would be a good search target, but today it would list three tools: the
[roommate rent splitter](https://marisstella.app/roommate-rent-splitter/), the tenant deposit
deadline checker and rent vs buy. The site doesn't publish an audience page with fewer than four
tools, because a "free tools for renters" heading over three links is a thin page and a poor answer
to the person who searched. It waits for a fourth real tool.
