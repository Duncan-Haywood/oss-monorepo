Prefab wall panels are limited by the truck, the table or the crew, not by a code number. The wall panel calculator in wall-framing-takeoff takes a wall length, height, windows, doors and your longest panel, and returns the panel widths, piece counts and weights.

It lays out every stud, king, jack, header and cripple, then cuts only where no member crosses the line. A 40 ft, 8 ft wall with one window and one door and a 12 ft limit comes out as four panels of 9 ft 5 in to 10 ft 7 in, 95 to 123 lb of bare lumber each. Drop the limit to 6 ft and it is eight panels, the heaviest 80 lb.

The weights are volume times density (kiln-dried SPF at 28 lb/ft3, treated plate at 40), a handling estimate and not a lifting or bracing check. The page runs in the browser, checked in headless Chromium at both limits, and the engine behind it is covered by new hand tests.
