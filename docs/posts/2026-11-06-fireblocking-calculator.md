Tall walls need horizontal blocks so a stud cavity is no taller than the limit your code or inspector sets. Counting them by hand means walking every bay. The new page in wall-framing-takeoff does it from the same wall layout as the whole-house takeoff: rows per wall, one block per bay cut to the clear gap, packed into boards with the saw kerf.

Worked example from the page: three 147 in walls at a 10 ft limit need 27 blocks and 8 boards (44.00 at 5.50 a board); tighten the limit to 5 ft and it is 54 blocks and 12 boards. Both figures are pinned to the engine by a test.

The limit is the number you type, not a code lookup, and ceilings, soffits, stairs, chases and penetrations are not counted. Invented walls only; no real house or inspector has checked it. Free, in your browser, MIT licensed: `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/fireblock-calculator.html`.
