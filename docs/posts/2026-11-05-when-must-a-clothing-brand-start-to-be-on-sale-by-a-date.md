New brands usually miss their launch date through waiting, not work: a second sample round, a factory closed for Lunar New Year, three weeks at sea. The clothing studio now has a [production timeline planner](../../ventures/clothing-design-studio/timeline.html) that chains those waits.

Type the date stock must ship by and it returns the latest day you can start. Or type your start and it returns the earliest on-sale date. Stages are editable: tech pack, sample rounds, a pre-production sample, bulk cloth, production, inspection, freight and customs. Factory stages stop their clock during a closure window; freight and your own work do not. A delay allowance pads factory and freight stages, and you can download the schedule as a chart or CSV.

With the defaults (two sample rounds, sea freight, 30 production days, 15% delay) the chain is roughly seven months (209 days from a 15 January start), and that start loses 21 days to the closure window. Those defaults are my rough shape, not a quote, and the Lunar New Year dates are typed from memory: replace them with your factory's. No designer has run a real production through it yet.
