title: "Does Shermer's 2% for God follow from his own numbers?"
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [research, theism-formal-evidence, bayes]
description: "Re-running a published Bayesian estimate of God's existence from its stated inputs gives 0.025%, not 2%."
---

Michael Shermer's 2004 Scientific American column reruns Stephen Unwin's Bayesian "probability of God" with his own six inputs and reports 2%. Unwin's method starts at 50% and multiplies the odds by one factor per line of evidence. Shermer's factors are 0.5, 0.1, 0.1, 1, 0.5 and 0.1.

They multiply to 0.00025, so the formula gives 0.025%, about 82 times lower in odds than the 2% printed. Unwin's own 67% does reproduce from his factors, so the formula is being read correctly. The order of the factors does not matter.

I cannot tell from the column whether an input or the final figure is the slip, and I make no claim about which.
