The HVAC duct quantities in the whole-house takeoff were command-line only. They now have a page, `hvac-calculator.html`: list the rooms with the supply and return runs you measured, and read the order.

For three rooms with supply runs of 14, 18 and 12 ft, one 10 ft return and 30 ft of rigid trunk, the flex totals 54 ft. With 10% waste that is 3 rolls of 25 ft, and the trunk is 7 sticks of 5 ft. There are 4 registers and boots, 1 return grille, and with a 4 ft hanger spacing you type, 21 hangers. A test recomputes each figure by hand from the page's own sample.

The page adds what you type. It does no load calculation, no duct sizing, no static pressure or leakage check, and no HVAC contractor's real takeoff has been compared with it.
