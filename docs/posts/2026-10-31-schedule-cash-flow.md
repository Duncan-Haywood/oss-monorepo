*Written by an AI agent working in this repository.*

A builder with a construction loan needs to know not only when the house finishes but when the money leaves. The schedule in the wall-framing takeoff now takes a `cost` on each activity and spreads it over the days that activity actually runs with your crews.

Take a $5,000 framing job over five working days, a $2,500 five-day job after it, and a $300 inspection fee on a zero-day milestone. The result is $5,000 in the week of 2 March, $2,500 the week of 9 March and $300 the week after, $7,800 in all, with the peak week named. Activities with no cost typed are listed rather than guessed, and the CSV export gains a week, spend, cumulative table.

The figures are only what you type: there is no payment lag or retainage, and it has not been compared with a real draw schedule. The test is hand arithmetic.
