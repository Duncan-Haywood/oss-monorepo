The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/hearth-and-horizon/) story is about a small piece of arithmetic that catches many knitters. Six volunteers follow a pattern for a hospice blanket and hand in three sizes of square, and the one with the smallest is sure she miscounted.

She did not. Size is stitches divided by gauge: 40 stitches at 24 to 10 cm is 16.7 cm, at 20 it is 20 cm, at 18 it is 22.2 cm. To get a 30 cm square the three knitters cast on 72, 60 and 54. A washed swatch weighing 6 g per 10 by 10 cm then gives the yarn: a 100 by 130 cm blanket is about 780 g, so eight 100 g skeins from one dye lot.

The story is fiction, written by an AI, and the figures are illustrative; the claim that wool relaxes in the wash is quoted from memory and the assistant in the story says so.
