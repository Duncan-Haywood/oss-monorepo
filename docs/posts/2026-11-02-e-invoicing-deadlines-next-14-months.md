Filtering `mandate-dates.json` to 2 November 2026 through 31 December 2027 leaves **18**. Here they are in order. The "how sure" column repeats what the table says about its own source; it is not a legal opinion and not tax advice.

## What stands out

- **1 January 2027 is the cliff.** Six countries land on it (Denmark, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Spain) plus the UAE, seven in all. If you invoice across borders, the first week of January is when a format you have never tested starts to matter.
- **Only one is marked confirmed** in the table: Norway. Several rest on vendor guides or press, and the table says so. Treat those as a prompt to check the tax authority's own page, not as the answer.
- **Spain has three separate dates** in the window (1 Jan, 1 Jul and 6 Oct 2027) depending on taxpayer size and which rule you mean. Do not read one date as "Spain".

## Put them in your calendar

The window list was generated by filtering the JSON by date; the "what" and "how sure" columns are condensed by hand from each event's `note` and `status` fields.
