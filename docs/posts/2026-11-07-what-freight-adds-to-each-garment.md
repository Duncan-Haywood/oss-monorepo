A small clothing brand's landed cost has a line that is usually a guess: freight. Carriers bill the larger of what a carton weighs and the room it takes, and folded hoodies are light and bulky, so the bill follows the size.

The Clothing Design Studio now has a [freight calculator](https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/freight.html). Type the folded piece, the carton and the rates from a forwarder's quote. It counts cartons, finds the billable weight and shows sea LCL, a full container, air and express per garment. In the example (1,000 tees, 39 cartons, 2.81 cbm, 297 kg) air is billed on 468 kg of volume weight, not 297 kg, and costs 2.34 a piece against 0.34 by sea. It then asks the question that matters: air lands 29 days sooner for 2,003 more, so it pays if a week of selling earns over 483, counting lost sales only.

No rate is built in as a fact; they are yours to type. The tests recompute the carton packing and every bill by hand. No real forwarder quote has gone through it yet.
