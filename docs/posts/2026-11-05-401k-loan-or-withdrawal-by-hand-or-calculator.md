# 401(k) loan or withdrawal: when a spreadsheet is enough

Tax and penalty on a withdrawal are easy to gross up by hand. Three things are easier to forget: the growth the money
would have earned, the plan loan limit (greater of $10,000 or 50% of the vested balance, capped at $50,000), and what
happens to an open loan if you leave the job. The free [401(k) loan vs withdrawal calculator](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/401k-loan-vs-withdrawal-calculator/)
now has a [comparison page](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/401k-loan-vs-withdrawal-calculator/vs-working-it-out-by-hand.html)
that lays this out. In its placeholder example ($15,000, 20 years, 6% growth) the loan costs about $34,300 and the
withdrawal about $61,100; your numbers will differ. Not tax or financial advice.
