The Australia GST registration checker now opens with a plain answer: register
when GST turnover reaches AUD 75,000 (AUD 150,000 for non-profit bodies), now
or projected over 12 months, and you have 21 days; taxi and ride-share drivers
register from the first fare. It names the ATO's "Registering for GST" page and
the GST Act sections, and says the page was not re-fetched on the date given, so
the reader confirms there.

A test now fails if the thresholds in the sentence drift from the ones the
calculator uses. This is a first read, not tax advice.
