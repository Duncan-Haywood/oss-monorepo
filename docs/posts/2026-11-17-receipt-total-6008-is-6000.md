[ai-bookkeeper](../../ventures/ai-bookkeeper/README.md) reads a receipt photo four times with
tesseract and lets the receipt's own arithmetic pick the total. The last run found that our
photo rules had been tuned to the same 100 validation photos, and moved development to
CORD's train photos. This run read the remaining 600 train photos, none seen before.

**The unseen rate holds.** Through the command line, 356 of 583 labelled totals came out
right (61.1%). Shard 0 read 59% and the held-out test set 58%. So about 60% is the honest
rate for a phone photo of an Indonesian receipt with a free OCR engine and no model.

**Most misses can't be fixed by the parser.** For 148 of the 227 misses, the right total's
digits are not in any of the four readings. Last time's idea for those photos was to read
the bottom of the photo again on its own. We tried it: a reading of the bottom half found the
total on 5 of 149 photos, and the bottom third on 4. These totals are lost to blur, folds and
camera angle, not to text above them. We dropped the crop.

**Two things the parser could fix:**

- *A total ending in 00 and one more digit.* "Total 6.008", "Total 31.508", "Total 43.006":
  tesseract read the last 0 as an 8, 6, 9 or 3. Of CORD's 972 labelled totals, 8 end that way
  and 604 end in 000. When some reading prints the round amount on any line, we now take it
  and add a note asking for a check. One of the six photos it fixed was different: it really
  printed "Before Rounding 110,003" above a total of 110,000. A real total of 6,008 stays
  6,008 unless a reading shows 6,000.
- *The page undid its own result.* When the readings settled on a total that had no line of
  its own (say, from the arithmetic), the page wrote `Total 110000.00` into the receipts box.
  On a rupiah receipt that reads back as a different amount, so the page fell back to
  "the readings disagree" and showed the first reading's wrong total. It now writes
  `Total 110,000` when that reads back correctly. That one change gave the page 14 more
  correct totals on these photos.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 600 new train photos, command line | 356 / 583 | 362 |
| 600 new train photos, page | 341 / 583 | 357 |
| validation photos, page | 67 / 98 | 70 |
| held-out test photos, command line / page (scored once) | 55 / 53 of 95 | 56 / 55 |

Nothing was lost on any set, and the CORD text sets did not change. The held-out gain is
small but points the same way as the development gain. The page now trails the command line
by 5 totals, not 15.

What's left is mostly OCR: a second engine or a model reading the 148 photos tesseract
can't. Data, commands and every changed receipt are in the
[evaluation note](../../ventures/ai-bookkeeper/eval/cord-train-shards-2026-11-17.md). CORD
is Clova AI's dataset (CC BY 4.0). The photos are read at run time and not copied into the
repository.

ai-bookkeeper is a bookkeeping aid, not tax or accounting advice. Check any total it flags.
