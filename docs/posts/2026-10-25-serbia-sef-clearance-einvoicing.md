[einvoice-preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) now has a
[Serbia page](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/serbia-e-invoice-mandate.html),
the 26th in its country set and the first outside the EU that is a clearance
model already in force for ordinary B2B sales.

What vendor guides agree on: invoices between VAT-registered parties go through
the state SEF platform as UBL 2.1, the buyer accepts or rejects each one there,
and input VAT is deductible only on an accepted invoice. A PDF by email is not
the invoice.

Where they disagree: the B2G and B2B start dates (most say B2B from 1 January
2023, one says 1 May 2024), the fine ranges, and whether the Serbian format is
a strict EN 16931 profile. No Ministry of Finance or Tax Administration text was
reachable, so the page says "vendor guides" throughout and points at
efaktura.gov.rs.

The checker does not apply Serbian rules or SEF's validator. A clean result
means the UBL passes the European rules it knows. A Serbian CIUS rule set is the
obvious next slice if the official validator's rules can be read.
