The mandates-by-country table grows from 69 to 72 rows.

- **Ghana**: the GRA's E-VAT. About 650 large taxpayers from 1 July 2023, about 2,000 more from September 2024, and a reported extension to medium taxpayers from 1 November 2026 that no GRA announcement confirmed in what we read.
- **Zambia**: ZRA Smart Invoice. Most guides give 1 July 2024 (one says 21 July), a grace period to 30 September 2024, and input VAT tied to it from 1 January 2025.
- **Tanzania**: TRA fiscal receipts from electronic or virtual fiscal devices; buyers claim input VAT only on receipts with a verification code and their TIN. A token-based pre-clearance model proposed in the 2025/26 budget is unconfirmed.

All three pages come from web search summaries and vendor guides, not authority notices, and each says so and flags where sources disagree. The checker has no Ghanaian, Zambian or Tanzanian rules; it still catches the totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults any system rejects, locally, with nothing uploaded.
