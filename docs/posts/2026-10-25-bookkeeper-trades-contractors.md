A trades business's bank statement mixes deposits from field-service apps with
bills from supply houses, tool stores and fuel. [ai-bookkeeper](../../ventures/ai-bookkeeper/README.md)
left Jobber and Housecall Pro deposits, Ferguson and Johnstone Supply parts and
ServiceTitan blank. Keyword rules now cover them.

On a synthetic August (made up, not a real contractor's books) all eleven lines
get a suggestion with nothing taught: $4,755.50 of sales, $1,186.19 of
supplies and materials, $245.00 of software, $96.00 of lead marketing, $71.30
of fuel, $135.00 of permits and $3,022.01 profit. The rules come from company
names, so a real export will find gaps; that is the next thing to measure. A
[landing page](../../ventures/ai-bookkeeper/trades-bookkeeping.html) walks through the month.
