The site has "for" pages that gather tools by who uses them: [roofers](https://marisstella.app/c/for-roofers/),
[landlords](https://marisstella.app/c/for-landlords/), and since this morning
[retirees](https://marisstella.app/c/for-retirees/). Most are trades. Two groups of people had a
good set of tools on the site and no page that put them together.

## Parents and families

[Parents & families](https://marisstella.app/c/for-parents/) lists nine tools: the
[Child Tax Credit](https://marisstella.app/child-tax-credit-calculator/) and
[EITC](https://marisstella.app/eitc-calculator/) calculators, the
[dependent care FSA vs credit](https://marisstella.app/dependent-care-fsa-vs-credit-calculator/)
comparison, the [nanny tax calculator](https://marisstella.app/nanny-tax-calculator/), the
[529 to Roth rollover](https://marisstella.app/529-to-roth-rollover-calculator/), and four UK tools:
[Statutory Maternity Pay](https://marisstella.app/uk-statutory-maternity-pay-calculator/),
[Shared Parental Pay](https://marisstella.app/uk-shared-parental-pay-planner/),
[Tax-Free Childcare](https://marisstella.app/uk-tax-free-childcare-calculator/) and the
[High Income Child Benefit Charge](https://marisstella.app/uk-child-benefit-charge-calculator/).

The tools that sell childcare (a daycare's break-even, a party business's packages, a babysitting
kit) stay on the [childcare providers](https://marisstella.app/c/for-childcare/) page. A test now
pins that split, so a later slug rule can't drift a business tool onto a parents' page.

## Changing or losing a job

[Changing or losing a job](https://marisstella.app/c/for-job-changers/) lists eight: the
[severance package calculator](https://marisstella.app/severance-package-calculator/),
[COBRA vs marketplace](https://marisstella.app/cobra-vs-marketplace-calculator/), a
[401(k) loan vs early withdrawal](https://marisstella.app/401k-loan-vs-withdrawal-calculator/), the
[final paycheck deadline](https://marisstella.app/final-paycheck-deadline-calculator/) by state,
statutory redundancy pay in [Great Britain](https://marisstella.app/uk-redundancy-pay-calculator/) and
[Australia](https://marisstella.app/au-redundancy-pay-calculator/), a
[job offer comparison](https://marisstella.app/job-offer-total-comp-calculator/) and a
[job application tracker](https://marisstella.app/job-application-tracker/).

The page opens with the order a person who has just been laid off should probably go in: the
severance offer and the final paycheck date first, because both have deadlines, then COBRA against
a marketplace plan before the election window closes.

## What this is and isn't

No new calculator was written. These pages are an index: a title and description written for the
query ("free calculators for a layoff, severance or a new job offer"), a short lead, and links. Each
tool page now links back to the audience pages it belongs to. We have no traffic numbers for these
pages yet, so we can't say whether anyone finds them.
