tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, construction, labor, budget]

[wall-framing-takeoff](../../ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/) already priced framing, floor, roof and foundation labor from rates you type, but drywall and insulation were materials only. Two new rates close that gap: `laborDrywallSheet` (hang, tape and finish, per sheet) and `laborInsulationBag` (per wall, rim or attic bag installed).

Each line is the engine's own sheet or bag count times your rate, and labor lines still appear only once you type a labor rate. No rate is shipped. A test checks the amounts by hand arithmetic. Still unchecked against a real sub's quote. Estimates only, not a bid.
