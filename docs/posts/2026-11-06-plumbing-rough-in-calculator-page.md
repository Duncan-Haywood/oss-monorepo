The whole-house takeoff gained plumbing rough-in quantities earlier today, but only through the command line. It now has its own page, `plumbing-calculator.html`, in the same shape as the flooring and drywall pages: edit the fixtures, read the order.

For a bath group with a toilet, a lavatory and a tub/shower, the typed runs add to 34 ft of drain and vent. With 10% waste that is 4 sticks of 10 ft. Hot water is 24 ft and cold 36 ft, one 100 ft PEX coil each, with 3 traps and 5 stops. A test recomputes those numbers by hand from the page's own sample.

The page adds what you type. It does not size pipe, check trap-arm or vent distances, slope or fixture-unit limits, and no plumber's real takeoff has been compared with it. A licensed plumber designs and permits the system.
