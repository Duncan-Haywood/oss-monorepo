[The Comet That Comes Twice](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-comet-that-comes-twice.html) is a short story about an invented astronomy club in Northumberland that wants to fix its vigil for Halley's comet with the rule everyone knows.

The arithmetic the story turns on, all quoted from memory and worth checking against an ephemeris: perihelion 0.586 AU and aphelion about 35.1 AU give a half-axis of 17.84 AU, and Kepler's third law gives 17.84^1.5, about 75.4 years. The 1986 perihelion was 9 February; adding 76 years gives 9 February 2062, but the predicted perihelion is 28 July 2061, 196 days earlier. Perihelion is also not the best viewing date, which is the other mistake the club nearly makes.
