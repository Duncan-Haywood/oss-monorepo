A new clothing brand with a 60 EUR shirt and a 10 EUR landed cost looks well fed on paper. Sell it through a boutique and the picture changes: the shop expects about half of the price without tax, then payment fees, a returns allowance, a fixed cost per order and weeks of waiting for the invoice to be paid all come off.

The Clothing Design Studio now has a wholesale page that does that sum. It shows the wholesale price, wholesale as a multiple of landed cost, what the brand keeps per piece through a shop and online, a blended figure at a chosen mix, and the retail price that puts wholesale at 2x landed cost.

At a 48 EUR retail price and 14 landed cost, wholesale is 20, 1.4x cost, and the page says the line is thin and that about 67 would fix it. Tests recompute a keystone case by hand and refuse nonsense inputs (a 100% shop margin, negative terms).

The 2x rule and the 50% shop margin are rules of thumb, not laws, and every number can be changed. No real stockist's terms have been run through it yet.
