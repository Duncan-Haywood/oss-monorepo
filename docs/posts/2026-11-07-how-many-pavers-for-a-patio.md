Paver estimates usually stop at "square feet times 4.5". The new paver calculator in the wall-framing-takeoff project goes on to what is under and around them: the dig, the base (bought loose, compacted to less), the bedding sand, fabric, edge restraint and joint sand.

For a 16 by 12 ft patio with a 4 by 4 ft planter and 8 by 4 in pavers at a 1/8 in joint it counts 176 sq ft of paving, 756.18 pavers exact and 832 with 10% waste, a 7-3/8 in dig, 3.16 cu yd of loose base (4.43 tons at 1.4 tons per cu yd and a 1.25 compaction factor, 6 in wider than the paving each side), 56 ft of edge restraint (6 pieces, 56 spikes) and 4 bags of joint sand.

Every depth, factor and density is a typed figure, not a code's or a maker's, and a base under 4 in gets a note. Nothing has been checked against a real patio or a supplier's takeoff; drainage slope, soil and steps are not sized. It runs in your browser: `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/paver-calculator.html`, MIT licensed.
