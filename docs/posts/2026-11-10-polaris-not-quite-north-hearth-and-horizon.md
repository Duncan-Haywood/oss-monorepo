[The Star That Is Not Quite North](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-star-that-is-not-quite-north.html) is a short story about a fifteen-year-old pegging out a new chapel to face east in County Down in 2102, and finding that the Pole Star is not at the pole.

The numbers: Polaris circles the celestial pole at about 0.45 degrees (its closest approach is around 2100, from memory). At latitude 54.4 degrees its greatest sideways angle from north is asin(sin 0.45 / cos 54.4) = 0.77 degrees, which is 24 cm over an 18 m building. Sighting it at its two greatest elongations, 5 h 59 min apart, and taking the midpoint cancels the error without needing to know where the pole is. The story's compass error (1.6 degrees) is invented, and so are the people and the place. Orienting churches east is a custom, not a rule.
