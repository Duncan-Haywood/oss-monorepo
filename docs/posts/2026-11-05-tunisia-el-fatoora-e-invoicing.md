The mandates-by-country table grows from 85 to 86 rows.

- **Model**: invoices go through the government-run Tunisie TradeNet (TTN) platform as TEIF XML with the issuer's qualified electronic signature. Onboarding needs a qualified certificate from ANCE and an El Fatoora subscription (EDICOM, Idaraty).
- **Scope**: guides describe a phase-in from B2G and some B2B to large enterprises, then VAT-liable services from 1 January 2026 under the 2026 Finance Law. They differ on the enactment date and on enforcement for smaller firms.
- **Left out on purpose**: penalty amounts, because the guides disagree and the Finance Laws were not read.

The page says it was written from search summaries, not the Finance Laws, DGI notices or the TTN portal, and that the checker has no TEIF rules. For UBL or CII it still catches totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults locally. The mandate calendar gained two dated entries.
