# The soup for ninety

A recipe for twelve becomes a recipe for ninety by multiplying by 7.5: 4.2 litres of
stock becomes 31.5, and the heat needed (about 2.6 kWh) takes 75 minutes on a 3 kW ring.
That part scales cleanly. The salt arguably should not, and the cooling certainly does
not: a pot 33 centimetres deep keeps its middle warm far longer than a tray 5 centimetres
deep, because heat has further to travel and the time grows roughly with the square of that distance.

[The Soup for Ninety](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-soup-for-ninety.md)
is fiction with arithmetic a reader can check. The efficiency, the salt habit and the
temperature window are labelled assumptions or quoted from memory, and the story defers to
