# The six percent that was eleven

A parish minibus costs 18,000. "6% over five years" can mean 6% a year on the balance you
still owe, which gives 347.99 a month and 2,879 of interest. Or it can mean 6% a year on the
original 18,000 for all five years, which gives 390 a month and 5,400 of interest. The second
only looks like 6%: the monthly rate that makes 390 clear the loan in 60 months is about
0.904%, roughly 10.85% a year (11.4% effective), because you keep paying interest on money
you have already repaid.
