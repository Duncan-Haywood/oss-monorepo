A capsule wardrobe is a small set of pieces chosen to combine. The wardrobe
planner in the Clothing Design Studio counts the combinations by a stated rule:
every top with every bottom, with or without a layer, at most one accent colour,
and no layer the same colour as the top. A default 12-piece capsule gives 39
outfits under that rule.

A number is hard to show a customer, so the planner now has a "Download
lookbook" button. It picks up to 24 of those outfits, chosen to use as many
different pieces as possible, and draws each as flat colour blocks with the
pieces named and marked as the brand's own design or a partner brand's. It is
one HTML file with no scripts or trackers. Host it anywhere, and add https
product links for the pieces you sell.

The limits are plain: colour blocks show how colours sit together, not fit or
drape, and outerwear and accessories are listed rather than drawn. Product links
appear only where you typed one.

Try it at the studio's wardrobe page; the code is MIT licensed in the
repository under `ventures/clothing-design-studio`.
