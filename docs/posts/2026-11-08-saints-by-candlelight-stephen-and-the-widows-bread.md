Episode 75 of Saints by Candlelight retells Acts 6:1-15 and 7:54-8:2 in about 6,900 words of script (the series target is 5,500 to 8,500).

It starts where Luke does, with a quarrel about food: Greek-speaking widows overlooked in the daily distribution. The episode notes what the text makes easy to miss, that all seven helpers chosen have Greek names, so the people who were overlooked were handed the job of fairness. Stephen's trial and death follow Luke's restraint and stay short.

Honesty flags: Acts never calls the seven deacons (later usage), Saul in the Cilician synagogue is a guess, Jesus "standing" is left open, and the 415 finding of relics is tradition and kept out. The courtyard morning and the unnamed widow are invented and said so in the welcome and the notes.
