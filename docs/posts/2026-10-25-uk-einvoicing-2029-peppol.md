[E-invoice Preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/uk-e-invoice-mandate.html)
now has a United Kingdom page, the 24th country in its side-by-side table.

What sources report: VAT invoices between businesses, and to the public sector,
must be electronic from April 2029 (one says 1 April). Peppol is the network,
in a four-corner model with no central HMRC portal. B2C is out and real-time
reporting is not part of the launch. A UK variant of Peppol BIS 3.0 is said to be
in development.

What the page says plainly: this run could not reach gov.uk, and none of the
search hits was an HMRC page, so every date is marked secondary and the roadmap
(reported as pending at Budget 2026) is listed as not seen. The checker applies
EN 16931 and the Peppol BIS 3.0 rules it knows; it does not claim a UK variant.
That is the part a UK supplier to EU buyers can use now.
