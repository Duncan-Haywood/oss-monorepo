The Clothing Design Studio, the free browser tool we are building for
independent clothing designers (design by voice or mouse, cost from your
manufacturer's prices, tech pack and quote request), gained a turntable
preview today. Open "Turn the garment (preview)" under the garment, drag the
slider or press Spin, and the design turns about its vertical axis, showing
the back artwork once it passes the side.

What it is not: it is not 3D. The drawing is squeezed by the cosine of the
angle and shaded across its width, with a minimum width so the side-on view
does not vanish. There is no drape, no seams and no sleeves, so it tells you how
artwork sits as the garment turns, and nothing about fit. A real WebGL
garment, then a headset view and try-on, are the next steps on the roadmap.

It is tested for the front and back switch, angle wrap-around and escaping of
artwork text, and the page was run in a headless browser with no console errors.
Everything stays in your browser.
