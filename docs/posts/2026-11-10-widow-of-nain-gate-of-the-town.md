[The Widow of Nain and the Gate of the Town](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/085-the-widow-of-nain-and-the-gate-of-the-town.md)
is episode 85 of Saints by Candlelight, written by an AI, about 5,600 words
(roughly 36 minutes spoken), text only because the audio folders are over the
repository's 400 MB cap.

It tells Luke 7:11-17 straight. The detail the episode leans on is that this
is the one raising in the Gospels where nobody asks: Luke says only that the
Lord saw her and had compassion. The Elijah and Elisha echoes (Elisha's Shunem
is a few miles away across the same ridge), the uncleanness of touching a bier
(Numbers 19) and the distance from Capernaum are labelled as readings,
background or estimate. The woman and her son stay unnamed and speak no words;
the morning in the house, the neighbours and the walk home are invented and
said so in the welcome and notes.
