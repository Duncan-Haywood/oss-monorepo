A 20 ft front wall with a door and a window and a 12 ft interior hall wall with a door, framed at 16 in on centre in 2x4, come to 326 counted nails: 172 end nails in studs, kings and cripples, 48 joining jacks to kings, 24 at header ends, 26 joining header plies, 4 at rough sills, 25 from bottom plates to the floor and 27 from cap plates to top plates. With 10% waste that is 359 nails, one 2,500-nail box. The counts are common-practice defaults you can change, not a code table, so your engineer's nailing schedule governs. The page runs in your browser at `nail-calculator.html` on the wall framing takeoff site, using the same fastener engine as the whole-house takeoff.
