If you invoice customers abroad, the next two months hold more e-invoicing
dates than most calendars show. This list comes from the
[mandate table](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/mandate-dates.json) behind
the [calendar feed](2026-11-02-a-calendar-feed-of-e-invoicing-mandate-dates.md).
Each country page says how its sources were read. Most are advisory and news
summaries, not the official text, so check the page before acting.

**Already started this month**

- **Ghana, 1 Nov 2026:** E-VAT reported to extend to medium taxpayers.
  Vendor guides disagree on the overall start; GRA notices were not read.
- **Dominican Republic:** paper invoice sequences ended 31 Oct for large and
  medium taxpayers. Small and micro taxpayers follow on **15 Nov 2026** after
  a reported final extension (secondary sources, DGII notices unread).

**Next**

- **Philippines, 31 Dec 2026:** the first BIR EIS deadline, as reported.

**1 January 2027** (seven countries on one day)

- **Norway:** bookkeeping-liable businesses must be able to send B2B
  e-invoices. This is the firmest of the set.
- **Poland:** KSeF reaches the smallest taxpayers.
- **Slovakia:** domestic B2B and B2G, per vendor guides.
- **UAE:** businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more.
- **Spain:** the Verifactu invoicing-software rules for corporate taxpayers.
  A postponement was announced on 5 Oct 2026 but, as of today, is not in the
  official gazette, so plan to the enacted date.
- **Portugal:** PDF invoices need a qualified signature, per most sources.
- **Denmark:** Bookkeeping Act systems and SAF-T 2.0; default NemHandel
  registration is reported for 2027 and is partly draft.

None of this is tax advice. The checker runs in your browser and uploads nothing; a clean result is a content check, not acceptance by any tax authority.
