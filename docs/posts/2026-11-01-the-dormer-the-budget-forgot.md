title: "The dormer the budget forgot"
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, budget, construction]
description: The framing takeoff's budget now prices a dormer's lumber by board foot and its roof by area, from typed prices only.
---

The wall-framing takeoff has a dormer engine, and its budget priced almost everything else: walls, floor, roof, foundation, stairs, drywall, siding. The dormer's framing never reached it, because dormer parts are not in the wall cutting-stock order.

The budget now adds a Dormer section. Framing is the board feet of the dormer's listed parts (nominal thickness x width x length / 144, times count) at a `dormerBoardFoot` price you type. The roof is the dormer's roof area at a `dormerRoofSqFt` price you type. No prices are shipped, and with none typed both lines are listed as unpriced, never guessed.

Hand check from the test: one 2x8 at 96 in is 10.67 board feet, four 2x4 at 48 in are 10.67 more, 21.33 in all, and at $2 that is $42.67. Board feet carry no stock-length waste, so this is a floor, not an order. No real dormer or lumber price was used.
