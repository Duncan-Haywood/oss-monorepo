A supplier sheet carries one freight number per piece, usually a guess. The [freight page](https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/freight.html) works it out from cartons and a forwarder's rates, but until now you had to retype the answer into every other tool.

There is now a "Use this freight" button. Pick a mode (sea LCL, a container, air, express) and it replaces the freight in your saved design. Landed cost moves by the difference per piece, profit per sale moves the other way, and the cash-flow, pre-order, wholesale and reorder pages fill their boxes from the new figure.

By hand: 100 pieces, sheet freight 1.00 each, landed cost 10.00, profit 20.00. A sea quote of 2.50 a piece adds 1.50, so landed cost is 11.50 and profit 18.50. The unit test checks exactly that. Duty is left as the sheet works it out, and the lead time is not changed, so transit days are not counted twice.

It is a free, open-source tool and it runs in your browser. The numbers are only as good as the rates you type, so check them against a real quote.
