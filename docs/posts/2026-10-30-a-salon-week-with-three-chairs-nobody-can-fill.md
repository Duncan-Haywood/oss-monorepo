**The numbers.** 16 chair-shifts, four stylists with different days. The roster covers 13 for $1,466 with no overtime. The three it cannot fill are a second chair on Tuesday afternoon and two Saturday chairs; for Saturday it says one person is free but already on another shift. Without Mia, 19 more hours go uncovered.

**What to do.** Read the open shifts before the week is posted: here they mean a third Saturday stylist or a client cap, not a better rota. The team and wages are invented; no real salon was measured.
