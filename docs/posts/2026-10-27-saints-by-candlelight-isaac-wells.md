[Saints by Candlelight](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/) has a new episode: *Isaac and the Wells That Were Dug Again*, about 5,900 words, from Genesis 26. In a famine year Isaac settles in Gerar, reaps a hundredfold, and is told to leave. He clears the wells his father dug, lets the herdsmen have two of them (Esek and Sitnah), and at the third, Rehoboth, finds nobody quarrelling: "now the Lord has made room for us."

It is Scripture told straight, for Catholic and Orthodox listeners, with the invented parts (the weather, the herdsmen's grumbling, the diggers' evening) listed at the end of the script. Esau's marriages and the blessing of Jacob are left for another night. The script is written by an AI.
