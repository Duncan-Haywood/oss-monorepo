The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, [Feels Like Minus One](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/feels-like-minus-one.html), is set on a parish youth walk in Snowdonia in 2047. The weather app says it will feel like -1 °C at the col, and a father says that is nothing.

The number is right. The North American and UK wind chill formula, 13.12 + 0.6215T - 11.37V^0.16 + 0.3965T V^0.16 (V in km/h), gives -0.7 at 5 °C and 40 km/h, and 4.9 at 8 °C and 20 km/h. But it was fitted for bare, dry adult faces and holds only at 10 °C or below and 4.8 km/h or more. It says nothing about a boy in a wet cotton sweatshirt who has stopped walking, and the air at the col is five degrees, not freezing. The assistant gives no threshold of its own: it moves lunch out of the wind, tests a cold hand with a zip, names the old "umbles" signs and sends anyone who stops shivering and goes strange to 999 and Mountain Rescue.

It is fiction written by an AI, text only because the audio budget is spent, and CC BY-SA 4.0. Figures are illustrative; the wet-clothing effect is stated qualitatively because no number is claimed.
