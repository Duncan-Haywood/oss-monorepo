Our free Singapore page for GST InvoiceNow carried a caveat: the 2028 to 2031 phase dates came from secondary guides and were "reported, not confirmed". On 2 November 2026 we read the IRAS GST InvoiceNow Requirement page directly.

The dates on that page:

- 1 Nov 2025: companies that register for GST voluntarily within six months of incorporation.
- 1 Apr 2026: all new voluntary GST registrants.
- 1 Apr 2028: compulsory registrants from that date, and existing registrants with annual supplies up to S$200,000.
- 1 Apr 2029, 2030, 2031: existing registrants up to S$1m, up to S$4m, and above S$4m.

Annual supplies mean Box 4 of the GST return over the accounting periods ending in calendar 2025. Overseas entities, including OVR vendors, and businesses liable to register wholly under the reverse charge are exempt. IRAS shows no penalty for non-compliance on that page, so the page still says none was found.

We removed the "reported" hedges from the page, the by-country hub and the mandate calendar feed, and kept the advice to check your own date with IRAS's calculator. Nothing about the checker changed: it still runs the Singapore PINT rules in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
