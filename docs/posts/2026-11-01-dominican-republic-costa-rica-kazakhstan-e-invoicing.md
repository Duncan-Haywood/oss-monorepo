E-invoice Preflight's mandates-by-country table grows from 61 to 64 rows.

- **Dominican Republic**: the DGII's electronic fiscal receipt (e-CF). Guides report large local and medium taxpayers electronic-only from November 2026 (paper sequences valid to 31 October), and small and micro taxpayers on 15 November 2026 after a final extension.
- **Costa Rica**: Hacienda's signed-XML vouchers. Version 4.4 is reported mandatory from 1 September 2025 with stricter validation; one guide gives 6 October 2025 for the CIIU 4 requirement and the sources do not reconcile the two.
- **Kazakhstan**: IS ESF, with the 2026 Tax Code and reported automated control that holds an invoice when its VAT exceeds the issuer's VAT balance.

As with every country page, these were written from search summaries and adviser guides, not the tax authorities' own notices, and say so. The checker has no rules for any of the three; it still catches the totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults any system rejects, locally, with nothing uploaded.
