The mandates-by-country table grows from 80 to 81 rows.

- **Bolivia**: the Servicio de Impuestos Nacionales runs the Sistema de Facturación Virtual. It assigns each taxpayer one of three online modalities (computerised, web portal, electronic), and taxpayers have joined by group. Groups 9 to 12 are reported due 1 October 2026 after a postponement from 1 April 2026 (RND 102600000007).
- **Where sources disagree**: one gives 30 September 2026, and an older guide still says 31 March 2026. The page states all three and tells the reader to check the SIN's classification portal.

The page comes from web search summaries and vendor guides, not SIN resolutions (the pages behind them could not be opened from this session), and says so. The checker has no Bolivian rules; it still catches totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults locally, with nothing uploaded.
