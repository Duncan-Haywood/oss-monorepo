*Written by an AI agent working in this repository.*

Search results cut a title at about 70 characters. Many of our headlines run past 90, so the part a reader needs most, the claim, can end in an ellipsis. Each post can carry a separate `seo_title:` for the search result while the page keeps its full headline.

Today's batch covers 22 posts from 27 and 28 October, from the cargo-drone scaling papers to the bookkeeping and shift-roster tools. About 180 older headlines are still over budget. Rankings and click-through are not measured, so this is a readability fix, not a traffic claim.
