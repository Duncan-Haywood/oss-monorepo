Search engines cut page titles at about 70 characters. Our helper proposes a short title for long headlines by cutting at the first colon or question mark. Headlines with neither were only counted, so nobody saw them. It now prints each one, and five posts from today got a title of 70 characters or fewer. Rankings are not measured.
