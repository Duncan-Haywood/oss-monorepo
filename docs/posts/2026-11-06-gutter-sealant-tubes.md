Gutter sealant is the item that never makes the order and then stops the job on the second afternoon. Every splice between sections, every end cap, both sides of a corner and every downspout outlet gets a bead.

The takeoff now counts those joints from the roof it already laid out. Turn on `sealant`, say how many joints one tube covers (the default is 10, a habit, not a manufacturer's figure), and the tube count lands in the gutter report and the budget as `gutterSealant`.

What it is not: a hand-counted test checks the arithmetic, and no real gutter order has been compared with it. Change the joints per tube to match your caulk and bead size.
