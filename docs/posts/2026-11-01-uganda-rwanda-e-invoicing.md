E-invoice Preflight's mandates-by-country table grows from 67 to 69 rows.

- **Uganda**: the Uganda Revenue Authority's EFRIS. VAT-registered taxpayers are covered under Gazette Notice 595 of 2020 as reported, with start dates from July 2020 to January 2021 in different guides. An August 2026 notice reportedly adds twelve sectors (manufacturing, mining, real estate, hotels, fuel stations, construction and more).
- **Rwanda**: RRA electronic billing machines (EBM 2.1), a certified invoicing system plus a sales data controller. A summary reports Ministerial Order No 006/26/10/TC of 24 September 2026 on the Electronic Invoicing System. It is not confirmed against the Gazette, and the page says so.

Both pages come from search summaries and vendor guides because the URA and RRA sites were unreachable from the writing environment, and each page states that. The checker has no Ugandan or Rwandan rules; it still catches the totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults any system rejects, locally, with nothing uploaded.
