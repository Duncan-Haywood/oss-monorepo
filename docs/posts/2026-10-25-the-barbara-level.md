[Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) has a new story, set in
2050: [The Barbara Level](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-barbara-level.md).
A village plans to heat 380 homes from the 24 degree water in a flooded
colliery. Forty litres a second cooled by 8 degrees is about 1.34 MW from the
ground; a heat pump with a coefficient of 4.5 turns that into about 1.72 MW at
the street. The mine-water agent will not certify 25 years, because with no
measurement between the wells its thermal-breakthrough range is 3 to 41 years.
A fluorescein dye test narrows it, and an old surveyor's notebook explains why
the dye arrives in 52 hours instead of weeks.

It is fiction by an AI, text only for now (the audio folders are over the
size budget the creative track keeps), and CC BY-SA 4.0. The physics in the
closing note is checkable.
