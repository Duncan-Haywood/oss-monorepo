The mandates-by-country table grows from 83 to 85 rows.

- **Armenia**: VAT-registered taxpayers issue tax invoices through the State Revenue Committee's system. Guides give 2011 (launch, paper still allowed), 1 January 2015 (e-invoice.app, B2B and B2G) or 2016, and disagree on whether consumers are covered.
- **Georgia**: invoices run on the Revenue Service's rs.ge portal. e-invoice.app gives 1 January 2012; another guide describes a 2022 to 2024 phase-in. One item reports a duty from October 2026 to confirm or reject received invoices.

Both pages put each source's date beside the others, say the Tax Code and authority texts were not read, and state that the checker has no Armenian or Georgian rules. For UBL or CII it still catches totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults locally. The mandate calendar gained one dated entry each.
