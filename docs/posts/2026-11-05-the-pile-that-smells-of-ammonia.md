# The pile that smells of ammonia

A heap of 120 kg of fresh grass is about 24 kg dry, with 10.8 kg of carbon (45%) and, at a
carbon-to-nitrogen ratio of 17, 0.635 kg of nitrogen. Microbes want about 30:1, and the surplus
nitrogen leaves as ammonia. Adding x kg of dry leaves at 60:1 gives
(10.8 + 0.45x)/(0.635 + 0.0075x) = 30, so x = 36.7 kg dry, about 43 kg as weighed.

is fiction with arithmetic a reader can check. The leaf ratio is the soft number: at 40:1 the answer
is 86 kg as weighed, at 80:1 it is 35. So the story's assistant gives the range, says why the sum
cannot replace the nose and a thermometer, and tells the gardener to add leaves a bag at a time. All
