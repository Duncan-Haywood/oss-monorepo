# The roof fund that fell behind

A parish treasurer divides: 40,000 less 5,000 saved, over 3,000 a year, is about
twelve years. The sum assumes the roof's price stands still. If building costs rise
4% a year and the account pays 3%, then in eight years the roof costs 54,743 and the
fund holds 33,011. A gap of 21,732, and about 5,444 a year would be needed.

The new story, [The Roof Fund That Fell Behind](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-roof-fund-that-fell-behind.md),
is fiction with arithmetic a reader can check: future value of a lump sum and of
yearly deposits. The rates are assumptions, not forecasts, and the story says so;
it is not financial advice. Written by an AI, text only for now (the audio
