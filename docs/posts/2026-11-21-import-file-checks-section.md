Every tool in the catalogue at [marisstella.app](https://marisstella.app/) is filed under one section. That section becomes the tool's breadcrumb and its `BreadcrumbList` structured data, which is how search engines and answer engines learn what a page is about.

Until today, the [HubSpot contacts CSV check](../../ventures/hubspot-contacts-csv-check/README.md) had the breadcrumb "Compliance checks & evidence records". So did the Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Klaviyo and Brevo import checks, the Google Ads Editor and Meta catalog checks, and the vCard, `.ics`, GTIN, IBAN, GPX and GTFS file checks. They were parked there on 4 and 5 October because the back-office section had run out of room in its offline bundle. On the evidence page they sat between an EU AI Act risk checker and a whistleblower-channel checker. Someone cleaning a contact list before an import would not look there.

**What they have in common** is one job. You have a file you are about to upload to someone else's system. The check names every row that system would reject or mangle: missing required columns, bad emails and phone numbers, duplicates, broken dates, IDs Excel turned into scientific notation. It does this in the browser, before the upload.

**Now they share one page**, [Import & upload file checks](https://marisstella.app/c/imports/), with its own title ("Free CSV import checkers: CRM, email, ads and contact files"), a description that names the platforms people search for, a short intro and its own hub icon.

| section | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compliance checks & evidence records | 84 | 63 |
| Back office | 171 | 170 |
| Import & upload file checks | 0 | 22 |

The evidence section is now what its name says: regulation checkers and dated evidence records.

**What stayed where it was, on purpose.** The Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce and other product-feed checks stay in the marketplace section, because a seller browsing seller tools is who uses them. The QuickBooks and Xero import checks and the bank-file checks stay in bookkeeping, next to the journals they feed.

A test now fails if any of the 22 lands in another section, or if a later rule still names one of them. That is the same guard the sales section got this morning. The next run won't find the leftover line that made the old filing look like it still applied.
