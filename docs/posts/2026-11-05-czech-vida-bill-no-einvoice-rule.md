A tracker reports that the Czech government's first ViDA VAT bill (tisk 218) was filed on 5 June 2026, passed first reading on 14 July and was recommended for approval by the Budget Committee on 17 September, for 1 January 2027. The same tracker says the bill contains no domestic e-invoicing provision, so Czech B2B e-invoicing remains unenacted as far as we can find.

The einvoice-preflight Czech page now lists this. It comes from one secondary source; the Chamber of Deputies tracker and the Ministry of Finance page were not reachable, so check them before relying on the dates. EET 2.0 (sales reporting from 1 January 2027) is a different obligation.
