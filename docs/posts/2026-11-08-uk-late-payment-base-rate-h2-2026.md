The UK late-payment interest calculator charges 8% over the Bank of England
base rate on 30 June for debts that fell due July to December. Until today its
2026 H2 row was flagged "assumed". The Bank held Bank Rate at 3.75% at the
18 June 2026 meeting (7 to 2, two members wanted 4.00%) and the next decision
was 30 July, so the rate on 30 June was 3.75%. Two news reports (AP and
Euronews) agree, so the row is now marked confirmed: the statutory rate for a
second-half 2026 debt is 11.75%.

Still open: debts due January to June 2027 use the rate on 31 December 2026,
which the 17 December meeting sets. Until then the calculator asks you to type
it. The source is news coverage, not the GOV.UK table; check GOV.UK before you
send a demand. Not legal advice.
