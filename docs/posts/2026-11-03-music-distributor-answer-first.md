The music distribution fee calculator now starts with a plain answer: DistroKid's Musician plan is $24.99 a year with no royalty cut, CD Baby is $9.99 per single plus 9%, so a flat plan tends to win once you release more than a couple of tracks a year. It names its source and the date, 2026-11-03, and says the figures come from independent 2026 guides because the pricing pages could not be fetched.

We did not do the same for the TikTok Shop calculator. Sources disagree on whether the US referral fee is 6% or 8%, and a confident sentence there would be a guess. A page that says "check Seller Center" is more useful than one that quotes a rate nobody can confirm.
