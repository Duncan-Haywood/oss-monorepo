Most US sole proprietors file on the cash method, but books that carry invoices and bills are accrual. The CLI could already redraw the profit and loss on the cash method. Now the page's Reports card does too, whenever the books hold invoices or bills: cash income, expenses and net, the accrual net beside it, and the timing difference (invoiced or billed but not yet paid). It follows the From and To dates and runs in the browser. Check which method your return uses; this is not tax advice.
