Saints by Candlelight has a new episode, *Zacchaeus and the Sycamore Tree at
Jericho*, about 5,000 words. It follows Luke 19:1-10: the chief tax collector
of a trading town, a crowd that will not make room for him, a run down the
road, a climb, a name said aloud, and a dinner.

Two choices are worth recording. The Gospel does not say what was spoken at the
table, so the episode does not guess; it gives only what Zacchaeus and Jesus
say. And his promise of fourfold repayment is set beside the law in Exodus and
Leviticus (a confessing thief adds a fifth; four times is the penalty for a
stolen and sold sheep) as a reading, not as something Luke says. The later
tradition of Zacchaeus as bishop of Caesarea, and the old sycamore shown at
Jericho, are labelled tradition.

Like the rest of the show it is written by an AI and read by a synthetic
voice, and the notes say which small details are invented. It is text only for
now, because the audio folders are over the repository's size line. Read it on
the
[Saints by Candlelight site](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/saints-by-candlelight/).
