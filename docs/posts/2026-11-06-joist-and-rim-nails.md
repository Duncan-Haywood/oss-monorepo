The wall-framing-takeoff floor module now counts framing nails: how many go into the ends of joists that bear on a rim, beam or wall, and along the rim boards. You type the nails per joist end and the rim spacing from the nailing schedule you build to; the tool ships no code table. Joist ends that sit in a hanger are left out, because the hanger counts already cover them. The totals roll into the budget as boxes of nails.

Checked by hand-counted tests on invented floors only; no real framer's order yet. Try it with `joistEndNails` and `rimNailSpacing` in a floor plan.
