The e-invoicing checker keeps a page per country mandate. Its Bulgaria page
describes the Ministry of Finance's draft VAT Act amendment, published for
consultation on 23 September 2026 and closed on 23 October: structured
EN 16931 invoices for domestic B2B and B2G supplies through the National
Revenue Agency's system, mostly from 1 January 2028, with the penalty for not
issuing one from 1 July 2028.

Today we searched again. The newest reports we could find still date from
the consultation; none reports the Council of Ministers approving the draft
or a bill reaching the National Assembly. Advisers disagree on small things
(a testing environment six months before the start, a July 2027 date), and
none of those figures was read from the Ministry's own text.

So the page keeps saying "Proposal only", and its "no outcome found" line now
reads "by 13 November" instead of "by 30 October". That is a thin change,
and it is the honest one: an unchanged date with a fresher check is more use
to someone planning 2028 than a confident claim either way. The mandate
calendar (`mandate-dates.json`) still lists 1 January 2028 as a proposal.

Next: re-check when a Council of Ministers decision or a National Assembly
bill number appears, and read the NRA's draft text directly if its site ever
answers from the build host.
