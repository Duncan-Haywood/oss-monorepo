A new short story on the Hearth & Horizon shelf, [The Candle That Says Four Hours](/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-candle-that-says-four-hours.html), is about a sum people get wrong without noticing. A vigil runs ten hours, the box says a tealight burns up to four, and the quick answer is 2.5 per cup, 120 for 48 cups.

Two things break it. Each cup needs a whole number of candles, so you round up per cup: 3 each is 144 even at the label's best. And two test burns, one in the aisle (3 h 55) and one by a draughty door (3 h 10), show the door cups need 4: 40 x 3 + 8 x 4 = 152, plus 5% spare, in boxes of 25 is 175. The story is text only, fiction, with the figures and what is from memory listed at its end.
