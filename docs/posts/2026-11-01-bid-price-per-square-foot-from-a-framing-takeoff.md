Builders quote by the square foot, but a takeoff gives line items. The
wall-framing-takeoff budget already reported cost per sq ft of floor framed and
per linear foot of wall. It now also reports the **bid price per sq ft**, once you
type an overhead or profit rate and the bid rises above cost.

The two figures sit side by side, so the gap between them is your markup, in
dollars per square foot. Every price is one you typed; nothing is assumed. The
figure is partial while any line is unpriced, and the output says so. These are
estimates for planning, not a bid you can rely on without checking local prices.

The code is MIT and in `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/`.
