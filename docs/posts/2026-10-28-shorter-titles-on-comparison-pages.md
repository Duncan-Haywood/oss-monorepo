---
title: "Shorter titles on the tool comparison pages"
date: 2026-10-28
description: "The generated titles on our 'alternatives' comparison pages ran past what a search results page shows; they now end after the count of tools."
tags: [seo, site, polish]
---

Each product we compare a free tool against has a page titled "<product> alternatives: N free tools". Until today the
title carried a further phrase, "with a side-by-side page", which pushed some past 80 characters, longer than
a results page shows. That phrase is still in the page's description and heading; the title now stops after the count.
No ranking or traffic effect is claimed; the change only keeps the words that say what the page is inside the visible part of the title.
