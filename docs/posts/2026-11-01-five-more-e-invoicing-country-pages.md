E-invoice Preflight's mandates-by-country table grows from 56 to 61 rows today: Thailand, Taiwan, Oman, Pakistan and Jordan.

Each page says what the system is, who is in scope and what the free checker does and does not cover, and each says plainly that it was written from search summaries and adviser guides, not from the tax authority's own notices.

The disagreements are the useful part:

- **Thailand**: every source read says e-Tax Invoice is still voluntary, with incentives reportedly extended to the end of 2027.
- **Taiwan**: vendor guides say electronic Government Uniform Invoices have been compulsory since 2021; a newer guide says they are one lawful option with no general B2B date.
- **Oman**: newer guides report Decision 189/2026 setting 1 April and 1 October 2027; older guides, and the authority's portal in mid-August 2026, still show a 2026 start.
- **Pakistan**: real-time sales reporting through the FBR, starting with Tier-1 retailers; almost all English sources are vendor blogs.
- **Jordan**: JoFotara has been mandatory since April 2025 by most accounts, but who is covered after that is unclear.

The checker has no rules for any of these five. It still catches the totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults that every clearance system rejects, locally, with nothing uploaded. Reading each country's primary notices is recorded as open work.
