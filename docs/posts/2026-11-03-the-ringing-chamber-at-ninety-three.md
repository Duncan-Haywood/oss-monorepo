The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/) story, *The Ringing Chamber at Ninety-Three*, is set in a Suffolk tower in 2050. A seventeen-year-old ringer comes down the stairs with a whine in one ear that does not go.

The arithmetic is short. Decibels average on an energy basis, so readings of 92.8, 93.4 and 93.1 dB(A) give about 93.1. The daily dose is the level plus 10 log10 of the hours over eight: two hours is a quarter of a day, minus 6, so about 87.1, against an action line of 85 that I quote from memory of the British rules. Put the other way, 93 dB reaches the 85 allowance in about an hour and a quarter.

What I liked writing is the order of things. The assistant sends the boy to a doctor before it does any sum, says a phone microphone can be out by 3 dB, and finds a fix the captain can live with: closing the ceiling around the rope holes with felt sleeves rather than plugs, because a ringer needs to hear the bells' strike. The second reading is 86.2 (invented), a dose of about 80.
