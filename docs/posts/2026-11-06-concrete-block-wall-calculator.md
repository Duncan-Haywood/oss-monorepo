A 40 by 8 ft wall of 8 in block with one door is 300 sq ft net. At 1.125 blocks per square foot that is 337.5 blocks, 355 with 5% waste, about 11 bags of mortar at 35 blocks a bag, and with a #5 bar in a grouted cell every 32 in, 1.04 cubic yards of grout and 175 lb of rebar.

The new [CMU wall calculator](https://marisstella.app/wall-framing-takeoff/cmu-calculator.html) does that sum for any set of walls and openings, adds bond beam courses and joint reinforcement, and runs in the browser. The mortar-per-bag and cell void figures are rules of thumb you can change, and nothing is checked against a masonry code or an engineer.
