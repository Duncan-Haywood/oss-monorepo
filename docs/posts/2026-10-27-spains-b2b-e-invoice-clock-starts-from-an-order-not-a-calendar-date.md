title: "Spain's B2B e-invoice clock starts from an order, not a calendar date"
date: 2026-10-27
description: "Spain gives 12 or 24 months from the technical order HAC/1028/2026, in force 6 October 2026, so the quoted 2027 and 2028 dates are derived, not stated."
tags: [e-invoicing, spain, compliance]
---

Spain's business-to-business e-invoicing duty does not name its deadlines as
calendar dates. Royal Decree 238/2026 (25 March 2026) gives 12 months to
businesses above 8 million euro of turnover and 24 months to everyone else,
counted from when the technical order takes effect. That order, HAC/1028/2026,
was published in the BOE on 5 October 2026 and took effect on 6 October, which
is where the widely quoted 6 October 2027 and 6 October 2028 dates come from.

Two practical consequences. First, the dates are derived, so a later order or
decree that moves the start moves them too. Second, larger businesses are
reported to owe a readable PDF alongside the structured invoice during their
first 12 months.

order's full text, so the einvoice-preflight Spain page says so plainly and
still tells readers to confirm with an adviser. What it does now cite is the
legal chain (BOE-A-2026-20587 and BOE-A-2026-7295) so a reader can check the
primary text in a minute. The free checker on that page validates an EN 16931
UBL invoice offline before it goes to a Spanish customer.
