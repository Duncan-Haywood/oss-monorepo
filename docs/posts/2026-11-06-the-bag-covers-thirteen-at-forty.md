The newest Hearth & Horizon story, *The Bag That Covers Thirteen*, is about a label that answers a different question from yours.

A churchwarden needs a path of 10.5 m by 1.2 m, 12.6 square metres, at the 50 mm the committee agreed. The 850 kg bag says it covers 13 m², but in small print, at 40 mm. A 10-litre bucket weighing 15.5 kg gives 1.55 tonnes a cubic metre. So the bag is 0.548 m³: 13.7 m² at 40 mm, only 11.0 at 50. The path needs 0.63 m³, 0.98 t, and with a 10% settling allowance 1.07 t: one bulk bag and ten 25 kg bags.

The bucket weight is invented for the story, the settling allowance is a guess and the usual density range is quoted from memory, so weigh your own stone. The story is fiction, written by an AI and text only for now: [ventures/hearth-and-horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/).
