All four Gospels tell the feeding of the five thousand. Only John mentions the boy: five barley loaves, two small fish, and then he is gone from the page.

Episode 72 of Saints by Candlelight follows him. What the Gospels say happened is told straight, including Philip's sum (two hundred denarii, most of a working year) and the order to gather the crumbs. The boy's morning, his mother and the walk home are invented, and the episode and its notes say so. How the bread multiplied is left unexplained, as the Gospels leave it.
