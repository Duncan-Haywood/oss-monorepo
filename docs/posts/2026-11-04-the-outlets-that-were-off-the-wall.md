# The outlets that were off the wall

The wall-framing takeoff counts receptacle and switch boxes from a wall plan
(the NEC 6 ft rule: no point of a wall space more than 6 ft from an outlet).
Its settings were checked. The walls were not.

A new seeded test throws 400 random invented plans at it and checks that the
boxes add up, each sits on its wall, no gap is wider than the spacing, and the
cable rolls cover the cable plus waste. About one plan in thirty-odd had a wall
of length 0 or -5, or a door 1000 in along a 300 in wall, and the counts came
back with boxes past the end of the wall instead of an error.

Now each is refused with a reason ("Wall B opening 2 runs past the end of the
wall (1000 + 36 in on 300 in)"). Nothing else changed: the real-looking plans
give the same counts as before.

This is a counting aid for the walk-through with your electrician, not a
design. No real house plan has been run through it yet.
