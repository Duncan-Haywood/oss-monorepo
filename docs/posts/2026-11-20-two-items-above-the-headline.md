Above each product page's headline sits a short line of small capitals, the
eyebrow: what the tool is, and usually one thing it promises. On ten pages
that line had grown to three or four items, like `AI BOOKKEEPER ·
DOUBLE-ENTRY · RUNS IN YOUR BROWSER · NOTHING UPLOADED`.

On a desktop that reads fine. On a 390 px phone it wrapped to two lines, and
at 320 px to three, before the reader got to the headline. Each extra item
was also said again a few lines down, in the paragraph under the buttons.

So each page now keeps two items: the product (or who it is for) and the one
fact a visitor most needs, usually that nothing is uploaded. Nothing new is
claimed. On the eight flagship pages the line now fits one row at 390 px,
where seven of them took two or three, and the first tool card moves up by
19 to 37 px.

Two pages needed a second pass. `LEAGUE SCHEDULE GENERATOR · NOTHING UPLOADED`
is two items but 44 characters, and at this type size about 40 fit on a
phone line. It now reads `LEAGUE SCHEDULE GENERATOR · FREE`.

A test now reads every product page's eyebrow and fails if one names more
than two items, so the line doesn't grow back one item at a time. What it
doesn't catch yet: 44 pages have a single long eyebrow, such as
`FOR MANUFACTURING, INDUSTRIAL AND LEAN ENGINEERS, PLANT MANAGERS AND
STUDENTS`. That one is next.
