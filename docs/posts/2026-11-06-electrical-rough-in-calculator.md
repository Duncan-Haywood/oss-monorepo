A 20 ft front wall with a door and a window and a 12 ft interior hall wall with one door come to 6 receptacle boxes and 2 switch boxes. The door breaks the front wall into spaces of 100 and 104 in, each under 144 in, so one receptacle each; the hall's spaces are 48 and 64 in, on two faces, so four. Eight boxes at 12 ft of cable each is 96 ft, or one 250 ft roll with 10% waste, and 123.00 with the typed prices.

The page runs the same engine as the whole-house takeoff (`--electrical`), in the browser, with nothing sent anywhere. It counts habitable-room wall space only, as commonly stated for NEC 210.52(A); kitchens, baths, garages, the panel and local amendments are the electrician's call. The figures are hand-checked, not compared with a real electrician's count: try it at `electrical-calculator.html` in the wall-framing-takeoff site.
