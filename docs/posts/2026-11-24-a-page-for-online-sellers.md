The site has dozens of free tools for a marketplace or a seller
job: an eBay fee calculator, a Poshmark sales report analyzer, a 1099-K
threshold checker and so on. Each one is a door for one search. Until today
none of them led anywhere bigger. A reseller who found the Mercari fee
calculator had no way to learn that the same site was working on the thing
they actually need: one set of books across every place they sell.

[The new online sellers page](https://marisstella.app/industries/online-sellers/)
is that room behind the doors.

## Problems first

The page opens with six problems, in the words a seller would use:

1. "I sold $3,000 last month. What did I actually make?" (fees, ads, labels
   and refunds, by platform)
2. "My payouts never match my sales." (a payout bundles orders, holds back
   fees and nets refunds)
3. "I have no idea what I paid for half my inventory." (no cost of goods, no
   profit figure)
4. "Do my sourcing trips count?" (mileage)
5. "Tax time: which number goes where?" (Schedule C lines)
6. "Will I get a 1099-K, and do I owe sales tax in other states?"

Each one says what the product will do about it and which free tools help
today.

## The product, stated as it is

Seller Books is the hosted product being built for this (bet #5 in
`portfolio/monetization/bets.md`). It is **in development**. The page says so,
shows no price because none is set, and takes no money. What works today is
the free [Reseller Books](https://marisstella.app/reseller-books/) tool, which
already reads eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Whatnot and Shopify reports together in
the browser and gives a monthly profit and loss and a Schedule C style
worksheet. Early access is a form: tell us where you sell and which reports
you download.

## No 1099-K scare copy

A lot of seller marketing leans on the 1099-K. The federal threshold is
back at more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions a year
([IRS](https://irs.gov/newsroom/irs-issues-faqs-on-form-1099-k-threshold-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-dollar-limit-reverts-to-20000)),
and some states report at lower amounts. The page states that once, cites
it, and adds the point that actually matters: income is income whether a
form arrives or not. A test fails if the page ever grows a "$600 threshold"
or "crackdown" line.

## Fifteen, not all of them

The free tools section is curated, not a dump: the per-platform fee
calculators sellers search for by name (eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari,
Whatnot, Depop), the cross-platform comparison, report analyzers for eBay
and Poshmark, a sourcing buy picker, a mileage log and deduction calculator,
the 1099-K reconciliation worksheet and threshold checker, and the economic
nexus tracker. The longer lists stay on the audience pages for resellers,
eBay, Etsy, Amazon and Shopify sellers, which the page links. A tool the
build did not publish is dropped from the page rather than linked dead.

Not tax advice: the tools organise and estimate. What to deduct and how to
file is for you or a tax professional.
