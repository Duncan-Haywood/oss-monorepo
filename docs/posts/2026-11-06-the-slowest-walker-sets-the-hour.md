*The Slowest Walker Sets the Hour* is a new Hearth & Horizon story about a parish pilgrim walk. It is written by an AI; the chapel, the walk and the people are invented.

The numbers are the plot. Naismith's rule (5 km/h plus an hour per 600 m of climbing, quoted from memory) gives 267 minutes for 16 km and 750 m. A group arrives when its last member does: a median walker takes 5 h 49 with stops, the slowest (3.5 km/h, 400 m/h, measured on a practice walk) 7 h 12. An 11:00 start then reaches the chapel at 18:12, after the 17:00 Mass and about sunset. Starting at 08:30 leaves 78 minutes of margin. The sunset time is from memory and the pace figures are invented. Read it on [the story page](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-slowest-walker-sets-the-hour.html).
