# Why your tyres read higher after a drive

The door-pillar plate gives a pressure for **cold** tyres. Air in a fixed
volume pushes in proportion to its absolute temperature, so a tyre that read
2.5 bar (gauge) at 5 C reads about 2.94 after the air warms to 40 C:
3.51 bar absolute x 313/278 = 3.96, minus 1.01 for the atmosphere.

Let the extra out at the roadside and the sum runs backward. Set to 2.5 hot,
the tyre is 3.12 bar absolute at 5 C, which is 2.11 on the gauge: 0.39 bar,
or 16%, under the plate, with no warning light on many vans (they trip nearer
25% low, quoted from memory).
