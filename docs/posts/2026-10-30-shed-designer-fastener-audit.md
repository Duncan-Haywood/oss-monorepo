The cost breakdown for Shed Designer's default 8x12 put fasteners and hardware near 14% of the materials. That looked high for a 96 sq ft shed, so I went through every line: the count the build needs, the box it comes in, and whether a different box or a different part would do the same job for less.

| Line | Bought | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16d framing nails | 840 needed; four 5 lb boxes | $100.80 | Right. Plates every 16", four nails a stud. A 1 lb box of 16d galvanized is $8.55 for about 42 nails, twice the per-nail price, so three 5 lb boxes plus 1 lb boxes would cost $109.80. |
| VPA2 rafter connectors | 14 | $89.46 | A design choice, not padding. See below. |
| Gable vents, 12" x 8" | 4 | $91.92 | The largest hardware line. Two vents flank the ridge-beam post at each gable. Lowe's lists a plastic vent far cheaper, but only in copies up to 1,790 days old. |
| 8d siding nails | 600 needed (10 sheets); **five 1 lb boxes** | $27.85 | **Padding.** One 5 lb box (Grip-Rite 8HGSTHS5, about 770 nails) is $18.87 at Home Depot. The tool now buys whichever mix of 1 lb and 5 lb boxes covers the count for less: **-$8.98**. |
| 8d sheathing nails | 420 needed; one 5 lb box (478) | $26.85 | Right. Siding nails are cheaper per nail but thinner than the 8d common the sheathing schedule calls for, so the two are not merged. |
| Caulk, adhesive, flashing tape, Z-flashing, shims, screws | | $105.45 | Counted from the openings and sheets. They look right. |
| Door hardware (site-built door) | 3 hinges, pull, hasp | $39.48 | Right. |

The pack-size rule applies to every design. Seven of the nine presets are $8.98 cheaper; the 8x8 lean, with fewer siding sheets, is $3.41 cheaper, and the 10x10 barn, with more, is $14.55 cheaper. That is small, but it was money spent on small boxes: the 5 lb box holds more nails for less.

The bigger lever is the rafter seat. The default shed seats its rafters on variable-pitch connectors (Simpson VPA2, $6.39 each, 14 of them), which spares you cutting a notch in every rafter. The alternative is the traditional birdsmouth notch, with a Simpson H2.5A hurricane tie at $0.58 holding each rafter down. On the default design that saves **$121.81**: the connectors, their nails, and a different board plan. Every structural check still passes. It costs about 28 more angled cuts. The tool already weighed this in its "simplify the build" search; it is now also offered as a priced swap in the store panel, with a button that applies it.

These are screening estimates from listed prices, and the prices are search-result copies of store pages. Check the store, your code's nailing schedule and each connector maker's instructions before you buy. [Try Shed Designer](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/shed-designer/).
