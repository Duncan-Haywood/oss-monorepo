title: "Twenty-five more search titles trimmed to fit"
date: 2026-10-30
tags: [seo, writing]
description: "Search results cut titles at about 70 characters. Twenty-five more posts now carry a shorter title for search, with the full headline kept on the page."
---

Search engines cut a page title off at roughly 70 characters, so a long headline loses its ending in the
results. Twenty-five more posts now have a shorter `seo_title`, and the full headline still shows on the
page. About 466 older posts are still over the limit. We have not measured any change in clicks.
