A factory quote prices garments. It does not price the pattern, the second sample round and its courier, the label set-up, the photos, the shop or the trademark search. Those are paid first, and none of them sits in a garment's landed cost.

The clothing studio now has a start-up budget page. You type those one-off lines, the run size, landed cost and shelf price. It returns cash at order (the deposit plus the one-offs), cash out before the first sale, the one-off cost carried by each garment made and each one you expect to sell, and the number of sales that earns everything back. It warns when the one-offs are a large share of the run, or when break-even is more than the run can sell.

Worked by hand: 600 of one-offs on a 100-piece run at 10 landed and a 30 price puts 1,600 out before the first sale and breaks even at 54 sales. The tests check that figure and the unsold-stock and ad-budget variants.
