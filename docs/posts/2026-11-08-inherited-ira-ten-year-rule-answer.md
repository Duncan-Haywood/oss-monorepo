The inherited IRA planner explained the 10-year rule only further down the page. It now opens with one sentence: a non-spouse heir who is not an eligible designated beneficiary must empty the account by December 31 of the tenth year after the death. If the owner had already reached their required beginning date, yearly minimums apply in years 1 to 9, and a missed minimum costs 25%, or 10% if corrected within two years. The sentence names IRC 401(a)(9)(H) and 4974, IRS Publication 590-B, and an as-of date.
