Under Japan's qualified invoice system (since 1 October 2023), a buyer claims the consumption-tax input credit only on invoices from sellers with a registration number (the letter T and 13 digits). A transitional measure let buyers still claim part of the tax on purchases from unregistered sellers.

Per the vendor and advisory guides we read (not the tax agency's own text), that measure stepped down on **1 October 2026**: 80% until 30 September 2026, **50%** from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2029, then nothing. A 2026 tax reform is reported to revise these figures; we have not read it.

The E-invoice Preflight country hub and its downloadable mandate calendar now state the current 50% period instead of listing the 80% window as the headline. The [Japan page](https://marisstella.app/einvoice-preflight/japan-invoice-system-e-invoice-mandate.html) has the T-number check, which runs locally in your browser.
