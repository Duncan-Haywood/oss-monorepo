The site now has a page for small nonprofits at
[`industries/nonprofits/`](https://marisstella.app/industries/nonprofits/).
It is the first of the industry pages in the 2026-11-24 redesign. Each one
starts from what a particular kind of organization struggles with, not from
a list of calculators.

The reason for this page is in Candid's own data. Candid looked at very
small nonprofits (the ones filing the IRS e-Postcard, Form 990-N, which is
for gross receipts normally of $50,000 or less). Between 2019 and 2023, 22% of
them received at least one foundation grant, against 72% of other nonprofits
([Candid](https://candid.org/blogs/data-insights-very-small-nonprofits-make-up-majority-us-nonprofits/)).

The page takes four problems in the order a small team meets them:

1. **Finding grants that fit.** Grant Autopilot ranks federal grants daily
   and takes foundation opportunities by link or RFP upload. Each one is
   ranked for fit, with its reasons and deadline.
2. **Rewriting the same story.** Drafts come only from a fact base built
   from your own documents or a guided interview. Each fact cites its
   source, and unsupported sentences are flagged.
3. **Deadlines and the reports afterwards.** There are free
   [deadline](https://marisstella.app/grant-deadline-tracker/) and
   [reporting](https://marisstella.app/grant-reporting-tracker/) trackers
   that run in the browser, plus Grant Autopilot's reminders.
4. **A small budget.** Plans start at $5 a month, there are free tools for
   donor letters, donor retention, event nets and volunteer rosters, and
   nothing is sent without a person's approval.

The prices on the page are copied from Grant Autopilot's
[pricing page](https://marisstella.app/grants/pricing), and a test pins
them. There is no free trial, but there is a 30-day money-back guarantee.
The page makes no promise about awards: the product drafts and organizes,
and funders decide.
