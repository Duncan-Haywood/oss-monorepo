Trim is bought by the board and cut by the piece, so dividing lineal feet by the board length undercounts. A 7 ft casing leg wastes most of an 8 ft board, and a long baseboard run needs a splice.

The new [baseboard and casing calculator](https://marisstella.app/wall-framing-takeoff/trim-calculator.html) uses the trim module of the wall framing takeoff. For a 40 x 30 ft house with one exterior door, two windows and a 20 ft interior wall with three 30 in doors:

| Item | Length | Boards (10% waste) |
|---|---|---|
| Baseboard (outside walls one face, interior wall two, door widths left out) | 162 ft | 17 of 12 ft |
| Casing (7 door faces, 2 window faces, 2.25 in wide, 3/16 in reveal) | 143 ft | 25 of 8 ft |

Pieces are packed into boards longest first, then your waste percentage is added. Mitres, door stops, crown and exterior trim are not counted, and board lengths and widths are what you type. These figures are pinned in the page test against the engine; no real supplier order has been compared yet, so treat them as an estimate.
