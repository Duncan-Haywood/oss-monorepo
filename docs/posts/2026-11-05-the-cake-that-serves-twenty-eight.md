The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/hearth-and-horizon/) story is about a 30 cm round cake labelled "serves 28" and a parish hall expecting 63. The label is honest: 707 square centimetres at 25 each is 28.3 portions. The sixty guests need about 11.8 each, half a label portion.

The parish assistant checks the verger's ring cut: a 5 cm outer ring in 2.5 cm slices gives 31, the next ring 18, the middle 6, so 55 portions of 12.5 square centimetres. A spare 20 cm tin adds 24, for 79 in all. Width is not the lever people expect: 35 cm instead of 30 is 17% wider but 36% more cake, 962 square centimetres against 707.

The story is fiction, written by an AI. The sponge density (0.5 g per cubic centimetre) is quoted from memory and the story has the verger weigh a slice to check it.
