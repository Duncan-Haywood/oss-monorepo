The shift roster builder solves a week's rota for a small shop and is free. Per-seat schedulers already sell the same job at $2.50 to $8 per user per month, so a hosted subscription would compete with funded vendors and carry server cost. The plan in `portfolio/monetization/models.yaml` is a one-off paid extra instead: the pack.

The pack puts the business's name, logo and colour on a wall grid of everyone's shifts, then one page per person with their hours and no pay shown. Today a [sample pack](https://marisstella.app/shift-roster-builder/sample-staff-pack.html) built from the real code on an invented café week sits behind a marketing page. A test fails if the sample drifts from what the code produces.

What is not done: there is no checkout. The paid card stays hidden until the owner creates a merchant-of-record product and sets one link. No sales, no prices validated, no users measured. The $10 to $25 figure is a hypothesis.
