People who search for a 401(k) fee limit want one number, and there isn't one.
The plan fee calculator now opens with that answer: no law sets a maximum
401(k) fee, ERISA requires the fees paid from a plan to be reasonable for the
services received, and service providers must disclose their pay under section
408(b)(2). It cites the Department of Labor's fee guide and carries an as-of date.

The page deliberately states no dollar figure, because the benchmark table in
the calculator is an editable planning aid, not a standard. A test keeps the
lead honest: it fails if a figure creeps in or the calculator stops saying
there is no safe-harbor percentage.
