A landslide cuts the road to an invented Alpine hamlet on All Souls' Day. A cooperative's delivery assistant has to decide whether a 17 kg drone can carry heart tablets up 1,300 metres on a 900 watt-hour battery.

The sums are the story: hover power from momentum theory (about 1,870 W electrical at 17 kg), thinner air at altitude, a planning rule of three-quarters of the usable pack, and a headwind limit of about 5.4 m/s. A ridge anemometer reads 7 m/s when the regional forecast said 5, so the flight waits for dawn. All figures are illustrative and the story says so.
