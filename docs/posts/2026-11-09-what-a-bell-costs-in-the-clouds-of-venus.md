[Twelve Kilos of Bell](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/twelve-kilos-of-bell.html) is a short story about an invented station floating in the Venusian clouds, where every kilogram is paid for in lifting gas.

The numbers: at about 50 km the air is carbon dioxide near 1.07 bar and 348 K (from memory), density 107,000 x 0.04401 / (8.314 x 348) = 1.628 kg/m3. Breathable air at the same pressure and temperature is 1.071, so each cubic metre of air in a cell lifts 0.556 kg. A 60 kg bell needs 107.9 m3 of cell, a sphere 5.9 m across; the station's free margin is 12 kg, 21.6 m3. For geometrically similar bells, mass goes as width cubed and pitch as one over width, so a 9.5 kg bell sounds (60/9.5)^(1/3) = 1.85 times higher, 10.6 semitones. The station, people and figures are invented.
