The free Shift Roster Builder has pages for salons, daycares and coffee shops. It now has one for restaurants, built from a real run on an invented seven-person team with 30 service-shifts (weekday lunch, daily dinner, weekend brunch).

With the default 10 hours of rest the builder covers 28 of 30 for $2,800 and no overtime. The two open shifts are both Monday, because only two people can work Mondays at all. That points to a hiring or availability question, not a longer week for the same people. Set rest to zero and it covers 29 of 30 for $2,886, so a close-then-open shift buys one service for $86.

The team is invented, so this shows how the tool reasons, not what your restaurant will see. A test recomputes both figures from the solver on every run: `ventures/shift-roster-builder/page-limits.test.mjs`.
