Malaysia's MyInvois e-invoicing was phased in by annual turnover. The
einvoice-preflight "which mandate applies" page now covers it: type a turnover
in ringgit and it returns the phase date, from 1 August 2024 for turnover above
RM100 million to a reported 1 July 2026 for businesses up to RM1 million.

The smallest band is the shaky one. Sources disagree: some say businesses
below RM1 million are exempt, and a RM500,000 exemption was announced with
the phase. The page says so and tells the reader to check LHDN rather than
picking a side. Each date is tested against the phases table on the Malaysia
country page, so the selector and the page cannot drift apart.

It runs in the browser, nothing is sent, and it is not tax advice. The same
page covers Germany, Spain, the UAE, Poland, Oman and France.
