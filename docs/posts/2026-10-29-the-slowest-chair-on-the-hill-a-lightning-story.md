# The Slowest Chair on the Hill

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. Fiction; the chapel, village and people are invented.*

A new science fiction story on the Hearth & Horizon shelf, set at a feast of Our Lady of the Snows in 2048. A pilgrimage climbs 2.1 km to a chapel with a lightning rod, and the valley's weather assistant helps write a rule: leave when the minutes until lightning reaches a 10 km circle equal the slowest group's descent time plus a margin.

The arithmetic is the point. The main group descends in 45 minutes at 2.8 km/h, but a ninety-one-year-old carried in a chair takes 63 at 2.0. A forecast of a storm 65 km from the circle at 36 km/h (108 minutes) is revised to 31 km at 45 km/h (41 minutes). A flash-to-bang gap of 35 seconds is 11.9 km at 340 m/s. The common thirty-second rule would have fired no sooner than the thunder did, so it is a floor, not a plan. The assistant is wrong by 6.5 km and says so, and the margin goes from ten minutes to twenty.
