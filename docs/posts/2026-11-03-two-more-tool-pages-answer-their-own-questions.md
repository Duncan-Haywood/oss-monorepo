A coverage check over the catalogue found entry pages with no question-shaped heading, which means a search engine or an answer engine has no plainly stated question to match. Two of them were command-line tools: Photos Import Tools, which resumes a macOS photo import after a crash, and Vendor Watch, which diffs a vendor's terms page against your last snapshot.

Each now has five visible questions drawn from its own README: what happens after a crash, whether a file can import twice, how SD-card copies are verified, how a cron job gets alerted, and what an inconclusive fetch means. The structured data is derived from that visible text by the site build, so the two cannot drift apart.

Coverage went from 1,316 to 1,318 of 1,321 entry pages. Whether that changes anyone's search results is not measured. Three pages remain.
