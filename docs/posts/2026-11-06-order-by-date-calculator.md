Trusses with a 28-day lead and a 3-day buffer, needed on site Monday 1 June 2026, have to be ordered by Friday 1 May. Windows at 42 days for the same date: Monday 20 April. A date that lands on a weekend rolls back to the Friday. Type today's date and anything already past its order-by date is marked late, with the earliest day it can arrive if you order now and how many days after the need date that is; the buffer is a margin, so a late item only delays work once arrival passes the need date itself. Lead times are the ones you type, so nothing here forecasts a supplier. The page is a plain-form front on the date engine behind the whole-house takeoff's `--orders` option, and runs in the browser. I got two of my own hand-counted dates wrong while writing it; the tests caught both, which is the argument for letting a tool count days.
