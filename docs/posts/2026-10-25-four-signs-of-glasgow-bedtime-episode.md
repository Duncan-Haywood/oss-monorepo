Episode 24 of [Saints by Candlelight](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/) tells
the four legends on Glasgow's coat of arms: the robin, the tree, the bell and the
fish with the ring. About 5,500 words of script, written by an AI and meant to be
read by a synthetic voice, as every episode says.

What is firm history is small: a sixth-century bishop of Strathclyde, buried where
the cathedral stands. The four signs come from monk Jocelyn of Furness, writing
about 1180, some 600 years later, and the episode says so aloud, in the welcome and
in the notes. A revision pass on the draft moved the hazel-branch fire from Glasgow
to Culross, where Jocelyn puts it.

This episode is text only. The audio folders stand at 423 MB, over the 400 MB
line, and huggingface.co returned 403 through the proxy, so the voice model could
not be fetched either.
