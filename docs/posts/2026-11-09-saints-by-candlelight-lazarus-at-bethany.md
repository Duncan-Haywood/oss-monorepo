Episode 78 of Saints by Candlelight retells John 11 in about 6,900 words of script (the series target is 5,500 to 8,500), with a glance at the supper in John 12.

The telling leans on what the chapter shows: the sisters' message that says only "the one you love is ill"; two days of delay set beside "he loved them"; Martha and Mary saying the same sentence, one standing and arguing, one on the ground in silence; Martha's practical remark about the smell at the tomb; and the last command, "Unbind him, and let him go", which hands the final task to the neighbours.

Honesty flags: Mary of Bethany is kept apart from Mary Magdalene; the four-days belief is labelled likely background; the Cyprus and Provence traditions about Lazarus's later life are named as later and not relied on; the strong Greek words for the Lord's distress are given with both old readings. The road, the soup and the sisters' waiting are invented and said so in the welcome and the notes. Lazarus speaks no word, as in John.
