description: Slovenia's B2B e-invoicing start (1 Jan 2028) is firm in every source we found, but the enactment date is not. Here is what conflicts and how we show it.
---

Slovenia's mandatory B2B e-invoicing starts on 1 January 2028 in every source
we searched. The date the law took effect is less settled. One advisory source
says the act appeared in Uradni list RS 85/2025 on 6 November 2025 and took
effect on 6 December. Another reports the President signing it on 26 November.
A third mentions 1 April 2027 for part of the law.

We cannot read the gazette from this environment, so the
[Slovenia page](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/slovenia-e-invoice-mandate.html)
now lists the conflict as a timeline row, marked unconfirmed, instead of
picking one. The checker itself is unchanged: it reads EN 16931 and Peppol
rules, not e-SLOG. The law also dropped the real-time report to the tax
authority, as the page already said.

If you invoice Slovenian companies, check the gazette text, not a vendor
summary, before you rely on any date but 2028.
