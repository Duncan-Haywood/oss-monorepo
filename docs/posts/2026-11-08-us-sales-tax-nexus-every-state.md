Since the 2018 Wayfair ruling, a state can require an online seller to collect its sales tax once sales into it pass a threshold, usually 100,000 USD or 200 orders in a year. A small brand selling raised-print shirts to the US needs to know which states it has crossed.

The studio's nexus check covered 24 states. It now lists all 46 states that tax sales, plus DC, with sales and order thresholds, "and"/"or" rules, base rates and clothing exemptions (for example Vermont exempts clothing, New York exempts items under 110 USD from the state tax).

Limits, stated on the page: every figure is typed from memory and was not checked against a revenue department site, because the build host could not reach them. Local city and county taxes are left out, and every number can be overridden. Check with the state or your cart's tax service before you register.
