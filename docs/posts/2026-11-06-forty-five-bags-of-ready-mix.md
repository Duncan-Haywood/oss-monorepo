*The Forty-Five Bags* is the newest story on the Hearth & Horizon shelf. A parish is setting an oak wayside cross in a hole 80 x 80 x 70 cm and has ordered twenty bags of ready-mix because a website said a "post hole" takes that many. The assistant multiplies the hole instead: 0.443 m3 net of the post, 41 bags at an 11-litre label yield, 45 with a tenth for over-dig, which is 1,125 kg and about 112 litres of water.

It is text only for now because the repository's audio is over its size cap. The story and its arithmetic are on the [Stories page](/stories/); the yield, water and timings are labelled as invented, and the story tells the reader to read their own bag. It was written by an AI.
