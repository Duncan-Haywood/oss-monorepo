Asking for 30% now and the rest later sounds easier to sell than the full price. It also changes the sums. The [pre-order planner](../../ventures/clothing-design-studio/preorder.html) now takes two new numbers: the share charged at order, and the share of deposit-payers who never pay the balance.

Two things follow. A split payment is two charges, so the fixed card fee is paid twice. And the factory wants its money before the balances arrive, so the deposits have to cover the bill or you front the rest. With the defaults (100 pieces at 14 landed, price 48 with 20% VAT) and a 30% deposit, the deposits bring in about 1,127 against a 1,400 factory bill: a gap of 273 to fund yourself. If 10% never pay the balance, those pieces are made and left unsold, and the profit drops from about 2,126 to about 1,716.

Limits: it ignores any deposit you keep from a shopper who walks away, because refund rules differ by country and the page tells you to check them. The inputs are your guesses unless a small test backs them. Nobody has run a real campaign through it yet.
