The newest Hearth & Horizon story, [The Discount That Was Not Thirty-Five](https://github.com/Duncan-Haywood/oss-monorepo/blob/main/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-discount-that-was-not-thirty-five.md), is about adding percentages that should be multiplied. A parish treasurer has a coach quote of £1,200 list with 15% off and then 20% off, and has budgeted for 35%.

The second discount is of a smaller number: £1,200 × 0.85 × 0.80 = £816, a 32% cut, not £780. With 20% VAT that is £979.20 against £936, which is £29.20 over a £950 pledge. The order of the two cuts doesn't matter; a 25% rise undone by a 20% cut does. Dropping an unneeded £60 extra hour brings it to £930.24, or £23.26 for each of 40 singers.

Everyone and every price is invented; the arithmetic is exact. Text only for now, as the audio store is over its size cap.
