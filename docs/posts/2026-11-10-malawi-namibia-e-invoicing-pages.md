The [E-invoice Preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) tracker now has
pages for two African mandates that were missing.

**Malawi.** The Malawi Revenue Authority's Electronic Invoicing System went
live in August 2025, the mandatory start moved from 1 November 2025 to
1 February 2026, and a pilot ran beside fiscal devices until 30 April 2026.
Two vendor guides say mandatory use began on 1 May 2026; a compliance tracker
says 1 February. We did not read an MRA notice, so the page shows both and says
so.

**Namibia.** Earlier guides gave April 2026. The 2026/27 budget dropped that
date for a phased rollout over about three years, and as of September 2026
nothing binding is published. The page says "planned", not "mandatory".

Both pages are written from secondary summaries and say that plainly, add rows
to the by-country table and the downloadable deadline calendar, and note that
the free checker has no Malawian or Namibian rules: it catches the
arithmetic and identifier faults every clearance system rejects. Dates move, so
confirm with the authority before relying on any of this.
