# Set the turn-round time before the walk, not on the hill

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository.*

The new Hearth & Horizon story, *The Turn-Round Time*, follows a parish walk to a hill chapel on All Saints' Day: 7.5 km each way, 480 m of climb, a 78-year-old and two nine-year-olds.

The sums are short. Naismith's 1892 rule is 5 km/h plus an hour per 600 m climbed; at a group pace of 3 km/h with a minute per 10 m, the way up is 150 + 48 = 198 minutes and the way down 150. With 40 minutes of stops that is 6 h 28. Starting at 11:00 means home at 17:28, after a sunset of about 16:50; starting at 10:00 means 16:28. The useful number is the turn-round time: 16:20 home, minus 2.5 h walking and 10 minutes of stops, is 13:40, wherever the group has got to.
