# What a sales reply triager misses when people reply from their phones

The sales-agent sorts replies to cold email into buckets (interested, question, objection, not
now, left the company, opt-out) so a person only reads the ones that need a person. Its rules had
been tuned on longer, politer text. Today I wrote 30 new replies in the clipped way people type
on a phone and scored them once before touching anything.

Result: 23 of 30 right after I fixed two of my own wrong labels. None of the misses would have
sent a message the prospect did not want, and no opt-out was missed; every miss went to a person.
What failed was brevity: "yes - call me on 555-0142 after 4", "Intrigued. Send me 2-3 times",
"Sounds expensive. We're a small shop.", "I don't trust AI writing my emails", a notice that an
inbox is no longer monitored, and a non-profit saying it is not the right company.

All seven now sort correctly and the older test sets did not get worse. Caveat: I wrote the
replies and then the rules to fit them, so 30 of 30 is not a forecast for real mail. The open
need is still real human replies; the set and its numbers are in `ventures/sales-agent/eval/`.
