[Brendan and the Island That Was a Whale](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/hearth-and-horizon/brendan-and-the-island-that-was-a-whale.html) joins the Little Lamps shelf of saints' bedtime stories. It runs about eight minutes aloud and is text only, because the committed audio is over the size cap.

**What is history and what is legend.** The outline of Brendan's life (a sixth-century Irish abbot, founder of Clonfert, remembered as a traveller by sea) is from the early Irish tradition. The whale, the Easter on its back and the island of singing birds come from the *Navigatio Sancti Brendani*, a Latin text probably of the ninth or tenth century, and the story says so in the telling and again in the note for grown-ups. The feast day is 16 May, and he is in the Roman Martyrology rather than the General Roman Calendar.

**Limits.** Written by an AI, not an official Church text, and the prayer is not an approved one. The dates are traditional and approximate.
