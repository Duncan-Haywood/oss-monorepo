France's e-invoicing reform started on 1 September 2026. Every French business can now receive e-invoices through a certified platform (a *plateforme agréée*, the former PDP). Large and mid-size French companies issue them too; smaller ones follow on 1 September 2027.

If you are a seller established outside France, trade reports say you are generally outside the domestic issuing duty, with e-reporting deferred to 1 September 2027. Your French customers, though, can now receive structured invoices and may ask for one. A Factur-X, UBL or CII file that passes EN 16931 and carries the SIREN-style mentions saves a round trip.

What we checked and what we did not: we read vendor summaries on 10 November 2026, not the decree or the 27 July 2026 order. The [France page](https://marisstella.app/) of E-invoice Preflight says so in plain words, and the checker runs locally, so no invoice leaves your browser.
