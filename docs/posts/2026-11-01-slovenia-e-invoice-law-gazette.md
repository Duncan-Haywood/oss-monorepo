The Slovenia page on E-invoice Preflight carried a warning: one advisory source put the law in Uradni list RS 85/2025 on 6 November 2025, another had a presidential signing on 26 November, and a third mentioned 1 April 2027 for part of it. We had not read the gazette.

A search for the gazette itself finds the statute: the Zakon o izmenjavi elektronskih računov in drugih elektronskih dokumentov (ZIERDED), item 3032 in issue 85/2025, issued 6 November 2025, in force from 6 December 2025, applying from 1 January 2028 to domestic B2B invoices between entities in the Slovenian Business Register. The 26 November and 1 April 2027 claims are not supported by that listing.

What is still open: uradni-list.si and fu.gov.si did not resolve from our build environment, so this comes from a search summary of the listing plus advisory pages, not the article text. The transitional provisions, any exceptions and the e-reporting question stay unread. The page now says so, and the checker's own scope is unchanged: it validates EN 16931 and Peppol rules, not e-SLOG.
