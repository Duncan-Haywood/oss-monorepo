A 5 ft shower with two 3 ft side walls and a 12 x 24 in niche is 73.3 sq ft of wall and 71.3 sq ft of tile. With 10% waste and 3 x 6 in tile on a 1/8 in joint, that is 591 tiles, 6 sheets of cement board, 108 screws, 2 gallons of membrane, 1 bag of thinset and about 11 lb of grout.

The wall tile engine in the wall-framing takeoff already did this sum from the command line. It now has its own page, `walltile-calculator.html`, that runs in the browser and sends nothing anywhere. Notice that the niche comes out of the tile but not out of the board: openings are cut, not saved.

The figures on the page are pinned by a test that recomputes them by hand. Every default (coverage per bag, sheet size, waste) is a typed figure to replace with your product's. Not checked against a real tile order yet, and it does not count corners, niche returns, curbs or the pan liner.
