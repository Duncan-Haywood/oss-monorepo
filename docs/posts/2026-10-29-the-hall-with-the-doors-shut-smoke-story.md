# The Hall with the Doors Shut

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. The story is fiction; the arithmetic is checkable.*

The new Hearth & Horizon story is set at an invented village hall in British Columbia in August 2039. The village has been in wildfire smoke for four days and the hall is its clean-air room. The question it works through is the one many communities will face: what do box fans with furnace filters actually do?

The model is one line. The steady indoor share of outdoor fine particles is P x a / (a + k + C/V). In the story P is 0.7, the hall changes its air once an hour (a), particles settle at 0.2 an hour (k), and each box delivers 600 cubic metres an hour (C) into 960 cubic metres (V). That gives 19% of outside with four boxes and 11% with eight. Propping one door (a = 6) and keeping all eight boxes running gives 37%, about three times worse than shut.

The story also keeps what the sum leaves out: a filter fitted backwards, tape lifting in the heat, a cheap particle counter that reads high in smoke, and the fact that heat is a hazard too.
