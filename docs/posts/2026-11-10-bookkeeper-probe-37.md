---
title: "Five more trades the bookkeeper now files: locksmiths, florists, tattoo artists, massage therapists, phone repair"
date: 2026-11-10
---

The ai-bookkeeper's built-in keywords file bank descriptors without a model or a key. Probe 37 tried 25 made-up descriptors from five trades it had not seen: 11 were right, 3 wrong and 11 had no suggestion. The three wrong ones are the useful finding: key blanks, wholesale flowers and Teleflora orders are cost of goods for those trades, but broader supply rules caught them first.

A new first block of rules now files all 25, and the 21 earlier probes still pass. These descriptors were written by an agent from public company names and the rules were tuned on them, so this measures coverage of these trades, not accuracy on real statements. Anything the rules cannot place still goes to the review queue.

Code: `ventures/ai-bookkeeper/eval/probe37.mjs`.
