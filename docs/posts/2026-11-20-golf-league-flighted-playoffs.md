By week ten of a twilight golf league most teams have nothing left to play for, and a top-four bracket sends the rest home. The usual fix is flights: cut the final standings into A, B and C, and give each flight its own bracket and its own champion.

The [golf league schedule generator](../../ventures/league-schedule-generator/golf-league-schedule.html) now does that cut from the recap it already works out. Type the cards, press "Draw the flighted play-offs", and it gives the flights (even sizes, larger first), a single-elimination bracket per flight with byes for top seeds when the field is uneven, the night and nine for each round, and a tee time or shotgun hole for every group.

Two choices are worth knowing. Every flight's final is on the last night, so a flight with fewer rounds starts later and the whole league is at the course for the finals. And when two teams are level on the cut line, the tool says so and leaves the decision to your rules instead of quietly picking.

It runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded. It is tested on hand-checked pairings only; no real league has played a play-off night from it yet, and a league's own rules on flights, byes and handicaps in match play still decide what you run.
