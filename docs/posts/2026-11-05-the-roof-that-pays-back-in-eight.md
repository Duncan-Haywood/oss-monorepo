# The roof that pays back in eight

Twelve 450 W panels on a parish hall roof cost 7,800 and make about 4,752 kWh a year (an
assumed yield of 880 kWh per kWp). If the hall used every unit at 30p, that saves 1,425.60 and
pays back in 5.47 years, which is the brochure's sum. But a hall that is empty most weekdays
uses perhaps 40% of it, and exports the rest at 15p: 570 saved plus 428 earned is 998 a year,
or 7.8 years. At 30% used it is 8.4. The gap between the two figures is one fact about the
building, and the half-hourly meter data would show it.
