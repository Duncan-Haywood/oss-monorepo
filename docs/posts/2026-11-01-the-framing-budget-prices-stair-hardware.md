---
title: "The framing budget now prices stair connectors and risers"
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, budget, stairs, construction]
description: Stringer connectors, foot anchors and solid riser boards join the stair labor line in the framing budget, and a duplicate stair labor line is gone.
---

Two sessions added a per-riser stair labor line to the budget at the same time, so on main it was counted twice. This change keeps one and adds what was still missing: the connector at the top and the anchor at the foot of every stringer (`stairConnector`) and solid riser boards (`stairRiserBoard`, when a stair names a `riserBoard`), across every flight. Stair lumber was already in the cutting-stock order. Guards and handrails are still unpriced, and no price ships with the tool.
