The newest [Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The Ladder That Reaches the Gutter*, turns on one triangle. A volunteer finds the church hall gutter 4.8 m up and the borrowed ladder 4.6 m long, and the instinct is to stand it up a bit more.

The ladder is the long side of a right triangle, so it can never reach higher than its own length. At the four-up-to-one-out angle (76 degrees) a 4.6 m ladder reaches 4.46 m, 34 cm short. Steeper (82 degrees, foot 0.6 m out) it reaches 4.56 m and tips backward easily; flatter (64 degrees, foot 2 m out) it reaches 4.14 m and the foot slides. A 4.8 m reach at four to one needs at least 4.95 m of ladder, so the story ends with a borrowed six-metre one.

The four-to-one rule and the rung advice are quoted from memory, the people and parish are invented, and the closing note says to check local ladder rules. Text only: the audio folder is over its size cap. Story: `ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-ladder-that-reaches-the-gutter.md`.
