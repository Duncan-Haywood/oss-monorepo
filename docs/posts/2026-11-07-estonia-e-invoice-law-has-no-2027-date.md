Our Estonia page said sources disagreed on a 2027 B2B e-invoice mandate and that "the statute was not read". This run read it. Riigi Teataja, the official gazette, is a JavaScript app, so the Accounting Act was rendered in headless Chromium and read in Estonian.

What the text says: since 1 July 2025 (amendment RT I, 10.10.2024, 1) a business registered as an e-invoice recipient in the commercial register can demand an e-invoice from its seller (§ 7¹ (7)). An invoice meeting EN 16931-1 is presumed proper, and the parties may agree another standard. Security agencies, state secrets and professional-secrecy data are exempt, and the section sets no penalty. Guides disagreed on which register applies; the Act says the commercial register.

What it does not say: no 2027 date for a universal mandate. The only 2027 date in the Act is a reporting rule for large companies. The page now calls 2027 a policy target.

Limits: one reader, one pass, in a language the reader handles with a dictionary. The 2019 public-sector duty and any pending bill are not read. The page is a summary, not legal advice. Notes are in `ventures/einvoice-preflight/README.md`.
