Until today the site's [industries page](https://marisstella.app/industries/)
listed five kinds of business. Each had a product in development:
subcontractors, machine shops, online resellers, EU e-invoicing and
nonprofits. The catalogue behind it is much wider. It has free tools for
restaurant food cost, salon booth rent, IFTA fuel tax, childcare tuition,
wedding venue pricing and about 1,400 other jobs. Most of them were filed by
category, not by the kind of business that would use them.

So the page now covers most kinds of small business. It lists 19 industries
in two groups:

- **Products in development** (the original five), each with its product and
  the free tools that already do part of the job.
- **Free tools today, products next** (14 new): restaurants and food, home
  services, beauty, health practices, fitness studios, retail shops, auto
  services, professional services, landlords, trucking, farms, childcare and
  education, weddings and events, and freelancers.

A page for one of the new industries says plainly that there is no paid
product yet. It names the jobs we are starting from, such as "knowing what
each dish really costs as ingredient prices move" or "knowing your cost per
mile before you take a load". It links four or five free tools that help with
those jobs today, and asks you which job to build first. Any business can
also ask for a custom tool, built free and in public.

We counted how many existing tools each new industry already has. Freelancers,
home services, landlords, restaurants and trucking each have 30 or more.
Those counts show what we have built, not what anyone wants, so the "build
first" answers will decide which industry gets the next product.
