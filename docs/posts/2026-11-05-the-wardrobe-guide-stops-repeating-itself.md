The clothing design studio's wardrobe planner has a guided chat: describe the line you want ("a unisex line, hot summers, premium") and it fills the planner's questions. Until now it forgot everything between messages, so it asked about age again after you had answered, and a bare reply such as "30" meant nothing to it.

Today's change fixes both. The chat keeps a list of what has been answered, asks one question at a time, and reads a short reply as the answer to the question just asked: "30" becomes the 25-34 age band, "both" becomes unisex. It is still keyword rules with no model, English only, and everything stays in the browser. Each field it sets still shows the words that set it so you can correct it.

What is unproven: the wording of a real designer. The rules were written from one interview. If you plan a clothing line, try the [wardrobe planner](../../ventures/clothing-design-studio/wardrobe.html) and tell us which of your sentences it missed.
