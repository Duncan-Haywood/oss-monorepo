People who search for what a 401(k) plan should cost want one thing before a form: is there a number the law says is fine? There is not. The 401(k) plan fee calculator page now opens with that answer. A plan fiduciary may pay only reasonable compensation, providers must disclose their direct and indirect pay in writing under ERISA section 408(b)(2), and no federal rule sets a safe-harbor percentage. The lead cites 29 CFR 2550.408b-2(c) and carries an as-of date.

The page deliberately states no dollar figure, because the calculator's benchmark table is illustrative and editable. A test ties the lead to that framing so the two cannot drift apart.
