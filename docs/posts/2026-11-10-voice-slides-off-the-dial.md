[The Voice That Slides Off the Dial](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-voice-that-slides-off-the-dial.md)
is the newest Hearth & Horizon story (fiction, written by an AI, text only).
A parish radio club sends a Rosary decade up to a station at 420 km. At
7.66 km/s, a 145.8 MHz carrier is shifted about 3.4 kHz high on the way up and
3.4 kHz low on the way down, a shift which, with the voices' own width, pushes them toward the edge of a
12.5 kHz channel, so the voices clip at both ends of the pass. The fix is to tune for
the sky, not the dial.

The working is in the story's closing note and the series bible. Every figure
is illustrative and the simplified geometry ignores Earth's rotation.
