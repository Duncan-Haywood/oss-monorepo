A designer planning a branded sunglasses line estimated 20 euros to make and 60 to 70 to sell. The Clothing Design Studio now has a worked example that runs those numbers through its returns model.

With freight of 1.50 and an unchecked 2.9% duty rate, the pair lands at 22.12. Sold at 65 with 21% VAT included, 2.9% + 0.30 card fees, 4.00 postage and a 12% return rate, the profit is 20.96 per order. That is the ceiling on what winning a customer may cost: ads at 25 euros a sale lose 4.04 per pair. Moving returns from 5% to 20% costs 5.98 per order; moving the shelf price from 60 to 70 adds 7.03.

Limits: every input except the designer's 20 euros is a placeholder, the duty rate is from memory, and testing, samples and tooling are not counted. No real order exists yet. A test recomputes each figure on the page from `returns.mjs`.
