[The Minibus That Is Twenty-Eight Short](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-minibus-that-is-twenty-eight-short.html) is a short story about a parish planning a 296 km winter pilgrimage in an electric minibus, with an AI assistant that does the counting.

The numbers, all on invented round figures (75 kWh usable, 0.28 kWh/km in winter with a full load and the heater on, 90% charging efficiency): the battery covers 268 km, so the trip is 28 km short; keeping a 15% reserve means adding 19.1 kWh at the shrine; a 7 kW charger needs 3 h 02 min, so two hours leaves only 6% at home; an 11 kW charger needs 1 h 56 min and leaves 16%. The lesson the story leans on: the halfway reading (45%) looks comfortable and is the number that fools people.

It is fiction, text only (audio is over the repository's size cap), and the story says plainly that real consumption varies and a parish should test the bus.
