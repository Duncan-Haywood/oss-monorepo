The site's "for" pages gather free tools by who uses them: [roofers](https://marisstella.app/c/for-roofers/),
[freelancers](https://marisstella.app/c/for-freelancers/), and lately stages of life like
[homeowners](https://marisstella.app/c/for-homeowners/), [parents](https://marisstella.app/c/for-parents/),
[retirees](https://marisstella.app/c/for-retirees/) and [people changing jobs](https://marisstella.app/c/for-job-changers/).
Each opens with a heading, a short intro, a "starting" note, and then a "Start here" list of the
best tools on the page.

On a 390 by 844 phone screen we measured where the first "Start here" card begins on all 31 pages.
On 27 it starts at 820 px or higher, so it is on the first screen. On the four life-stage pages
it did not: 978 px on homeowners, 951 on parents and retirees, 873 on job changers. Their intros
ran 66 to 85 words because each one tried to name every tool on the page, so a visitor's first
screen was a paragraph of text and no tool to tap.

The page already lists every tool, so the intro doesn't need to. We cut each intro to about 45
words and trimmed two of the "starting" notes, keeping every claim and the note on which tools
follow UK rules. Now all 31 pages put the first tool card at or above 820 px:

| page | first card before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| homeowners | 978 px | 794 px |
| parents | 951 px | 820 px |
| retirees | 951 px | 820 px |
| job changers | 873 px | 794 px |

At 794 to 820 px, the card's top edge shows at the bottom of the screen and its title sits just
below it, the same as on the other 27 pages. Making the whole card fit is a job for the shared hero
layout, not more cutting.

A test now caps the intro at 50 words and the intro plus the starting note at 85, so the next
audience page can't regrow the problem. The next thing to fix on these pages is further down:
on most of them the full A-to-Z list of tools still starts about three screens in.
