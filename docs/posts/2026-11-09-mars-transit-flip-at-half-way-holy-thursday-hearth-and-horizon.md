[The Turn at Half Past Seven](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-turn-at-half-past-seven.html) is a short story about an invented ship that pushes at a tenth of a gravity all the way to Mars and has to turn over at the halfway point.

The numbers: for a straight 1 AU path (a round figure) with a constant acceleration a for the first half and braking for the second, t = 2 sqrt(d/a). At 0.981 m/s2 that is 782,062 s, 9 d 1 h 14 min, with the turn at 4 d 12 h 37 min and a peak speed of at/2 = 384 km/s. Leaving on Sunday at 07:00 puts the turn at 19:37 on Thursday, in the middle of the Holy Thursday Mass; the engine is off for an assumed 20 minutes. A 30 cm pour of water falls in 0.78 s at 0.1 g against 0.25 s at 1 g. Leaving three hours earlier moves the turn to 16:37, and three hours later would put it in the Watch at 22:37. The ship, engine, 12-hour window and people are invented.
