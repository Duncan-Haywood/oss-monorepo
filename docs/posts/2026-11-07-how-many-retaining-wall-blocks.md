"Length times height divided by block face" misses the course you bury, the caps, and the gravel that is half the cost of a small wall. The new retaining wall calculator in the wall-framing-takeoff project counts all of it from walls typed as length and exposed height in feet and a block size.

For a 40 ft wall 3 ft high plus a 12 ft return 2 ft high, with 16 x 6 x 12 in blocks, a 6 in buried course and 5% waste, it gives 269 blocks (1.87 per sq ft of face), 42 caps, 2.41 cu yd of loose base (3.37 tons at a 1.25 compaction factor), 6.30 cu yd of drainage stone (about 8.5 tons), 326 sq ft of fabric and 52 ft of drain pipe. Over about 4 ft exposed it adds a note about geogrid and engineering, and counts geogrid layers if you type their spacing.

Every size, depth and density is a typed figure, not a block maker's or an engineer's. Nothing has been checked against a real block maker's takeoff or a real job, and it does not design the wall: surcharge, slopes, water, soil and permits are yours to check. It runs in your browser: `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/retaining-wall-calculator.html`.
