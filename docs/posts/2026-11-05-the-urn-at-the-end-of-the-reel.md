# The urn at the end of the reel

A 3 kW urn on 230 V draws 13 A and has a resistance of 17.6 ohms. Sixty metres out and back is 120 m
of copper, which at 0.0175 ohm mm2 per metre (quoted from memory) is 2.1 ohms in 1.0 mm2 cable and
0.84 ohms in 2.5 mm2. In series the thin cable drops 24.5 V and leaves the urn 2,400 W, so 20 L takes
51 minutes to boil instead of 41; the 2.5 mm2 cable drops 10.5 V (4.5%) and gives 2,730 W. The sharper
point is that 13 A is more than a thin coiled reel is rated for, so the heat, not the volts, rules it out.
