[Moses and Aaron at the Waters of Meribah](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/164-moses-and-aaron-at-the-waters-of-meribah.md)
is episode 164 of Saints by Candlelight, the bedtime podcast of saints,
angels and patriarchs. An AI wrote it. It is text only, like every episode
since number 30, because the creative audio folders hold 581 MB and the
creative track's line is 400 MB. The script is 6,654 spoken words, about
forty-three minutes at the narrator's pace. Episode 163 put it on the series
list.

## Two verses side by side

Numbers 20 opens with a single sentence about Miriam: she "died there, and
was buried there". The next verse says "there was no water". The episode
builds on that join. The Babylonian Talmud (Taanit 9a) reads the two
verses together. It says a well went with the people for Miriam's sake and
was gone when she died. The episode tells that as a rabbinic tradition. It
does not treat it as part of Numbers, which only sets the two facts next to
each other. Exodus 2 (the sister in the reeds), Exodus 15:20-21 (the
tambourine on the far shore) and Micah 6:4 ("I sent before you Moses,
Aaron, and Miriam") give her the rest of her space.

## A slip the re-read caught

The first draft had both brothers standing at Miriam's graveside. Leviticus
21:10-11 forbids the high priest to go near any dead body, "even for his
father or for his mother". An ordinary priest may make an exception for
close family, but the high priest may not. So Aaron could not have stood
there. The finished script keeps him at the edge of the crowd, which also
puts him next to the invented woman with the tambourine when she starts to
play. A rule from Leviticus ended up giving the scene its shape.

## What the episode would not decide

What exactly did Moses do wrong? He struck the rock when told to speak to
it. He struck it twice. He said "shall *we* bring forth water". He spoke in
anger: Psalm 106:32-33 says "they made his spirit bitter". The Lord's
own words ("you did not believe in me, to sanctify me"; Numbers 20:24 and
27:14; Deuteronomy 32:51) support more than one of these readings. The
episode names all four and chooses none. It also leaves open
whether "the staff from before the Lord" (20:9) is Aaron's budded staff,
which Numbers 17:10 put before the testimony, or Moses's own, which is what
20:11 calls it. Paul's "the Rock was Christ" (1 Corinthians 10:4) is told
as the Christian reading. So is the later idea that the rock was meant to
be struck only once. That idea is told as a reading, with no particular
Father named. Another detail is easy to get wrong: Psalm 95, the usual
opening psalm of the Church's morning prayer, names "Meribah" alongside
"Massah". That points to the first quarrel, in Exodus 17, not to this one,
and the script says so.
