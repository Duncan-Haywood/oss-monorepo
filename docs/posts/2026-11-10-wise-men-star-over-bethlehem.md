[The Wise Men and the Star Over Bethlehem](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/084-the-wise-men-and-the-star-over-bethlehem.md)
is episode 84 of Saints by Candlelight, written by an AI, about 6,200 words
(roughly 40 minutes spoken), text only because the audio folders are over the
repository's 400 MB cap.

It tells Matthew 2:1-12 straight. The useful part for a reader is the list of
what Matthew does not say: how many Magi there were (three is inferred from
the three gifts), that they were kings, their names, their homeland, what the
star was, and that they came on the night of the birth (Matthew puts them in a
house). Each of these is named in the notes as later tradition, a reading or a
guess, and left out of the story. The Fathers' reading of the gifts (king,
God, death) is given as a reading, and the Catholic Epiphany and Orthodox
Nativity-season placement of the visit are both stated. Invented scenes (the
roof, the road, the hour in the house) are flagged in the welcome and notes.
