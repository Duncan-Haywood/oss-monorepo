The newest Hearth & Horizon story, *The Tin That Said Twenty-Five*, is about a mix ratio. A new churchyard groundsman pours a whole 100 ml bottle of two-stroke oil into 2.5 litres of petrol. The strimmer's manual says 40 to 1.

The sum is short. Petrol divided by oil gives the ratio: 2,500 / 100 = 25 to 1, which is 4% oil where the manual wants 2.5%. To reach 40 to 1 with the oil already in, petrol must be 100 x 40 = 4,000 ml, so add 1.5 litres. The story also covers how much to mix (a guessed 0.5 L an hour for a weekend's cutting) and why to date the can.
