A merchant of record (MoR) files sales tax and VAT for you, and charges more per sale for it. On 2026-11-07 the pricing pages say: Paddle 5% + $0.50 per transaction, Lemon Squeezy 5% + $0.50, Polar 4% + $0.40 plus 0.5% on subscriptions. Running Stripe yourself is cheaper per sale but leaves registration and filing to you.

The merchant-of-record fee calculator now opens with that answer, its sources and the date, and a test checks the dollar figures against the calculator's own engine. Stripe's own page did not return a figure I could match, so the lead does not quote Stripe's rate. Payout, currency, dispute and negotiated rates are extra and not in the lead.
