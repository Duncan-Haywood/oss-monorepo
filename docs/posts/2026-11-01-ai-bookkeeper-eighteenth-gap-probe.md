The [AI bookkeeper](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/ai-bookkeeper/) files bank lines with built-in keyword rules before any learning happens. To find where they are thin, we write descriptors for a kind of business not yet covered and count what the rules do with them.

This round covered wedding photographers, notaries, pool cleaners, court reporters and beekeepers. Of 25 descriptors, the rules filed 16 right, 3 wrong and 6 not at all. Two of the "wrong" were our own expectations (WeddingWire ads and a farmers-market booth already have rules, which we had mislabelled); the third was a notary supply house caught by an older rule. The real gaps were pool parts and chemical houses, pool and court-reporting software, a pool operator certification, and a court-reporting agency payout.

After the rules, all 25 descriptors file correctly. That is tuned on the same list, so it counts coverage, not accuracy. The descriptors are made up from public company names; no real bank export was used. Labelled real books are still what the project needs most.
