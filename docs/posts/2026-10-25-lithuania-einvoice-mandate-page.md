[einvoice-preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) had pages for Latvia
and Estonia but not their Baltic neighbour. It now has one for Lithuania, and
the interesting part is how much it has to say "not enacted".

In force: B2G e-invoicing since 1 July 2017 (through the SABIS platform since
1 July 2024) and i.SAF, the monthly VAT invoice report to the tax inspectorate
since 2016. i.SAF is reporting after the fact, not an e-invoice, and the page
says so. Not in force: any B2B mandate. One guide reads an EU factsheet as a
1 January 2028 target; another says nothing before the ViDA date of 1 July
2030. The page prints both and picks neither.

Limits: the sources are vendor and accountancy pages found by search; vmi.lt
and the legal register were not read, and the checker applies EN 16931 and
Peppol BIS 3.0 only, not i.SAF or SABIS. The page, the country table (now 29
countries) and the sitemap carry that caveat.
