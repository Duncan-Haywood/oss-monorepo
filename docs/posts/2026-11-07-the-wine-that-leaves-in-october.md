The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The Wine That Leaves in October*, is set on Mars in 2142. A cargo office wants to cut the station's altar wine from the one ship that leaves Earth next week. A Sister who used to be a flight engineer shows why that ship matters: a Hohmann transfer to Mars takes about 259 days, Mars must lead Earth by 44.3 degrees at departure, and the windows repeat every 779.9 days.

Four bottles last 28.6 weeks at seven Masses a week and 40 weeks at five, against 37 weeks of transit, so the priest gives up two weekday Masses. Orbits are idealised, the Church-practice points are from memory, and the people and cargo are invented; the end note lists every figure.
