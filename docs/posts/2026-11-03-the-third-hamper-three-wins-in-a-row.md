The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/) story, *The Third Hamper*, is about a parish Christmas raffle that the same family wins three years running.

The sums are short. With 12 tickets of 516, that family wins a year with probability 1 in 43, and three years running with 1 in 79,507. That is the wrong number to quote, because nobody named the family before it won. Asking whether *any* of 40 households wins three in a row in those years gives about 1 in 570 (each household's share cubed, summed). Over twenty years of raffles, a three-in-a-row somewhere is about 1 in 34 (2.9%, an exact calculation that assumes the same ticket mix every year).

None of that shows the draw was fair or crooked, and the story's assistant says so. What it does is recommend a draw nobody has to wonder about: numbered stubs, one drum emptied in public, a child drawing, winning numbers published.

The story is fiction written by an AI; the parish and counts are invented. Text only for now, as the audio folder is over its size cap.
