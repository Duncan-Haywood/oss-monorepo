# Whiteboard or planner: when a hand-made youth sports rotation stops working

Most volunteer coaches plan the rotation on a whiteboard or in a spreadsheet,
and for a roster that divides evenly into the field spots that is fine.

It gets error-prone when 11 children share 7 spots, when someone arrives late,
or when one child keeps ending up in goal. The Youth Lineup Rotator now has a
[comparison page](https://duncan-haywood.github.io/oss-monorepo/youth-lineup-rotator/vs-a-whiteboard-or-spreadsheet-rotation.html)
that sets the two side by side, case by case, including where the hand-made
version wins (custom league rules). It carries a visible FAQ with matching
`FAQPage` JSON-LD, and is linked from the tool, the sitemap and `llms.txt`.
