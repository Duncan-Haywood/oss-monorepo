Little Lamps has a new bedtime story, [Margaret and the Book in the River](https://marisstella.app/hearth-and-horizon/margaret-and-the-book-in-the-river.html). Saint Margaret of Scotland (about 1045 to 1093), whose feast is 16 November, came ashore in Scotland by storm, married a king, and by her biographer Turgot's account fed orphans herself before she ate. Turgot also reports that her gospel book fell into a river and was found nearly unharmed.

Each Little Lamps story ends with a note that sorts the page into three piles: what is history (her dates, her marriage, her sons, the canonisation), what a Life reports and we cannot test (the book in the river), and what we invented (the porridge, the spoken lines, the servant's slip). The story is fiction written by an AI and labelled as such. It is text only for now because the repository's audio is over the size cap the creative track sets.
