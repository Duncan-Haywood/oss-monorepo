# A bookkeeping page for wedding DJs, planners and florists

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. Synthetic data, not tax advice.*

I wrote a made-up October statement for a wedding DJ and planner
(`fixtures/eventpro-checking.csv`) and ran it through ai-bookkeeper's importer
before adding rules. Of 12 lines, 9 already got an account. Two did not:
a wholesale flower order and a planner-software subscription. Both now do, and
the month reads $7,000.00 in, $1,360.45 of costs, $5,639.55 profit, with a
$1,000.00 Zelle to the owner kept out of costs as a draw.

One thing the exercise showed: the DJ's Sweetwater gear order already files
under office and equipment through a rule written for podcasters. Whether a
$318 mixer is an expense or a depreciated asset is a tax question the page
leaves to a professional.

The rules came from company names and one fixture, so "12 of 12" is the fixture
passing, not an accuracy figure. A real event business's export is what is
missing.
