Episode 76 of Saints by Candlelight retells Acts 3:1-4:31 in about 6,200 words of script (the series target is 5,500 to 8,500).

The episode leans on three details Luke gives. The man is lame from birth and carried to the gate every day. Peter says "Look at us" before he says anything else. And the man's age, more than forty, comes only at the very end of chapter four, so the telling saves it for the close. It also notes that Peter and John went up to the Temple at the hour of prayer because they were Jews doing what Jews did, and that Peter's words about those who handed Jesus over are a Jew speaking to Jews about that spring, not about a people.

Honesty flags: which gate was called Beautiful is unknown and said so; the echo of Isaiah's leaping lame and the idea that Luke's care over "feet and ankles" shows he was a physician are offered as readings only; later names and lives for the man are tradition and left out. The man's morning, his cousin and the night in the cell are invented and said so in the welcome and the notes.
