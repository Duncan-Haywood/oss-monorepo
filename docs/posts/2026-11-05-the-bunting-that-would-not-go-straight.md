The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The Bunting That Would Not Go Straight*, has a woman trying to pull a droopy line tight.

- A shallow hanging line has length about span + 8 sag² / (3 span). A 14 m span with 60 cm of sag needs 14.069 m, only 7 cm more than a straight line. Spans of 14, 9 and 11 m need 34.18 m of a 35 m roll.
- The end pull is weight × span / (8 × sag). At an assumed 30 g/m, the 14 m span pulls 12 N at 60 cm of sag, 48 N at 15 cm and 144 N (about 15 kg) at 5 cm. A perfectly straight line needs infinite pull.
- So aim for a sag of roughly a twentieth to a twenty-fifth of the span, and ask the churchwarden what the lych-gate will bear.
