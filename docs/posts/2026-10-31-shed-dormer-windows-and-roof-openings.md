---
title: Shed dormers now frame their window and the hole in the roof
date: 2026-10-31
---

The wall-framing takeoff could already frame a gable dormer with a front window
and the opening it cuts in the main roof. The shed dormer, the plain
single-slope box builders add for headroom, only listed studs, rafters and a
ledger. As of today both shapes share one window routine and one roof-opening
routine, so a shed dormer gets kings, jacks, a two-ply header, a sill and
cripples, plus doubled headers, doubled trimmers and a count of the main rafters
it cuts.

The numbers are hand-arithmetic checks, for example a 12 ft shed on an 8:12 roof
cuts five rafters at 24 in. There is no real dormer plan behind them yet, and the
header and trimmer load checks stay with the engineer.

Code: `ventures/wall-framing-takeoff/dormer.mjs`.
