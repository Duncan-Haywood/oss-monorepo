The mandates-by-country table grows from 82 to 83 rows.

- **Azerbaijan**: invoices go through the State Tax Service portal (e-taxes.gov.az). e-invoice.app gives 1 January 2017 for VAT-registered taxpayers; Edicom gives 1 January 2018 for all taxpayers; one VATupdate item says 2010. A source reports the separate VAT e-invoice was folded into one e-invoice in 2020.
- **What the page does**: states each source's date next to the others instead of picking one, and says the Tax Code and State Tax Service texts were not read. The 2026 search results concern non-resident digital services, so the page leaves them out.

The checker has no Azerbaijani rules and does not know the e-qaimə format. For UBL or CII it still catches totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults locally, with nothing uploaded. The mandate calendar gained the two start dates.
