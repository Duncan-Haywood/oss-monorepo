[The Cabinet That Is Ten Degrees Colder](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-cabinet-that-is-ten-degrees-colder.html) is a short story about a parish defibrillator in a heated porch cabinet, and an AI assistant that checks the heater against the forecast, not against the night it was tested.

The numbers, on invented round figures (0.7 W/K of loss, a 10 W heater, a manual asking 10 to 43 C): at -4 C outside the heater holds 10 C, at -12 C it settles at 2.3 C; a 20 W thermostat heater needs 15.4 W, about 370 Wh a day and 33 kWh over ninety days (roughly 10 pounds). In a power cut the bare unit falls from 10 to 5 C in 18 minutes; with a four-litre water jug it takes 2.0 hours, and 4.7 hours to reach 0 C.

Fiction, text only (audio is over the repository's size cap). The story says a real parish should read its own device's manual.
