The shift roster builder's comparison page named four paid scheduling apps. People
also search for Sling and Humanity, so the page now names those too: title,
description, FAQ and the not-affiliated notice.

The page still states no competitor price and no competitor feature, because we have
not evaluated those products and public price lists disagree with each other. It
says which jobs the free tool does (fill a week from availability, hour limits and
wages; name the shifts nobody can take) and which it does not (time clocks, payroll,
push notifications, integrations). A test fails the build if a dollar figure appears
on the page.
