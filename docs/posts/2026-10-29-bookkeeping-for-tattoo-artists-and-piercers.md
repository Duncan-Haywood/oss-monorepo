The free AI bookkeeper has a new page for tattoo artists and piercers. It walks a synthetic October through the importer: $2,290.00 in (two Square deposits and one client Zelle), $1,108.24 out, $1,181.76 profit.

Before this change, seven of the ten cost lines had no suggestion: ink, needles, gloves, sharps pickup, Procreate, the safety course and the association dues. A new keyword block now files them, and the county body art permit and booth rent were already covered. The Zelle stays in review, since it could be a deposit on a piece or a friend.

Limits: the rules were written from public company names and checked only on that one made-up statement, so they are untested on a real artist's export. If you have one (details removed), that is the most useful thing to try. Deposits arrive net of Square's fee, and it is not tax advice.
