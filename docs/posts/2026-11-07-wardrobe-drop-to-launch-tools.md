The capsule wardrobe planner in the Clothing Design Studio picks a small set of pieces that combine into many outfits and fills a production budget with the factory's minimum order of the pieces that appear in the most outfits. Until now a designer then retyped those numbers into every launch tool.

A new button, "Use this drop in the launch tools", saves the drop as one run in the browser: total pieces, the quantity-weighted average landed cost, and one shelf price you type. The cash-flow, pre-order, wholesale, reorder and time-and-action pages then start from it.

By hand: 60 pieces at 10 and 40 at 15 cost 1,200 over 100 pieces, a landed cost of 12.00. At a 50 price with 20% sales tax and a 3% plus 0.30 payment fee that leaves 27.87 per sale (tested). Pieces without a quote are left out, and every figure is an example until you type your factory's quotes. Nothing leaves the browser.

Limits: one price for every piece, and the example costs are placeholders. Not financial advice.
