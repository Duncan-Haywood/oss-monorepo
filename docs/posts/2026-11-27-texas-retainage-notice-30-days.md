On a private Texas job, a subcontractor's monthly notices of claim protect the money billed each
month. Retainage, the 5 or 10 percent the GC holds back until the end, needs a notice of its own.
It also runs on a different, shorter clock. That clock starts when your work ends, which is often
when the office has stopped watching the job.

This post quotes Texas Property Code chapter 53 as amended by H.B. 2237, which covers original
contracts entered into on or after 1 January 2022. We read it from the Texas Legislature's statute
site on 2026-11-27 ([chapter 53](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.53.htm)). Every
date below comes from the same code PayApps runs.

## Who needs the retainage notice

Section 53.057(a): "To the extent that a claim for unpaid retainage is not included wholly or partly
in a notice provided under Section 53.056, a claimant other than an original contractor whose
contract provides for retainage must give notice under this section for a lien for unpaid retainage
to be valid."

Three things follow from that sentence:

- It applies to subcontractors and suppliers at any tier, not to the GC (the "original contractor").
- It applies only if your contract provides for retainage.
- A monthly notice that already claimed the retainage covers it. Most monthly notices claim the
  month's unpaid billing, which leaves the retainage out, so most subs need this notice too.

## When it is due

Section 53.057(a-1): the notice goes "to the owner or reputed owner and the original contractor not
later than the earlier of: (1) the 30th day after the date the claimant's contract is completed,
terminated, or abandoned; or (2) the 30th day after the date the original contract is terminated or
abandoned."

So the date usually runs from **your** contract, not the whole project. A drywall sub that finishes
in March has until about mid-April, even if the building keeps going for six months.

The second branch only shortens the deadline. If the GC's contract with the owner is **terminated or
abandoned** before yours ends, your 30 days run from that date instead. An original contract that is
simply **completed** does not start this clock; it starts the affidavit's (below).

A deadline that lands on "a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday" moves to the next day that is none of
them (§ 53.003(e)). Texas legal holidays are those in Gov't Code § 662.021, which include
Confederate Heroes Day, Texas Independence Day, San Jacinto Day, Emancipation Day and LBJ Day as well
as the national holidays.

| Your contract ended | 30th day | Last day to send |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Dec 2026 | Sun 3 Jan 2027 | Mon 4 Jan 2027 |
| 15 Jan 2027 | Sun 14 Feb 2027 | Tue 16 Feb 2027 (past Presidents' Day) |
| 21 Apr 2027 | Fri 21 May 2027 | Fri 21 May 2027 |
| 1 May 2027 | Mon 31 May 2027 | Tue 1 Jun 2027 (past Memorial Day) |
| 26 Oct 2027 | Thu 25 Nov 2027 | Mon 29 Nov 2027 (past Thanksgiving and the Friday after) |

By certified mail, "deposit or mailing of the notice in the United States mail in the form required
constitutes compliance" (§ 53.003(c)). The last day to mail is the last day.

## The form

Section 53.057(a-2) gives the wording, headed "NOTICE OF CLAIM FOR UNPAID RETAINAGE", with the
warning "This notice is provided to preserve lien rights." It has lines for the date, the project
description or address, the claimant's name, the type of labor or materials, the original
contractor's name, the party you contracted with if different, the "Total retainage unpaid", and your
contact person and address. You may attach an invoice or billing statement (§ 53.057(a-3)).

## The affidavit comes later

A notice is not a lien. If the retainage is still unpaid, § 53.052(d) sets the filing date: "not
later than the 15th day of the third month after the month in which the original contract under which
the claimant performed was completed, terminated, or abandoned." This one runs from the **GC's**
contract, including a normal completion. Section 53.057(f) then requires "the notice of the filed
affidavit as required by Section 53.055."

| Original contract ended in | 15th of the third month after | Last day to file |
|---|---|---|
| December 2026 | Mon 15 Mar 2027 | Mon 15 Mar 2027 |
| February 2027 | Sat 15 May 2027 | Mon 17 May 2027 |
| May 2027 | Sun 15 Aug 2027 | Mon 16 Aug 2027 |
| September 2027 | Wed 15 Dec 2027 | Wed 15 Dec 2027 |
