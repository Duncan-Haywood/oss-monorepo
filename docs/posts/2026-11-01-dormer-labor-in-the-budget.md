title: "Dormer labor joins the framing budget"
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, budget, construction]
description: The framing takeoff's budget now prices dormer labor per square foot of dormer roof, from a typed rate only.
---

Yesterday's budget priced a dormer's lumber and roof area but left out the labor to build it. Every other framing engine (walls, floor, roof, foundation, drywall, siding, trim, eaves, gutters, deck, stairs) already had a labor line.

Type `laborDormerSqFt` and the budget adds "Dormer framing and roofing": the dormer's roof area times your rate. With no rate typed the line is listed as unpriced; nothing is guessed and no rate ships. Hand check from the test: 50 sq ft of dormer roof at $12 is $600. Labor is not taxed or given contingency, same as the other labor lines. The rate is yours to type from a real sub's bid; no real dormer labor rate was used.
