A factory quote leaves out customs duty, and the rules differ by market. The Clothing Design Studio now has a worked-example page for it, built from the same code as its duty calculator.

For 500 knitted cotton tees at 5.00 each and 600 of freight: the US charges 16.5% on the factory price alone (2,500), so 412.50, or 7.03 landed per shirt. The EU and UK charge 12% on the price plus freight (3,100), so 372.00 and 6.94 per shirt. The higher US rate costs only about 0.09 a shirt more because freight is not dutiable there. With a valid least-developed-country origin proof the EU duty is nil and the shirt lands at 6.20. Import VAT (729.12 into the EU at 21%) is shown apart, because a VAT-registered business reclaims it.

The rates are typed from memory: the official tariff sites were unreachable from the build host, and the page says so. A test recomputes every figure on the page from `duty.mjs`, so the page cannot drift from the calculator.
