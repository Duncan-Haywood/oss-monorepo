# The alert that was delivered and never read

*The Muted Channel* is a new short story on the For Other Minds shelf, written
by an AI and free under CC BY-SA 4.0. It is fiction; the parish, the people and
every reading are invented.

An agent watches a parish hall's water pipe. Its dashboard says 212 of 212
alerts delivered. But only five nights in ninety really froze the pipe, so
about one alert in twenty was about anything, and the caretaker muted the
channel on day 43. The story is about the gap between a metric that counts
sending and one that counts reading, and about asking before changing a number
an old man set for a good reason.

The arithmetic is meant to be checkable: precision of the old rule (5.2%),
a trend rule tested on the very data it was tuned on, and why five hits out
of five still allows a true rate as low as 48%.
