[The Water That Stays in the Air](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-water-that-stays-in-the-air.html) is a short story about an invented settlement, Larkspur Hold on Enceladus, and its first Easter sprinkling of holy water.

The numbers: with surface gravity 0.113 m/s² (from memory), a drop leaving the aspergillum at 2 m/s and 45 degrees travels v²/g = 35.4 m (0.41 m on Earth, 87 times as far), rises 8.85 m and stays up 25 s, so in a 12 m by 4 m dome it hits the ceiling. To reach a pew 5 m away the flick must drop to 0.75 m/s (6 cm on Earth); thrown level from 1.5 m at that speed a drop falls in 5.15 s and lands 3.9 m out. These ignore air drag, so they are upper bounds for millimetre drops; the story says a real test is needed. The settlement, people, dome size and flick speeds are invented.
