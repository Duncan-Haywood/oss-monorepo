The newest Hearth & Horizon story, *The Apples Off the Ground*, is about a parish pressing day. Windfalls can carry *E. coli*, and pressing doesn't remove it. The parish assistant won't call the cider safe or unsafe. It works out the cost of heating instead: 20 litres from 12 to 71 degrees (a figure quoted from memory of home-cider guidance) takes about 1.34 kWh, or 57 minutes on a 2 kW ring at 70 per cent efficiency.

Two small traps carry the story. A thermometer that reads 2 in ice water means 71 on the dial is only 69 in the pot, and heat does not undo the patulin toxin from rotten fruit, so the soft apples go in the bin. The assistant suggests heating the children's 40 litres and selling the rest labelled raw, and leaves the choice with the drinker.
