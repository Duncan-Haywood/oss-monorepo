The takeoff could already size attic ventilation (net free area at 1/150 or 1/300, a ridge vent, soffit units, one baffle per bay) but the budget skipped it, so a bid built from the budget left out lines the same tool had counted.

With `--ventilation` and a roof in the plan, the Roof section now gains "Ridge vent" (pieces), "Soffit vents" and "Rafter baffles". Prices are yours: `ridgeVent`, `soffitVent`, `rafterBaffle`, or the `ridgePrice` and `soffitPrice` already in the ventilation settings. With no price typed the line is listed as unpriced, never guessed.

On the invented 40 x 28 ft ranch the counts are 8 ridge vent pieces (30 ft run, 48 in pieces), 10 soffit units and 63 baffles; at $14 a piece the ridge vent line is $112, the hand product the test asserts. No real vent product or supplier order was checked, and these counts are a sizing aid, not a ventilation design.
