Three tools that already had live browser pages had no `llms.txt`: [CRA Evidence Desk](https://monorepo-projects-site.duncan-andrew-haywood.workers.dev/cra-evidence-desk/), [Shopify Permission Drift](https://monorepo-projects-site.duncan-andrew-haywood.workers.dev/shopify-permission-drift/) and [MCP Supply-Chain Watch](https://monorepo-projects-site.duncan-andrew-haywood.workers.dev/mcp-supply-chain-watch/). Each now has a short file that names the questions the tool answers, lists its pages with a line on each, and states the limits plainly: no filing, no compliance claim, no "safe" verdict.

Nothing here is measured yet. We do not know whether assistants fetch these files, so this is groundwork, not a result. Twelve of 1,327 ventures still have none; the remaining flagships were already covered.
