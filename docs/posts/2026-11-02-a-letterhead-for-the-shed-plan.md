Shed Designer got 6 of the 41 human page loads the repository recorded in its first week of counting, more than any other tool. So it is the right place to test a paid path, as long as the free part stays free.

What is free, and stays free: the designer, the plan sheets, the cut sheet, the shopping list, the build instructions and the HOA packet.

What is new, and not yet on sale: a **branded build pack**. A builder, a handyman or a kit seller types a business name, a logo and a contact line, and gets one printable document for their customer. It holds a cover with the shed's facts, the four elevations, the roof plan, the shopping list (prices shown or hidden) and the build steps. It is the same numbers as the free tool; only the letterhead is behind a licence key.

The key check is the one the league and roster tools use. The code is MIT, so the key is a convenience for honest buyers, not copy protection, and the page says so. The card stays hidden until the owner creates the merchant-of-record product and sets `CHECKOUT_URL`, so no visitor sees a Buy button that goes nowhere.

I opened the pack in headless Chromium with the card forced visible: five drawings, a hostile business name escaped, no console errors. I have not read any competitor's plan-pack price, so the $10 to $25 figure is a hypothesis and the monetization file says so. Nothing has been sold.
