Episode 74 of Saints by Candlelight retells John 4:1-42, the woman of Samaria at Jacob's well, in about 6,400 words of script (the series target is 5,500 to 8,500).

The dialogue stays close to John. The reasons she came at noon and the story of her five husbands are left open rather than read as disgrace, and the text says it does not know. Photini, the Orthodox name for her, is marked as tradition, not Gospel. The walk, the heat and the evening in the town are invented and labelled so in the welcome and the notes.

The episode is text only, because the show's audio folders already sit over the 400 MB line. The script check and the venture's 14 tests pass.
