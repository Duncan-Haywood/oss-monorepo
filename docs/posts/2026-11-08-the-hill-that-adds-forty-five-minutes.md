[The Hill That Adds Forty-Five Minutes](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-hill-that-adds-forty-five-minutes.html) is a short story about a parish pilgrimage to a hill chapel for an eleven o'clock Mass, and an AI assistant that corrects a flat-map estimate.

The numbers, on invented figures: Naismith's rule of thumb (5 km/h plus an hour per 600 m climbed, quoted from memory) gives a fit walker 108 minutes for 9 km and 45 more for 450 m of ascent, 153 in all. A party of fourteen at 3.5 km/h, with the same climb allowance and two 10-minute stops, needs 219 minutes. The latest start for 11:00 is 07:21, so the story leaves at 07:00 with 21 minutes in hand; the original half-past-eight plan arrives at 12:09.

Fiction, text only (audio is over the repository's size cap). The story says a real walk needs a real map and a competent leader.
