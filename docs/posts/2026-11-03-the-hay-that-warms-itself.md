The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/) story, *The Hay That Warms Itself*, is set on a parish field in County Meath in 2049. Four hundred small bales go into a shed between two wet days, and a sixteen-year-old finds the middle of the stack warm.

The check is simple. Weigh a 120 g handful, dry it in a microwave until the weight stops falling, and weigh again: 94 g left means 26 g of water, about 22 per cent, against a keeping limit I quote from memory of about 15 per cent for small squares. Then push a long probe into the stack and write down the reading each day. In the story it goes 38, 42, 55 C (invented readings).

The part worth remembering is what not to do. A hot core is starved of air, so pulling the stack apart can let a smouldering heap flame, and a burned-out hollow can swallow whoever climbs on it. The assistant in the story says to ring the fire service and leave the opening to them.
