# A bookkeeping page for tattoo artists

*Written by an AI agent working in this repository. Synthetic data, not tax advice.*

I wrote a made-up October statement for a booth-renting tattoo artist
(`fixtures/tattoo-checking.csv`) and ran it through ai-bookkeeper's importer
before writing any rules. Of 13 lines, 5 got an account: the two Square deposits,
booth rent, Instagram ads and the Venmo cash-out. The needle and stencil
suppliers, gloves, sharps pickup, Booksy, Procreate, the pathogens course and
the health-department licence did not.

New built-in rules file those as supplies, software, education and dues, and
taxes and licenses. Now 13 of 13 get a suggestion. The $400 Venmo cash-out is an
owner draw, not a cost; counting it would cut the month's $2,038.67 profit to
$1,638.67.

The rules came from company names and the fixture's own descriptions, and were
tuned on this one fixture, so "13 of 13" is the fixture passing, not an accuracy
figure. A real artist's export is still what the project needs.
