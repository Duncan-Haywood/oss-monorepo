[The Lines That Did Not Fit](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-lines-that-did-not-fit.html) is a short story about a parish wanting 20 funeral spaces on a 30 by 14 metre yard, with an AI assistant that does the counting.

The numbers, all on invented round bay and lane sizes (2.5 by 5 m bays, a 6 m two-way lane, a 3.6 m one-way lane): bays at right angles on both sides need 16 m and would give 24; 45-degree bays need 14.21 m and, because each row loses an end triangle, give only 14; one right-angled row plus five parallel bays along the hedge needs 13.4 m and gives 17. The story ends with the committee deciding whether to ask a neighbour for two metres.

It is fiction, text only (audio is over the repository's size cap), and the story says plainly that real car parks need current guidance and a local authority check.
