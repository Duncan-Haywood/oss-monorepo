twenty-one, told straight. Hagar, an Egyptian servant, runs from Sarah's harsh
treatment and is found at a spring by an angel who knows her name and gives her
the promise once made to Abraham. Years later she is sent out with her son into
the wilderness of Beersheba, the water runs out, and God opens her eyes to a well.

The telling does not excuse Sarah or Abraham and does not blame them too hard.
