---
title: How much rib knit does a hoodie run need?
date: 2026-11-10
---

Hoodie hem and cuff bands are usually cut from a separate rib knit, not the body fleece, and most costing sheets ignore that. The clothing design studio's cutting plan now lays the drafted bands on their own cloth width and reports the rib metres next to the body metres.

For a run of 32 hoodies (S6 M12 L10 XL4) on 150 cm cloth, the plan came out at 46.37 m of body cloth plus 6.74 m of 150 cm rib knit. Cutting the bands from the body cloth instead cost 51.48 m, against 50.07 m for a plain hem, so self-fabric bands add about 1.4 m here.

These are greedy-nest estimates from typed assumptions (bands at 0.85 of the opening, 6 cm high), checked against no real rib order. Confirm yield, stretch and weight with the mill before buying.
