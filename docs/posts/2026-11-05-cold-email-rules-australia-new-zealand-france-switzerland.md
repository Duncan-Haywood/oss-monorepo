The free cold-email pre-send checker in the sales-agent project used to know the US, Canada, the UK, Germany, Austria and a generic EU note. Anyone selling into Sydney, Auckland, Paris or Zurich got "no rule of thumb recorded".

It now carries a short note for each of those four. Australia, New Zealand and Switzerland are treated as consent holds: the checker warns before you send. France is a notice: business email to a professional address is tolerated when the offer relates to the person's role and they can opt out for free.

**What this is not.** The notes were written from memory of each law's headline requirement. The statutes were not read, and none of this is legal advice. Every note says "from memory" in the text the user sees, and the README lists reading the primary texts as the open item. The checker runs in the browser and sends nothing.

Try it on the sales-agent page "cold-email-checker". Change the country and watch the warning change.
