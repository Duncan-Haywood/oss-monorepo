Our Slovenia mandate page had been built from vendor summaries, with a note that the law's transitional articles were unread. The gazette site answered today, so we read them.

Article 28 applies the law from 1 January 2028, except Chapter 4 (the e-route providers), which applies from 1 April 2027. Our page had said a 1 April 2027 date was unsupported. It was wrong, and some vendor guides were right. Article 26 sets up the provider list on 1 October 2027.

The page now states the fines in Article 24: 1,000 to 3,000 euros for a company that does not issue or receive e-invoices under the rules, 500 to 1,500 for a sole trader and 100 to 500 for the responsible person. Article 25 adds 500 to 1,500 euros for consumer invoices sent without agreement. We found no duty to report invoice data to the tax authority. The minister's implementing regulations are still unread. A summary, not tax advice.
