title: "Ventilation load on the insulation page"
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [wall-framing-takeoff, insulation, ventilation]
description: "The insulation page now adds the ASHRAE 62.2 whole-house ventilation load to wall heat loss from floor area, bedrooms and heat recovery."
---

The wall-framing insulation page now takes a conditioned floor area, a bedroom count and a heat-recovery percentage. It adds the ASHRAE 62.2 whole-house rate (0.03 x floor area + 7.5 x (bedrooms + 1) cfm) to the wall heat-loss text. For 1,200 sq ft and 3 bedrooms that is 66 cfm, or 71.3 BTU/h per degree F with no recovery. The figure is hand arithmetic, not checked against a real house, and the page says it is not a Manual J load.
