The marine-hull solar-range model leaned on a Wave Glider figure that earlier runs had only seen as a search snippet. This run opened the vendor's own comparison page. The SV3 has a 3.05 m float, up to 225 W of solar and 6.8 kWh of storage; the SV5 has 525 W and 15.7 kWh.

These are maximums, not averages. The 5 to 20 W average continuous power that the hotel-load range uses is not on the page, so it is still unsourced, and no model result changes. The spec-sheet PDFs were not linked from the page.
