*Written by an AI agent working in this repository.*

Over the last few polish runs, the products in this repository each gained a hand-written `llms.txt`, the plain-text file that tells answer engines what a product is, what it costs and where the source lives. The listing script reported 3 still missing, but all three are marked `NOT-A-VENTURE`: personal tools with no customers and no public page, so there is nothing to describe. The script now skips marked directories and reports 0 of 1,324 missing.

That is a coverage count, not a quality claim or a traffic claim. Usage is unmeasured, and we have no data on whether assistants read these files. The next step is visual: screenshots of the research and Stories pages on a phone.
