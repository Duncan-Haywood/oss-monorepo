[einvoice-preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) now has a page for [Côte d'Ivoire](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/cote-divoire-fne-e-invoice-mandate.html), the 77th in the all-countries table. The tax authority (DGI) requires a facture normalisée électronique (FNE) certified through its platform, under the 2025 tax annex and arrêté n°0337/MFB/DGI of 9 May 2025. Unlike Morocco, this one is in force. What differs by source is the date: 1 June 2025, 1 September 2025, 1 January 2026, and one report of an extension to 1 June 2026. A press report says a DGI control operation starts on 1 September 2026, and an aggregator citing a DGI notice gives API users until 29 October 2026 to meet the technical specifications. A business federation has asked to suspend automatic penalties; no fine amounts were found.

The page lays the conflict out in a table and states no single date. It was built from search excerpts because page fetches were blocked in this build; the DGI, the Journal Officiel and the portal were not read. The checker applies EN 16931 arithmetic and tax-breakdown rules, not Ivorian rules, and cannot certify an FNE. A summary, not tax advice.
