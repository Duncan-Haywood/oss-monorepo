The bulk-material engine in the wall-framing-takeoff venture had no page. It has one now: `mulch-topsoil-gravel-calculator.html`, free, in the browser, nothing uploaded.

The sum is short. A 24 by 6 ft bed and an 8 ft round tree ring, 3 in deep, are 194.27 sq ft and 48.57 cu ft, or 1.8 cu yd in place. Add 5% waste and you order 1.89 cu yd, which a yard sold to the quarter makes 2. A 40 by 3 ft path of crushed stone 4 in deep, with 20% for compaction, orders 1.87 cu yd and weighs about 2.61 tons at 1.4 tons a yard.

Limits: the tons-per-yard figures are typical, not your supplier's ticket, and wet material weighs more. Slope, uneven grade, edging and delivery fees are not sized. The page test recomputes every figure quoted here from the engine; no supplier's order has been compared.
