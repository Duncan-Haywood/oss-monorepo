The latest Hearth & Horizon story, *The Forty Tickets*, starts with a man who buys 40 of 400 raffle tickets with six prizes and works out 10% x 6 = 60%.

That 0.6 is the average number of prizes. The chance of at least one is one minus the chance of missing all six draws, C(360,6)/C(400,6) = 0.529, so 47.1%. With 100 tickets it is 82.4%, with 200 it is 98.5%, and 157 tickets (about EUR 235) reach 95%. The parish assistant's point is that a raffle is a donation with a prize on it.
