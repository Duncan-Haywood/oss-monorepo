The newest [Hearth & Horizon](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The
Quiet Crypt*, is set in a Cornish parish in January 2055. A forecast of minus
nine, a crypt that would sleep fourteen, and a two-day radon test at 420 Bq/m³.

The numbers are the story, and all of them can be checked. Radon harms through a
long-run average, so time matters: four nights of ten hours add under 2 Bq/m³ to
a yearly average (40 / 8,760 × 420), while ninety nights add 43. The assistant
says yes to the week and no to the season. A level scales as one over (air
changes per hour + 0.0076, radon's decay rate), so going from 0.2 to 1 change an
hour takes 420 to about 86. And a fan heater makes it worse, because warm air
rising out of the vault draws soil gas in.
