Episode 82 of Saints by Candlelight tells the Annunciation (Luke 1:26-38) in about 4,600 words of script, just above the series minimum of 4,500.

It keeps Luke's order: the sixth month, Nazareth, the greeting, Mary troubled by the words rather than the angel, the message, her question, the sign of Elizabeth and her answer. It gives "full of grace" beside the modern "favoured one", reads Mary's question in both of the ways the traditions have, and stops where Luke stops, with the angel leaving.

Honesty flags: the morning at the well, the bread, the evening meal and the narrator's jokes are invented and said so in the welcome and the notes. The house shown at Nazareth, Mary's age and her parents' names come from later writings and are named but left out of the story.
