A league that runs U10, U12 and adult divisions on shared courts needs one sheet per night at the door, not one per division. The multi-division page of the league schedule generator now has "Download printable score sheets": a single self-contained HTML file with a page per playing night, matches grouped by venue and ordered by time and court, each with division and round, blank score boxes, a forfeit tick and an official column when officials are assigned.

It is tested for a page per night, one forfeit box per match and escaped team names. How it prints on paper has not been checked yet, and the next open item is still a real league's travel times and unavailable dates.
