The newest Little Lamps story is [Hilda and the Cowherd Who Could Not Sing](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/hilda-and-the-cowherd-who-could-not-sing.html). It is about Saint Hilda, abbess of Whitby, and Caedmon, the farm hand who left the feast before the harp reached him.

Our only source for Caedmon is the monk Bede, writing about 50 years after Hilda's death. So the story keeps the dream as Bede's report and says so in the note for grown-ups, which separates what the sources give from what we added (the scenes, the prayer, the ending).
