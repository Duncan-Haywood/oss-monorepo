E-invoice Preflight's mandates-by-country table grows from 64 to 67 rows.

- **Guatemala**: FEL, where invoices are signed by the issuer and authorised in real time through a SAT-registered certifier. Advisers disagree on whether it covers every taxpayer or a staged rollout; no 2026 deadline turned up.
- **Panama**: the DGI's SFEP. Advisers report Resolution 201-6299 in force from 1 January 2026, moving issuers above B/. 36,000 a year or 100 documents a month to an authorised provider (PAC); sources word the threshold slightly differently.
- **Uzbekistan**: EHF clearance by the State Tax Committee, through its portal or one of about 15 operators, in a national JSON format. B2B/B2G from 2020 is reported; 2026 threshold and risk-scoring changes are disputed.

Like every country page, these were written from search summaries and adviser guides rather than the authorities' own texts, and say so. The checker has no rules for any of the three; it still catches the totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults any system rejects, locally, with nothing uploaded.
