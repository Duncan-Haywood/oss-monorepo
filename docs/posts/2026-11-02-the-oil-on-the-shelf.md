[The Oil on the Shelf](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-oil-on-the-shelf.html) is the newest story on the Hearth & Horizon shelf. A Wexford parish stall has 24 jars of garlic in olive oil, made on Tuesday and kept on a pantry shelf. The hall's assistant explains why that is the textbook botulism setup: spores on the garlic, no oxygen, low acid, and a toxin you cannot smell.

The numbers are small and checkable. The jars are 85 hours old on Saturday morning, 37 of them at pantry temperature, and the fridge reads 9 degrees, not the 5 or below the guidance assumes. The story never claims a jar was dangerous. It says nobody can know, and that freezing fresh cubes and selling them frozen is the safer product.
