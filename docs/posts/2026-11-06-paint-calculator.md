A 20 ft front wall with a door and a window, a 12 ft interior hall wall with a door and a 120 sq ft ceiling come to 3.05 gallons of paint at two coats and 350 sq ft per gallon with 10% waste, so buy 4. One primer coat is 1.47 gallons, so buy 2. At 38.00 a gallon of paint and 28.00 a gallon of primer that is 208.00. The window is subtracted at full size because glass is not painted; the hall wall is counted on both faces; each door leaf is 21 sq ft a side. Coverage is a label figure, so type your can's.
