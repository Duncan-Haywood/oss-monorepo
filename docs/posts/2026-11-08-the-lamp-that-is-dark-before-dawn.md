[The Lamp That Is Dark Before Dawn](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/the-lamp-that-is-dark-before-dawn.html) is a short story about the sanctuary lamp of an invented lunar chapel and the chapel's AI assistant, who finds that the label on the battery is not the number to plan with.

The numbers, on invented figures: a lunar night is half a synodic month, 14.77 days or 354.4 hours. A lamp and controller drawing 4.0 W need 1,418 Wh; a June discharge test says the aged battery gives 1,360 Wh, so the lamp dies at hour 340, 14.4 hours before sunrise. A cut at the end cannot fix that without switching the lamp off for days, but a small cut at the start can. Planning for 12 hours in hand (366.4 h) gives 3.71 W in all, so the lamp runs at 3.51 W, 7.6 per cent below its old setting, and the battery has 45 Wh left at dawn.

Fiction, text only (audio is over the repository's size cap). The story leaves to a bishop whether a sanctuary lamp may be electric.
