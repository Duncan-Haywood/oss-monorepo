# Fence posts: one more than panels

A run of n bays has n + 1 posts, because the last bay needs a post on its far
side. That is the commonest short count when ordering a fence.

The second trap is that posts take length. With 1.83 m panels between 100 mm
posts, each bay is 1.83 + 0.1 = 1.93 m from post centre to post centre. For a
24 m run that is 24 / 1.93 = 12.4, so 12 full bays (23.16 m) and a last bay of
0.84 m, which takes a panel cut to 0.74 m. Dividing by the bare panel width
(24 / 1.83 = 13.1) gives 13 full panels that would run to 25.1 m. The order is
13 panels (12 whole, one cut) and 14 posts. With 95 mm planed posts the
numbers shift slightly (1.925 m bays, a 0.9 m last bay), so measure the real
posts and the real last gap on the day.
