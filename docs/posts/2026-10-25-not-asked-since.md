[Not Asked Since](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/not-asked-since.md)
is the next piece in the twenty-years-ahead strand. A ward assistant with
perfect memory shows "NO CHAPLAIN VISITS" in red for a man who said it in 2043
and twice since, the last time 21 months ago. Then a stroke leaves him
with two words, *aye* and *no*.

The numbers in the story are small enough to check. In the hospital's invented
records 29 of 212 people who declined a chaplain later accepted one (13.7%,
about one in seven). The assistant treats that as a reason to ask, not to
answer. It asks with control questions it knows the answers to: five right in
a row by guessing is 1 in 32. The refusal turns out to be about one dead
school priest.

The design point is the speculative step: assistants with memory exist today,
and the failure is not forgetting but a record that never says how old it is.
