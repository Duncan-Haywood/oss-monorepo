# Does a wool sweater need a different label?

In the US, yes. Wool is covered by the Wool Products Labeling Act, a separate
law from the textile rules, and it wants to know how much is new wool and how
much is recycled. In the EU and UK, a leather patch or fur collar means the
label has to say the garment contains non-textile parts of animal origin.

The free label checker in the Clothing Design Studio now notices these. Type
the fibres, add trims such as "leather patch" or "down fill", pick a market,
and it lists the extra rules and the regulation to read. It points at the law;
it does not rule on compliance, so confirm with the factory or a specialist
before printing labels.

Tested in `labels.test.mjs` (wool in the US, no wool note for cotton, leather,
fur and down notes by market).
