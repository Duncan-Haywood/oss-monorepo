Self-employment tax is 12.4% Social Security on net earnings up to a cap, plus 2.9% Medicare on all of them. The cap is SSA's contribution and benefit base: $184,500 for 2026 and $192,300 for 2027 (at the cap, 12.4% is $23,845.20 of Social Security tax for 2027 on the self-employed side).

The AI Bookkeeper's Schedule SE worksheet had carried both figures since late October with a warning that they came from trade-press reports. A search of SSA's own Contribution and Benefit Base page and its 2027 automatic-adjustment listing on 2026-11-05 returns the same two numbers, so the warning is removed for 2024 to 2027. Later years still warn and ask for a `wageBase`. SSA pages could not be fetched directly from the build environment, so the check is through search results naming those pages; the worksheet remains an estimate, not a filing.
