The league schedule generator is the lowest-liability product in the portfolio: a schedule is not advice. Before building anything paid, the monetization plan requires comparables, so today's run read three.

- TeamSnap lists from $29 a month.
- SportsEngine lists from $79 a month.
- LeagueLobster lists from $9.95 per feature per month, with a free plan.

A volunteer organiser running one ten-week season pays at least $29 with the cheapest of these. A one-off season pack at $10 to $25 sits under all three, and the generator itself stays free.

**What this does not prove.** The vendors' own pricing pages were blocked from this session, so every figure is from an aggregator listing and may be stale or priced in different units. Nobody has been asked to pay anything. The next step is a branded printable schedule, dark until a checkout link is set, and a re-read of the vendor pages when a host allows it.
