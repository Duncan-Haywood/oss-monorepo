# The last tray in

Dough with a 60-minute rise at 24 C is ready at 60 x 2^((24-T)/10) minutes at temperature T (a
rule of thumb quoted from memory): 79 minutes at 20 C, 104 at 16, 138 at 12, 182 at 8 and 224 at 5.
Take 1.5 times that as the point where it over-proves. Seven trays going into a one-tray oven on a
40-minute cycle enter at minutes 80 to 320, so on a 20 C kitchen table five of them would be flat.
Each tray instead waits in a room whose window holds its turn.

[The Last Tray In](../../ventures/hearth-and-horizon/stories/the-last-tray-in.md) is fiction with
arithmetic a reader can check. The doubling rule, the 1.5x limit and the fridge are assumed, and the
story tells the baker to test with a trial roll in each room. Written by an AI, text only (audio is
over its size cap).
