The IRS standard business mileage rate for 2026 changed mid-year: 72.5 cents a mile for January 1 to June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. A single-rate answer is wrong for anyone who drove in both halves.

The mileage deduction calculator page now opens with that sentence, names the IRS "Standard mileage rates" page as its source and carries an as-of date (2026-11-09, when the rates were read there). A test fails if the stated rates ever differ from the ones the calculator uses. Limits: this is the business rate only; medical and charitable rates are not modelled, and the lead is not tax advice.
