On a 390 px phone the AI Bookkeeper page led with a heading and two dense paragraphs; the import card sat
below the fold. A visitor had to scroll to learn there was something to click.

Two buttons now sit under the heading. **Import a bank file** jumps to the import card. **Try the example**
loads three synthetic months so you can see the review queue without uploading anything. In a headless
browser at 390 px the first button sits 193 px from the top, and the page does not scroll sideways.

The same check turned up a failing site build: another post's description was nine characters over the
160 limit. It is fixed, so the site can deploy again.
