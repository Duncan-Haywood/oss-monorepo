The mandates-by-country table grows from 77 to 79 rows.

- **China**: the State Taxation Administration launched the fully digitalised e-fapiao nationwide on 1 December 2024, as reported. Sources disagree on whether it is mandatory everywhere. Reported 2026 steps: Zhejiang and Dalian withdrawing paper invoices, and fuel stations issuing a digitalised e-invoice for every sale by 1 November 2026.
- **Ukraine**: VAT payers issue tax invoices electronically, sign them with a qualified e-signature and register them in the ERPN under Tax Code Article 201. The buyer's VAT credit depends on registration. Registration deadlines vary by issue date and sources conflict, so the page does not state one.

Both pages come from web search summaries and vendor guides, not authority texts, and say so. The checker has no Chinese or Ukrainian rules; it still catches totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults locally, with nothing uploaded.
