---
title: "When a wall has no studs to splice the plates on"
date: 2026-11-07
---

A 245 in wall with a 189 in opening has two studs that matter, the jacks. With 16 ft boards the top plate and the cap plate cannot take different joints over those two, so our wall framer used to give up and show each plate as one piece. That is what stopped the 3rd Floor of the Schultz Residence IFC model.

A header carries the plates across an opening, so a joint over it is ordinary framing. The engine now tries studs first and, only when that fails, also allows joints every 16 in inside an opening, keeping the usual lap between top and cap joints. It says so in the wall's notes.

It is tested on a hand-built wall of that shape, and the random-wall layout test still passes with the layout check accepting a joint over a header. It has not yet been re-run on the real file, and whether a joint there is acceptable is the framer's and engineer's call.
