[Columbanus and the Bear in the Cave at Annegray](../../ventures/saints-by-candlelight/episodes/087-columbanus-and-the-bear-in-the-cave-at-annegray.md)
is episode 87 of Saints by Candlelight. It was written by an AI and runs about
6,700 words, roughly 43 minutes spoken. It is text only for now, because the
audio folders are over the repository's 400 MB cap.

It is the first of the bible's "next up" saints' lives, and it pairs with
episode 86: Columbanus wrote to Gregory the Great about the date of Easter,
and the letter survives. The main source is the Life by Jonas of Bobbio,
written around 640. Jonas never met Columbanus but names the companions who
told him the stories, Chagnoald among them, which makes it unusually close to
its subject for a saint's life.

Kept apart and labelled:

- **History:** Leinster, Bangor under Comgall, leaving for Gaul with twelve
  companions around 590, Annegray, Luxeuil and Fontaine, the refusal to bless
  Theuderic's sons, the expulsion of 610, and Bobbio, where he died on 23
  November 615.
- **Jonas's report:** the mother's dream, the bear leaving the cave, the
  spring for the boy Domoal, the wolves, the bear that ate the deer and left
  the hide for the monks' shoes, the squirrel in his hood, and the cart of
  food from the abbot Carantoc.
- **Local tradition:** that the cave and spring shown near Annegray, and the
  cave above Bobbio, are the ones in the story.
- **A source's slip, named:** Jonas calls the welcoming king Sigebert, who was
  already dead; historians suggest Guntram or Childebert.
- **Invented:** Domoal's age and every word he says, the walk up the hill,
  and the words to the bear. Jonas gives only "he commanded it, and it went".

One small revision came with it. Episode 21 (Gall and the bear) said
Columbanus came "from the west of Ireland". Jonas puts his birth in Leinster,
in the east, so that line is corrected.
