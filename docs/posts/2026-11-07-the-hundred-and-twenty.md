The latest Hearth & Horizon story, *The Hundred and Twenty*, is about a parish survey on moving Sunday Mass. 66 of 120 replies from 400 households favoured the later time: 55%.

Two checks change the reading. The margin for 120 replies is 8.9 points, or 7.5 with the finite-population correction, so 47.5% to 62.5% and a tie is inside it. And households with someone over 70 are 40% of the roll but 23% of the replies; they favoured the change 8 of 28 (29%) against 58 of 92 (63%) for everyone else. Weighted back to the roll that is 49.3%, with a margin near 9 points. Weighting fixes only the one thing measured, so the parish in the story counts heads at the door instead.
