---

Metal T-braces are bought each, but a let-in 1x4 is bought by the foot. `fasteners.wallBracing.pricePerFoot` now prices the brace length the takeoff already computes, and the budget shows a "Wall braces (by the foot)" line in feet. If you also type a price each, that wins.

Checked by hand arithmetic only: feet times the price you type. No real brace order was compared, and no price ships with the tool.
