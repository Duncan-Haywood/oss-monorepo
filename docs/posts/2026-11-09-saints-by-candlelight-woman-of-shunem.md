Episode 77 of Saints by Candlelight retells 2 Kings 4:8-37 in about 7,600 words of script (the series target is 5,500 to 8,500). It picks up where episode 38, the widow's jars of oil, left the woman of Shunem in her kitchen.

The telling leans on what the chapter shows: her one word, "Shalom", said to her husband, to Gehazi and to herself; Elisha admitting "the Lord has hidden it from me"; the staff that does nothing while the prophet's prayer behind a shut door does; and "Take up your son", the shortest speech in the chapter.

Honesty flags: the distance to Carmel is our own estimate; the rare Hebrew word for "sneezed" is named as uncertain; the Church's reading of the story as a sign of the resurrection is labelled a reading; the Nain-on-the-same-hill remark is geography, not a claim. The sounds of the field and the thoughts on the road are invented and said so in the welcome and the notes. Nobody in the story is given a name the book does not give.
