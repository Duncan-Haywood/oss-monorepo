A dormer's studs and rafters were already in the lumber order, but the plywood on its roof and walls was in no budget line. The budget now reads the dormer's areas (roof, and front wall plus gable plus cheeks), adds 10% spare and rounds up to 4x8 sheets of 32 sq ft.

Prices are the same `roofSheet` and `wallSheet` you already type for the main house. No price typed means the line is listed as unpriced, never guessed.

Not covered: dormer shingles and flashing, and a labor line. The test checks the sheet counts against the dormer's reported areas and the amounts against the typed prices; no real dormer plan has been checked.
