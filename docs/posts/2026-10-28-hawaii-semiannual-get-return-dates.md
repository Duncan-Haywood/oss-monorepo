Hawaii's general excise tax return is due the 20th of the month after the
period, and semiannual filers use the Jan-Jun and Jul-Dec halves. AI Bookkeeper's
worksheet already produced those dates (July 20 and January 20), but a code
comment said they were not modelled. The comment is fixed and a test now pins
the two dates.

The evidence is one 2026 third-party guide and the history of a 2009 bill that
moved the due date from month end to the 20th. The statute (HRS 237-30) and the
Department of Taxation site were not read, so check your own notice before filing.
