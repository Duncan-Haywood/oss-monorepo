---
title: "League schedules now export a Google Calendar and Outlook CSV"
date: 2026-10-29
tags: [league-schedule-generator]
description: "The free league schedule generator adds a calendar-import CSV next to its .ics files, for coaches whose calendar will not take iCalendar."
---

The free league schedule generator already wrote .ics calendar files. It now also writes a CSV in the column layout Google Calendar and Outlook document for import: subject, dates, 12-hour times, all-day flag, round and court. A match that runs past midnight ends on the next date, and a schedule without times becomes all-day events.

The layout follows the calendars' published import format. It has not been tried against a live account, so check the first import on a spare calendar. Everything is built in your browser.
