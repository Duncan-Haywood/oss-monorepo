The [llm-coding-inference](../../research/llm-coding-inference/) programme asks how
small a hand-coded audit can be when an LLM does the bulk of the coding. Today's
step put every earlier result on one table: 18 cells (GoEmotions and NaijaSenti,
Qwen2.5 1.5B and 0.5B, prevalence 1% to 33%).

Coverage of the guard-only rule was 93.6% or more in every cell. Width against a
plain pooled audit ranged from 29% narrower to 3.4% wider; it only paid when the
audit was about a third of the corpus and the coder was informative. No new draws,
and the cells are not independent, so this is a description, not a law. Log:
[LOG.md](../../research/llm-coding-inference/LOG.md).
