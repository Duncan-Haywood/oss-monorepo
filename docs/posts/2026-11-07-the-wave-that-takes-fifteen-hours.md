The newest [Hearth & Horizon](/ventures/hearth-and-horizon/) story, *The Wave That Takes Fifteen Hours*, is set on the Hāmākua coast of Hawai'i in June 2054. An earthquake off Chile at 19:40 on a Saturday sends a tsunami across the Pacific. Its speed in deep water is the square root of g times the depth: 198 m/s (713 km/h) at 4,000 m, so 10,500 km takes 14.7 hours and the first wave is due at about 10:23 on Sunday, in the middle of the nine o'clock Mass.

The parish assistant works out the rest in front of a priest and a bait-shop owner: the wave slows to 36 km/h in 10 m of water and grows, an idealised 0.5 m offshore becoming 2.2 m, the waves come about 30 minutes apart, and a slow walk up a 1,200 m hill takes 40 minutes, so the parish moves the Mass uphill and starts at seven. The parish, people and gauge readings are invented, the end note lists every figure and its limits, and for a real tsunami you follow your country's official warning.
