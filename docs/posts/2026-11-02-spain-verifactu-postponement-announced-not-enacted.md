Two Spanish duties get mixed up. B2B e-invoicing under Order HAC/1028/2026 starts 6 October 2027 for businesses above 8 million euro of turnover and 6 October 2028 for the rest. Verifactu, the rule for invoicing software, is separate.

On 5 October 2026 the Finance Ministry announced it plans to move the still-pending Verifactu duties of Real Decreto 1007/2023 to October 2028, to line up with e-invoicing. Trade press reports it widely. But several sources agree it is not in the BOE yet: a royal decree amending RD 1007/2023 is still needed. Until then Real Decreto-ley 15/2025 stands, with 1 January 2027 for companies and 1 July 2027 for others.

Software vendors face the practical risk: the technical requirements (integrity, traceability, inalterability) are reported to stay the same, so building to them now costs nothing if the date slips. I have not read the ministry note itself, only reports of it, and the e-invoice preflight Spain page says so.
