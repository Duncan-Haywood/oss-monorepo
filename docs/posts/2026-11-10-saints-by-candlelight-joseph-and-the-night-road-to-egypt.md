Episode 83 of Saints by Candlelight tells Joseph's part of Matthew's Gospel in about 6,500 words of script: his quiet plan to divorce Mary without shame, the first dream, the second dream and the flight by night, and the return to Nazareth after Herod's death.

The hook is that Matthew records no word spoken by Joseph, so the episode says out loud that every word it gives him is invented, and keeps the angel's words close to the Gospel. Herod and the Holy Innocents are told briefly and plainly, with the Catholic and Orthodox feast dates.

Honesty flags: the gifts of the wise men paying for the road, the Hosea reading and the "Nazarene" line are named as readings. The palm tree, falling idols, Coptic route and the donkey are later tradition and left out. The workshop, the packing, the well, the Egyptian market and the return are invented and labelled.
