Floor tile was already in the takeoff. A shower is different: the walls are tiled, a niche takes tile out but not board, and the wall behind needs cement backer board and often a membrane.

`--wall-tile` takes each surface as a width and height in inches, with its openings. For a tub surround of 60 x 72, 30 x 72 and 30 x 72 in with a 12 x 12 in niche, that is 60 sq ft of wall and 59 sq ft of tile. With 3 x 6 in tile, a 1/8 in joint and 10% waste, it comes to 488 tiles, one bag of thinset and one of grout. The board is cut round the niche, so it is bought for all 60 sq ft plus waste: five 3 x 5 ft sheets and 90 screws.

Every coverage, waste and screw rate is a typed default, not a manufacturer's figure, and nothing here has been checked against a real tile order. Type your product's numbers. Edge trim, corners, curbs, benches and the pan liner are not counted. The lines also go into the budget.
