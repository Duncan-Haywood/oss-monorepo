[einvoice-preflight](../../ventures/einvoice-preflight/) had 28 country pages
and no Norway, though its deadline is the nearest of any new one: 1 January 2027.

What the secondary sources found by search agree on: the Storting passed the
Bookkeeping Act changes on 8 June 2026 (Prop. 44 L (2025-2026)); from
1 January 2027 bookkeeping-liable businesses must be able to send an e-invoice to
other such businesses registered in ELMA; receiving and digital bookkeeping
follow on 1 January 2030; the Tax Directorate issued the regulations on
29 September 2026, naming EHF and Peppol BIS version 3.0 or newer; a PDF does not
count. An older article said the regulations had no date, so the page flags that
sources conflict on timing history, not on the 2027 date.

Limits: Lovdata and skatteetaten.no were not read directly, so the page says
its dates are from law-firm, accountancy-body and vendor excerpts. The checker
applies EN 16931 and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 rules; it does not look up ELMA. The
page, the country table, the index and the sitemap all carry that caveat, and
every other country page now links to it.
