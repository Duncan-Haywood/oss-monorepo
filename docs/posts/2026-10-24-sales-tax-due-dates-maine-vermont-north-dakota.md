# Maine is the 15th, Vermont the 25th, North Dakota month end

The open-source ai-bookkeeper sales tax return assumes the 20th of the month
after the period unless a state is listed otherwise. We added three states that
differ:

- **Maine:** the 15th (Maine Revenue Services Rule 304), moved to the next
  business day on a weekend or holiday.
- **Vermont:** the 25th (Department of Taxes FAQ). Monthly returns for January
  are due the 23rd; that exception is not modelled yet.
- **North Dakota:** the last day of the month after the period (third-party
  guides; the state site was not read).

**Iowa stays out.** Quarterly and most monthly returns look like month end, but
monthly prepayments are the 20th and guides conflict, so a single number would
mislead.

These come from search excerpts, not the full state forms. Check your own
notice before filing. This is an estimate tool, not tax advice.
