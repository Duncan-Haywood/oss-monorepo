---
title: "Does recovering energy from an excavator boom pay back in fuel?"
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [research, hydraulics, construction]
description: Priced 1 to 5 kW recovery generators against the diesel an excavator boom could save; payback is thousands of hours.
---

A small recovery generator on an excavator boom captures at most 3 to 10% of the work done, per our heavy-hydraulics model. Pricing 1.5 to 5 kW 48 V generators from retail listings ($300 to $1,700) and valuing the diesel saved gives a payback of 8,000 to 46,000 hours at a typical duty, and 2,700 to 15,000 hours in the best case. On fuel alone it does not pay. These are retail wind-generator prices, a floor, not quotes for construction-grade hardware, and the fuel figures use an assumed 250 g/kWh. Method and numbers: `research/heavy-hydraulics/LOG.md`, milestone 5f.
