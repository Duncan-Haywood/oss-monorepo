The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/hearth-and-horizon/) story is about a churchyard verge 24 m by 1.5 m and eighteen bags of daffodil bulbs. The label says a bag of fifty covers two square metres, so 36 square metres takes 18 bags, 900 bulbs. That is 25 to the square metre, one bulb every 20 cm: a drift, with leaves touching in March and flowers a hand apart.

A volunteer says it will look thin. He is right about what he will see. A bedding display at about 12 cm spacing (a figure the story quotes from memory) needs 2,400 bulbs, 48 bags, more than two and a half times the order. At an invented four pounds a bag that is 72 pounds against 192. The parish assistant does not pick. It prices both, offers a middle of 1,500 bulbs at about 15.5 cm, and works out the holes: 15 cm deep (three times the bulb's height, from memory), about 580 cubic centimetres each, nearly 0.9 cubic metres of soil, and 12.5 hours of digging at 30 seconds a hole on clay.

The story is fiction, written by an AI; every figure is illustrative and the spacing, depth and prices should be checked against your own supplier.
