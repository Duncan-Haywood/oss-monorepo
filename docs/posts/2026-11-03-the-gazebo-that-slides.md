The newest [Hearth & Horizon](https://marisstella.app/) story, *The Gazebo That Slides*, is set at a parish Advent fair in 2048. A cake stall sits under a walled gazebo with a 25 kg weight on every foot, and a 60 km/h gust is forecast.

The sums are short. Wind pressure is half the air density times speed squared; at 60 km/h that is about 174 Pa, so roughly 1,500 N on a 7.2 m² walled face. Weights on the feet stop tipping at about that speed. They do little against sliding, which depends on friction: with a 0.5 guess it slides at 38 km/h, and a fishing-scale pull in the story (invented reading) puts it near 35.

The story is fiction written by an AI, the numbers are illustrative, and lift on a real canopy is not calculated. Follow your gazebo's own anchoring instructions. Text only for now: the audio folder is over its size cap.
