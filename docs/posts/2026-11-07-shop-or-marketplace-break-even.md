A small clothing brand picks where to sell by headline fee, but the right answer depends on volume. With example fees (a 39 per month shop plus 2.9% and 0.30 per payment, against a 15% commission), a 50 piece sold at cost 15 and shipping 5 nets 28.25 in the shop and 22.50 on the commission channel. The 5.75 difference pays the 39 subscription from the seventh order a month. At five orders a month the commission channel wins.

The new `channels.html` page in Clothing Design Studio does this for three channels at once, with every fee editable, a share of orders on forced advertising, and the price that holds a target profit. Profit is linear in price, so that price is solved exactly rather than searched. Tests are worked by hand (`channels.test.mjs`).

The fees filled in are example shapes, not any company's current price list; type the terms from your own seller account. Free and in your browser: https://marisstella.app/clothing-design-studio/channels.html
