# Market Research

## Total Addressable Market (TAM)

The global wall art and art print market is massive and growing, driven by millennial and Gen Z consumers shifting away from mass-produced posters toward more unique, "authentic" decor.
- **Global Art Market:** ~$65 Billion
- **Online Art Sales:** ~$10-12 Billion (Growing at ~10% CAGR)
- **Art Print Market (SAM):** Estimated at $3-5 Billion globally, specifically targeting high-quality reproductions and open/limited editions.

## Target Audience

### Supply: The Artists
- **Who:** Emerging to mid-career painters, illustrators, and mixed-media artists.
- **Pain Points:** 
  - They love creating but hate marketing, shipping, and customer service.
  - Selling originals is inconsistent and hard to scale.
  - Existing print platforms (Society6, Redbubble) feel cheap, dilute their brand, and offer terrible margins (often <10%).
- **Why us:** We offer a "white-glove" service. They don't have to scan their own work or manage a storefront. They get a premium brand association and a fair 30% of *net profit*.

### Demand: The Buyers
- **Who:** 
  - **The Decorator (B2C):** Millennials and Gen Z (ages 25-45), buying their first home or upgrading an apartment. They want art that tells a story and supports a real artist, but cannot afford $2,000+ originals. Target price point: $50 - $300.
  - **The Professional (B2B):** Interior designers, boutique hoteliers, and set decorators needing high-quality, curated art at scale without the friction of sourcing from 50 different galleries.
- **What they want:** Museum-quality paper/canvas, beautiful framing options, and a connection to the artist's story.

## The "White Space" (Our Wedge)

The biggest bottleneck for traditional painters entering the print market is the **digitization step**. A large canvas is incredibly difficult to photograph correctly without professional lighting, a color checker, and post-processing skills. 

By solving the *physical-to-digital bridge* as a service, we unlock supply that is not available on any other print platform. We are not just a marketplace; we are the digitization layer.
