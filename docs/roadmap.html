Roadmap

Phased delivery for leather boutique pilot

Phase 1 — Camera + POS Now

  • Door line-crossing counts (entry, exit, bounce heuristic)
  • POS CSV/API adapter for one vendor
  • SQLite store + daily rollups
  • LAN dashboard: entries, conversion, top SKUs
  • Privacy defaults: no raw video retention

Phase 2 — Product engagement Next

  • Fixture zones in CV or scanner/RFID events
  • Touch / look proxies per SKU
  • Desirability gap & rule-based recommendations
  • Simple A/B experiment registry

Phase 3 — Window & display Later

  • Outdoor wide-angle: pass-by, look-in, enter funnel
  • Display content scheduling hooks (manual v1)
  • Window conversion optimization suggestions

Phase 4 — Statistical rigor & ops Later

  • Power-aware experiment planning
  • Margin-aware recommendations (COGS if provided)
  • Optional encrypted aggregate backup (EU region only)
