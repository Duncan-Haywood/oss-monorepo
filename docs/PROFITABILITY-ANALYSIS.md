# Profitability Analysis

This document expands on the unit economics to project broader business profitability, break-even milestones, and the transition from Print-On-Demand (POD) to in-house fulfillment.

## Unit Economics (Deep Dive)

Let's model three different print sizes using an average high-quality POD provider (e.g., Prodigi, PrintSpace3D, or White Wall).

| Metric | Small (8x10) | Medium (16x20) | Large (24x36) |
|--------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Retail Price** | $45.00 | $85.00 | $150.00 |
| Payment Fee (3%) | $1.35 | $2.55 | $4.50 |
| POD Cost (Print) | $8.00 | $18.00 | $35.00 |
| POD Cost (Shipping)| $5.00 | $10.00 | $15.00 |
| **Net Profit** | **$30.65** | **$54.45** | **$95.50** |
| **Artist Share (30%)** | $9.20 | $16.34 | $28.65 |
| **Atelier Share (70%)**| **$21.45** | **$38.11** | **$66.85** |
| *Blended Margin* | *47.6%* | *44.8%* | *44.5%* |

*Note: Atelier Share must cover customer acquisition (CAC), software, and operations.*

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. LTV

To achieve profitability, our advertising economics must strictly adhere to a sustainable ratio.

- **Target CAC:** $15.00 - $20.00 per acquired customer.
- **AOV (Average Order Value):** $85.00 (Assuming Medium is the standard seller).
- **Atelier Gross Profit per Order:** ~$38.00.
- **Contribution Margin 1 (Post-Ad):** $38.00 - $20.00 = **$18.00**.

If LTV increases (repeat buyers), this model becomes highly lucrative. Art buyers often buy in sets (e.g., gallery walls), so incentivizing multi-piece purchases (bundling) is critical to boosting AOV.

## Break-Even Analysis

### Phase 1: The Bootstrapped Pilot
- **Fixed Costs:** Software (Shopify, AI tools, domains) = ~$150/mo.
- **Variable Costs:** Photographer capture sessions.
  - Assume we pay a photographer $50 per session (capturing ~5 pieces). Cost per piece digitized = $10.
- **Goal:** Digitize 100 pieces ($1,000 capex). 
- **Break-Even:** To recoup the $1,150 (capex + 1 mo fixed), we need to sell approximately **30 Medium prints** (30 * $38 = $1,140).

### Phase 2: Scale & Operations Team
- **Fixed Costs:** Operations Manager ($4k/mo), Software & Servers ($500/mo) = $4,500/mo.
- **Variable Costs:** CAC ($20/order).
- **Break-Even Volume:** We need to generate $4,500 in Contribution Margin 1.
  - $4,500 / $18.00 CM1 = **250 print sales per month**.
  - At a 2% conversion rate, this requires ~12,500 highly targeted website visitors per month.

## Margin Expansion Strategy (In-House vs. POD)

Currently, POD providers take a significant cut of the margin to cover their own overhead and profit. As volume scales, bringing printing in-house dramatically shifts the economics.

### The In-House Tipping Point
A commercial fine art printer (e.g., Epson SureColor or Canon imagePROGRAF) costs ~$3,000 - $5,000.
- **POD 16x20 Print Cost:** $18.00
- **In-House 16x20 Print Cost (Ink + Fine Art Paper):** ~$3.50
- **Margin Gained:** +$14.50 per print.

**When to switch?** 
If we are selling 250 medium prints a month, bringing printing in-house saves $3,625 per month in print costs (excluding labor/shipping materials). This easily pays off the printer in under two months.

*Strategic note:* We only bring *best-selling sizes and papers* in-house initially, routing edge cases (canvas, massive sizes, international orders) to the POD network.
