# Artist Pitch (Digital First)

## One-Liner

*Upload a photo from your phone. We use AI to create museum-quality prints, sell them online, and send you 30% of the profits — you keep painting.*

## The Conversation

### Their reality

You finish a piece. It sells once — maybe. Or it sits in the studio. Either way, the hours you put in don't keep paying you. Setting up a print shop, dealing with professional photographers, and managing shipping is a massive headache.

### Our offer

1. **You snap a photo** of your painting with your smartphone and upload it to our portal.
2. **Our AI enhances it** — correcting lighting, fixing perspective, and upscaling the image to a high-fidelity, print-ready digital master.
3. **We merchandise it** — AI generates stunning room mockups and listing copy.
4. **We list and sell** prints through our store and B2B channels.
5. **We fulfill** — print, pack, ship via our global fulfillment API. You touch nothing.
6. **You earn** 30% of net profit on every print sold.

### Materials support (optional)

If covering canvas, paint, or supplies is a barrier, we add **up to 30% materials reimbursement** on top of the profit share. Terms defined per agreement.

## What We Need From the Artist

- A smartphone photo of the finished piece (following our simple 3-step guide).
- Basic details (Title, Medium, Dimensions, Year).
- Permission to sell prints of the digitized image (scope defined in contract).
- Bank details or preferred payout method for revenue share.

## What the Artist Keeps

- The original painting — always.
- Credit and attribution on all listings.
- Right to sell the original independently.
- Complete transparency via your real-time sales dashboard.

## What We Handle

- AI upscaling, color correction, and perspective fixing.
- Automated creation of lifestyle mockups.
- Listing copy, SEO, and storefront presentation.
- Print production and global shipping.
- Customer service on fulfillment issues.
- Digital marketing and B2B outreach.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Can a phone photo really print well?" | Yes. Our proprietary AI upscaling models are trained specifically on fine art to preserve brushstrokes and texture, turning a 12MP photo into a 300 DPI large-format print file. |
| "I already sell prints myself" | Great — we expand your reach without replacing your direct sales. Non-exclusive options available. |
| "30% sounds low" | Compare to a gallery taking 50% on one original sale. This is 30% on *every* print, forever, with zero labor, zero photography costs, and zero shipping from you. |
| "What if it doesn't sell?" | Zero cost to you. Worst case: you spent 2 minutes uploading a photo. |

## Pilot Ask (Phase 0)

We're starting with a small group of artists to prove the AI pipeline and economics. Pilot artists get:

- Priority placement in the founding catalog
- Input on proofing and paper options
- Founding-artist badge/story on the storefront

**Ask:** Upload 3–5 photos of finished pieces to our portal today.

## Contract Outline (Draft — Not Legal Advice)

1. **Grant of license** — non-exclusive (or exclusive, TBD) print reproduction rights for specified works
2. **Revenue share** — 30% of net profit per sale; definition of "net" documented
3. **Materials clause** — optional 30% reimbursement with cap and approval process
4. **Attribution** — artist name prominently on all listings
5. **Term & termination** — duration, notice period, what happens to listed inventory
6. **Original ownership** — explicitly retained by artist

> **Note:** Final agreements require review by a qualified attorney before use.
