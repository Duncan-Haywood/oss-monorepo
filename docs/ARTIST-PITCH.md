# Artist Pitch

## One-Liner

*Download our app, scan your finished painting in minutes, and we automatically sell museum-quality prints online, sending you 30% of the profits — you keep painting.*

## The Conversation (Digital Outreach)

### Their reality

You finish a piece. It sells once — maybe. Or it sits in the studio. To sell prints, you have to pay for expensive professional photography, manage a Shopify store, deal with printers, and pack boxes. It's a second job you didn't ask for.

### Our offer (The "Zero-Effort" Print Business)

1. **You Scan:** Download the Print Atelier app. Our AI guides you to take a perfect, glare-free, color-accurate scan using just your smartphone.
2. **We Process:** Our software instantly stitches, color-corrects, and upscales the image to a massive, print-ready digital master.
3. **We Market:** Our AI generates hyper-realistic room mockups and lists your art across our global storefronts.
4. **We Fulfill:** When a customer buys, our global print partners produce and ship the archival print directly to them.
5. **You Earn:** You get 30% of the net profit on every print sold, deposited directly to your bank account.

## What We Need From the Artist

- A smartphone (iOS or Android).
- Finished, dry work ready to be scanned in even lighting.
- 5 minutes to use the app and sign the digital agreement.
- Bank details (via Stripe) to receive payouts.

## What the Artist Keeps

- The original painting — always.
- Credit and attribution on all listings.
- The right to sell the original independently.
- A real-time dashboard showing views, sales, and earnings.

## What We Handle (Everything Else)

- The expensive digitization process (replaced by our AI app).
- Listing copy, SEO, and interior design mockups.
- Print production, shipping, and global logistics.
- Customer service and returns.
- Paid advertising and digital marketing.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Can a phone really capture my art?" | Yes. Our app takes multiple overlapping photos and uses AI super-resolution to create a file larger and sharper than most professional DSLRs. We also send you a color-calibration card to ensure perfect hues. |
| "I already sell prints myself" | Great — we expand your reach. Our agreement is non-exclusive. We are just another revenue channel for you. |
| "30% sounds low" | Compare this to a gallery taking 50% on one original sale, or the hours you spend packing boxes for 100% of a print sale. This is 30% of *passive* income, forever, with zero labor or upfront cost from you. |
| "I don't want my work everywhere" | You control which pieces you scan and publish. |
| "What if it doesn't sell?" | It costs you nothing but 5 minutes of scanning. There are no listing fees or subscription costs. |

## The Onboarding Flow

1. Artist clicks link in our automated outreach DM/email.
2. Lands on a mobile-optimized pitch page.
3. Downloads the app.
4. Creates account & connects Stripe.
5. Scans first piece.
6. Piece is live and selling within 10 minutes.

## Contract Outline (Digital Click-Wrap)

1. **Grant of license** — non-exclusive print reproduction rights for the scanned works.
2. **Revenue share** — 30% of net profit per sale, clearly defined in the app.
3. **Attribution** — artist name prominently on all listings.
4. **Term & termination** — cancel anytime, listings removed within 48 hours.
5. **Original ownership** — explicitly retained by artist.

> **Note:** Final agreements are presented as a frictionless click-wrap terms of service during app onboarding.
