# ShootFlow Terms and Conditions Draft

This is a product-design draft, not legal advice. Before public release, counsel
should review it against the final app behavior, billing model, privacy posture,
and jurisdictions where the product is offered.

## Intended use

ShootFlow is intended to help users ingest, organize, analyze, color prepare, and
generate draft social-media deliverables from media they own or are authorized to
process.

## User responsibilities

- Users are responsible for maintaining independent backups of all source media.
- Users should verify ingest manifests and copied files before reformatting,
  erasing, deleting, or physically handing off any camera card or drive.
- Users are responsible for confirming they have rights, releases, licenses, and
  permissions for media, music, likenesses, trademarks, captions, and exports.
- Users must review AI-generated edits, captions, hashtags, timelines, and
  deliverables before publishing or delivering them to clients.

## File safety limitations

ShootFlow should be designed to avoid automatic source deletion, but no software
can guarantee protection from device failure, operating-system errors,
misconfiguration, power loss, hardware defects, or user action outside the app.

The product should present destructive operations, if ever added, as separate
explicit workflows with clear warnings and verification gates. The default
workflow is copy, verify, manifest, then review.

## AI and creative output disclaimer

AI analysis and generation may be incomplete, inaccurate, biased, aesthetically
unsuitable, or inconsistent with a client's brand requirements. Generated grades,
edits, reels, carousels, captions, and selections are drafts until approved by
the user.

## Indemnification concept

For a commercial release, include counsel-reviewed language requiring users to
indemnify and hold the company harmless from claims arising from:

- media the user uploads, imports, edits, publishes, or distributes;
- rights, releases, copyright, music, trademark, or likeness disputes;
- client delivery decisions made from AI-generated or user-approved outputs;
- misuse of the app outside documented workflows;
- deletion, formatting, or loss of source media caused by user action or third
  party tools.

## Privacy and processing concept

The app should disclose which processing happens locally and which processing, if
any, uses cloud VLMs, render workers, analytics, crash reporting, or support
uploads. Users should be able to keep sensitive client shoots local unless they
explicitly opt into cloud processing.

## Release checklist before using these terms

- Align terms with final product name, entity, billing, cancellation, and refund
  policy.
- Add privacy policy and data-processing disclosures.
- Add limitation-of-liability caps appropriate to the business model.
- Add jurisdiction, arbitration, DMCA, acceptable-use, and age requirements if
  needed.
- Make in-product file-safety warnings match the terms exactly.
