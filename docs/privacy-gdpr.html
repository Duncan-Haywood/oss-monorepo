Privacy & GDPR (Italy / EU)

Technical and organizational controls — not legal advice

Important: Video analytics in a retail environment typically involves processing personal data (images of identifiable individuals). This project is designed to minimize risk via on-device aggregation and purpose limitation, but the controller must still establish a lawful basis, transparency, and DPIA where required. Consult qualified counsel and, where needed, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Design principles

  1. Data minimization — Process video in memory; persist counts and SKU-level events, not faces
  2. Purpose limitation — Store operations analytics only; no marketing resale of footage
  3. Storage limitation — No raw video by default; short debug retention if enabled
  4. Integrity & confidentiality — LAN segmentation, encrypted disks, access logs
  5. Accountability — Document processing activities (Art. 30 record)

Prohibited / out of scope

  • Facial recognition or persistent “shopper ID”
  • Biometric categorization (age/gender inference for profiling)
  • Sharing raw or identifiable imagery with third parties without DPA
  • Automated decisions with legal/similar effect on individuals (not applicable to v1)

Lawful basis (indicative — confirm with counsel)

Controllers often evaluate:

  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) with LIA balancing test for security and limited analytics
  • Where cameras are prominent, transparency and opt-out expectations for non-essential analytics
  • Staff areas may need separate basis or exclusion zones

DPIA checklist (pilot)

  • ☐ Describe processing: door camera, optional outdoor, POS join
  • ☐ Necessity & proportionality — why aggregates suffice
  • ☐ Risks to individuals — re-identification from rare events
  • ☐ Mitigations — aggregation, retention, access, vendor DPAs
  • ☐ Consult Garante if high risk remains

POS data

Ingest only operational fields needed for analytics. Exclude payment card numbers, full customer PII unless explicit consent and separate purpose. Pseudonymous basket_id is sufficient for funnel joins.

Subprocessors

Default pilot: no cloud subprocessor. If backup or remote support is added, execute Art. 28 DPAs and document transfers (SCCs if outside EEA).
