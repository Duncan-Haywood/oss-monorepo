# Strategy

## Problem

Working artists face a structural income ceiling: every painting is a one-time transaction tied directly to hours in the studio. Marketing, fulfillment, and repeat revenue require skills and infrastructure most painters do not want to build.

## Solution

Print Atelier sits between artists and buyers as an **AI-powered software and distribution layer**:

1. **Software-First Onboarding:** Artists upload smartphone photos of their work.
2. **AI Processing:** We use AI upscaling, color correction, and lighting normalization to transform standard photos into high-fidelity, print-ready digital masters.
3. **Automated Merchandising:** AI generates photorealistic room mockups, detail shots, and listing copy.
4. **Digital-First Distribution:** We programmatically list prints across multiple commerce channels and leverage AI-driven digital sales outreach.
5. **Print-on-Demand:** Fulfill via global print-on-demand APIs (zero inventory).
6. **Transparent Payouts:** Pay artists a transparent revenue share on every sale via an automated dashboard.

## Positioning

- **Not** a gallery taking 50% on a single original sale
- **Not** a manual photography service that requires scheduling and logistics
- **Is** a scalable software platform that turns finished work into long-tail inventory with zero friction for the artist.

## Growth Tracks

### Track 1: Supply (Artist Acquisition)

- **Digital-First Outreach:** Automated, personalized email and DM campaigns targeting artists on Instagram, TikTok, and portfolio sites.
- **Self-Serve Portal:** Artists can sign up, upload photos, and see their work transformed into products in minutes.
- **Pitch:** Upload a photo from your phone, we handle the AI enhancement, merchandising, and sales. Zero logistics burden.

### Track 2: Demand (Consumer Commerce)

- **Algorithmic Advertising:** AI-optimized ad creative and bidding across Meta, Google, and Pinterest.
- **Direct-to-Consumer Print Store:** High-converting storefront (Shopify/custom) integrated with our backend.
- **SEO & Content:** Programmatic generation of artist stories, collections, and interior design content.

### Track 3: B2B & Wholesale (Digital Outreach)

- **Interior Designers & Hospitality:** Automated outbound campaigns to interior designers, boutique hotels, and corporate offices offering curated, trade-priced print packages.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Artists onboarded | Supply pipeline depth (CAC should be near zero) |
| Pieces processed via AI | Catalog size (inventory) |
| Print conversion rate | Demand-side efficiency |
| Revenue per artist per month | Supply-side retention signal |
| Blended gross margin | Profitability of the software layer |
| CAC / LTV (buyers) | Ad spend viability |

## Competitive Moat (Over Time)

1. **AI Processing Pipeline** — Proprietary workflows for turning smartphone photos into museum-quality print files.
2. **Scale of Catalog** — Removing the photography bottleneck allows for exponential catalog growth.
3. **Automated Merchandising** — Generating thousands of high-quality lifestyle images at zero marginal cost.
4. **Artist Trust** — Transparent splits, reliable payouts, and a magical "it just works" onboarding experience.

## Phase Plan

| Phase | Focus |

| **0 — Foundation** | Build AI upscaling/correction pipeline prototype. Validate with smartphone photos from 5-10 artists. |
