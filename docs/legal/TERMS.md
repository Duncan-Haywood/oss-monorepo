# ShootFlow Personal Tool Terms and Conditions Draft

This document is a working draft for product development. Have qualified counsel
review it before public launch or paid use.

## 1. Purpose

ShootFlow is intended to help photographers and videographers ingest, organize,
color grade, edit, and package media into social-ready deliverables. The product
may operate on valuable original files, including RAW photo files, camera logs,
video clips, project files, previews, and generated edits.

## 2. User Responsibility for Backups

Users are responsible for maintaining independent backups of all source media
before using ShootFlow. ShootFlow should be treated as an assistant to a
professional workflow, not as the only copy or archive of a shoot.

 Recommended minimum backup posture:

 - Keep the original camera card untouched until ingest verification completes.
 - Maintain at least two independent local copies before formatting any card.
 - Keep at least one offsite or cloud backup for paid client work.
 - Review conflict reports and manifests before deleting or overwriting anything.

 ## 3. No Automatic Deletion of Source Media

 ShootFlow's safety posture is non-destructive by default. The application should
 not delete, format, overwrite, or mutate source media automatically. Any future
 destructive operation must require explicit user confirmation, visible file lists,
 and verification evidence.

 ## 4. AI-Generated Outputs

 AI-generated grades, selects, edits, captions, reels, carousels, and related
 outputs may contain errors, omissions, artifacts, style mismatches, or licensing
 issues. Users are responsible for reviewing all outputs before publication,
 client delivery, advertising, or commercial use.

 ## 5. Professional Judgment

 ShootFlow does not replace a professional editor, colorist, producer, lawyer, or
 rights-clearance process. Users are responsible for confirming that generated
 content meets artistic, contractual, release, usage-rights, platform, and brand
 requirements.

 ## 6. Indemnification

 To the fullest extent permitted by law, users agree to defend, indemnify, and hold
 harmless the ShootFlow developers, owners, affiliates, contractors, and service
 providers from and against claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and
 expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from or related to:

 - User media, prompts, metadata, captions, music, likenesses, trademarks, or other
   content processed through ShootFlow.
 - Publication, delivery, sale, or distribution of outputs generated or assisted by
   ShootFlow.
 - User failure to maintain backups or verify ingest, edits, exports, rights, or
   delivery requirements.
 - User violation of applicable laws, contracts, releases, platform rules, or third
   party rights.

 ## 7. Limitation of Liability

 ShootFlow should not be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special,
 exemplary, or punitive damages, including lost profits, lost business, lost media,
 lost opportunities, reputational harm, or replacement editing costs. Liability
 should be capped to the amount paid for the product or service during a defined
 lookback period, subject to legal review and applicable law.

 ## 8. Future Commercialization Notes

 Before selling ShootFlow, add complete terms covering:

 - Subscription billing, refunds, cancellation, and trial policy.
 - Privacy, telemetry, model-provider processing, and data retention.
 - DMCA, takedown, and rights-dispute handling.

 - Enterprise/client confidentiality requirements for agencies and studios.
