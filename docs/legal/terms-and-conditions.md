# Draft Terms and Conditions Notes

This is product-planning language for legal review, not legal advice and not a
ready-to-publish agreement. Before shipping, have counsel adapt it to the actual
company, jurisdictions, data handling, subscription model, and app-store terms.

## Core user obligations

- Users are responsible for maintaining independent backups of original media.
- Users must confirm they have rights to process, edit, and publish submitted
  photos, videos, audio, music, names, likenesses, logos, and other materials.
- Users must review generated edits, captions, and exports before publishing.
- Users may not use the product to violate privacy, publicity, copyright,
  trademark, platform, or client-contract obligations.

## File safety disclosure

The product should state plainly that automated media workflows can involve
large files, external drives, removable cards, proxies, caches, and generated
exports. The intended design is copy-then-verify and non-destructive handling of
originals, but users remain responsible for backup practices and final review.

## Indemnification draft

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the user agrees to defend,
indemnify, and hold harmless the product provider, its affiliates, officers,
directors, employees, contractors, and agents from and against any claims,
damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses, including reasonable
attorneys' fees, arising out of or related to:

1. the user's media, prompts, instructions, exports, captions, or published
   content;
2. the user's use of the product in violation of these terms or applicable law;
3. alleged infringement or violation of intellectual property, privacy,
   publicity, contractual, or other third-party rights by the user's materials or
   outputs;
4. the user's failure to maintain backups or review generated outputs before
   publication; or
5. disputes between the user and clients, editors, collaborators, brands, or
   social platforms.

## Limitation-of-liability considerations

Counsel should draft limits for indirect, incidental, consequential, special,
exemplary, and lost-profit damages. For a media workflow product, the agreement
should explicitly address lost files, corrupted media, missed publishing windows,
client disputes, lost revenue, and social-platform account outcomes.

## Product promises to avoid until proven

Avoid absolute claims such as:

- "will never lose files"
- "guaranteed viral reels"
- "professional color grade with no review needed"
- "safe to delete your SD card after import"

Safer claims should be evidence-based and describe the workflow:

- "non-destructive ingest planning"
- "copy-then-verify workflow"
- "draft edits for review"
- "editable intermediates"
- "audit logs for media operations"
