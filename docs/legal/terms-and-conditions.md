# Draft Terms and Conditions

This document is a product-planning draft, not legal advice. Have qualified counsel review before using it with customers.

## User responsibility for media and backups

Users are responsible for maintaining independent backups of all photos, videos, audio, project files, presets, sidecars, and related media before using the app. The app is designed to copy and organize media conservatively, but no software workflow can guarantee protection against hardware failure, filesystem corruption, user error, operating-system bugs, third-party tool defects, or interrupted transfers.

Users should verify that originals and copies are complete before formatting cards, erasing drives, deleting files, or delivering work to clients. The app should not be treated as the sole archive of any shoot.

## No source deletion by default

The product's default ingest workflow does not delete source media. Any future source-clearing workflow must be separate from ingest, require explicit confirmation, and verify that redundant copies and manifests exist before recommending deletion.

## AI-generated outputs

AI-assisted grading, culling, reel assembly, captions, hashtags, and other generated outputs are recommendations. Users are responsible for reviewing outputs for quality, accuracy, copyright compliance, client obligations, likeness rights, platform rules, and professional suitability before publishing or delivering them.

## Third-party services

The product may integrate with local editing tools, cloud inference providers, storage providers, social platforms, or other third-party services. Users are responsible for complying with third-party terms, maintaining appropriate account permissions, and understanding that third-party outages or policy changes may affect the product.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, users agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the product owner, developers, affiliates, contractors, and licensors from and against claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or related to:

- user media, client materials, or other content processed through the product;
- user publication, delivery, licensing, or commercial use of AI-assisted outputs;
- user failure to maintain backups or verify copies before deleting, formatting, or overwriting media;
- user violation of law, third-party rights, platform rules, or client agreements;
- misuse of the product or use outside documented safety guidance.

## Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the product is provided without warranties of uninterrupted operation, perfect output quality, fitness for a particular professional engagement, or freedom from data-loss risk. Liability for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages should be disclaimed where legally permissible.

## Safety language for the app

The app should use clear, plain-language confirmations:

- "This import copies files and verifies checksums. It does not delete anything from the source."
- "Do not format or erase this card until you have verified at least two independent copies."
- "AI edits are drafts. Review before publishing or client delivery."
- "Conflicting files were preserved separately instead of overwritten."

## Implementation note

Keep legal copy aligned with product behavior. If future features add deletion, direct social posting, cloud uploads, or client collaboration, update these terms and add matching in-app consent screens.
