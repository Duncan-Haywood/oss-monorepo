# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Our GTM strategy is engineered around a **software-first, AI-driven** approach. Instead of manual sales and traditional advertising, we leverage automation to build both the supply (artists) and demand (buyers) sides of the marketplace simultaneously.

## Phase 1: Algorithmic Supply Acquisition (Artists)
Before we can sell, we need a premium catalog. Traditional galleries rely on manual scouting. We use software.

### AI-Driven Sales Outreach
1. **Scraping & Lead Generation:** We deploy scrapers to monitor Instagram, TikTok, and artist portfolios for visual artists meeting specific criteria (follower count, engagement rate, medium).
2. **AI Analysis:** Computer vision models analyze their portfolios to ensure the art style fits our curation parameters. Natural language processing evaluates their bio/captions to gauge if they currently sell prints.
3. **Automated Personalized Outreach:** AI drafts highly personalized DMs and emails referencing specific pieces of their work. Example: *"I love the texture in [Piece Name]. We help artists like you turn originals into passive income via museum-quality prints..."*
4. **Self-Serve Onboarding:** Interested artists are routed to a digital onboarding portal. They sign contracts via e-signature, upload existing high-res scans, or schedule a capture session entirely via software.

## Phase 2: Demand Generation (Buyers)
Once the catalog is seeded, we use a digital-first marketing engine to acquire customers.

### Programmatic Advertising & Content
1. **Dynamic Ad Generation:** AI systems automatically take the master artwork files and generate thousands of ad creatives:
   - Placing the art in diverse virtual room mockups (mid-century modern, minimalist, industrial).
   - Creating short-form video slideshows combining the art with the artist's process footage.
   - Writing ad copy tailored to different buyer personas.
2. **Algorithmic Bidding:** Programmatic ad buying across Meta, Pinterest, and Google. The system automatically shifts budget to the artist/artwork combinations that yield the lowest CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost).
3. **SEO Automation:** AI generates rich, SEO-optimized product descriptions, artist biographies, and blog content around art trends, capturing organic search intent.

## Phase 3: The B2B / Trade Expansion
A significant revenue driver will be B2B sales to interior designers, hotels, and corporate offices.

### Digital B2B Sales Outreach
1. **LinkedIn & Email Automation:** AI agents build lists of interior designers and procurement managers, initiating automated email sequences offering trade discounts and bulk ordering capabilities.
2. **Trade Portal:** A dedicated B2B software portal where designers can visualize art in their specific projects, manage client approvals, and order in bulk with automated invoicing.

## Flywheel Effect
Our software-first approach creates a compounding advantage:
- **More Artists** → **More Art Data** → **Better AI Ad Generation** → **More Sales** → **Higher Artist Payouts** → **Easier to Recruit Top Artists**

## Key GTM Metrics
- **Automated Outreach Conversion Rate:** % of AI-generated messages that result in a signed artist.
- **Time-to-Catalog:** Hours from artist signature to live listing (driven down by software automation).
- **Creative Testing Velocity:** Number of AI-generated ad variants tested per week.
- **CAC to LTV Ratio:** Strictly monitored via our analytics dashboard to ensure profitable scale.
