# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Because Print Atelier is a two-sided marketplace, our GTM strategy must address both Supply (Artists) and Demand (Buyers) simultaneously. However, **Supply must lead Demand**. We cannot run ads without a robust, high-quality catalog.

## Phase 1: The Local Pilot (Months 1-3)
**Goal:** Prove the operational pipeline, establish the quality bar, and validate unit economics.

### Supply (0 to 50 pieces)
- **Direct Network:** Onboard 2-3 local artists (including founding connections) who have a backlog of finished, unmonetized work.
- **The "White Glove" Treatment:** Founder acts as the field photographer. We handle 100% of the capture, color correction, and uploading. 
- **Output:** A founding catalog of 30-50 high-quality pieces, spanning a few distinct styles.

### Demand (First 100 Sales)
- **Leveraging Artist Audiences:** We provide our pilot artists with beautiful AI-generated room mockups and process videos of their work. They share these with their existing Instagram/newsletter audiences, driving warm traffic to our storefront.
- **Outcome:** We validate that the POD provider's quality is acceptable, the Shopify routing works, and the net-profit split math is accurate.

## Phase 2: Digital Scale (Months 3-9)
**Goal:** Build a scalable, software-driven acquisition engine for both sides.

### Supply (50 to 500 pieces)
- **AI-Driven Outbound:**
  1. Scrape Instagram hashtags (`#oilpainting`, `#contemporaryart`, `#studioflow`) and local gallery websites to build a database of target artists.
  2. Use LLMs to draft highly personalized DMs/emails referencing specific pieces in their portfolio.
  3. **The Pitch:** "We love *[Specific Painting]*. We want to send a photographer to your studio, digitize it for free, and pay you 30% of every print we sell of it."
- **The "City Blitz":** Instead of random geographic growth, we target one city at a time (e.g., Austin, TX). We hire a freelance photographer in that city, line up 10 artists via our outbound engine, and have the photographer capture all 10 in a single week.

### Demand (Scaling beyond the artist's network)
- **Paid Acquisition (Meta & Pinterest):**
  - Use our AI pipeline to generate hundreds of dynamic room mockups.
  - Run conversion-optimized campaigns targeting interior design interests, new homeowners, and fans of similar artists.
  - Track CAC strictly. If CAC > $20, pause and refine targeting/creative.
- **Content Engine:** Post the "behind the scenes" process videos (captured during the photographer sessions) on TikTok and Instagram Reels. Process videos inherently go viral more often than static images of finished art.

## Phase 3: B2B & Channel Expansion (Months 9-18)
**Goal:** Increase Average Order Value (AOV) and secure recurring revenue streams.

### Demand (B2B Sales)
- **Interior Designers & Stagers:** Create a trade program offering a 15% discount for bulk orders. Reach out to boutique interior design firms. They need high-quality art to stage homes or design commercial spaces (hotels, cafes) and hate dealing with 50 different artists. We become their single point of contact.
- **Corporate Gifting / Office Decor:** Pitch tech startups and modern offices to decorate their spaces with local, curated art. 

### Supply (Institutional)
- **Art Schools & MFA Programs:** Partner with graduating MFA students. They are producing high-quality work but typically have zero distribution. We become their default monetization engine the day they graduate.
