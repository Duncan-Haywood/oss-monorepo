# Draft Terms and Conditions

This document is a working product draft, not legal advice. Have qualified counsel review before release.

## Service scope

Shotflow helps users ingest, organize, color-grade, and assemble photo and video content. Output quality depends on source media, device performance, model availability, user prompts, third-party services, and export targets.

## User responsibility for media

Users remain responsible for maintaining their own backups. Shotflow should be used with at least two independent copies of important media before any source card, SSD, NAS, or cloud folder is reformatted, erased, or repurposed.

## No automatic deletion

Shotflow must not delete source files automatically. Any future destructive operation must require explicit user action, clear warnings, a restorable audit trail where possible, and verification that at least one destination copy exists.

## Indemnification

To the maximum extent permitted by law, users agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the product owner, contributors, affiliates, and service providers from claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising from:

- user-provided media, music, likenesses, trademarks, captions, or other content;
- publication of generated reels, carousels, photos, videos, or metadata;
- user instructions that cause copyright, privacy, publicity, licensing, or platform-policy issues;
- edits, color grades, crops, captions, or automated selections approved or published by the user;
- misuse of the software, including use without adequate backups.

## AI-assisted output disclaimer

AI-generated selections, grades, captions, timelines, and exports can be wrong, biased, incomplete, or visually undesirable. Users must review outputs before delivery, publication, or client use.

## Limitation of liability

The product should be offered without warranties of uninterrupted operation, perfect media preservation, platform acceptance, or fitness for a particular project. Liability should be limited to the amount paid for the service over a defined period, subject to counsel's review and applicable law.

## Third-party services

Future integrations may use cloud AI providers, local ML runtimes, social platforms, codec libraries, storage providers, or payment processors. Users are responsible for complying with each provider's terms, licensing rules, and usage limits.

## Release checklist before commercialization

- Counsel-reviewed terms, privacy policy, and data-processing language.
- Clear backup guidance in onboarding and pre-ingest flows.
- Permissions copy for removable drives, Photos library, network volumes, and cloud folders.
- Explicit confirmation gates for publish, overwrite, relink, transcode replacement, or destructive workflows.
- Platform-specific disclosure for App Store review if local or cloud AI models process user media.
