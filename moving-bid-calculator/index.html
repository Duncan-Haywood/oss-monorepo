      FOR LOCAL MOVING COMPANIES

      Bid the walk, not the bedroom count.

      Price a local move from the hours the crew will actually spend: inventory on a first-floor short carry, then the extra trips stairs and a long carry create, then packing and drive time. You get the job price, what it works out to per hour and per cubic foot, and the number below which the Saturday loses money.

      How much should movers charge for a local move? · this calculator vs. SmartMoving · this calculator vs. a bedroom-count quote.

      What you are moving

      One row per room or specialty item. Clock hours are first-floor, short-carry load and unload for the whole crew. Pick a type to start from a common figure, then replace it with a time from your own crew.

      Room or itemCubic feetClock hours

      Access, packing, and drive

      Stairs and a long carry add hours from cubic feet — more cubes, more trips. Elevator wait is flat: the crew sits through it together. Minutes per 200 cu ft are starting points; time your own crew and replace them.

      Your costs

      Payroll burden covers employer taxes, workers’ compensation, and paid time off — the part of a mover’s cost that never shows up on their pay rate. Matching a cheap “3-bedroom for $800” quote without seeing stairs is the most common way a Saturday loses money.

      Questions movers actually type

      Why can’t I bid by bedroom count?

      Two 3-bedroom houses can be a first-floor short carry or a third-floor walk-up with an 80-foot carry. Bedroom count cannot see the extra trips. That is how a Saturday that looked like $900 becomes a loss.

      How much extra should stairs add?

      Stairs add trips, so the extra hours should scale with how much you are moving, not a flat surcharge. Start from minutes per flight per 200 cubic feet, then replace it with a time from your own crew.

      Is this the price I should charge?

      No. The output is arithmetic on the numbers you entered. It is not a recommended price, a tariff, or a guarantee. Verify every figure against your payroll, insurance, and times before sending a bid.

        Common questions

        How do movers price a local job?

        From clock hours: load and unload time for the inventory, plus extra hours for stairs and a long carry that scale with cubic feet, plus packing and drive time. Hours times crew size times loaded wage, then truck, fuel, materials, overhead, and margin.

        Why can't I bid a move by bedroom count?

        Two 3-bedroom houses can be a first-floor short carry or a third-floor walk-up with an 80-foot carry. Bedroom count cannot see the extra trips. That is how a Saturday that looked like $900 becomes a loss.

        How much extra should stairs add to a moving quote?

        Stairs add trips, so the extra hours should scale with how much you are moving, not a flat surcharge. This tool starts from minutes per flight per 200 cubic feet and lets you replace that with a time from your own crew.

        Is this the price I should charge?

        No. The output is arithmetic on the numbers you entered. It is not a recommended price, a tariff, or a guarantee. Verify every figure against your own payroll, insurance, and times before sending a bid.
