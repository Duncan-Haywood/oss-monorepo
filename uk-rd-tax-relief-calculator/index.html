    Common questions
    
    
    What is R&D tax relief?
    
    
    A UK corporation tax relief for companies doing work that seeks an advance in science or technology. Since April 2024 most companies claim the merged scheme: a 20% taxable credit on qualifying costs.
    
    
    What is the difference between a profitable and a loss-making claim?
    
    
    A profitable company uses the credit to reduce its corporation tax bill, netting about 15p per £1 of qualifying cost at the 25% rate. A loss-maker can take it as cash at about 16.2p per £1, subject to a cap tied to PAYE and NIC.
    
    
    What is ERIS?
    
    
    Enhanced R&D Intensive Support: an extra 86% deduction and a 14.5% payable credit for loss-making companies whose qualifying R&D is at least 30% of total spend. It can be worth more than the merged scheme.
    
    
    Why are subcontractor costs only 65%?
    
    
    Only 65% of payments to unconnected subcontractors and externally provided workers counts as qualifying expenditure.
    
    
    What is the payable credit cap?
    
    
    Cash credits are capped at £20,000 plus 300% of the company's PAYE and NIC liability for the period. Anything above carries forward.
    
    
    Should I use a claims agency?
    
    
    Many charge 10-20% of the benefit. This calculator shows what the fee would be so you can compare it with doing the claim yourself or with an accountant on a fixed fee.
