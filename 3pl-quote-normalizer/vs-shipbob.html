3PL Quote Normalizer vs. ShipBob

ShipBob is one specific 3PL you would sign a fulfillment contract with. This page is a free worksheet for a different moment — you already have a quote in hand, from ShipBob or anyone else, and want to know whether it actually beats doing it in-house.

What each one is for

ShipBob is publicly documented as a third-party logistics provider: a network of fulfillment centers that receives, stores, and ships inventory on a brand's behalf under a signed contract, priced per account after you submit your volume, SKU count, and shipping profile. It is one option among many 3PLs a brand might quote, and its own pricing is not published as a flat rate card -- like most 3PLs, it is quoted per account, so no dollar figure for ShipBob is invented here.

This calculator does not compete with ShipBob for your fulfillment business, and it cannot get you a ShipBob quote. It does the opposite job: once you have a quote -- from ShipBob, from a competing 3PL, or from several you are weighing against each other -- you type its rate-card lines in here: receiving, storage, pick and pack, packaging, shipping, returns and return handling, restocking, fixed fees, and any monthly minimum. The calculator turns those into a comparable monthly total, a true cost per order, and the order volume where that quote's minimum charge or per-order pricing stops beating an in-house estimate you supply. Its dedicated pages cover the fine print a single blended rate usually hides: leftover pallets aging into long-term storage, restocking a return versus letting the customer keep it, paying to kit versus skipping the bundle, and which inbound method is cheaper at your volume -- questions that apply the same way whichever 3PL sent the quote. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or ShipBob's own quote?

They are not substitutes for each other. ShipBob is a third-party logistics network: a company you would sign a fulfillment contract with, publicly documented as providing warehousing, pick and pack, and shipping across a network of fulfillment centers, priced by requesting a custom quote for your volume and SKUs. This calculator does not provide a quote from ShipBob or any 3PL. It takes a rate-card quote you already have in hand -- from ShipBob, from a competing 3PL, or from several you are comparing -- and turns it into a comparable monthly total, a true cost per order, and the order volume where that quote stops being cheaper than doing it in-house. Someone who already has a ShipBob quote, or one from any other 3PL, and wants to check it against in-house costs or against a competing bid is in the right place here; someone who does not yet have a quote needs to request one from ShipBob or another 3PL first.

Does this calculator get pricing from ShipBob?

No. It has no connection to ShipBob, requests no data from them, and does not know ShipBob's rates. ShipBob, like most 3PLs, quotes custom rates per account rather than publishing a price list, so this page states no dollar figure for ShipBob. You type in the rate-card lines from a quote you already received -- receiving, storage, pick and pack, packaging, shipping, returns, fixed fees, and any monthly minimum -- from ShipBob or any other 3PL, and the calculator does the arithmetic.
