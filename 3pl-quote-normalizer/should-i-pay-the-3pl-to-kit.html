Common questions

Should I pay a 3PL to kit or skip the bundle?

The signed pick-and-pack line prices the first unit. Ops then bills assembly, extra component picks, and extra receiving. Pre-kitting before inbound keeps those extra 3PL lines at zero and pays only your labor. Skipping the kit keeps the hero SKU and forgoes the AOV lift. On a typical $6 lift / $2.80 extra COGS / $3.50 kit fee / $1 extra picks / $0.36 extra receiving bundle, 3PL assembly loses $1.66 per kit versus skipping it, while pre-kitting keeps $2.30.

When does paying the 3PL to assemble beat skipping the kit?

Subtract extra COGS, extra picks, and extra receiving from the AOV lift. That leftover is the highest kit fee that still beats skip. At the default $6 lift, $2.80 extra COGS, $1 extra picks, and $0.36 extra receiving, 3PL assembly first beats skip at a $1.84 kit fee or a $7.66 AOV lift.
