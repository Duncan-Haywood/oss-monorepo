    Common questions
    Does the total include returns and restocking, not just outbound fees?
    Yes. The returns line is orders × returns rate × (return handling fee + restock fee), using the return handling and restock inputs above the calculator. It does not include chargebacks, refunded product cost, or inventory written off on returns you can't resell — the dedicated return-disposition calculator covers that.
    What happens when my minimum monthly spend is higher than the line items add up to?
    The total switches to your minimum, and the “Minimum charge applied?” row tells you when that happened. Below the volume where the order-driven line items alone clear the minimum, the minimum is what's really setting your bill, not your order count — that's the threshold the sensitivity and negotiation pages linked above use.
    What does “in-house cost per order” need to include for a fair comparison?
    It's a single number you type in, not a breakdown. If it leaves out warehouse rent, software, packaging materials, or a share of your own labor, the in-house side looks artificially cheap and the break-even volume shown will be wrong. Build it up from your own costs before trusting the comparison.
    Does my quote data leave this browser?
    No. Everything is computed client-side; your inputs are saved to this browser's local storage only so the form survives a reload, and nothing is uploaded. Turn off usage counting with the button in the results panel if you don't want anonymous feature events recorded either.
