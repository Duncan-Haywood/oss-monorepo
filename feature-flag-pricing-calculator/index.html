    Common questions

    Does this read my feature-flag account?

    No. It uses only the monthly flag-evaluation volume (thousands of requests) you type and rate cards you can edit. It cannot see your plan, seat count, contract or invoice. Treat every figure as a way to compare options, never as what you will be charged.

    Where do the prices come from?

    They are approximate public list prices as best known in 2025, shown with an as-of line, and every number is editable. Vendors meter flags differently (requests, config downloads, seats, monthly active users) and change packaging often, so check each vendor's pricing page before deciding.

    What is not included?

    Longer seats, projects, experimentation and analytics add-ons, SSO, support plans and committed-use discounts. These are often the real bill, so use this to compare the per-request line, not to forecast the whole invoice. Targeting rules, SDK quality, self-hosting options and experimentation features are not priced here and often matter more than a few dollars.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. The page calculates in your browser and sends nothing. The CLI reads local files and makes no network call.
