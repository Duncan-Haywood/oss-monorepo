Common questions

Why not just track Twitch payouts in a spreadsheet?

Plenty of streamers do, and a spreadsheet is a fine tool for a monthly income log. The failure mode is not the spreadsheet itself — it is that your revenue split is not a constant across a career. It changes the day you qualify for Partner Plus, and a formula copied down a column keeps using whatever split was true the month you typed it, silently understating income every month after the split changes.

Does this calculator replace my income-tracking spreadsheet?

Not necessarily. This tool prices one month at a time and does not save a running history across months the way a spreadsheet's rows do. Many streamers use both: this calculator to get one month's split-and-Bits arithmetic right whenever their split or sub counts change, and their own spreadsheet to log the result alongside every other month for the year.
