      InputHandling

      Common questions

      Does it upload my event list?

      No. The browser page works on the text you paste, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

      Why skip an event with big sales?

      A big event can overlap two smaller ones that together net more, or use one of your limited weekly slots. The tool compares the total net of every allowed combination and tells you what each swap would cost.

      What counts as an overlap?

      Two events conflict when their times overlap after adding your setup hours before and teardown hours after each, so a market that ends at noon and a festival that opens at 12:30 across town cannot both be worked.

      Is the plan guaranteed to be best?

      It is the exact best answer for the numbers you typed, found by search and checked against trying every combination in the tests. The sales figures are your estimates, so the result is a decision aid, not a forecast. The page also shows what the plan earns if sales come in lower.

      Who is it for?

      Food trucks, craft and farmers-market vendors, pop-up shops, caterers, buskers, DJs and bands, anyone choosing between events that charge a fee or a cut and cannot be in two places at once.
