          Common questions

          Why isn't a single commission percentage enough to compare estate sale quotes?

          Most estate sale companies price on a tiered schedule -- a higher rate on the first few thousand dollars of sales, a lower rate above that -- plus separate flat fees for setup/staging, credit-card processing, and hauling away or donating unsold items. Two quotes that both mention "30%" can settle very differently once their tiers and fees are applied to the same estimated sale total. This calculator applies each company's actual schedule rather than a single headline rate.

          What is a minimum guarantee?

          Some contracts promise the family a minimum payout no matter how the sale actually goes. If you enter one, this calculator floors the settlement at that amount whenever the computed commission-and-fees math would leave less. Whether that guarantee is actually binding depends on the contract's own wording -- this tool only applies the number you give it.

          Does this replace reading the actual contract?

          No. This is a worksheet for comparing quotes before you sign, built from the numbers you enter. The company's written contract is what actually governs the sale -- read it, and ask the company to explain any fee or tier this calculator doesn't have a field for.
