      Common questions

      Why does the calculator price the staging job at the included term instead of how long I expect the listing to take?

      Because that is what a staging agreement actually promises: a price for an included term, with anything beyond it billed separately as an extension. Pricing the base fee from your expected time-on-market instead would double-count carrying cost for months that are supposed to be billed as an extension, not baked into the sticker price.

      What happens if my extension fee is lower than my furniture carrying cost?

      Every month the listing runs long then costs you more to keep that furniture package off the market than you charge the seller for it, so the fee meant to protect your margin instead erodes it — and the loss has no ceiling the longer the listing takes to sell. The calculator flags this directly and reports how many extension months would erase the job's entire built-in profit.

      Why is the design consultation fee treated as pure profit with no added cost?

      Because in this model the consultation is billed once regardless of term length and adds no furniture, storage, or crew cost of its own. If your business does incur a real added cost for it (a second visit, printed materials), fold that into the flat other-cost field.

      Does anything about my job details get uploaded?

      No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and nothing you type is saved anywhere — it exists only in the page while it is open. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
