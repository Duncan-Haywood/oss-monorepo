Oman: Fawtara

Oman's Tax Authority is building a national e-invoicing system called Fawtara on the Peppol network. The dates are now read from the decision itself: Decision 189/2026 makes electronic tax invoices compulsory from 1 April 2027 for the largest taxpayers and from 1 October 2027 for the rest. Below: what the decision says, what is still only reported, and what a free local file check can and cannot tell you.

How it works

TopicWhat the sources say
ModelFive corners: seller, the seller's accredited service provider, the buyer's accredited service provider, the buyer, and the Tax Authority, which receives the data in near real time. The Tax Authority became a Peppol Authority in January 2026, as reported.
FormatPINT-OM, an Omani adaptation of Peppol PINT, as reported. Since PINT builds on UBL, a UBL file is the likely shape; confirm with your provider.
Dates (Decision 189/2026, read)Issued 3 August 2026 by the Chairman of the Tax Authority, published in Official Gazette issue 1660 of 9 August 2026. Article 3: in effect from 1 April 2027 for taxable persons whose annual supplies exceed OMR 5 million, and from 1 October 2027 for those whose supplies do not.
What it requires (read)It amends the VAT Executive Regulation (Decision 53/2021). New Article 143: a tax invoice is issued in an approved, secured electronic format with a unique number, for a supply (including to a non-taxable person or for own use), a deemed supply, or consideration received before the supply, within 15 days; Article 146 puts simplified tax invoices on the same 15 days. Article 143 bis: the Tax Authority announces the companies licensed to provide electronic tax invoicing. Article 143 bis 1: the taxpayer secures the issuing system and plans for outages and data recovery. Article 143 bis 2: the Chairman may exempt a taxpayer on a reasoned request, for a period, if returns are filed and tax paid on time. Article 147 gains item 11: any other data the Authority specifies. It sets no penalty of its own.
Older scheduleEarlier guides, some still online, give phases from August 2026, February 2027 and August 2027. Those dates are not in Decision 189/2026, whose Article 2 repeals anything that conflicts with it.
PilotReported: a pilot for about 100 selected large VAT-registered taxpayers from 1 August 2026. Not in the decision.
Government salesA B2G phase is reported as planned with no confirmed date.

The date and requirement rows are from the Arabic text of Decision 189/2026 and its annex, read on 2026-11-14 on qanoon.om, which reproduces the Official Gazette and says it is not itself an official source (eval/mandate-primary-sources-taiwan-oman-2026-11-14.md). The model, format, pilot and B2G rows are from search summaries and adviser guides, not from Oman Tax Authority technical texts, which were not read. Check the Gazette or the Tax Authority before acting.

What this checker does and does not cover

The main checker reads UBL, CII and Factur-X and applies EN 16931 and Peppol rules. Since 12 November 2026 it applies OpenPeppol's PINT OM 1.0.1 rules (324 checks) to a UBL file whose CustomizationID names urn:peppol:pint:billing-1@om-1 or the self-billing profile. It does not connect to the Peppol network or report to the Tax Authority. Line amounts in baisa (three decimals) under two-decimal totals are summed as written since 12 November 2026, and all 49 of OpenPeppol's OM examples pass. It is still useful on the numbers: wrong totals, a tax breakdown that does not add up and mistyped identifiers are faults any accredited provider will reject.

Common questions

When does Oman e-invoicing become mandatory?

Decision 189/2026 of the Oman Tax Authority, issued 3 August 2026 and published in Official Gazette issue 1660 of 9 August 2026, takes effect on 1 April 2027 for taxable persons whose annual supplies exceed OMR 5 million and on 1 October 2027 for those whose supplies do not (its Article 3, read in the Gazette text as reproduced on qanoon.om). The August 2026, February 2027 and August 2027 phases in older guides are not in it.

What is Fawtara?

Oman's national e-invoicing system, run by the Oman Tax Authority, as reported on the Peppol network in a five-corner model: your accredited service provider exchanges the invoice with the buyer's provider, and the Tax Authority receives the data in near real time. Decision 189/2026 has the Authority announce the companies licensed to provide electronic tax invoicing; the name Fawtara and the five-corner model are not in its text.

What format does Oman use?

Reported as PINT-OM, an Omani adaptation of the international Peppol PINT standard. Decision 189/2026 itself only says an approved, secured electronic format with a unique number per invoice and leaves the providers and the data to the Tax Authority; it names neither Peppol nor PINT.

Can this checker validate an Omani invoice?

No. It applies OpenPeppol's PINT OM rules offline but does not connect to Peppol. For a UBL or CII file it applies EN 16931 and Peppol checks that catch totals, tax breakdown and identifier faults. A clean result is not an acceptance by your provider or the Tax Authority.
