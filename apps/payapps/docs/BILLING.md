# PayApps billing

Paid plans ship **dark**. Until the owner sets the Paddle secrets below, `createBillingProvider`
returns `null`: checkout answers 503, the Billing page says paid plans are not on sale yet, and every
organization is on the Free plan. Nothing in this repository holds a key.

Built on [`packages/billing`](../../../packages/billing/README.md), imported as source through the
`@billing` path alias (`aliases.ts`, `tsconfig.json`), so `package.json` and the lockfile are
unchanged.

## Plans (`src/shared/plans.ts`)

| Plan    | Monthly | Annual  | Active jobs | Users     | Sold |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ----------- | --------- | ---- |
| Free    | $0      | –       | 1           | 1         | no (default) |
| Solo    | $99     | $1,069  | 5           | 2         | yes  |
| Crew    | $179    | $1,933  | 20          | 3         | yes  |
| Company | $249    | $2,689  | unlimited   | unlimited | yes  |
| Comp    | $0      | –       | unlimited   | unlimited | no (operator grant) |

Prices are the proposals in the public repository's `portfolio/monetization/models.yaml` (row
`app: private-monorepo/apps/payapps`), set against PayAppHub, Trimble Pay, SubLien and JobTread. They
are untested hypotheses. The unit is the **active job** (a project with status `active`): closing a
finished job frees its slot, which matches how subcontractors think about their billing load.

`src/shared/plans.test.ts` fails CI when a paid plan falls under the 40% margin floor at full
allowance (PayApps makes no LLM calls; cost is Cloudflare hosting plus ~$0.05 storage per job-month,
plus Paddle's 5% + $0.50 and a 3% refund reserve). Margins are above 90% on every plan.

## What the limits do

- **New or reopened job** over the active-job limit: 402 `payment_required`.
- **New member** over the user limit: 402.
- **New pay application** while the org has more active jobs than its plan allows (after a
  downgrade or a lapsed subscription): 402.
- Nothing is ever hidden or deleted. Reading, editing drafts, submitting, PDF and CSV export keep
  working on every job whatever the plan, so a lapsed customer is never locked out of their own
  billing records.

A subscription grants its plan while `active`, `trialing` or `comp`, and while `past_due` for 7 days
from the first failed renewal (the Billing page shows the deadline). After that, or when
`canceled`/`paused`, Free limits apply.

## Routes

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/billing/plans` | anyone | Public plan list (for the pricing page) |
| `POST /api/billing/webhook` | Paddle | Signature-verified; always 200 once verified |
| `GET /api/orgs/:orgId/billing` | members | Plan, status, usage, limits, plans |
| `POST /api/orgs/:orgId/billing/checkout` | admins | `{plan, interval, promoCode?}` → hosted checkout URL |
| `POST /api/orgs/:orgId/billing/portal` | admins | Paddle customer portal URL |

Webhook security follows `packages/billing` exactly: the org is bound only by an existing
subscription id or a signed, unexpired, single-use checkout token (never `custom_data.orgId`); the
plan comes only from the billed price; `comp` is never granted by a webhook; another customer cannot
take over an org's subscription; events are idempotent on `(provider, event id)` and an event that
threw is retried on redelivery. `test/worker/billing.test.ts` covers each of these.

## Turning it on (owner steps)

1. Paddle account verified (needs live pricing, terms, privacy and refund pages: the marketing site,
   PR #18, and the legal pages at `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy` and `/legal/refunds`, drafts that
   need the owner's contact email and the agent legal review first).
2. The deploy workflow (`payapps-deploy`, [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md)) syncs
   `billing/paddle-catalog.json` to Paddle when `PAYAPPS_BILLING_API_KEY` is set and passes the price
   ids read back to the Worker as `BILLING_PRICE_IDS`. Products and prices are code (regenerated from
   `PLAN_LIST`; a unit test fails when it is stale).
3. Worker secrets (owner only, app-prefixed in GitHub, e.g. `PAYAPPS_BILLING_API_KEY`):
   `BILLING_API_KEY`, `BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, plus the variable `PAYAPPS_BILLING_PROVIDER=paddle`
   (a var, not a secret). `AUTH_SECRET` (it also signs checkout tokens) is generated by the deploy.
4. A Paddle notification destination at `https://<payapps host>/api/billing/webhook` for
   `subscription.*` and `transaction.completed`.

Grant a pilot or partner unlimited use with a `comp` row:

```sql
INSERT INTO subscriptions (org_id, plan_key, status, cancel_at_period_end, created_at, updated_at)
VALUES ('org_…', 'comp', 'comp', 0, unixepoch() * 1000, unixepoch() * 1000);
```

## Local demo

`.dev.vars.example` sets `FAKE_EXTERNALS=true`, which selects the signed fake provider: checkout
returns to `/orgs/:orgId/billing?checkout=success&fake_checkout=…` without charging. The fake is
refused when `APP_ENV=production`.
