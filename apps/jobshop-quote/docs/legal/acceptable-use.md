# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 2026-11-26** · Maris Stella Labs LLC

Job Shop Quote exists to help shops quote their own work quickly and accurately. You agree not to use it to do any of the following.

## Honest quoting

- Send quotes for a company you are not authorized to represent, or impersonate another person or company.
- Present a quote, a price or an AI-drafted cover note to a customer without one of your people having checked it.

## Other people's data

- Upload drawings, CAD files or RFQs you do not have the right to share with us, or that your customer has forbidden you to share with service providers.
- Upload classified information, controlled unclassified information (CUI), or technical data controlled under ITAR or the EAR.
- Enter personal information about others unless you have the right to share it with us for this purpose.
- Invite people to your shop who should not see its rates, customers or quotes.

## Email

- Use the quote email to send spam or messages your customers did not ask for, or give your shop's RFQ address to lists or senders you do not expect RFQs from.

## The Service

- Attempt to access another shop's data, probe for vulnerabilities, or bypass access controls, plan limits or AI spending limits.
- Overload the Service, or use it to send RFQs or files designed to make the AI model misbehave.
- Resell or share access outside your shop without our agreement.

## Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy, with notice where practical. Report misuse or security issues to us.
