# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 2026-11-26** · Maris Stella Labs LLC

Seller Books exists to help resellers keep honest, accurate books of their own selling. You agree not to use it to do any of the following.

## Honest books

- Keep books for selling accounts you do not own or are not authorized to keep books for.
- Import altered reports, or enter records you know are false, to produce figures for a tax return, a lender or anyone else.

## Other people's data

- Enter personal information about others (for example in an expense memo or a mileage note) unless you have the right to share it with us for this purpose.
- Invite people to your shop who should not see its sales, costs or profit.

## The Service

- Attempt to access another shop's data, probe for vulnerabilities, or bypass access controls or plan limits.
- Overload the Service, or upload files designed to make it misbehave.
- Resell or share access outside your business without our agreement.

## Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this policy, with notice where practical. Report misuse or security issues to us.
