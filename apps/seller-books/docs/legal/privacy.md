# Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-11-26** · Mary Stella Labs, LLC

This policy explains what information Seller Books (operated by Mary Stella Labs, LLC, "we") collects, why, who processes it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account information:** name, email address, whether the address is verified, sign-in sessions (passwords are stored only as secure hashes), shop memberships and roles, and invitations addressed to an email address (only a hash of each invitation link is stored).
- **Your shop's name.** (The standard mileage rate you choose for a worksheet is used for that worksheet and not stored.)
- **Imported reports:** for each file you import, its file name, a checksum (SHA-256) used to spot the same file imported twice, row counts and any warnings. **The file itself is not kept.** From each row we keep what your books need: the platform, date, order and transaction ids, item title, SKU, quantity, item price, shipping charged, sales tax the marketplace collected, refunds, postage, each fee as the report itemised it (with the column it came from), the net amount, and the description of account-level charges. Buyer names and addresses are not stored; where a report has no order id, the buyer's username is used only inside a one-way hash to recognise a row imported twice.
- **Records you enter:** inventory items and bulk lots (descriptions, SKUs, costs and dates), mileage trips (date, purpose, start and end places, miles), and expenses (date, category, amount, vendor and memo).
- **Billing:** your plan, subscription status, and payment events from the merchant of record. We do not receive or store card numbers.
- **Technical data:** the IP address and browser user-agent of each sign-in session (used to secure your account), and security logs.
- **Acceptance records:** which version of these policies each user accepted, and when.

## Why we use it

- To provide the Service: import your reports, match sales to costs, and show your profit, logs and Schedule C worksheet.
- To secure the Service and prevent abuse.
- To bill you and apply plan limits.
- To support you when you ask us to.
- To meet legal obligations.

- To send account emails: confirming your address when you sign up, resetting your password when you ask, and telling you when someone tries to sign up with an address that already has an account. An invitation is still a link the shop's admin shares; the app does not email it. We do not send marketing email from the app.

## Subprocessors

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf to run the Service:

| Subprocessor | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Hosting, database, logs and sending account emails |
| Paddle (merchant of record) | Checkout, payments, invoicing and sales tax |

**No AI model reads your books.** The Service does not send your reports or records to an AI provider. We do not use your data to train AI models.

We will update this list before adding a new subprocessor.

## What we do not do

- We do **not sell** personal information or business data.
- We do **not use your data for advertising**.
- We do **not share your sales, costs or profit with other customers**, the marketplaces, or anyone else.

## Retention

- Your data is kept while your shop's account is active.
- You can delete an import (which removes the sales and charges it created), inventory items and lots, mileage trips and expenses yourself at any time.
- When a shop is deleted, by its owner or by us at your request, all of its data is deleted from our database. Provider backups and logs roll off on their normal schedules.
- We keep billing records the merchant of record or the law requires.

## Security

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (provider-managed storage encryption).
- Tenant isolation: every record belongs to one shop and is checked against your membership on every request.
- Role-based access within each shop (owner, admin, editor, viewer).

No system is perfectly secure. We will notify affected customers of a security incident involving their data as required by law.

## Your rights and choices

- **Access and export:** see every imported sale and record in the app, and download your Schedule C worksheet as CSV.
- **Correction:** edit your inventory and costs at any time; delete a wrong mileage trip, expense or import and enter or import it again.
- **Deletion:** delete records in the app, or contact us to delete your account or your shop's data.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights. Contact us to exercise them.
