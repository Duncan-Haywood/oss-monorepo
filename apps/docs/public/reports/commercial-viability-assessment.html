What this project is (today)

Retail Pilot Intel targets owner-operated luxury micro-boutiques (~8–50 m², Italy first) with a store-local analytics layer: door footfall aggregated on edge (no identity), POS basket normalization, joined conversion funnel, and (planned) SKU desirability gap and experiment registry. Revenue model: pilot setup €1.5k–€3k + monthly Core €149–€249 / Margin+ €299–€449 per store.

Is it scalable?

Moderately scalable — not hyper-scalable SaaS.

Scaling constraints

  • Physical install: Door camera + edge PC per site; founder or installer visit for commissioning (2h target playbook).
  • POS fragmentation: Each vendor/export format needs adapter maintenance; Italy has many regional POS systems.
  • Low ACV (€149–€449/mo): Requires digital-first acquisition and partner referrals; field sales economics fail.
  • Success = behavior change: CS is coaching a 15-minute weekly ritual, not ticket deflection — does not scale like pure software.
  • Privacy reviews: Per-store DPIA and owner trust; replicable process but not zero marginal cost.

Scaling enablers (already planned in repo)

  • Standard event schemas (packages/schemas) — good foundation for adapters.
  • Installer + POS reseller economics (10–15% Y1 sub or €200 bounty) — correct motion for ACV.
  • Street-cluster referrals: one visible Milan street → 3–5 warm intros without enterprise procurement.
  • Digital funnel: docs site, password portal, PQL signals — reduces founder demo load.

Market opportunity

TAM

EU independent specialty retail with POS + storefront — hundreds of thousands of doors. Too broad to operate.

SAM

Single-site luxury/accessories <50 m², AOV €150+ — Italy low tens of thousands; EU multiple larger.

SOM (36 mo)

50–200 stores via direct + installer — this is the operable market for a bootstrap team.

Market attractiveness: Fragmented buyers favor a narrow wedge. Tourism seasonality in Italy creates urgent “conversion this week vs last season” buying triggers. Privacy culture supports edge-local differentiation vs US cloud analytics. Risk: market is large enough to attract POS vendors and footfall vendors to add “good enough” join features, compressing differentiation.

Is it worth pursuing?

Yes — if you accept these constraints:

  1. Stage gate: Do not raise capital or hire until M4 (joined funnel live 7 days + owner weekly € action).
  2. Positioning: Sell outcomes (conversion, dead SKU cash, AOV), not cameras or “AI retail.”
  3. Scope discipline: Ship ranks 1–3 from ROI matrix only; defer window CV and multi-store benchmarks.
  4. Business type: Plan for a €150k–€300k ARR niche B2B company in 3–4 years, not a unicorn trajectory.
  5. Time investment: Founder-engineer + design partner for 6–9 months before expecting channel scale.

No — or pause — if any of these are true:

  • You cannot secure a design partner with POS export and appetite for a DPIA-backed camera install.
  • You need revenue within 90 days without setup fees or consulting adjacent work.
  • You want pure software margins with no hardware/install/partner dependency.
  • You plan to sell to chains or surveillance-oriented buyers (conflicts with privacy ICP).

Recommendations for a more successful business

Ordered by impact on viability and revenue — aligned with the project’s own ROI ranking.

Critical path (next 90 days)

  • Sign design-partner term sheet before hardware spend — setup fee, M4 pricing, publicity, exit/buyback. Without a committed owner, the repo remains a prototype.
  • Ship one real POS path (API or nightly export) for the pilot vendor — revenue join is the product; footfall-only commoditizes you.
  • Implement minimal edge ingest (RTSP → line-cross → aggregate events) with a manual tally validation week.
  • Wire portal or a simple LAN UI to live SQLite — replace illustrative demo for the pilot store only; keep demo mode for prospects.
  • Complete DPIA with counsel before camera go-live — existential risk in Italy; documented posture is not enough.

Revenue & retention

  • Automate the “weekly owner report” (1 page PDF/email) from real rollups — retention is tied to € actions, not logins.
  • Instrument experiment registry in analytics before selling Margin+ — desirability gap is the upsell justification.
  • Define hard churn early-warning: 14 days no login + no experiment → founder call, not email drip.
  • Capture objections from 3 discovery calls in a living doc — refine term sheet and portal copy weekly.

Scale & moat

  • Publish anonymized case study within 60 days of M4 — unlocks cluster stage and consultant channel.
  • Lock one installer + one POS integrator in Milano with economics sheet — test channel-led close without founder travel.
  • Ship installer BOM + 2h commissioning checklist as repo artifacts (PDF/markdown) — reduces founder bottleneck.
  • Second POS adapter only after first pilot renewal intent — unlocks adjacent verticals (eyewear, jewelry).
  • Italian SEO on docs (conversion + POS boutique long-tail) — low-cost inbound per GTM plan.

What to avoid (protects viability)

  • Demoing window/outdoor funnel before rank-1 baseline exists in the live pilot.
  • Building multi-tenant cloud video or cross-store shopper identity — destroys positioning.
  • Trade-fair booth spend at this ACV — poor CAC fit; use association newsletters instead.
  • Raising pre-seed before 3 paying stores beyond design partner — weak proof for investors, dilutes focus.
  • Expanding to anti-ICP segments (chains, low AOV, no POS export) before playbook repeatability.

Funding perspective

Bootstrap path is coherent: setup fees fund hardware/DPIA; recurring margin improves at ~5–9 paid stores covering fixed opex (founder salary excluded). Optional €150k–€300k pre-seed should prioritize POS engineering (45%) and install playbook (25%) per business plan — only after pilot proves owner weekly € actions.

For angels or micro-VCs: attractive as a profitable niche infrastructure play with ESG/privacy narrative in EU retail. Not attractive as a high-growth SaaS bet without ACV expansion or platform partnerships.
