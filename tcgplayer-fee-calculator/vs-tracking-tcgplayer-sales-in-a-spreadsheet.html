Common questions

Can I track TCGplayer fees in a spreadsheet?

Yes, and many sellers do. The catch is that the fee is not one percentage: it is a commission with a per-product cap on cards plus shipping, a second percentage on the order total including tax, and a flat charge per order, so a single "fee %" column is wrong for cheap and expensive orders alike.

When is a spreadsheet better than this calculator?

When you need history: every sale in a row, totals by month, and tax records. This calculator prices one order at a time and keeps nothing unless you save it on your own device; it is for deciding a price or a minimum order, not for bookkeeping.

What should a spreadsheet formula include?

Commission as the lesser of 10.75% of the subtotal and $75 per product, plus 2.5% of the order total (subtotal and tax) plus $0.30 once per order. Check it against one real payout before trusting it.
