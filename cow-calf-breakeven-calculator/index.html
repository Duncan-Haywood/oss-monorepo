    Common questions

    What does it cost to keep a cow a year?

    On the default numbers $830 per cow exposed: $280 of hay (2 tons at $140), $200 of pasture, and the rest supplement, health, bull, labor, other costs and the net cost of replacing culled cows. Hay is the largest line at 33.7 percent. Enter your own figures; the defaults are invented.

    What calf price do I need to break even?

    $171.49 per hundredweight on the calves you wean, at 88 percent weaned and 550 lb. At your own weaning rate and weight the calculator finds the price where margin is zero, and the weight you would need at your current price.

    How much does hay price matter?

    Every $10 per ton on 2 tons per cow is $20 per cow. At double the default hay price the margin falls from $380 to $100 per cow and the breakeven price rises to $229.34 per cwt.
