How much does Whatnot take per sale?

Whatnot publishes two fees, not one. Commission is a percentage of the item’s final sale price — the hammer. Processing is 2.9% + $0.30 of the checkout total, which is the hammer plus whatever the buyer paid for shipping and tax. A page that says “Whatnot takes 8%” is describing the first line and silently dropping the second.

The formula

commission = commission rate × min(hammer, cap) — no cap means the whole hammer.

processing = 2.9% × (hammer + shipping charged + tax) + $0.30

payout = hammer − commission − processing

That last line is Whatnot’s own “net earnings” definition. Shipping the buyer paid is not added to payout; it is there to cover the label. Leftover after your costs is payout + shipping charged − label − what the lot cost you.

Worked example 1: Whatnot’s own $50 sale

No shipping, no tax, 8% commission, 2.9% + $0.30 processing — the numbers on Whatnot’s US help article.

LineAmount
Hammer$50.00
Commission (8% of $50)$4.00
Processing (2.9% of $50 + $0.30)$1.75
Total fees$5.75
Payout$44.25

Effective take on the hammer: 11.5%, not 8%.

Worked example 2: a $20 TCG card with $5 postage

The lot most live-sellers actually run. Same 8% + 2.9% + $0.30.

LineAmount
Hammer$20.00
Buyer-paid shipping$5.00
Commission (8% of $20)$1.60
Processing (2.9% of $25 + $0.30)$1.03
Total fees$2.63
Payout$17.38

Effective take on the hammer: 13.1%. Of the $1.03 processing, $0.15 is from the postage. That slice is why “just charge shipping” is not free, and why the free-shipping comparison exists.

Worked example 3: a $2,400 graded lot under the $1,500 cap

On the published US comics / TCG / sports / toys row, commission is 8% only up to $1,500. The $900 above the cap is 0%.

Commission is $120.00, not 8% of $2,400 ($192). Processing on $2,400 with no shipping is $69.90. Total fees $189.90, payout $2,210.10. The cap kept $72.00 that a flat 8% page would have taken. The cap is labelled limited-time — confirm it on the lot.

Common questions

How much does Whatnot take on a $50 sale?

On Whatnot’s published US default, a $50 hammer with no shipping or tax is $4.00 commission (8%) plus $1.75 processing (2.9% of $50 + $0.30) = $5.75 in fees and a $44.25 payout. That is Whatnot’s own worked example.

Does Whatnot charge processing on shipping?

Yes. Processing is 2.9% + $0.30 of the checkout total — hammer plus buyer-paid shipping and tax — not of the hammer alone. A $20 card with $5 postage is $1.60 commission + $1.03 processing = $2.63, 13.1% of the hammer, not 8%.

Does the $1,500 cap apply to every category?

No. Whatnot’s published US help article applies the 0%-above-$1,500 rule to comics, TCG, sports singles, toys & hobbies, and coins. Other categories still commission the whole hammer. The cap is labelled limited-time; confirm it on the lot.
