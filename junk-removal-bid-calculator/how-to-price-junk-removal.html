JUNK REMOVAL PRICING

How to price a junk removal job without giving the dump ticket away.

The search phrase is usually “junk removal cost calculator.” Almost every result is a lead form that asks for a zip code and a phone number. This page is the method behind a number you can stand on, and it pairs with a free browser calculator that does the arithmetic locally.

The landfill does not care how full the truck looks

Customers think in rooms and truck fractions. Transfer stations think in tons. Loose mixed household junk often lands around 150–250 pounds per cubic yard. The same yard of concrete is closer to two tons. Quote both as “half a truck” and one job prints a $45 minimum ticket while the other prints a $400 ticket. The driveway photograph cannot tell you which.

Build the ticket first

Estimate pounds from each pile (volume × a density you have actually seen on your tickets, or a count × typical item weight). Convert to tons. The dump charge is the greater of the landfill’s per-ton rate and its minimum ticket, plus any environmental surcharge, plus the special fees many sites charge for appliances, mattresses, and tires. That sum is the part of the job you cannot negotiate after the scale.

Then price the carry

On-site clock time, plus stairs, plus a long carry from the back yard, plus the round-trip drive, plus each dump run. Multiply by crew size. A two-person crew sitting in traffic is two wages, and a load that will not fit the trailer is a second dump run you did not quote. Payroll burden — employer tax, workers’ compensation, liability — belongs on the wage. Fuel is its own line.

Walk-away is cost, not the special

Overhead as a share of direct cost, then a target margin as a share of price, is how the customer number is built. A posted minimum (“$149 to roll the truck”) is a floor on that number. It is not a substitute for knowing cost. If a half-truck special is $199 and the ticket plus loaded labor is $230, the special is a donation. Hold the walk-away and let the customer decide whether the pile is worth it.

What this is not

Not a quote on your behalf, not a landfill tariff, not legal or business advice. Densities and fees vary by region, by how wet the load is, and by what the scale actually prints. Verify every figure against your own tickets before you send a price.

Common questions

How do junk removal companies charge?

Many advertise by the fraction of a truck. Transfer stations usually bill by the ton, with a minimum ticket and extra fees for appliances, mattresses, and tires. A volume quote that ignores weight underprices heavy debris.

How much does junk removal cost per cubic yard?

There is no single number. Mixed household junk is often a few hundred pounds per cubic yard; concrete can be around two tons per yard. Price per yard is the bid divided by volume after the dump ticket and labor are known, not a starting rate.

Why do half-truck specials lose money?

A posted special assumes a light mixed load and one dump run. Stairs, a long carry, a second dump, or a yard of concrete can put the dump ticket and loaded labor above the special before anyone is paid.
