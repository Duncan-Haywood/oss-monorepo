Is Etsy Plus worth it this month?

Sellers search for “is Etsy Plus worth it” because the $10.00 subscription looks cheap next to a pile of $0.20 listing fees. The cash Plus returns is only the credits you would have paid for anyway. Unused credits expire. Shop banners and restock requests are not a fee discount unless you assign them a dollar value.

Open the Etsy profit calculator, keep your listing fee, and enter how many listings you will create or renew and how much Etsy Ads you would buy without Plus. The table below uses the calculator defaults: $0.20 listing fee, 15 listing credits, $5.00 Ads credit, $10.00 Plus fee.

Use every credit, spend the Ads credit anyway

Fifteen listings and $5.00 of Ads you would have bought: stay off Plus and you pay $8.00. Subscribe and you pay $10.00. Plus costs $2.00 more (−$2.00). The included credits are worth at most $8.00 against a $10.00 fee, so volume past 15 listings cannot close the gap — those extra listings cost $0.20 on both paths.

When Plus can keep more cash

If you assign $3.00 to Plus shop features on top of the same 15 listings and $5.00 Ads spend, Plus keeps $1.00 more (+$1.00). If your listing fee is $1.00 and you use all 15 credits with no Ads, stay off Plus costs $15.00 and Plus costs $10.00, so Plus keeps $5.00 more (+$5.00).

Rates and credits change. This page does not publish Etsy’s live Plus schedule. Enter the numbers from your own Plus invoice and Ads dashboard. The calculator is arithmetic, not advice, and it does not claim Plus will raise your search rank or sales.

Common questions

Is Etsy Plus worth it if I use every credit?

At a $0.20 listing fee, 15 listing credits and a $5 Ads credit are worth $8 against a $10 Plus fee. If you would have paid for those listings and that Ads spend anyway, Plus still costs $2.00 more this month. Extra listings past the 15-credit cap cost the same on both paths.

Do unused Etsy Plus credits roll over?

No. Unused listing credits and unused Ads credit expire at the end of the billing cycle. A shop that renews five listings and runs no Ads keeps only $1.00 of credit value and pays $10.00, so Plus costs $9.00 more that month.

Does Etsy Plus lower transaction fees?

No. Plus does not change Etsy's transaction or payment-processing rates. It is a subscription with listing credits, an Ads credit, and shop-customization features. Offsite Ads are a separate sale fee and are not the Plus Ads credit.
