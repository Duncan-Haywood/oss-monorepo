    Common questions

    Is this a market rate or professional advice?

    No. Every hour, rate, percentage and budget in the answer is a number you typed. It does not look up what other designers charge and gives no legal, tax or licensing advice. It does the arithmetic on your own time so you can see what each fee model would earn you.

    Which interior design fee model is best?

    It depends on your hours and how much the project overruns. Hourly protects you from scope creep because the client pays for the extra time; a flat fee, percent of cost, per square foot or product margin all keep the fee fixed while your hours grow. The calculator shows each model's effective hourly at your expected overrun and at a stress overrun, and how much overrun each can absorb before dropping below your target.

    Does it upload my projects?

    No. The browser page works on the numbers and text you enter, in your own browser, and never sends them anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.

    How is the effective hourly calculated?

    Baseline hours are design hours + site visits x hours each + items x minutes each + meetings x hours each. Expected hours add your overrun percent. Effective hourly = what the model bills / expected hours. Hourly bills your rate x hours less the write-off, so overrun does not lower it.

    What is the walk-away fee?

    Expected hours x a floor rate (a percent of your target, 75 percent by default). Below it the project costs you more than it is worth to you, so you decline or re-scope rather than discount.
