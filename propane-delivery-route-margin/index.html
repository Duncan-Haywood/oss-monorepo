    Common questions

    Why does average fill size matter as much as annual gallons?

    Because your annual gallon volume tells you the fuel margin, but the average fill size decides how many separate truck trips it takes to deliver that volume. A customer who takes the same annual gallons in fewer, larger fills costs you far less in drive time and mileage than one who takes it in many small top-offs — and a small enough average fill can also trigger a minimum-delivery fee, if you charge one.

    Why is tank rental a fixed cost instead of something charged per gallon?

    Because that is how most company-owned tank placements actually work — you carry the tank's lease or amortized cost every month whether the customer takes one delivery a year or four. This calculator applies it as a flat monthly cost so it shows up next to the fuel margin it makes possible, rather than being folded invisibly into the price per gallon.

    Does this tool tell me how much propane a tank needs or when a customer will run out?

    No. This calculator only prices the route economics of a delivery you have already scheduled, from numbers you supply. It does not size a tank, forecast degree-day usage, determine a safe fill level, or replace your own licensed propane supplier's delivery-scheduling and safety judgment.

    Does anything about my customers or pricing get uploaded?

    No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and the customer list you type into the route section is never saved anywhere — it exists only in the page while it is open. The customer inputs above and the target monthly income setting are different: those are saved locally on this device, so you don't retype them, and a shareable link can carry them to another device. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
