Common questions

Why not just track propane customer margins in a spreadsheet?

Plenty of small dealers do, and a spreadsheet is a fine tool for tracking a customer list over a season. The failure mode is not the spreadsheet itself — it is the minimum-delivery-fee threshold, which is a conditional (does this fill size trigger the fee, yes or no) that a copied-down formula has to re-implement correctly for every row, and easy to get wrong once for a new customer with an unusual fill size. A calculator that re-evaluates the condition on every input avoids that particular drift; a spreadsheet formula copied down a column does not check itself.

Does this calculator replace my customer spreadsheet?

Not necessarily. This tool prices one customer at a time and does not save a running list across sessions the way a spreadsheet's rows do. Many small dealers use both: this calculator to get one customer's arithmetic right, including the minimum-delivery-fee edge case, and their own spreadsheet to log the result alongside address, tank ownership, and delivery history for every customer over a season.
