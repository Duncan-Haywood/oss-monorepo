This calculator vs. Vagaro

Two different shapes: Vagaro runs a whole salon, spa, or barbershop's booking, payments, and staff schedule as an ongoing subscription account, bought by the business. This page is a free, private worksheet for one stylist's own question — commission or booth rent, on their own book — before they sign a lease.

What each tool is for

Vagaro is salon, spa, and barbershop business-management software, sold under an ongoing subscription account to the business, not to an individual stylist. It runs an online booking page clients reserve appointments from directly, takes payments and processes card fees at checkout, schedules staff and chairs across a shop, sends marketing and appointment-reminder messages, and can track retail inventory, all synced across owners, employees, and booth renters on one account. Setting it up means creating a business account, building a service and booking calendar, and connecting payment processing — not a one-off comparison. Vagaro's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names, feature tiers, or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not take bookings, process payments, or schedule staff. It takes one stylist's own book — average ticket, clients per week, retail, working weeks, and minutes per service — alongside either a commission split or a booth-rent lease with its rent, card fees, backbar, and other weekly cost, and works out weekly, monthly, and annual take-home under each path, the take-home per hour once unpaid laundry and booking time is counted, the break-even client count, the maximum rent that still matches the current split, and how payroll tax — employee FICA against self-employment tax — changes the answer. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and nothing typed in is uploaded.
