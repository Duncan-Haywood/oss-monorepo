This calculator vs. Procare

Procare Solutions is a well-known paid childcare management platform, built to run a center's day-to-day operations for a director and staff — enrollment records, attendance and check-in, staff scheduling, and billing families once a tuition rate is already set, sold as an ongoing per-center subscription described on procaresoftware.com. It is the kind of tool a center reaches for once it needs to run enrollment, attendance, and billing across many families and staff at once.

That is a different job from the one this page does. This calculator has no enrollment records, no attendance tracking, and no billing. It exists for the narrower task that comes before any of that: working out what a classroom's tuition needs to be from your own child:staff ratio, staffing cost, hours, overhead, and occupancy, and seeing what an empty seat is actually costing you at your current rate — with nothing to sign up for.

I already have a rate. I need to invoice and collect tuition from families every month. Procare or a comparable platform — billing and collection are not this calculator's job.

Most directors get different value from each: Procare (or a comparable platform) for running enrollment, attendance, and billing as an ongoing subscription, and a private calculator like this one for an instant, no-login check of what a classroom's rate should be before it is entered anywhere for billing. This calculator's own output is a pricing check, not a record of your center's enrollment or billing history.

It needs no account and no sign-in: it is a static page, the arithmetic runs in your browser, and once it has loaded it keeps working with the connection off.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or a paid platform like Procare?

They answer different questions. Procare Solutions is a paid subscription platform built to run a childcare center's day-to-day operations — enrollment records, attendance and check-in, staff scheduling, and billing families once a tuition rate is already set, described on Procare's own site. This calculator does none of that. It is a free, browser-only page for a narrower job that comes before any of it: working out what a classroom's tuition needs to be from its own ratio, staffing cost, and occupancy, with no account and nothing uploaded. A center running enrollment, attendance, and billing across many families and staff is looking for the wrong tool here; a director who needs to set or check a rate before typing it into a billing system is in the right place.

Does Procare tell me what my tuition rate should be?

Procare bills families at whatever rate a center enters into it; it manages collecting that rate, not deriving it from staffing cost and occupancy. This calculator does the opposite: it takes your ratio, staffing wage, hours, overhead, and occupancy and works out the tuition needed to hit a target margin, and what an empty seat is actually costing you at your current rate — the arithmetic a director does before a rate exists to bill, not the billing itself.
