    Common questions

    What does it cost to produce a pound of honey?

    On the default numbers $3.88 in cash per pound sold: $450.00 to replace winter losses, $400.00 of equipment, $250.00 of mite treatment and feed, $495.00 of jars and labels and $150.00 of insurance and fees, plus 0.44 hours of your time per pound. Jars and labels is the largest cash line at 28.4%. Enter your own figures; the defaults are invented.

    How much should I charge for a pound of honey?

    $12.77 a pound to pay yourself $20 an hour on those numbers, against a cash breakeven of $3.88. Selling at $10.00 leaves $6.12 a pound and $13.78 an hour before any wage is deducted.

    What happens in a bad honey year or a bad winter?

    A poor honey year (20 lb a hive instead of 50) costs $8.04 a pound and needs $30.27. Losing 60% of hives over winter lifts the price you need to $14.17.
