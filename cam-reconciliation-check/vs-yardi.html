This calculator vs. Yardi Voyager CAM reconciliation

Two different shapes: Yardi Voyager runs CAM recovery on top of a full property-management and accounting platform, built for a company operating every lease at a property inside one system of record. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — does a single year's CAM statement match what the area-share math and credits say it should, before you raise it with the other side.

What each tool is for

Yardi Voyager is Yardi Systems' flagship property-management and accounting platform. CAM recovery is one module among many that sit on top of the same general ledger and tenant-ledger data: recurring charge schedules, lease administration, tenant billing, and year-end reconciliation runs are all generated from transactions the platform has already recorded for the property over the year. It is accurate to the extent every charge, credit, and lease term was entered correctly, and it requires the platform itself, a chart of accounts, and a management company's ongoing data entry to produce a reconciliation at all. Yardi's own pricing and licensing terms are not repeated here, since this session cannot verify today's figures against Yardi's current terms; only its well-documented scope as a property-management and accounting suite is described.

This calculator does none of what Voyager's CAM module does. It has no ledger, no lease database, and no memory between visits. It takes the figures already on one year's CAM statement or lease exhibit — annual expenses, total rentable area, tenant area, billed amount, credits, and optionally the variable-expense share and occupancy percentages for a gross-up clause — and returns the area share, the modeled tenant share, the net billed amount, and the variance between them, plus a review checklist. It runs free in any browser, needs no account and no property set up in advance, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Yardi Voyager?

They solve different problems. Yardi Voyager is a full property-management and accounting platform: tenant ledgers, lease administration, recurring charge schedules, and CAM recovery all run on top of the same general ledger, so a reconciliation reflects every transaction the system has recorded for the property over the year. It is built for a management company or landlord running many properties and leases through one system of record, and it requires the platform, the chart of accounts, and the ongoing data entry underneath it to be accurate. This calculator does none of that. It takes the five or eight numbers already on a CAM statement or in a lease exhibit — annual expenses, total rentable area, tenant area, billed amount, credits, and an optional occupancy gross-up — and returns the area share, modeled tenant share, net billed amount, and variance, instantly, with no account, no ledger, and nothing uploaded. A management company already running Voyager gets its CAM recovery from the platform it already operates in; a landlord or tenant checking one statement by hand, or without access to the property's Voyager instance at all, is in the right place here.
