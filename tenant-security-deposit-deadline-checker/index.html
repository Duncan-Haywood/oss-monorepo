    Common questions
    
    
    How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit?
    
    
    It depends on the state: 14 days in New York, 21 in California and Washington, 30 in Texas, Georgia, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Ohio and Pennsylvania, and 45 in Maryland, Virginia and Washington, D.C. This page lists the period for each state it covers with the statute, and counts from your move-out date.
    
    
    What if my landlord missed the deadline?
    
    
    Many states let a tenant recover the deposit plus a penalty, often two or three times the amount wrongfully withheld, and some say a landlord who misses the deadline loses the right to keep any of it. A written demand is the usual first step before a small-claims filing. This page drafts one; a court decides whether any penalty applies.
    
    
    Do I have to give a forwarding address?
    
    
    In some states, including Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Michigan and Minnesota, the landlord's clock depends on you giving a forwarding address in writing. If you have not, this page says so and drafts a letter that gives it.
    
    
    Can a landlord keep money for normal wear and tear?
    
    
    Generally no: ordinary wear from living in a place is not a lawful deduction in most states, but unpaid rent and damage beyond normal wear usually are. The dispute letter this page writes asks the landlord to justify each deduction with receipts.
