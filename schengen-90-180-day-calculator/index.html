    Common questions
    
    
    Do I need to check my Schengen days before a trip?
    
    
    Yes. Short-stay visitors may spend at most 90 days in any rolling 180-day period in the Schengen area. Overstaying can lead to fines, entry bans and visa refusals.
    
    
    Do the days of entry and exit count?
    
    
    Yes. Both the day you enter and the day you leave count as full days of stay.
    
    
    Which countries count?
    
    
    The Schengen states. Ireland, Cyprus and the United Kingdom are outside it, so time there does not count. Check the current member list, which has changed over time.
    
    
    How does the rolling 180 days work?
    
    
    For any day, look back 179 days plus that day. Count your days in the area in that window. If it is more than 90, you are over. Days drop out as the window moves forward, which is how your allowance returns.
