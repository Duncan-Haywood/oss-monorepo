    Common questions

    Is this a market rate or professional advice?

    No. Every price, minute and cost in the answer is a number you typed. It does not look up what other pool companies charge and gives no legal, tax or contract advice. It does the arithmetic on your own route.

    How do I know if a pool service account is profitable?

    Divide what it pays per month, minus chemicals, truck cost and callback parts, by every hour it takes: visit time, the drive into the stop and the unpaid callback trips. Compare that with the hourly you need. The calculator does it for every account on your route.

    Should I drop a pool service customer who is far from the rest of my route?

    Look at the route's hourly with and without the account. A far stop costs drive hours as well as visit hours, so dropping it can raise the whole route's hourly even though its revenue goes away; the calculator shows the route hourly if each account were dropped, and the price that would make the stop worth keeping.

    Does this upload my customers?

    No. The browser page works on the text you enter, in your own browser, and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
