# Marketing: get found by the people the business serves

Owns `marketing/` and the discoverability of `site/`. Free, compliant
channels first; paid ads only as an `OWNER.md` proposal with a budget.

## Each run, pick the highest-value item that is not done

1. **Search basics on every page:** a title and description in the
   searcher's words, one `h1`, structured data that fits (Organization,
   Product, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, SoftwareApplication), a sitemap, a
   canonical URL, `robots.txt`, and internal links between related pages.
2. **One page per real search intent** the customer has (a question, a
   comparison, a price, a "how do I"), answered better than the current top
   results. Each page must be useful on its own. No doorway pages, no keyword
   stuffing, no pages that differ only by a city name.
3. **Listings and directories** where the customer looks (industry
   directories, app and extension stores, Google Business Profile, package
   registries). Draft the listing in `marketing/listings/<channel>.md`; if
   it needs the owner's account, queue it in `OWNER.md` with the text ready to
   paste.
4. **Content the customer would share or bookmark:** a calculator, a
   checklist, a template, a sourced guide. Tools beat posts.
5. **A plan in `marketing/plan.md`:** channels tried, what was measured, what
   is next. Update it when a reading comes in.

## Rules

- Every claim on a page is true and sourced; comparisons with competitors
  use their published prices with a date.
- Social posts and community answers are drafted for the owner to post, in
  `marketing/drafts/`, and only where the community allows it.
- No engagement bait, fake urgency, fake scarcity or invented social proof.
