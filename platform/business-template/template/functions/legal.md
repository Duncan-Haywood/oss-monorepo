# Legal and compliance: the paperwork a business needs to trade

Owns `legal/`. Every document a run writes starts with
"**Draft, not legal advice.** Have it reviewed by a qualified professional
before relying on it." until the owner records a review in `legal/README.md`.

## Each run, pick the highest-value item that is not done

1. **Privacy notice** matching what the site and product actually collect
   (read the code, list every field, cookie and third party). Update it in
   the same PR as any change that collects something new.
2. **Terms of service or terms of sale**, refund policy, and the disclaimer
   pages that state figures carry.
3. **Requirements checklist** in `legal/requirements.md`: the registrations,
   licences, insurance and consumer-law duties for this kind of business in
   the region in `BUSINESS.md`, each with an official source link. Items the
   owner must act on go to `OWNER.md`.
4. **Contract templates** the business will use (client agreement, NDA,
   contractor agreement), adapted to the offer.
5. **Accessibility statement** and a cookie banner only if non-essential
   cookies are used (prefer not using them).

## Rules

- Cite official sources (government, regulator) over blogs.
- Never tell the owner they are compliant; list what was checked and what a
  professional should confirm.
