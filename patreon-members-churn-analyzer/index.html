    Who is leaving your Patreon, and what is it costing you?

    Drop in your Patreon Members export CSV and get active, declined and former patrons, monthly income by tier, churn by tenure cohort, the income at risk from declined payments, how concentrated it is, and what Patreon's fees leave you.

    The file is read in this browser tab and never leaves your device. Independent of Patreon: not affiliated with or endorsed by Patreon, Inc. Column names are matched by alias from memory of Patreon exports; if yours is not read, the page tells you which rows were skipped and why. Patron names and emails in your file are not shown in the results except declined patrons you may want to contact.

    1. Your Members export

    Needs a Patron Status (or a Pledge amount) and ideally Patronage Since Date. Used when present: Tier, Pledge, Currently Paying $, Lifetime $, Last Charge Date, Last Charge Status, Charge Frequency, Name. Other columns (Email, Twitter, Country and so on) are ignored. US and day-first dates, comma, tab and semicolon files, quoted fields and UTF-16 exports are read. Rows that cannot be read are listed with a line number and reason.

    Left blank, the latest date found in the file is used, so an old export is measured at the time it was taken.

    2. Fee assumptions (edit these)

    Assumptions, not Patreon's terms. Defaults: 10% platform fee (the standard plan for newer pages; legacy Lite, Pro and Premium pages differ) and 2.9% plus $0.30 payment processing per charge. Check them against your own plan and payout statement.

    The model, and what it leaves out

    • Active, declined, former come from the Patron Status column. Without one, status is inferred: a failed last charge is declined, a pledge above zero is active, otherwise former. Free members and unrecognised statuses are counted separately and left out of every paying figure.
    • Reconciles. The tier rows add up to total MRR, and MRR minus estimated fees equals net to you.
    • MRR is the sum of active patrons' pledges, with annual pledges divided by 12. Per-creation pledges depend on what you post and are left out. Declined patrons are shown as at-risk income, not MRR.
    • Tenure runs from Patronage Since Date to the as-of date for active and declined patrons, and to the last charge date for former patrons. Patrons with no usable date are left out of tenure figures.
    • Churn is former patrons over everyone who ever paid. Cohort churn groups patrons by how long ago they joined. Survival counts only patrons who joined long enough ago to have been measured. Groups under five are shown but not used for suggestions.
    • Fees apply your platform percentage to monthly-equivalent pledges, and your processing percentage plus a fixed amount per charge (once a year for annual pledges).
    • Not modelled: taxes, currency conversion, payout fees, chargebacks and refunds, paused pledges, patron pledge changes over time, add-ons and shipping, and anything not in the file. An export shows each patron's current state only, so tier changes and re-joins are not visible.

    Common questions

    How do I see how many Patreon patrons I am losing?

    Export your Members list from Patreon (the Audience or Members page has an Export option) and drop the CSV here. The tool counts active, declined and former patrons, works out monthly recurring income from the active pledges, and shows how many patrons joined in each period and how many of them have left. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

    What is a declined patron and can the money be recovered?

    A declined patron is one whose latest card charge failed. They are still a member, so their pledge is shown as at-risk monthly income rather than lost. Patreon retries the charge and emails the patron; a short personal note asking them to update the card is often what recovers it. The tool lists these patrons and adds up what is at stake.

    How is churn measured here?

    Former patrons divided by everyone who ever paid, overall and for patrons grouped by how long ago they joined. It also shows survival: of the patrons who joined at least 1, 3, 6 or 12 months before the file's latest date, how many paid for that long. Groups under five patrons are shown but not used for suggestions.

    Are the fee figures exact?

    No. They apply the percentages you enter (defaults: 10% platform fee, 2.9% plus $0.30 processing per charge) to monthly-equivalent pledges. Your plan, country, currency conversion, taxes, payout method and chargebacks change the real figure, so check it against your own payout statement.

    Are the column names right for my export?

    They are matched by alias from memory of Patreon Members exports, and no real export was available when this was built. If your file is not read, the page names the rows it skipped and why; a sheet with the headers Patron Status, Tier, Pledge and Patronage Since Date always works.
