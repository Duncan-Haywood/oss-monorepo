This calculator vs. TurboTenant

TurboTenant is a landlord property-management platform: listing a vacancy, screening applicants, signing an e-lease, collecting rent, and, on its paid plans, tracking a security deposit against that lease record. It is a system for running a rental end to end.

This page is not a property-management system and never will be. It answers one narrower question, on the spot: what does this deposit settle to, or what does this partial month prorate to — typed in your browser, with no account, no property record, and nothing saved anywhere.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or TurboTenant?

They solve different problems, and a landlord who uses TurboTenant for everything else can still land here for one calculation. TurboTenant is a property-management platform: listings, tenant screening, e-signable leases, online rent collection, and (on its paid plans) security deposit tracking held alongside a specific lease record in its own system. This page is a single free form that does none of that. You type a deposit amount and list each deduction, or a monthly rent and a day count, and it does the arithmetic — refund or balance owed, or a prorated partial-month figure — with nothing signed up for and no lease on file anywhere.

Do I need a property-management account just to work out a deposit refund?

No, and that is the gap this page exists to close. A landlord managing one unit, or a tenant checking a landlord's math from the other side of the table, does not need an account, a listed property, or a stored lease to add up deductions against a deposit. This calculator runs the same subtraction TurboTenant's deposit tracking runs, without the account, the property record, or the plan tier deposit tracking sits behind.

Does this replace TurboTenant's property management?

No. Listing a vacancy, screening applicants, signing a lease, and collecting rent every month are real, recurring jobs, and a platform built around a persistent record of the tenancy is the right tool for that. This page sells nothing, stores nothing, and has no concept of a property or a lease term. It answers one arithmetic question — what does this deposit settle to, or what does this partial month prorate to — the same question a landlord who already uses full property-management software still has to answer at move-out.
