A round is one set of games in which every team plays at most once, usually one night or one weekend. "Home/away breaks" counts the times a team has two home or two away games in a row across the whole single round robin; for an even league the minimum possible is the number of teams minus two, and the generator reaches it. A double round robin repeats the rounds with every venue reversed. If you have fewer courts than games per round, a round takes more than one night, so check the number of courts against the "games per round" column before you book.

Questions

How many weeks does a round robin take?

With an even number of teams, one fewer week than there are teams (8 teams: 7 weeks); with an odd number, as many weeks as teams (7 teams: 7 weeks), because one team has a bye each week. A double round robin takes twice as many. If you have fewer courts than matches per round, a round needs more than one night; the generator spreads courts and time slots for you.

How many games does each team play?

In a single round robin, every team plays every other team once, so n-1 games for n teams (a team with a bye plays one fewer than the number of rounds). In a double round robin, 2(n-1).

Is it better to have an even or an odd number of teams?

Even is simpler: nobody sits out. Odd leagues give every team exactly one bye in a single round robin and a perfect home and away pattern with no two home or two away games in a row. Neither is unfair; both are fully balanced.
