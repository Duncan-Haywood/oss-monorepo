Youth soccer league schedule: fields, kickoff times and byes

A youth or rec soccer league has two jobs: decide who plays whom, and decide which field and what kickoff time. A round robin does the first. With an odd number of teams, one team sits out each week, and the field and kickoff slots are where hand-made schedules go wrong. Nine teams is a common size: 9 Saturdays, 4 matches a week, one bye each.

Questions

How do you make a youth soccer league schedule?

List the teams, pick the Saturday, and use a round robin so every team plays every other team once. Nine teams play 9 rounds of 4 matches, and one team has a bye each week. Then give each match a field and a kickoff time so no field is booked twice.

What do you do with an odd number of youth soccer teams?

One team sits out every round. A single round robin of nine teams has 9 rounds, and every team gets exactly one bye, so nobody misses more than one week.

How many fields does a nine-team league need?

Four matches a week need four field-and-kickoff slots: two fields at two kickoff times, or four fields at one. Count fields times kickoff times, not fields alone.

How do you keep the 09:00 and 10:30 kickoffs fair?

Count how many early kickoffs each team gets across the season and keep the counts within one of each other, so the same team is not always the one up at dawn.

What if a Saturday is rained out or a field is closed?

Add the date as a skip date, list the field as closed on that date, or move a rained-out round with the rainout box; the tool keeps every field and kickoff free of clashes.
