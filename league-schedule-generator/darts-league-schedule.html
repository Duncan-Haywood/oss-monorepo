Darts league fixtures: a home-and-away schedule for a six-team pub league

A pub darts league is a double round robin: every team plays every other team twice, once on its own board and once on the opponent's. The job for the captain or league secretary is making sure each team hosts as often as it travels, nobody has a run of away nights, and every pair meets exactly twice. Doing it on a spreadsheet takes an evening and the first mistake usually shows up in week four. This guide works one season through: 6 pub teams, Thursdays, 10 weeks.

Questions

How do you make a darts league fixture list?

List the teams, pick the league night, and tick double round robin. The tool plays every team against every other once in the first half of the season and again in the second half with the venues swapped, with the home team shown first.

How many weeks does a six-team darts league last?

A double round robin of six teams takes 10 weeks, because each team plays the other five twice. Add makeup nights for blackout dates and rainouts, or end-of-season finals, on top.

How do you keep home and away fair in a darts league?

Each pair meets once at each team's board, so every team in the six-team example hosts 5 matches and travels for 5. The tool counts home and away per team and the longest home or away run, so you can see it before you send the fixtures.

What if a pub cannot host on some night?

Add the team and the date under blackout dates. Only the match that clashes moves to a makeup night; the rest of the night goes ahead. Two pubs that share a premises can be listed as sharing a ground so they are not both at home on the same night.

Can I send each team only its own fixtures?

Yes. The per-team download gives every captain a list of its own dates, opponents and home or away, and the calendar export puts them in a phone calendar.
