    Common questions

    Is this a market rate or professional advice?

    No. Every price, depth, storm count and cost in the answer is a number you typed. It does not look up what other contractors charge and gives no legal, tax, insurance or contract advice. It does the arithmetic on your own route so you can see what each contract model would pay you in a low, normal and high snow year.

    Should I price a snow plow contract per push or seasonal?

    It depends on how many storms the winter brings. A per-push price pays for every visit, so a heavy winter earns more. A seasonal flat price is fixed, so it pays best in a low-snow year and can lose money in a high one. The calculator shows effective hourly for each model in a low, normal and high year, the storm count where a model first falls under your target, and the flat price that loses money in a high year.

    What is the walk-away price for a seasonal contract?

    Normal-year cost to run the route plus the normal-year hours multiplied by your target hourly and a floor percent (75 percent by default). Below it the contract costs you more than it is worth to you, so you decline or switch to per-push or a capped or hybrid structure.

    Does it upload my sites or past seasons?

    No. The browser page works on the numbers and text you enter, in your own browser, and never sends them anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
