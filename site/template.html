Incentives for verifiable decentralised ML

Research code and white papers on incentive-compatible verification of decentralised ML training: proper scoring rules, peer prediction, inspection games, prediction markets and expert routing. Each project states a question, derives closed-form results, checks them numerically, and lists its limitations. Builds on algorithmic economics and information elicitation (CU Boulder, Frongillo, Waggoner) and Gensyn's verifiable-ML work.
