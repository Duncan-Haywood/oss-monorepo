    What this checks

    Merchants search “Shopify pickup vs shipping” because a $5.00 label looks expensive and a customer who “just comes in” looks free. Eight minutes at $18/hour is $2.40. A $0.40 bag is not a box. The $6.00 postage you stop charging is the larger hole.

    On the defaults — $48 item, $6 shipping charged, $5.00 label, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $14 cost, $1 ship pack, $39 / 100 plan, 8 minutes, $0.40 bag, 12% no-show, 40 pickup orders — the label keeps $31.74. Free pickup keeps $29.12 if they show and $25.29 after no-shows ($6.46 less, −$258.25 / month). A $3.00 paid pickup keeps $32.03 if they show and $27.85 after no-shows ($3.89 less). Even a 0% no-show cannot match the label on these rates, because you gave up $6.00 of postage. The outbound label has to cost about $11.46 before shipping matches free pickup. Already bake postage ($0 charged, $8.00 label) with a 5-minute pick and a 5% no-show and free pickup keeps $28.42 against $22.92 on the label.

    Common questions

    Should I offer pickup on Shopify?

    Pickup replaces a postage label with staff time and a no-show risk. Card fees still sit on the ticket you charge. On the calculator defaults ($48 item, $6 shipping / $5 label, 2.9%+$0.30, 8 minutes at $18/hour, $0.40 bag, 12% no-show) the label keeps $31.74 and free pickup expects $25.29. The outbound label has to cost about $11.46 before shipping matches free pickup. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn Local Pickup on or off.

    Does a paid Shopify pickup fee cover the no-shows?

    A $3 pickup fee on the defaults keeps $27.85 after the 12% no-show mix — still $3.89 less than the label. You gave up $6 of postage and only collected $3. If they show, paid pickup keeps $32.03, which beats the $31.74 label, but the refunded no-shows pull the mix back under.

    When does Shopify pickup beat a carrier label?

    When you already bake postage into the item. The same SKU with $0 shipping charged, an $8 label, 5 minutes, and a 5% no-show keeps $22.92 on the label and $28.42 on free pickup. Local delivery (you drive) is a different comparison.
