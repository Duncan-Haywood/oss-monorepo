    What this checks

    Wholesale and high-ticket Shopify merchants search “Shopify bank transfer fees” and “Mark as paid Shopify fees” because a manual payment method looks free. On the defaults — $48 item, $6 shipping, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $20.39 cash in the order, 95% collected — the card keeps $31.74. Bank transfer, if they pay, keeps $33.61 ($1.87 more, $186.60 / month). Hold until paid still keeps $31.93. Ship first and the 5% that ghost leaves $30.91. The fee save survives down to about 94.4% collected if you wait, and about 96.5% if you already shipped.

    Common questions

    Does Shopify charge fees on bank transfer or Mark as paid?

    A Shopify manual payment method (bank deposit, money order, or Mark as paid) does not take a Shopify Payments percentage or the extra Shopify third-party transaction fee. You skip those processor fees. You still have the monthly plan, cost of goods, and postage. This page compares that zero-fee path with taking a card on the same order. It is arithmetic on your rates, not Shopify's live schedule.

    When does a Shopify bank transfer still beat Shopify Payments?

    It beats the card when enough invoices actually pay. On a $54 ticket the 2.9% + $0.30 card fee is $1.87. Hold the order until the transfer clears and a 95% collection rate still keeps a few cents more than the card. Ship first and the same 5% unpaid slice loses, because you already spent cash.
