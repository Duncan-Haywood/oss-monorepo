    What this checks

    Merchants search “Shopify post purchase upsell” because a $18 add-on looks like free leftover and a one-click after they paid looks free. A second 2.9% + $0.30 on $16.20 is $0.77. A $0.50 extra pack is not the hero box. A 2% extra abandon on a $31.74 ticket is $0.63 — enough to flip a thin checkout lead.

    On the defaults — $48 item, $6 shipping charged, $18 add-on at 10% off ($16.20), $6 add-on cost, Basic 2.9% + $0.30, $14 cost, $5 + $1 ship, $39 / 100 plan, 18% checkout take, 12% one-click take, 2% extra abandon, 80 checkouts — skipping keeps $31.74. If they add at checkout the combined leftover is $40.97. After the 18% take and 2% drop, checkout expects $32.74 ($0.99 more). One-click expects $32.82 ($1.07 more, $8.93 on the second swipe) — $0.08 more than checkout, $85.73 / month versus skipping. Checkout take has to land about 7.0% before it matches skipping. One-click take has to land about 11.1% before it matches the checkout offer. Extra abandon can rise to about 1.8% before checkout matches one-click. Drop the extra abandon to 0% and checkout expects $33.41 against $32.82 on one-click.

    Common questions

    Should I add a Shopify post-purchase upsell?

    A one-click add-on after they already paid keeps the hero leftover and adds a second swipe. On the calculator defaults ($48 item, $18 add-on at 10% off, 2.9%+$0.30, 18% checkout take, 12% one-click take, 2% extra abandon) skipping keeps $31.74, the checkout offer expects $32.74, and one-click expects $32.82. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to install an upsell app.

    Does a Shopify checkout upsell beat a post-purchase offer?

    The first swipe saves a second $0.30 and one plan allocation. A 2% extra abandon can wipe that lead. On the defaults, checkout keeps $0.99 more than skipping if they still pay, but after the 2% drop one-click keeps $0.08 more ($32.82 vs $32.74). Drop the extra abandon to 0% and checkout expects $33.41.

    When does a Shopify upsell lose money?

    When the add-on leftover after the discount, second card fee, and extra pack is negative, or when the checkout offer's extra abandon zeros tickets that would have completed. Checkout take has to land about 7.0% on the defaults before it matches skipping. Recovering a cart they already left is a different comparison.
