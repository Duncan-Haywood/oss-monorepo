    What this checks

    Generic “Shopify fee calculator” pages often stop at one processing rate. The decision most merchants actually face is Shopify Payments vs PayPal or Stripe: the third-party path can add Shopify’s extra transaction fee on top of the processor. Why PayPal can cost more on Shopify walks through that in search-query words. Selling the same SKU at a market or retail counter is a different question — Shopify POS vs online fees compares the cheaper tap with checkout after postage. Buying the label is a third question — Shopify Shipping vs your own label compares admin postage with a Pirate Ship, Shippo, or carrier-account quote. Shop Pay Installments is a fourth — card vs Shop Pay Installments shows the extra fee, the blended month, and the conversion lift that covers it. A requested return is a fifth — store credit vs a cash refund compares sending cash, issuing credit, and exchanging on the same order. A Capital offer is a sixth — Shopify Capital vs waiting shows the fee, days of holdback, simple annualized cost, and the extra monthly profit that covers the fee. A bank-transfer invoice is a seventh — bank transfer vs the card shows the fee you skip, the leftover if invoices pay, and the collection rate that wipes the save. A chargeback is an eighth — refund vs fight vs accept shows the $15 fee, the leftover on each path, and the win rate that makes representment beat a refund. Local delivery is a ninth — local delivery vs the carrier label shows driver time, mileage, and the run cost that still beats postage. A gift card is a tenth — gift card vs selling the SKU now shows sale-day fees, the second swipe after the face, and the mix that only looks better because of breakage. International duties are an eleventh — DDP vs DAP vs skipping international shows the card fee on the collected duty, the door-refuse leftover, and the take rate that flips collecting duties at checkout. An abandoned checkout is a twelfth — leave vs recovery emails vs a coupon shows the recover rate, allocated send cost, cheaper ticket, and the lift that still beats leaving it. A subscription is a thirteenth — one-time vs monthly vs a prepaid pack shows the subscribe-and-save leftover, billing fee, skip rate, and the stay that still beats a one-time checkout. In-store pickup is a fourteenth — pickup vs the carrier label shows staff time, the bag, no-show refunds, and the label cost that still beats a customer who comes in. An add-on offer is a fifteenth — checkout vs post-purchase upsell shows the second swipe, extra checkout abandon, and the drop that still beats one-click. A sale is a sixteenth — 10% off vs $10 off vs free shipping shows leftover after fees on the same order, because free shipping still pays the label. A typical refund rate is a sixteenth — what a return still leaves mixes completed-order profit with a full-refund outcome so you can see whether PayPal keeping its fee flips the path.

    Common questions

    Why does PayPal cost more on Shopify?

    When a Shopify store uses a third-party processor such as PayPal or Stripe, Shopify may also charge a third-party transaction fee on the order total, on top of the processor's own percentage and fixed fee. Shopify Payments replaces that extra Shopify fee with the Shopify Payments processing rate. Enter the rates from your current statements — this page does not publish Shopify's live schedule.

    How do I calculate Shopify fees on an order?

    Add the item price and shipping charged. Apply the payment-path rates that apply to you: Shopify Payments percentage plus fixed fee, or the third-party processor rates plus any Shopify third-party transaction fee. Subtract cost of goods, shipping you pay, packaging, and a share of the monthly Shopify plan. What remains is the order's net contribution.

    When does upgrading from Shopify Basic to Grow pay for itself?

    Grow costs more each month but usually charges a lower Shopify Payments rate and a lower third-party transaction fee. The upgrade starts paying from rates alone when monthly orders times the per-order fee savings exceed the extra monthly plan cost.
