    Common questions

    Should I offer subscriptions on Shopify?

    A subscriber who stays is worth more than one checkout even after a subscribe-and-save discount and a billing fee. On the calculator defaults ($48 item, $6 shipping, 2.9%+$0.30, $14 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% monthly off, 15% prepaid off, 1% billing fee, 5 months stay, 20% skip) one-time keeps $31.74, monthly expects $106.36 across 4.0 paid cycles, and a prepaid 3-month pack keeps $74.23. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to turn Recharge, Skio, or Shopify Selling Plans on.

    When does a Shopify subscription lose to a one-time checkout?

    When they do not stay long enough to cover the cheaper ticket. Monthly has to land about 1.2 paid cycles before it matches a $31.74 one-time leftover, and skip can rise to about 76.1% before monthly matches one-time. After one paid cycle with no skip, monthly keeps $26.59 against $31.74 one-time.

    When does a prepaid Shopify subscription pack beat monthly?

    When skip or cancel cuts monthly down to about one paid cycle. At an 80% skip rate monthly keeps $26.59 and the 3-month prepaid pack still keeps $74.23. The billing fee can rise to about 38.9% before monthly matches one-time on the default stay.
