    Common questions

    How much is a Shopify chargeback fee?

    Shopify Payments and most card processors charge a chargeback fee on top of pulling the original order. This page defaults to $15 so the table is immediately useful. Replace it from your own statement — it is not Shopify's live dispute schedule.

    Should I fight a Shopify chargeback or just refund?

    Refunding before the chargeback lands skips the network fee and can still return the processing percentage. Fighting only wins if your representment win rate covers that fee plus the processing fees a refund can return. On a $54 ticket and a $15 fee, that floor is about 31%. A 25% win rate still loses to refunding. This page does the arithmetic from your rates; it does not tell you to fight or refund.
