    What this checks

    The order-profit calculator answers “what does this sale leave if it sticks.” Apparel, home, and made-to-order shops also need the mix: a typical refund rate, a return label, and whether the processor gives the processing fee back. That last piece is why PayPal can cost more on Shopify even after the sale is reversed.

    It does not recommend a processor, a refund policy, or a price, and it is not tax or financial advice. The mix is arithmetic from the rates you entered.

    Common questions

    Does Shopify refund processing fees when I refund an order?

    Shopify Payments often returns its processing fee on a full refund. A third-party processor such as PayPal or Stripe often keeps its fee. Shopify's own third-party transaction fee may reverse with the order. Enter the percentages from your statements — this page does not publish a live refund policy.

    How do I calculate Shopify profit after returns?

    Take completed-order profit on each payment path. For a full refund, subtract remaining processor or Shopify fees, outbound shipping, packaging, return postage, unsalvaged cost of goods, and the allocated plan share. Mix the two outcomes by your refund rate. That mix is expected profit per order, not a forecast.
