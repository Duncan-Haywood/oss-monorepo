    Common questions

    What does a Thumbtack job really cost me in lead fees?

    Lead price after credits, divided by your close rate. At $50 a lead, 10 percent credited back and a 20 percent close rate that is $225.00 in lead charges per job won. Your quoting time adds more: at 20 minutes a lead and $40 an hour the all-in cost is $291.67 per job.

    What close rate do I need for paid leads to pay off?

    Lead charges alone break even at 9.4 percent on the default numbers. Once you count the time spent quoting, the break-even rises to 12.2 percent, which is the number that matters.

    What is the most I should pay for a lead?

    At a 20 percent close rate, $480.00 earned per job and 20 minutes of quoting, a lead stops paying above about $91.00. The scenarios table shows how the month changes at other lead prices.
