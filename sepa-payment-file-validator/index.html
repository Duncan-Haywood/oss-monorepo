    The model, and what it leaves out

    • Structure: Document → CstmrCdtTrfInitn → one GrpHdr and one or more PmtInf blocks, each with CdtTrfTxInf transactions. Versions pain.001.001.03 and .09 are read; others are flagged.
    • Counts and sums are recomputed from the transactions, in integer cents, for each block and for the group header, and compared with NbOfTxs and CtrlSum.
    • IBAN uses the mod-97 check and the country's fixed length. A failing IBAN is reported with the check digits that would pass if only they were wrong, but nothing is ever changed for you.
    • Execution dates on a Saturday, Sunday, Good Friday, Easter Monday, 1 May, 25 or 26 December are flagged with the next banking day.
    • Rebuild rewrites NbOfTxs and CtrlSum only. It never edits a payment, an IBAN, an amount or a name.
    • Not checked: your bank's schema list, limits and cut-off times, whether a BIC matches its IBAN, sanctions screening, instant payments, direct debits (pain.008), non-euro and international payments, and anything your bank adds to the EPC rules.

    Common questions

    How do I check a SEPA payment file before I upload it to my bank?

    Paste the pain.001 XML or drop the file onto this page. It checks the XML structure, the group header and payment blocks, every IBAN with the mod-97 check, the transaction count and control sum at both levels, the execution date against weekends and TARGET2 closing days, the euro currency and amount format, and the characters allowed in names and references, then lists each problem with its line number.

    Why was my SEPA XML file rejected with a control sum or NbOfTxs error?

    The group header and every payment block repeat the number of transactions and the sum of the amounts. After someone edits an amount or deletes a payee by hand, those two numbers no longer match what the file holds and the bank refuses it. This tool recomputes both and can rewrite just those fields.

    How is an IBAN checked?

    Move the first four characters to the end, turn letters into numbers (A is 10, B is 11 and so on), and divide the long number by 97. A valid IBAN leaves a remainder of 1. The country also fixes the length, for example 22 characters for Germany and 18 for the Netherlands. The check catches a mistyped or swapped character; it cannot tell you the account belongs to the person you meant to pay.

    Why does the bank complain about a character in the payee name?

    The basic SEPA character set is the letters a to z without accents, digits and the symbols / - ? : ( ) . , ' + and space. Accented letters, an ampersand and many other symbols fall outside it. Some banks transliterate them and some reject the whole file.

    Can this tool fix my SEPA file?

    It can recompute NbOfTxs and CtrlSum in the group header and each payment block, which is what is wrong after a hand edit. It never edits a payment: a wrong IBAN, amount or name is a payment decision, so it is reported and left to you.

    Is it safe to put a file with bank account numbers into a web page?

    The page runs in your browser, the file is read locally and nothing is uploaded; a test in the code asserts there are no network calls. Account numbers are masked to their last four characters in the report and the downloaded findings list. Your own policies still decide whether you may open a payment file in a browser.

    What does this not check?

    Whether your bank accepts your schema version, your daily limits and cut-off times, whether the creditor's BIC matches the IBAN, sanctions screening, instant-payment (SCT Inst) limits, direct debit (pain.008) files, non-euro and international payment layouts, and any rule your bank adds on top of the EPC scheme.
