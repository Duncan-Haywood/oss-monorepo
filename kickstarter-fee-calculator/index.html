        Common questions

        How much does Kickstarter take from a funded project?

        On Kickstarter's published US schedule: a 5% platform fee on the total amount pledged, plus payment processing charged per pledge — 3% of the pledge plus $0.20 for pledges of $10 or more, or 5% plus $0.05 for pledges under $10. Combined, most US campaigns land around 8-10% of the total raise, and fees are charged only if the project is funded.

        Why do two campaigns with the same total raised pay different fees?

        Because the processing fee's $0.20 (or $0.05) component is charged once per pledge, not once per dollar. A campaign that raises $10,000 from 100 backers averaging $100 each pays that flat charge 100 times; the same $10,000 from 1,000 backers averaging $10 each pays it 1,000 times. More, smaller pledges keep a smaller share of an identical total raised — reward-tier pricing changes the fee, not just the marketing.

        What happens if the project does not reach its funding goal?

        Nothing is charged. Kickstarter is all-or-nothing: no pledge is collected and no fee applies unless the funding goal is met by the deadline.

        Is my data uploaded anywhere?

        No. Parsing and arithmetic happen entirely in your browser. The total raised, backer count, and any pledge amount you enter are never uploaded. The optional usage counter records only which features were used and the day, never any of your numbers.
