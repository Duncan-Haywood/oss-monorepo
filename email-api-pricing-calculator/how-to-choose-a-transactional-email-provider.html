How to choose a transactional email provider by send volume

Put your emails per month and growth rate into the calculator to price Resend, Postmark, SendGrid, Mailgun, Amazon SES and Brevo side by side. These are the questions to settle before you trust the answer.

Why do email API prices differ so much at the same volume?

Some providers sell tiered monthly plans with an included volume and a steep overage rate, while Amazon SES sells raw sending at about ten cents per thousand and leaves deliverability tooling, logs and support to you. At 100,000 emails a month the gap between the cheapest and most expensive list price can be ten times.

What matters more than the per-email price?

Deliverability and time. A provider that lands receipts and password resets in the inbox, with clear bounce handling and logs, saves more than a few dollars a month. Check shared versus dedicated IP, log retention and how fast support answers.

When should I switch providers?

When the 12-month spend under your growth rate differs enough to pay for the migration, which means moving templates, DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and warming a new sending reputation. Run the calculator with your growth rate before deciding.
