    Common questions
    
    
    What is the CSRD?
    
    
    The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (EU) 2022/2464 requires in-scope companies to publish audited sustainability information under the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS), including a double-materiality assessment.
    
    
    Did the Omnibus remove most companies from scope?
    
    
    The Omnibus package agreed in late 2025 narrows mandatory scope to companies with more than 1,000 employees and more than EUR 450M net turnover, and delays the later waves. How and when each Member State puts this into national law varies, so this tool shows the original and the Omnibus reading side by side.
    
    
    What if I am out of scope?
    
    
    Large customers, banks and investors may still ask you for data. The Commission's voluntary standard for smaller companies (VSME) is the format the Omnibus makes the ceiling on such requests, so preparing one data set saves answering each questionnaire from scratch.
    
    
    Does it cover non-EU companies?
    
    
    Yes in outline: non-EU groups are tested on EU turnover (more than EUR 450M) and an EU subsidiary or branch (more than EUR 200M turnover). Check both the group and the EU entity.
    
    
    Is my data uploaded anywhere?
    
    
    No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent; a test in the code asserts there are no network calls.
