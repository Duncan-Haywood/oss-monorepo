    Should I use Amazon AWD?

    Sellers search this after a supplier MOQ lands and Amazon offers Warehousing and Distribution. Sending the whole lot to FBA is simple. AWD parks the bulk at a cheaper cubic-foot rate and replenishes a working FBA stock. You-hold is the garage or 3PL version of that drip. The main calculator’s inbound / unit line is a blended number, so it cannot show whether the extra transfer is cheaper than parking 400 units in FBA.

    On the default $29.99 large-standard unit, AWD keeps $165.33 more on 400 units than sending everything to FBA now ($4723.93 vs $4558.60). AWD receive is $0.15 and the $0.68 transfer on units that move still beats $1.20 FBA inbound plus five months of FBA storage on the pile. The transfer can rise to about $1.09 before FBA wins. A $1.20 transfer flips FBA ahead by $42.67. You-hold and drip loses $161.33 because $0.25 / unit / month at home is more than FBA storage on this carton.

    Common questions

    Should I send a bulk PO to FBA or use Amazon AWD?

    On the calculator defaults — a $29.99 large-standard unit, 15% referral, $4.15 FBA pick, $8 cost of goods, $1.20 FBA inbound, 400 units selling 80 a month, AWD receive $0.15, AWD storage $0.48 / cu ft, and $0.68 AWD-to-FBA — AWD keeps $165.33 more on the lot than sending everything to FBA now ($4723.93 vs $4558.60). The transfer can rise to about $1.09 before FBA wins. You-hold and drip loses $161.33 because $0.25 / unit / month at home is more than FBA storage on this carton.

    Is Amazon AWD the same as inbound placement?

    No. Inbound placement is which fulfillment centers a shipment goes to, and the fee Amazon charges when you send to fewer of them. AWD is a cheaper bulk warehouse in front of FBA: you receive the PO there, then Amazon replenishes a working FBA stock. This page holds placement at zero so the table stays an inbound-bulk question. It does not recommend AWD and it does not publish Amazon's live AWD schedule.

    When does sending the whole lot to FBA beat AWD?

    When the AWD-to-FBA transfer plus AWD receive is more than FBA inbound plus the extra FBA storage on the pile. On these defaults a $1.20 transfer flips FBA ahead by $42.67 on 400 units.
