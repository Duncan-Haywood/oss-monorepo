    Should I liquidate aged FBA inventory?

    Sellers search this after Amazon offers FBA Liquidations on a leftover pile. The keep vs remove vs dispose page treats a return as a fee with no resale. This page adds the two recoveries: Amazon pays a percent of list, or you pay the return-removal fee and sell the units on eBay, your site, or a jobber.

    On the default 80 leftover $29.99 units, dispose is −$25.60. FBA Liquidations at 8% recovery and a $0.30 fee keeps $167.94. Pulling them and selling elsewhere at $4 leftover keeps $236.80 — $68.86 more than liquidations. Elsewhere leftover can fall to about $3.14 before liquidations match. Keeping at 5% monthly sell-through is −$109.82; it needs about 10.9% a month to match liquidations. At 20% sell-through, keeping flips to $530.94. With no other channel, liquidations still beat dispose by $193.54. A $0.80 fee and 0% recovery flips dispose ahead; recovery then needs about 1.6%.

    Is FBA Liquidations better than disposing leftover units?

    Usually, when recovery times list still beats the disposal fee after processing. On these defaults liquidations keep $193.54 more than dispose if you have no other channel. A $0.80 processing fee and 0% recovery flips dispose ahead; recovery then needs about 1.6% of list to match. Paste the recovery percent from Amazon's liquidation estimate.

    When does keeping aged inventory still beat liquidations?

    When monthly sell-through covers storage and the aged-inventory surcharge. On these defaults keeping needs about 10.9% a month to match an 8% liquidation.
