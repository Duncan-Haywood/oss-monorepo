    Common questions

    Will this tell me how many customers I will lose?

    No. How customers react is the one number nobody can read off past sales. The tool shows how many units you can lose before the raise stops paying, and the elasticity at which that happens, so you can judge whether your customers are that sensitive.

    What is elasticity here?

    The percentage fall in units sold for each 1% rise in price. 1.0 means a 5% raise loses about 5% of units. Commodity goods with many substitutes are often above 1; specialised services with loyal clients are often below it. 1.0 is a placeholder, not a benchmark.

    How do I get the numbers?

    Export items from your POS, store or price list: price, what each unit costs you to supply (materials, labour, fees that scale with a sale), and units sold over a recent period. Fixed overhead is left out on purpose, because it does not change when units change.

    Does it account for competitors or other items?

    No. Each item stands alone and costs stay flat. If a raise on one item pushes customers to another item or a rival, that is outside this arithmetic: test on a few items first.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. The browser page runs on the text in your own browser and never sends it anywhere. The CLI reads a local file or stdin and makes no network call.
