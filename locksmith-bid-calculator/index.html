      Common questions

      Why does the after-hours premium apply to labor and travel time but not to parts?

      Because a cylinder or key blank costs the same at 2am as it does at 2pm — what changes at 2am is your own availability. This calculator applies the emergency premium to billed time only, so the parts markup stays honest regardless of when the call happens.

      Why does the calculator compare the calculated price against a minimum service charge?

      Because a short job — a five-minute car unlock, a single cut key — can price below what the trip itself costs once you count drive time, fuel, and the overhead that applies whether or not this specific call happens. A minimum service charge is a floor: whichever number is higher, the calculated price or your stated minimum, is what you should quote.

      Why is there a separate callback reserve on top of overhead?

      A rekey or hardware install occasionally needs a free return visit — a key that doesn't turn quite right, a strike plate that needs adjusting. The callback reserve sets aside a percentage of the job's direct cost to absorb that risk instead of it silently eating into margin on every job where it happens.
