    Does this fuel surcharge match the contract?

    Fuel is billed as a percent of linehaul. The invoice often shows a dollar amount and no rate. A flat rate card skips FUEL because the base is the linehaul line, not a code. This page multiplies linehaul by the percent on the confirmation and compares that to the billed FSC line.

    On the default $1,200.00 linehaul at 28%, the contract expects $336.00. A $412.00 billed line is 34.33% of linehaul — $76.00 above the contract. A typed bulletin of 26% plus 2 points is the same 28% and expects the same $336.00. Paste the confirmation percent. This page does not fetch DOE, EIA, or a carrier portal.

    Does this fuel surcharge match the contract?

    Multiply linehaul by the contract fuel percent and compare that to the billed FSC line. On a $1,200.00 linehaul at 28%, the contract expects $336.00. A $412.00 billed line is 34.33% of linehaul — $76.00 above the contract. That is a review of the line, not a determination that the charge is wrong.

    How do I check a fuel surcharge against the DOE or carrier bulletin?

    Type the index percent from the bulletin you actually use and any points the contract adds. 26% plus 2 points is 28% and expects the same $336.00 on a $1,200.00 linehaul. The page does not fetch an index. Leave the index blank if the confirmation states a fixed percent.
