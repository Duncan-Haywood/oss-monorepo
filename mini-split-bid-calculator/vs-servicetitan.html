This calculator vs. ServiceTitan

Two different shapes: ServiceTitan is an account-based field service management platform for running an HVAC company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one mini-split install cost, given its heads, height, and extras, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

ServiceTitan is a field service management platform, founded in 2007 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan, headquartered in Glendale, California. It serves HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and other trades with scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, marketing, reporting, and job costing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. ServiceTitan's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — field service management software serving the HVAC trade among others, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceTitan appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceTitan does. It does not schedule technicians, dispatch crews, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one job's head count and BTU size, head type (wall, floor, cassette, ducted, or mixed) and story height, extra line-set feet past the included allowance, electrical extras (an existing disconnect through a new subpanel), and a condensate pump where gravity drainage is not available, plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, permit, travel and lift, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per head, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
