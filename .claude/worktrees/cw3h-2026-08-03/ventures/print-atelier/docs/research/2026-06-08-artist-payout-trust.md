# Artist Payout Trust Benchmarks

Run: 2026-06-08 19:30 UTC

## Signals

- Society6 announced a March 18, 2025 earnings change that sets artist markup at 10% for wall art products including art prints, framed art prints, recessed framed prints, and posters, while trade, wholesale, and marketplace orders pay a 5% net sale commission [Society6 artist earnings update](https://help.society6.com/en-US/updates-to-pricing-and-artist-earnings-effective-march-18-2025-1261599).
- Society6 also moved from an open marketplace toward an approved submission process, with new artwork submission scheduled to start July 22, 2025 after approval by a curation team [Society6 artist account update](https://help.society6.com/en-US/updates-to-society6-artists-account-effective-march-18-2025-1261598).
- Redbubble benchmark coverage describes a tiered account fee model where Standard accounts pay a 50% platform fee on monthly earnings, Premium accounts pay 20%, and Pro accounts are exempt [Redbubble profit guide](https://abetterfounder.com/redbubble-print-on-demand-complete-profit-guide-fees-margins-calculator/).
- A 2026 artist-platform comparison lists Artfinder at 40-45% commission and Society6 at 5-10% fixed royalty, while noting Redbubble tiered fees can reach 50% [Foundmyself platform comparison](https://www.foundmyself.com/blog/where-to-sell-artwork-online/).
- Hunter's 2025 cold-email report says the average cold email sequence reply rate is 4.5%, and email bodies with two custom attributes see a 5.6% reply rate versus 3.6% with no personalization [Hunter state of cold email](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email).
- PitchBrand's 2026 creator-pitch analysis reports a 4.1% overall reply rate, an 8.1% reply rate for campaigns targeting 1-25 brands, and a 21.4% reply rate for the top 10% of campaigns [PitchBrand state of pitching](https://www.pitchbrand.co/statistics/state-of-pitching-2026).

## Implication

Print Atelier should turn payout transparency into an artist acquisition tool, not a static promise. The outreach surface should calculate whether a SKU's 30% net-profit share actually beats POD marketplace royalty benchmarks before reps or automations lead with the claim.
