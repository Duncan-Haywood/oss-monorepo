How much to charge for mold remediation

The number a homeowner wants is a published “$999 mold special.” That number is not a takeoff. An 80-square-foot closet with one containment zone and one equipment day is not the same job as an 800-square-foot finished basement with two zones, four dry-out days, 240 square feet of demo, and a clearance. The word “mold job” is the same. The hours are not.

Count the containment and the demo, not just the special

Basement-waterproofing calculators price wells, a pit, and discharge. Radon-mitigation calculators price suction, membrane, and circuit. Pest-control calculators price bait stations. Drywall calculators price hang-and-finish of new board. A mold job is a contained remediation: the included closet, the extra zone the special never mentions, the demolition, the equipment days, and the clearance. Quoting all of that from a published special is how the first extra zone eats the field margin.

A job with a similar first zone can cost much more

Each extra zone is poly, a zipper, and a negative-air setup that never appears on the brochure. Extra square feet over the included closet are billed as their own hours. Demo, HVAC cleaning, a dumpster, occupied-home access, and a third-party clearance are adders that get forgotten on a closet special. On the sample rates an 800 sq ft finished basement with two zones, four dry-out days, 240 sq ft of demo, HVAC cleaning, a dumpster, occupied access, and a clearance prices about $11,543 ($14.43 per square foot of affected area, 39.0 crew-hours). The same shop’s 80 sq ft closet prices about $1,724 ($21.55 per square foot, 6.3 hours, walk-away $1,207). Matching a $3-per-square-foot internet quote on the basement is how that job becomes a loss of about $5,680.

A sewage-backed room is not automatically the expensive one

A 400 sq ft category-3 sewage-backed room with two zones, 200 sq ft of demo, three equipment days, HVAC cleaning, a dumpster, occupied access, and a clearance is not the basement plus 20%. On the sample rates that sewage job prices about $9,333 ($23.33 per square foot, 32.0 crew-hours) — more per square foot than the basement, less as a total, because the extra 400 basement squares and the extra dry-out day do not live in a published sewage adder. A typical 200 sq ft bedroom with 40 sq ft of demo and two dry-out days prices about $2,948 (10.5 hours). Treating either as a blended add-on is how the special that looked fine turns into a loss after the first extra zone.

Load the wage, then keep a walk-away number

A $45/h tech loaded at 36% payroll burden is $61.20. Margin is a share of the price you send, not a markup on cost. The walk-away price is total cost — below that the job makes nothing before you take a dollar home. On the sample rates even the brochure closet walk-away is $1,207, which is why a $999 Facebook special is already a loss of about $208 before the first extra zone. Replace every rate with your HEPA invoices before you send either number.

What this is not

This is not a mold inspection, a health determination, a clearance certificate, a guarantee that any building will test below a spore or moisture threshold, or a recommended market price. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type. Job type, affected area, containment zones, demolition, and whether the house needs a third-party clearance are yours to confirm.

Common questions

How much to charge for mold remediation?

Charge from the included closet, extra square feet over that include, extra containment zones, demolition, HEPA and dehumidifier days, third-party clearance, HVAC cleaning, dumpster, occupied-home access, loaded labor, overhead, and a margin that is a share of price. A default 80 sq ft closet at the sample rates in this calculator prices around $1,724 — about $21.55 per square foot of affected area.

Why is a flat mold-special quote dangerous?

Homeowners count 'a mold job is a mold job.' Extra containment, demolition, equipment days, HVAC cleaning, a dumpster, and a third-party clearance do not live in a $999 special. Matching a cheaper closet quote written for a brochure bath is how a finished basement becomes a loss.

Does a larger house always cost more to remediate?

Not in proportion. On the sample rates an 800 sq ft finished basement with two zones, four dry-out days, 240 sq ft of demo, HVAC cleaning, a dumpster, occupied access, and a clearance prices around $11,543 ($14.43 per square foot), while an 80 sq ft closet prices around $1,724 ($21.55 per square foot).
