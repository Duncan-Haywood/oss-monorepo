This calculator vs. Bonsai

Two different shapes: Bonsai runs your whole freelance business — proposals, contracts, time tracking, invoicing — as an ongoing subscription account. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what does this quote actually pay per hour, once every task is counted — before you send it.

What each tool is for

Bonsai is client-management software, sold under an ongoing subscription account to freelancers and small agencies running a full client roster. It builds and sends proposals and contracts with e-signatures, tracks billable and non-billable time across every project, generates and chases invoices, tracks expenses, and estimates quarterly taxes in supported regions, all synced across every client you run. Setting it up means creating an account and configuring your proposal, contract, and invoice templates — not a one-off calculation. Bonsai's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names, feature tiers, or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not send proposals, collect e-signatures, track time across a roster, or chase invoices. It takes one proposed quote — price, delivery hours, admin and revision time, overhead, and subcontractor cost — and works out the effective hourly rate you are really being paid once every hidden task is counted, the price that hits your target rate, what a requested discount or a scope change actually costs, and what a mid-project cancellation is worth compared with finishing. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and nothing you type is uploaded.
