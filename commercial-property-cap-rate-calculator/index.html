    Common questions
    
    
    What is a cap rate and how do I use it?
    
    
    The capitalization rate is a property's net operating income divided by its value. Dividing NOI by a market cap rate gives an income-approach value: $145,740 of NOI at a 7% cap rate is about $2.08 million. A lower cap rate means a higher price for the same income.
    
    
    What counts in net operating income?
    
    
    Rent and other income after vacancy, minus operating costs: property tax, insurance, utilities, repairs, management and replacement reserves. It excludes mortgage payments, depreciation and income tax, which is why the same NOI can serve cash and financed buyers.
    
    
    What debt service coverage ratio do lenders want?
    
    
    Many commercial lenders want NOI at least 1.20 to 1.25 times the annual loan payment, and they set the loan size by that test as well as by loan-to-value. If your DSCR is lower, the loan shrinks and you need more equity.
