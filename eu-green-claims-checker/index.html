    Common questions
    
    
    When do the EU green claims rules start to apply?
    
    
    Directive (EU) 2024/825 had to be turned into national law by 27 March 2026 and the national rules apply to consumer marketing from 27 September 2026.
    
    
    Can I still say a product is eco-friendly or green?
    
    
    Not as a bare generic claim. A generic environmental claim is banned unless you can show recognised excellent environmental performance, for example an EU Ecolabel. Say what is better and by how much instead.
    
    
    Can I call a product climate neutral if I offset the emissions?
    
    
    No. A claim that a product has a neutral, reduced or positive climate impact because of offsetting is banned. You may still describe offset spending separately as a contribution, not as the product's footprint.
    
    
    Does this apply to a small shop or a seller outside the EU?
    
    
    Yes if you market to consumers in the EU. The rules amend consumer-protection law, which has no small-business exemption, and they follow the consumer, not the seller's address.
