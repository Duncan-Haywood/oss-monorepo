    Common questions

    Does overtime on a Davis-Bacon job include fringe benefits?

    It depends on how the fringe is paid. A bona fide contribution to a health, pension, or apprenticeship plan is excluded from the overtime "regular rate" under 29 CFR 5.32 and 29 CFR 778.7, so it is owed at the same rate for every hour and never gets a time-and-a-half premium. Fringe paid directly to the worker in cash instead of into a plan is included in the regular rate, so it does get the overtime premium on hours over 40. Paying cash-in-lieu-of-fringe at straight time during overtime hours — which is what many payroll systems do by default — underpays every week it runs.

    What counts as the "basic hourly rate" on a wage determination?

    The wage determination for a classification states two numbers: a basic hourly rate and a fringe benefit rate. The contractor must provide at least their combined total, through any mix of cash wages, bona fide plan contributions, or cash paid in lieu of fringe. This calculator asks for both required figures and both actual figures so it can check the combined total and, separately, get the overtime math right — the two figures play different roles.

    How does the apprentice-to-journeyman ratio work on certified payroll?

    A registered apprenticeship program's wage determination sets an approved ratio, such as one apprentice per three journeymen, applied per classification at the site. Apprentices beyond that ratio are not entitled to the reduced apprentice rate — they must be paid the full journeyman prevailing wage for the hours they worked. This calculator reports how many apprentices exceed the approved ratio from the counts you enter; it does not look up the approved ratio for you, since that comes from the specific apprenticeship program's registration.

    Does this generate my WH-347 certified payroll report?

    Not the signed filing. This page checks one worker and one week; the WH-347 report builder fills the weekly payroll columns for every worker (hours by day, gross, deductions, net) with the same overtime rule and a prevailing-wage check on each row. It does not submit or sign anything on your behalf, and the Statement of Compliance stays yours to sign.

    Does this tell me which wage determination applies to my project?

    No. The applicable wage determination comes from the contracting officer and the project's own solicitation, published on SAM.gov. This calculator takes the required base and fringe rates as inputs you already looked up — it never guesses a rate or a classification for you.
