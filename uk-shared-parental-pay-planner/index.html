    Common questions
    
    
    How does Shared Parental Pay work?
    
    
    The mother's 39 weeks of maternity pay are a pool. If she ends her maternity pay early, the weeks she does not use can be paid as Shared Parental Pay to her or her partner, in blocks of at least one week. Each week pays the lower of the standard rate (about £194.32 from April 2026) and 90% of the parent's average weekly earnings.
    
    
    How many weeks can be shared?
    
    
    Up to 37: the mother must take at least 2 weeks of maternity leave and pay after the birth. Taking 26 weeks of SMP leaves 13 shared weeks.
    
    
    Which parent should take the shared weeks?
    
    
    Statutory pay is the lower of the flat rate and 90% of earnings, so the parent with higher earnings gets the flat rate and a lower earner may get less. If both earn over about £216 a week the pay is the same either way, and employer enhanced pay, childcare and leave rights usually decide.
    
    
    Why does the first SMP block matter?
    
    
    The mother's first 6 weeks of SMP are paid at 90% of her earnings, well above the flat rate for most people. Giving those weeks up costs more than the later flat-rate weeks.
