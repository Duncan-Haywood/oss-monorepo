THIS CALCULATOR VS ANKORSTORE

Two different marketplaces. This tool prices Faire orders specifically.

Ankorstore is a separate B2B wholesale marketplace, not a competitor to this calculator — it is a competitor to Faire itself. Founded in France in November 2019, Ankorstore grew to unicorn status on a large funding round led by Index Ventures, and connects independent brands with retailers mainly across Europe, with its own commission structure that it has continued to change (it simplified its pricing and moved to 0% commission on reorders in January 2026, according to its own published updates). Ankorstore's current full rate card is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact figures, so none of its numbers are entered here or invented on this page — only its well-documented identity and category are stated.

This calculator does not price an Ankorstore order at all. It is built specifically from Faire's own published North America fee schedule — the 15% marketplace commission, the one-time $10 new-customer fee on a retailer's first order, Faire Direct's 0% commission for retailers you bring yourself, and Faire's payout-speed processing rates — and turns one wholesale invoice into net payout, margin, and ROI across those Faire-specific paths. A brand selling through both marketplaces owes each one its own separate fees on its own orders; this tool does not combine, convert, or compare the two marketplaces' rates against each other.

Common questions

Is Ankorstore the same thing as Faire?

No. Both are B2B wholesale marketplaces connecting independent brands with retailers, but they are separate companies with separate commission structures, separate retailer bases, and separate payout mechanics. Ankorstore was founded in France in 2019 and reached unicorn status backed by Index Ventures, focused heavily on European brands and retailers; Faire is a separate marketplace with its own published North American fee schedule. A brand selling on both would owe each marketplace its own fees on its own orders — this calculator does not combine or convert between the two.

Does this calculator work out my Ankorstore fees too?

No. This calculator's commission, new-customer fee, and payout-speed math is built specifically from Faire's own published North America fee schedule. Ankorstore publishes its own separate rates, which change independently of Faire's and are not reachable from this session to confirm today's figures, so none of Ankorstore's numbers are entered here or invented on this page. If you sell on Ankorstore, use its own current rate card and calculator.
