0-10 dimensions (AI leverage, autonomous ops, time to revenue, gross margin, defensibility,
distribution). Ties break on the floor (weakest dimension), then distribution, then time to
revenue. A failed liability/ethics gate ranks last regardless of the mean.

> not a customer measurement: no venture records revenue, and `portfolio/usage/usage.yaml`
> has no visitor reading for any of them (a `null`, never measured -- not a zero). The
> scoring file notes that every score anyone has since measured moved *down*.
