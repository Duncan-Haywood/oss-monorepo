# Candidate: Tender radar

**One line.** Public-tender monitoring plus first-draft bid assembly for small
specialist firms who are eligible for public contracts and never bid because the
paperwork costs more than the odds justify.

**Verdict: park; needs a channel partner.** The economics work and the ethics
are clean. The problem is that the buyer is diffuse and the incumbents own the
one channel that reaches them.

## The shape

Tender data is published. The barrier for a five-person specialist firm is not
finding contracts — it is that qualifying and responding takes days of unpaid
work per bid, so they do not bid, so the same incumbents win. A tool that
(a) filters to what this firm can actually win and (b) produces the first draft
of the response from their existing documents changes the bid-cost arithmetic.

Pricing: €99–€299/month, ideally with the association reselling into its
membership. Success fees are tempting and should be refused — see below.

## Liability and ethics

- **The firm submits, signs, and warrants.** We produce drafts. Anything else
  puts an unattended agent inside a public procurement declaration, where
  misstatement is not a refund conversation.
- **No success fees, no contingency pricing.** Contingency arrangements around
  public contracting attract procurement-integrity rules in several
  jurisdictions and would need counsel per market. Flat subscription avoids the
  entire category.
- **Never generate claims the firm cannot substantiate.** Drafting from the
  customer's own certifications and past performance only; a bid that
  overstates capability is fraud with our tooling on it. The draft should flag
  every unsupported claim rather than filling it in plausibly.

## Where it can fail

- **Incumbent aggregators.** They already have the associations, the feeds, and
  the sales motion. Competing head-on is a marketing budget this portfolio does
  not have.
- **Lumpy value perception.** A customer who loses three bids cancels, even if
  the tool did its job. Framing has to be cost-per-bid-attempted, and the
  product should make attempts cheap enough to be worth several.
- **Jurisdiction sprawl.** Each market has its own portal, format, and
  eligibility rules. One market done properly beats five done shallowly.

## What would unpark it

A trade association or an existing vertical community willing to introduce it to
members. That is the only variable that matters; everything else here is
buildable in a fortnight. Absent a partner, this stays parked rather than
becoming a marketing project.
