# Contractor bid readiness

## Target user and problem

Independent general, specialty, and public-works contractors preparing an RFP
submission often track insurance, licenses, forms, signatures, references,
pricing sheets, and addenda across email and folders. A missed artifact can
consume a bid opportunity; the search phrase is "contractor bid checklist" or
"RFP submission checklist".

## Value and differentiation

The first useful job is a deadline-aware matrix with explicit ownership and a
portable private handoff. It is narrower than project-management software and
does not require an account or upload. Existing alternatives are Word/Excel
templates and broad bid-management suites; local-first, solicitation-specific
status and no setup are the wedge.

## Business and acquisition

The product can rank for contractor bid checklist, RFP submission checklist,
and bid document checklist. A future paid tier could add saved templates,
team sharing, or customer-owned integrations, but the free browser workflow is
complete. Hosting is static with zero per-user inference or storage cost.

## Automation, risks, and fit

The deterministic matrix is fully agent-operable and contains no customer data
on our servers. It does not certify compliance, interpret a solicitation, file
on behalf of a contractor, or promise an award. Users remain responsible for
reviewing the source requirements. This is distinct from quote calculators,
retainage, e-invoice preflight, and evidence monitors: it coordinates a bid's
own artifact readiness rather than pricing, payment, file-format, or external
change evidence.

## Launch experiment

Ship a static vertical slice, record local aggregate checks and report exports,
and look for repeat use by contractors across two bids within 90 days. No paid
API, account, or public publishing is required for the experiment.
