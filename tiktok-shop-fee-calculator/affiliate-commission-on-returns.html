A 15% affiliate and a 12% return rate on the same $19.99 SKU

“TikTok Shop takes 6%” is true and incomplete. The number that decides whether a creator listing makes money is usually the commission you set, times the share of orders that come back without clawing that commission back.

The SKU

A $19.99 beauty item. The buyer pays no postage. It cost $5.50 to buy, $4.28 to fulfill, $0.40 to pack, and $1.50 of ads are allocated to the order. Referral is the mid-2026 US default of 6%. You offer creators 15% of item price. 12% of orders come back. 80% of those returned units cannot be sold again. The creator keeps the commission on a return.

If the order is kept

Illustrative rates. Yours depend on category, region, and the commission you actually set.
Buyer paid$19.99
TikTok referral, 6%−$1.20
Creator commission, 15%−$3.00
Item, fulfill, pack, ads−$11.68
You keep$4.11

That looks fine. A seller who stops here raises the commission to get more creators on the SKU.

If the order comes back

The buyer is refunded. TikTok typically returns most of the referral and keeps a refund-admin fee (20% of that referral, capped at $5). The outbound fulfillment, packaging, and ads have already been spent. In this example 80% of the $5.50 item is unsellable. The $3.00 creator commission stays with the creator.

Refund to the buyer$0.00 kept
Refund admin (20% of $1.20)−$0.24
Creator commission, not clawed back−$3.00
Unsellable goods (80% of $5.50)−$4.40
Outbound fulfill, pack, ads−$6.18
You lose$13.82

Expected value at a 12% return rate

88% of the time you keep $4.11. 12% of the time you lose $13.82. Expected profit is $1.96 — less than half of the “kept order” figure, and a 9.8% margin on the revenue you actually retain. The same SKU sold organically (no creator cut) expects about $5.00. The affiliate path costs roughly $3.04 more per order after returns, almost the entire 15% you offered.

Open the calculator and change the return rate or the claw-back toggle. The gap is the product: a listing that looks profitable at 6% + 15% is a different listing once returns keep the commission.

What the announced 1 October 2026 rates do to this SKU

Under the announced 8% referral plus 1.5% creator facilitation on affiliate-sourced orders, the same affiliate path keeps about $1.36 instead of $1.96. That is still not a pricing recommendation — it is the arithmetic on these assumptions. Confirm the live schedule in Seller Center before you reprice.

Common questions

Does a TikTok Shop affiliate commission still cost you on a returned order?

Usually yes. In the worked example the creator keeps the 15% commission when the order comes back, TikTok returns most of the 6% referral but keeps a refund-admin fee, and the outbound fulfillment, packaging, and ads are already spent. A kept order leaves $4.11 and a returned order loses $13.82.

What is the real margin on a 15% affiliate SKU at a 12% return rate?

Expected profit is $1.96 per order, not the $4.11 a kept order shows, because 88% of the time you keep $4.11 and 12% of the time you lose $13.82. That is a 9.8% margin on the revenue you actually retain. The same SKU sold organically with no creator cut expects about $5.00.
