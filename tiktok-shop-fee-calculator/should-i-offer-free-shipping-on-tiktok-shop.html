    Common questions

    Should I offer free shipping on TikTok Shop?

    On the calculator example — a $19.99 item, $4.99 shipping charged, 6% referral, 15% creator commission on item price only, 12% returns — charging shipping keeps more than baking the $4.99 into the item. Referral is a tie (same fee base). Affiliate is not: the creator is paid on the higher item price. Eating the label at $19.99 drops leftover further and needs extra orders to match.

    Is moving shipping into the item price free on TikTok Shop?

    No when commission is on item price only. TikTok's referral sits on item plus shipping charged, so $19.99 + $4.99 and $24.98 + $0.00 take the same referral. The creator payout rises by 15% of the $4.99 you moved. Tick 'commission includes shipping charged' if your own statements already pay creators on the full customer total — then the same customer total is a leftover tie.

    Does free shipping need extra orders to stay even?

    Yes when leftover per order falls. The page shows how many extra orders per month would match charging shipping. If the free-shipping path has a non-positive leftover, extra volume cannot catch up.
