# Print Atelier

A two-sided art commerce business that uses AI to digitize original paintings and sell high-quality prints — without artists trading time for a single sale.

## The Idea: Software-First, AI-First

Traditional painting is one-to-one: an artist spends days or weeks on a canvas, sells it once, and starts over. Historically, digitizing art required expensive professional photography, creating a bottleneck.

Print Atelier flips that model with software. Artists use our proprietary AI-powered mobile app to scan their work in minutes. The app handles glare reduction, color correction, and super-resolution upscaling to produce museum-quality digital masters. We then monetize each piece repeatedly through automated print sales and global distribution.

Artists keep creating. Our software handles capture, cataloging, marketing, and fulfillment.

## Two Sides of the Business

| Side | What we do | Who we serve |
|------|------------|--------------|
| **Supply** | Automated outreach, AI app-based capture, and instant onboarding | Painters and visual artists |
| **Demand** | Programmatic SEO, AI-generated room mockups, and global POD commerce | Buyers of fine art prints |

## Artist Value Proposition (The "Zero-Effort" Print Business)

- **Instant Digitization:** Scan your art with your phone; our AI makes it print-ready.
- **30% of net profits** on every print sold from your work, paid automatically via Stripe.
- **Zero Logistics:** API-integrated print-on-demand partners handle global fulfillment.
- **AI Marketing:** We automatically generate beautiful interior design mockups of your art.
- Your original stays yours; we monetize the digital master.

## Why This Scales Infinitely

Because we replace manual field photographers with a mobile app, our marginal cost to acquire and digitize a new piece of art is near zero. Growth means:

1. Scraping and automating outreach to thousands of artists globally.
2. Generating programmatic SEO pages and Pinterest pins for every piece digitized.
3. Running high-margin dynamic ads using AI-generated lifestyle mockups.
