This tool vs. Chargeflow

Chargeflow is a well-known automated chargeback-management platform, described on chargeflow.io as connecting directly to your payment processors and store to detect a dispute, pull relevant evidence — order details, shipping confirmations, delivery signatures, and more — and assemble and submit a card-network-compliant response automatically, with little or no manual work. It prices on a success basis: a percentage of whatever it recovers, rather than a flat subscription, and you can start it for free with no credit card.

That makes Chargeflow the right choice for one job: connecting to your systems so disputes are detected and responded to automatically, on an ongoing basis, without you assembling each packet by hand. This page does not do that job, and does not try to. It has no integrations, connects to no processor or store, and detects nothing on its own — it takes the facts you already have for one dispute and turns them into a reviewable checklist and draft.

QuestionWhere to answer it
I get disputes across many orders and want them detected and answered automatically, ongoing. Chargeflow or a comparable platform — that requires connecting your processor and store, and paying a share of what it recovers.
I have one dispute in front of me right now and the order, fulfillment, and delivery facts to back it. This tool — pick the dispute reason, check off what you hold, and get a reviewable draft in your browser, free, no signup.
I want a system that keeps working across every dispute without me touching it each time. Chargeflow or a comparable platform — this tool remembers nothing between disputes and connects to nothing.
I want to know exactly what evidence I am missing before I submit, without paying a percentage of the recovery. This tool's gap review, which marks what you do not yet hold as "do not submit" rather than guessing.

This tool is deliberately the opposite shape from an automated dispute-management platform: nothing to connect, no percentage of a recovery owed, and nothing you type ever leaves your browser. That makes it useful for a single dispute you already have facts for, and the wrong tool for detecting and answering disputes at volume across every order without manual review — that is exactly the job a platform like Chargeflow is built for.

It needs no account, no integration, and no sign-in: it is a static page, the checklist and draft run in your browser, and once it has loaded it keeps working with the connection off.

Common questions

Should I use this tool or a platform like Chargeflow?

They answer different questions. Chargeflow is a paid, automated dispute-management platform: it connects to your payment processors and store, pulls evidence such as order, shipping, and delivery data on its own, and submits card-network-compliant responses without you writing them, charging a percentage of whatever it recovers — described on Chargeflow's own site. That is an ongoing integration you connect once and let run. This tool does not connect to anything and recovers nothing on its own. It takes the order, fulfillment, delivery, and support facts you already have, walks you through the checklist your dispute reason actually needs, and gives you a draft to review and submit yourself, in your browser, for free.
