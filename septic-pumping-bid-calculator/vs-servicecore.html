This calculator vs. ServiceCore

Two different shapes: ServiceCore routes, dispatches, and invoices a septic fleet across a whole company. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one pump-out cost, given its gallons, hose pull, access, and disposal, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

ServiceCore is business software built for septic, portable restroom, and dumpster rental operators — a narrower niche than a general field-service platform, and one that covers the pumping side of this trade directly. Its published feature set covers route optimisation and dispatch, scheduling, job and asset management, invoicing and payment collection, compliance record-keeping, and a mobile app that puts the day's schedule, driving directions, job notes, signature capture and photo upload in the driver's hand. It is sold as an ongoing subscription for a company. ServiceCore's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's plan names or dollar figures, so none is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — septic and liquid-waste business management software, sold as a subscription — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceCore appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceCore does. It does not route a truck, dispatch a driver, hold a compliance record, or store a customer on a server. It takes one stop's tank gallons, any extra tanks on the same site, hose pull distance, how hard the lid is to reach, riser count and cost, baffle replacement, disposal cost per gallon, the dump-trip fee, an after-hours premium, loaded wage and payroll burden, overhead, travel, and target margin, and turns that into the per-job price and the walk-away price below which the stop loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears that floor, and a printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or ServiceCore?

They do different jobs, and a septic business of any size may end up using both. ServiceCore is business software built for septic and liquid-waste companies: it optimises truck routes, dispatches and schedules jobs, tracks assets, handles invoicing and payment, keeps compliance records, and gives drivers a mobile app carrying the day's schedule, directions, job notes, signature capture and photos. It is sold as an ongoing subscription for a company. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from tank gallons, any extra tanks on the same site, hose pull distance, how hard the lid is to reach, riser and baffle work, disposal cost per gallon, the dump-trip fee, after-hours premium, loaded wage and payroll burden, overhead, travel, and target margin, it turns that into the per-job price and the walk-away price below which the stop loses money. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A septic company already routing trucks, dispatching drivers, and invoicing across a fleet is better served by ServiceCore or a comparable liquid-waste platform; an operator who wants an instant, private gut-check on what one pump-out should cost before opening a business system is in the right place here.

Does this calculator price disposal and the dump trip?

Yes, and separately, because they scale differently. Disposal is entered per gallon, so it rises with the tank you actually pumped rather than with the stop. The dump trip is entered as its own fee, because the drive to the treatment plant costs roughly the same whether the truck is carrying one tank or three — which is exactly why a second tank on the same site is usually more profitable than a second stop across town, and why the calculator lets you add extra tanks to one job rather than pricing each as a fresh call. Both feed the same walk-away price as the labour, so a stop that clears its labour and loses money at the plant shows up before you quote it.
