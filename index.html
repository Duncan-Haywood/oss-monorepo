    Your saved bids

    Bids you saved in any tool on this site, newest first. Opening one takes you to the tool that priced it with your saved bids showing, ready to restore. These are kept in this browser only — never uploaded, and clearing your browser data clears them, so download a backup below if they matter. Mark one won or lost in the tool that priced it and it stops counting as waiting here — and once a few have been decided, this page says how often your quotes are landing, whether that is the same across the tools you use or one trade pulling it down, and once a trade has a longer history, whether it has been landing differently lately than it used to. Add what a won job came to once it was delivered and it also says which of your trades keeps the most of what it charges — and whether that is the same trade your quotes actually land on, which is the one thing no single tool here can tell you.

      Move your saved bids to another browser

      Saved bids live in this browser only. Download them as a file to keep a copy, or to open them on your laptop, your phone, or a new device.

      The file holds the job names and numbers you typed, so keep it somewhere you would keep a customer list. Restoring adds to what is already here and never deletes a bid.
