Stripe decline codes: which to retry, which never to

When a Stripe subscription payment fails, the decline_code says what recovery can do. Some codes mean a retry may work tomorrow. Others mean every retry is wasted and the only fix is a new card. This is the table Dunning Engine, a free self-hosted recovery server, acts on.

The five classes and the default plan

Day numbers count from the failed payment, using the default policy (14-day grace period). Everything is configurable.

Retry days for funds are shown after the optional payday nudge (the 1st, 2nd, 15th, 16th or a Friday), so they shift with the calendar. The days above are for a failure on Monday 5 January.

Any code not listed, and a failure with no decline code at all, is treated as soft. call_issuer and transaction_not_allowed also land there: the bank has to act, so if the spaced retries keep failing the customer's emails ask for another card.

Meanings are paraphrased from Stripe's public documentation of decline codes and can change; Stripe's own page is the authority, and the classification above is a policy choice, not a Stripe statement. Not affiliated with Stripe.

Common questions

Which Stripe decline codes should I retry?

Retry only decline codes where the payment can succeed later without the customer doing anything: insufficient_funds, the velocity and withdrawal-limit codes, and the generic or temporary issuer declines such as generic_decline, do_not_honor, try_again_later and processing_error. Do not retry lost_card, stolen_card, pickup_card, fraudulent or restricted_card, and do not retry expired or incorrect card details until the customer has replaced the card.

What does do_not_honor mean in Stripe?

do_not_honor is the issuer's vague refusal: the bank declined the payment and chose not to say why. It is not proof the card is bad. Dunning Engine treats it as a soft decline: it retries on a spaced schedule and emails the customer an update-card link in case the bank keeps refusing.

Where do I find the decline code for a failed Stripe payment?

Open the failed payment in the Stripe Dashboard, or read last_payment_error.decline_code on the PaymentIntent behind the invoice, or the same field on the charge. Some failures have only an error code and no decline code; Dunning Engine treats a missing code as a soft decline.
