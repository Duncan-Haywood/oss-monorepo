This tool vs. Printful

Two different shapes: Printful is account-based print-on-demand fulfillment for running a print storefront. This page is a free, private check of one file's resolution and filename before you upload it anywhere.

What each is for

Printful is a print-on-demand and warehousing company, founded in 2013, that lets a seller upload artwork, connect it to an online store — Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce and others — and have each order printed and shipped only after a customer buys it, so the seller holds no inventory. It is used through a free account, with production and shipping charged per order rather than a flat subscription. Printful's own upload dashboard runs its own checks on a file after you submit it, and this page does not restate its current published resolution guidance or pricing, because printful.com is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact wording: only its well-documented identity and category — print-on-demand fulfillment, connected to a seller's own storefront — are stated here.

This tool does none of what Printful does. It does not host a storefront, print anything, or ship anything. It reads a candidate file's pixel dimensions, format, byte size, and filename directly in your browser, checks the implied DPI at the print size named in the filename against a documented 150 DPI floor, ranks up to five candidate masters head-to-head at one target size, and exports a dated technical record — all before the file is ever uploaded anywhere. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and never transmits the artwork.
