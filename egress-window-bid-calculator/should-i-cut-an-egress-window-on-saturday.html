Should I cut an egress window on Saturday?

Owners call with a weekday number: “Can you start Saturday? We need the legal bedroom before the listing. Same $3,500?” That sounds like the same opening. It is not the same crew day. After-hours is time-and-a-half. You cannot leave a four-foot foundation hole overnight, so it is a second trip to plate or fence it. Working around a family that still uses the basement on a weekday is the same wage on a slower cut.

Saturday is not the weekday bid

A weekday they leave still carries the wage you already timed. After-hours raises that wage to time-and-a-half and adds a starting second trip. On a typical masonry legal bedroom — vinyl slider, steel well, cover, drain, ladder, $42 wage, 30% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, 14 miles — the weekday bid is $3,976.62. After-hours is $4,990.68. The walk-away jumps from $3,181.30 to $3,992.54. Taking $3,500 to “just do Saturday at the weekday number” covers the empty weekday and loses $492.54 after hours.

A poured-wall stone well loses even on the weekday

The Saturday special is more tempting when they already have the $1,800 Facebook number in mind and the wall is poured concrete. The weekday bid is $7,494.94. They-stay-in-the-basement is $7,757.87. After-hours is $9,045.85 and the walk-away is $7,236.68. Taking $1,800 to “just cut what I bought, this weekend” still loses money — even on the weekday.

This is not a customer-supplied sash or a walk-in shower

Customer-supplied kits are their sash and an install-only number — see should I install a customer-supplied egress window. After-hours is when you can stand in the yard and whether the hole stays open overnight. A walk-in shower Saturday is tile overtime in the only bath they still use. A toilet Saturday is an overflowing closet. A boiler Saturday is a no-heat call. This prices an egress window: the opening, the cut, and the well.

Common questions

Should I cut an egress window after hours or on Saturday?

Only after you price time-and-a-half plus a second trip to plate the hole. You cannot leave an open foundation excavation overnight. A $3,500 Saturday special on a typical masonry legal bedroom covers an empty weekday and loses $492.54 after hours. A $1,800 Facebook special on a poured-wall stone well sits under that walk-away number even on an empty weekday.

Does working around a basement they still use change the bid?

Yes. Same wage, slower cut, set, and punch-list. On a typical masonry slider with a steel well the they-stay bid is $4,163.03 versus $3,976.62 when they leave.

Can I take the weekday $3,500 number on Saturday?

Usually not on the job they actually want. On a typical masonry legal bedroom the after-hours walk-away is $3,992.54. $3,500 covers an empty weekday and loses $492.54 after hours. On a poured-wall stone well, $1,800 still loses money even on the weekday.
