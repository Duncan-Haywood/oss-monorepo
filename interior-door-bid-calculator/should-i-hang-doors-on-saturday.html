Should I hang doors on Saturday?

Owners call with a weekday number: “Can you come Saturday? We both work. Same $150 a door?” That sounds like the same opening. It is not the same crew day. After-hours is time-and-a-half. A Saturday hang is not a stop you combine with a weekday route, so it is a second dispatch. Working around furniture, pets, and a homeowner who stays to watch is the same wage on a slower hang.

Saturday is not the weekday bid

A weekday they-leave slot still carries the wage you already timed. After-hours raises that wage to time-and-a-half and adds a starting second trip. On a typical hollow-slab 80-inch hang — $42 wage, 30% burden, 15% overhead, 20% target margin, 6 miles — the weekday bid is $339.14. After-hours is $523.98. The walk-away jumps from $271.31 to $419.19. Taking $349 to “just do Saturday at the weekday number” covers the weekday slot and loses $70.19 after hours.

A pocket-conversion French pair loses even on the weekday

The Saturday special is more tempting when they already bought a French pair and want a pocket conversion “this weekend before guests.” The weekday bid is $2,823.66. Weekday while they stay home is $2,865.85. After-hours is $3,360.52 and the walk-away is $2,688.41. Taking $150 to “just hang what I bought, Saturday” still loses money — even on the weekday.

This is not a customer-supplied slab or a patio door

Customer-supplied leaves are their millwork and a hang-only number — see should I hang a customer-supplied door. After-hours is when you can stand in the opening. A patio slider is panels and impact glass. A kitchen backsplash Saturday is tile overtime in a kitchen they still cook in. A toilet Saturday is an overflowing closet. This prices an interior door replacement.

Common questions

Should I hang interior doors on Saturday or after hours?

Only after you price time-and-a-half plus a second Saturday dispatch. A Saturday hang is not a stop you combine with a weekday route. A $150 Saturday special on a pocket-conversion French pair sits under that walk-away number even on a weekday they-leave slot.

Does a weekday they-stay hang change the bid?

Yes. Same wage, slower hang and punch-list. On a typical hollow-slab 80-inch hang the they-stay bid is $360.73 versus $339.14 when they leave you the house.

Can I take the weekday $349 number on Saturday?

Usually not on the job they actually want. On a cheap hollow-slab hang the after-hours walk-away is $419.19. $349 covers a weekday they-leave slot and loses $70.19 after hours. On a pocket-conversion French pair, $150 still loses money even on the weekday.
