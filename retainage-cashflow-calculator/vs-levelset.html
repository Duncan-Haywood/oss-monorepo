This calculator vs. Levelset

Two different shapes: Levelset is an account-based platform built around the legal and administrative side of getting paid on a construction project. This page is a free, private, no-account worksheet for one number — what retainage and a payment delay are costing you right now.

What each tool is for

Levelset is a construction-payments and lien-rights company — it began as zlien and was acquired by Procore Technologies in 2021. Its tools are built around the legal and administrative side of getting paid on a construction project: preliminary notices, mechanics lien filings and waivers, payment applications, and collections, used by contractors, subcontractors, and suppliers across many projects over time. It is an account-based platform — you sign up and use it as an ongoing service, not a one-off calculator. Levelset's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of that. It does not file a notice, does not track lien deadlines, and does not manage payments across a portfolio of jobs. It takes one project's contract value, retainage percentage, amount paid, and days outstanding, and turns that into a private, instant cash snapshot — what is withheld, what a further delay is modeled to cost, what bid would keep the same progress cash at a different retainage rate, and whether a partial check offered today beats waiting for the rest. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
