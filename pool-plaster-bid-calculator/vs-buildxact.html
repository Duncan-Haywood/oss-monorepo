This calculator vs. Buildxact

Two different shapes: Buildxact is account-based estimating and project-management software for running a construction business across trades and jobs. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this pool replaster cost, given its surface, finish, and chip-out, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

Buildxact is construction estimating and project-management software founded in Australia in 2011, with North American headquarters in Austin, Texas. It serves builders, remodelers, and contractors across trades — including carpentry, painting, roofing, and flooring — combining material takeoffs, quoting, job costing, scheduling, and client communication into one subscription system meant to run a whole business across every job and every crew. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. Buildxact's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — multi-trade construction estimating and project-management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Buildxact appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Buildxact does. It does not manage takeoffs across trades, schedule crews, track job costs over time, or store any project record on a server. It takes one pool replaster — floor-plus-wall plaster surface (not the homeowner's length times width), finish (white plaster, quartz, pebble, or glass), chip-out scope, a spa, steps, waterline tile, coping, and a drain-and-refill, plus loaded wage, material rates, and target margin — and turns that into the walk-away price below which the job loses money, the price per square foot of plaster and of water surface, a breakdown of where the hours and cost actually go, and a copyable customer-facing bid summary, plus a printable customer proposal with scope lines and a signature block. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
