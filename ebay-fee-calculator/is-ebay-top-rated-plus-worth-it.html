    What this checks

    Resellers search “is eBay Top Rated Plus worth it” because the 10% final-value discount looks like free money. Seller-paid return labels and 1-day handling can wipe the fee save you thought you were capturing.

    On the defaults — $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, a 10% final-value discount, 60% standard sell-through vs 75% Top Rated, 8% buyer-paid returns vs 16% free returns, $0 vs $5 return postage, 8 minutes at $18/hour — staying standard expects $11.49 and Top Rated Plus expects $10.72.     Common questions

    Is eBay Top Rated Plus worth it?

    Top Rated Plus trades a typed final-value discount and a badge sell-through lift for seller-paid return labels, a higher return rate, and 1-day handling time. On the calculator defaults ($40 item, $6 shipping, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% discount, 60% vs 75% sell-through, 8% buyer-paid returns vs 16% free returns, 8 minutes at $18/hour) staying standard expects $11.49 and Top Rated Plus expects $10.72. Top Rated Plus keeps $0.77 less. Free returns can rise to about 12.9% before the badge matches staying standard. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to enroll.

    When does eBay Top Rated Plus beat staying standard?

    When the fee save is large enough to cover extra return labels. On a $180 item with $12 shipping, $70 cost, a $14 label, 6% buyer-paid returns versus 10% free returns, and 55% vs 72% sell-through, Top Rated Plus expects $48.74 against staying standard at $40.02 — $8.72 more.

    Does the Top Rated discount apply to promoted listings and international fees too?

    This page only discounts the final-value rate you type. Promoted-listings and international percentages stay on both paths. Enter the discount your account actually shows; this is not eBay’s live Top Rated schedule.
