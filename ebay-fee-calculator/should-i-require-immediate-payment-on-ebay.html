    What this checks

    Clothing, card, and small-goods sellers search “should I require immediate payment on eBay” because unpaid-item cases sit in Seller Hub for days and the listing setting looks free. Immediate payment looks like protection. It also turns away buyers who pay after they bid or after they get home, and that conversion hit can cost more than the unpaid cases you avoided.

    On the defaults — $40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $0.40 fixed, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, $5 return label, 10% returns, 8% unpaid, 55% later-pay sell-through, 45% immediate-pay sell-through, $0.35 relist, 20 minutes at $18, forty listings — allowing later checkout expects $8.88 and requiring immediate payment expects $8.15. Immediate payment keeps $0.73 less on this decision and −$29.39 across forty listings. Unpaid checkouts have to reach about 13.5%, or immediate payment has to convert about 49.1%, before those paths match. If a checkout starts, leftover on the later mix is $16.15 versus $18.11 when they pay now — $1.96 more — because an unpaid case never buys a label and keeps the item. That unpaid case still costs $6.35 ($0.35 relist plus $6.00 of time).

    Matching conversion already flips. Keep the same listing and hold later-pay sell-through at 45%. Immediate payment still expects $8.15; later checkout expects $7.27 — $0.88 less. A 20% unpaid rate expects $7.27 even at a 55% later-pay lift. A 50% immediate-pay sell-through flips require-now ahead ($9.05 vs $8.88). The unpaid cases you thought you avoided did not cover the checkouts you never started.

    It does not tell you to flip the listing setting, and it is not tax or financial advice. A seller-initiated offer, a coupon, local pickup, and a return-rate mix are different questions.     Common questions

    Should I require immediate payment on eBay?

    Immediate payment stops unpaid checkouts. It also turns away buyers who will not pay now. On the calculator defaults ($40 item, $6 shipping charged, 13.6% final value fee, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 10% returns, 8% unpaid, 55% later-pay sell-through, 45% immediate-pay sell-through, $0.35 relist, 20 minutes at $18) allowing later checkout expects $8.88 and requiring immediate payment expects $8.15. Immediate payment keeps $0.73 less after sell-through. On leftover — if a checkout starts — later mix leaves $16.15 and immediate payment leaves $18.11, $1.96 more, because unpaid cases never ship. Unpaid checkouts have to reach about 13.5%, or immediate payment has to convert about 49.1%, before those paths match. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to flip the listing setting.

    When does requiring immediate payment beat unpaid checkouts?

    When unpaid cases eat more leftover than the conversion hit returns. On the same $40 listing, matching the 45% immediate sell-through already loses on later checkout ($7.27 vs $8.15). A 20% unpaid rate expects $7.27 against $8.15 immediate even at a 55% later-pay lift. A 50% immediate-pay sell-through flips require-now ahead ($9.05 vs $8.88).

    Is immediate payment the same as local pickup or a seller coupon?

    No. Local pickup is a no-show after they committed to a meetup. A seller coupon changes the price for redeemers. A seller-initiated offer is a private sent price. Immediate payment keeps the listed price and changes who can start a checkout.
