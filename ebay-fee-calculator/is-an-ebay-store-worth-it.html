    Common questions

    Is an eBay store worth it?

    A store trades a monthly subscription for a lower final-value rate. On the calculator defaults ($40 item, $6 shipping, 13.6%+$0.40, $12 cost, $5 outbound, $1 pack, 40 sales, $0 current subscription) staying off a store keeps $853.76 a month. Example Starter at $7.95 / 12.95% keeps $857.77 — $4.01 more. Starter still ties around 27 of these sales. That is arithmetic on your inputs, not advice to subscribe.

    How many sales recover a $7.95 Starter store?

    On these defaults the 0.65-point rate cut saves $0.299 per $46 order. $7.95 / $0.299 is about 27 sales. At 80 of the same orders Starter keeps $15.97 more than staying off a store.

    Is this the same as Promoted Listings or Top Rated Plus?

    No. Promoted Listings Standard is a percent of a completed sale. Top Rated Plus trades a fee discount for free returns. This page compares a monthly subscription against the final-value rate it unlocks.
