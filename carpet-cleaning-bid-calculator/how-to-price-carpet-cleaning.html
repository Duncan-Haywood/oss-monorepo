CARPET CLEANING PRICING

How to price carpet cleaning without giving the drive away.

The search phrase is usually “carpet cleaning cost per room.” Almost every result is a lead form or a $99 three-bedroom special. This page is the method behind a number you can stand on, and it pairs with a free browser calculator that does the arithmetic locally.

The customer thinks in rooms. The clock does not.

A vacant 200 sq ft bedroom extracts faster than the same room after a dog has lived on it. Stairs are not a room. A sofa is not “included.” Forty minutes of windshield time is two wages if you brought a helper, and it is unpaid if you priced only the carpet. Quote three bedrooms as a package and you have hidden the lines that decide whether the stop prints money.

Build extraction first

Measure each room. Divide square feet by the rate your truck actually hits on that soil — vacant/light, normal household, or heavy / restoration. Those starting figures in the calculator are ballpark, not an IICRC standard. Time your own stops and replace them. The rooms that look the same on a floor plan are not the same job once soil is on the clock.

Then price the work a special cannot see

Flights of stairs, an extra extraction pass on pet-urine rooms plus the chemistry those rooms consume, each upholstery piece in minutes, furniture moving, and the round-trip drive. Payroll burden — employer tax, workers’ compensation, liability — belongs on the wage. A technician at $20 an hour is not a $20 hour.

Walk-away is cost, not the special

Overhead as a share of direct cost, then a target margin as a share of price, is how the customer number is built. A posted minimum (“$149 to roll the truck”) is a floor on that number. It is not a substitute for knowing cost. If a three-bedroom special is $129 and loaded labor plus chemistry plus the drive is $180, the special is a donation. Hold the walk-away and let the customer decide whether the house is worth it.

What this is not

Not a quote on your behalf, not an industry rate card, not legal or business advice. Rates vary by fibre, machine, region, and how long the soil has sat. Verify every figure against your own tickets before you send a price.

Common questions

How do carpet cleaners charge?

Many advertise a per-room or three-bedroom special. The work is extraction time (square feet divided by a soil-specific rate), plus stairs, extra passes for pet urine, upholstery, furniture moving, and the drive. A room price that ignores those lines underprices a lived-in house.

How much should I charge for carpet cleaning per room?

There is no single number. Price per room is the bid divided by the rooms after labor, chemistry, overhead, and the drive are known, not a starting rate. Two 200 sq ft bedrooms are not the same job if one has pet urine and the other is vacant.

Why do three-bedroom specials lose money?

A posted special assumes light soil, no stairs, no pets, no sofa, and a short drive. Stairs, a second extraction pass, upholstery, furniture moving, or a 40-minute deadhead can put loaded labor above the special before anyone is paid.
