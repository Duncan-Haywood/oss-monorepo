This calculator vs. ServiceMonster

Two different shapes: ServiceMonster is account-based field-service management software for running a carpet-cleaning company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one stop cost, given its rooms, soil level, and add-ons, before you quote it.

What each tool is for

ServiceMonster is field-service management software with roots going back to 2002 as software built specifically for carpet cleaners, headquartered in Bellingham, Washington. It markets today to carpet cleaning, window cleaning, pressure washing, and related cleaning trades. It combines customer and account management, job scheduling and technician routing, estimates and invoicing, and automated marketing campaigns into one subscription system, with a mobile app for crews in the field. ServiceMonster's own marketing says it is trusted by thousands of service pros, and industry coverage reports it was acquired in 2023 by Teamfront. It is sold as an ongoing subscription account. ServiceMonster's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — cleaning-industry field-service management software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for ServiceMonster appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what ServiceMonster does. It does not manage customer accounts, route technicians across a schedule, or run marketing campaigns, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one stop's rooms, each with its own square footage and a soil-specific extraction rate (vacant/light, normal household, heavy/restoration), with stairs as flights, a second extraction pass for pet-urine rooms, upholstery pieces timed in minutes, furniture moving, and round-trip drive time, plus loaded wage, chemistry cost, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the per-stop price, the price per room and per square foot, the effective hourly rate including the drive, the on-site rate without it, and the walk-away price below which the stop loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a copyable plain-text bid summary. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.
