This calculator vs. GorillaDesk

Two different shapes: GorillaDesk is an account-based platform for running a pressure-washing (or lawn-care, or pest-control) company's whole operation, including scheduling and invoicing a job once it is priced. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this wash job cost, before you offer it to the customer.

What each tool is for

GorillaDesk is a field-service management platform marketed to pressure washing, lawn care, pest control and other route-based service businesses. It is used to run CRM and customer records, scheduling and route planning, invoicing and payments, and a mobile app for technicians in the field, tied to one account used on an ongoing basis across every job the company runs. GorillaDesk's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity and category are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page. Public descriptions of that scheduling and invoicing software describe running and billing a job once its scope and price already exist; none describe working out what a new wash job should cost from surface square footage, a production rate, chemical cost and drive time before you quote it — which is the one thing this calculator does.

This calculator does none of what GorillaDesk does. It does not store a customer record, plan a route across a season, or invoice anyone. It takes one job's surfaces — each one's square footage and the production rate your crew actually hits — plus chemical cost, round-trip drive, setup, loaded technician wage, water, overhead, target margin and a minimum job, and turns that into the bid price, the price per square foot, the after-travel hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Should I use this calculator or GorillaDesk?

They do different jobs, and a pressure-washing business of any size will likely end up touching both.
