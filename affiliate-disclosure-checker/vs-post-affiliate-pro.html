Affiliate Disclosure Checker vs. Post Affiliate Pro's Disclosure Generator

Opposite halves of the same problem: Post Affiliate Pro's Disclosure Generator writes new disclosure text from a template, for someone who does not have wording yet. This page is a free, no-account checker for the other half — whether a post you already published actually has its disclosure, and whether it appears before the first affiliate link.

What each tool is for

Post Affiliate Pro's Disclosure Generator is a free page that produces new FTC-compliant disclosure text from a template — useful for a blog, YouTube channel, or social account that does not have disclosure wording yet. Post Affiliate Pro itself, of which the generator is one free page, is affiliate-network-management software sold under an ongoing subscription account to merchants running their own affiliate programs. Post Affiliate Pro's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific plan names or pricing, so only its well-documented category and identity are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This checker does the opposite job. It does not write or template any text. It scans a post you already published — pasted HTML, or a whole build-output directory via the CLI — for recognised affiliate-link signals (Amazon Associates tag= links, amzn.to, rel="sponsored", and known affiliate-network tracking domains), and reports whether a disclosure phrase actually appears before the first one, in the order a visitor reads the page, plus whether any Amazon link separately carries Amazon's own required exact statement. It runs free in any browser, and the same logic ships as a zero-dependency CLI you can wire into a pre-publish check. No account, no upload, nothing stored.
