This calculator vs. JobNimbus

Two different shapes: JobNimbus is an account-based CRM and job management platform for running an exterior contractor's whole lead-to-job pipeline. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one siding job cost, given its walls, waste, openings, and tear-off, before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

JobNimbus is a CRM and job management platform, founded in 2013 by Ben Hodson, Nick Wood, and Jason Wood, headquartered in Lehi, Utah. It is built for roofing, siding, gutter, solar, and other exterior contractors, covering lead and pipeline tracking, estimates and proposals, scheduling, job costing, and invoicing across a whole business, with a mobile app for crews in the field. It is an account-based platform: you sign up and use it as an ongoing service across a whole company, not a one-off calculator. JobNimbus's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a CRM and job management platform serving exterior trades including siding, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for JobNimbus appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what JobNimbus does. It does not track a lead, schedule a crew, or invoice a customer, and it stores no customer or job record on a server. It takes one job's wall area by elevation, openings, stories, and material (vinyl, fiber cement, engineered wood, or aluminum), with waste, tear-off, housewrap, linear trim, scaffold, and soffit/fascia as their own line items, plus loaded installer wage, payroll burden, travel, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the price, the price per billable square and per net square foot, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a price on the table, a check of whether it clears that walk-away floor, and a single printable customer proposal for that one job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or JobNimbus?

They do different jobs, and a siding company of any size will likely end up touching both eventually. JobNimbus, founded in 2013 by Ben Hodson, Nick Wood, and Jason Wood and headquartered in Lehi, Utah, is a CRM and job management platform built for roofing, siding, gutter, and other exterior contractors, covering lead tracking, estimates, scheduling, and invoicing across a whole business, sold as an ongoing subscription account. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from per-elevation wall measurements, waste, stories, openings, and tear-off, with linear trim, housewrap, dumpster, and add-ons as their own line items, plus loaded installer wage, payroll burden, travel, overhead, and target margin, it turns that into the price, the price per billable square, the effective hourly rate, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, before any of it touches a business-management system. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A siding company that already tracks leads, schedules crews, and invoices across every job is better served by JobNimbus or a comparable CRM; a contractor who wants an instant, private answer to what one siding job should cost from its walls, waste, and extras before opening a business system is in the right place here.
