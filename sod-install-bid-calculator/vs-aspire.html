This calculator vs. Aspire

Two different shapes: Aspire is an account-based business-management platform for running a landscape company's whole operation. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this sod job cost, given its grass, pallets, and prep — before you send the bid.

What each tool is for

Aspire is a cloud business-management platform built for landscape maintenance and construction contractors, irrigation companies, snow and ice management operations, and commercial cleaning businesses, and has been part of ServiceTitan since ServiceTitan's 2021 acquisition of it. It bundles CRM and lead tracking, estimating, scheduling and crew dispatch, purchasing, job costing, mobile crew time tracking, invoicing, and accounting integration into one ongoing subscription account, with plans tiered by a contractor's annual revenue rather than one flat published price — you request a demo to get a quote. Aspire's own site is not reachable from this session to confirm today's specific feature list, plan names, or pricing, so only its well-documented identity, category, and ownership are stated here: nothing about it is invented, and no dollar figure for it appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Aspire does. It does not manage a lead list, dispatch a crew across a season, or run payroll. It takes a lawn's length and width, grass choice (fescue, bermuda, or zoysia), a waste allowance, pallet coverage, soil prep, delivery method, slope, and watering visits, plus loaded crew wage, payroll burden, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the installed price, the pallets ordered after waste, the price per square foot and per pallet, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money — plus, if you already have a number on the table, a check of whether it clears your own break-even floor, and a printable customer proposal. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Aspire?

They do different jobs, and a landscape company of any size will likely end up touching both eventually. Aspire is a cloud business-management platform built for landscape maintenance and construction, irrigation, snow and ice management, and commercial cleaning contractors, and has been part of ServiceTitan since ServiceTitan's 2021 acquisition of it. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from a lawn's length and width, grass choice, waste allowance, pallet coverage, soil prep, delivery, slope, and watering visits, plus loaded crew wage, overhead, and target margin, it turns that into the installed price, the pallets ordered after waste, the price per square foot and per pallet, and the walk-away price below which the job loses money, plus a printable customer proposal for that one lawn — before any of it touches a business-management system. A landscape company that already tracks leads, schedules crews, and invoices across a whole season is better served by Aspire or a comparable platform; a crew that wants an instant, private answer to what one sod job should cost from its grass, pallets, and prep before opening a business system is in the right place here.
