This calculator vs. Onfleet

Two different shapes: Onfleet is an account-based dispatch and route-optimization platform for running a delivery fleet's day. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — does this one route, priced from its stops, wait, deadhead, and costs, actually pay before you quote it or accept it.

What each tool is for

Onfleet is a last-mile delivery management platform, founded in 2012 and headquartered in San Francisco. It is built for businesses that dispatch a fleet of drivers, and it specifically markets a medical delivery offering used by pharmacies, clinical labs, and hospitals — automated route optimization across many stops and drivers at once, live driver tracking, delivery-window and ETA notifications, and proof-of-delivery capture such as signatures, barcode scans, and ID verification to keep a chain-of-custody trail on every stop. It is an account-based platform: a business signs up and runs its whole delivery operation through it as an ongoing subscription, not a one-off calculator. Onfleet's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — a dispatch and route-optimization platform marketed to medical courier, pharmacy, and lab delivery operations, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for Onfleet appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what Onfleet does. It does not dispatch a driver, optimize a multi-stop sequence, track a vehicle live, or capture proof of delivery, and it stores no route or chain-of-custody record on a server. It takes one route's revenue, stop count, miles, scheduled time, wait time against a contracted grace period, deadhead miles and minutes to and from the first and last stop, loaded labor rate, vehicle cost per mile, per-stop consumables, overhead, and target margin, and turns that into the contribution margin, the margin per vehicle-hour, the cost per stop, and the per-stop rate the route needs to break even or clear a target margin — the arithmetic a courier needs before deciding whether to run the route at all. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or Onfleet?

They do different jobs, and a medical courier operation of any size may end up using both. Onfleet, founded in 2012 and headquartered in San Francisco, is a last-mile delivery platform that pharmacies, clinical labs, and courier fleets use for dispatch, real-time driver tracking, proof of delivery, and automated route optimization across every driver and every day, sold as an ongoing subscription account. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from a route's revenue, stops, miles, scheduled time, wait against a grace period, deadhead, loaded labor rate, vehicle cost per mile, per-stop consumables, overhead, and target margin, it turns that into the contribution margin, the margin per vehicle-hour, and the per-stop rate the route needs to break even or clear a target margin, before any of it touches a dispatch or tracking system. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A courier operation that already needs to dispatch drivers, optimize multi-stop routes live, and prove chain of custody across a fleet is better served by Onfleet or a comparable platform; a courier who wants an instant, private answer to whether one route's offer is worth taking before it is ever dispatched is in the right place here.
