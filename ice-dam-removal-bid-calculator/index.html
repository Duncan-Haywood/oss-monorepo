      Common questions

      Why can a per-linear-foot rate card lose money on a short job?

      Because sending a crew, a truck, and a steam machine costs close to the same whether the eave that needs clearing is 15 feet or 150 feet. A rate that clears its cost comfortably on a long run gets spread across far fewer feet on a short one, so the same rate can fall short of the job's real, largely fixed cost.

      What does breakEvenRatePerFoot actually tell me?

      It is the per-foot rate this specific job's footage would need, ignoring your minimum service charge, to just cover its real cost with no profit. If your rate card is below that number, only your minimum service charge is protecting you on a job this short — and if the minimum itself is set too low, the job is a real loss.

      Why is the emergency premium applied to labor and travel but not to fuel or equipment?

      Because the premium compensates for being available outside normal hours, not for extra difficulty or extra fuel use — the steam machine burns the same fuel at 2am as at 2pm. It only changes what billed crew time costs.

      Does anything about my job details or prices get uploaded?

      No. Every field on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and nothing you type is saved anywhere — it exists only in the page while it is open. Usage counting only tallies anonymous feature events, and you can turn it off with the button above.
