    Common questions
    
    
    What is s455 tax on a director's loan?
    
    
    A tax charged on a close company when a director or shareholder owes it money nine months and a day after the company's year end. It is 33.75% of the loan for loans made before 6 April 2026 and 35.75% for loans made on or after that date, and it is repaid to the company once the loan is cleared.
    
    
    When is s455 due?
    
    
    Nine months and one day after the end of the accounting period, the same date as the corporation tax. For a 31 March year end that is 1 January the following year.
    
    
    How do I avoid s455?
    
    
    Clear the loan before the due date, by repaying it, or by declaring a dividend or paying a bonus that is set against it. Repaying and then borrowing again within 30 days does not work, and larger repayments with arrangements to re-borrow are ignored.
    
    
    Is there tax on a director's loan over £10,000?
    
    
    Yes. If the balance goes over £10,000 at any point in the tax year, the interest you do not pay compared with HMRC's official rate is a taxable benefit for you, and the company owes Class 1A NIC on it. Paying interest at the official rate removes it.
    
    
    Is a dividend or a bonus cheaper for clearing the loan?
    
    
    Usually a dividend, because a bonus carries employee and employer NIC. The calculator shows both, after corporation tax relief on the bonus. A dividend needs distributable profits.
