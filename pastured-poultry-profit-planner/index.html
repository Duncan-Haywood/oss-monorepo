    Questions people ask

    Is raising pastured chickens profitable?

    It can be, but the margin per bird is thin and labour is the swing factor. The planner shows profit before and after paying yourself, so you can see the hourly return on your own time, not just a profit figure.

    How much does it cost to raise a pastured broiler?

    Chicks, feed, processing, bedding and fuel, then your time. Feed is usually the largest line. The planner prices each batch and gives a break-even price per dressed pound.

    How many batches a year can I run?

    That depends on your climate and brooding set-up. Enter the number you will actually run; the planner multiplies one batch out to a season and spreads equipment over its life.

    Is this agricultural or financial advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you type in. Licensing, processing rules, avian disease, predators, insurance, sales-tax and labelling are out of scope.
