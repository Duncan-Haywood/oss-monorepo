This calculator vs. NotaryGadget

Two different shapes: NotaryGadget is a subscription platform for running a notary's whole book of appointments, mileage, and tax records. This page is a free, private worksheet for one question — what should this one trip cost, and does the offered fee actually clear it, before you confirm the appointment.

What each tool is for

NotaryGadget is a subscription platform built specifically for loan signing agents. It tracks appointments, mileage, and expenses across a notary's whole calendar, generates invoices, and exports the records a 1099 filing needs at year end. NotaryGadget's own pricing page is not reachable from this session to confirm today's exact plan names or dollar figures, so no price for it is invented here: only its well-documented identity and category — notary-specific scheduling and bookkeeping software, sold as a subscription account — are stated, and no dollar amount for NotaryGadget appears on this page.

This calculator does none of what NotaryGadget does. It does not track a calendar, generate a 1099 export, or hold a record across jobs. It takes one trip — travel miles and drive time, appointment labor, a minimum fee, state-set signing fees, witness fees, after-hours work, waiting time, and print costs — and turns that into what the trip should cost, the effective hourly rate once vehicle cost is subtracted, and whether a signing service's flat offered fee actually clears that number. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type.

Common questions

Should I use this calculator or NotaryGadget?

They do different jobs, and a mobile notary of any size may end up using both. NotaryGadget is a subscription platform built specifically for loan signing agents: it tracks appointments, mileage, expenses, and invoicing across a notary's whole book of business, and exports the records a 1099 filing needs at year end. This calculator does one narrower thing: starting from travel miles and drive time, appointment labor, a minimum fee, state-set signing fees, witness fees, after-hours work, waiting time, and print costs, it turns that into what one trip should cost, the effective hourly rate once vehicle cost is subtracted, and whether a signing service's flat offered fee actually clears that number before you accept the job. It runs free in any browser, keeps no account, and uploads nothing you type. A notary already tracking a full calendar of appointments and preparing a year of tax records is better served by NotaryGadget or a comparable notary-specific tool; a mobile notary who wants an instant, private check on whether one offered trip fee is worth taking is in the right place here.
