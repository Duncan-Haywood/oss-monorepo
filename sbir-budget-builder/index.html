    Questions people ask

    What is an SBIR or STTR budget?

    The cost section of a Small Business Innovation Research or Technology Transfer proposal: personnel and fringe, equipment, materials, travel, subawards and consultants, indirect costs and a fee. Agencies use the SF424 R&R budget form or their own template, and the solicitation sets the limits.

    How much of the work must the small business do?

    Under the SBA policy directive the small business must perform at least two thirds (66.67%) of the work in SBIR Phase I and half in Phase II. For STTR the small business must perform at least 40% and the partnering research institution at least 30%. The solicitation controls; every figure here is an editable input.

    How much fee or profit can I charge?

    Most agencies allow a fee of up to about 7% of total costs. Some agencies and some topics allow less or none, so read the solicitation. The tool warns when the fee you enter is over the ceiling you enter.

    What is modified total direct cost (MTDC)?

    A common indirect cost base that excludes equipment and the part of each subaward above $25,000. Your negotiated or provisional indirect rate applies to the base your rate agreement names; pick the base that matches it.

    Does this submit or store my proposal?

    No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. You can download the budget as a CSV to paste into the agency's form.

    Is this grant-writing or legal advice?

    No. It is arithmetic on figures you type. Eligibility, cost allowability, rates and the final budget are decided by your solicitation, your accountant and the agency.

    Not decided

    Whether your company is eligible, whether a cost is allowable, your negotiated indirect rate, the agency's own template and page limits, cost-sharing, human-subjects or animal costs, and how an agency counts "percentage of work" for a particular topic. Rules and award ceilings change; the solicitation controls.
