    Common questions
    
    
    What is the Digital Services Act?
    
    
    Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 sets duties for online intermediaries (hosting, platforms, marketplaces, search engines) by category and size. It has applied to all in-scope services since 17 February 2024.
    
    
    Do small companies have to comply?
    
    
    Yes for the baseline duties (contact points, terms, notice and action for hosts). Micro and small enterprises (under 50 staff and at most EUR 10M turnover or balance sheet) are exempt from the heavier online platform and marketplace duties and from the annual transparency report, but not from publishing their monthly active EU users.
    
    
    What makes a platform very large?
    
    
    An average of 45 million or more monthly active EU recipients, followed by a Commission designation. Duties apply four months after designation and the exemption for small enterprises does not apply.
    
    
    Does it apply to a company outside the EU?
    
    
    Yes, if it offers intermediary services to people in the EU. Without an EU establishment, Art. 13 requires an EU legal representative.
